## Cross-Source Summary

**High convergence (score spread ≤ 8 points):**
- OUTPUT_GAP: 40–45 — all sources agree on below-potential, gap persisting.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 35–40 — all below full employment; Staff-T slightly lower on payroll disappointment.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 62–62 — Disc-T and Disc-M identical.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 63–65 — Disc-T and Disc-M effectively identical.
- PRODUCTIVITY: 62–63 — all three sources consistent.
- INFLATION_PRESSURE (excluding Staff-M): 38–42 — three of four sources tightly clustered.

**Moderate divergence (score spread 9–17 points):**
- INFLATION_PRESSURE full range: 38–48. Staff-M higher on observed "edged up" 12-month reading vs. Staff-T and discussion sources emphasizing recent moderation.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 50–58. Disc-T at 50 reflects the "mixed/not as strong as earlier" characterization; Staff-M at 58 reflects "rose sharply" and "significant pace" language.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 42–48. Largely methodological: discussion sources anchor on the sub-2% PCE level; staff sources emphasize "well-contained" framing.

**Notable divergence (score spread > 17 points):**
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS/TIGHTNESS: 25–43. Staff-T alone at 43, reflecting the Stockton presentation's forward-looking view of diminishing accommodation (fiscal drag, rate path). All other sources at 25–30 on current conditions being loose.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 40–52. Transcript captures the intensity of expressed uncertainty by individual speakers that the minutes summary compresses.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 15–32. The minutes did not capture Greenspan's carry-trade and mortgage-duration risk remarks or Geithner's external vulnerability framing.
- DOLLAR (across polarity dimensions): 22–50. Reflects the difference between observed intermeeting stability (Staff-M) and structural concern about external adjustment trajectory (Staff-T, Disc-T).
