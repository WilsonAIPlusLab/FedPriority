# Side-by-Side Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 1996-05-21

## Meeting Context
Mid-1996. Economy in a mature expansion. Unemployment ~5.4%, near estimated NAIRU. GDP growth strong in Q1 1996. Core inflation quiescent but food/energy prices rising. Fed cut rates 25bp in January 1996; debate now centers on whether to reverse. Long-term rates backed up ~80bp since January. Stock market rising sharply. Vote: unanimous hold, symmetric directive (Alternative B).
