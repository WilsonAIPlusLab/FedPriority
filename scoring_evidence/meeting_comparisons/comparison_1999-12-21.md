# FOMC Meeting Comparison: 1999-12-21

## Meeting Context

December 21, 1999. The U.S. economy is in its fourth consecutive year of 4%+ GDP growth. Labor markets are extremely tight (national unemployment ~4.1%, some districts at 3.25%). The dot-com equity boom is at full strength. The Fed has raised rates three times in 1999 (June, August, November). At this meeting the Committee holds rates steady at 5.5% — unanimously — but the explicit reason is Y2K: virtually all participants signal further tightening beginning at the February 2000 meeting. The meeting also features an extended procedural debate about reforming disclosure practices (symmetric vs. asymmetric directive language).
