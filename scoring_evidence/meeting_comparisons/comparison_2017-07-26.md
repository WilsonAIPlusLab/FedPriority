### Dimension 1: INFLATION_PRESSURE

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 32 ±7 [High confidence] | 35 ±7 [High confidence] |

**Center of gravity — Transcript**: The entire committee is focused on below-target inflation at 1.4% PCE, representing five consecutive years of undershooting. Most view it as likely transitory but several express growing concern about persistence. Unambiguously below-target inflation, squarely in the 26–40 range.

**Center of gravity — Minutes**: Inflation running clearly below 2% target with downside risks, though most expect medium-term return to target. Idiosyncratic factors provide partial explanation.

**Representative quotes — Transcript**:
- Mester: "Core PCE inflation also stood at 1.4 percent in May... some of the weakness reflects some special factors, including drops in the prices of cell phone service plans in March and the prices of prescription drugs in April."
- Kashkari: "core 12-month PCE inflation is running at 1.4 percent, which, in my mind, is a significant shortfall below our 2 percent target... Since the FOMC adopted its 2 percent target in 2012, headline PCE inflation has averaged 1.2 percent."
- Brainard: "both overall and core PCE prices are estimated to have risen 1.4 percent for the year through June, well short of our objective... the decline occurs against the backdrop of a sustained shortfall of inflation from our target despite a sharp improvement in resource utilization."
- Rosengren: [described as emphasizing upside risks from very low unemployment, cautioning against "betting on quiescent inflation with a sub-4 percent unemployment rate."]

**Representative quotes — Minutes**:
- "Overall inflation and the measure excluding food and energy prices had declined and were running below 2 percent."
- "Many participants noted that much of the recent decline in inflation had probably reflected idiosyncratic factors."
- "Many participants, however, saw some likelihood that inflation might remain below 2 percent for longer than they currently expected, and several indicated that the risks to the inflation outlook could be tilted to the downside."
- "Most participants indicated that they expected inflation to pick up... to stabilize around the Committee's 2 percent objective over the medium term."
