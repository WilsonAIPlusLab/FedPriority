# Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 1998-12-22

## Meeting Context
December 1998. The Fed had cut rates 75bp in three moves (September–November 1998) in response to the LTCM collapse, Russian default, and Asian financial contagion. By December, domestic financial markets had largely recovered; the economy was growing above trend with very tight labor markets and subdued inflation. Brazil remained an external risk. The Committee voted unanimously to hold the federal funds rate at 4.75% with a symmetric directive.
