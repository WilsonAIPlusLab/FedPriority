# Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 1995-03-28

**Meeting context**: March 1995. The Fed had tightened 300bp from February 1994 to February 1995, bringing the funds rate to 6%. This meeting was a pause to assess, amid early signs of slowdown in interest-sensitive sectors (housing, autos, retail) while core inflation was creeping upward and the dollar was hitting post-WWII lows against the yen and mark.
