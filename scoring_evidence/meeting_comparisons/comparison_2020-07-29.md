# FOMC Meeting 2020-07-29: Transcript vs Minutes Evidence Comparison

**Meeting date:** 2020-07-29
**Era context:** COVID-19 recession. Economy in early partial recovery from catastrophic Q2 2020 contraction. Federal funds rate at ELB (0–0.25%). Large-scale asset purchases active. Unemployment approximately 11%. Core PCE near 1%. Meeting notable for finalizing the new monetary policy framework (average inflation targeting / FAIT) and for a dedicated financial stability go-round.

---

## IMPORTANT SOURCE NOTES

**Discussion Minutes (Source B):** The minutes file for this meeting is severely truncated. It contains only an attendee list, a brief framework review section, and the beginning of the SOMA manager report. The core participant discussion sections — "Participants' Views on Current Conditions and the Economic Outlook," the policy discussion, and the vote — are entirely absent. All discussion-minutes scores carry Low confidence with very wide uncertainty bands. Scores are based on minimal participant language ("persistent disinflationary pressures," "Participants agreed") supplemented by era context.

MEADE presented a paragraph-by-paragraph walkthrough of the Draft Statement on Longer-Run Goals and Monetary Policy Strategy. No Tealbook/Greenbook macroeconomic data or forecasts are present. All staff-transcript dimensions are scored N/A under the rubric's special-topic meeting rule.

**Staff Minutes (Source D):** Contains a complete set of staff sections: "Staff Review of the Economic Situation," "Staff Review of the Financial Situation," and "Staff Economic Outlook." This is the only source with substantive, scoreable economic and financial assessment content beyond the participant transcript.
