## Cross-Source Summary

### Areas of Agreement
- **OUTPUT_GAP**: Tight 4-point range (23–27) across all four sources. All describe active GDP contraction with substantial slack.
- **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: 3-point range (28–31). All four sources agree on rapid, broad-based deterioration from worst payroll losses since the mid-1970s, unemployment at 5.4% and rising.
- **INFLATION_PRESSURE** (within rubric pairs): Discussion sources agree at 25/25; staff sources agree at 35/35. The 10-point gap between rubric types reflects different emphasis (deflation-risk framing in discussion vs. "subdued and declining" framing in staff).
- **HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: Both discussion sources firmly dovish (14 and 20). Gap of 6 points attributable to transcript capturing stronger individual dovish statements; minutes including data-dependence caveats.
- **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Staff transcript and discussion transcript both score 55; staff minutes 52. Convergence on "modest concern, not acute distress."

### Areas of Divergence
- **FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS vs. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Discussion sources: 60/60. Staff sources: 42/41. This ~18-point gap is partially structural (the discussion rubric's single FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS dimension aggregates what the staff rubric separates into FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS plus CREDIT_SPREADS). Participants emphasize impaired passthrough and credit spread widening; staff emphasize the real funds rate below equilibrium and steep yield curve.
- **PRODUCTIVITY**: Discussion minutes (60) is a significant outlier relative to the other three sources (38–40). The minutes' framing of long-term productivity prospects as "bright" with near-term slowing as secondary drives the above-neutral score; the other sources weight the near-term "significant slowing" more heavily.
- **BANKING_STRESS**: Discussion transcript (28, U.S. banks sound per examiner reports) vs. staff transcript (47, near-neutral due to limited direct U.S. bank coverage and heavy weighting of Japan banking discussion). The discrepancy reflects different information available to each source rather than disagreement about U.S. bank health per se.
- **DISSENT_INTENSITY**: Discussion transcript (40) vs. discussion minutes (30). Transcript reveals five participants with explicit reservations, two of whom (Jordan, Minehan) entertained no change; minutes condense this to "three favored smaller move" and "a number noted close call."
- **UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: Transcript (75) vs. minutes (65). The transcript records multiple individual statements invoking "unprecedented situation," model failure, and deflationary scenarios by name; the minutes translate this into structured categories of uncertainty sources.
