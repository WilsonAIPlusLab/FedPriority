**Notable gaps (gap > 10 points):**

- DOLLAR_CONCERN/STRENGTH: 20 (Disc-T) vs. 45 (Disc-M) vs. 28–40 (staff) — largest cross-source divergence; reflects mix of framing differences and genuine scale interpretation variation

**Structural observation:** The discussion transcript consistently produces higher or more differentiated scores than the minutes on dimensions requiring qualitative judgment (stability emphasis, hawkish lean, dollar concern), while the minutes and staff sources converge more tightly on quantitative dimensions (labor, financial conditions, credit). This is consistent with the transcript preserving individual-voice heterogeneity that minutes synthesis compresses.
