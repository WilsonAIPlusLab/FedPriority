# FOMC Meeting 2015-09-17: Transcript vs Minutes Evidence Comparison

**Meeting context**: September 16–17, 2015. Two-day meeting. Final decision: hold at 0–0.25% (alternative B). One formal voting dissent (Lacker, preferred alt C). Strong preferences for alternative C from several other members. Meeting set against August 2015 China devaluation and global equity market volatility.

**Staff note**: The staff transcript (Day 1, special-topic session on reinvestment strategy and financial market developments) contains no standard economic assessment. All staff transcript dimensions scored N/A by the scorer. Staff minutes contain a full economic assessment and are the sole source for Part B.
