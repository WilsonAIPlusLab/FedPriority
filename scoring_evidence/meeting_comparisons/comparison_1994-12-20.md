## Notable Gaps and Observations

1. **FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS gap (Discussion, 12 points):** The transcript captures Greenspan's direct observation that banks are not noticing higher interest rates and are easing credit terms. The minutes use the phrase "generally supportive of vigorous economic activity," reflecting a more compressed summary. The gap suggests the transcript conveys more nuance about the divergence between policy rates and actual lending conditions.

2. **FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS gap (Discussion, 13 points):** The transcript includes extended testimony from Parry on Orange County and from Phillips on Bankers Trust mark-to-market risks. The minutes condense this to brief language about "fairly readily weathered" disruptions. The transcript reflects these concerns more prominently.

3. **DISSENT_INTENSITY gap (Discussion, 7 points):** Greenspan's direct acknowledgment of "a very sharp split on symmetric versus asymmetric" in the transcript is absent from the minutes, which enumerate preferences without characterizing the internal split with the same directness.

4. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH gap (Staff, 21 points):** The largest discrepancy in the full comparison. See note under B6.

5. **Staff vs. Discussion alignment:** Staff scores for OUTPUT_GAP (56-58) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (52) are consistently below the discussion scores (61-64 and 60-64 respectively), consistent with the staff presenting aggregate national data while participants report district-level observations of more extreme tightness.

6. **ANOMALY FLAG (Staff transcript):** The staff transcript scorer flagged an anomaly: Orange County bankruptcy and Mexico peso devaluation on meeting day dominated portions of the staff discussion. This context is relevant to interpreting the staff transcript scores, particularly DOLLAR_STRENGTH and FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS.
