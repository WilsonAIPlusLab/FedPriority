## Part A: Participant Discussion

### Dimension 1: INFLATION_PRESSURE

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 64 ±6 (High confidence) | 62 ±7 (High confidence) |

**Transcript evidence:**
- Geithner: "Almost every measure of underlying inflation that we look at is now above the level we generally associate with price stability."
- Bies: "core PCE up 2.4 percent in the past couple of quarters. We have core CPI up 2.7 percent."

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** Broad consensus that inflation is above the implicit ~2% target, running in the mid-2% range on core measures, with multiple participants citing elevated readings across PCE, CPI, and GDP deflators. **Minutes evidence:**
- "Core consumer inflation lately had been a little higher than expected."

- "Recent developments suggested that upside risks to inflation had risen somewhat since the time of the March meeting."

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** Participants perceive inflation as modestly above comfort levels with rising upside risks from energy, commodities, and a weaker dollar, though restraining factors (moderate wages, productivity, wide margins) provide some offset. Most expected core inflation to remain near recent levels.
