**Transcript evidence:**

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 63 ±6 [High] | 60 ±6 [High] |

**Transcript evidence:**
- Kaplan: "contacts continue to indicate they are facing challenges in finding people to hire. Two-thirds of our surveyed firms report difficulty in hiring workers. These firms expect continued wage pressure."
- Mester: "all of our contacts have consistently told us that their biggest concern is the difficulty in finding workers, and this is beginning to affect the level of activity. One-third of District contacts reported that tight labor market conditions have impeded keeping pace with recent increases in product demand."
- Mester: "unemployment rate is low, and the job openings rate is at its highest level since the series started in 2000."
- Rosengren: "wages have continued to rise, and I view the increase in excess of the sum of inflation and productivity as being driven more by tight labor markets."

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** Labor market firmly tight. Unemployment at 3.8%, well below natural rate estimates of 4.0–4.5%. Widespread employer difficulty finding workers. Wage growth at 3.4% annualized. However, not at the extreme tightness of 1999–2000 or 2022–23; some discussion of possible benchmark revision downwards in employment.

**Transcript minority views:** Kashkari: "I continue to believe that there's still slack in the labor market." Harker notes benchmark revisions.

---

**Minutes evidence:**
- "Participants cited evidence that conditions remained strong, including the very low unemployment rate, a further increase in the labor force participation rate, a low number of layoffs, near-record levels of job openings."
- "Reports from business contacts predominantly pointed to continued strong labor demand, with firms offering both higher wages and more nonwage benefits to attract workers."
- "A few participants cited the combination of muted inflation pressures and expanding employment as a possible indication that some slack remained in the labor market."

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** Labor market clearly tight with strong demand, low unemployment, firms competing for workers. Beyond full employment by most accounts.

**Minutes minority views:** A few see possible remaining slack given muted inflation, pulling the band down slightly.
