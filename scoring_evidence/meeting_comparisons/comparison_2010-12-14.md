### Dimension 1: INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript — Score: 22 ±5 [High confidence]**

Quotes:
- Rosengren: "core CPI inflation rate for the last 12 months, at 0.6 percent, is the lowest since 1957 when the index began. The diffusion index and a wide variety of inflation measures provide ample evidence of continued disinflation."
- Moore (SF First VP): "measures of core inflation…running below 1 percent…inflation is on a clear downward trend, and measures of underlying inflation are running below 1 percent. Many of our business contacts report rising prices of materials…but the economic climate does not appear to support much pass-through."
- Yellen: "the data on core inflation have once again surprised to the downside, and wage increases remain very well contained. Even if temporary factors are partly responsible…the pace of core inflation still seems to be moving downward."
- Bernanke: "core inflation measures continue to drift down with the slowdown in price increases quite well spread across categories."

Center of gravity: Near-unanimity that inflation is well below the 2% PCE target and still declining. Core PCE at ~0.6–1.0%, lowest in decades. Inflation expectations have risen somewhat (TIPS breakevens) but from very low levels and not alarming. Minority: Hoenig and Plosser express concern about future inflation from QE, but their concern is prospective, not about current inflation being elevated.

**Minutes — Score: 24 ±5 [High confidence]**

Quotes:
- "Underlying inflation had continued trending downward... slowdown in price increases evident across categories of goods and services"
- "Most participants expected that underlying measures of inflation would bottom out around current levels and then move gradually higher as the recovery progresses"
- "Inflation would remain for some time below levels judged to be most consistent... with maximum employment and price stability"

Center of gravity: Participants see inflation well below target, with broad-based deceleration, though deflation risk is seen as receding. Minority: "A few participants pointed to the risk that... the Federal Reserve's balance sheet and the sustained low level of short-term interest rates could trigger undesirable increases in inflation expectations."

**Transcript — Score: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**

Quotes:
- Bernanke: "unemployment is, after all, 9.8 percent, which is 0.3 percentage point higher than it was in June 2009 when the recession officially ended…we are still missing our objectives by a wide margin…we are starting from a very deep hole, and we remain far from our objectives."

Center of gravity: The economy is firmly below potential. Recovery is ongoing but modest, with Tealbook projecting unemployment remaining above 9% through at least 2011. No participant argued the economy was at or above potential.

**Minutes — Score: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**

Center of gravity: Economy clearly operating below potential with persistent slack, but some improvement noted (retail, manufacturing). Consistent with the 2010-14 recovery anchor range. Minority: "A few mentioned the possibility that growth could pick up more rapidly than expected."

### Dimension 6: HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN

**Transcript — Score: 20 ±5 [High confidence]**

Quotes:
- Vote: 10 Yes (continue QE2 at $600B), 1 No (Hoenig). Not a single voting member supported scaling back or stopping.
- Evans: "Inflation is too far below our mandate and resource slack remains very large…I do support alternative B." Open to stronger forward guidance and additional tools.
- Rosengren: "I remain concerned that disinflationary pressures may not abate quickly…I can easily envision circumstances in which we would want to expand our large-scale asset purchases next year."

Center of gravity: Firmly dovish. QE2 ongoing, rates at zero with "extended period" guidance, 10/11 vote to maintain. Several participants explicitly open to more accommodation if needed. Minority: Hoenig (formal dissent), Plosser (accepts B reluctantly), Lacker (prefers C — scaling back), Fisher (accepts B but not a fan), Warsh (accepts B with reservations).

**Minutes — Score: 20 ±6 [High confidence]**

Quotes:
- "Nearly all Committee members agreed to continue expanding the Federal Reserve's holdings of longer-term securities"
- "The Committee also decided to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 0 to 1/4 percent and to reiterate its expectation that economic conditions are likely to warrant exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate for an extended period"

Center of gravity: Strong dovish consensus maintaining QE2 and near-zero rates. The Committee is in active accommodation mode. Minority: "One member dissented from the Committee's policy decision, judging that, in light of the improving economy, a continued high level of [asset purchases was unwarranted]."
