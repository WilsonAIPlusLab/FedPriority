# FOMC Meeting Evidence Comparison — 1995-12-19

**Meeting action**: 25bp ease (funds rate 6.00% → 5.75%). Unanimous vote; no formal dissents, though a number of members preferred no change.

---

## Part A: Discussion-Level Evidence (Transcript vs. Minutes)

### Dimension 1 — INFLATION_PRESSURE

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 40 ±7 [High] | 38 ±7 [High] |
| **Center-of-gravity** | Inflation ~3% CPI well-contained and not accelerating; expectations declining; participants generally comfortable. Some (Broaddus, Melzer) dislike 3% as a permanent state. | Members perceive inflation subdued and below expectations, with favorable recent trends; high resource utilization provides a floor; several note upside risks. |

**Transcript quotes:**
- BOEHNE: "The outlook for inflation remains about as it has been, neither accelerating nor decelerating much during the next year or so... price pressures remain remarkably subdued."
- KELLEY: "inflation continues to be remarkably well contained. I am particularly impressed with the way that unit labor costs continue to behave. Productivity is still growing very nicely, and the ECI is flat."
- GREENSPAN: "I am not worried about product price inflation... the CPI is currently running under expectations or forecasts."

**Minutes quotes:**
- "consumer price increases had remained relatively subdued and below expectations in recent months, despite the generally high levels of utilization of both labor and capital resources"
- "Several pointed to subdued increases in labor compensation and to anecdotal indications that upward pressures on wages and benefits remained scattered despite tightness in many labor markets"
- "many members anticipated that inflation would remain relatively stable... and some believed that it might record a somewhat improved performance"
- "several did refer to risks in that direction, including the possibility of greater pressures on resources stemming from faster than currently anticipated economic growth"

### Dimension 5 — FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 37 ±8 [High] | 28 ±5 [Moderate] |
| **Center-of-gravity** | Financial conditions loose — long-term rates fallen sharply, stock market surging, credit readily available. Real short rate perceived as restrictive due to declining inflation expectations. | Financial conditions clearly accommodative — ample credit, strong equity/bond markets, lower interest rates. Only tightening element is elevated real funds rate from declining inflation. |

**Transcript quotes:**
- MELZER: "Aggregate credit remains readily available... growth in M2 since the beginning of 1995 seems consistent with an economy growing at potential"
- KELLEY: "Credit availability still seems to be quite ample"
- GREENSPAN: "we are at the edge of a bond and stock bubble" and "the sharp runup in stock prices is very heavily determined by the climb in long-term bond prices"
- HOENIG: "lower yields in long-term debt markets have been stimulative"

**Minutes quotes:**
- "favorable effects on business and consumer spending of lower interest rates, higher equity prices, and an ample availability of credit"
- "Strong profits and cash flows, along with the ample availability of financing on attractive terms"
- One concern: "real federal funds rate, was becoming somewhat restrictive as inflation and inflationary expectations moderated"
- "a few members also expressed concern about the possible repercussions in financial markets of an easing action that would follow an already strong rally"

### Dimension 6 — HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 37 ±8 [High] | 38 ±7 [High] |
| **Center-of-gravity** | Clear majority supports 25bp ease as adjustment to declining inflation expectations. Several prefer no change but acquiesce. No tightening advocates. **Transcript quotes:**

- YELLEN: "the current level of the real funds rate is on the high side... monetary policy should be forward-looking when we are lowering interest rates"
- BROADDUS: "I would come out with Cathy and Tom... our best move today is no move"
- STERN: "I favor no change in policy at this meeting"

**Minutes quotes:**
- "all the members either favored or could accept a slight easing in the degree of pressure on reserve positions"
- "A number of members, though willing to accept a slight easing, preferred an unchanged policy stance"
- "they indicated that in light of the uncertainties that were involved and the small amount of easing that was proposed they would not dissent from the majority position"

### Dimension 8 — FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS

- "The risks to the outlook seemed generally in balance, and the direction of the next policy move was not clear in the view of some members"
