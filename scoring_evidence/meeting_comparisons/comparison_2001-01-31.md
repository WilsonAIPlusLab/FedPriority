## Staff Transcript Anomaly

The staff transcript for this meeting is a confirmed special-topic meeting exception. The January 30-31, 2001 two-day session was entirely devoted to the Federal Reserve asset portfolio study. Staff presentations by Kohn (portfolio study overview) and Fisher (short-run strategy) addressed balance sheet composition, credit allocation concerns, Treasuries-only vs. private assets tradeoffs, and RP mechanics. No Greenbook/economic outlook was presented at this session. The standard economic and financial scoring dimensions are all N/A for this source.

The staff minutes (`minutes_2001-01-30.md`) do contain a standard economic review section ("The information reviewed at this meeting indicated...") drawn from the Greenbook/staff analysis and cover inflation, output, labor, spending, and financial conditions in full.
