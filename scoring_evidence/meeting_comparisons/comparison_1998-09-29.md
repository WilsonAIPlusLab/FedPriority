# Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 1998-09-29

**Meeting context**: September 1998 — height of the LTCM/Russian crisis. The Committee voted unanimously for a 25bp rate cut. The asymmetric directive (toward ease) passed by a small majority. LTCM near-collapse and its systemic implications were discussed at length.
