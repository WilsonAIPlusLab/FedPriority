**Transcript anomaly**: The discussion transcript file (pages 164–195 of 253) covers ONLY the special session on PDCF/TSLF liquidity facility extension and regulatory reform — NOT the standard economic go-around or the fed funds rate policy decision. Accordingly, the transcript contains essentially no substantive participant discussion of inflation, output, labor markets, consumer spending, or the monetary policy stance. The minutes document, by contrast, contains the full synthesized record including the economic go-around. This structural asymmetry is the dominant feature of this meeting's cross-source comparison.
