**TRANSCRIPT — Score: 60 ±7 | Confidence: High**

Quotes:
- Yellen: "core PCE price inflation has slowed over the past year; the most recent reading of 1.8 percent over the past twelve months is down 0.3 from the preceding year...core PCE inflation running at only 1.6 percent over the past six months."
