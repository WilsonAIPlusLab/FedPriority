## Part A: Discussion — Side-by-Side Evidence

### D1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Transcript: 30 ±7 [High confidence] | Minutes: 42 ±8 [High confidence]**

| Transcript (Speaker Quotes) | Minutes (Synthesized) |
|---|---|
| GREENSPAN: "There is no evidence of which I am aware of any acceleration in prices... inflation effectively at zero" | "members commented that they saw no evidence of any acceleration in price inflation despite the continuing strength of the economic expansion and the tightness of labor markets" |
| MCTEER: "Contemporary measures of inflation remain flat. The CPI rose 0.1 percent in both January and February; inflation in the pipeline and leading indicators of inflation continue to be favorable" | "increases in unit labor costs...had declined to a very low rate over the past year" |
| MCDONOUGH: "with a complete absence of inflation" | "many of the members commented that the risks to their forecasts were tilted toward the eventual emergence of somewhat greater inflation pressures" |
| MEYER: "stronger-than-expected growth and lower-than-expected inflation" | [difficulty or inability of most business firms to raise prices implied throughout] |

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** Participants universally see current inflation as very low, well below any reasonable target, with no signs of acceleration in the pipeline. Several use "zero" or "absent" language.

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** Current inflation is quiescent — no acceleration, firms unable to raise prices — but forward risks of pressure are recognized by many. Slightly below midpoint reflects current benign readings with acknowledged upside risk.

**Gap note:** The 12-point gap reflects the transcript's "virtually zero / complete absence" language pulling the score lower, versus the minutes' greater forward-risk emphasis ("many... risks tilted toward emergence of greater inflation pressures") pulling the score higher.
