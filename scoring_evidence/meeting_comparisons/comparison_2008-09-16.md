# FOMC Meeting 2008-09-16: Transcript vs Minutes Evidence Comparison

**Meeting context**: September 16, 2008 — the day after Lehman Brothers filed for bankruptcy. The Fed held the funds rate at 2.0%. AIG was also in distress. This is one of the most acute moments of the Global Financial Crisis.

---

| **Score** | 65 ±8 [High confidence] | 57 ±8 [Moderate confidence] |

**Transcript evidence:**
- Bullard: "The headline CPI inflation rate...is about 6¼ percent year-to-date. This is against the federal funds target of 2 percent."
- Lacker: "we're still in the midst of an upsurge in core inflation, which the Greenbook expects is going to tick up near 3 percent this quarter."
- Yellen: "core inflation will remain uncomfortably high for a while longer."

**Minutes center-of-gravity**: Participants acknowledge inflation has been elevated and firms are still passing through costs, but falling commodity prices, stronger dollar, and weakening labor market are expected to bring moderation. Net perception: modestly above target but improving.
