### D1. INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript: 18 ±5 [High confidence]**

Center-of-gravity: Virtually all participants accept that inflation has fallen sharply and that deflation risk is a real concern warranting explicit policy response; the debate is about how prominently to communicate it, not whether it exists.

Representative quotes:
- MR. FISHER: "minus 1.7 on the headline rate"
- MR. LACKER: "monetary policy at the zero bound is all about discouraging expectations of deflation. If we haven't tried first announcing an explicit inflation objective, we don't have any excuses if we fail to prevent a fall into a deflationary equilibrium."
- CHAIRMAN BERNANKE: "the risk of inflation's declining below optimal levels" — bracketed language under discussion; "inflationary pressures have diminished appreciably" (adopted per Stern's edit)
- MR. BULLARD: advocates inflation target to "reassure and anchor expectations in this very fluid situation, which may rapidly unravel on us"

Minority views (transcript): Mr. Hoenig: "I do not think that we should have inflation below optimal in this statement. I don't think we're there."

---

**Minutes: 15 ±5 [High confidence]**

Center-of-gravity: Participants perceive a sharp decline in inflation pressures with meaningful deflation risk, placing this firmly in the "meaningful deflation risk; well below target" range.

Representative quotes:
- "Inflation pressures had diminished appreciably as energy and other commodity prices dropped and economic activity slumped."
- "Several saw risks that inflation could drop for a time below rates they viewed as most consistent over time with the Federal Reserve's dual mandate."
- "Some members saw significant risks that inflation could decline and persist for a time at uncomfortably low levels."

Minority views (minutes): "Some saw inflation leveling out near desired levels."

### D2. OUTPUT_GAP

**Transcript: 12 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**

Center-of-gravity: Participants unanimously treat the current economic contraction as historically severe — the zero lower bound has been reached, full QE is being launched, emergency lending programs are proliferating — all consistent with the deepest output-gap anchor.

Representative quotes:
- MR. ROSENGREN: "The bleak outlook calls for aggressive action."
- MS. PIANALTO: "counteract the powerful forces that are weakening our economy and financial markets, we should provide maximum monetary policy stimulus as quickly as possible"

- MR. PLOSSER: "troubling times...it's even more important that we be as transparent and clear as possible"

Minority views (transcript): No participant argues the economy is performing near potential. Confidence is Moderate because the transcript focuses on policy/communications rather than economic assessment; specific GDP/output data are not quoted by members.
