|---|---|---|
| **Score** | 26 ± 8 [Moderate confidence] | 27 ± 8 [High confidence] |

**Transcript — Center-of-gravity:** The consensus is that current inflation is below target — "undesirably low" per Yellen — but a vocal hawkish minority (Plosser, Bullard, Kocherlakota, Hoenig) warns of medium-term upside risk from TIPS break-even drift, large balance sheet, and potential commodity price pressure.

**Minutes quotes:**

- "Wage growth continued to be restrained, and unit labor costs were still falling."

- "Some noted that the high degree of slack in resource utilization posed a downside risk to inflation."
