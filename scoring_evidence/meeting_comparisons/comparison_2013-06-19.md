**Minutes: 30 ±7 [High confidence]**

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** Participants broadly recognized that inflation was running below the 2% target on a broad-based basis, with most expecting it to remain below target for some time, placing this firmly in the "below target; disinflation" range.
