### D1. INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript score: 22 ±5 [High confidence]**
**Minutes score: 28 ±8 [High confidence]**

#### Transcript — verbatim quotes

- Pianalto: "The 12-month increase in the median CPI has fallen precipitously, to a record low of 0.6 percent in March—evidence of disinflationary forces at work."
- Yellen: "I expect core PCE prices to rise a little less than 1 percent this year... the very low recent readings on core measures of inflation are disconcerting."
- Rosengren: "My forecast also has continued disinflation, and many of the models used in my research department imply more disinflation than in the Greenbook. Wages and salaries continue to decline, and employers I have talked to report little pricing power."
- Bernanke summary: "Inflation has generally been slowing and is expected to stay low."

**Transcript center of gravity:** Participants broadly describe inflation as well below target with ongoing disinflation, driven by substantial slack in labor and product markets; only a small minority flags medium-term upside risks via long-run inflation expectations.

**Transcript minority views:** Plosser and Lacker express concern about medium-term upside inflation risk; Kocherlakota cites the 10-year-10-year forward breakeven rising to near 3 percent.

#### Minutes — verbatim quotes

- "Participants cited a wide array of evidence as indications that underlying inflation remained subdued."
- "Most participants anticipated that substantial resource slack and stable longer-term inflation expectations would likely keep inflation subdued for some time."
- "The latest readings on core inflation... were generally lower than they had anticipated."
- "Inflation remaining below rates that would be consistent in the longer run with the Federal Reserve's dual objectives."

**Minutes center of gravity:** Participants perceive inflation as subdued and below the longer-run objective, with the latest readings even lower than expected. Substantial slack expected to keep inflation low.

**Minutes minority views:** "Some" saw downside risks (deflation concern); "others" saw upside risks from commodity prices and inflation expectations.

### D2. OUTPUT_GAP

**Transcript score: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
**Minutes score: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**

#### Transcript — verbatim quotes

- Rosengren: "we are very far from attaining either element of our mandate... we will not achieve either element of our mandate over the period we usually forecast."

**Transcript minority views:** Plosser and Lacker are more upbeat (Plosser: "growing confidence that the recovery is on a sustainable path"; risks "tilted to the upside"), but do not argue the output gap is closed.

- "Substantial resource slack" cited multiple times.

**Minutes center of gravity:** Economy is recovering but far below potential, with "quite elevated" slack expected to persist. Moderate growth pace insufficient to close the gap quickly.

**Minutes minority views:** "Some of the recent data on economic activity had been better than anticipated" — modest upside but not enough to change the picture.
