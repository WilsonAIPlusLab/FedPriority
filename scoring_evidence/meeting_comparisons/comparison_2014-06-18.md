### Dimension 1: INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript — Score: 40 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**

> "I think inflation and inflation pressures remain low. I think they are low for the PCE. I think they are low for the CPI. I think they are low around the world." — Evans

> "Inflation appears to be moving up, with the three-month core PCE increasing 1.8 percent... trending upward toward the Committee's target." — Plosser

> "recent data releases show that the deceleration we saw last year has ended, and we're seeing a broad-based increase in inflation over the past few months." — George

> "many of you noted the higher recent readings we've seen on core CPI and PCE inflation... as providing positive evidence that inflation is in fact moving back up toward our goal." — Chair Yellen (summary)

**Center-of-gravity (Transcript):** Inflation is below target (~1.5-1.8% PCE) but recently firming; the majority view is slow, gradual return to 2% with notable dovish concern about persistent below-target outcomes and hawkish minority seeing faster convergence or overshoot.

---

**Minutes — Score: 39 ±7 [High confidence]**

> "Inflation was still seen as running below the Committee's longer-run objective, longer-run inflation expectations remained stable"

> "readings on a range of price measures... appeared to provide evidence that inflation had moved up recently from low levels earlier in the year"

> "Some participants expressed concern about the persistence of below-trend inflation"

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Inflation below 2% target but showing recent improvement from earlier lows. The center of gravity acknowledges below-target inflation with gradual improvement expected.

**Minority views (Minutes):** "Some others expected a faster pickup in inflation or saw upside risks to inflation and inflation expectations because they anticipated a more rapid decline in economic slack."

### Dimension 2: OUTPUT_GAP

**Transcript — Score: 38 ±7 [High confidence]**

> "Even with the assumed strong second quarter, real GDP growth for the first half of this year has been quite disappointing... 2014 real GDP growth of only 2.1 percent." — Rosengren

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Economy recovering but still operating below potential, with elevated slack. Q1 weakness was a setback but Q2 is rebounding. Growth expected to be moderate, gradually closing the gap.

### Dimension 3: LABOR_TIGHTNESS

**Transcript — Score: 40 ±8 [High confidence]**

> "substantial labor market slack persists." — Williams

> "The question of the amount of remaining labor market slack will be central... the improving but still-elevated level of involuntary part-time employment is very important to the question of how much slack remains." — Lockhart

> "there certainly continues to be improvement in the labor market... [but] it's still hard to say just how much less slack there is." — Tarullo

> "several of you, including Vice Chairman Dudley, noted that you agree [the unemployment decline overstates improvement]... an unusually large share of the work force is involuntarily underemployed." — Chair Yellen

**Center-of-gravity (Transcript):** Labor market improving steadily (~230K jobs/month), unemployment at 6.3% and falling, but broad measures (U-6, PTER, participation rate) indicate meaningful remaining slack.

**Minority views (Transcript):** Fisher's Texas District has 5% unemployment and acute labor shortages — a clear outlier from the national picture. Plosser believes decline in participation is structural, implying less slack than headline U-3 suggests.

---

**Minutes — Score: 36 ±7 [High confidence]**

> "Many judged that slack remained elevated, and a number of them thought it was greater than measured by the official unemployment rate, citing, in particular, the still-high level of workers employed part time for economic reasons or the depressed labor force participation rate"

> "Aggregate wage measures continued to rise at only a modest rate"

> "Labor market conditions generally continued to improve over the intermeeting period"

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Labor market improving but still well below full employment. Significant slack remains, especially when accounting for underemployment and LFPR. Wage growth confirms slack.

**Minority views (Minutes):** "Several participants pointed out that both long- and short-term unemployment and measures that include marginally attached workers had declined." Some reports of "tight labor markets or shortages of skilled workers... in some areas or occupations."

### Dimension 4: CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT

**Center-of-gravity (Transcript):** Consumer spending at a moderate but not robust pace; housing investment notably soft; business investment expected to pick up but not surging. Net position is near trend but slightly soft — housing consistently flagged as a persistent drag.

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Consumer spending moderate with some soft spots; business investment showing positive signs; housing still weak. Net position is slightly below trend.

### Dimension 5: FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS

**Transcript — Score: 28 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**

> "implied volatility for equities, currencies, and long- and short-term fixed income are at historically low levels around the globe... Conditions in the leveraged finance markets in the United States and Europe are extremely loose." — Powell

> "our gradual, predictable taper of our asset purchases and our forward guidance with respect to liftoff does help reduce uncertainty... this contributes to less volatility... low volatility also contributes to rising stock prices and lower term premiums that make financial conditions more accommodative." — Dudley

> "financing conditions remaining supportive of growth." — George

**Center-of-gravity (Transcript):** Financial conditions are loose — low volatility, compressed spreads, reach-for-yield behavior, supportive equity and bond markets. Multiple participants characterize conditions as "accommodative" or "extremely loose" in leveraged segments.

---

**Minutes — Score: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**

> "Favorable financial conditions appeared to be supporting economic activity"

> "a number of participants reported increases in C&I lending by banks... a pickup in loan demand at banks, or better credit quality for borrowers"

> "low implied volatility in equity, currency, and fixed-income markets as well as signs of increased risk-taking were viewed by some participants as an indication that market participants were not factoring in sufficient uncertainty"

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Financial conditions are clearly loose/accommodative, supporting economic activity. Low volatility and risk-taking behavior noted. However, mortgage lending is mixed and housing credit remains restrictive for some borrowers. Overall: loose conditions.

**Minority views (Minutes):** "restrictive credit conditions, particularly for households with low credit scores" in housing.

### Dimension 6: HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN

**Transcript — Score: 42 ±8 [High confidence]**

> "inflation remains below our longer-run target, and these circumstances call for maintaining the current course of policy. This includes a further measured reduction in the pace of asset purchases, combined with forward guidance that signals a highly accommodative future path for the funds rate." — Williams

> "My preferred policy rate liftoff is delayed until I have high confidence that one-year-ahead inflation is at least at our target, and is sustainable thereafter." — Evans (Q1 2016 liftoff)

**Center-of-gravity (Transcript):** The Committee unanimously supports continuing QE taper (~$10B/meeting) and maintaining "considerable period" language. QE ongoing, funds rate at zero, dovish majority on liftoff timing. Hawks want earlier liftoff but accept the current decision.

---

**Minutes — Score: 44 ±8 [High confidence]**

> "a number of participants noted their concern that a more gradual approach might be appropriate if forecasts of above-trend economic growth later this year were not realized"

> "some other participants expressed concern that economic growth over the medium run might be faster than currently expected... calling for a more rapid move to begin raising the federal funds rate"

**Center-of-gravity (Minutes):** Maintaining accommodation via near-zero rates, continuing taper at measured pace. Forward guidance maintained. Center of gravity is lean-dovish to balanced — still in accommodative mode, but tapering signals gradual normalization ahead.
