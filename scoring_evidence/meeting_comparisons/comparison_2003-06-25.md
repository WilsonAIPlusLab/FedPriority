# Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 2003-06-25

## Meeting Context

Two-day meeting (June 24–25, 2003). Day 1 was a special session on zero-lower-bound operational tools; Day 2 was the standard go-around and policy discussion. Rate cut 25 bp from 1.25% to 1.00%. Deflation risk was a live public concern following the May 6 statement. The economy was in a jobless recovery with core PCE running near 1%.
