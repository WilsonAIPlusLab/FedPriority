### D1. INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript: 33 ±6 [High confidence]**

Center-of-gravity: Participants nearly universally characterize inflation as well below target (~1.1–1.3% PCE) with no near-term acceleration signals and limited pricing pressure from businesses or wages.

| Quote | Speaker |
|---|---|
| "the Tealbook's estimate over the past six months is still just 1.3 percent. That's really not much to get excited about. A 1.3 percent run rate is still well below where inflation ought to be." | Evans |
| "I see inflation running below target until 2018" (under Tealbook policy assumptions). | Kocherlakota |
| "With PCE inflation well below our target and many advanced economies also experiencing unusually low inflation rates, we should be particularly attentive." | Rosengren |
| "We're not seeing any clear signs at this point that it will move up quickly back to our 2 percent objective." | Yellen (summary) |

Minority views: George and Dudley note factors (shelter, import base effects, medical) that will push inflation higher. Bullard says inflation "will now turn upward in coming reports."

---

**Minutes: 36 ±5 [High confidence]**

Center-of-gravity: Inflation is running below target with signs of stabilization, but remains a concern on the downside. The majority expects a gradual return to 2%.

| Quote |
|---|
| "Inflation continued to run below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective over the intermeeting period." |
| "Many participants saw the recent behavior of the prices of food, energy, shelter, and imports as consistent with a stabilization in inflation." |

| "A few others expressed the concern that the return to 2 percent inflation could be even more gradual." |

Minority views: A few worried about even slower return to target, pushing the uncertainty band slightly downward.

### D2. OUTPUT_GAP

| Quote | Speaker |

Minority views: Plosser is most optimistic, noting economy "shows itself to be quite resilient" and rates may need to rise faster. Powell notes "high 2s... starts to feel pretty close to the speed limit" — approaching potential but not there.
