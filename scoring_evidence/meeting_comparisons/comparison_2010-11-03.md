### A1. INFLATION_PRESSURE

| | Transcript | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| **Score** | **25 ±8** | **24 ±7** |
| **Confidence** | Moderate | High |

**Transcript evidence:**
- Yellen: "inflation, as far as my eye can see, to run below the 2 percent level I consider most consistent with the Committee's dual mandate…core inflation remains extremely low, and disinflation has been broad-based. Incoming data reveal that wage trends are extremely subdued."
- Bernanke (summary): "Inflation has drifted down some this year, especially as reflected in core measures…Near-term headline inflation may be lifted by energy and import prices."

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** The vast majority of participants perceive inflation as meaningfully below target, with several explicitly invoking deflation risk. The outlier (Warsh) sees commodity/supply-side cost pressures building, but this is a distinct minority view.

---

**Minutes evidence:**

- "Many participants pointed to substantial slack in resource utilization, along with well-anchored inflation expectations, as likely to contribute to subdued inflation for some time."
- "A few participants expected that continuing resource slack would lead to some further disinflation in coming years."

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** The broad majority views inflation as subdued and below mandate-consistent levels, with slack expected to keep it low. Small probability of deflation acknowledged.

**Minutes minority views:** "A few others thought that the exceptionally accommodative stance of monetary policy...made it more likely that inflation would increase" — this widens the band slightly upward.
