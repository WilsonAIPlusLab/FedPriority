# FOMC Meeting Evidence Comparison: 1993-11-16

## Preamble

**Meeting context**: November 1993. Economy in early-to-mid expansion following the 1990-91 recession. Unemployment around 6.7-6.8%. GDP growth moderate (~2.5-3%). Core CPI ~3%, slowly declining. Fed funds rate at 3%, historically accommodative. Long bond rates had backed up ~40 basis points during the intermeeting period. Policy vote: near-unanimous for alternative B, symmetric directive (hold). Only Melzer formally preferred asymmetric bias toward tightening.

**Structural note**: The first approximately two-thirds of the meeting transcript was devoted to a procedural discussion of FOMC records and disclosure policy. The economic go-around and policy vote are compressed into the final third. Prell's formal economic briefing was submitted to the appendix rather than transcribed directly.
