### Dimension 1: INFLATION_PRESSURE

**Transcript score: 54 ±6 [High confidence]**
**Minutes score: 52 ±5 [High confidence]**

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** Inflation has just returned to target after years below; participants welcome it, perceive mild upside risks from tight labor markets and tariff pass-through, but no outbreak. Score is slightly above 50 reflecting the "at target with upside risks" framing rather than the "below target" framing that dominated earlier in 2017–2018.

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** Inflation is perceived as essentially at the 2% target, with modest upside pressures from tariffs and strong demand partially offset by below-target expectations measures. The net assessment is very slightly above neutral.

| Transcript quotes | Minutes quotes |
|---|---|
| Mester: "The rates of both headline and core PCE inflation are at our longer-run goal of 2 percent... Inflation expectations are well anchored near target." | "On a 12-month basis, both overall inflation and inflation for items other than food and energy remained near 2 percent." |
| Kashkari: "Core PCE inflation is now at 2 percent on a yearly basis. This is a milestone; I welcome it." | "Several participants commented that inflation may modestly exceed 2 percent for a period of time." |
| Rosengren: "Suppliers may now feel they have more price flexibility — posing an upside risk to the inflation forecast." | "Some contacts indicated that input prices had been bolstered by strong demand or import tariffs." |
| Williams: "Inflation appears to be settling in at our 2 percent longer-run objective... there's little if any evidence of substantial inflation pressures building." | "A couple of participants emphasized that... some measures of trend inflation or longer-term inflation expectations were below levels consistent with the 2 percent objective." |

**Transcript minority views:** Bullard and Evans note inflation expectations not yet fully symmetrically anchored, implying some residual downside risk. Brainard emphasizes importance of sustainably achieving 2%, acknowledging uncertainty.

**Minutes minority views:** A couple see below-target expectations as a concern (pulling score down); several see potential for modest overshoot (pulling up).

### Dimension 2: OUTPUT_GAP

**Transcript score: 64 ±6 [High confidence]**
**Minutes score: 58 ±5 [High confidence]**

**Transcript center-of-gravity:** Essentially universal agreement that the economy is running clearly above trend/potential, with growth of 3%+ against potential of ~1.75–2%, warranting a score in the 61–68 range.

**Minutes center-of-gravity:** The economy is clearly running above potential with "above-trend GDP growth" supported by fiscal stimulus, strong labor markets, and accommodative financial conditions. The "strong rate" characterization and explicit "above-trend" language place this solidly above 50.

| Transcript quotes | Minutes quotes |
|---|---|
| Williams: "I expect real GDP growth to come in around 3 percent this year and 2½ percent next year, well above my estimate of potential growth of 1¾ percent... the economy operating above potential for the remainder of this decade." | "Economic activity rose at a strong rate." |
