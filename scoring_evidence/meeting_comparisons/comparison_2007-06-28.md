The discussion transcript file for this meeting covers pages 147–218 of the full 261-page transcript. This portion is **entirely devoted to a special FOMC deliberation on communication strategy** — specifically, the design of enhanced economic projections, disclosure formats (histograms vs. narrative, quarterly vs. semi-annual, 3-year vs. 3-to-5-year horizon), whether to disclose the fed funds rate path, anonymity of individual projections, inflation objective disclosure, statement governance, and minutes expediting. There is **no conventional economic assessment go-around** in this document.
