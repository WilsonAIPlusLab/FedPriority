# Side-by-Side Evidence Comparison: FOMC Meeting 1995-08-22

**Meeting context:** August 1995. Funds rate at 5.75% following a 25bp cut in July 1995 — the first ease after the 1994-95 tightening cycle (3% → 6%). Economy had slowed in Q2 via inventory correction; the soft-landing narrative was emerging. Unemployment near 5.7%, CPI near 3%. Budget deficit confrontation in Congress was a major source of uncertainty.
