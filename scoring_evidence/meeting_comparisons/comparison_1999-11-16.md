# FOMC Meeting Comparison: 1999-11-16

## Preamble

**Meeting context**: November 16, 1999. The Fed raised the federal funds rate 25 basis points (third hike of the cycle, following June and August 1999). Unanimous vote on the action; symmetric directive adopted, partly for Y2K tactical reasons. Late-1990s technology/productivity boom at its peak. Y2K concerns constrained near-term policy signaling.

**Sources compared**:
- **Discussion Transcript**: Individual participant statements, go-around format, ~16 named speakers
- **Discussion Minutes**: Synthesized summary of participant views (pre-2009 unsegmented format)
- **Staff Transcript**: Briefings from Mr. Fisher (financial markets), Ms. Johnson (international), Mr. Prell (domestic outlook), Mr. Stockton (oil sensitivity)
- **Staff Minutes**: Staff data review and forecast paragraphs extracted from the unsegmented minutes
