Trade deal phase-one had just been signed, and the coronavirus was just emerging as a new risk. Business investment had been weak due to trade uncertainty but consumer spending remained solid.

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 38 +/-5 [High confidence]

**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation is running persistently below the 2% target with no near-term prospect of reaching it; risks skewed to the downside.
**Direction confirmation**: INFLATION_PRESSURE is below neutral because PCE and core PCE are both well below 2% and the staff forecasts them to remain below target.
