### Era Context
December 2003: The US economy is recovering from the 2001 recession and the prolonged jobless recovery of 2002-2003. The fed funds rate is at 1% — an emergency low. Growth has recently surged (extraordinary Q3 GDP) but labor markets are only beginning to improve. Inflation is very low, with deflation having been a concern earlier in the year. The dollar has been weakening.
