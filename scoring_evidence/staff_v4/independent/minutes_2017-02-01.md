### Era context
Early 2017: The economy is in the later stages of the post-GFC recovery. The unemployment rate is at 4.7%, near the staff's estimate of the natural rate. The Fed just raised rates in December 2016 to 0.50-0.75%. Inflation remains below the 2% target. Equity markets are elevated post-election. The dollar had appreciated but is now slightly declining. This is a period of moderate growth approaching full employment with persistent below-target inflation.

CPI measures are slightly higher (total CPI above 2%, core CPI 2.25%), but the staff focuses on PCE as the primary measure. Inflation is running below target but moving up — this is in the 38-44 range.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 40 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Total U.S. consumer prices, as measured by the PCE price index, increased a little more than 1.5 percent over the 12 months ending in December." "Core PCE price inflation... was 1.75 percent over those same 12 months." "Inflation was projected to be marginally below the Committee's longer-run objective of 2 percent in 2019."
- **Center-of-gravity**: Inflation is running below the 2% target at 1.5-1.75% on PCE measures, with a gradual uptrend expected but not reaching target even by 2019.
- **Direction confirmation**: INFLATION_PRESSURE is below neutral because PCE inflation is below the 2% target and projected to remain so.

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 53 +/-4 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "The staff continued to project that real GDP would expand at a modestly faster pace than potential output in 2017 through 2019." "The unemployment rate was forecast to... run below the staff's estimate of its longer-run natural rate." The economy was "projected to continue operating above its longer-run potential."
- **Center-of-gravity**: The staff sees the economy as modestly above potential, with the gap expected to widen slightly as GDP grows faster than potential.
- **Direction confirmation**: OUTPUT_GAP is above neutral because staff explicitly says the economy is operating "above its longer-run potential." Bright-line rule satisfied.

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 53 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "The unemployment rate edged up to 4.7 percent but remained near its recent low." "Total nonfarm payroll employment increased at a solid pace." "The employment cost index for private industry workers rose 2.25 percent... and average hourly earnings for all employees increased almost 3 percent." UE projected to fall below the natural rate.
- **Center-of-gravity**: Labor market is near full employment with UE at 4.7% and compensation growing moderately. The market is tight enough that UE is near the natural rate and heading below it, but wage pressures remain moderate.
- **Direction confirmation**: LABOR_TIGHTNESS is slightly above neutral because the unemployment rate is near and projected to fall below the natural rate.

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 53 +/-5 [High confidence]**

"Consumer sentiment... moved up to an elevated level."
- **Center-of-gravity**: Consumer spending is moderate, residential investment brisk, business investment recovering from prior weakness. Elevated consumer sentiment points to continued strength. Overall slightly above trend.
- **Direction confirmation**: CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT is slightly above neutral because spending indicators are moderate to brisk with positive forward-looking signals.
