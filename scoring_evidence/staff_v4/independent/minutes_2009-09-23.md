The economy has likely hit its trough (June 2009 per NBER) and the very earliest signs of recovery are emerging, but the labor market remains deeply impaired with unemployment at 9.7%. Financial conditions are improving from crisis extremes but remain stressed. The fed funds rate is at the zero lower bound. I will score based on what the text says, not external knowledge.

The staff forecasts "core inflation to slow somewhat further over the next two years." Energy prices boosted headline, but core is clearly subdued. This is consistent with 2009 deflation-fear era. Score should be in the 20-28 range — subdued but not acute deflation risk, as expectations remained anchored.

Bright-line: "significant underutilization" = must be < 50. Score should be in the 18-24 range.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Staff discusses dollar movement. Bright-line: depreciation → must be < 50. Score should be around 30-35.
