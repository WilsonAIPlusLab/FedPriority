### Era Context
March 2023 — This meeting occurred immediately after the Silicon Valley Bank and Signature Bank closures (March 10-12, 2023). The economy was still running hot with tight labor markets and elevated inflation from the 2021-2022 surge, but the banking stress event introduced significant new uncertainty. The Fed had been hiking rates aggressively. Inflation was well above target but beginning to show some signs of deceleration. Unemployment was near historical lows at 3.6%.
