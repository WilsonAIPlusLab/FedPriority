## Part 1: Thinking Process

### Document Type

I must score ONLY staff-attributable content, identified by cues such as "the information received," "the staff forecast," direct economic data reporting in impersonal/passive voice, and the explicit staff forecast paragraph. ### Era Context
December 2004: Mid-expansion recovery from the 2001 recession. The Fed was in the early stages of a tightening cycle, raising the funds rate from 1% to 2.25% by this meeting. The economy was growing moderately, unemployment was around 5.4%, and core inflation was subdued but slightly higher than the prior year. Oil prices had risen significantly, the dollar was depreciating, and housing was strong.

### Dimension-by-Dimension Analysis

**1. INFLATION_PRESSURE**
Staff language: "Core inflation measures remained subdued, albeit running at a slightly higher pace than last year, owing, in part, to the indirect effects of higher energy prices." Also: "Consumer prices jumped in October, as hurricane damage contributed to higher prices for food and energy... Core consumer prices increased much more modestly... although the twelve-month change for core prices was also somewhat higher than it was a year before." Staff forecast: "core inflation was seen in the staff forecast as remaining stable" with "upward pressure on inflation from a slight step-down in structural productivity growth and a narrowing margin of resource slack was expected to be about counterbalanced by diminishing pressure from the pass-through of the earlier rise in energy prices and decline in the dollar."

Direction: Staff sees inflation as slightly below neutral -- core is "subdued" but "slightly higher than last year." The headline jumped due to hurricanes/energy but core remains modest. Staff expects core to remain stable. This is below-target but edging up. Score range: 42-48. "Subdued" pulls toward lower end, "slightly higher" pulls toward mid. I'll score 44.

Polarity check: Higher = more inflation pressure. "Subdued" core inflation with slight increase = modestly below midpoint. Score 44 matches.

**2. OUTPUT_GAP**
Staff language: "the economy expanded at a moderate pace over the third quarter and into the current quarter." Staff forecast: "economy was seen as likely to expand at a moderate pace" and "economic activity projected to expand at a pace a little above that of its longer-run potential over the coming year, hiring was projected to continue to firm, causing the unemployment rate to edge down next year." Also: "a narrowing margin of resource slack" in the forecast.

Direction: Staff sees economy as still below potential but narrowing. "Narrowing margin of resource slack" explicitly says slack remains (below potential) but is closing. Bright-line rule: "slack remains" -> MUST be < 50. UE at 5.4% is still somewhat elevated for this era (NAIRU ~5%). Growth is "moderate" and "a little above potential" suggesting gradual closing. Score: 44.

Polarity check: Higher = more above potential. Slack remains = below potential = < 50. Score 44 satisfies bright-line.

**3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS**

"The unemployment rate ticked down to 5.4 percent in November." Also employment was "fairly widespread" but manufacturing "edged lower."

Direction: UE at 5.4% is near but slightly above estimated NAIRU of ~5%. Market is "improving gradually" but not tight. Score range per anchors: UE 5.4% maps to 38-44 range (below full employment, slack remains). Score: 42.

Polarity check: Higher = tighter. UE 5.4% with gradual improvement but still some slack = below midpoint. Score 42 fits.

**4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**

"Consumer spending appeared to be expanding at a more moderate pace in the fourth quarter, after growing at a robust rate in the third quarter." "Shipments of nondefense capital goods stepped up in October" with "solid expansion in business output, low user cost of capital, and an ample stock of liquid assets." Computer shipments "jumped." Outside high-tech and transportation, shipments "moved up considerably... from their robust third-quarter pace." Housing "remained strong." Staff forecast: consumption expected to grow "at a pace somewhat above that recorded this year"; business investment to "resume vigorous growth."

Direction: Consumer spending is solid but moderating from robust; investment is strong with stepped-up shipments. Overall picture is above trend. "Solid" + "strong" + "stepped up considerably" = above trend. Score: 58.

Polarity check: Higher = stronger spending/investment. "Solid" consumer + "strong" investment = above trend = above 50. Score 58 fits.
