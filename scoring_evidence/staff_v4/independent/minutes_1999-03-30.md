### Era Context
March 1999: Late-1990s expansion/boom. Unemployment around 4.4%, strong GDP growth, dot-com era equity gains, subdued inflation despite tight labor markets. Fed funds rate at 4.75%. Asian financial crisis effects lingering but diminishing. This is a period where the economy was running above potential with tight labor markets but inflation remained remarkably low due to productivity gains.
