### Era Context
December 1993: The U.S. economy is in the middle of the 1990s expansion following the 1990-91 recession. Unemployment has been declining but remains above the natural rate (6.4% in November). The Fed funds rate is at 3%, which was considered quite accommodative. Inflation was low. This is before the Fed began its tightening cycle in February 1994.
