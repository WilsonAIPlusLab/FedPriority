### Era Context
December 2017 — late-cycle expansion. Unemployment at 4.1%, below most estimates of the natural rate. Inflation running persistently below 2% target despite tightening labor markets. Tax reform legislation pending. Fed in gradual rate-hiking cycle. Financial conditions accommodative. This is the 2017-19 neutral-to-modestly-above-potential anchor period.

### Dimension-by-Dimension Directional Assessment

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Staff sees inflation as BELOW the midpoint. Total PCE inflation "remained below 2 percent" and was "lower than early in the year." Core PCE was "nearly 1.5 percent." Staff projected inflation would be "very close to the Committee's 2 percent objective in 2019 and at that objective in 2020," implying it was currently meaningfully below target. Staff noted "transitory factors that held down those prices in 2017." This is clearly below-target inflation. Score should be in the 35-42 range per the 2013-19 anchor of 38-42.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: Staff sees the economy as ABOVE potential. "Real GDP would increase at a modestly faster pace than potential output through 2019." "The unemployment rate was projected to... continue running below the staff's... estimate of the longer-run natural rate." The staff also noted upside risk that "inflation could increase more than expected in an economy that was projected to move further above its potential." Bright-line rule: "above potential" language requires score > 50. Score should be 52-56 — modestly above potential.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Staff sees the labor market as TIGHT. Unemployment at 4.1% — below the staff's estimate of the natural rate. "Labor market conditions continued to strengthen." Job openings and quits "at relatively high levels," initial claims "at a low level." However, "wage gains remained modest" — compensation per hour only 1%, AHE 2.5%. The level of UE at 4.1% with the rubric guidance (below 4.5% = 53-65 range) suggests tightness, but modest wage growth moderates the signal. Score ~57.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Staff sees spending/investment as AT or SLIGHTLY ABOVE trend. Real GDP "rising at a solid pace." Real PCE "increased modestly in October after expanding strongly in September." Consumer sentiment "remained at a high level." Business equipment and IP "appeared to be rising further." Business sentiment "remained upbeat." Residential investment "edging up." Government purchases "rising." Overall, "solid pace" language maps to 50-55 range. Score ~53.

**PRODUCTIVITY**: Not directly discussed. N/A.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Staff sees conditions as ACCOMMODATIVE/SUPPORTIVE. "Financing conditions for businesses and households remained broadly supportive of continued growth." "Financing conditions for large nonfinancial corporations continued to be accommodative on balance." "Consumer credit remained readily available." Bright-line rule: "accommodative"/"supportive" requires score < 50. But the 2017-19 anchor is 46-54 neutral. The language is "broadly supportive" and "accommodative" which is on the easy side of neutral. Score ~38-42.

Wait — the rubric says the 2017-19 period was underscored at 34-42 and should be 46-54. But the bright-line rule says "accommodative"/"supportive" MUST be < 50. These create tension. The bright-line rule is mandatory and overrides. The text explicitly says "accommodative" and "broadly supportive." Score must be < 50. I'll score 40 — somewhat accommodative, reflecting the "broadly supportive" and "accommodative" language while respecting both the bright-line rule and the guidance that 2017-19 shouldn't be scored too low.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: Treasury yield curve flattened — short yields rose, 10-year up slightly. This is normal price adjustment from policy expectations and tax legislation odds. No dysfunction language whatsoever. "Market participants did not attribute any price changes in Treasury and agency MBS markets to the implementation of reductions in reinvestments." Short-term funding markets "remained stable." The debt ceiling issue is mentioned but no market stress from it. Score in normal functioning range, ~40.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads on both investment- and speculative-grade corporate bond yields over comparable-maturity Treasury yields were about flat on net." CMBS spreads "remained near the lower end of the range seen since the financial crisis." ABS spreads "about unchanged." Corporate bond issuance "robust." This describes compressed/favorable spreads. Bright-line: compressed/narrow requires < 50. Score ~30-35 (generally favorable, 2013-19 anchor range).

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad equity price indexes rose over the intermeeting period" due to tax legislation expectations. VIX "at levels close to historical lows." No staff valuation concern language — just price movement description. Default to 45-50 per rubric guidance. Score ~45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Not discussed directly as a topic. CRE loan growth at largest banks "continued to slow" while smaller banks' CRE growth was "strong." Delinquency rates on CMBS loans "continued to decrease." Credit quality "remained solid." Mortgage credit standards were mixed — loose for strong borrowers, tight for subprime. Auto loan conditions "continued to tighten" for subprime. This is a healthy-to-neutral banking picture. Bright-line: "sound"/"healthy" language requires < 50. Score ~35.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "The broad index of the foreign exchange value of the dollar was little changed." "The euro appreciated modestly against the U.S. dollar." Bright-line: little changed maps to stable/neutral range 46-54. Score ~47 (broadly unchanged with slight weakness implied by euro appreciation against dollar).

**Center-of-gravity**: Staff describes inflation running meaningfully below target, with core PCE at ~1.5% and total PCE slightly above 1.5%, though expected to return to target by 2019-2020.
**Direction confirmation**: INFLATION_PRESSURE is below neutral because inflation is running well below the 2% target with no current upward pressure.

**Center-of-gravity**: Staff projects the economy modestly above potential and moving further above, with unemployment below the natural rate.
**Direction confirmation**: OUTPUT_GAP is above neutral because staff explicitly states economy is above potential. Bright-line rule satisfied (>50).

**Direction confirmation**: LABOR_TIGHTNESS is above neutral because UE is well below 4.5% and below the natural rate.
