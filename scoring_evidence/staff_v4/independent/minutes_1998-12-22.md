### Document Type

**Staff-attributable content** is found in:
- "The information reviewed at this meeting suggested..." (lines 38-154) — factual economic data review

### Era Context
December 1998: Late 1990s expansion/boom. The U.S. economy was growing briskly despite the Asian financial crisis, Russian default (Aug 1998), and LTCM near-collapse (Sept 1998). The Fed had cut rates three times in fall 1998 to stabilize financial markets. Unemployment was very low (~4.4%). Inflation was subdued. The economy was running above potential with tight labor markets.
