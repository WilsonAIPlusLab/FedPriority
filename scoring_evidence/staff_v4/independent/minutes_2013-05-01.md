### Era Context
May 2013: The U.S. economy is in the post-GFC recovery phase, roughly four years after the trough. Unemployment remains elevated at 7.6%, GDP growth is moderate, inflation is well below target at about 1% PCE year-over-year, and the Fed is conducting large-scale asset purchases (QE3). Fiscal sequestration is a headwind. This is a classic "recovery with slack" environment.
