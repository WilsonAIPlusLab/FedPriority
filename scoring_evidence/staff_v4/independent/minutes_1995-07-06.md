### Document Type
This is **minutes** (synthesized summary), **unsegmented** (pre-2005). Staff content is mixed with participant discussion and policy action. I must carefully distinguish staff-attributable language ("the information reviewed," "the staff forecast," "staff projection") from member/participant language ("members commented," "members noted," "in the view of most members").

### Era Context
Mid-1995. The economy had been growing above potential in 1994 after the early-1990s recession, and the Fed had been tightening through 1994 and into early 1995. By mid-1995, the economy was experiencing a marked slowdown -- a "soft landing" scenario. The Fed funds rate was at 6%. Unemployment was at 5.7%. Inflation had picked up somewhat from 1994 but signs of moderation were emerging. The dollar had been weakening.

Staff sees inflation as recently elevated but moderating. I'll score ~53.
