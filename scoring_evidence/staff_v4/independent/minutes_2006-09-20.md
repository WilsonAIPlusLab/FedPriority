### Era Context
September 2006: Late expansion period. Fed funds rate at 5.25% after a long tightening cycle. Housing market cooling significantly. Core inflation running somewhat above comfort. Unemployment at 4.7%. Economy decelerating but not yet in recession. Pre-financial-crisis period.

Compensation per hour rose ~7.75% y/y. Inflation expectations mixed -- turned up in August but preliminary September readings declined. Staff forecast: "Core consumer price inflation was projected to drop back somewhat later this year and next, reflecting the emergence of slack in the economy and lower energy costs." But current readings are clearly elevated. Direction: above 50 -- staff describes current core inflation as elevated/higher than year-ago, but easing somewhat. Score range: 58-65.

**2. OUTPUT_GAP**

Key staff forecast language: "By 2008, output was projected to be expanding at a pace about equal to the staff's forecast of potential output growth." This means the staff sees the economy currently at or slightly above potential, with a small gap opening going forward. The phrase "high levels of resource utilization" appears in the Committee statement context. UE at 4.7%. Direction: slightly above potential currently. Bright-line check: no explicit "above potential" or "below potential" language in staff content, but "high levels of resource utilization" and the forecast of "opening of only a small gap" implies currently at or slightly above.
