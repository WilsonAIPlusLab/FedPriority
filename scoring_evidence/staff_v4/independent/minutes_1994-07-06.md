### Era Context
Mid-1994: The Fed was in the middle of a tightening cycle that began in February 1994. The economy was expanding solidly, with the expansion well-established. Unemployment had fallen to 6.0%. Inflation remained moderate but there were upward pressures from materials prices and a weakening dollar. This was a mid-cycle environment with the economy operating near capacity.
