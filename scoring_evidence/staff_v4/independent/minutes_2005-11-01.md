Evidence: "Despite the large increase in consumer energy prices since midyear, core price inflation was restrained through September." "The twelve-month change in core consumer prices through September was about unchanged from its year-earlier level." Staff forecast: "core inflation to pick up modestly over coming quarters owing to the lagged effects of higher energy prices but then to return to near current levels in 2007." Direction: slightly below midpoint for core, but energy creates upside risk. Staff language says "restrained" and "favorable incoming data led the staff to reduce its forecast for near-term core inflation a bit." Score around 48-52. The staff acknowledges energy price pass-through risk but core remains contained. I'll score 50 -- at target with upside risk noted.

Polarity check: Higher = more inflation pressure. Core restrained but energy elevated with pass-through risk. Net = roughly neutral with slight upside risk. Score 52.

No explicit "above potential" or "below potential" language. The economy is growing at a solid pace with hurricane disruptions being temporary. The committee discussion (which I note but don't score from) mentions "underlying economic slack was likely quite limited" -- but this is participant language. Staff language suggests solid growth, near potential. Score: 51-53. "Good deal of forward momentum" suggests modestly above.

Bright-line check: No explicit "above/below potential" from staff. Inference from "good deal of forward momentum" and capacity utilization "noticeably above year-earlier level" suggests modestly above trend. Score 53.
