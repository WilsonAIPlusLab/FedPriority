## Part 1: Thinking Process

### Document Type
This is a **minutes** document (synthesized summary). It is an **unsegmented pre-2009** document, meaning staff and participant content are mixed together. I must score ONLY staff-attributable content.

### Staff-Attributable Content Identification
The following sections are staff-attributable:
- "The information reviewed at this meeting suggested..." through the data review paragraphs (economic activity, labor, industrial production, consumer spending, housing, business investment, inventories, international trade, inflation, financial markets, domestic nonfinancial debt)
- "The staff forecast prepared for this meeting showed..."
- Financial market descriptions ("Investors anticipated the FOMC's decision...", "The trade-weighted exchange value of the dollar...", "Domestic nonfinancial sector debt...")

The following are **participant/member content** and must be EXCLUDED:
- "In their discussion of the economic situation and outlook, meeting participants saw..."
- "Some participants noted...", "Several participants remarked...", "Participants discussed..."
- "Although the Committee discussed...", "all members believed..."
- All policy action paragraphs
