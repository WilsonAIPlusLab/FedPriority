Polarity check: Higher = more inflation pressure. Staff sees gradual increase in core inflation, rising labor costs. Score above 50 matches.

Capacity utilization in manufacturing "fell to a level slightly below its long-term average." However, unemployment was 3.9% and "remained very low."

The staff forecast explicitly says growth is now "a little below" potential. This is about growth rate relative to potential growth, implying the output gap is closing or narrowing from above. With unemployment at 3.9% (very low), the economy is still operating above potential in levels even if the growth rate has slowed below potential growth rate.

The bright-line rule: The staff doesn't say "above potential" or "below potential" in level terms explicitly. The forecast says growth rate is below potential growth rate, meaning the gap would be closing. But the level is still above potential given 3.9% unemployment and the fact that growth was rapid earlier.

Given 3.9% unemployment (very tight), manufacturing capacity utilization slightly below average but overall economy still above sustainable levels -- I score this modestly above 50. The economy is still running above potential in levels but the gap is narrowing.

Score: 53. Above potential in levels but closing.

Bright-line check: No explicit "above potential" or "below potential" level language. Growth rate is "a little below" potential growth rate, meaning gap is closing. Level is still above potential given labor market tightness. Score > 50 is appropriate.
