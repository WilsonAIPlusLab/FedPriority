### Era Context
December 2007 — the economy is on the cusp of what will become the Great Recession (which officially began in December 2007). The housing correction is intensifying, subprime mortgage markets are shutting down, and financial turmoil from the summer of 2007 is worsening. The Fed has already cut rates 75bp to 4.5% and is cutting another 25bp to 4.25% at this meeting. I will score what the staff actually says, not what I know in hindsight.
