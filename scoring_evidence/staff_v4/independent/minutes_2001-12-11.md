### Document Type

**Staff-attributable content** is found in paragraphs using phrases like "The information reviewed at this meeting indicated," "The staff forecast prepared for this meeting suggested," and factual data-reporting paragraphs about employment, industrial production, consumption, trade, prices, and financial markets. Participant content uses "members commented," "members cited," "members referred to," "members supported," etc.

### Era Context
December 2001: The U.S. is in recession (NBER-dated March-November 2001). The September 11 terrorist attacks occurred three months prior. The Fed has been aggressively cutting rates throughout 2001 and is now at 1.75%. Global weakness is synchronized. This is a recessionary environment with declining activity, rising unemployment, and subdued inflation.
