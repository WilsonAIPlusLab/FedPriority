### Era Context
November 2020 — approximately 8 months into the COVID-19 pandemic. The economy experienced an unprecedented contraction in Q2 2020 followed by a sharp but incomplete rebound in Q3. Unemployment remains elevated at 7.9%, GDP remains well below pre-pandemic levels, and inflation is running below target. The Fed funds rate is at the effective lower bound. A second wave of COVID cases is emerging in Europe. No additional fiscal stimulus package has been enacted.

Trimmed mean at 1.9% is closer to target but the overall picture is subdued. Inflation expectations "moved lower" though within recent ranges. Staff projects inflation finishing the year at "a relatively subdued level" reflecting "substantial margins of slack." This is clearly below target — the language is "subdued," "held down," "below." Direction: below 50. Magnitude: "subdued" and specific data (1.4%, 1.5%) suggest moderate distance from 50. Score range: 30-35.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Staff sees labor market as BELOW the midpoint (loose). Unemployment at 7.9% — well above NAIRU. Labor force participation declined. ECI growth slowed. With UE at 7.9%, the anchor range is 16-30 per the rubric (UE > 7%). Score range: 25-30.

Net exports were a "substantial negative contribution." Housing was a bright spot, moving above Q1 levels. The picture is a strong rebound from a deep hole but still below pre-pandemic. "Strong" growth language pushes this up, but the level remains below trend. Score range: 38-43.
