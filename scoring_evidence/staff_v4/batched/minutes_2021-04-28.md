# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-04-28

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: April 2021. GDP increased in Q1 faster than Q4 but not yet at pre-pandemic level. UE 6.0%. PCE 1.6% (Feb). Payrolls surged in March. Bank lending easing markedly. VIX approaching pre-pandemic median. Supply bottlenecks emerging. Staff notes "asset valuation pressures" — corporate spreads at lower ends of historical distributions, equity risk premium declined further.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.6%, core 1.4%. Trimmed mean 1.6%. CPI 2.6% (energy-boosted). Median wage 3.1% (below pre-pandemic). Staff expects core to remain "above 2 percent at the end of 2021, boosted by large increases in import prices...and the temporary effects of supply bottlenecks." Still largely "transitory." Score: 33.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "had not yet returned to its pre-pandemic level." Staff projects "substantial increase this year" with rapid decline in UE. Still below potential but closing. Score: 40.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 6.0%. Payrolls "surged in March." "Retraced almost two-thirds of the losses." LFPR and E-P increased but "remained below their pre-pandemic levels." Initial claims at lowest since pandemic onset. Score: 37.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE declined in Feb but "large increase in March." Housing above pre-pandemic. Equipment/intangibles increased. Nonresidential structures declined further. Manufacturing "rebounded strongly in March." Score: 43.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Investor sentiment improved." Equities rose notably. Spreads narrowed. "Market-based financing conditions remained accommodative, and bank lending conditions eased markedly." Standards "remained tighter than pre-pandemic levels." Highly accommodative for IG. Score: 42.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Domestic short-term funding markets remained stable." Rates very low. ON RRP take-up increased to $134B quarter-end. No emergency facility usage. Excellent functioning. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "narrowed moderately." "Corporate bond spreads...at the lower ends of their historical distributions." Defaults "at low levels." Default expectations "low." Spec-grade upgrades exceeding downgrades. Score: 35.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose. VIX "approaching the median level observed in the decade before the pandemic." Staff: "measures of the equity risk premium declined further," "nonprice measures, such as high IPO volume, also indicated elevated investor appetite for risk." "Valuation pressures" elevated. Hedge fund leverage elevated. Score: 38.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks "reported easing standards, on net, for C&I loans across firms of all sizes." Demand weakened at large firms. Credit quality improving for lower-rated. Small businesses "improved" but "many small businesses remained under financial stress." Bank capital above pre-pandemic. Hedge fund leverage elevated. Score: 47.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index fell modestly." Dollar weakening. Score: 47.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common inflation expectations index "returned to the level that prevailed in 2018." Score: 50.
