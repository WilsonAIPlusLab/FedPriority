# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2016-12-14

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: December 2016. GDP expanding moderate pace. UE declined to 4.6%. Post-election: equities rose, yields rose considerably, dollar appreciated notably, inflation compensation moved up. Fiscal stimulus expectations. Second hike (25bp) at this meeting.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE almost 1.5% over 12 months ending October. Core about 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. Compensation per hour 3%. Inflation "increased further." Projected "marginally below" 2% in 2019. Score ~39.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "expanding at a moderate pace." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output in 2017 through 2019." UE to "edge down gradually" and run "below" natural rate. Fiscal stimulus assumption added. Score ~50.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.6%. "Labor market conditions had tightened further." Payrolls "solid pace." AHE 2.5%. Compensation per hour 3%. Productivity "flat." Job openings/hiring/quits "above those seen during much of the current economic expansion." Score ~53.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "rising at a moderate pace." Vehicle sales higher. But equipment "soft." Residential investment "picking up in the fourth quarter." Score ~47.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Post-election: equities rose, spreads narrowed, yields rose. "Financing conditions remained broadly accommodative." But private borrowing rates "increased somewhat." Score ~38.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "moved up considerably." Inflation compensation "continued to move up." Yield curve steepened. No dysfunction. Score ~48.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads "narrowed" for both IG and speculative-grade. Score ~38.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "rose over the intermeeting period." Financial sector "outperformed." "Improved risk sentiment." Score ~38.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities from inflation risks "roughly balanced." Banks assumed healthy. Dollar appreciation and possible further rise noted. Score ~34.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated notably against both AFE and EME currencies." Yen: ~10%. Peso: ~5%. Score ~62.
