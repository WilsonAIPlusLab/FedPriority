# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2022-07-27

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: July 2022. 75bp hike (second). GDP declined over H1. UE 3.6%. PCE 6.3%, core 4.7%. Trimmed mean 4.0%. CPI 9.1%. AHE 5.1%. Labor market "very tight." Staff downgrades financial vulnerability from "notable" to "moderate." Output gap closing — expected to close by H2 2023. Recession risk rising. Dollar appreciating further.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 6.3%, core 4.7%. Trimmed mean 4.0%. CPI 9.1%, core 5.9%. AHE 5.1%. "Short-run inflation expectations remained elevated." Staff expects 4.8% PCE in 2022, step-down to 2.6% core in 2023. Score: 78.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "had declined over the first half of the year." But "projected level of real GDP remained above potential this year, the gap was expected to have closed by the second half of 2023." UE projected to start rising in H2 2022. Gap narrowing toward potential. Score: 52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6%. Job openings "declined further" but "remained at a high level." AHE 5.1%. LFPR and E-P "ticked down." "Labor demand remained strong." Score: 60.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "rose at a modest pace." Business investment, residential investment, and government purchases "all posted declines." Manufacturing "moved lower." Forward-looking indicators "weakened broadly." Score: 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Treasury yields declined. Equities rose. But corporate spreads "ended the period slightly wider." Borrowing costs declined for IG but higher for lower-rated. "Credit availability appeared to tighten for most businesses and some households." July SLOOS: banks expect tightening in H2. Score: 52.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were stable." ON RRP elevated. CP spreads narrowed. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "slightly wider." Muni spreads "widened slightly." Defaults "low." Rating downgrades for leveraged loans exceeded upgrades. Banks increasing loan loss provisions. Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose. VIX "decreased but remained significantly above its pre-pandemic levels." Staff: "Declining risk appetite has also led to sharp declines in the price of some digital assets." Financial vulnerabilities downgraded to "moderate." "Equity and corporate debt prices declined significantly since the last assessment." Score: 50.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I and CRE loans "expanded at a rapid pace." Banks expect tightening standards in H2. Credit quality "remained strong" but banks "increased loan loss provisions." Auto delinquencies for subprime "rebounding notably to slightly above their historical averages." Small business access tightening. Score: 45.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Dollar appreciated somewhat further against most currencies and particularly against the euro." Multi-decade highs against euro, yen. Score: 62.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Short-run inflation expectations remained elevated; by contrast, some measures of longer-term inflation expectations moved lower in recent weeks." Score: 55.
