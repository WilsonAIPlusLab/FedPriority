## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: November 2021. GDP growth slowed markedly in Q3 from H1 rapid pace. UE 4.8%. PCE 4.4%, core 3.6%. Trimmed mean 2.3%. ECI 4.1% (12-mo) — fastest since 2001. Supply constraints more persistent. Staff expects inflation to step down to 2% in 2022. Asset valuations "generally high." VIX near pre-pandemic. Tapering announced.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 4.4%, core 3.6%. Trimmed mean 2.3%. CPI 5.3% (implied from Sep data). ECI 4.1% — "fastest 12-month change since 2001." "Inflationary pressures had broadened." Staff inflation risks "skewed to the upside." Score: 65.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP growth "slowed markedly in the third quarter." But "level of real GDP was expected to remain well above potential throughout the projection period." GDP above potential. Score: 53.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.8%. Payrolls "retraced three-fourths of losses." LFPR edged lower. Job openings "well above pre-pandemic levels." ECI 4.1% — fastest since 2001. Initial claims approaching pre-pandemic. Score: 50.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "posted a modest increase in the third quarter after having risen sharply over the first half." Motor vehicle purchases plunged. Residential investment "dropped further" — supply constraints. BFI growth "slowed sharply" — bottlenecks. Manufacturing declined in Sep. Score: 42.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Market-based financing conditions were accommodative, and bank lending standards eased for most loan categories." Equities increased. Corporate spreads "little changed...at low levels." VIX "near pre-pandemic levels." Score: 38.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR at 8bp, SOFR at 5bp. ON RRP averaged $1.4T (high of $1.6T). No stress. Score: 33.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "little changed, on net...and remained at low levels." Muni spreads "narrowed slightly." Upgrades outpaced downgrades. Default rates "decreased from already low levels." Market default expectations "remained benign." Score: 33.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. VIX "near pre-pandemic levels." Staff: "asset valuations remained generally high relative to historical norms" — "bond and leveraged loan spreads remained narrow, while equity prices continued to increase." "House prices rose rapidly, outpacing rents." Score: 35.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks easing standards. C&I loans declined (PPP). Credit quality "remained strong." Corporate leverage "largely returned to pre-pandemic levels." Banks "strongly capitalized." Hedge fund leverage "noteworthy." Small business delinquencies "roughly in line with pre-pandemic levels." Score: 40.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar appreciated a touch." Slight appreciation. Score: 52.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "at its highest level since 2014." "Little changed relative to the second quarter." But staff notes "risk that longer-run inflation expectations would move appreciably higher." Score: 52.
