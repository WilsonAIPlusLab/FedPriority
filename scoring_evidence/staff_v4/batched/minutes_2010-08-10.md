# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2010-08-10

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: August 2010 — recovery slowing. Revised data showed recession deeper. GDP level lower. Private employment growth slowing. Housing "quite soft." Consumer confidence fell. Inflation very low. European concerns easing. QE2 debate.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass.

---

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 23 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Headline consumer prices declined in May and June." "Year-over-year increase in core index was lower." "Unit labor costs remained well below their level one year earlier."
- Direction: Well below neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 38 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Pace of the economic recovery slowed." "Recession was deeper than previously thought." Capacity utilization "still substantially below." Staff lowered outlook.
- Direction: Below neutral — recovery slowing, massive slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 19 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Private employment gains "considerably less than the preceding three months." UE ~9.5%. "Initial claims remained elevated."
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 44 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer spending continued to rise at a modest rate." E&S "rose strongly." Housing "quite soft."
- Direction: Slightly below neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 44 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Output per hour declined in the second quarter after rising rapidly."
- Direction: Slightly below neutral — recent decline.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 56 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financial conditions became somewhat more supportive." Rates and dollar improved. But bank loans contracting.
- Direction: Modestly above neutral.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 41 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Treasury auctions were generally well received." No dysfunction.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 51 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Spreads...declined moderately." Near pre-crisis levels.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Stocks "increased slightly." "Equity risk premium at an elevated level."
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 57 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS spreads "narrowed moderately." CRE "under pressure." Bank loans contracting but slowing.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Value of the dollar declined on balance." Reversal of safe-haven flows.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 20 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core consumer goods continued to decline."

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 40 ±7 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Non-energy services rose moderately."

**INFLATION_HOUSING: 35 ±7 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "House prices were largely stable."

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 42 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Survey measures "largely stable." TIPS compensation "moved down further."
