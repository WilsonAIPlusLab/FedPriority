# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-01-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — Wilcox (domestic outlook), Wilson (international), Potter (financial markets/operations). Segmented. 949 lines.

**Era context**: January 2013. Fiscal cliff deal enacted. QE3 ongoing ($85B/month). Threshold-based forward guidance adopted. UE ~7.9%. European crisis stabilizing. Sequestration looming March 1. China recovering. Fiscal cliff deal unexpectedly raised payroll taxes.

**Wilcox key points**: "In a departure from recent practice, the data... led us to mark down our estimate of fourth-quarter real GDP" by 3/4pp. But "PDFP... appears to have risen at a solid rate of 3½ percent." "Downward surprise has limited signal content." Fiscal deal "postponed the implementation of the sequester to March 1." "Considerable fiscal uncertainty" remains. Employment "increased in the fourth quarter at an average pace of around 150,000 per month." UE "now expect the unemployment rate to be just over 7 percent at end of next year." Core PCE at 1.5%. "Inflation expectations well anchored." Natural rate revised down. Supply-side adjustments made.

**Wilson (international)**: "Six months ago... worrying about crisis in Europe and Chinese hard landing." "Today the financial and economic climate feels a bit more pleasant." European sovereign spreads fallen sharply. Equity prices up. Volatility dropped. ECB OMT, Spain's steps, Greek aid, banking union progress all helping. China growth accelerated to 9.5% Q4 — "China bears into hibernation." But foreign output "decelerated sharply in second half of last year" from domestic weakness in US/EU/China. "Anticipate growth will strengthen gradually."

**Potter (financial markets)**: "Investor sentiment improved markedly." "Fiscal cliff" deal supported risk assets. "S&P 500 reached its highest level since 2007." "VIX fell to 2007 levels." Credit spreads narrowed. European stress easing. Long-term rates rising from reduced risk aversion. Treasury yields up 30bp. Dealers expect ~$1 trillion in total QE purchases. Basel Committee decision on liquidity helped European bank shares.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36** ±5 [Moderate confidence]

- Direction: Below neutral but improving.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30** ±5 [Moderate confidence]

- Direction: Below neutral — still substantial slack.

- Direction: Slightly below neutral — mixed signals.
