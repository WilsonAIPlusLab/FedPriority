# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2020-11-05

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: November 2020. GDP rebounded rapidly in Q3 but remains well below pre-pandemic. UE at 7.9%. COVID cases surging in Europe, rising in US. Election uncertainty. Inflation recovering — PCE at 1.4%. Fed at 0-0.25% with QE.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.4%, core 1.5%. Trimmed mean 1.9%. Monthly basis "a little lower in September." ECI at 2.4% (below pre-pandemic). "Inflation was still projected to finish the year at a relatively subdued level, reflecting substantial margins of slack." Score: 33.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "U.S. real GDP had rebounded at a rapid rate in the third quarter but remained well below its level at the start of the year." Staff projects real GDP to "outpace potential" over medium term — currently well below potential. Score: 33.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 7.9%. "Payroll employment had retraced only about half of the decline seen at the onset of the pandemic." LFPR declined. ECI gains "noticeably below the average pace seen over 2017 through 2019." Score: 32.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE "rose strongly in the third quarter, though the increase was not sufficient to return consumer spending to its pre-pandemic level." Housing "advanced" above Q1 levels. Business equipment spending expanded "strongly." But structures "continued to move lower." IP growth turned negative in September. Score: 38.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Yield curve steepened modestly. Corporate spreads narrowed "somewhat below their historical median levels." Equities increased. "Financing conditions... remained generally accommodative, although conditions remained tight or tightened somewhat for other borrowers." Score: 47.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." CP/CD at pre-pandemic levels. Quarter-end "muted." Zero emergency facility usage. Excellent functioning. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "narrowed across the credit spectrum and stood somewhat below their historical median levels." Bond issuance solid. Credit quality showing "signs of stabilization." Defaults at pre-pandemic levels. Score: 40.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Stocks increased on balance. "Asset valuations appeared moderate, with measures of compensation for risk generally in the middle of their historical ranges." But "risk of sizable declines in asset prices, should adverse shocks materialize, has remained significant." Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks continued tightening. "Small businesses' liquidity needs were high." "Small business loan performance generally deteriorated further." "Vulnerabilities arising from financial leverage as moderate." "Banks' profitability... could be affected by future losses." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index was little changed." Mixed movements. Score: 50.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Surveys "moved lower, though each remained within the range." TIPS "edged up, on net, remaining close to their pre-pandemic levels." "Both the 5-year and 5-to-10-year measures remained near the lower ends of their historical ranges." Score: 47.
