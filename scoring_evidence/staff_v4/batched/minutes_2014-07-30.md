# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-07-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: July 2014. Q2 GDP rebounded. UE declined to 6.1%. Employment gains strongest since Q1 2012. PCE ~1.5% y/y (firming). Consumer spending rising more quickly. Housing turning up. 10/30yr yields declined ~20bp (geopolitical risks, lower long-run growth expectations). VIX at low levels. Staff noted "low and declining risk premiums" and "loosening of underwriting standards" raising risk of correction. Tapering continuing.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "increased at a faster pace in the second quarter"; "about 1½ percent higher than a year earlier"; core "also rose more quickly" and "1½ percent higher than a year earlier"; "gains in nominal wages and employee benefits remained modest"; longer-term expectations "declined"; but energy futures "trending down"
- Direction: Below neutral — inflation firming toward 1.5% but still below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "real GDP rebounded in the second quarter"; "expanded at only a modest pace, on balance, over the first half"; employment gains "the largest since the first quarter of 2012"; UE "declined to 6.1 percent"; staff: "real GDP would expand at a faster pace in the second half...and over the next two years"; "resource slack...somewhat narrower this year"; capacity utilization "moved higher"
- Direction: Below neutral — slack narrowing more rapidly.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 38 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "6.1 percent in June"; "average monthly gain for the second quarter was the largest since the first quarter of 2012"; "rate of long-duration unemployment moved down"; "rate of job openings rose further"; initial claims "declined further"; but "rate of hiring was unchanged and remained at a modest level"; part-time for economic reasons "edged up" and "remained elevated"
- Direction: Below neutral but approaching normal — substantial improvement.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 47 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose more quickly in the second quarter"; key factors "remained positive"; "gains in equity values and home prices boosted household net worth"; residential investment "turned up"; E&S "increased in the second quarter"; nonresidential construction "also increased"; manufacturing output expanded; capacity utilization rose
- Direction: Near neutral — broad-based improvement approaching trend.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 47 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Staff "lowered its assumed pace of potential output growth this year by more than it marked down GDP growth" — implies continued weak productivity.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financial conditions eased somewhat"; longer-term yields "fell"; equity prices "rose"; VIX "remained at low levels"; but staff: "low and declining risk premiums, low levels of market volatility, and a loosening of underwriting standards...raised somewhat the risk of an eventual correction in asset valuations"
- Direction: Below neutral — accommodative, but staff flagging risks.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 38 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: 10-year and 30-year yields "both declined about 20 basis points"; geopolitical concerns (Ukraine, Middle East) contributed; "continuation of a pattern that began last year"; "reduction in investors' expectations for longer-run economic growth and declines in term premiums"
- Direction: Below neutral — yields declining, no stress.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 27 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Credit flows to nonfinancial corporations remained strong"; issuance "stayed brisk"; C&I loans "continued to increase at a robust pace"; July SLOOS "easier lending standards and terms as well as stronger loan demand"; leveraged loans "remained solid"; CRE conditions "improved further"; banks "continued to ease their standards"
- Direction: Well below neutral — very narrow spreads, loosening underwriting.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: S&P 500 "rose about 1½ percent"; VIX "remained at low levels"; profits "increased modestly"; staff: "low and declining risk premiums...raised somewhat the risk of an eventual correction"
- Direction: Above neutral — rising valuations with compressed risk premiums.

**BANKING_STRESS: 38 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "relatively strong capital positions of U.S. banks"; "subdued use of maturity transformation and leverage"; "relatively low levels of leverage for the aggregate nonfinancial sector"; lending conditions easing broadly.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "broad trade-weighted dollar was little changed, on net"
- Direction: Neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Energy prices "rose in the second quarter" but gasoline "subsequently moved lower"; food inflation "increased"; core goods firming.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core PCE 1.5% y/y; services component firming.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Near-term inflation expectations...were little changed"; but "longer-term expectations declined"; TIPS-based measures "about unchanged"
- Direction: Near but slightly below neutral — longer-term expectations edging down.
