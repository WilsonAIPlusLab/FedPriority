# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2012-12-12

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: December 2012. UE 7.7%. Economy moderate but Hurricane Sandy disrupted data. QE3 ongoing ($85B/month with Treasury purchases added). Fiscal cliff concerns dominating. European crisis less acute. Staff revised down near-term slightly.

**Directional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Consumer price inflation slowed as consumer energy costs fell." Core "remained subdued." "gains in nominal wages remained slow." Staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2015." Below target.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace." Staff revised down slightly. "only a gradual decline in slack." Below potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 7.7%. "Private nonfarm employment increased at a slightly faster rate." But "share of long-duration unemployment...remained elevated." "Indicators of firms' job openings and hiring plans were little changed." Improving but still weak.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE "declined in October" (Hurricane Sandy). E&S "decreased in the third quarter." Housing "continued to improve gradually." Consumer sentiment "fell back." Below trend.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: "moderate rise in productivity" — brief mention.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "U.S. financial conditions were little changed, on balance." Fiscal cliff concerns created volatility but reversed. CRE "still generally strained." Mortgage refinancing "expanded a bit further." Stable.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Yields "declined, on net." Normal functioning. No dysfunction.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Investment-grade "widened modestly." Speculative-grade "fell to historical lows" and spreads "decreased slightly." Mixed but overall favorable.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad U.S. equity price indexes edged up, on net." Bank equity "decreased a little." Flat to slightly positive.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Bank CDS "continued to move lower." C&I loans "expanded notably." Bank credit "about flat." "Valuations...did not appear stretched." "Indicators of risk-taking...remained notably below their levels before the financial crisis." Improving.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "edged lower on balance." Slight depreciation.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation slowed"; core "remained subdued"; "gains in nominal wages remained slow"; "unit labor costs were essentially unchanged"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2015"
- Direction: Below neutral — subdued across the board.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace"; staff revised down slightly; "only a gradual decline in slack"; "progress in reducing unemployment was expected to be relatively slow"
- Direction: Below neutral — persistent wide slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "declined to 7.7 percent"; "Private nonfarm employment increased at a slightly faster rate"; but "long-duration unemployment...remained elevated"; "Indicators of firms' job openings and hiring plans were little changed"
- Direction: Below neutral — improving but still elevated.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "declined in October" (hurricane); E&S "decreased in the third quarter"; housing "continued to improve gradually"; "consumer sentiment fell back"
- Direction: Below trend — hurricane disruption plus weak investment.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 52 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "moderate rise in productivity"
- Direction: Near neutral — moderate gains.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 50 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "U.S. financial conditions were little changed, on balance"; CRE "still generally strained"; but mortgage refinancing improving; funding markets "stable"; "Valuations...did not appear stretched"
- Direction: Neutral — balanced.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Yields declined on net; safe-haven demand from fiscal cliff concerns; no dysfunction.
- Direction: Below neutral — orderly.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Speculative-grade "fell to historical lows"; spreads "decreased slightly"; bond issuance "increased further"; C&I loans "expanded notably"
- Direction: Below neutral — very favorable, near record lows.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 50 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad U.S. equity price indexes edged up, on net"; "Valuations for broad asset classes did not appear stretched"
- Direction: Neutral — flat, not stretched.

**BANKING_STRESS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS "continued to move lower"; C&I loans "expanded notably"; but CRE "still generally strained"; bank credit "about flat"; "Indicators of risk-taking and leverage...remained notably below their levels before the financial crisis"
- Direction: Near neutral — continued improvement.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "edged lower on balance"
- Direction: Near neutral — slight depreciation.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods subdued; energy declining.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 32 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not separately detailed; overall core subdued.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
