**Era context**: March 2011. Recovery continuing at moderate pace. QE2 ongoing. Oil prices rising. Unemployment ~8.9%. Inflation still below target but energy prices pushing headline up.

But discounting weakness — defense spending, severe weather, renewable energy distortions.
