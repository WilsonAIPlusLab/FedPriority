- Direction: Well below neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 38 ±4 [High confidence]**

- Direction: Below neutral — moderate recovery, substantial slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 18 ±3 [High confidence]**

- Direction: Far below neutral — first improvement but still extremely slack.

- Direction: Below neutral.
