**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.7%. Payrolls "robust." LFPR and E-P "both rose." Job openings "remained at a high level." ECI 5.5%. AHE 5.2%. "Labor market continued to be very tight." Score: 62.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "on track to post a modest gain." Housing "another sharp contraction." BFI "rising at a tepid pace." Net exports contributing positively. Score: 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "decreased slightly." VIX "increased somewhat...elevated by historical norms." Corporate spreads "narrowed slightly...roughly at the midpoints of their historical distributions." Borrowing costs rising across sectors. Mortgage rates "highest levels since 2008." "High borrowing costs appeared to reduce the demand for credit." Score: 55.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP elevated. CP spreads "changed little." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "narrowed slightly...roughly at the midpoints of their historical distributions." Defaults remained low. But leveraged loan downgrades > upgrades. Banks "increased loan loss provisions somewhat." Market-implied defaults "increased markedly" for spec-grade. Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "decreased slightly." VIX "elevated by historical norms." No explicit financial stability valuation update. Score: 50.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks "tightened credit standards on C&I lending for the first time in two years." But C&I and CRE loans "expanded at a strong pace." Credit quality "remained generally strong." Small business access "tightening somewhat." Subprime auto delinquencies "rebounding notably to slightly above their historical averages." Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "U.S. dollar appreciated further against most major currencies, reaching multi-decade highs against the euro, the British pound, and the Japanese yen." Score: 68.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Short-run inflation expectations declined in recent weeks, while measures of longer-term inflation expectations remained roughly stable or moved lower." TIPS inflation compensation "declined substantially at short horizons." Score: 55.
