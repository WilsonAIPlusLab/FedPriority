# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-09-22

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: September 2021. GDP increasing in Q3 at slower pace than Q2. UE 5.2%. PCE 4.2%, core 3.6%. Trimmed mean 2.0%. ECI 3.6% (6-month annualized). Delta variant slowing recovery. Supply constraints taking longer to resolve. Inflation risks now "tilted to the upside." Staff expects step-down to below 2% in 2022 but with upside risk. Chinese property developer concerns (Evergrande).

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 4.2%, core 3.6%. Trimmed mean 2.0%. CPI 5.3%, core 4.0%. ECI 3.6% (6-mo ann). Median wage "well above the growth rates seen before the pandemic." Staff: "supply constraints were putting a larger amount of upward pressure on prices than previously anticipated." Inflation risks "tilted to the upside." Score: 62.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increasing in the third quarter at a slower pace than in the second quarter." But "level of real GDP was expected to remain well above potential throughout the projection period." GDP above potential. Score: 52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 5.2%. LFPR edged up, E-P rose further. Job openings at "extraordinarily high" levels. "Strong labor demand and increased difficulties in hiring." ECI 3.6%. But "employment had retraced three-fourths of the losses." Score: 47.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "declined in July" but retail sales "rose strongly in August." Consumer confidence moved lower. Housing demand "very strong" but materials shortages restraining activity. BFI growth "slowing somewhat" — supply bottlenecks. Manufacturing rose strongly. Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Market-based financing conditions were robust, and credit availability improved for riskier borrowers." Corporate spreads at low end. Bank standards easing. "Highly accommodative." Score: 38.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR at 8-10bp, SOFR at 5bp. ON RRP averaged $1.08T. No stress. Score: 33.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: HY spreads "narrowed slightly." IG "little changed." Muni spreads "remained well below pre-pandemic levels." Upgrades "outpaced downgrades noticeably." Default rates "remained low." Score: 35.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "about unchanged." VIX "spiked to reach a two-month high" then recovered. No explicit financial stability section in this meeting's text about valuations. Score: 42.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks easing standards. C&I loans declining (PPP forgiveness). Credit quality "remained strong with a positive outlook." Small business loan originations "above pre-pandemic levels." But small business owners in COVID-sensitive sectors "became more pessimistic." Score: 42.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar appreciated a touch." Slight appreciation. Score: 52.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "little changed relative to the second quarter and was near its average over the decade before the pandemic." Inflation compensation "declined modestly." Score: 50.
