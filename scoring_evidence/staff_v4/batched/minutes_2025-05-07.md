PCE 2.3%, core 2.6%. AHE 3.8%. ECI 3.4%. Tariffs "significantly larger and broader than anticipated." Risky asset prices "extremely volatile." Equities ended "little changed" on net. VIX "modestly elevated." Spreads widened, especially HY. Dollar "depreciated against many AFE currencies despite widening yield differentials." Staff projection: "output gap was projected to widen significantly," UE "expected to weaken substantially," recession "almost as likely as the baseline." Asset valuations now "notable" (down from "elevated"). Leverage "notable." Funding risks "moderate."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 2.3%, core 2.6%. "Lower than their year-earlier levels." ECI 3.4%, AHE 3.8%. But tariffs "expected to boost inflation markedly this year." Near-term inflation compensation "rose notably." Staff: tariffs expected to "boost inflation markedly this year and to provide a smaller boost in 2026." Score: 55.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "declined slightly in the first quarter" but PDFP "posted a solid gain...similar to its average rate of increase over 2024." Staff: "output gap was projected to widen significantly over the forecast period." UE "forecast moving above the staff's estimate of its natural rate by the end of this year." Recession "almost as likely as the baseline forecast." Score: 45.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.2%. LFPR risen 0.2pp. E-P edged up. Payrolls "solid and roughly in line with the average pace seen over 2024." Vacancies/UE 1.0, "slightly below its average over 2019." ECI 3.4%, AHE 3.8%. But contacts "limiting or pausing hiring because of elevated uncertainty." Score: 48.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PDFP "solid gain in the first quarter." Consumer spending "grew solidly in March." But business contacts report "pausing or delaying capital expenditure plans amid increased uncertainty." Sentiment "sharp declines." Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "little changed" on net after extreme volatility. VIX "modestly elevated." Spreads "widened, on net, especially for speculative-grade." Treasury short/medium yields declined, longer-term rose. Borrowing costs "increased notably." Dollar depreciated. Score: 52.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained orderly." TGA dynamics ongoing. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY "widened, on net, especially for speculative-grade bonds, consistent with increased concerns about economic growth." But "spreads, however, remained at low levels." CRE: "concerns broadened beyond office properties." CMBS delinquencies "little changed." Credit card and auto delinquencies "remained at elevated levels." Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities ended flat after extreme volatility. VIX elevated. Staff: "asset valuation pressures were notable" (downgraded from "elevated"). "Equity valuations declined but still stood at the upper end of their historical distribution." HY spreads "stood around the middle of their historical distribution." Score: 42.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I standards "unchanged across all loan categories." Large banks "easing lending standards or leaving them unchanged" for CRE. "Regulatory capital ratios...remained high." Banks "still seen as exposed to some interest rate risk." Hedge fund leverage "stayed high." Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "depreciated against many advanced foreign economy currencies despite widening U.S.-AFE yield differentials." Market participants attributed to "concerns about potential adverse effects of trade policy on the U.S. growth outlook." Score: 42.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Near-term "rose notably." Longer-term "appeared to remain well anchored." But some participants saw "risk that they could drift upward." Score: 53.
