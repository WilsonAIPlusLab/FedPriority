### 2. Era Context

February 1999: Late-cycle U.S. expansion. The economy has been growing strongly (~4% in 1998) but staff are forecasting deceleration. The financial backdrop is shaped by post-Russian-default market stress (August 1998), the Brazilian real crisis (January 1999), and an extraordinary equity market boom. Unemployment is near 28-year lows. Inflation is running well below CPI measures due to PCE methodology. This is NOT a recession meeting; it is a boom/overheat-risk meeting with crosscurrents from international financial stress.
