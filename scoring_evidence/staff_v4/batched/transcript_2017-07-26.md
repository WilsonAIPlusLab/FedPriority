# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring -- 2017-07-26

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript -- staff economic briefing. July 2017 meeting.

**Era context**: July 2017. Rate at 1.00-1.25%. UE ~4.4%. Core PCE ~1.5%. Global growth solid and synchronized. Dollar depreciating. Inflation soft. Fiscal placeholder halved. ECB signaling possible taper. VIX near record lows. Asset valuations elevated to "elevated" from "notable."

**Staff speakers**:
- MR. POTTER: Markets -- "relatively uneventful." Dollar depreciated against most G-10 currencies. Bund yields rose on Draghi comments but no spillover. Inflation expectations/compensation declining. Survey: 60% probability of core PCE at or below 2% for 2-3 years ahead. December seen as most likely next hike.
- MR. WILCOX: U.S. outlook -- "forecast of real GDP growth is essentially unrevised since June." Halved fiscal placeholder. More accommodative financial conditions offset. Dollar weaker. r* revised down to 0.5%. Economy "running a little hotter than its longer-run sustainable level." UE projected to 3.75% by end-2020. Natural rate at 4.8%.
- MS. KLEE: Financial stability -- valuation pressures upgraded to "elevated" (from "notable"). VIX "at the bottom of its historical range and last week reached near-record lows." HY spread "narrowed and remained near the bottom of its range of the past 20 years." CRE cap rate spreads "near historical lows." Equity risk premium at 22nd percentile. But leverage low, maturity transformation low. Overall "moderate."
- MR. KAMIN: International -- growth "further reinforced" the narrative. "Solid and widespread expansion." AFE growth even higher than expected. EME growth came down slightly. Phillips curve puzzle abroad too. Dollar depreciated, net export drag diminishing. Foreign QE suppressing U.S. term premiums by ~71bp.

**Dimensional analysis**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Core PCE ~1.5%. Inflation expectations declining. "60 percent probability to 12-month PCE inflation over the period 2 to 3 years ahead coming in at or below 2 percent." Staff projects gradual return to 2%. Score: 30 -- well below target with expectations declining.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Economy "running a little hotter than its longer-run sustainable level." UE projected to fall to 3.75% by 2020. GDP near potential growth rate. Score: 53 -- above potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE ~4.4%. Natural rate 4.8%. Tight and getting tighter. Score: 55 -- tight.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Wilcox: "forecast essentially unrevised." Fiscal placeholder halved but offset by easier financial conditions. "Nothing burger" for data. Score: 50 -- at trend.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Wilcox: implicit in r* discussion. r* revised down to 0.5%. Low productivity growth implied. Score: 30 -- below trend.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Klee: conditions very accommodative. VIX near record lows. Spreads at 20-year lows. Equity risk premium at 22nd percentile. Dollar depreciating. "Investors currently appear to require unusually low premiums." Score: 34 -- very accommodative.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Not discussed as stress. Yields remained low. Score: N/A.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Klee: HY spread "narrowed and remained near the bottom of its range of the past 20 years." Leveraged loan spreads at "lower end of historical ranges." "Nonprice underwriting terms remain quite accommodative." Score: 26 -- very narrow.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Klee: valuation pressures "elevated." P/E for small-cap at 98th percentile, S&P 500 at 85th. Equity risk premium at 22nd percentile ("unusually low"). VIX near record lows. Composite index above 90th percentile. Score: 70 -- elevated.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Klee: "well capitalized." CET1 ratios "above fully phased-in requirements and edging higher." Hedge fund leverage "remained at the high end of its recent range" but contained. Overall "low." Score: 25 -- low stress.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Kamin: dollar depreciated against most currencies. Wilcox made dollar "weaker than in June projection." But still elevated historically. Score: 52 -- moderately strong, depreciating.

**Bright-line checks**: OUTPUT_GAP 53 above potential -- consistent. CREDIT_SPREADS 26 narrow -- correct. BANKING_STRESS 25 -- correct. DOLLAR_STRENGTH >50 -- correct but just barely.
