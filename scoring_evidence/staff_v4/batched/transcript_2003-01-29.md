# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2003-01-29

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff: Sack/Tetlow/etc (policy smoothing special briefing), Slifman (economic outlook/productivity). Era: January 2003. Meeting featured special briefings on interest rate smoothing and optimal policy near zero bound. Economic outlook presented by Slifman with detailed productivity analysis. Structural MFP raised — productivity "stunning." Structural productivity revised to ~2.5%. Payrolls "about flat over the next few months" then 100K, 225K, 300K progression. Unemployment projected to peak 6.2%. Core PCE projected to slow further. Iraq war scenarios discussed. Dollar weakening further. Fiscal policy expansionary — defense spending up.

**Key signals**: Slifman: "structural MFP increased 1.8 percent last year." Structural productivity revised to "almost 2½ percent in 2004." Payrolls "flat over the next few months, hiring averages about 100,000 per month in the first half of this year." Unemployment "to peak at 6.2 percent in the first half." Core PCE "projected to slow" — about 0.5pp lower over two years. Hourly compensation "downtrend." Deflation risk still material. Sack: optimal policy analysis — "immediate easing of policy to zero" in some scenarios. Iraq scenarios in Greenbook. Dollar declining.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 25 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Slifman: core PCE projected to decline further. "Continued wide margin of slack in product markets." Hourly compensation in "downtrend." Deflation risk from ZLB analysis still present.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — very subdued, deflation still a risk.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 32 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Slifman: unemployment projected to peak at 6.2%. GDP growth sluggish near-term. Output gap wide and not closing until late 2003/2004. Significant slack.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — wide gap. Bright-line satisfied.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 27 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Slifman: payrolls "about flat over the next few months." Unemployment peaking at 6.2%. Hiring very slow to resume. "Stretched work forces" but firms not hiring yet.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — significant slack.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 38 ±6 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Economy still in sluggish growth pattern from late 2002. Defense spending providing boost. But private sector demand weak. Iraq uncertainty weighing on confidence.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — subdued demand.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 68 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Slifman: structural MFP raised. "Structural labor productivity to 2¼ percent in 2002 and 2003 and to almost 2½ percent in 2004." "Technology has continued to improve in recent years." But cyclical component means "hiring will pick up as the prospects for sales and profits improve."
- **Direction**: Above neutral — strong structural.

### FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Funds rate at 1.25%. ZLB analysis shows policy already quite accommodative. But Iraq uncertainty, stock market weakness, and business caution still weighing. Fiscal policy expansionary (defense). Mixed conditions.
- **Direction**: Above neutral — rate accommodation vs. uncertainty/confidence drag.
