# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2016-11-02

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: November 2016 (pre-election meeting). GDP expanded faster in Q3 than H1. UE 5.0%. Inflation increasing. Asset prices little changed. Markets calm. December hike probability ~65%. Productivity flat. Michigan longer-run inflation expectations fell to "new historical low."

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE about 1.25% over 12 months ending September. Core about 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.25%. Inflation "increased further above its pace early in the year." Projected "marginally below" 2% in 2019. Score ~37.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "expanded at a faster pace in the third quarter." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output in 2017 and 2018." UE to "edge down gradually." Score ~49.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 5.0%. Payrolls "expanded at a solid pace." ECI 2.25%. AHE 2.5%. Part-time "still slightly elevated." Productivity "flat." Score ~50.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "increased at a moderate pace." Vehicle sales "rose sharply." Equipment "about flat." Residential investment "decreased." Housing "weak." Score ~45.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Domestic financial markets were relatively calm." Asset prices "little changed." Volatility "mostly low." Conditions "remained accommodative, on balance." Score ~37.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "edged up, on net." No dysfunction. Score ~44.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: IG and speculative-grade spreads "declined a bit" to "levels close to their medians during the economic expansions of the past two decades." Score ~44.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "little changed, on net." CRE "notable valuation pressures." Corporate equity/bond valuations "in the middle of their historical ranges." Score ~46.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate." Largest banks "strong regulatory capital and liquidity positions." Maturity/liquidity transformation "reduced" (MMF reform). Score ~34.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated against most AFE currencies." Appreciated notably against pound. But depreciated against commodity exporters. Score ~55.
