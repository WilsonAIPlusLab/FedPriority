# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-04-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: April 2014. Q1 GDP near zero (weather + trade). UE still 6.7%. PCE ~1% y/y, core ~1.25%. Tapering continuing. Financial conditions "did not change appreciably." Staff stability report: "vulnerability...moderate levels overall" but highlighted valuation pressures in some equity segments, underwriting standards loosening. Social media/biotech stocks fell sharply. Long-end yields declined (trend lower all year).

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "rose at a slow rate in the first quarter"; "about 1 percent higher than a year earlier"; core "around 1¼ percent higher than four quarters earlier"; "employment cost index and average hourly earnings...increased only a little more than consumer price inflation"; staff: "inflation would remain below...2 percent over the next few years"
- Direction: Well below neutral — inflation persistently low.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "growth in economic activity paused in the first quarter" but "activity stepped up late in the quarter"; "temporary effects of the unusually cold and snowy weather"; staff: medium-term "essentially unrevised"; "real GDP would expand at a faster pace...than it did last year"; "slowly reduce resource slack"
- Direction: Below neutral — weather-depressed Q1 but underlying trajectory unchanged.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 34 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "stayed at 6.7 percent"; participation rate and E-P ratio "increased slightly"; long-duration unemployment "declined somewhat"; but part-time for economic reasons "moved up"; "hiring rate was flat and continued to be subdued"
- Direction: Below neutral — mixed, gradual improvement.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "expanded slightly less rapidly"; housing "remained soft"; residential investment "decreased again"; E&S "declined in the first quarter"; but retail sales "rose briskly in March"; consumer sentiment "improved"; real disposable income "continued to increase"; "further gains in house prices likely bolstered household net worth"
- Direction: Below neutral — Q1 weakness mostly weather-related.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Staff continued to assume low potential output growth. No explicit productivity discussion.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "financial conditions did not change appreciably, on net"; "corporate bond spreads narrowed"; S&P 500 "increased slightly"; but "prices of social media and biotechnology stocks...fell sharply"; "vulnerability...moderate levels overall"; "valuation pressures in some segments of the equity market"; "continued strong demand for corporate debt instruments and associated pressures on underwriting standards"
- Direction: Near but below neutral — broadly accommodative, some pockets of concern.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Short/medium-term yields "rose, on balance"; but "yields at the long end of the curve declined, continuing a downward trend"; "portfolio reallocation by large institutional investors"; no dysfunction.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "corporate bond spreads narrowed"; C&I loan growth "remained robust"; leveraged loans "continued at a brisk pace"; CRE conditions "improved further"; banks "again eased standards on CRE loans"; "ongoing gradual easing of credit terms and deal structures"
- Direction: Below neutral — narrow and narrowing spreads.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: S&P 500 "increased a bit"; but "broader stock market indexes edged down"; social media/biotech "fell sharply"; staff noted "valuation pressures in some segments of the equity market"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — mixed signals, some froth correcting.

**BANKING_STRESS: 42 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Relatively strong capital profiles of large domestic banking firms"; "low levels of aggregate leverage"; "moderate use of short-term wholesale funding"; bank earnings "mixed"; lending standards continuing to ease.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "broad nominal exchange rate index for the dollar was about unchanged"; appreciated vs euro/yen/renminbi, depreciated vs most others.
- Direction: Neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 23 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Energy prices "increased markedly" in Q1 (natural gas); but core goods very subdued; overall PCE ~1% y/y.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 28 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core at ~1.25% y/y; services component subdued.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near- and longer-term inflation expectations from the Michigan survey were unchanged"; TIPS-based measures "little changed"
