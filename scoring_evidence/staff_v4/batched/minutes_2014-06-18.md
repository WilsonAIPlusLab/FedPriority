# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-06-18

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: June 2014. Strong rebound from Q1 weather effects. UE dropped to 6.3%. Employment gains accelerating. PCE inflation ~1.5% y/y (picking up from earlier lows). CPI ~2%. VIX near historical lows. Long-end yields continued declining (lower long-run expectations). Equity prices rose. Credit very accommodative. Tapering continuing on schedule.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "about 1½ percent over the 12 months ending in April"; core PCE "also around 1½ percent"; CPI "about 2 percent"; "In May, the consumer price index...increased at a faster pace"; "Increases in measures of labor compensation remained modest"; "compensation per hour...rose about 2¼ percent"; staff revised near-term up but medium-term "down slightly, reflecting a reassessment...of the underlying trend"
- Direction: Below neutral — inflation picking up but still below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 39 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "real GDP had dropped significantly early in the year but that economic growth had bounced back"; "average pace of employment gains stepped up"; staff: "real GDP would expand at a faster pace in the second half...and over the next two years"; "slowly reduce resource slack"; UE "expected to decline gradually"
- Direction: Below neutral but narrowing — strong rebound, slack diminishing.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 37 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "dropped to 6.3 percent in April"; "Total nonfarm payroll employment expanded...at a faster rate"; "Initial claims for unemployment insurance decreased slightly"; "rate of job openings stepped up in April"; but "rate of hiring was unchanged and remained at a modest level"; participation "declined in April"; part-time for economic reasons still "high"
- Direction: Below neutral — significant improvement, approaching normal levels.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 44 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "declined a little in April following strong gains in February and March"; "light motor vehicle sales moved up briskly"; "Real disposable income continued to rise"; housing "remained subdued"; but E&S "increased slowly"; industrial production "increased, on balance"; capacity utilization "rose"
- Direction: Near neutral — consumer recovering, investment mixed.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 47 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "small gains in labor productivity"; staff "slightly lowered its assumptions for real GDP growth and the level of equilibrium real interest rates" — implies weak productivity.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "financial conditions in the United States remained supportive of growth"; longer-term yields "moved down modestly"; equity prices rose; "corporate bond spreads narrowed"; VIX "continued to decline and ended the period near its historical lows"; "Measures of uncertainty in other financial markets also declined"
- Direction: Below neutral — very accommodative, low volatility.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Short/medium-term yields "increased slightly"; but "yields at the long end of the curve edged lower, continuing a downward trend"; market participants discussed "lower expectations for potential growth and policy rates in the longer run"
- Direction: Below neutral — yields declining at long end, no stress.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "gross issuance of investment- and speculative-grade bonds rebounded"; C&I loans "increased"; leveraged loans "remained strong"; CRE loans "continued to increase amid some further easing of underwriting standards"; consumer credit "solid"; credit card balances "increased"
- Direction: Well below neutral — very narrow spreads, aggressive credit flows.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad stock price indexes rose"; "boosted by a more optimistic assessment of near-term economic prospects"; VIX "near its historical lows"; "upward revisions in analysts' forecasts"
- Direction: Above neutral — rising valuations, very low volatility.

**BANKING_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Credit broadly available; lending standards easing; CRE standards easing; consumer credit accommodative; auto and student loans expanding robustly.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "foreign exchange value of the dollar was little changed, on balance"; appreciated vs euro, depreciated vs Canadian dollar and EME currencies.
- Direction: Neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Energy prices "rose faster than total consumer prices"; food "climbed more slowly"; core goods still subdued but firming from very low levels.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core PCE ~1.5%; CPI core ~2%; services component firming.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations from the survey were little changed"; near-term "declined slightly"; TIPS-based measures "remained about steady"
