## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: December 2021. GDP growth picking up in Q4 after Q3 slowdown. UE 4.2%. PCE 5.0%, core 4.1%. Trimmed mean 2.6%. CPI 6.8%. AHE 4.8%. "Inflationary pressures had broadened." Omicron variant emerging. Staff assumes taper acceleration. Market pricing rate hikes pulled forward into 2022. Corporate spreads widened moderately. GDP "well above potential."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 5.0%, core 4.1%. Trimmed mean 2.6%. CPI 6.8%, core 4.9%. AHE 4.8%. "Inflationary pressures had broadened in recent months." Staff projects PCE to end year ~5%. Expects 2.1% in 2022 — but "supply bottlenecks were assumed to resolve more gradually" and "higher inflation readings was assumed to raise the underlying trend." Score: 70.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "picking up in the fourth quarter." "Level of real GDP was expected to remain well above potential throughout the projection period." "Labor market conditions were projected to remain very tight." Above potential. Score: 55.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.2%. LFPR and E-P "both moved up in November." Job openings "well above pre-pandemic levels." Quits rates "elevated." Initial claims approaching pre-pandemic. AHE 4.8% "with sizable wage gains observed across most sectors." Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "picking up in the fourth quarter" despite headwinds. Retail goods rose solidly in Oct. Housing demand "remained strong" but activity "little changed." BFI "rising at a slow pace" — supply bottlenecks. Manufacturing increased in Oct. Score: 48.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Financing conditions for businesses and households remained accommodative except for small businesses and nonprime borrowers." Corporate spreads "widened moderately." Equities "little changed." Market pricing less accommodative policy. Score: 42.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR at 8bp, SOFR at 5bp. ON RRP averaged $1.5T. No stress. Score: 33.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "widened moderately." Muni spreads "little changed." But defaults "declined to close to historical lows." Upgrades outpaced downgrades. Default expectations "remained benign." Score: 38.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "little changed." "Equity market volatility increased markedly." Corporate spreads widened. Pandemic concerns + inflation/policy concerns weighing on risk assets. No explicit financial stability valuation update in this text. Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans "grew for the first time since the beginning of the year." Credit quality "remained solid amid strong earnings growth." S&P 500 earnings "exceeded analyst expectations." Small business credit supply "stable but tighter than before the pandemic." Delinquencies "roughly in line with pre-pandemic levels." Score: 42.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index increased modestly." Dollar appreciating. Score: 53.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based measures "leveled off after having risen over the past year." Inflation compensation "declined moderately since the November FOMC meeting." Score: 52.
