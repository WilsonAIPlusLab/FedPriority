Score ~42.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real personal consumption expenditures apparently declined in September for the fourth consecutive month." "Business spending on equipment and software appeared to have declined again in the third quarter." Housing continuing descent. "Consumer sentiment dropped... near or below their low levels of midyear." Direction: contracting sharply. Score ~18.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not prominently discussed. N/A.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Funding markets for terms beyond overnight largely ceased to function at times." "Investors pulled back from risk-taking." CDS spreads at "unprecedented levels." 7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: "Strong flows into government-only money market mutual funds." "Treasury bills traded with very low yields." "Failures to deliver Treasury securities reached record highs." "Repo rates on agency collateral also were volatile." "Overnight repo rate for Treasury general collateral was near zero." Direction: severe dysfunction. Score ~85.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "CDS spreads for investment-grade financial and nonfinancial firms reached unprecedented levels." "Liquidity in the corporate bond and CDS markets was strained." "Prices of syndicated leveraged loans falling to new lows." "Little issuance of speculative-grade bonds." Direction: extreme widening → must be > 50. Score ~90.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad equity price indexes declined sharply." "Option-implied volatility on the S&P 500 index rose well above its previous record high." Direction: severely depressed. Score ~82.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Lehman, AIG, Washington Mutual, Wachovia. "Intensified market concerns about the condition of financial institutions." "Failure or near failure of a number of major financial institutions." "Negative spiral in which financial strains lead to weaker spending." Capital purchase plan, FDIC guarantees. Direction: severe systemic stress → must be > 50. Score ~88.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Dollar appreciated against most currencies, with the prominent exception of the Japanese yen." Direction: strengthening → must be > 50. Score ~65.
