# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-05-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: May 2013. Moderate expansion. UE 7.6%. Inflation very low — PCE "rose just 1 percent from a year earlier." Sequestration ongoing but effects moderate. Financial conditions improved slightly. BoJ massive easing. QE3 ongoing. Taper discussions beginning.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 27 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "rose just 1 percent from a year earlier"; core "flat in March"; "gains in nominal wages remained subdued"; "employment cost index were modest"; staff: "inflation would remain subdued through 2015"
- Direction: Well below neutral — inflation notably below 2% target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity expanded at a moderate pace"; "only somewhat exceed the growth rate of potential output in 2013"; "anticipated to slowly reduce the slack"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.6 percent"; "rate of long-duration unemployment and the share of workers employed part time for economic reasons declined somewhat...but remained well above pre-recession levels"; "rate of gross private-sector hiring...were subdued"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 42 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "expanded at a solid pace"; "real disposable income increased"; housing "continued to improve" with "real expenditures for residential investment expanded briskly"; E&S "Growth...slowed"; but consumer sentiment "remained relatively downbeat"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not directly discussed.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financial conditions improved a little"; CRE "continued to improve in recent months"; CRE loans at banks "edged up"; lending standards eased; "Cyprus" concerns proved temporary

**TREASURY_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Treasury yields "declined appreciably"; no dysfunction.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Corporate yields "fell roughly in line with...Treasury securities, generally leaving their spreads little changed"; bond issuance "remained robust"; leveraged loans "issued at a record pace"

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad equity price indexes increased, on net"; option-implied volatility "remained in a range that was low by historical standards"

**BANKING_STRESS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Bank equity "declined somewhat" and CDS "edged a bit higher"; but C&I lending strong; standards easing; CRE improving.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 47 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "After appreciating, on balance, since early this year, the dollar depreciated against most currencies"

**INFLATION_GOODS: 25 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods very subdued; overall PCE only 1% y/y.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core ex food/energy "only edged up" — very low services inflation.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations...were little changed and remained within the narrow range"
