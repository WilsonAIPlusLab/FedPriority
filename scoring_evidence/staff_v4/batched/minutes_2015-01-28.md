# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2015-01-28

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: January 2015. Pre-liftoff. GDP expanded at solid pace in H2 2014. UE declined to 5.6%. Inflation well below 2% due to energy price declines. Market-based inflation compensation declined substantially. Dollar strengthened notably. ECB announced sovereign bond purchases. Oil prices collapsed. Staff projects GDP faster than potential in 2015-2016.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE 1.25% over 12 months ending November. Core 1.5%. CPI 0.75% over 12 months ending December. AHE "increased only slightly faster than core consumer price inflation." Energy prices declining sharply. Projected to remain below 2% but reach 2% "over time." Score ~32.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "expanded at a solid pace over the second half of 2014." Staff projects GDP to "expand at a modestly faster pace in 2015 and 2016" and "rise more quickly than potential output." UE expected to "move slightly below" natural rate. Score ~44.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 5.6%. Payrolls expanded with Q4 gains above Q3. LFPR decreased. Part-time for economic reasons "declined." Job openings "moved up" to elevated level. Score ~42.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Real PCE "appeared to have risen at a robust pace over the second half of 2014." Vehicle sales "robust." Consumer sentiment "highest level in more than a decade." But business equipment "appeared to decelerate." Nonresidential structures "edged down." Housing "improved somewhat but remained slow." Score ~51.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Treasury yields "declined." Equity markets "volatile" but "moved higher." Corporate bond spreads "little changed" for IG, "ended the period lower" for speculative-grade. Financing "generally remained strong." Score ~37.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "continued to move lower." Yield curve "flattened." Interest rate volatility "increased somewhat." TIPS inflation compensation 5-10 years ahead "declined further to its lowest level in a decade." Score ~40.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: IG spreads "little changed." Speculative-grade "ended the period lower." But leveraged loan issuance at "slowest pace in two years" with "spreads on newly issued loans increased." Score ~36.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "moved higher" but "volatile." Option-implied volatility "remained in the upper half of the range seen over the past year." Bank stocks "moved lower." Score ~37.
- BANKING_STRESS: "Relatively high levels of capital and liquidity." "Moderate levels of maturity transformation." "Relatively subdued pace of borrowing." But "valuation pressures in some asset markets" notably corporate debt and CRE. Score ~28.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "strengthened against the currencies of most other advanced economies." "Largely unchanged" against EMEs. ECB easing, multiple central banks cutting rates. Score ~63.
