**Era context**: March 2013. QE3 ongoing. Sequester took effect March 1. UE ~7.7%. European crisis: Italian elections produced hung parliament, Cyprus banking crisis. China slowing from above 9%. Japan — Abenomics beginning. Financial stability concerns emerging re: leveraged loans, reach for yield.

**Wilcox key points**: Domestic outlook "very similar to January." Employment "now estimated to be 330,000 above the January Tealbook." "Nudged up pace of monthly payroll." Housing starts better. Manufacturing "come in stronger." Sequester assumed to have "roughly half the effect of a full sequestration." Core inflation "close to expectations." "No material revisions to core PCE forecast." Uncertainty "relatively elevated" but risks to inflation "roughly balanced."

**Kamin (international)**: Italian elections — "pro-reform parties failed to gain decisive majority." Berlusconi and Grillo gains. Cyprus — "€10 billion assistance program" but "plans to impose taxes on insured deposits... shocked and dismayed observers." Euro area "recession deeper than previously anticipated." GDP "declined 2.3 percent." UE rose to 11.9%. But "expect euro area will limp out of recession by mid-year." Japan revised up on Abenomics. China "tepid" — "new measures to cool property market."

**Liang (financial stability)**: Banking sector "generally able to withstand severe credit or interest rate shocks." "Tier 1 capital BHCs has been climbing since 2008." But "signs of imbalances" in leveraged finance — CLO issuance, high-yield bond flows rising. "Covenant-lite issuance" increasing. Agency REITs growing rapidly — "significant maturity transformation." "Valuations in major asset classes do not appear rich." "Equity expected real return remains near 8 percent." "Spreads on corporate bonds have narrowed but far-forward premium not far from historical average." Overall: "low rates have contributed to gains in asset prices and debt growth, though from weak levels."
