# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2007-08-07

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format. Scoring only staff-attributable content.

**Era context**: August 2007. Financial turmoil erupting — subprime crisis spreading to ABCP markets, leveraged loan markets seizing, spec-grade spreads widening sharply. Economy still growing moderately. This is the inflection meeting where financial conditions cross into restrictive territory.

**Dimensional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Overall inflation receded in June because of a decline in energy prices." "Core PCE price index rose a bit less than its average pace over the past year." Core improving. But staff "left its projection for core PCE inflation unchanged for 2008" due to slower productivity and import prices. Direction: near neutral, modestly above. Score ~52.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Staff: "economic activity picked up in the second quarter." "Economy expanded at a moderate pace during the first half." Staff lowered forecast "somewhat" for H2 2007 and 2008, "less accommodative financial conditions." Still near potential. Direction: near but slightly below potential. Score ~46.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.6%. "Private nonfarm payroll employment continued to increase at a healthy pace." But "construction and manufacturing employment" declining. Direction: above neutral — still tight. Score ~53.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Growth of real consumer spending slowed considerably in the second quarter." But "business spending on equipment and software... posted a solid increase." "Outlays for nonresidential construction rose rapidly." Direction: mixed, near trend. Score ~47.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Staff "trimmed its estimates of the growth rates of structural productivity and potential GDP." Direction: below trend. Score ~38.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: KEY INFLECTION. "Financial market conditions were volatile." "Conditions in markets for subprime mortgages... deteriorated sharply." "Credit conditions for speculative-grade corporate borrowers tightened substantially." But "credit conditions for investment-grade businesses and prime households were relatively little affected." This is the crossing point from accommodative to restrictive for risky segments. Direction: crossing into tighter than neutral. Score ~55.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: "Yields on nominal Treasury securities fell on balance, possibly reflecting an increased preference by investors for safe assets." Flight to quality but not dysfunction. Score ~45.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads on speculative-grade bonds increased to near their highest levels in the past four years." Investment-grade spreads widened modestly. "A number of high-yield bond and leveraged loan deals intended to finance leveraged buyouts were delayed or restructured." Direction: widening into elevated territory → must be > 50. Score ~58.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad stock price indexes declined substantially." "Share prices of financial firms fell especially sharply." Direction: declining, stress emerging. Score ~58.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Subprime exposure concerns growing. "Financial firms fell especially sharply, reportedly a reflection, in part, of concerns about exposures to subprime mortgages." "Investors had become much more uncertain about the likely future cash flows from subprime." Segments of ABCP market deteriorating. Direction: emerging stress → crossing 50. Score ~52.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Foreign exchange value of the dollar... fell, on balance." Direction: weakening → must be < 50. Score ~40.

**Bright-line checks**: OUTPUT_GAP < 50 (near but slightly below potential) ✓. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS > 50 (tightening) ✓. CREDIT_SPREADS > 50 (widening) ✓. BANKING_STRESS > 50 (emerging stress) ✓. DOLLAR_STRENGTH < 50 (weakening) ✓.

**Coherence checks**:
- OUTPUT_GAP (46) ↔ LABOR_TIGHTNESS (53): Divergence of 7. Acceptable.
- OUTPUT_GAP (46) ↔ CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (47): Aligned ✓.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (55) ↔ CREDIT_SPREADS (58): Both moving into tighter ✓.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 52 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core PCE price index rose a bit less than its average pace over the past year." "Overall inflation receded in June." Staff left core PCE forecast unchanged for 2008 due to offsetting factors.
- Center-of-gravity: Core inflation improving but still modestly above target.
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 46 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Economy expanded at a moderate pace during the first half." Staff "lowered somewhat its forecast of real GDP growth in the second half of 2007 and in 2008."
- Center-of-gravity: Near but slightly below potential, with downward revisions.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 53 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE at 4.6%. "Private nonfarm payroll employment continued to increase at a healthy pace." Construction and manufacturing shedding jobs.
- Center-of-gravity: Still tight but softening at the margins.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 47 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Consumer spending "slowed considerably." But business investment "posted a solid increase." Nonresidential construction "rose rapidly."
- Center-of-gravity: Mixed — near trend, slightly below.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 38 ±10 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Staff "trimmed its estimates of the growth rates of structural productivity and potential GDP." NIPA revisions showed "somewhat less rapid growth in output and productivity."
- Center-of-gravity: Below trend.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 55 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Conditions in markets for subprime mortgages and related instruments... deteriorated sharply." "Credit conditions for speculative-grade corporate borrowers tightened substantially." But investment-grade and prime household credit "relatively little affected."
- Center-of-gravity: Crossing into tighter territory — selective tightening spreading.
- Direction: Above neutral — tightening.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 45 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Flight to quality driving Treasury yields lower. No dysfunction described.
- Center-of-gravity: Normal functioning, some safe-haven flows.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 58 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Spreads on speculative-grade bonds increased to near their highest levels in the past four years." "Several high-yield bond and leveraged loan deals... delayed or restructured." Investment-grade spreads widened modestly.
- Center-of-gravity: Significantly widened in high-yield; investment-grade widening.
- Direction: Above neutral — widening.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 58 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad stock price indexes declined substantially." "Share prices of financial firms fell especially sharply."
- Center-of-gravity: Sharp declines, especially financials. Stress emerging.
- Direction: Above neutral — depressed.

**BANKING_STRESS: 52 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Concerns about exposures to subprime mortgages." ABCP market segments deteriorating. "Markets for securities backed by subprime... had become illiquid." But system still functioning.
- Center-of-gravity: Crossing into stress territory. Subprime exposures weighing on banks.
- Direction: Above neutral — emerging stress.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Foreign exchange value of the dollar... fell, on balance."
- Center-of-gravity: Moderate depreciation.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 48 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 55 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 52 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
