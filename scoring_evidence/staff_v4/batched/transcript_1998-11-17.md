# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1998-11-17

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. PRELL (domestic), MR. FISHER (operations), MS. JOHNSON (international/Brazil). Post-LTCM meeting — Fed has already cut 75bp (Sept 29, Oct 15, and this meeting). Financial markets beginning to normalize but risks remain.

**Era context**: November 1998. Post-LTCM rescue. Markets stabilizing but credit conditions tighter. Brazil IMF package being negotiated. Stock market has rallied ("rousing rally"). Domestic economy still strong — Q3 GDP apparently strong though with "inventory story." Staff sees deceleration ahead. Profits weakening.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: "ECI for the third" quarter raises "questions"; but "favorable" news on wages and prices "even in the face of tighter labor markets." Inflation very low. Score ~40.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Prell: Q3 GDP strong but "much an inventory story"; "manufacturing sector has been quite" weak; "we've once again" seen strength in economy; but staff expects slowdown. Economy still above potential. Score ~54.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Prell: implied still tight but "slowed more than we expected"; some "downsizing announcements." Still tight but easing. Score ~60.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: spending outlook depends on market behavior; "trend of profits is still weak"; "corporate cash flow is a negative for capital spending"; firms "do not see current and prospective demand warranting" expansion; "many firms — especially manufacturers — are" cutting programs. Mixed — consumer stable but business weakening. Score ~50.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not directly discussed. Score ~57.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Prell: "bond market" recovering "in terms of new issuance activity"; "spreads" still wide; "commercial paper market is having some" difficulty; banks "filling in — sometimes reluctantly." "Credit terms overall have deteriorated" despite rate cuts. "Trend toward ever easier access" to credit has ended. Score ~60.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: "rousing rally" in stock market but "market is skating on thin ice, at best"; "prediction of a 10 percent" further decline may be "conservative." Score ~30 (rally but still overvalued per staff).

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Prell: spreads still wide; commercial paper market stressed; banks reluctantly filling in. Score ~60.

9. **BANKING_STRESS**: Banks filling in for capital markets "reluctantly"; tightening lending. Score ~55.

10. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Johnson: dollar "about 3 percent lower in the near term" and "about 2 percent lower toward the end of" forecast. Dollar weakened. Score ~42.

**Bright-line checks**: FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 60 — "credit terms have deteriorated," tighter → >50. CHECK. CREDIT_SPREADS 60 — still wide → >50. CHECK.
