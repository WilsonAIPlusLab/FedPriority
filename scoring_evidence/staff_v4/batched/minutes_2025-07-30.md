**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE est 2.5%, core 2.7%. "Similar to their year-earlier levels." AHE 3.7% (slightly lower than year-earlier). "Disinflation appeared to have stalled, with tariffs putting upward pressure on goods price inflation." Staff: tariffs "expected to raise inflation this year and to provide some further upward pressure in 2026." Inflation projected to decline to 2% by 2027. Score: 55.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanded at a tepid pace in the first half of the year." Growth "slower than last year." PDFP also slowed. Staff: GDP growth "projected to be faster next year than this year" as tariff drag wanes. UE "projected to move slightly above the staff's estimate of its natural rate." Score: 47.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.1%. LFPR edged down. E-P unchanged. Payrolls "solid in June, though the pace of private payroll gains stepped down noticeably." Vacancies/UE 1.1 (narrow range). AHE 3.7%. "Labor market conditions remained solid." Score: 48.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PDFP "slowed in the second quarter, as a step-down in investment growth offset faster PCE growth." Activity "slowed in the first half of the year, driven in large part by slower consumption growth and a decline in residential investment." Score: 42.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities increased. Credit spreads narrowed to "exceptionally low levels relative to their historical distribution." VIX "just below the median." Treasury yields "little changed." Borrowing costs "eased moderately but remained elevated relative to post-GFC average levels." Dollar "little changed." Score: 40.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained orderly." TGA increase post-debt limit. SRF take-up $11.1B at quarter-end. ON RRP "little changed." Score: 38.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Spreads narrowed to "exceptionally low levels" for all categories except lowest-quality. Credit card delinquency "a little below its year-earlier value." Auto delinquencies stable. FHA delinquencies "continued to be elevated." Corporate performance "generally stable but somewhat weaker than pre-pandemic." Leveraged loan defaults "below post-GFC medians." Score: 40.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. Staff: "asset valuation pressures were elevated." P/E ratios "at the upper end of their historical distribution." HY spreads "very low relative to their historical distribution." Housing valuations "edged down but remained elevated." Score: 35.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Bank stress tests: "all participants stayed above minimum capital requirements even under stress conditions." Banks "more exposed to interest rate risk than had been historically typical, albeit to a lesser degree than was the case earlier in the decade." Life insurers leverage "somewhat elevated." Hedge fund leverage "remained high and concentrated in the largest firms." Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Broad dollar "little changed on net." Score: 50.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Longer-term "continued to be well anchored." Near-term compensation "rose, particularly at shorter horizons, reflecting perceived risks associated with trade policy." Score: 53.
