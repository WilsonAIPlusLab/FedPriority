# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-07-31

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — Wilcox (domestic), Kamin (international). Segmented. 373 lines.

**Era context**: July 2013. Post-taper tantrum. QE3 ongoing, taper expected. UE ~7.6%. Rates have risen significantly since May. Housing: starts weak but permits up. Consumer spending weaker than expected Q1-Q2. Fiscal drag diminishing. Europe — "signs of life" in AFEs. EMEs slowing.

**Wilcox key points**: "Real GDP increased about ½ percentage point less in the first half than we were expecting." "PCE weaker" — interpreted as partial "adjustment to tax increases." Housing starts "puzzled by how weak" but permits up; "not inclined to attribute" weakness to mortgage rate increase. "Business investment: orders surprised us to the upside." GDP acceleration expected in second half. Medium-term outlook "substantially unrevised." "Financial conditions little changed on net" — "higher stock market" offsetting "higher path of long-term interest rates." "Core inflation below 1 percent" — expects "step up" as transitory factors recede. UE decline "one quarter earlier" to Q2 2015 for threshold. "Payroll employment surprised us by 100,000 to the upside."

**Kamin key points**: "Signs of life in the advanced foreign economies." Japan 4% growth Q2. UK GDP 2.5%. Euro area PMI "broke into expansionary territory for the first time since January 2012." "We are feeling more confident about outlook for advanced foreign economies than we have for some time." But EME growth "stepped down from nearly 4 percent to 2½ percent." China growth uncertain. "Cannot be said for the emerging market economies."
