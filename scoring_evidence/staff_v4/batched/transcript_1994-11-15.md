**Era context**: November 1994. Late tightening cycle — funds rate at 4.75%, hiked 75bp to 5.5%. Growth strong. Inflation pressures building. Staff forecast calls for further tightening.

**Dimension-by-dimension**:

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Fisher: "extraordinarily high real rates" globally. Significant tightening. But Prell: housing hasn't weakened as expected. Score ~57.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: equities "held up in face of rising bond yields" on "strong earnings." Expects "not pleasant surprises in 1995" and "weakening profit margins." Near fair value. Score ~48.

8. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Fisher: dollar weak but stabilizing. Yellen: "risen a bit against mark and yen." Mixed. Score ~47.
