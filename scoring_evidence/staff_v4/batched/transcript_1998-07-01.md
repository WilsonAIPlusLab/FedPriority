# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1998-07-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. PRELL (domestic, extensive charts), MR. HOOPER (international/Asia), MR. FISHER (markets). July 1998. Comprehensive chart presentation.

**Era context**: July 1998. Fed at 5.50%. Prell: extensive chart-based presentation; forecasts "marked slowing" of expansion; GDP below potential (~2-3/4%) over next 6 quarters; unemployment rising to 4.5% by year-end, 5% by end-1999; CPI 1-3/4% this year, 2-1/4% next. Stock market: P/E "at an historic high" and past trough — staff predicts 6% decline. Housing: "all signals on demand are still flashing green"; home sales at "new high." Inventory correction expected. Hooper: Asia GDP falling 3%; crisis "substantially larger than previously believed"; pessimistic scenario with yen to 175, China/HK pegs breaking. Labor market "extremely tight"; health insurance costs rising 3-1/4%. Oil prices "sharply lower"; commodity prices "plummeted." Productivity: trend growth 2-3/4% with capital deepening and multifactor improvements.

**Dimension-by-dimensional assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: CPI 1-3/4% this year; oil "sharply lower"; commodities "plummeted"; "further disinflation among consumer commodities." Very benign. Score ~38.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP above potential currently; forecasts deceleration to below potential. Score ~55.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Prell: anecdotal reports "continue to stress the difficulties" in finding/retaining workers; Conference Board jobs plentiful "outstripped" hard to get "by a wide margin"; health insurance costs rising 3-1/4%. Score ~65.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: PDFD at 4%+; housing "all signals flashing green"; home sales at record; "fundamentals at this point are still highly favorable." Score ~58.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Prell: extensive analysis; potential output growth ~2-3/4%; capital deepening contributing; multifactor productivity at 1/3pp above prior 15 years. Score ~58.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Stock market at historic highs; rates relatively low; commodity prices down. Very accommodative. Score ~32.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: P/E "not only at an historic high" but past trough; "extraordinary"; staff predicts 6% decline. Score ~15.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Not directly discussed. Some Asia-related widening. Score ~52.

9. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Hooper: dollar strong; trade-weighted average up; yen declining. Score ~62.
