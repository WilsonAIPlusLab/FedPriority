# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2024-03-20

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: March 2024. Hold at 5.25-5.50%. GDP expanding at solid rate in Q1 but slower than Q4. UE edged up to 3.9%. PCE 2.5%, core 2.9%. Trimmed mean 3.2%. AHE below year-ago. But month-over-month readings "firmed a bit." Market rate path increased markedly (reversed Dec declines). Treasury yields moved up. Equities increased notably. Spreads narrowed. Dollar little changed. CRE deterioration sparking investor concerns about some banks.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 2.5%, core 2.9%. Trimmed mean 3.2%. "Continued to trend down, although the month-over-month readings...had moved up." AHE "well below its level a year ago." Staff expects ~2.5% total and core at end of 2024. Score: 50.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanding at a solid rate in the first quarter, although slower than its robust fourth-quarter pace." Staff projects output growth "below potential" in 2024, "in line with potential" in 2025-2026. Higher projected population from immigration. UE "roughly flat over the next several years." Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE edged up to 3.9%. Payrolls "increased at a faster average monthly pace" in Jan-Feb vs Q4. LFPR and E-P "essentially unchanged." Openings and quits "far below their year-earlier levels." AHE "well below its level a year ago." Atlanta Wage Growth Tracker lower. Score: 50.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: GDP solid but "some slowing in PCE growth" — expenditures declined in Jan. "Some deceleration in business fixed investment." Residential investment "modest pickup." Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "increased notably." Spreads narrowed. But Treasury yields "moved up" markedly. Borrowing costs "remained elevated." Rates on household loans "generally rose." Score: 50.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP "continued to decline" but "rate of decline could be slowing." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG "little changed." HY "narrowed slightly." Credit card delinquencies "above levels seen just before the pandemic." CRE nonfarm nonresidential delinquency "increased at the end of 2023 to a level last seen in late 2014." CMBS office delinquency "continued to increase." Small business credit quality "deteriorated further." Score: 48.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "increased notably amid upbeat corporate earnings." VIX "remained low by historical standards." Score: 38.

**BANKING_STRESS**: CRE deterioration "sparked investor concerns about the health of a few small U.S. and foreign banks." Banks reported tightening in Jan SLOOS. Credit card delinquencies above pre-pandemic. CRE loan extensions masking true delinquency rates. But "regulatory risk-based capital ratios remained high." Score: 52.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Broad dollar "little changed on net." Score: 50.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Broadly in line with the levels seen in the decade before the pandemic" at both shorter and longer horizons. Score: 48.
