**Era context**: May 2018. Labor market continued to strengthen. GDP rose at moderate pace in Q1. PCE inflation reached 2% in March. Core 1.9%. UE at 4.1% for six months. Trade tensions with China emerging. Equities declined on trade/tech concerns but partially recovered on earnings. Dollar appreciated moderately. VIX elevated vs 2017 but declining. Asset valuation pressures "elevated." Nonfinancial business leverage "elevated."

- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Consumer expenditures "rose at a modest pace in the first quarter following a strong gain." Equipment "increased at a moderate pace." Nonresidential structures "rose at a robust pace." Residential investment "was unchanged." Score ~49.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Equities declined (trade/tech). Spreads widened a bit for IG, unchanged for speculative-grade. Dollar appreciated. But "financing conditions... largely remained supportive of spending." Score ~42.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "continued to flatten." 2-year up 17bp, 10-year up 7bp. TIPS compensation "increased." Oil prices rising. VIX declined but elevated vs 2017. Score ~47.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: IG spreads "widened a bit"; speculative-grade "unchanged." CMBS "little changed" and "near their post-crisis lows." Nonfinancial corporate debt-to-assets "near multidecade highs." Score ~42.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: S&P 500 "decreased." Trade tensions and tech concerns. VIX "declined but remained at elevated levels relative to 2017, ending the period at approximately 15 percent." Asset valuation pressures "continued to be elevated" though "come down a little." Score ~46.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation "elevated" though come down. Financial leverage/transformation "low." Household leverage "low-to-moderate." Nonfinancial business leverage "elevated." Score ~35.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "appreciated moderately." Early period slight depreciation (NAFTA positive), then broad-based appreciation on rising US yields and Mexican peso decline. Score ~55.
