# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2018-12-19

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: December 2018. GDP growth strong. UE at 3.7%. PCE at 2.0% but core slipped to 1.8%. Significant market turmoil: equities declined significantly, Treasury yields fell considerably, VIX increased, corporate spreads widened to near middle of historical distribution. Dollar appreciated modestly. Trade war concerns escalating. Fed signals shift toward more gradual path. Oil prices declined sharply. Yield curve flattening (10-2 spread near 20th percentile since 1971). Fourth hike of 2018 at this meeting.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "2 percent" over 12 months ending October. Core 1.8%. CPI 2.2%, core CPI 2.2%. AHE 3.1%. Compensation per hour 2.2% (volatile). Staff expects "both total and core PCE price inflation to be just a touch below 2 percent in 2018." Core to "move up to 2 percent in 2019." Score ~49.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "growth was strong." Staff projects growth "at a rate above the staff's estimate of potential output growth" through 2020 then "slow to a pace below it in 2021." UE to "decline further below" natural rate and "bottom out by 2020." Score ~57.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 3.7%. Payrolls "expanded further" with "strong" recent gains. Unemployment for minorities "below their levels at the end of the previous economic expansion." Part-time "still close to the lows reached in late 2007." AHE 3.1%. Job openings/quits "still at high levels." Claims "still close to historically low levels." Score ~65.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Household spending "continued to increase at a strong pace." PCE "brisk" in October. Retail sales "rose considerably" in November. Equipment spending "picking up solidly." But residential investment "appeared to be declining further." Nonresidential structures "declined modestly." Score ~52.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Investors' perceptions of downside risks to the domestic and global outlook appeared to increase." Treasury yields "declined notably." Equities "declined notably." "Financing conditions for businesses and households tightened a bit but generally remained supportive of economic growth." Score ~52.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "fell considerably" with declines "most pronounced in longer-dated maturities." 10-2 spread "near the 20th percentile of its distribution since 1971." TIPS "decreased notably" along with oil prices. Score ~42.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Corporate bond spreads "widened to near the middle of their historical distribution." Speculative-grade bond issuance saw "significant step-down." Leveraged loan issuance "also slowed." CMBS spreads "widened slightly but remained near post-crisis lows." Score ~50.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities declined significantly ("significant drop"). VIX "increased." "Declines were largest in the technology and retail sectors." Concerns about "sustainability of corporate earnings growth." Score ~58.
- BANKING_STRESS: Not explicitly discussed in stability section here. Financing "remained accommodative, on net." But corporate spreads widened notably. Earnings projections revised down. Score ~38.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated modestly." Declines in US yields weighed but "deteriorating global risk sentiment provided support." Pound weak on Brexit. Score ~54.
