# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2020-09-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: September 2020. GDP rebounding rapidly in Q3. UE at 8.4%. New FAIT framework adopted. Inflation recovering but still low. Markets accommodative for IG borrowers.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.0%, core 1.3%. Trimmed mean 1.8%. Monthly readings bolstered by durable goods price increases. "Inflation was still projected to be subdued this year, reflecting substantial slack." Score: 28.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "U.S. real GDP was rebounding at a rapid rate in the third quarter." But "employment was still below its level at the beginning of the year." Recovery far from complete. Staff projects GDP to "exceed potential output growth" over medium term — implying currently below potential. Score: 30.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 8.4%, down from 14.7% peak. Payrolls "retraced only about half of the jobs lost." LFPR and E-P ratio improving. ECI at 2.4% (below 2017-19 pace). Score: 30.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE "expanded strongly in July." Housing "continued to expand" near pre-pandemic levels. Business investment "beginning to recover on balance." IP expanding. But "real consumer spending remained well below the levels that prevailed at the beginning of the year" (from context). Score: 35.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Financial market sentiment improved." Equities rose. Spreads narrowed to "near their historical medians." "Financing conditions for businesses with access to capital markets and households with high credit scores remained broadly accommodative, although conditions remained tight for other borrowers." Score: 48.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were stable." CP/CD at pre-pandemic levels. "Little activity in the emergency liquidity facilities." Fed repo at zero. "Near-dated implied volatility on 10-year Treasury securities... remained near the bottom of its historical range." Excellent functioning. Score: 38.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads of investment- and speculative-grade corporate bond yields over comparable-maturity Treasury yields narrowed somewhat and remained near their historical medians." Bond issuance strong. Credit quality showing "tentative signs of stabilization." Defaults declining. Score: 46.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad stock price indexes rose." VIX roughly unchanged. No specific staff valuation commentary in this meeting's financial stability section. Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "C&I loans outstanding declined." Banks continued tightening. "Financing conditions for small businesses remained tight." Small business delinquencies comparable to early 2008. Credit quality stabilizing but still stressed. Score: 52.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index declined moderately." Euro at highest since 2018. Dollar weakening. Score: 38.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Surveys "moved up a bit but remained within their ranges." TIPS compensation "continued to increase, likely reflecting the general improvement in investor sentiment." 5yr and 5-10yr "close to their pre-pandemic levels." Score: 48.
