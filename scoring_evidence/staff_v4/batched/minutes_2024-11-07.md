# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2024-11-07

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: November 2024. 25bp cut to 4.50-4.75%. GDP expanded solidly. UE 4.1% (edged down from Aug). PCE 2.1%, core 2.7%. AHE 4.0% (down 0.3pp from year-earlier). ECI 3.6% (down 0.7pp). Payrolls "held down noticeably by strikes and hurricanes." Treasury yields "increased significantly" (higher term premiums). Equities "increased notably." Spreads narrowed. VIX rose then retraced. Dollar "increased significantly." Asset valuations "elevated." Financial vulnerabilities "notable."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 2.1%, core 2.7%. AHE 4.0% (down 0.3pp YoY). ECI 3.6% (down 0.7pp). "Well below its year-earlier pace." Staff: inflation "expected to remain at about the same rate as in 2024" in 2025 due to trade policy assumptions. Score: 47.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "posted a solid gain in the third quarter that was similar to its second-quarter pace." PDFP "rose slightly faster than real GDP." Staff revised up potential output growth (productivity revisions). Growth "slightly slower than potential" in 2025-2027, "very small increase in the unemployment rate." Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.1%. Payrolls affected by strikes/hurricanes. LFPR and E-P "ticked down." Vacancies/UE 1.1, "slightly lower than pre-pandemic." AHE 4.0%, ECI 3.6%, both declining. Score: 47.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: GDP solid, similar to Q2. PDFP rose faster than GDP. Score: 50.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "increased notably." Treasury yields "increased significantly." Spreads "narrowed." VIX rose on election uncertainty then retraced to "moderately lower and slightly below the median." Dollar "increased significantly." Borrowing costs "rose over the intermeeting period from already elevated levels." Score: 48.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained generally stable." Sept rate cut "fully passing through." ON RRP "declined significantly." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate and municipal spreads "narrowed." But CRE CMBS delinquency "rising further." Small business delinquencies "continued to increase." Credit card delinquencies "increased a bit further." Score: 42.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "increased notably." VIX below median. Staff: "asset valuation pressures were elevated, with estimates of risk premiums across key markets low by historical standards." House prices "elevated relative to fundamentals." CRE "fundamentals continued to deteriorate across multiple categories." Score: 35.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I standards "basically unchanged." CRE standards "basically unchanged" for first time since early 2022. Banks "continued to hold large quantities of long-duration assets, leaving them more exposed than usual." Hedge fund leverage "remained high." Life insurers leverage "somewhat elevated." Funding risks "notable." But regulatory capital "remained high." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Broad dollar "increased significantly, as U.S. interest rates rose more than foreign rates." Score: 58.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based longer-term "little changed." Inflation compensation "moved up." "Continued to suggest an expectation that inflation would return over time to the Committee's 2 percent objective." Score: 48.
