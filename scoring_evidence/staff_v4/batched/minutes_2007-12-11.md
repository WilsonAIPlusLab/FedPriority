**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format. Scoring only staff-attributable content.

**Era context**: December 2007. Financial conditions worsening again after brief improvement. Consumer spending stalling. Housing correction deepening. Staff revises down GDP forecast significantly. 25bp cut to 4.25%.

**Dimensional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Core inflation also rose but remained moderate." "On a twelve-month-change basis, core consumer price inflation was down noticeably from a year earlier." But "recent readings on inflation generally were seen as slightly less favorable than in earlier months, partly due to upward revisions." Energy prices surging. Direction: near neutral, edging up from recent lows. Score ~50.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Staff: "real GDP was anticipated to increase at a rate noticeably below its potential in 2008." "Most available indicators of activity in the fourth quarter were more downbeat." 3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 4.7%. "Slowing in private employment gains." "Employment in residential construction posted its fourth month of sizable declines." But "unemployment rate held steady." Direction: near equilibrium, softening. Score ~47.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real consumer spending was nearly flat in September and October." "Real spending on equipment and software posted a solid increase in the third quarter" but "orders and shipments of nondefense capital goods excluding aircraft declined" in October. Housing deepening contraction. Direction: below trend. Score ~35.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not discussed. N/A.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "After showing some signs of improvement in late September and October, conditions in financial markets worsened over the intermeeting period." "Spreads over risk-free rates rose to levels that were, in some cases, higher than those seen in August." "Both investment- and speculative-grade credit spreads widened considerably." "Subprime mortgage markets stayed virtually shut." Direction: tight → must be > 50. Score ~72.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Yields declining from flight to quality. No specific dysfunction described beyond yield moves. Score ~50.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Both investment- and speculative-grade credit spreads widened considerably." "Spreads on jumbo loans apparently widened further." "Spreads on conforming mortgage products also widened." Direction: widely widened → must be > 50. Score ~68.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad-based equity indexes were volatile and ended the period down noticeably. Financial stocks were especially hard hit, dropping substantially more than the broad indexes." Direction: depressed. Score ~62.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: "Sizable losses at large financial institutions." Banks experiencing "unanticipated growth in loans" from illiquid leveraged loan and ABCP markets. "Balance sheet pressures." "Concerns about rating-agency or regulatory capital requirements." "Diminished liquidity in interbank markets." Direction: stressed → must be > 50. Score ~65.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Trade-weighted foreign exchange value of the dollar against major currencies moved up, on balance." Direction: strengthening → must be > 50. Score ~55.
