# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2017-11-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: November 2017. GDP expanded at solid pace in Q3 despite hurricanes. UE declined to 4.2%. Inflation still below 2% and lower than early in year. Core especially soft at 1.25%. Equities rose notably on tax reform expectations. VIX near historical lows. Dollar appreciated nearly 3%. Balance sheet normalization begun. December hike probability ~85%.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "a bit more than 1.5 percent" over 12 months ending September. Core "about 1.25 percent." CPI 2.25%, core CPI 1.75%. AHE "almost 3 percent" (but hurricane-distorted). ECI 2.5%. Core PCE notably weak. Projected to "reach the Committee's 2 percent objective in 2019." Score ~36.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "expanded at a solid pace in the third quarter despite hurricane-related disruptions." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output through 2019." UE to "continue running below" natural rate. Score ~52.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.2%. Payrolls declined in September (hurricane) but underlying still strong. AHE "almost 3 percent." ECI 2.5%. LFPR rose. Unemployment rates for each group "close to the levels seen just before the most recent recession." Consumer sentiment "highest level since before the most recent recession." Score ~56.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "growth slowed in the third quarter, likely reflecting in part temporary effects of the hurricanes." Business equipment "continued to rise at a brisk pace." Residential investment "declined further." But fundamentals supportive. Score ~48.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Equities "rose notably." Corporate bond spreads "narrowed modestly." Treasury yields "increased modestly." "Domestic financing conditions generally remained accommodative." Tax reform boosting sentiment. Score ~32.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "shifted up and flattened somewhat." Yields increased modestly. Option-implied volatility "near historically low levels." No dysfunction. Balance sheet normalization had "little effect." Score ~44.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads on both IG and speculative-grade "narrowed modestly." CMBS spreads "near the lower end of the range seen since the financial crisis." Score ~35.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "rose notably." VIX "near historically low levels." Tax reform expectations boosting prices. Asset valuation pressures "increased slightly" and "remained elevated." Score ~33.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate." Asset valuation "elevated." Leverage and transformation "low." Nonfinancial sector leverage "moderate." Foreign vulnerabilities "moderate." Score ~33.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "rose nearly 3 percent." Canadian dollar and euro depreciated. EME currencies "generally depreciated as well." Score ~60.
