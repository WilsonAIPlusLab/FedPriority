# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2020-03-15

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: March 2020. Emergency 100bp cut to 0-0.25% (after 50bp cut on Mar 3). COVID-19 pandemic onset. Pre-outbreak data: labor market strong through Feb, UE 3.5%, PCE 1.7%, core 1.6%. Financial markets in extreme stress — equities "plummeted," VIX "sometimes reaching levels not seen since the fall of 2008." Treasury market "severely impaired." Short-term funding "signs of stress." Corporate bond issuance "came to a near standstill." Dollar "strengthened notably." Staff provided two scenarios — recession possible.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.7%, core 1.6%. Below target. AHE 3.0%, ECI 2.7%. But "inflation was projected to weaken" under both scenarios — "deterioration in resource utilization and sizable expected declines in consumer energy prices." Score: 38.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Real GDP was forecast to decline and the unemployment rate to rise, on net, in the first half of this year." Pre-outbreak: GDP "appeared to have been increasing at a moderate pace." Staff provided two scenarios — one with rebound in H2, one with recession. Score: 38.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Through Feb: UE 3.5%, payrolls "expanded strongly." But "initial claims for unemployment insurance benefits skyrocketed in the second half of March." Massive deterioration expected. Score: 40.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Pre-outbreak: PCE "moderate." Business investment "increased solidly in January." But "consumer sentiment...started to decline notably in early March." Spending shutdowns underway. Score: 35.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "plummeted." VIX "soared, sometimes reaching levels not seen since the fall of 2008." Corporate spreads "widened significantly." Treasury yields "plummeted across the maturity spectrum" — 10y and 30y at "all-time lows." Massive flight to safety. Score: 80.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Trading conditions for Treasury securities and MBS were impaired." "Treasury market functioning was severely impaired late in the intermeeting period." "Market depth was extremely thin, and bid-ask spreads widened sharply." "Normal linkage between cash and futures markets broken." Short-term funding "showed signs of stress." EFFR exceeded target range. Repo rates "elevated." Score: 85.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Corporate bond spreads over comparable-maturity Treasury yields widened significantly." Speculative-grade energy bonds "widened especially sharply." "Speculative-grade issuance and leveraged loan issuance virtually stopped after late February." "Expected default rate increased in March." Score: 75.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad stock price indexes plummeted." "Extremely volatile." VIX at 2008 levels. Score: 55.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Banking sector...was resilient coming into this period." But "some notable vulnerabilities" exacerbated. Institutional prime MMFs "experienced heavy redemptions." "Credit conditions for nonfinancial businesses and for households...pointed to tightening." Score: 60.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index strengthened notably...predominantly against EME currencies." "Strong demand for dollars amid flight to safety globally." Brazilian real and Mexican peso "depreciated sharply." Score: 60.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based "little changed, on balance." TIPS "fell sharply and reached a historical low at the 5-to-10-year horizon." Score: 40.
