# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2022-03-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: March 2022. First rate hike (25bp). GDP increasing in Q1 at slower pace. UE 3.8%. PCE 6.1%, core 5.2%. Trimmed mean 3.5%. CPI 7.9%. AHE 5.1%. Russia invaded Ukraine — commodity prices surging, financial markets volatile, liquidity strained in some markets. Staff projects PCE at 4% for 2022. GDP "well above potential."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 6.1%, core 5.2%. Trimmed mean 3.5%. CPI 7.9%, core 6.4%. AHE 5.1%. "Persistently high and broad-based levels of domestic inflation, import price inflation, and wage growth." Ukraine pushing up energy/food further. Staff common expectations index "close to its 2014 average." Score: 75.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increasing in the first quarter at a pace that was slower than the rapid gain posted in the fourth quarter." But "level of real GDP was expected to remain well above potential...labor market conditions were expected to remain very tight." Above potential. Score: 55.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.8%. Payrolls "grew strongly." LFPR and E-P increased. Job openings elevated. Quits elevated. AHE 5.1%. Score: 60.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "rising at a faster pace in the first quarter." Housing demand "remained strong" but constrained. BFI "picking up." Motor vehicle production "declined sharply." Manufacturing bottlenecks "showed little sign of improvement." Score: 47.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Financing conditions remained accommodative, although borrowing costs increased further." Markets "highly volatile." "Strained liquidity in some markets." Corporate spreads "widened noticeably...close to the medians of their historical distributions." Muni spreads "widened significantly." Score: 52.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets were mostly stable" but "spreads in some segments widened." CP longer-tenor spreads increased. "Liquidity conditions became strained in some financial markets" — market depth deteriorated in Treasuries, equities, crude oil. "Strained liquidity at times amplified the volatility." Score: 48.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "widened noticeably...close to the medians of their historical distributions." Muni spreads "widened significantly." But defaults "remained very low." Upgrades outpaced downgrades. Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "declined modestly." VIX "surged briefly" after invasion then settled. "Valuations of many assets remained elevated." CRE "somewhat elevated." House prices "risen rapidly." Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks easing standards, stronger demand. Credit quality "remained strong." C&I and CRE loans growing. Small business loan originations "roughly matched pre-pandemic levels." Banks "well capitalized with strong liquidity." NBFI leverage "elevated." Score: 40.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Moderate increase in the broad dollar index." Dollar appreciating on safe-haven flows. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "close to its 2014 average." Near-term TIPS rose sharply (Ukraine/energy). "Possibility that continued high inflation would cause longer-term inflation expectations to become unanchored was seen as another upside risk." Score: 55.
