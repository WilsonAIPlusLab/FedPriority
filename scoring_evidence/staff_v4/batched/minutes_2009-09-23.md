# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2009-09-23

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: September 2009 — early recovery. GDP growth resuming. UE at 9.7%. Housing gradual recovery. Cash-for-clunkers boosted autos. Financial conditions improving.

### Key assessments:

- INFLATION: "Core consumer price inflation remained subdued." Staff: "core inflation to slow somewhat further over the next two years." Labor costs "decelerated markedly."
- OUTPUT: "Economic activity was beginning to pick up." Factory utilization "only modestly above its recent historical low." UE projected ~9.25% by end-2010.
- LABOR: UE 9.7%, rose in August. Payrolls still declining. "Labor markets still weak."
- SPENDING: Consumer spending "increased modestly." Housing "gradual recovery." E&S "appeared to be stabilizing."
- FINANCIAL: Libor-OIS "returned to near pre-crisis levels." But bank credit "contracted further." Loan spreads "rose noticeably."
- TREASURY: "Functioning showed no material change...continued to be somewhat impaired."
- CREDIT SPREADS: Corporate yields fell more than Treasuries. Leveraged loans improving.
- EQUITY: Risk premium "remained high." Vol at "level comparable with previous recessions."
- BANKING: Mixed — large banks up, regional/smaller banks concerned. Regional failures increasing.
- DOLLAR: "Depreciated against major foreign currencies, notably against the euro and Japanese yen."

### Bright-line verification: All pass.
