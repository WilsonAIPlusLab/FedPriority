# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2012-09-13

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: September 2012. QE3 launched at this meeting. UE 8.1%. Economy moderate but slow employment growth. European concerns easing somewhat (ECB OMT plan). Financial conditions improving on policy expectations. Staff sees downside risks.

**Directional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Consumer price inflation stayed subdued." Core "also were flat in July." "increases in nominal wages remained modest." Staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014." Below target.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace." "significant margin of slack in labor and product markets only slowly." "unemployment rate was anticipated to still be elevated at the end of 2014." Below potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 8.1%. "Employment rose slowly." "Indicators of job openings and firms' hiring plans were little changed." "long-duration unemployment continued to be high." Weak.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Spending "increased in July" after being flat. E&S "appeared to be decelerating." Housing "continued to improve." Sentiment "remained more downbeat." Mixed, below trend.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: "small gains in productivity" — brief.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Sentiment in financial markets improved somewhat." Concerns about Europe eased. But CRE "still somewhat strained." Mortgage market tight. QE3 expectations supportive.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Yields "rose notably" on net (some unwinding of safe-haven). Normal functioning.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "spread of yields on corporate bonds over those on comparable-maturity Treasury securities narrowed." Investment-grade "near-record low levels." Very favorable.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad price indexes for U.S. equities rose moderately." Option-implied volatility "fell in early August to levels not seen since the middle of 2007." Favorable.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Bank CDS "continued to move down." C&I loans growing briskly. Bank credit expanding "at a moderate pace." Standards easing. Improving.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "declined." Depreciation.

**Bright-line checks**: All satisfied.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation stayed subdued"; core "flat in July"; "increases in nominal wages remained modest"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014"
- Direction: Below neutral — flat prices with subdued wages.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 34 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace"; "significant margin of slack"; "unemployment rate was anticipated to still be elevated at the end of 2014"
- Direction: Below neutral — wide persistent slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 27 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "8.1 percent"; "Employment rose slowly"; "long-duration unemployment continued to be high"; "labor force participation rate edged down further"
- Direction: Well below neutral — still very weak despite slight UE decline.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 38 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Spending "increased in July" after being flat; E&S "appeared to be decelerating"; forward indicators "pointed toward only muted increases"; housing "continued to improve"; sentiment "remained more downbeat"
- Direction: Below trend — mixed with decelerating investment.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "small gains in productivity"
- Direction: Near neutral.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 50 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Sentiment in financial markets improved somewhat"; European concerns eased; "Valuations for broad asset classes did not appear stretched"; but CRE "still somewhat strained"; mortgage "tight underwriting conditions"
- Direction: Neutral — improving sentiment balanced by residual tightness.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Yields rose on net; normal functioning; no dysfunction.
- Direction: Below neutral — orderly markets.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Spreads "narrowed"; investment-grade "near-record low levels"; speculative-grade "edged down"; debt issuance "strong"; C&I loans "rose further"
- Direction: Below neutral — very favorable credit conditions.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad price indexes for U.S. equities rose moderately"; option-implied volatility "fell...to levels not seen since the middle of 2007"; "Valuations for broad asset classes did not appear stretched"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — rising with low volatility, not stretched.

**BANKING_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS "continued to move down"; bank credit "moderate pace"; C&I loans "brisk"; standards "eased somewhat"; CRE "still somewhat strained"
- Direction: Near neutral — significant improvement, residual CRE weakness.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "declined"; safe-haven demands "eased"
- Direction: Below neutral — depreciation as risk appetite improved.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods flat; energy declining.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 32 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not separately detailed; overall core subdued.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations in the survey edged up but remained within the narrow range"
