# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1997-11-12

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. PRELL (domestic), MR. TRUMAN (international/Asia). November 1997. Asian crisis intensifying. Staff assumes 75bp tightening by mid-1998 plus 20% stock decline.

**Era context**: November 1997. Fed at 5.50%. Asian crisis intensifying — Hong Kong under pressure, Korea deepening. Prell: economy will "continue to power ahead — perhaps not indefinitely, but for long enough to exacerbate inflationary pressures"; housing demand "positive signals"; capital goods manufacturers report "heavy" orders; commercial property prices rising. Staff assumes 75bp rate hikes + ~20% stock decline to slow economy. "Price-earnings multiples incorporate unrealistic expectations." Inflation "accelerating" but "even more gradual" than prior forecasts due to dollar/productivity revisions. Truman: Asia $50B current account adjustment needed; "ripple not a wave" for US; dollar weaker against yen, stronger vs European currencies.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: "prices accelerating" but "even more gradual than in previous forecasts"; "favorable" news on wages; inflation expectations low. Score ~44.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Prell: economy will "continue to power ahead"; above potential; unemployment traversing toward 4.6%. Score ~58.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Prell: "sharp increase in average hourly earnings"; "continued low unemployment"; "tighter" labor market; but "decline in price inflation" damping wage increases. Score ~64.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: housing "positive signals"; capital goods "heavy" orders; investment "very strong going into 1998"; NFIB survey "extremely upbeat." Score ~58.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Prell: "reassessment of the trend in labor productivity" upward; "actual productivity growth has not changed much" but trend raised. Score ~56.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Prell: "recent upheavals have produced some widening of risk premia" but "most projects can get funding on what evidently are regarded as attractive terms." Widening from crisis but still accommodative. Score ~42.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: "current price-earnings multiples incorporate unrealistic expectations"; "large equity premiums of the past will not be restored"; staff predicts ~20% decline from Monday's close. Score ~18.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Prell: "widening of risk premia in the securities markets." Widening from Asia. Score ~55.

9. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Truman: dollar weaker vs yen but stronger vs European currencies. Net: mixed. Score ~55.
