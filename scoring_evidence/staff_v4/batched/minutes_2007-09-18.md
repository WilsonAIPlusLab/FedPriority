# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2007-09-18

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format. Scoring only staff-attributable content.

**Era context**: September 2007. Full financial crisis mode. Interbank markets impaired, ABCP market seizing, subprime markets illiquid. Fed cuts 50bp to 4.75%. Economy still growing but staff marks down forecasts.

**Dimensional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Core PCE prices rose 0.2 percent in June and 0.1 percent in July." "On a twelve-month-change basis, core PCE inflation in July was below the comparable rate twelve months earlier." Expectations declining. Direction: near neutral, slightly below. Score ~48.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Staff "continued to estimate that real GDP increased at a moderate rate in the third quarter." But "marked down the fourth-quarter forecast" and "trimmed its forecast of real GDP growth in 2008." Growth "projected to firm in 2009 to a pace a bit above the rate of growth of its potential." Direction: below potential → must be < 50. Score ~42.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 4.6%. "Private payroll employment rose only modestly in August, and the levels of employment in June and July were revised down." "Trend growth in jobs had fallen off." But "labor markets across the country generally remained fairly tight." Direction: still above neutral but softening. Score ~50.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Consumer spending appeared to have strengthened early in the summer." But "housing sector remained exceptionally weak." Business investment solid. Direction: mixed, slightly below trend. Score ~43.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not prominently discussed. N/A.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Short-term financial markets came under pressure." "Asset-backed commercial paper outstanding contracted substantially." "Term interbank funding markets were significantly impaired." "Pressures eased a bit in mid-September, but short-term financial markets remained strained." Investment-grade credit still flowing. Direction: tight → must be > 50. Score ~68.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: "Investors sought the safety and liquidity of Treasury securities, and yields on Treasury bills dropped sharply for a period; trading conditions in the bill market were impaired at times." Actual dysfunction. Direction: above neutral. Score ~58.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Investment- and speculative-grade corporate bond spreads edged up; they were near their highest levels in four years." "Issuance of speculative-grade bonds was scant." "Leveraged loans slated to finance leveraged buyouts continued to be delayed or restructured." Direction: widened → must be > 50. Score ~62.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad stock price indexes were volatile but about unchanged, on net." Direction: near neutral. Score ~52.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: "Banks took measures to conserve their liquidity and were cautious about counterparties' exposures." "Banks might need to take a large volume of assets onto their balance sheets." "Banks' concerns about the implications of rapid growth in their balance sheets for their capital ratios." Direction: stressed → must be > 50. Score ~60.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Foreign exchange value of the dollar... fell, on balance." Direction: weakening → must be < 50. Score ~38.

- Center-of-gravity: Core inflation improving, near or slightly below target.
- Direction: Slightly below neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 42 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Staff "marked down the fourth-quarter forecast" due to "financial turbulence." Expected growth "below its potential" through 2008.
- Center-of-gravity: Below potential with downside risks increasing.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE at 4.6%. "Private payroll employment rose only modestly in August." "Labor markets across the country generally remained fairly tight."
- Center-of-gravity: At or near equilibrium — tight but softening rapidly.
- Direction: At neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 43 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Consumer spending strengthened in summer but "housing sector remained exceptionally weak." Business investment solid but expected to slow.
- Center-of-gravity: Below trend, housing drag intensifying.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: N/A**

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 68 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Term interbank funding markets were significantly impaired." "Asset-backed commercial paper outstanding contracted substantially." But investment-grade credit still flowing.
- Center-of-gravity: Tight conditions — interbank and structured finance impaired, investment-grade less affected.
- Direction: Above neutral — tight.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 58 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Trading conditions in the bill market were impaired at times." "Yields on Treasury bills dropped sharply" from flight to quality.
- Center-of-gravity: Some actual market dysfunction in bill market.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 62 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Corporate bond spreads edged up; they were near their highest levels in four years." "Issuance of speculative-grade bonds was scant." "Markets for nonconforming mortgages were impaired."
- Center-of-gravity: Significantly wide, especially high-yield and mortgage.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 52 ±8 [Low confidence]**

- Direction: Near neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 60 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Banks took measures to conserve their liquidity." "Banks' concerns about the implications of rapid growth in their balance sheets for their capital ratios." Subprime and ABCP exposures.
- Center-of-gravity: Significant emerging stress — balance sheet and liquidity concerns.
- Direction: Above neutral — stressed.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 38 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**

- Center-of-gravity: Notable depreciation.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 42 ±8 [Moderate confidence]**

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 52 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
