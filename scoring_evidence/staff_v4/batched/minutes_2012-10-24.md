# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2012-10-24

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: October 2012. QE3 underway (MBS purchases). UE dropped to 7.8%. Financial conditions eased after September FOMC. European situation somewhat calmer. Fiscal cliff concerns emerging. Hurricane Sandy about to hit. Staff revised up near-term and medium-term forecasts.

**Directional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Consumer price inflation picked up, reflecting higher consumer energy costs." But core "remained subdued." "increases in nominal wages stayed relatively modest." Staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014." Below target.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace." Staff revised up growth projection. "Progress in reducing unemployment over the projection period was expected to be relatively slow." Below potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 7.8%. "Private nonfarm employment expanded modestly." "long-duration unemployment...remained high." "Other indicators...did not show decided improvement." Still weak.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "rose at a solid pace." Motor vehicle sales "expanded." E&S "appeared to edge down." Housing "improved more generally." Below trend overall.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not specifically discussed.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "financial conditions generally eased appreciably" post-September FOMC. But "toward the end of the period investor sentiment deteriorated somewhat." CRE "remained weak." Mortgage refinancing increased.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Yields "up slightly" for short/medium; "about unchanged" for long. Normal.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Yields on investment-grade corporate bonds reached a record low level, and their spreads...narrowed." Speculative-grade also "decreased." Very favorable.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad equity price indexes were a little lower." Option-implied volatility "increased, on net." Slight decline.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: CDS "declined." C&I loans growing. Standards easing. Reports of stronger demand. But CRE "remained weak." Improving.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "little changed, on net." Flat.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Headline "picked up" from energy; core "remained subdued"; "increases in nominal wages stayed relatively modest"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014"
- Direction: Below neutral — subdued core despite energy uptick.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate pace"; staff revised up projections; "Progress in reducing unemployment...expected to be relatively slow"; "significant margin of slack"
- Direction: Below neutral — slack narrowing slowly.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "fell to 7.8 percent"; "labor force participation rate rose slightly"; but "long-duration unemployment...remained high"; "Other indicators...did not show decided improvement"
- Direction: Below neutral — notable UE improvement but still elevated.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose at a solid pace"; motor vehicle sales expanding; but E&S "appeared to edge down"; forward indicators "pointed toward roughly flat real expenditures"; housing "improved more generally"
- Direction: Below trend — solid consumption but weak investment.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 50 ±10 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not discussed.
- Direction: Neutral.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "financial conditions generally eased appreciably early in the intermeeting period"; QE3 effect; but "toward the end of the period investor sentiment deteriorated"; CRE "remained weak"
- Direction: Near neutral — eased significantly from QE3 but some late-period reversal.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Yields slightly higher to unchanged; normal functioning.
- Direction: Below neutral — orderly.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Investment-grade "reached a record low level"; spreads "narrowed"; speculative-grade also "decreased"; bond issuance "picked up significantly"
- Direction: Below neutral — record favorable conditions.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 48 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad equity price indexes were a little lower"; option-implied volatility "increased, on net"; concerns about corporate profitability
- Direction: Near neutral — slight decline.

**BANKING_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS "declined in recent months"; lending standards "continued to report having eased"; "stronger demand...relatively widespread"; but CRE "remained weak amid elevated vacancy and delinquency rates"
- Direction: Near neutral — significant healing, CRE residual weakness.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "little changed, on net"
- Direction: Neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods "subdued"; energy boost temporary.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 33 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not separately detailed; overall core subdued.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations in the survey moved down to near the lower end of the narrow range"
