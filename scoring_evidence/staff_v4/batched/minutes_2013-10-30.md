# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-10-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: October 2013. Government shutdown (Oct 1-16). Debt ceiling scare. UE 7.2%. Inflation modest ~1.5%. Rates declined 30bp+ as taper expectations pushed out. Equity prices rose. EME outflows stabilizing. Data delayed by shutdown. Staff revised down near-term (shutdown) but up medium-term (easier financial conditions).

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation continued to be modest"; core "increased modestly"; "Nominal average hourly earnings...increased slowly"; staff: "inflation was projected to run somewhat below the FOMC's longer-run inflation objective of 2 percent through 2016"

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to rise at a moderate pace"; shutdown "revised down somewhat" near-term; but medium-term "revised up slightly"; "slowly reduce resource slack"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 32 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "edged down to 7.2 percent"; employment rose but "at a slower pace"; "share of workers employed part time for economic reasons was...elevated"; "rate of gross private-sector hiring remained flat"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 41 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose moderately in August"; retail sales "increased significantly" in September; but E&S "appeared to be tepid"; housing "appeared to continue" recovering; "consumer sentiment declined"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 50 ±10 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not discussed.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term interest rates declined" 30bp+; equity prices rose; "expectations for more-accommodative monetary policy"; but Treasury bill market was "particularly affected" by debt ceiling; short-term markets "strained" temporarily

**TREASURY_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "yields on bills maturing between mid-October and early November rose sharply"; "bill auctions saw reduced demand"; repo market "strained"; but "effects eased quickly" after debt deal. Temporary dysfunction.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term Treasury yields" decline reflected in corporate/MBS; "access to credit generally remained ample for large firms"; C&I and CRE loans expanding; lending standards easing.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "equity prices rose" significantly; response to September FOMC "more accommodative than expected"

**BANKING_STRESS: 43 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Third-quarter earnings reports for large banks generally met or exceeded...expectations"; CDS stable; lending standards easing. But bank credit "declined slightly."

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 45 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "the dollar depreciated against most other currencies"; "Mutual fund flows to emerging markets stabilized"

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods modest.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core services modest.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near- and longer-term inflation expectations...were little changed"
