# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2003-09-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff: Stockton (economic outlook), Johnson (international). Era: September 2003. Economy accelerating — GDP growth revised UP to 4.5% H2. E&S spending up 8% and rising. But labor market still very weak — payrolls declining, claims above 400,000. Structural productivity revised up further to 2.5-2.75%. Inflation expected to fall to ~1%. Fed funds held at 1%.

**Key signals**: Stockton: "an acceleration of activity is under way" — "revised up projected growth in this year from 4 percent to 4½ percent." E&S spending "up about 8 percent at an annual rate" with Q3 "even stronger 16 percent gain." But "Private payrolls declined again in August, and initial claims for unemployment insurance have edged back up over 400,000." "Jobless recovery" continues. Structural productivity raised to 2.5-2.75%. "Inflation over the next two years as more likely to be down than up." Core PCE expected at ~1% in 2004-05.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 22 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "pressures on inflation over the next two years as more likely to be down than up." Core PCE expected at "about 1 percent in 2004 and 2005." "15 percent chance that we'll get headline CPI at or below zero in 2004."
- **Direction**: Below neutral — very subdued, deflation still a risk.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: GDP growth accelerating to 4.5%. But "slightly higher output gap than in our August forecast, a gap that is not expected to be eliminated by the end of 2005." Below potential but closing.
- **Direction**: Below neutral. Bright-line satisfied.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 25 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "Private payrolls declined again in August." Initial claims "back up over 400,000." "The biggest surprise... has been the weakness of the labor market." Unemployment ~6.1%.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — significant slack, jobless recovery.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 50 ±6 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: E&S spending "up about 8 percent at an annual rate" accelerating to 16%. "Increase in real GDP that we experienced over the first half has been more than accounted for by a rise in final sales." Consumer spending supported by tax cuts.
- **Direction**: At neutral — spending accelerating to trend-like levels.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 73 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "productivity in the nonfarm business sector is estimated to have risen more than 4 percent." Structural raised to "2½ and 2¾ percent per annum." Historical revisions "pointed to larger increases in output per hour."
- **Direction**: Well above neutral — exceptional structural and cyclical readings.

### FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 38 ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "Low interest rates," "partial-expensing provision," "corporate profitability has provided a lift to equity prices." Fed funds at 1%. "Risk spreads have come in." Conditions improving significantly.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — accommodative/supportive. Bright-line: accommodative → < 50.

### TREASURY_STRESS: N/A
### CREDIT_SPREADS: 40 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Improved corporate earnings, risk spreads narrowing. Credit conditions improving.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — favorable.

### EQUITY_VALUATION: 42 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Heightened corporate profitability has provided a lift to equity prices." Market rally continues. Not described as overvalued or distressed.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — recovering, no particular concern.

### BANKING_STRESS: N/A
### DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 25 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "relatively elevated level of the dollar" still restraining. Dollar declining but still above long-run equilibrium.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — depreciating.

### INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 43 ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "inflation expectations are moving down" — "little evidence... dramatically one way or the other." "A little further decline in inflation expectations."
- **Direction**: Below neutral — expectations edging lower.
