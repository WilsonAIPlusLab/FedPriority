# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2001-10-02 (V4)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff speakers: Stockton (economic outlook), Kos (Desk operations post-9/11), Reinhart (brief).

**Era context**: October 2, 2001 — three weeks after 9/11 attacks. Economy in recession, now compounded by terrorist attacks. Fed cut 50bp on 9/17. Massive market disruptions post-9/11. Manufacturing declining. Consumer confidence plunging. Auto production cut. Air travel shut down. Initial claims surging. Staff projects GDP "at best, eking out a meager gain" before attacks, now much worse. Not yet forecasting recession officially but close.

**Dimensional analysis**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Not focus. Economy in contraction, energy prices falling. Inflation pressures dissipating rapidly. Score ~38.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Stockton: Pre-9/11, GDP was "at best, eking out a meager gain." Post-9/11, "1/2 percentage point" direct disruption to Q3 GDP. "Confluence of factors combining to produce a period of very subdued growth and rising unemployment over the next several quarters." Economy clearly below potential. Score ~32.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Stockton: "steep rise in initial claims for unemployment insurance." "Rising unemployment" projected. Auto layoffs, manufacturing job losses. Score ~38.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Stockton: "consumers are likely to pull back on spending." "Plunge in consumer sentiment in December." Capital spending declining — "high-tech recession." "Real E&S spending to decline." Auto production cuts severe. "Medium and heavy trucks to be only about one half the level of a year ago." Very weak. Score ~25.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Stockton: "Businesses may be moving more aggressively on the cost-cutting side." Structural productivity still believed intact. "1 percent growth in multifactor productivity" projected. Score ~45.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Kos: Post-9/11 market disruptions. "Money market funds had trouble investing money." Fed blew up balance sheet. "Tightening of financial conditions this fall." Very tight from disruption + uncertainty. Score ~70.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Kos: Post-9/11 disruptions. "Intermediation wasn't functioning when four large government securities firms were put out of service." Fed funds market dysfunctional. Score ~68.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Stockton: "widening scope of downward revisions in expectations of corporate earnings." Credit conditions tightened significantly. Score ~65.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Stock market declined sharply post-9/11. Score ~58.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Kos: "problems for investors. Money market funds had trouble investing money; there were complaints from investors who could not get banks to take their money." Post-9/11 disruption to money markets. Score ~58.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar declined. Score ~40.

**Bright-line checks**: OUTPUT_GAP below potential → < 50. ✓. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS tight → > 50. ✓. TREASURY_STRESS: actual dysfunction → elevated. ✓.
