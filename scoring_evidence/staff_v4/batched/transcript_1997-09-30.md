# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1997-09-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speaker: MR. PRELL (extensive domestic). September 1997. Prell's famous "new era" rebuttal. Economy booming; Asian crisis deepening but staff sees limited US impact.

**Era context**: September 1997. Fed at 5.50%. Prell delivers extensive "new era" analysis: acknowledges "marvelous technological advances" but insists "supply-demand imbalances can occur and give rise to inflationary pressures." Economy "still running pretty hot"; exports "zipping along"; GDP growth "3 to 3-1/2 percent range." Staff expects moderation via "reverse accelerator" but acknowledges "boom generates a momentum of their own." Staff raised productivity assumption. Stock market: "financial exuberance" — staff predicts decline. Inflation expected to accelerate "only a couple of tenths per year." Dollar strong.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: "core inflation accelerating only a couple of tenths per year"; "lowered our inflation forecast"; compensation practices changing. Inflation benign despite tight labor. Score ~42.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Prell: "economy is still running pretty hot"; GDP 3-3.5%; "levels of some types of household and business investment expenditures are high enough that stocks might soon begin to get out of kilter." Score ~60.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Prell: "continuous sub-five-percent unemployment"; "taut" markets; "complaints about the quality of available workers"; "efforts of businesses to attract and retain workers." Score ~65.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: "massive gains in stock market wealth" driving consumption; consumers "doing so to an even greater degree than we thought." Score ~60.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Prell: "somewhat more optimistic view of productivity prospects"; "room for optimism regarding the outlook for growth in output per hour"; high-tech investments having "more significant payoff." Score ~56.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Prell: "financial exuberance"; "stock prices moving ever further above"; "bond yields ever further below." Very accommodative. Score ~30.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: "new era fever and stock prices moving ever further above"; "financial exuberance"; staff predicts needed decline. Score ~15.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Not directly discussed. Normal. Score ~45.

9. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Prell: dollar appreciation "significant factor tending to trim domestic inflation"; "not swallowing" another 20% rise. Dollar strong. Score ~65.
