### 2. Era Context

**February 2, 2000** — This is the peak of the late 1990s boom / dot-com era. Key macro backdrop:
- Unemployment around 4 percent (very low, well below NAIRU)
- Strong GDP growth — Q4 1999 BEA advance estimate came in at 5.8% real, exceeding staff's Greenbook by ~0.5 pp
- Inflation modestly rising — core PCE running around 1.5%, but staff projecting upward drift
- Fed tightening cycle underway — markets pricing ~50 bps of tightening in Q1 2000
- Yield curve inverted (10-30 year) partly due to Treasury buyback program
- S&P 500 at elevated P/E ratios; NASDAQ at very high valuations
- Y2K transition just completed; short-term money markets normalizing post-year-end

This is NOT a crisis meeting. The staff is assessing a strong, possibly overheating economy.
