# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-06-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: June 2021. GDP expanding faster than Q1. UE 5.8%. PCE 3.6% (Apr) — "transitory factors." CPI 5%. ECI jumping to 4% annualized. Supply constraints and bottlenecks driving prices. Staff expects inflation "transitory." VIX at lowest since Feb 2020. Spreads at historical lows. ON RRP at record levels.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 3.6%, core 3.1%. Trimmed mean 1.8%. CPI 5%, core 3.8%. "Largely reflecting transitory factors" — base effects + supply bottlenecks. ECI at 4% annualized (3-month). Median wage "stepped up significantly in April." Staff expects PCE "to gradually move down" but "still be well above 2 percent at the end of this year." Score: 55.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanding in the second quarter at a pace that was faster than in the first quarter." Staff projects "substantial" growth. Still closing gap — UE at 5.8%, staff expects decline "to historically low levels." Not yet fully closed. Score: 44.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 5.8%. "Retraced two-thirds of the job losses." LFPR and E-P "recovered only partially." Payroll gains slowed vs Feb-Mar pace. ECI 4% annualized. Multiple compensation measures stepping up. Score: 40.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE "increased substantially in March" then "little changed from that high level in April." Housing "robust" above pre-pandemic. Equipment/intangibles "increasing solidly." Materials shortages holding back residential investment. Nonresidential structures still weak. Score: 50.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Overall financial conditions eased." Equities edged up. Spreads narrowed. "Financing conditions for businesses and households remained accommodative, particularly for large firms and households with high credit ratings." Capital markets "highly accommodative" — "historically low corporate bond and leveraged loan yields." Score: 40.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets continued to function smoothly." Rates at historical lows. ON RRP "surged...to record levels." No emergency facility usage. Excellent functioning. Score: 33.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Spreads "narrowed a little for all credit ratings." Municipal yields "reached historical lows." "No reported corporate defaults in April, and defaults were low in May." Upgrades outpacing downgrades. Default expectations "low relative to their historical range." Score: 33.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities edged up. VIX "reached its lowest level since February 2020" — "a bit below the middle of its historical distribution." "Historically low corporate bond and leveraged loan yields as well as high price-to-earnings ratios." No explicit financial stability section in this text but valuations clearly elevated. Score: 37.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans fell (PPP forgiveness). "Tepid demand for loans." Credit quality "remained largely stable." Small business loan performance "improved." But "in certain sectors...small businesses remained stressed." CRE loan growth weak. Score: 45.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index decreased modestly." Dollar weakening. Score: 47.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common inflation expectations index "returned to the level that prevailed in 2014, a time when inflation was modest." Inflation compensation "declined a bit at shorter horizons but held steady at longer horizons." Score: 50.
