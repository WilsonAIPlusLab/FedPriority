# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2008-08-05

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format. Scoring only staff-attributable content.

**Era context**: August 2008. Fed holds at 2%. GSE crisis intensifying — Fannie/Freddie under pressure. Oil at peak. Inflation concerns at their highest. Economy weak but not yet in freefall. Pre-Lehman.

**Dimensional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Overall consumer prices rose rapidly for a third straight month in July." "Core consumer price inflation remained elevated." Oil near peak. Energy and food driving headline. Direction: clearly elevated. Score ~63.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: "Economic activity decelerated considerably in recent months." Staff "left its projection for real GDP growth in the second half of 2008 little changed" — expected slow growth below potential. Direction: below potential → must be < 50. Score ~35.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 5.7% (from September source: "unemployment rate jumped 0.4 percentage point, to 6.1 percent, in August" — at August meeting UE was around 5.7%). "Private payrolls declined." Direction: below neutral. Score ~32.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Consumer spending weakened noticeably." "Real personal consumption expenditures turned down in June and declined more noticeably in July." Housing steep decline. But business investment "generally held up through July." Direction: below trend. Score ~30.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: "Solid growth in productivity" mentioned in September recap. Direction: above trend. Score ~55.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: GSE crisis. "Tight credit conditions." "Ongoing housing contraction." "Restrictive financial conditions." "Conditions in the housing market remained quite strained." But broader credit markets not yet in freefall. Direction: tight → must be > 50. Score ~70.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Flight to quality but no specific dysfunction. Score ~48.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads elevated. "Risk spreads on corporate bonds jumped." GSE spreads very wide. Mortgage spreads at multi-decade highs. Direction: wide → must be > 50. Score ~68.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad equity indexes declined." "Financial sector equity indexes were volatile and ended the period down sharply." Direction: depressed. Score ~65.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: GSE crisis. "Increasing concerns about the health of financial institutions." "Banks tightened lending standards further." Direction: stressed → must be > 50. Score ~70.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: From September source: dollar was weak through most of the period but "on a trade-weighted basis, the dollar rose against the currencies of our major trading partners" near the end. At August meeting, dollar still weak. Score ~38.

**Bright-line checks**: All satisfied.
