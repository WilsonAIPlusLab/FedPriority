# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2015-12-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff economic briefing (MR. ROBERTS), international (MR. GRUBER). This is the liftoff meeting.

**Era context**: December 2015. Liftoff meeting — first rate hike since 2006. UE at 5.0%. Core PCE 1.3%. Dollar appreciated significantly. Oil prices falling to ~$38. Euro area, Japan at zero rates. Strong payrolls (~220K/month).

**Staff speakers**:
- MR. ROBERTS: U.S. economic outlook — labor market, GDP, inflation, consumer spending, recession risk simulations
- MR. GRUBER: International outlook

**Dimensional analysis**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Roberts: core PCE 1.3% this year. Core inflation "rising from 1.3 percent this year to 1.9 percent by 2018." Headline near zero due to energy. "Unusually low medical price increases will restrain inflation." Import and energy prices dragging. Score: 32 — well below target.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Roberts: "we expect real GDP to increase about in line with the pace of recent years" (~2-2.5%). GDP forecast "to exceed the staff's estimate of potential growth." UE "continues to fall and reaches 4½ percent by the end of 2018." Output exceeding potential growth implies gap closing. UE at 5.0%, near natural rate. Score: 48 — near potential but still slightly below.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Roberts: payrolls "increased at an average monthly pace of 220,000." UE 5.0%. Participation rate declining at trend pace. JOLTS, quits improving. ECI "rose about 2 percent." Productivity & costs compensation "up 3½ percent over the most recent four quarters." UE 5.0% ≈ near NAIRU. Score: 48 — near full employment.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Roberts: real PCE "increased at a 2½ percent annual rate" in Q4. Expects "real PCE to rise about 3 percent." Manufacturing "edged up only slightly." GDP "about a 2 percent annual rate over the second half." Consumer spending solid but manufacturing weak. Score: 48 — at trend.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Roberts: potential output expected to "rise somewhat more rapidly in the medium term." Implied sluggish productivity. "Same increase in real GDP leads to a smaller change in resource utilization." Score: 35 — below trend.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Net exports "a substantial drag on GDP growth in 2016 and 2017, reflecting the ongoing effects of the appreciation of the dollar." Fiscal policy turning slightly supportive. Household wealth high. Interest rates still very low. Score: 46 — near neutral, approaching liftoff.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Not discussed. Score: 40 — normal, approaching liftoff.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Not directly discussed but Gruber references "easing credit conditions" abroad. N/A for U.S. specifically.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Not discussed. N/A.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Not discussed. N/A.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Roberts: "ongoing effects of the appreciation of the dollar since the middle of last year." Net exports substantial drag. Gruber: major theme. Score: 66 — notable strength.

**Bright-line checks**: All satisfied. OUTPUT_GAP near 50 — UE at natural rate; DOLLAR_STRENGTH > 50 for appreciation.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 32 ±4 [High confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: core PCE "1.3 percent this year." Headline near zero. "Declines in energy and import prices have held down core inflation by about ½ percentage point." Core expected to rise to 1.9% by 2018.
**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation significantly below 2% target, dragged by energy and import prices.
**Direction confirmation**: Below neutral — well below target.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 48 ±4 [High confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: GDP ~2% growth, "forecast to exceed the staff's estimate of potential growth." UE at 5.0%, "continues to fall and reaches 4½ percent by the end of 2018." Output gap closing.
**Center-of-gravity**: Economy approximately at potential — UE at/near natural rate, growth near potential.
**Direction confirmation**: Near neutral — approximately at potential, slight remaining gap.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 48 ±4 [High confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: UE 5.0%. Payrolls averaging 220K/month. "Unemployment rate has fallen ¾ percentage point" over past year. ECI rose 2%. Compensation from P&C up 3.5%.
**Center-of-gravity**: Labor market near full employment. UE at approximately the natural rate.
**Direction confirmation**: Near neutral — at or very near NAIRU.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 48 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: PCE "increased at a 2½ percent annual rate." Expects 3% going forward. Manufacturing "edged up only slightly." GDP about 2% second half.
**Center-of-gravity**: Consumption solid; investment mixed. Net at trend.
**Direction confirmation**: At neutral — spending near trend pace.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 35 ±8 [Low confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: potential rising "somewhat more rapidly in the medium term than has been the case in the recent past." Implied current productivity sluggish. "Same increase in real GDP leads to a smaller change in resource utilization."
**Center-of-gravity**: Productivity below historical trend.
**Direction confirmation**: Below neutral.

### FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 46 ±6 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**: Roberts: dollar drag on exports. Fiscal turning supportive. Household wealth high. Interest rates still very low but liftoff imminent.
**Center-of-gravity**: Near neutral — accommodative rates offset by dollar tightening. Approaching liftoff.
**Direction confirmation**: Near neutral, slightly accommodative.

### TREASURY_STRESS: 40 ±8 [Low confidence]
**Evidence**: No specific discussion. Normal market functioning implied.
**Center-of-gravity**: Normal functioning.
