# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2018-01-31

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: January 2018. GDP expanded ~2.5% in Q4 2017. UE at 4.1%. Inflation still below 2%. Tax cuts just enacted. Equities rose substantially. Dollar declined nearly 4%. Spreads narrowed further to well below historical averages. Inflation compensation moving up. VIX increased but remained low. Asset valuation pressures "elevated." Strong global growth. Staff revised up GDP forecast for tax cuts.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "about 1.75 percent" over 12 months ending December. Core 1.5%. CPI ~2%, core CPI 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. Projected core to reach 2% in 2019. Score ~38.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "expanded at about a 2.5 percent pace in the fourth quarter." Growth of real final domestic purchases "solid." Staff projects "somewhat faster pace than potential output through 2020." Tax cuts boost. UE to "run well below" natural rate. Score ~53.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.1%. Payrolls "increased solidly." Unemployment rates for minorities "lower than earlier in the year and close to the levels seen just before the most recent recession." Part-time "close to its pre-recession level." LFPR held steady (against aging trend = further strengthening). AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. Job openings/quits high. Claims "low level." Score ~57.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Real PCE "increased strongly in the fourth quarter." Residential investment "rose briskly." Business equipment "increased at a solid pace." Nonresidential structures "rose modestly." Consumer sentiment "remained upbeat." Score ~54.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Eased considerably further." Equities "rose substantially." Spreads narrowed further. Dollar declined. "Financing conditions... remained generally accommodative... continued to be supportive of economic activity." Score ~27.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "shifted up." Both 2- and 10-year "moved up about 30 basis points." Inflation compensation "moved up." No dysfunction. Score ~48.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads on both IG and speculative-grade "declined slightly and remained well below their historical averages." CMBS spreads "near their lowest levels since the financial crisis." Score ~30.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "rose substantially." VIX "increased but remained low by historical standards." Tax legislation boosting sentiment. Asset valuation pressures "continued to be elevated." Score ~32.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation "elevated" reflecting "broad-based appetite for risk." Financial-sector leverage and transformation "low." Nonfinancial leverage "moderate." Score ~33.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "declined nearly 4 percent." Decline "most pronounced against AFE currencies" but "notably against most EME currencies as well." Score ~35.
