# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2017-12-13

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: December 2017. GDP rising at solid pace in H2 2017. UE at 4.1%. Inflation still below 2%. Tax legislation passage perceived as increasingly likely. Equities rose (tax reform). VIX near historical lows. Dollar little changed. Third hike of 2017 at this meeting. Staff revised up GDP forecast modestly for tax cuts. Balance sheet normalization proceeding smoothly.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "slightly more than 1.5 percent" over 12 months ending October. Core "nearly 1.5 percent." CPI 2.25%, core CPI 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. Compensation per hour only 1%. "Wage gains remained modest." Projected "very close to" 2% in 2019 and "at that objective in 2020." Score ~37.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "rising at a solid pace in the second half of 2017." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output through 2019." Tax cuts expected to boost growth. UE to "continue running below" natural rate. Score ~52.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.1%. Payrolls "increased strongly." Unemployment rates for each group "close to the levels seen just before the most recent recession." Job openings and quits "relatively high levels." AHE 2.5%. But compensation per hour only 1%. Claims "low level." Score ~56.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "increased modestly in October after expanding strongly in September." Vehicle sales slowed but above year-earlier. Business equipment "rising further." Residential investment "edging up" after two quarters of declines. Nonresidential structures rose. Score ~50.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Yield curve flattened. Equities "rose." VIX "little changed, on net, at levels close to historical lows." Spreads "about flat." "Financing conditions for businesses and households remained broadly supportive." Tax legislation perceived as more likely. Score ~32.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "flattened." Short yields rose, 10-year "moved up only slightly." TIPS compensation "little changed." Balance sheet normalization no effect. No dysfunction. Score ~43.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads on both IG and speculative-grade "about flat on net." CMBS spreads "near the lower end of the range seen since the financial crisis." Financing "accommodative." Score ~37.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "rose." VIX "levels close to historical lows." Tax reform boosting prices. Score ~34.
- BANKING_STRESS: Not explicitly discussed in stability section for this meeting. Financing conditions "accommodative on balance." Credit quality "solid." Score ~33.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "little changed." Euro appreciated modestly. Pound and peso volatile but ended little changed. Score ~50.
