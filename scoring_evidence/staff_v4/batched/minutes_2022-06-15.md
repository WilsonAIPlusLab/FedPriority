# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2022-06-15

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: June 2022. 75bp hike. GDP rebounding in Q2 after Q1 decline. UE 3.6%. PCE 6.3%, core 4.9%. Trimmed mean 3.8%. CPI 8.6%. AHE 5.2%. "Some signs that momentum was slowing." Equities "fell sharply." VIX elevated. Corporate spreads "widened notably." Borrowing costs increased significantly. GDP "well above potential" but gap narrowing.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 6.3%, core 4.9%. Trimmed mean 3.8%. CPI 8.6%, core 6.0%. AHE 5.2%. Staff expects 5.0% PCE in 2022, 2.4% in 2023. "Boost to inflation from supply-demand imbalances...would be more persistent." Score: 78.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "rebounding to a moderate rate of increase." GDP "expected to remain well above potential" but "gap was projected to narrow significantly this year." Above potential but narrowing. Score: 54.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6%. "Labor market remained very tight, but there were some signs that momentum was slowing." Job openings "edged lower" but "remained at a high level." AHE 5.2%. LFPR edged down. Score: 60.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Production and spending indicators were mixed but generally remained strong." PCE and IP gained in April. But retail sales declined in May, housing data moved down, consumer sentiment "decreased noticeably." "Private domestic final purchases were increasing at a slower pace." Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Financing conditions in domestic credit markets remained generally accommodative" but "borrowing costs increased in many sectors." Equities "fell sharply." VIX elevated. Corporate spreads "widened notably." Mortgage rates at highest since 2010. "Recent and prospective tightening of financial conditions led the staff to reduce its GDP growth forecast." Score: 55.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." EFFR and SOFR passed through rate hike. CP spreads narrowed. ON RRP take-up increased. No stress. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG spreads widened, HY "widened notably, on net, reaching levels comparable with those at the end of 2018." Muni spreads "narrowed substantially." Leveraged loan downgrades exceeded upgrades. Default expectations "deteriorated to some extent." Score: 55.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "fell sharply." VIX "increased moderately...elevated...significantly above average pre-pandemic levels." No explicit financial stability valuation update. Market repricing risk assets lower. Score: 52.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I and CRE loans "expanded at a rapid pace." Credit quality "remained sound." Banks provisioning for losses (reversing prior releases). Small business originations at pre-pandemic levels. Household credit quality "remained solid." Score: 42.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar edged up." Dollar appreciating. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Surveys "generally suggested that inflation was expected to remain high in the short run but then fall back toward levels consistent with a longer-run rate of 2 percent." TIPS inflation compensation "declined." Score: 55.
