# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2002-01-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff: Reifschneider/Williams (ZLB special briefing), Wilcox/Slifman/Johnson (economic outlook). Era: January 2002. Meeting featured a special zero-lower-bound briefing analyzing scenarios where funds rate hits zero. Economy still in recession aftermath. Baseline has recovery past 2003. Stock market could fall 50%. Unemployment projected to rise through 2003, not below 6% until late 2004. Deflation risk scenarios explored. Funds rate at 1.75%.

**Key signals**: Reifschneider: ZLB scenarios — stock crash could push funds rate to zero, unemployment rises "through 2003." "Downward trajectory of core CPI inflation would be more pronounced." FRB/US model analysis. Wilcox: economy beginning to recover. Fiscal stimulus (tax cuts) supporting. Productivity strong — "5¼ percent" Q4 2001, "5¾ percent" current quarter. But employment still declining. Dollar still elevated.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Core inflation moderating. ZLB scenarios highlight deflation risks. Inflation trajectory "more pronounced" downward in adverse scenarios. But not yet acute — core CPI still around 2%.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — moderating, deflation risks discussed.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Unemployment projected to rise "through 2003." Economy just emerging from recession. Significant slack. Recovery beginning but below potential.
- **Direction**: Below neutral. Bright-line satisfied.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 35 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Employment still declining. Unemployment rising. Payrolls weak. Firms cutting — "responded swiftly and aggressively" to downturn by cutting payrolls.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — significant slack.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 42 ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: ZLB briefing dominated. Household spending resilient relative to recession — "motor vehicles remain at elevated levels." Housing "only edged down." But business investment still contracting. Mixed picture.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — recession hangover.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 65 ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Productivity surging — output per hour rising strongly as firms squeeze more from existing workforces. Structural estimates being revised up. But meeting focused on ZLB, not productivity directly.
- **Direction**: Above neutral.

### FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 52 ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Funds rate at 1.75% — accommodative. But stock market weak, ZLB risks discussed. Financial conditions mixed — low rates but weak equities and corporate stress lingering.
- **Direction**: Near neutral — accommodative rates offset by equity/corporate weakness.
