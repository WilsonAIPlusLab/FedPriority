# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2010-03-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: March 2010 — moderate recovery. E&S picking up. Consumer spending increasing. IP expanding. UE 9.7%. Housing flat. Core inflation very low. Greek crisis intensifying. Fed winding down facilities.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass.

---

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 25 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core consumer price inflation was quite low." "Wide variety of goods and services exhibited persistently low inflation or outright price declines." "Deceleration in hourly compensation was even sharper."
- Direction: Well below neutral — very low core, broad-based deceleration.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Economic activity expanded at a moderate pace." But "capacity utilization remained well below its longer-run average." UE 9.7%.
- Direction: Below neutral — recovering but substantial slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 16 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE 9.7%. "Labor market might be stabilizing." But "initial claims remained high."
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 43 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer spending continued to move up." E&S "increased at a solid pace." But housing flat, nonresidential construction poor.
- Direction: Slightly below neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: N/A**

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 56 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Funding stable. Spreads near late-2007 levels. Facilities winding down smoothly. But "bank credit contracted substantially."
- Direction: Modestly above neutral.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: No dysfunction. Fed winding down "with no apparent adverse effects."
- Direction: Below neutral — approaching normal.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Investment-grade spreads "near the levels that prevailed late in 2007."
- Direction: Near neutral — largely normalized.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 51 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Stocks rose. "Equity risk premium remained well above its average." Implied vol reached post-crisis lows then spiked on Greece.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 59 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Bank credit contracted substantially." CRE delinquencies rising. "Contraction in commercial mortgage debt accelerated."
- Direction: Above neutral.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad trade-weighted value of the dollar down a bit." Up vs. euro, down vs. EM.
- Direction: Near neutral — slightly weaker.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 22 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Wide variety of goods...exhibited persistently low inflation or outright price declines."

**INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A**

**INFLATION_HOUSING: 28 ±8 [Low confidence]**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 42 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Near-term unchanged. "Longer-term edged down to near the lower end."
