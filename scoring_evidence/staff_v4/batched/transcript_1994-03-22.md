# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1994-03-22

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff speakers: MR. PRELL (economic outlook, inflation, policy assumptions), MR. TRUMAN (international/Mexico swap). District: MS. MINEHAN (Boston), MR. KEEHN (Chicago). Early 1990s unsegmented transcript with OCR truncation. Mexico swap line discussion dominates early sections.

**Era context**: March 1994. Second meeting of tightening cycle (25bp in Feb). Growth strong. Bond market reacting.

**Dimension-by-dimension**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: upside growth surprises but inflation not accelerating as feared. Minehan: "selling prices are not going up... competition intense in almost every industry, so there is no automatic passthrough of higher costs." Keehn: "I don't sense that the price pressures are as great as might have expected," wages "continue to be very restrained." But steel prices rising. Direction: slightly below neutral. Score ~44.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Prell: repeated upside growth surprises. Keehn: steel "running at capacity," delivery delays, capacity constraints. Minehan: question of "how much slack does the economy have." Modestly above potential. Score ~55.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Keehn: shift from "no new hiring" to willingness to hire. Minehan: employment improving but layoffs continue. UE ~6.5%. Score ~46.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Keehn: retail Feb 17% above prior year, steel "flat out," heavy truck 195K units, ag equipment +18%. Minehan: "ambitious capital spending plans." Very strong. Score ~65.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Minehan: "companies using technology more intensively to improve productivity." Keehn: "productivity improvements continue." Above trend. Score ~55.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Bond market tightening from rate hikes but still broadly accommodative. Score ~35.

**Bright-line checks**: OUTPUT_GAP capacity constraints → > 50 CHECK. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS still accommodative → < 50 CHECK.

**Center-of-gravity**: Modestly above potential with binding capacity constraints.
**Direction confirmation**: Above neutral. Bright-line CHECK.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 46 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**: Keehn: shifting from "no hiring" to willingness to hire. Minehan: mixed — layoffs and hiring.
**Center-of-gravity**: Improving but not yet tight. UE ~6.5%.
**Direction confirmation**: Near/slightly below neutral.

**Evidence**: Keehn: retail +17%, steel flat out, heavy trucks 195K, ag equipment +18%. Minehan: ambitious capex plans.
**Center-of-gravity**: Well above trend — broad-based strength.
**Direction confirmation**: Well above neutral.

**Center-of-gravity**: Modestly above trend from technology/restructuring.
**Direction confirmation**: Above neutral.

**Evidence**: Minehan: "longer-end interest rate movement" constraining. But funds rate still very low.
**Center-of-gravity**: Broadly accommodative despite some tightening.
**Direction confirmation**: Below neutral (loose). Bright-line CHECK.
