# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-09-17

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: September 2014. Last meeting before QE ends (Oct). UE steady at 6.1%. Employment growth slowed from H1 pace. PCE ~1.5% y/y. Oil prices declining. Dollar appreciating notably (euro, yen, sterling). 10yr yields up ~15bp. High-yield experienced brief volatility but recovered. Housing picking up. Staff slightly revised down medium-term GDP (stronger dollar, weaker global growth). Liftoff expectations around mid-2015.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "about 1½ percent over the 12 months ending in July"; core "essentially the same as total inflation"; CPI "about 1¾ percent"; "compensation per hour...rose 2¾ percent"; "unit labor costs advanced more slowly"; near-term expectations "moved down a bit"; staff: "inflation to be lower in the second half...and to remain below...2 percent over the next few years"
- Direction: Below neutral — inflation at 1.5%, below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity was expanding at a moderate pace in the third quarter"; UE "6.1 percent"; payroll employment "at a slower pace than in the first half"; but "rate of job openings rose further"; housing "seemed to be picking up"; staff: "real GDP growth in 2015 and 2016 would exceed the growth rate of potential output"; "steadily reduce resource slack"; UE "expected to...temporarily move slightly below...natural rate"
- Direction: Below neutral — slack narrowing steadily.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 38 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "6.1 percent in August"; part-time for economic reasons and long-duration unemployment "declined a little"; "rates of job openings and of gross private-sector hiring moved up"; initial claims "at a relatively low level"; but employment "at a slower pace than in the first half"
- Direction: Below neutral — continuing improvement.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 47 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "appeared to be rising at a moderate pace"; retail sales "increased at a solid rate"; "light motor vehicle sales surged in August"; "Real disposable incomes continued to increase"; housing "seemed to be picking up" with starts/permits higher; E&S "appeared to rise further"; industrial production "edged up"; consumer sentiment "improved"
- Direction: Near neutral — solid consumer spending, housing improving.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 47 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "modest gains in labor productivity"; staff continued to revise down potential growth.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Treasury yields "rose somewhat"; equity prices "edged up"; corporate bond yields "rose about in line with...Treasury securities"; high-yield experienced "sharp outflows early" but spreads "returned to their initial levels"; dollar "appreciated against most currencies"; Fed communications seen as "slightly less policy accommodation than anticipated"
- Direction: Near neutral — mild tightening from rates/dollar but still supportive.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 45 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: 10-year yields "moved up about 15 basis points"; "boosted in part by Federal Reserve communications"; inflation compensation "edged down" reflecting oil price declines.
- Direction: Near neutral — modest orderly increase.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Credit conditions for domestic businesses remained favorable"; corporate bond issuance "rebounded strongly in the first week of September"; C&I loans "expanded briskly"; CRE conditions "continued to ease"; leveraged loan issuance "continued apace"; "new money" loans "strong" with "quite large" pipeline; CLO issuance "still a major source of demand"
- Direction: Well below neutral — very accommodative credit.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad measures of domestic equity prices were up modestly"; "investors were interpreting incoming economic data as implying that the economic recovery was strengthening"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Credit conditions broadly favorable; lending standards easing; consumer credit expanding; auto loans widely available.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 58 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "U.S. dollar appreciated against most currencies over the intermeeting period, including large appreciations against the euro, the yen, and the pound sterling"
- Direction: Above neutral — notable broad appreciation.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer energy prices declined in both July and August"; food prices rose; core goods at ~1.5%.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core PCE and CPI both ~1.5-1.75%; services firming.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Near-term inflation expectations from the Michigan survey moved down a bit"; longer-term "little changed"; TIPS measures "edged down, reportedly reflecting...lower-than-expected CPI data...and recent declines in oil prices"
