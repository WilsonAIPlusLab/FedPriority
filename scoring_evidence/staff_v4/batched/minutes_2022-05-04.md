# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2022-05-04

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: May 2022. 50bp hike. GDP declined in Q1 (but private domestic final demand strong). UE 3.6%. PCE 6.6%, core 5.2%. Trimmed mean 3.7%. ECI 4.8% (12-mo) — largest since 1990. Staff common expectations index "at the upper end of the range of values seen since 2005." Treasury yields up substantially. Equities declined. VIX elevated.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 6.6%, core 5.2%. Trimmed mean 3.7%. ECI 4.8% — "largest 12-month increase since 1990." Staff expects 4.3% PCE in 2022, 2.5% in 2023. Inflation "broad-based." Score: 78.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "declined in the first quarter" but driven by volatile categories. Private domestic final demand "was faster than in the previous quarter." GDP "expected to remain well above potential...labor market conditions expected to remain very tight." Score: 55.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6%. LFPR and E-P increased. Job openings "remained elevated." ECI 4.8% — largest since 1990. Score: 62.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE and residential investment "increased...at rates similar to those seen in the fourth quarter." BFI "picked up sharply." Inventory investment moved lower. Government purchases declined. Score: 48.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Credit remained widely available, and borrower credit quality continued to be strong overall." But borrowing costs "increased in many sectors and were at or somewhat above pre-pandemic levels." Equities declined. VIX elevated. Banks easing C&I terms. Score: 50.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP elevated. CP/CD longer-tenor spreads narrowed. "Reduced market concerns about the effects of Russia's invasion." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "narrowed...ending the period only slightly narrower, on net, and below the median of their historical distribution." Muni spreads "up modestly" at 90th percentile. Corporate credit quality strong. Score: 45.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "decreased." VIX "remained at elevated levels." Staff: "increased uncertainty and ongoing volatility had reduced risk appetite...although valuations of many assets remained elevated." CRE "somewhat elevated." House prices high. Score: 48.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I and CRE loans "grew robustly." Banks easing C&I terms. Credit quality "remained strong." Small business credit available. Banks "well capitalized with strong liquidity." NBFI leverage "elevated." Score: 40.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "U.S. dollar generally strengthened, with a more pronounced dollar appreciation against AFE currencies." "Dollar appreciated significantly against the Chinese renminbi." Score: 58.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "moved up in the first quarter and was at the upper end of the range of values seen since 2005." Score: 57.
