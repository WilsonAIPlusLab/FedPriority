# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1997-02-05

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. FISHER (operations/SOMA), MR. PRELL (domestic), MR. MADIGAN (monetary policy alternatives). February 1997. Economy coming off 4.7% Q4 surge. Staff sees "above-trend growth" continuing.

**Era context**: February 1997. Fed on hold at 5.25%. Q4 1996 GDP surged 4.7%, full year ~3.5%. Staff forecasts "real growth at about trend, with a continued upward creep in the CPI." Fisher discusses SOMA portfolio. Housing stabilizing at anticipated levels. Labor markets tight — "tight labor markets remain a problem for many employers." CPI inflation 2.5-3% range. Stock market concerns with "irrationally exuberant" valuations.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: CPI moved up "another couple of tenths"; discussion of 3% CPI; inflation "creeping up." Score ~52.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Prell: Q4 was 4.7% surge; "momentum from 1996"; economy above potential. Staff forecasts above-trend growth. Score ~57.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Multiple references: "tight labor markets remain a problem for many employers, especially in the construction and information technology industries." Score ~58.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Strong: Q4 consumer spending strong; "consumer debt may provide something of a constraint"; business investment "consistently underestimated"; "strong profits" and "ready availability of credit." Score ~57.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Discussed: staff "estimating the new trend growth line at .9 percent"; anecdotal reports of improvement but statistical data not confirming. Score ~48.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: No stress. Credit readily available. "Ready availability of credit." Score ~42.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Discussion of "irrationally exuberant" market; "PE ratios" above "norms"; "interest rate forecast" and "expected earnings growth." Score ~20.

8. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "makes U.S. goods more expensive." Dollar strong. Score ~58.
