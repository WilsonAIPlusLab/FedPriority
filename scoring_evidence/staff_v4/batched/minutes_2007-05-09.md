**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format. Scoring only staff-attributable content.

**Era context**: May 2007. Economy expanding at below-trend pace. Housing correction deepening but subprime contained. Fed holds at 5.25%. Core inflation elevated but improving.

**Dimensional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Staff: "twelve-month change in core PCE price index in March was just a touch higher than the increase over the year-earlier period." Core inflation elevated. 5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Staff "marked down slightly its estimate of structural productivity growth." Direction: below trend. Score ~40.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Subprime "had not spread to the rest of the mortgage market." "Mortgage rates available to prime borrowers remained well below their levels of last summer." Equity prices climbed steeply. Favorable conditions. Direction: accommodative → must be < 50. Score ~30.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: "Yields on nominal Treasury securities edged up at all maturities." Normal. N/A.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads on speculative-grade corporate bonds narrowed." Investment-grade spreads "little changed at fairly low levels." Strong issuance. Direction: compressed → must be < 50. Score ~22.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Equity prices climbed steeply amid solid earnings reports." No valuation concern. Direction: elevated valuations = low stress. Score ~28.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Subprime lenders troubled but "turmoil in the subprime market appearing to have remained relatively well contained." System sound. Direction: low stress. Score ~38.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Foreign exchange value of the dollar against other major currencies moved lower." Direction: weakening → must be < 50. Score ~38.
