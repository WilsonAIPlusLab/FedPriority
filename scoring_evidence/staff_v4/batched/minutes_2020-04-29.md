**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: S&P 500 "rose" on net over intermeeting period (rebound from March lows). VIX "fell" from record but "remained elevated." Corporate spreads "widened somewhat" on net but retraced most increases. Fed facilities "appeared to improve conditions." Still strained overall. Score: 65.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Treasury markets experienced extreme volatility in mid-March." But "Treasury market liquidity gradually improved through the remainder of the intermeeting period." Bid-ask spreads for short-term on-the-run "fell close to levels seen earlier in the year." Short-term funding markets stabilized. EFFR at target. Improving from crisis peak. Score: 65.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Spreads "widened sharply in the beginning...subsequently retraced most of their increases." Speculative-grade issuance "extremely low in March but resumed, at a slow pace, in April." IG issuance "robust" after late-March facility announcements. "Large volume of downgrades" forecast. Default expectations elevated. Score: 65.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: S&P 500 rose on net but "remained markedly below peaks registered earlier this year." VIX "reached a record high" then declined but remained elevated. Score: 50.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Banking sector...was resilient coming into this period." Banks "able to meet surging demand for draws on credit lines." But "notable vulnerabilities" — institutional prime MMFs "faced significant stress." Open-end mutual funds "experienced heavy outflows." Business debt "seemed poised to rise further." Mortgage servicers, insurance companies highlighted as risks. Score: 60.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index increased." Dollar "appreciating modestly against AFE currencies and notably against EME currencies." "Remained significantly higher than at the beginning of the year." Score: 58.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based "little changed on balance." Michigan 5-10yr "edged up in April." TIPS compensation "ended the period higher, on net, but were still low by historical standards." Score: 42.
