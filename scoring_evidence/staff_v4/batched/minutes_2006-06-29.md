# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2006-06-29

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, unsegmented. **Era context**: June 2006. Fed funds raised to 5.25% (final hike). Growth slowing from Q1 surge. Housing cooling notably. Core inflation elevated — key concern.

Staff: Growth slowed substantially in Q2. Consumer spending softened. Housing contracted. Core inflation elevated — shelter and energy pass-through. Staff projects growth below potential; core inflation stepped up but expected to drop back "albeit to a level higher than previously forecasted." Stock indexes fell sharply. Spreads widened.
