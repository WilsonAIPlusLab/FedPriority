# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2022-12-14

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: December 2022. 50bp hike (step down from 75bp). GDP increasing at modest pace in Q4. UE 3.7%. PCE 6.0%, core 5.0%. Trimmed mean 4.7%. CPI 7.1% (stepping down). AHE 5.1%. Wage growth "above the pace judged to be consistent with the FOMC's 2 percent inflation objective." Staff: output expected below potential near end 2024. Recession "plausible alternative." Equities rose on reduced inflation concerns. Dollar depreciated. VIX around middle of range.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 6.0%, core 5.0%. Trimmed mean 4.7%. CPI 7.1%, core 6.0%. AHE 5.1%. ECI 5.2%. "Consumer price inflation remained elevated but had eased in recent months." Staff expects 5.5% PCE in 2022, stepping down. "Core goods inflation was anticipated to slow further." Score: 72.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increasing at a modest pace." "Output was expected to move below the staff's estimate of potential near the end of 2024." Still above potential but narrowing. Recession "plausible alternative to the baseline." Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.7%. Payrolls "solid" but slower pace. LFPR and E-P "declined a little." Job openings "moved back down...but remained high." AHE 5.1%. "Labor market conditions eased somewhat...but remained quite tight." Score: 58.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "appeared to have picked up in the fourth quarter." Residential investment "contracting sharply further." BFI "slowing markedly." Manufacturing "increased only modestly." Score: 42.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Treasury yields declined. Equities rose. VIX "decreased notably and was around the middle of its range." Corporate/muni/MBS spreads all narrowed. Borrowing costs declined on net. But "credit flows moderated" and "high borrowing costs appeared to weigh on financing volumes." Score: 50.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP "declined modestly." Repo rates firming. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "narrowed over the intermeeting period." Municipal spreads also narrowed. Defaults "remained at very low levels." But expected default probabilities "had increased from their levels at the beginning of the year." Spec-grade downgrades slightly exceeded upgrades. Score: 48.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose. VIX "decreased notably." "Reduced concerns about the inflation outlook." No explicit financial stability valuation update in this text. Score: 48.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans "continued to grow" but decelerating. CRE loans increasing but at slower pace. Small business credit "appeared to have tightened further." Banks shifting CRE to multifamily/industrial away from office. Auto loan delinquencies "surpassed their pre-pandemic peak." Credit card delinquencies rising but still low. Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Dollar depreciated against most foreign currencies, particularly against those of advanced foreign economies." Score: 42.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "edged down in the fourth quarter but continued to be above pre-pandemic levels." Inflation compensation "declined notably, especially for shorter maturities." Score: 53.
