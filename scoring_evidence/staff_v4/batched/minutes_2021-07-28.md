# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-07-28

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: July 2021. GDP increased in Q2 at faster pace than Q1. UE 5.9%. PCE 3.9%, core 3.4%. Trimmed mean 1.9%. Job openings at record highs. Delta variant spreading. Staff still expects inflation "transitory." Asset valuations "elevated" — P/E at upper end, HY spreads near low end, house prices "stretched."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 3.9%, core 3.4%. Trimmed mean 1.9%. CPI 5.4%, core 4.5%. "Largely reflecting transitory factors" — base effects + supply bottlenecks. AHE rising further. Median wage "stepped up noticeably." Staff expects step-down "to a little below 2 percent in 2022." But inflation risks now "tilted to the upside." Score: 58.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increased in the second quarter at a faster pace than in the first quarter." Staff projects "substantial increase" for year, GDP to "outpace growth in potential output." GDP well above potential throughout projection. Score: 47.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 5.9%. Payrolls "rose sharply in June." "Retraced more than two-thirds of the losses." Job openings at highest recorded level. AHE rising, median wage stepped up. But LFPR and E-P "unchanged." Delta variant concerns. Score: 43.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "risen in the second quarter at a pace similar to that seen in the first quarter" but "held back some as producers struggled to meet demand." Residential investment "declined in the second quarter" due to shortages. BFI growth "slowed sharply" — motor vehicles, aircraft, structures. Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Market-based financing conditions were accommodative, and bank lending standards eased for most loan categories." Corporate spreads "near the low end of their historical ranges." C&I standards "returned to the easier end of the range." Small business conditions "relatively tight." Score: 40.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR at 10bp, SOFR at 5bp. ON RRP averaged ~$800B. No emergency facility concerns. Excellent functioning. Score: 33.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "little changed" and "remained below their pre-pandemic levels." Muni spreads "increased moderately" but "remained below pre-pandemic levels." Defaults "remained low." Upgrades outpacing downgrades. Score: 35.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose moderately. VIX "somewhat above its average pre-pandemic levels." Staff: "asset valuation pressures were elevated" — "forward price-to-earnings ratio for the S&P 500 index stood at the upper end of its historical distribution." HY spreads "near the low end of their historical range." House prices "stretched." Score: 35.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks "reported easing standards and nearly all terms" for C&I. Standards "returned to the easier end of the range...since 2005." Small business loan performance improving. Bank capital "significantly exceeded regulatory requirements." Hedge fund leverage elevated. Score: 42.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "The dollar broadly appreciated." Delta variant risk-off. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common expectations index "returned to the level that prevailed in 2014." But longer-term forward inflation compensation "fell notably." Score: 50.
