4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real personal consumption expenditures fell for the fifth straight month in October." "Sales of light motor vehicles... slumped." 9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Stock prices of financial corporations fell considerably." "Broad equity indexes declined, on net, amid high volatility." Direction: severely depressed. Score ~78.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: "CDS spreads for U.S. banks widened sharply." Citigroup rescue (Nov 23). "Loan delinquencies were increasing significantly in the consumer sector." "Banks generally adopting stricter lending standards and declining to renew or paring back existing credit lines." Direction: severe stress. Score ~85.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Dollar declined on balance against the currencies of major U.S. trading partners." Direction: weakening → must be < 50. Score ~40.
