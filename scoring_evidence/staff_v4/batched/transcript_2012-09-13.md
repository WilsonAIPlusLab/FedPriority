# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2012-09-13

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — Wilcox (domestic outlook), Kamin (international), Nelson (SEP), Lehnert (financial stability). Segmented. 706 lines.

**Era context**: September 2012. QE3 announced at this meeting. UE ~8.1%. European crisis somewhat less acute after ECB's OMT announcement. Fiscal cliff concerns rising. Drought affecting agriculture. Operation Twist concluding.

**Wilcox key points**: "Near-term outlook for real GDP growth... broadly similar to" June Tealbook. "PCE was better than expected." But "job gains... remained downbeat." "Underlying pace of private payroll employment growth has trended lower." UE declined 0.2pp but "more weakness than strength in the totality." GDP growth forecast ~1.8% — "1¼ percent" current quarter/next. "Small upward revision" to medium-term from better European outlook and financial conditions.

**Kamin key points**: ECB purchases/OMT "widely anticipated." "Less stringent conditionality." "More likely that Spain and/or Italy may request financial assistance." "Financial stresses over next several months likely to be less pronounced than... July Tealbook." But "still very far from being out of the woods." Greece still troubled. Euro area "continues to contract." Foreign growth dropped to 3% from 4% in Q1. China growth slowed. Oil prices rising on Iran tensions.

**Lehnert (financial stability)**: "Little evidence that the low rate environment has fostered additional vulnerabilities." "Valuations for broad asset classes are not stretched." Stocks "not trading at unusually high multiples." Housing "near all-time low" valuation. But leveraged loans increasing, covenants weakening. Banks taking on more interest rate risk. Life insurers under pressure. Mortgage REITs growing rapidly. "Dry powder" for potential future risk-taking.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 35** ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Core PCE "in line with expectations." Headline stepping down from lower energy but oil rising on Iran. Drought pushing food prices up. "Inflation expectations anchored." Slack large. Nelson SEP: inflation projections "staying close to or slightly below" 2%.
- Direction: Below neutral — near target but forward outlook constrained by slack.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 34** ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: GDP growth "anemic 1¼ percent." UE ~8.1%, expected to "average around 8¼ percent through first half of next year." "Little reduction in unemployment rate." "Pace of real GDP growth roughly in line with... potential." By end 2014, UE ~7.5%. Still well below potential.
- Direction: Below neutral.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 27** ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: UE ~8.1%. "Underlying pace of private payroll employment growth has trended lower over the course of this year." "Workweek was lower than expected." "Average hourly earnings were flat." "More weakness than strength."
- Direction: Well below neutral — persistent deep slack, weakening trend.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 38** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: "PCE better than expected" but vehicle assemblies weak. Capital goods orders "data as well as... business surveys have deteriorated." Forward-looking investment indicators weakening. Consumption somewhat better.
- Direction: Below neutral — mixed but softening.

**PRODUCTIVITY: N/A**

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 56** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: ECB OMT announcement easing European stress. "Financial stresses... likely to be less pronounced." Stock prices slightly higher. Dollar slightly weaker. But fiscal cliff uncertainty. "Still very far from out of the woods." QE3 about to be announced (easing).
- Direction: Above neutral — moderately tight but improving from summer peak stress.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 40** ±7 [Low confidence]
- Evidence: Term premiums at historical lows. "10-year Treasury yields are quite low." Safe-haven demand. No dysfunction. Yields could "increase quickly" if situation intensifies — risk but not current stress.
- Direction: Below neutral — no Treasury stress, yields very low from safe-haven/QE.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 52** ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Lehnert: corporate bond spreads "above-average spreads relative to Treasuries." "High-yield bond spreads suggest investors should be adequately compensated." Leveraged loan issuance "robust." Some reach for yield. Spreads moderating from summer peak.
- Direction: Near neutral — spreads above average but normalizing.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 48** ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Lehnert: "Stocks are not trading at unusually high multiples." "Forward price-earnings ratios are generally well within their ranges over the past 20 years." Equity risk premium "widened to a value outside recent decades" (i.e., stocks cheap relative to bonds). No froth.
- Direction: Near neutral — fairly valued, no stress.

**BANKING_STRESS: 55** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: "Some large banks remain weak despite substantial increases in capital." "Unstable funding model of broker-dealers." "Persistent risk of runs on money market funds." But U.S. banks better. European banking stress diminishing from ECB OMT. Banks taking on more interest rate risk from low rates.
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — lingering concerns but improving.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 54** ±7 [Low confidence]
- Evidence: Dollar expected to be "slightly weaker" than previous projection as European stress eases. Kamin expects "reversal of flight-to-quality" flows and dollar "depreciating." But still elevated.
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — still somewhat strong but expected to weaken.
