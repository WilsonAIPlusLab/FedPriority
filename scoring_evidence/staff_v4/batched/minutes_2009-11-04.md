# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2009-11-04

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: November 2009 — early recovery. GDP positive in Q3. IP up three months. UE at 9.8% rising. Housing recovering. Productivity surging. Regional bank failures increasing.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass.

---

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 29 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation remained subdued." Staff: "core inflation would slow somewhat further over the next two years."
- Direction: Below neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 31 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Economic activity continued to rise." But "considerable slack remained." Factory utilization "up only a bit from its historical low."
- Direction: Below neutral — growing but far below potential.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 14 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE 9.8%. "Labor force participation rate fell to its lowest level of the year." "Job losses remained widespread."
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 37 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Real PCE rose at a solid rate" in Q3. E&S "stabilizing." Nonresidential construction "quite poor."
- Direction: Below neutral but improving.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 64 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Output per hour rose sharply in the second and third quarters, contributing to a sizable decline in unit labor costs."
- Direction: Above neutral — strong gains.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 61 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Libor-OIS "near pre-crisis levels." But banks "continued to tighten standards." "Financing for new construction projects was very difficult."
- Direction: Above neutral.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Indicators...showed some signs of improvement...but trading conditions remained somewhat impaired."
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 56 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Corporate bond spreads narrowed further." "Bid-asked spreads remained at moderate levels."
- Direction: Modestly above neutral.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Stocks about unchanged. "Equity risk premium—remained elevated."
- Direction: Modestly above neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 64 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Investor sentiment toward the banking sector appeared to deteriorate." Bank shares fell. "Rising loan losses." CDS widened for regional banks.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Trade-weighted index...was little changed."
- Direction: At neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
**INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A**
**INFLATION_HOUSING: 32 ±7 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Some house price indexes had risen in recent months" but below year-ago.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 44 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Near-term "increased." Longer-term "about unchanged."
