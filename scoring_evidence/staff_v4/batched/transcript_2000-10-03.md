### 2. Era Context

**October 2000** — This is the final phase of the late 1990s expansion. Key macro backdrop:
- The dot-com/tech bubble had begun to deflate (NASDAQ peaked in March 2000 and had fallen sharply)
- The Fed had tightened significantly (fed funds rate at 6.5%, raised 175 bps since June 1999)
- GDP growth was slowing from the very hot 1999 pace
- Labor market was still very tight (unemployment around 3.9%)
- Oil prices had surged (the Iraqi/OPEC supply issue was ongoing)
- The euro was weak, prompting the G-7 joint intervention on September 22nd

I must score only what the staff presents, not overlay my external knowledge.

BRIGHT-LINE CHECK: Staff says "easing onto a path of below-trend growth" — this implies current conditions are near the threshold, with trajectory turning below. I will score slightly below 50 to reflect that the staff presents the current moment as transitioning to below-trend.
