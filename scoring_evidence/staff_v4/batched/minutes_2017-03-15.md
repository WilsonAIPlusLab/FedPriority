# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2017-03-15

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: March 2017. Labor market strengthened further. GDP continuing to expand but at slower pace in Q1 than Q4, with some transitory factors. Headline inflation close to 2%. Equities rose further with valuations above historical norms. Dollar about unchanged. Hike at this meeting widely expected (second of the cycle). Fiscal stimulus assumed but timing pushed back.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "a little less than 2 percent" over 12 months ending January. Core 1.75%. AHE 2.75%. Compensation per hour 3.25%. CPI 2.75%, core CPI 2.25%. Projected "slightly below" 2% in 2019. Headline close to target but core still below. Score ~42.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "continuing to expand" but "at a slower pace than in the fourth quarter, with some of the slowing likely reflecting transitory factors." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output in 2017 through 2019." UE to run "below" natural rate. Score ~51.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.7%. Payrolls "brisk pace." LFPR rose. AHE 2.75%. Compensation per hour 3.25%. Job openings "high level." Initial claims "very low level." Unemployment rates for minorities "close to the levels seen just before the most recent recession." Score ~55.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "rising at a slower pace in the first quarter than in the fourth quarter." Residential investment "increased at a solid pace." Business equipment "appeared to be rising." Business sentiment "upbeat." Score ~50.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Generally accommodative." Equity price indexes "rose further." Corporate bond spreads "little changed." Financing "remained generally accommodative." Score ~32.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "increased over the intermeeting period, particularly for shorter maturities." Inflation compensation "little changed." No dysfunction. Score ~44.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads "little changed." Credit flows "solid." Leveraged loans "brisk." Score ~40.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "increased." "Some measures of valuations, such as price-to-earnings ratios, rose further above historical norms." Equity risk premium "edged lower, declining into the lower quartile." Score ~35.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation pressures "notable" but "not by enough to warrant raising the assessment... to elevated." Leverage and transformation vulnerabilities "low." Score ~33.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar index "about unchanged" / "little changed over the period." Depreciated against EME, appreciated against AFE. Score ~50.
