# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2025-12-10

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: December 2025. Most supported 25bp cut to 3.75-4.00% (some preferred hold). GDP "expanded moderately over this year." UE ticked up to 4.4%. PCE 2.8%, core 2.8%. "Both total and core PCE price inflation were somewhat higher than earlier in the year." ECI 3.5%, AHE 3.8%. Core goods inflation picked up (tariffs). Housing services inflation moved down. PDFP slowed relative to last year. Government shutdown "expected to reduce real GDP growth around 1 percentage point in the fourth quarter." Equities "little changed." Spreads "increased somewhat but remained at low levels." VIX momentarily spiked then returned. Treasury yields "edged up." Dollar "little changed." Short-term funding "tighter" with reserves at ~$2.9T (~$500B below start of runoff).

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 2.8%, core 2.8%. "Somewhat higher than earlier in the year." "Core goods price inflation had picked up, which the staff largely attributed to the effects of higher tariffs." Services inflation moved down. AHE 3.8%, ECI 3.5%. Staff: tariffs "expected to continue to put upward pressure on inflation this year and next." Inflation projected to reach 2% in 2028. Score: 56.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanded moderately" but "average rate of increase over the first three quarters...was moderate and slower than its 2024 pace." PDFP "risen faster than GDP" but "also had slowed." Government shutdown to reduce Q4 GDP ~1pp. Staff: GDP growth "modestly faster through 2028." "Expected to remain above potential through 2028." Score: 47.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 4.4%. "Continuing the gradual upward trend." Payrolls "continued to be slower." Other indicators "consistent with a gradual cooling." ECI 3.5%, AHE 3.8%. "Close to their year-earlier rates." "Hiring had remained subdued." Score: 44.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PDFP "slowed relative to last year." Consumer spending "solid" but "some recent slowing." "Higher-income households" doing better; "lower-income households...more price sensitive." Business fixed investment "robust...technology sector in particular." Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "little changed." Spreads "increased somewhat but remained at low levels." VIX momentarily spiked. Treasury yields "edged up." Dollar "little changed." Financing "remained somewhat restrictive for households and small businesses." Large/midsize businesses "continued to access credit markets at a solid pace." Score: 45.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained orderly but were generally tighter over the intermeeting period." "Robust Treasury issuance, declining reserve balances in recent months, and month-end pressures." Reserve balances "around $2.9 trillion, about $500 billion lower relative to the level...at the start of the Federal Reserve's balance sheet reductions." Score: 45.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "increased somewhat but remained at low levels." Corporate performance "little changed." Leveraged loan defaults "remained at an elevated level." CRE delinquencies "largely unchanged" at banks, CMBS "moved sideways." Small business delinquencies "remained above pre-pandemic levels." Credit card delinquencies "little changed recently." Score: 43.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities little changed. Spreads low. Score: 38.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Bank loans "expanded at a solid pace." Credit performance "little changed." "Financing conditions remained somewhat restrictive for households and small businesses." Reserve balances declining. Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "little changed, on net." Score: 50.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Near-term "had been elevated earlier in 2025 but had declined from those peaks." Longer-term "remained stable." Anchored but concerns about prolonged above-target inflation. Score: 52.
