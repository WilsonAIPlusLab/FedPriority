# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2005-03-22

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, unsegmented. Staff cues: "The information reviewed..." and "In the staff forecast..."
**Era context**: March 2005. Fed funds raised to 2.75%. Economy stronger than expected. Inflation pressures picking up.

Staff sees economy expanding at a solid pace. Business investment revised up appreciably. Core inflation fairly subdued but some pickup. Productivity growth strong. Financial conditions very accommodative with narrow spreads.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 48 ±5 [High]** — "Core consumer price inflation over the same period was fairly subdued"; staff sees core "boosted a bit" but "still largely contained."
**OUTPUT_GAP: 51 ±5 [High]** — Staff: "economy was seen as likely to expand at a rate above the growth of potential this year and next." At/slightly above potential.
**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 44 ±5 [High]** — UE ~5.3-5.4%; "resource slack was diminishing" but still present.
**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 61 ±5 [High]** — Consumer spending "robust rate"; business spending "increased sharply"; housing expanding.
**PRODUCTIVITY: 58 ±8 [Moderate]** — "continued strong growth in underlying labor productivity."
**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 21 ±5 [High]** — "favorable borrowing conditions in the form of low interest rates and narrow risk spreads."
**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±8 [Moderate]** — Normal yield movements from policy expectations.
**CREDIT_SPREADS: 15 ±5 [High]** — "Risk spreads narrowed on balance, significantly so for speculative-grade debt."
**EQUITY_VALUATION: 47 ±10 [Low]** — "Broad stock market indexes edged up." No concern.
**BANKING_STRESS: N/A**
**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 44 ±7 [Moderate]** — "dollar depreciated slightly."
**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High]** — Long-term expectations "changed little."
