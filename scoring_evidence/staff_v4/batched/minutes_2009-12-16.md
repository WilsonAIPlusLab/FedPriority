# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2009-12-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: December 2009 — recovery gaining momentum. Consumer spending solid. Home sales rising. UE dropped in November but still very high. IP broad-based expansion. Dubai World scare. Early Greece concerns. Dollar roughly stable.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass.

---

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 28 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core consumer prices increased modestly in October and were unchanged in November." Staff: "core inflation would slow somewhat from its current pace."
- Direction: Below neutral — subdued, expected to slow.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 33 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Recovery in economic activity was gaining momentum." But "capacity utilization remained very low." Staff projects "very gradual reduction in economic slack."
- Direction: Below neutral — growing but far below potential.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 16 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "dropped in November but remained quite high." "Aggregate hours worked registered the first substantial increase since the recession began." "Average length of spells of unemployment continued to increase."
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 39 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer spending expanded solidly." Home sales increased. But nonresidential construction "plunged in the third quarter."
- Direction: Below neutral — consumer recovering, nonresidential very weak.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 65 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Quite rapid productivity growth and a further decline in unit labor costs."
- Direction: Above neutral — strong productivity gains.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 60 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Funding markets stable near pre-crisis levels. "Capital markets...more supportive." But "banks continued to raise spreads on business loans."
- Direction: Above neutral — market conditions improving, bank lending tight.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 51 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Indicators of functioning...were roughly stable." TIPS liquidity improved.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 54 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Corporate bond spreads "somewhat narrower." Bid-asked spreads "about unchanged."
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — approaching normal.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 54 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Stock prices increased. "Equity risk premium—remained...at a relatively high level." Financial stocks underperformed.
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 63 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Credit quality appeared to worsen as delinquency and charge-off rates increased further." But "aggregate profitability turned positive." "Regulatory capital ratios increased."
- Direction: Above neutral — worsening credit quality despite improving profitability.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 51 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "up slightly against the major foreign currencies and down a little relative to...other important trading partners."
- Direction: At neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 32 ±8 [Low confidence]**
**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Price increases for non-energy services...remained modest."

**INFLATION_HOUSING: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Some house price indexes declined a little" but above spring troughs.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 40 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Longer-term expectations "moved down to the lower end of the narrow range." Near-term "declined."
