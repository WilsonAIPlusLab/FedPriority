# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2015-06-17

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: June 2015. Pre-liftoff (September seen as most likely). GDP "increasing moderately in the second quarter after edging down" in Q1. UE 5.5%. Inflation well below 2%. Treasury yields rose notably (15-35bp) driven by euro-area yield spike. Dollar increased a bit. Greece concerns. September liftoff expected. Labor compensation showing "tentative signs" of acceleration.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "only edged up" over 12 months ending April. Core 1.25%. AHE 2.25%. ECI 2.75%. Compensation per hour 1.75%. "Tentative signs" of labor compensation acceleration. Projected below 2% in 2016-2017, reaching 2% thereafter. Score ~31.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "increasing moderately in the second quarter after edging down" in Q1. Staff projects GDP to "expand at a faster pace than potential output in 2016 and 2017." UE to "decline gradually to the staff's estimate of its longer-run natural rate." Score ~44.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 5.5%. Payrolls "expanded at a faster pace in April and May than in the first quarter." LFPR "rose a bit." Part-time "edged down." Job openings "moved up a little." AHE 2.25%. ECI 2.75%. Score ~44.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE growth "appeared to pick up early in the second quarter." Vehicle sales "much higher in May." Housing "improved somewhat" with starts and permits increasing. Equipment "relatively slow." Structures "fell" (drilling). Score ~44.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Yields "increased notably." Equities "moved down a bit." Spreads on BBB "widened somewhat." Speculative-grade "narrowed slightly." Financing "continued to be accommodative." Score ~36.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields rose "about 15 to 35 basis points." Curve "steepened appreciably." Rise driven by euro-area yield spike and rising term premiums. Inflation compensation "relatively stable." Liquidity concerns noted. Score ~47.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: BBB spreads "widened somewhat." Speculative-grade "narrowed slightly." Leveraged loan issuance "picked up significantly." Financing "continued to be accommodative." Score ~38.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "moved down a bit." Volatility "increased, on balance, but remained near the lower end of its historical range." Score ~48.
- BANKING_STRESS: Not explicitly detailed in stability report for this meeting. Financing "accommodative." CRE "broadly available." Score ~32.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "increased a bit, on balance." Rose against Canadian dollar, yen, EME currencies; declined against euro. Score ~55.
