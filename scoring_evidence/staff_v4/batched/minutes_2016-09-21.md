# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2016-09-21

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: September 2016. Labor market strengthened. GDP increasing faster in Q3 than H1. UE 4.9%. Financial conditions accommodative. MMF reform compliance deadline approaching. Spreads near historical norms. One hike expected for 2016 (December). Productivity "declined slightly."

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE about 0.75% over 12 months ending July. Core "a little above 1½ percent." AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. Projected "marginally below" 2% in 2019. Score ~34.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "increasing at a faster pace in the third quarter than in the first half." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output in 2016 through 2019." UE to run "below" natural rate. Score ~48.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.9%. Payrolls "expanded strongly, on average." AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. Compensation "continued to rise at a moderate pace." Productivity "declined slightly." Score ~50.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "appeared to be increasing solidly." But equipment "rising slowly." Housing "continued to be soft." Residential investment "soft in the third quarter." Score ~46.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Domestic financial conditions remained accommodative." Equities "moved down" slightly. Spreads "declined somewhat" to "levels fairly close to their historical norms." Score ~37.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "increased moderately." No dysfunction. MMF reform causing some term money market rate pressure. Score ~44.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: IG and high-yield spreads "declined somewhat to levels fairly close to their historical norms." Score ~44.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "moved down, on net." Volatility "relatively low." CRE valuations "notable." Corporate equity/bond valuations "in the middle of their historical ranges." Score ~47.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate." Largest banks "strong regulatory capital and liquidity positions." Maturity/liquidity transformation "somewhat higher" due to MMF reform. Score ~35.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Not specifically addressed for broad dollar. Global risk assets "broadly increased." Dollar appears roughly stable. Score ~50.
