# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2023-05-03

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: May 2023. 25bp hike to 5.00-5.25%. GDP expanded modestly in Q1. UE 3.5%. PCE 4.2%, core 4.6%. Trimmed mean 4.7%. ECI 4.8% (12-mo), AHE 4.2%. Banking stress abating. April SLOOS: further tightening. Staff still projects "mild recession starting later this year." Asset valuations "moderate." Banking system "sound and resilient."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 4.2%, core 4.6%. Trimmed mean 4.7%. ECI 4.8%. AHE 4.2% (12-mo). "Consumer price inflation remained elevated in March but continued to slow." Staff expects 3.1% total, 3.8% core in 2023. Score: 65.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanded at a modest pace in the first quarter." Staff projects "mild recession starting later this year." "Level of real output moving below the staff's estimate of potential output in early 2024." Currently above but expected to go below. Score: 48.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.5%. LFPR and E-P "edged up." Job openings "moved down markedly during February and March but remained high." AHE 4.2%, ECI 4.8%. "Labor market conditions remained tight." Score: 57.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Consumer spending "picked up" with Jan surge then decline. Light vehicle sales "picked up notably in April." BFI "slowed further." New orders declining. Residential investment "declined further but at a slower pace." Manufacturing fell in March. Score: 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Market sentiment improved...stress in the banking sector declined." Equities increased. VIX "decreased notably." But "financing conditions continued to be restrictive, and borrowing costs remained elevated." April SLOOS: "further tightening of standards for most loan categories." Banks "expected their lending standards to tighten further." Score: 55.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: CP/NCD markets "stabilized." Spreads for lower-rated CP "narrowed significantly." "Conditions in overnight bank funding and repurchase agreement markets remained stable." Score: 38.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate bond yields "declined moderately." But small business loan rates "reached their highest levels since the Global Financial Crisis." Leveraged loan issuance "remained subdued." CMBS issuance "very slow." Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. VIX "decreased notably." Staff: "asset valuation pressures remained moderate." "Equity risk premium and corporate bond spreads declined...but remained near historical medians." CRE "remained vulnerable to large price declines." Score: 48.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Banking system was sound and resilient." But April SLOOS: tightening across all categories. Mid-sized banks tightened "more than other banks." Banks expect further tightening. "Tangible common equity to total tangible assets at banks...had fallen sharply in recent quarters." Three bank failures. Hedge fund leverage "somewhat elevated." Score: 58.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Dollar continued its earlier depreciation as differentials between U.S. and AFE sovereign yields narrowed." Score: 45.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Near-term measures of inflation expectations from these surveys moved up but were still below their peaks." Longer-term "remained within the range." Score: 52.
