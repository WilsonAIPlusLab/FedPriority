# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-01-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: January 2013. Q4 GDP slowed (transitory factors/Hurricane Sandy) but private demand solid. UE 7.8%. Fiscal cliff partially resolved. European situation improving. Financial conditions improved. QE3 ongoing ($85B/month). Sequestration looming.

**Directional signals**: Inflation subdued; output gap wide; labor still weak but improving; financial conditions improved; credit favorable.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation was subdued"; "rise in consumer prices excluding food and energy slowed"; "gains in nominal wages remained relatively slow"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2015"

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "expansion in overall economic activity slowed" (transitory); "private domestic final demand grew at a solid rate"; "slack in labor and product markets over the projection period"; "progress in reducing the unemployment rate was anticipated to be gradual"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.8 percent"; "rate of long-duration unemployment...edged down...but...still elevated"; "rate of private-sector hiring...was generally muted"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 42 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Increases in real personal consumption expenditures picked up somewhat"; E&S "rose briskly in the fourth quarter"; housing "continued to improve"; but "consumer sentiment was more downbeat"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 50 ±10 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not discussed.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "U.S. financial market conditions improved on net"; partial fiscal cliff resolution; European developments positive; CRE "continued to be strained"; but funding markets stable.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Treasury yields "increased"; no dysfunction; normal functioning.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Risk spreads on speculative-grade bonds narrowed substantially"; investment-grade spreads "decreased as well"; corporate issuance expanding.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad U.S. equity price indexes increased on net"; option-implied volatility "dipped to its lowest level since early 2007"; strong earnings season.

**BANKING_STRESS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Indicators of the condition of domestic financial institutions generally improved"; bank stock prices rose; CDS spreads "moved lower"; lending standards continued to ease.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 48 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar depreciated against euro and most EM currencies but appreciated against yen. Mixed but slight net depreciation on broad basis.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Overall prices subdued; energy declining.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core excluding food/energy "slowed."

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations were unchanged"
