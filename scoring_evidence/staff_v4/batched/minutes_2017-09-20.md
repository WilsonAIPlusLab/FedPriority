# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2017-09-20

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: September 2017. Labor market continued to strengthen. GDP rising at moderate pace before Hurricanes Harvey and Irma. UE dipped to 4.3-4.4%. Inflation still below 2% and lower than start of year. Dollar depreciated 1.5%. Equities increased. VIX at historically low levels. Balance sheet normalization announced at this meeting. Geopolitical tensions (North Korea). Staff revised down natural rate slightly.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "nearly 1.5 percent" over 12 months ending July. Core also "about 1.5 percent." CPI "almost 2 percent," core CPI 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. Compensation per hour only 1.25% (significant downward revision). Inflation expected to "reach the Committee's longer-run objective in 2019." Score ~36.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "appeared to be rising at a moderate pace in the third quarter before the landfall of Hurricanes." Staff projects "modestly faster pace than potential output through 2019." UE to "continue running below" natural rate. Score ~51.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.3-4.4%. Payrolls "rose solidly." AHE 2.5%. ECI 2.5%. But compensation per hour only 1.25% (revised down). Job openings increased. Claims "very low level." Score ~54.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "likely increasing at a slower rate in the third quarter than in the second." Business equipment "increasing at a solid rate." Residential investment "decreasing." Nonresidential structures "poised to decline." Hurricanes to reduce activity temporarily. Score ~46.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Remained generally accommodative." Equities increased. Dollar depreciated. Financing "continued to provide support for growth." Score ~30.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "decreased modestly." Geopolitical tensions pushed yields down. Debt ceiling briefly elevated bill yields. No dysfunction. Score ~40.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads on IG and speculative-grade "widened a bit." CMBS spreads "near the bottom of their ranges." Score ~38.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "increased." VIX "remained at historically low levels despite brief spikes." Score ~34.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation pressures "elevated." Leverage and transformation "low." Nonfinancial leverage "moderate." Score ~33.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "decreased 1.5 percent." Decline "widespread, led by the strengthening of the euro and the Chinese renminbi." Score ~43.
