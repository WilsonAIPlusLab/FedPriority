## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: February 2023. Fed at 4.50-4.75%. GDP expanded at 2.9% in Q4 but PDFP only 0.2%. UE 3.5%. PCE 5.0%, core 4.4%. Trimmed mean 4.4%. ECI 5.1% (12-mo) but 3-mo slowing to 4.0%. Wage growth decelerating. Staff expects recession "plausible alternative." Dollar declining. Asset valuations "notable."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 5.0%, core 4.4%. Trimmed mean 4.4%. ECI 5.1% but 3-mo at 4.0% ann rate. AHE 4.6% (12-mo), 3-mo at 4.1%. "Consumer price inflation eased in November and December but remained elevated." Staff expects 2.8% total, 3.2% core in 2023. Decelerating. Score: 68.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP 2.9% Q4 but PDFP only 0.2%. "Real output was expected to move down to the staff's estimate of potential near the end of 2025." Still above potential but narrowing. Recession "plausible alternative." Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.5% — "historical low." Job openings "remained high." ECI 5.1%. AHE 4.6%. "Measures of nominal wage growth slowed at the end of last year but continued to be elevated." Score: 58.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PDFP growth "subdued" at 0.2% annualized in Q4. Real PCE "included" in that. Manufacturing declined "sizably" in Nov and Dec. GDP boosted by volatile categories (inventories, net exports). Score: 38.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "slightly higher." VIX "slightly above the median." Corporate spreads "narrowed somewhat." Treasury yields unchanged. Borrowing costs "elevated." Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards. Lending standards tightening further for bank-dependent borrowers. Score: 52.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP elevated. Repo rates "roughly the same as the ON RRP offering rate." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "narrowed somewhat." Muni spreads "narrowed substantially." But leveraged loan downgrades notable. Default rates "remained low." Expected default probabilities "elevated relative to their historical distributions." Score: 47.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities slightly higher. VIX "slightly above the median." Staff: "asset valuation pressures remained notable" — P/E "above median," property valuations "high," "potential for large declines in property prices remained greater than usual." Corporate bond/loan spreads "eased." Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards. C&I loans "decelerated relative to earlier in the year." Small business access tightening. Banks expecting "deterioration in the quality of business loans." Auto loan delinquencies "rose above pre-pandemic levels." Capital ratios "increased slightly." Score: 52.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Staff's broad dollar index declined over the intermeeting period." EME currencies appreciated amid inflows. "Narrowing yield differentials" contributing to depreciation. Score: 42.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based longer-term "remained within the range of their values reported in recent months." Near-term measures "moved down along with actual inflation." Score: 52.
