# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2007-10-31

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Stockton (domestic outlook — broad-based strength surprise, housing risks, inflation), Dudley (financial markets — ABX, CDOs, CLOs, ABCP, bank funding, credit spreads, oil, dollar), Sheets (international — euro area tightening, EM resilience, oil surge, exports).

**Era context**: October 2007 — funds rate at 4.75% after September 50bp cut. Incoming data surprisingly strong. Financial conditions mixed — improved from September panic but still stressed. Oil nearing $94. Dollar declining sharply.

**Key evidence**:
- Stockton: "From a forecast perspective, it's been a pretty wild ride." Data "stronger than our expectations in September" — "almost across the board." Consumer spending stronger, equipment spending stronger, exports surging at 17% annual rate. GDP revised up ~0.5pp for H2. BUT "below-trend growth late this year and next year results in some easing of pressures on resource utilization." Growth slowing to 1.75% in 2008 then 2.25% in 2009.
- Housing: "Still easier to see sizable downside risks than sizable upside risks." "Sales and starts of new single-family homes to decline another 8 percent." "Not difficult to envision a more painful period of adjustment."
- Senior Loan Officer Survey: "sharp jump in the fraction of banks reporting tighter terms and standards on loans to businesses and households."
- Inflation: Core PCE "to move sideways at a 1.9 percent pace in 2008 and 2009." "Incoming data slightly more favorable than expected." Despite higher oil, weaker dollar, and tighter utilization, inflation unchanged due to "negative residual" in models.
- Dudley: "Two crosscurrents" — "(1) greater discrimination by investors with respect to credit quality across asset classes versus (2) ongoing deterioration in credit quality within the class of mortgage-related assets." "Improved ability of investors to discriminate" BUT "subprime mortgage market, the mortgage-backed securities market, and financial entities such as mortgage and financial guarantors remain under pressure."
- CLO issuance recovering; CDO issuance "remains very depressed." "Corporate debt fundamentals remain good." Bank funding pressures "diminished" but "term funding pressures" remain — LIBOR-OIS spreads narrowed but still elevated.
- CDS spreads for large commercial banks "improvement has been reversed over the past couple of weeks." "Mortgage insurers and financial guarantors have widened significantly."
- Oil at $94/WTI — "shot higher." Dollar "continues on its gradual downward course." Broad index dropped >3% since last meeting.
- Sheets: Euro area bank lending survey showed "sharpest shift toward tightening in its five-year history." UK "growth of mortgage lending continued on a downward path." But EM "generally positive." Exports surging.

1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: Core PCE at 1.9% projected. Data "slightly more favorable." But oil up, dollar down add pressures. Score: 53 ±4.
2. OUTPUT_GAP: GDP stronger near-term (3.25% Q3) but slowing to 1.75% in 2008. "Below-trend growth" leading to "easing of pressures on resource utilization." Below potential. Score: 47 ±5.
3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: Not extensively discussed. UE around 4.75%, near NAIRU. Senior loan officer survey showing tightening but labor market holding. Score: 51 ±5.
4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Consumer "stronger than expected"; equipment spending up; exports surging 17%. BUT housing continuing steep decline; housing "major downside risk." Net: above-trend consumption offset by housing contraction. Score: 40 ±6.
5. PRODUCTIVITY: Not discussed separately. N/A.
6. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Mixed. "Improved" from September — CLO issuance up, investor discrimination improving. BUT mortgage/CDO markets "remain under pressure," Senior Loan Officer Survey shows "sharp jump" in tightening. LIBOR-OIS still elevated. "Financial market conditions have improved somewhat but remain far from normal." Score: 60 ±6.
7. CREDIT_SPREADS: Corporate credit fundamentals "good." CLO issuance recovering. BUT CDO issuance "very depressed." High-yield spreads still widened. CDS spreads for banks rising again. "Corporate debt spreads have increased markedly." Score: 58 ±6.
8. BANKING_STRESS: Bank CDS spreads "improvement reversed." "Mortgage insurers and financial guarantors widened significantly." Senior Loan Officer Survey: "sharpest shift toward tightening." Large bank earnings disappointing. Score: 58 ±6.
9. EQUITY_VALUATION: Stock market had "retraced most of its earlier increase." "Demand for downside protection in equity market has diminished" but "positive skew persists." Score: 52 ±7.
10. DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "continues on its gradual downward course." Broad index dropped >3%. "New lows against the euro and the Canadian dollar." Score: 25 ±5.
