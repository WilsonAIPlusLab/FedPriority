# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2015-04-29

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: April 2015. Pre-liftoff. GDP "only edged up in the first quarter" with transitory factors. UE unchanged at 5.5%. Inflation well below 2%. Dollar depreciated during period. Financial conditions eased on balance. Inflation compensation rose. September liftoff seen as most likely. Staff projects GDP faster than potential.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE 0.25% over 12 months ending Q1 (year-over-year). Core 1.25%. AHE 2%. Survey expectations "stable." Market-based compensation "increased somewhat but were still low." Projected below 2% in 2016-2017. Score ~30.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "only edged up in the first quarter" but "much of this weakness was attributed to transitory factors." Staff continued to project GDP "at a faster pace than potential output in 2015 and 2016." UE to "move a little below" natural rate. Score ~43.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 5.5%. Payrolls "expanded at a solid pace" on average in Q1 but March weaker. LFPR "edged down." Part-time "little changed." Job openings "edged up" above pre-recession level. Score ~43.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "increased... albeit at a much slower pace." Real disposable income "rose strongly." Consumer sentiment near pre-recession high. Equipment "rose modestly." Structures "fell considerably" (drilling). Housing "increased at a slow pace." Score ~40.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Eased, on balance." Interest rates lower. Inflation compensation "rose." Equities "increased somewhat." Dollar "declined." Financing "remained accommodative." Score ~33.
- TREASURY_STRESS: 5- and 10-year yields "decreased." TIPS declined more. Inflation compensation "rose significantly" at 5-year. No dysfunction. Score ~40.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Speculative-grade spreads "narrowed." Energy firm spreads retraced some. Financing "remained accommodative." Leveraged loans slowed but CLO issuance robust. Score ~32.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "rose somewhat." Volatility "declined." Expected real return on equities "moved down" (valuations stretched). Score ~33.
- BANKING_STRESS: "Leverage in the banking system remained relatively low." Household debt "modest." "Valuations remained stretched for some asset classes." Score ~30.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "depreciated during the intermeeting period" on weaker data, but "cumulative appreciation... since mid-2014 remained substantial." Score ~58.
