# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-01-27

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: January 2021. GDP continued to advance in Q4 but not yet at pre-pandemic level. UE at 6.7%. Payrolls fell in December. PCE 1.1%. Fiscal stimulus expectations (CAA + anticipated additional). Vaccines beginning. Asset valuations "elevated." Banks still tight on lending but easing.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.1%, core 1.4%. Trimmed mean 1.7%. "Continued to be held down by relatively weak aggregate demand." Median wage 3.5% (below pre-pandemic). Score: 28.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "continued to advance in the fourth quarter...albeit at a pace that was markedly slower than the rapid rate seen in the third quarter, while the level of real GDP had not yet returned to the level seen before the onset of the pandemic." Staff projects GDP to "outpace that of potential" over medium term — currently below potential. Score: 35.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 6.7%. Payrolls fell in December. "Retraced a little more than half of the losses." LFPR and E-P unchanged. Initial claims higher in mid-Jan than early Dec. Median wage 3.5% "well below its pre-pandemic pace." Score: 33.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE fell in Nov, pointed to further decline in Dec. Housing starts/permits "well above pre-pandemic levels." Equipment/intangibles "strong increase" in Q4. Nonresidential structures "declined further." IP advanced but below pre-pandemic. Mixed — housing strong, consumption weak. Score: 38.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Investor sentiment improved." Equities increased notably. Corporate/muni spreads narrowed. "Market-based financing conditions remained accommodative, while bank lending conditions continued to be tight." But smaller net share tightened than previous quarters. Bifurcated but improving. Score: 45.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable over the intermeeting period, including over the year-end." CP/CD at historically low levels. No take-up at emergency facilities. No repo participation. Excellent functioning. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads on corporate bond yields over comparable-maturity Treasury yields narrowed somewhat." "Corporate bond spreads had declined to pre-pandemic levels, which were at the lower ends of their historical distributions." Default expectations at pre-pandemic levels. Upgrades roughly matching downgrades. Score: 38.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Asset valuation pressures as elevated." "Corporate bond spreads had declined to pre-pandemic levels, which were at the lower ends of their historical distributions." "Measures of the equity risk premium declined further, returning to pre-pandemic levels." Staff explicitly calls valuations "elevated." Score: 40.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks tightened C&I standards but "a smaller net percentage...tightened lending standards...than in previous quarters." "Financing conditions for small businesses remained tight." Small business delinquency/default "remained elevated." "Vulnerabilities stemming from financial leverage were moderate." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index was little changed, on net." Renminbi appreciated notably. Score: 50.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Surveys "ticked higher." Michigan 5-10yr "moved back up to its late-summer level." TIPS "increased moderately, on net, continuing the upward trend." Score: 50.
