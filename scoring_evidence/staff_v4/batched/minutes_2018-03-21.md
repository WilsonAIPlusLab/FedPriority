# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2018-03-21

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: March 2018. Labor market continued to strengthen. GDP rising at moderate pace in Q1. UE at 4.1%. Inflation still below 2%. Major equity volatility event in early February (VIX spike to highest since 2015). Equities declined on net. Corporate bond spreads widened but remained near lower end of historical ranges. Dollar appreciated moderately. February budget agreement expected to boost GDP. March hike at this meeting.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE 1.75% over 12 months ending January. Core 1.5%. CPI 2.25%, core CPI 1.75%. AHE 2.5%. Compensation per hour 2.75%. Staff projected inflation to "reach the Committee's 2 percent objective in 2019." Score ~39.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "rising at a moderate pace in the first quarter." Staff projects "increase at a faster pace than potential output through 2020." Budget agreement boost. UE to "continue to run below" natural rate. Score ~53.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.1%. Payroll gains "strong." LFPR "stepped up markedly in February." AHE 2.5%. Compensation per hour 2.75%. Part-time "close to its pre-recession levels." Job openings/quits "increased slightly." Claims "continued to be low." Consumer sentiment "highest level since 2004." Score ~57.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Consumer expenditures "likely to rise at a modest pace in the first quarter following a strong gain." Tax refund delays holding back spending temporarily. Residential investment "slowing." Equipment growth "moderating." But forward indicators "point to further solid gains." Score ~48.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Turbulent. Equities "declined." Spreads "widened." Treasury yields "rose." But "financing conditions for nonfinancial corporations and households remained accommodative... continued to support further expansion." Score ~40.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "shifted up and flattened a bit." Both rates and option-implied volatility on longer-term rates "rose notably" after Feb 5 VIX spike but "mostly retraced." Score ~48.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads on IG and speculative-grade "widened but remained near the lower end of their historical ranges." Score ~40.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "decreased." VIX "rose to its highest level since 2015" on Feb 5. Subsequently "recovered about half" of decline. Volatility event but partial recovery. Score ~48.
- BANKING_STRESS: Not explicitly discussed in stability report for this meeting. Financing "accommodative." Score ~34.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Broad dollar "appreciated moderately." Canadian dollar depreciated significantly. Venezuelan bolivar devaluation. Yen appreciated (safe haven). Score ~55.
