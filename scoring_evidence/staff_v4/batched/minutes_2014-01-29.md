# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-01-29

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: January 2014. First meeting after taper began (Dec 2013). UE declined to 6.7%. PCE inflation still under 1% y/y. GDP growth picked up in H2 2013. Housing stabilizing after mortgage rate shock. EME stress ratcheting up (Turkey, Argentina). Dollar appreciated. Longer-term rates declined ~10-20bp. Staff revised down potential output growth.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 27 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "a little under 1 percent over the 12 months ending in November"; core PCE "increased slightly more than 1 percent"; "consumer energy prices declined"; staff: "inflation would run well below the Committee's 2 percent objective early this year"; near-term forecast "revised down a little"
- Direction: Well below neutral — inflation far below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 38 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "rate of economic growth picked up in the second half of 2013"; staff: "real GDP would expand more quickly over the next few years"; "slow reduction in resource slack"; "unemployment rate was expected to decline gradually"
- Direction: Below neutral — slack narrowing but still significant.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 35 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "declined to 6.7 percent in December"; "rate of long-duration unemployment declined"; "rate of job openings edged up"; "share of small businesses reporting...hard-to-fill positions trended up"; but "rate of gross private-sector hiring was still low"; part-time for economic reasons "little changed" and "elevated"
- Direction: Below neutral — improvement continuing but still slack.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 45 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose at a faster pace"; retail sales "increased strongly"; manufacturing "increased at a robust pace"; E&S "appeared to strengthen"; housing "tentative signs of stabilizing"; consumer sentiment "improved"; but "real disposable income was little changed"
- Direction: Near neutral — broad-based improvement, approaching trend.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Staff "revised downward its view of the pace at which potential output had increased" — implies weak productivity growth.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 47 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "financial conditions in the United States remained supportive of growth"; "Equity prices increased a bit, longer-term interest rates declined"; corporate bond spreads "continued to narrow"; investment-grade spreads "lowest levels in several years"; speculative-grade "approaching pre-crisis levels"; but EME concerns late in period
- Direction: Below neutral — accommodative conditions.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: 10-year yields "declined about 10 basis points"; 30-year "20 basis points"; "increase in safe-haven demands"; "decreased uncertainty about future longer-term interest rates"
- Direction: Below neutral — yields declining, calm conditions.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Corporate bond spreads continued to narrow"; investment-grade "lowest levels in several years"; speculative-grade "approaching pre-crisis levels"; bond issuance "generally stayed strong"; C&I loans "increased by a notable amount"; leveraged loans "continued apace"; CMBS "investor demand remained strong and spreads continued to be tight"
- Direction: Well below neutral — very narrow spreads.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad U.S. equity price indexes edged higher"; bank stock prices "rose notably"; but late-period pullback from EME concerns.

**BANKING_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Bank stock prices "rose notably"; CDS for largest banks "narrowed somewhat"; banks "continued to ease their lending standards"; "increase in demand, on net, in most major loan categories"; Moody's "more upgrades than downgrades"
- Direction: Below neutral — healthy banking conditions.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 55 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "appreciated against most other currencies"; "particularly large increases against the Argentine peso and the Turkish lira"
- Direction: Above neutral — notable appreciation, especially vs EME currencies.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 22 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "consumer energy prices declined"; core goods very subdued; PCE under 1%.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 28 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Services inflation subdued; overall core just above 1%.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near-term and longer-term inflation expectations from the Michigan survey were little changed"; 5-10yr inflation compensation "decreased somewhat" but near-term "increased a little"
