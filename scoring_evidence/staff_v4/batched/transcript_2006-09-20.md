## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Stockton (domestic outlook, housing, recession risk), Kos (financial markets).

**Era context**: September 2006 — funds rate at 5.25%, on hold. Housing clearly contracting. GDP slowing notably. Core inflation still elevated but energy prices declining.

**Key evidence**: Stockton: "economy eases into an extended period of slightly below-trend growth led by a retrenchment in the housing sector." GDP growth in H2 at "around 1½ percent" — revised down 0.5pp from August. "Output gap opens... by the middle of next year but does not trigger a more precipitous cyclical contraction." Core PCE projected at "2¼ percent in 2007 and 2 percent in 2008." Housing: "sales have contracted sharply in recent months, inventories of unsold homes surged, cutbacks in production, and prices are rapidly decelerating." But "financial conditions in the corporate sector remain solid." Explicitly discusses recession risk: "imbalances in the economy that cause substantial pain... housing increasingly looks like a sector that could play that role." But baseline is soft landing.
