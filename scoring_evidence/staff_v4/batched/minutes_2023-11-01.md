**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanded at a strong pace in the third quarter." Staff projects GDP to slow, "rise more slowly than the staff's estimate of potential over the next two years." UE "roughly flat through 2026." Near potential. Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.8%. Payrolls "rose at a faster pace in September." LFPR and E-P unchanged. "Labor demand and supply were slowly moving into better alignment." Wage measures declining. Score: 52.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: GDP "posted a strong gain in the third quarter." PDFP "posted a smaller but still-solid increase." Score: 50.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "decreased." VIX "increased notably." Longer-term yields rose notably. Corporate spreads "widened." Borrowing costs "continued to rise." Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards. "Financing conditions tightened further." Score: 58.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP "continued to decline." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "widened." HY "more than" IG. Corporate bond yields "rose more than Treasury yields, particularly for speculative-grade bonds." Delinquency rates rising across sectors. Score: 52.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "decreased." VIX "increased notably." Staff: "asset valuation pressures...notable." "Forward price-to-earnings ratio for S&P 500 firms increased to the upper quartile." House prices "increased to the upper end." CRE "valuations remained stretched." Score: 42.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards. Small firms finding it harder to get credit. "Fair value of banks' longer-term fixed-rate assets...decreased in the third quarter as longer-term interest rates rose." Hedge fund leverage "above historical averages." Funding risks "notable." But "banking system remained well capitalized." Score: 55.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Staff's broad dollar index" increased. Dollar appreciating on yield differentials. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Survey measures of consumers' short-run inflation expectations remained above their pre-pandemic levels." Medium-to-longer-term "in the range seen in the decade before the pandemic." Score: 50.
