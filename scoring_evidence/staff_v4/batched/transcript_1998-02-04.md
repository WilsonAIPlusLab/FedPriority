**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. PRELL (domestic, extensive with charts), MR. TRUMAN (international). February 1998. Post-Asian crisis. Staff forecasts sharp deceleration.

**Era context**: February 1998. Fed at 5.50%. Prell presents chart-based briefing. Staff predicts "sharp deceleration" — GDP to ~1% this year, ~2% next. Unemployment rising to 5-1/4% by late 1999. Factory utilization dropping to 78.5%. Stock market: staff expects 10% decline from yesterday's record; divergence between staff profit forecast (weak) and market consensus. Flat yield curve — "unusually flat" but "unusual way" of getting there (long rates fell, not short rose). Real funds rate rising due to declining inflation expectations. Inflation: core CPI "edges off slightly"; overall slows then accelerates. Housing: "high rate for a while longer and then" declining. Consumer sentiment historically high. Wealth-income ratio at extreme levels.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: core CPI "edges off slightly through 1999"; disparate signals from labor vs factory utilization. Split the difference. Score ~46.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Staff forecasts sharp deceleration to 1% this year; but currently still above potential; unemployment still low. Score ~55.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Unemployment still very low; rising to 5-1/4% projected. Currently tight. Score ~62.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: "almost 4 percent this year" for private domestic final demand; consumer sentiment historically high; wealth driving spending; housing strong. Score ~58.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Discussed: "favorable supply conditions — including strong productivity gains" keeping inflation low. Score ~56.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Flat yield curve; "unusually flat"; real rate rising; but stock market at record. Mixed. Score ~48.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: stock market at "new highs in the face of the recent Asian storms"; expects 10% decline; profit forecast diverges sharply from market consensus. Score ~18.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Not directly discussed. Some widening from Asia. Score ~52.

9. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Strong dollar implied — "availability of cheap imports." Score ~62.
