# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-06-19

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — Laubach (domestic outlook), Gruber/Campbell (international), Ihrig (SEP), Kiley (financial stability). Segmented. 1298 lines.

**Era context**: June 2013. Taper tantrum beginning — rates rising sharply since May. QE3 ongoing but taper expected to begin. UE 7.6%. House prices up 6.5% YTD. Equity prices up 14% YTD. Consumer sentiment at highest since 2007. Core PCE falling to ~1%. Treasury yields up ~120bp since April/May. Emerging market stress from taper expectations.

**Laubach key points**: "Mixed bag" of data. Consumer side better: "retail sales came in slightly above expectations." "CoreLogic house price index increased 6.5 percent over first four months." "Equity prices risen 14 percent." "Consumer sentiment at highest level since late 2007." Business side "more downbeat" — "capital goods excluding aircraft have flattened out." "Manufacturing output weaker." GDP first half ~2.25%, "unchanged from April." "Modestly stronger labor market." Total payroll employment "2.7 million" more than September 2012. UE rate "decline 0.8 percentage point" vs flat expected in September 2012. Natural rate revised down — "return to longer-run level of 5¼ percent" brought forward to late-2015 from late-2017.

**Core inflation**: "Core PCE inflation on 12-month basis through April was close to 1 percent." "About half of the recent downward surprise was concentrated in medical prices" from sequestration Medicare cuts — "transitory."

**Kiley (financial stability)**: "Low rates encouraging buildup in duration and credit risk." "Appetite for risk has increased modestly." "Risky asset prices have been rising." "Leverage easing." "Most valuation measures consistent with fundamentals." "Signs of froth emerging in some areas" — leveraged lending. But "standards remain firm."

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30** ±5 [High confidence]
- Evidence: Core PCE "close to 1 percent" — well below 2% target. "Half of downward surprise from medical prices" (transitory from sequestration). Expected to "step up" but "core inflation to only edge up to 1.6 percent this year and 1.7 percent in 2014." Headline also low. Significant undershoot.
- Direction: Below neutral — notable undershoot of target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 37** ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: GDP ~2.25% first half. UE 7.6%, declining. "Modestly stronger labor market." "Payroll employment 2.7 million" more than Sept 2012. "Employment-to-population ratio projected to improve." But still below potential. Fiscal drag from sequester.
- Direction: Below neutral but improving.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 32** ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: UE 7.6%. Declining faster than expected (0.8pp vs flat expected). "Total payroll employment rise 2.7 million" since Sept 2012. But "labor force participation rate" declining unexpectedly. Employment-to-population ratio improving only modestly. "Unemployment gap narrower than anticipated." Natural rate now 5.25%.
- Direction: Below neutral — improving but still slack.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 42** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Retail sales above expectations. House prices up 6.5%. Equity prices up 14%. Consumer sentiment highest since 2007. But "capital goods excluding aircraft have flattened." "Manufacturing weaker." Mixed — consumer strong, business weak.
- Direction: Slightly below neutral — consumer strength vs business weakness.

**PRODUCTIVITY: N/A**

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 50** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Treasury yields up ~120bp since April/May. Mortgage rates up ~26bp in intermeeting period. Corporate yields backed up. But equity prices up 14%. House prices up significantly. Dollar "little changed." QE3 ongoing. "Backup in Treasury and corporate yields" tightening conditions somewhat. Overall mixed — rate backup tightening vs asset price gains easing.
- Direction: Near neutral — rate backup offset by asset price gains.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 54** ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Potter: "10-year nominal Treasury yield ended period 30 basis points higher." "Longer-term nominal Treasury yields and mortgage rates now up roughly 120 basis points since April/May." "Deleveraging and convexity hedging likely amplified the interest rate increases." Sharp rate rise but driven by expectations, not dysfunction.
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — rates rising sharply from taper expectations, some market stress.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 46** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Corporate yields backed up but spreads still relatively tight. Leveraged lending robust. "High-yield" flows strong. "Standards remain firm." Reach for yield still present. No dramatic widening.
- Direction: Near neutral to slightly below — spreads still compressed despite rate backup.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 44** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: "S&P 500" and equity prices up 14% YTD. "Valuations consistent with fundamentals." Kiley: not overvalued. Strong gains but supported by earnings recovery and lower discount rates.
- Direction: Slightly below neutral — equities rallying, fairly valued.

**BANKING_STRESS: 42** ±6 [Moderate confidence]
- Evidence: Kiley: "Major financial institutions do not appear to have taken on excessive credit or duration risk." "Leverage and maturity transformation in shadow banking and asset markets have not been widespread." Banks better capitalized. European banking improving. "Signs of froth emerging in some areas" but contained.
- Direction: Below neutral — banking sector strengthening, modest reach-for-yield concerns.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 52** ±7 [Low confidence]
- Evidence: "Dollar a little stronger than in April Tealbook." EM currencies weakening from taper expectations. But dollar "little changed" over intermeeting period. Dollar strength from taper expectations affecting EM more.
- Direction: Near neutral — modest dollar strength.
