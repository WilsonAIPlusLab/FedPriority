# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2018-08-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: August 2018. GDP rose at strong rate in H1. UE at 4.0%. PCE "remained near 2 percent." Trade tensions weighing on foreign markets but modest domestic imprint. Dollar appreciated. Equities mixed. VIX little changed, just above pre-February lows. Asset valuation pressures "elevated." Nonfinancial business leverage "elevated." Staff projects above-trend growth continuing.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE "2.2 percent" over 12 months ending June. Core 1.9%. CPI 2.9%, core CPI 2.3%. AHE 2.7%. ECI 2.9% (up from 2.4% year earlier). Score ~50.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "rose at a strong rate in the first half of the year." Staff projects "output was projected to rise further above the staff's estimate of potential output." UE to "decline further below" natural rate. Score ~56.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 4.0%. Payrolls "expanded at a strong pace again." Part-time "edged down to its lowest level since late 2007." Unemployment rates for minorities "at or below the lows achieved during the previous expansion." AHE 2.7%. ECI 2.9%. Job openings elevated. Claims "continued to be low." Score ~62.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Real PCE "rose briskly in the second quarter." Equipment "rose at a moderate pace." Nonresidential structures "expanded at a solid pace." Residential investment "declined again." Government purchases "rose at a faster rate." Score ~52.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Trade concerns weighed at times but "modest imprint on domestic asset prices." Equities "mixed." Spreads "little changed" and "low by historical standards." Dollar moved up. "Financing conditions... remained supportive of economic activity on balance." Score ~38.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yield curve "flattened somewhat." Yields "little changed." TIPS "little changed on net." No dysfunction. Score ~44.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads "little changed, on net" for both IG and speculative-grade. "Remained low by historical standards." CMBS "little changed" at "post-crisis lows." Score ~38.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "displayed mixed results." VIX "little changed" and "remained only a bit above the very low levels that prevailed before early February." Asset valuation pressures "continued to be elevated, with no major asset class exhibiting valuations below their historical midpoints." Score ~37.
- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation "elevated." Financial leverage/transformation "low." Household leverage "low-to-moderate." Nonfinancial business leverage "elevated." Score ~35.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated against most currencies." Exceptions: Mexican peso. Chinese renminbi "unusually sharp depreciation" on trade tensions. Score ~57.
