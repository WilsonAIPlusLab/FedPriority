# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2019-05-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: May 2019. Hold at 2.25-2.50%. GDP solid Q1 (net exports/inventories boost). PCE 1.5%, core 1.6%. UE 3.8%. Equities rose notably, VIX at Sep 2018 lows. Asset valuations "increased significantly this year." Dollar increased modestly.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.5%, core 1.6%. "Both total and core PCE price inflation...stepped down." "12-month change in the trimmed mean measure...was 2.0 percent." AHE 3.2%. "Recent price data had been lower than expected." Below target and declining. Score: 42.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increased at a solid rate in the first quarter." But boost from net exports and inventories — PDFP "posted a smaller gain." Staff: GDP "expected to increase at a rate a little above the staff's estimate of potential output growth." Above potential. Score: 54.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.8%. "Strong labor market conditions." Payrolls "solid" in Q1. AHE 3.2%. LFPR "declined a bit." "Pace of job gains slowed from the strong pace posted in 2018 but remained solid." Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real PCE looked to have posted only a modest increase." Business fixed investment "appeared to be rising at a moderate pace." Residential "continued to be weak." Mixed signals. Score: 47.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities rose notably. VIX at Sep 2018 lows. Spreads narrowed. Treasury yields "were roughly unchanged." Dollar "increased modestly." Asset valuations "increased significantly this year." Score: 38.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR "moved up closer to the IOER rate." No stress. Score: 38.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Spreads on investment- and speculative-grade corporate bonds narrowed." Leveraged loan issuance "remained strong." Score: 40.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose notably. VIX at lows. Asset valuations "increased significantly this year." "Forward P/E ratio for the S&P 500...rose further above its historical median." Score: 38.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Financial-sector leverage remained moderate." Nonfinancial business debt "near historical highs." "Growth in business debt remained concentrated in riskier forms of lending." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "increased modestly." Score: 52.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Survey-based measures...were little changed." Market-based "moved down." Score: 46.
