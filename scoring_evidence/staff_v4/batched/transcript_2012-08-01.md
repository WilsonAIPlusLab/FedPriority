## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — Wilcox (domestic outlook), Kamin (international), Potter (financial markets), Lehnert (financial stability). Segmented. 516 lines.

**Era context**: July-August 2012. European crisis ongoing — Spanish yields spiked, then Draghi's "whatever it takes" sparked rally. UE ~8.3%. Growth sluggish. Fiscal cliff concerns. Drought affecting agriculture. Operation Twist continuing. QE3 debate underway.

**Wilcox key points**: "Spending data and other indicators... led us to mark down our near-term projection." "Real retail sales... about flat, on net, since February." "Household sentiment has been more downbeat." "New orders for capital goods have flattened out." "Forward-looking indicators... deteriorated in recent months." "More pronounced... softening than expected in manufacturing." GDP growth forecast about 1.8% through mid-next year — "roughly in line with our estimate of potential." UE rate "little reduction" expected. "Recession was a good bit deeper" after annual revisions. Core PCE "in line with expectations." Headline stepping down from lower oil. Fiscal cliff drag assumed.

**Kamin key points**: "Rollercoaster ride." European financial conditions worsening until Draghi intervention. "Elevated stresses and deep forebodings about the future." "Euro-area economy will fall deeper into recession." "Great deal of monetary easing" globally — Brazil, Korea, China cut rates. "Weakening" in EMEs. Foreign growth only 2.25%. Oil prices rising on Iran tensions. Drought pushing crop prices up.
