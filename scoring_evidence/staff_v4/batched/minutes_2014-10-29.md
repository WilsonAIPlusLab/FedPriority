# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-10-29

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: October 2014. Final QE purchase meeting. UE fell to 5.9%. Mid-October flash crash in Treasuries (Oct 15: 30bp intraday swing). Equities fell ~1%. Global growth concerns (euro area stagnation, China slowing). Oil prices declining further. Dollar appreciating. Market-based inflation compensation declining. Spreads widened modestly. Staff: "asset valuation pressures...broadening" and "loosening of underwriting standards."

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "about 1½ percent over the 12 months ending in August"; CPI and core CPI "about 1¾ percent"; energy prices "declined further"; food prices "rose solidly"; near-term expectations "declined"; market-based inflation compensation "declined somewhat"; staff: "inflation...to remain below...2 percent over the next few years"
- Direction: Below neutral — at 1.5% with declining inflation compensation.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 41 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity expanded at a moderate pace in the third quarter"; UE "declined to 5.9 percent"; employment gains in Q3 "similar to...the first half of the year"; industrial production "increased briskly in September"; staff: "real GDP was still expected to rise faster than potential output in 2015 and 2016"; "resource slack was anticipated to decline steadily"
- Direction: Below neutral but narrowing — UE approaching natural rate.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 40 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "5.9 percent"; part-time for economic reasons "decreased a little"; "rate of job openings moved up"; "measures of firms' hiring plans increased"; initial claims "remained low"; "some measures of household expectations for labor market conditions improved"; but "rate of gross private-sector hiring declined"
- Direction: Near neutral — substantial progress toward full employment.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 45 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "increased at a modest pace in the third quarter"; retail sales "little changed in September following solid gains in July and August"; motor vehicle sales "fell back in September following a steep increase in August"; housing "improving only slowly"; E&S "appeared to have risen at a moderate pace"; industrial production "increased briskly"
- Direction: Near neutral — moderate growth with mixed signals.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 47 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not explicitly discussed; staff continued to assume low potential growth.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 50 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "sharp pullback from risky assets" mid-October; but "subsequently reversed much of their declines"; Treasury yields "fell notably" (-30bp); equity prices "edged down"; spreads "widened modestly"; dollar "appreciated moderately"; "technical factors reportedly contributed to volatile interest rate moves in mid-October"; staff: "asset valuation pressures...broadening" and "loosening of underwriting standards"
- Direction: Neutral — tighter from global risk-off episode but rates lower; mixed signals.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Oct 15 flash event: "5- and 10-year Treasury yields dropping as much as 30 basis points in about an hour before retracing"; "Treasury market liquidity...worsened significantly"; "implied volatility of longer-term rates jumped"; but "subsequently fell back"; overall yields declined ~30bp over period. Temporary dysfunction.
- Direction: Near neutral — brief significant volatility event, but resolved quickly.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "spreads on both investment- and speculative-grade corporate bonds widened modestly"; but "Credit flows to nonfinancial business picked up"; C&I loans "continued to expand at a robust pace"; CRE lending "continued to ease"; "asset valuation pressures...broadening" and "loosening of underwriting standards in the speculative corporate debt and CRE markets"
- Direction: Below neutral — modest widening but still narrow levels; robust credit flows.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 52 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: S&P 500 "decreased about 1 percent"; VIX "increased moderately" but below long-run average; "during the mid-October volatility spike, it briefly touched high levels last seen in 2011"; earnings "generally viewed as positive"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — small decline but still elevated levels.

**BANKING_STRESS: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "relatively strong capital and liquidity profiles of large domestic banking firms"; "subdued aggregate leverage in the nonfinancial sector"; "relatively restrained use of short-term wholesale funding"; U.S. financial system "appeared resilient to shocks of the magnitude seen recently"

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 58 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "broad nominal dollar index ended the period moderately higher"; market-based inflation compensation declining partly due to dollar appreciation.
- Direction: Above neutral — continued appreciation.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Energy prices "declined further"; food "rose solidly"; core goods subdued; oil prices falling.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core CPI ~1.75%; core PCE ~1.5%; services component near these levels.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 47 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Near-term Michigan expectations "declined"; longer-term "little changed"; but market-based measures "moved lower...extending the declines seen since the summer" reflecting "concerns about global growth and the risk of building disinflationary pressures"
