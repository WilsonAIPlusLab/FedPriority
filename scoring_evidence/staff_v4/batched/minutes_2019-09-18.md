Treasury yields "posted very large declines." 10y-2y spread turned negative for first time since 2007. Equities decreased slightly amid "heightened volatility." Dollar "slightly higher." Repo market crisis on Sep 16-17 — EFFR exceeded target range.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.4%, core 1.6%. "Core PCE price inflation moved down to 1.6 percent." Average monthly change faster recently — "suggesting soft readings earlier were transitory." Trimmed mean 2%. AHE 3.2%. Below target, persistent miss. Score: 42.

But medium-term forecast "a bit weaker." Potential output revised up (BEA revisions) — "resource utilization were less tight than previously assumed." Score: 52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.7%. Payrolls "solid pace" but "slower rate than in the first half." LFPR and E-P "moved up." AHE 3.2%. Preliminary benchmark revision indicated payrolls "notably slower" than published. Score: 54.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Consumer spending "rising solidly." But business investment "looked to be declining further." Residential investment "seemed to be picking up a little." Forward indicators for business investment "generally pointed to continued softness." Score: 45.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "decreased slightly" amid heightened volatility — down 6% in early Aug then recovered. Spreads: IG "widened modestly," HY "little changed." Treasury yields "decreased, on net." Dollar "slightly higher." Score: 45.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions...remained stable until the Monday before the September FOMC meeting." EFFR "rose to the top of the target range on September 16 and exceeded it by 5 basis points on September 17." Significant tightening from corporate tax payments and Treasury settlements. Desk open market operations deployed. Score: 62.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG spreads "widened modestly." HY "little changed." Financing "remained accommodative." Credit quality "weakened slightly." Score: 47.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities decreased slightly. VIX "little changed, on net" after sharp increase in early August. Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans "ticked up." Financing "remained accommodative." But business leverage "at or near record-high levels" per earlier assessment. Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index slightly higher." Appreciated against EME currencies. Score: 52.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based "little changed." TIPS compensation "declined on net." Score: 46.
