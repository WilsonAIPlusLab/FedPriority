# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2014-03-19

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: March 2014. Chair Yellen's first meeting as chair. Harsh winter weather depressed activity. UE steady at 6.7%. Inflation ~1.25% y/y. Staff revised down near-term GDP (weather). Tapering continued ($10B/meeting). EME concerns easing. Ukraine crisis emerging. Forward guidance shift to qualitative. Dollar depreciated slightly.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "about 1¼ percent over the 12 months ending in January"; core PCE "increased just a bit more than 1 percent"; "increases in nominal wages remained subdued"; compensation per hour "increased slightly"; staff: "inflation would stay below the Committee's longer-run objective of 2 percent over the next few years"
- Direction: Well below neutral — inflation persistently below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic growth slowed early this year, likely only in part because of the temporary effects of the unusually cold and snowy winter weather"; staff "lowered its projection for near-term output growth"; but "continued to project that real GDP would expand at a faster pace"; "slow reduction in resource slack"
- Direction: Below neutral — weather-dampened but underlying trajectory improving.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 34 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "6.7 percent in February"; "labor force participation rate...increased, on net"; part-time for economic reasons "lower in February than...late last year"; long-duration unemployment "lower"; but payroll employment "at a slower pace than in the fourth quarter"; "rate of job openings stepped down"
- Direction: Below neutral — mixed signals but gradual improvement.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "increased a little, on net"; weather depressed activity; housing "appeared to soften"; starts "lower in January and February"; but nominal retail sales "rose at a faster rate in February"; consumer sentiment "remained above its average last fall"; E&S growth "stepped up in the fourth quarter"
- Direction: Below neutral — weather-dampened; underlying demand moderate.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 47 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "some gains in labor productivity"; "unit labor costs declined a little" — implies modest productivity growth; staff "lowered slightly the assumed pace of potential output growth"

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 47 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "U.S. financial conditions remained supportive of growth"; "expected path of the federal funds rate was little changed"; "longer-term yields on Treasury securities edged down"; "equity prices rose"; "speculative-grade corporate bond spreads narrowed"; dollar "depreciated slightly"
- Direction: Below neutral — accommodative conditions.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Short/intermediate yields "little changed"; longer-term yields "edged down"; "waning of flight-to-quality demands" offset by weaker data; calm conditions.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "speculative-grade corporate bond spreads narrowed"; bond issuance "rebounded in February"; C&I loan growth "increased over the period"; leveraged loans "continued at a brisk pace"; CRE loans "advanced at a solid pace"
- Direction: Below neutral — narrow spreads, robust credit flows.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad stock price indexes rose over the intermeeting period"; "boosted by a solid finish to the corporate earnings season"; supported by "broad increase in investors' willingness to take riskier positions"

**BANKING_STRESS: 42 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Financial conditions supportive; CRE lending expanding; credit standards easing; but no specific bank health metrics discussed in detail.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 47 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "depreciated slightly"; "most foreign currencies appreciated against the dollar as flight-to-safety flows reversed"; but renminbi depreciated.
- Direction: Below neutral — modest depreciation.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 23 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core PCE "just a bit more than 1 percent"; food "only edged up"; goods inflation very subdued.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 28 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Services inflation subdued; overall core just above 1%.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near- and longer-term inflation expectations from the Michigan survey were little changed"; but "inflation compensation based on Treasury inflation-protected securities also declined somewhat"
