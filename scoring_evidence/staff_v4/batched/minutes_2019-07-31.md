# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2019-07-31

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: July 2019. 25bp cut to 2.00-2.25% (first "insurance" cut). GDP moderate Q2. PCE 1.4%, core 1.6%. UE 3.7%. AHE 3.1%, ECI 2.6%. Business investment "edged down." Trade policy concerns weighing on investment. Equities increased. Spreads narrowed. VIX decreased. Treasury yields "little changed." Dollar "modestly lower." Asset valuations "notable." Business leverage "high." Leveraged loan market — share to risky borrowers at "record high."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.4%, core 1.6%. "Core PCE price inflation moved down to 1.6 percent." Average monthly change in Q2 faster than Q1 — "suggesting that some of the soft inflation readings early in the year were transitory." Trimmed mean 2%. AHE 3.1%, ECI 2.6%. Below target. Score: 42.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "increased at a moderate rate in the second quarter." Staff: GDP "forecast to expand at a rate a little above...potential output growth in 2019 and 2020 and then slow to a pace slightly below potential." UE "projected to be roughly flat." Risks tilted downside. Score: 53.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.7% — "historically low level." Payrolls "solid rate." AHE 3.1%, ECI 2.6%. LFPR moved up. "Labor market conditions remained strong." Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real consumer expenditures rose briskly in the second quarter." But business investment "edged down" — structures declined, equipment rose modestly. Residential investment "declined again." Trade concerns weighing on investment. Score: 47.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities increased. Spreads narrowed. VIX decreased. Treasury yields "little changed." Dollar "modestly lower." Communications "more accommodative than anticipated." Score: 38.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in domestic short-term funding markets remained fairly stable." Repo rates "elevated" around quarter-end, SOFR 8bp above IOER. EFFR "well within the target range." Score: 42.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate bond spreads narrowed. Leveraged loan issuance moderate. Financing "remained accommodative." Credit quality "showed signs of stabilization." Score: 42.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. "Asset valuation pressures to be notable in a number of markets." Leveraged loan market risk appetite elevated — "share of new loans to risky borrowers was at a record high." Score: 40.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Financial institutions were viewed as resilient." But business leverage "high." "Buildup in nonfinancial business-sector debt as a factor that could amplify adverse shocks." Private equity credit "continued to grow." "Some signs of rising leverage." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "modestly lower." Score: 48.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based "little changed." Inflation compensation "rose modestly." Score: 47.
