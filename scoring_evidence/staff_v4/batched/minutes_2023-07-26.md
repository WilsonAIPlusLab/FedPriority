Staff no longer projects recession — "no longer judged that the economy would enter a mild recession." VIX near 25th percentile. Financial vulnerabilities "notable." Dollar declining.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 3.8%, core 4.6%. Trimmed mean 4.6%. CPI 3.0%, core 4.8%. AHE 4.4% (down from 5.4%). "Consumer price inflation continued to show signs of easing but remained elevated." Staff expects further step-down in H2. Score: 60.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "rose at a moderate pace over the first half." Staff no longer projects recession. But "real GDP growth in 2024 and 2025 would run below...potential output growth." Near potential. Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6%. Payrolls "slowest average monthly increase since the recovery began" but "robust compared with those seen before the pandemic." Job openings fell to lowest since Mar 2021 but "remained well above pre-pandemic levels." AHE 4.4%. "Imbalance...gradually diminishing." Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PDFP "appeared to have decelerated in the second quarter." Manufacturing rose. Score: 42.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities increased. VIX "near the 25th percentile." Corporate spreads "narrowed moderately." But "financing conditions continued to be generally restrictive, and borrowing costs remained elevated." July SLOOS: tightened C&I and CRE standards further. Score: 52.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in domestic short-term funding markets remained generally stable." ON RRP take-up "declined notably — about $390 billion." Treasury bill auctions "met with robust demand." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG and HY spreads "narrowed moderately." Leveraged loan issuance "remained limited." CMBS "ticked down." Credit quality "solid" but CRE office sector deteriorating. Score: 45.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. VIX at 25th percentile. Bank stocks recovered — large banks "fully recovered" from SVB declines. Staff: "asset valuation pressures remained notable" — property valuations "high relative to fundamentals," house prices "started to rise again." Score: 40.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards further. C&I balances "contracted modestly in the second quarter." CRE loan growth moderating. Core deposits "held steady." Wholesale borrowing falling since May. "Overall banking system retained ample loss-bearing capacity" but some banks had "sizable declines in fair value." Leverage in financial sector "notable." Funding risks "notable." Score: 55.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Staff's trade-weighted broad dollar index declined moderately." Dollar weakening. Score: 45.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Measures of short-term inflation expectations had moved down alongside actual inflation but remained above pre-pandemic levels." Medium-to-longer-term "in the range seen in the decade before the pandemic." Score: 50.
