## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 43 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence:** "Inflation remained subdued in recent months." "consumer prices rose slightly less in the twelve months ended in November." "labor compensation had been climbing more slowly than last year." Staff forecast: "Core price inflation was projected to rise somewhat...that would increasingly outpace even continued rapid productivity growth."
**Center-of-gravity:** Currently subdued; core declining; compensation decelerating. Staff forecasts eventual acceleration.
**Direction confirmation:** Below neutral.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 60 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence:** "continued strong expansion of economic activity." Staff forecast: "expansion would gradually moderate from its currently elevated pace to a rate around or perhaps a little below the growth of the economy's estimated potential."
**Center-of-gravity:** "Currently elevated pace" clearly above potential.
**Direction confirmation:** Above neutral. Bright-line satisfied.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 69 +/-4 [High confidence]
**Evidence:** "very tight labor markets." UE "4.1 percent." "Nonfarm payroll employment rose substantially further in October and November."
**Center-of-gravity:** At cycle-low UE of 4.1%, robust job gains, "very tight."
**Direction confirmation:** Well above neutral.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 62 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence:** "Consumer demand was particularly robust." "Total nominal retail sales rose appreciably." "business fixed investment remained on a strong upward trend." "Housing activity...continued at a high level."
**Center-of-gravity:** Robust consumer spending, strong investment uptrend, elevated housing.
**Direction confirmation:** Above neutral.
