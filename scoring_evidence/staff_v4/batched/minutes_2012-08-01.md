# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2012-08-01

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: August 2012. Economy expanding slowly. UE 8.2% unchanged. European crisis ongoing but ECB signaling further action. Oil prices declining. Consumer spending slowing. QE3 being anticipated. Maturity extension program continued.

**Directional signals**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "consumer price inflation was markedly lower than in the first quarter." Core "increased more moderately." "nominal wage gains remained restrained." Staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014." Below target.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: "economic activity increased at a slower pace in the second quarter." Staff: "substantial margin of slack in labor and product markets only slowly." "unemployment rate was expected to remain elevated at the end of 2014." Below potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 8.2%. "rate of long-duration unemployment stayed elevated." "Indicators of job openings and firms' hiring plans were generally subdued." Weak.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "increased at a slower rate." E&S "rose in the second quarter at a faster pace." But forward indicators "suggested that increases in outlays for business equipment would slow." Housing "generally improved further." Consumer sentiment "more downbeat." Below trend.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not specifically discussed.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: CRE "remained somewhat strained." Mortgage refinancing "remained relatively muted." But corporate debt issuance "strong." Funding markets "stable." C&I loans "advanced further." Mixed.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: 10-year "declined to a historically low level." Flight to safety and policy expectations. No dysfunction.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Yields on investment- and speculative-grade corporate bonds fell further...approaching record lows." Spreads "narrowed." Very favorable.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad indexes of U.S. equity prices rose somewhat." Option-implied volatility "rose slightly." Modest gains.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Bank CDS "moved lower on net." C&I loans expanding. Bank credit and loans expanding. Lending standards easing. Improving.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "changed little, on net." Flat.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 30 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "consumer price inflation was markedly lower than in the first quarter"; core "increased more moderately"; "nominal wage gains remained restrained"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2014"
- Direction: Below neutral — declining inflation with restrained wages.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity increased at a slower pace"; "substantial margin of slack"; "unemployment rate was expected to remain elevated at the end of 2014"
- Direction: Below neutral — slowing growth with wide persistent slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 27 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "8.2 percent"; "rate of long-duration unemployment stayed elevated"; "Indicators of job openings and firms' hiring plans were generally subdued"
- Direction: Well below neutral — labor market stalled at elevated UE.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 38 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "increased at a slower rate"; E&S "rose at a faster pace" but forward indicators point to slowing; housing "improved further"; consumer sentiment "more downbeat"
- Direction: Below trend — mixed signals with slowing consumption.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 50 ±10 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not discussed.
- Direction: Neutral.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CRE "remained somewhat strained"; mortgage refinancing "muted" despite low rates; underwriting "tight"; but funding markets "stable"; corporate issuance "strong"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — residual tightness in CRE/mortgage despite easing corporate conditions.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 38 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: 10-year yields at "historically low level"; safe-haven demand; no market dysfunction.
- Direction: Below neutral — orderly markets with strong flight-to-safety demand.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Corporate bonds "approaching record lows"; spreads "narrowed"; leveraged loan market "solid"; C&I loans "advanced further"
- Direction: Below neutral — very favorable credit conditions.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 52 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad indexes of U.S. equity prices rose somewhat"; "Valuations for broad asset classes did not appear stretched"
- Direction: Near neutral — modest gains, not stretched.

**BANKING_STRESS: 50 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS "moved lower on net"; bank credit "continued to expand modestly"; C&I loans "robust"; lending standards "eased somewhat"; CRE/home equity still declining.
- Direction: Neutral — improving balance but CRE still strained.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "changed little, on net"
- Direction: Neutral.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 32 ±8 [Low confidence]**
