# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2021-03-17

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: March 2021. GDP expanding faster than Q4 2020 but not yet at pre-pandemic level. UE 6.2%. PCE 1.5%. ARP enacted ($1.9T). Treasury yields rose markedly — Feb 25 liquidity episode. Staff projects "substantial" GDP growth. Inflation expected to "transitorily move above 2%." Supply bottlenecks emerging.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.5%, core 1.5%. Trimmed mean 1.7%. ECI 2.6% (below pre-pandemic). Median wage 3.2% (below pre-pandemic). "Inflation was forecast to be temporarily boosted this year by...production bottlenecks and supply constraints." Still held down by slack. Score: 30.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanding...at a pace that was faster than in the fourth quarter" but "had likely not yet returned to the level seen before the onset of the pandemic." Staff projects "substantial" growth with unemployment declining "to historically low levels." Still below potential. Score: 37.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 6.2%. Payrolls "increased solidly." "Retraced somewhat more than half of the losses." LFPR edged down. ECI 2.6%, median wage 3.2% — both below pre-pandemic. Score: 34.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: PCE "expanded strongly in January." Housing starts/sales "well above pre-pandemic levels." Equipment/intangibles "continuing to increase." Nonresidential structures "remained weak." IP rose solidly in Jan, fell in Feb (weather). Bottlenecks in materials, labor, transportation. Score: 42.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Investor sentiment improved." Equities rose. HY spreads narrowed. "Financing conditions for businesses with access to capital markets and households with high credit scores remained broadly accommodative, but conditions stayed tight for other borrowers." Feb 25 Treasury liquidity episode but recovered. Score: 45.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." CP/CD near historically low levels. No take-up at emergency facilities. BUT "On February 25, yields rose especially sharply. Market depth became thin, and bid-ask spreads widened, but Treasury market liquidity gradually recovered." Score: 40.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG spreads "roughly unchanged," HY spreads narrowed. "Corporate bond defaults declined...to levels substantially below their 2019 average." Upgrades modestly outpaced downgrades. Default expectations at pre-pandemic. Score: 38.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities rose. VIX "moved lower on balance." Cyclicals outperformed. "Financing conditions...supported by low interest rates and high equity valuations." No explicit financial stability assessment in this meeting's text. Score: 42.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans "continued to decline." Small business conditions "remained tight." Demand depressed. Long-term delinquencies "elevated relative to recent years but...significantly below those experienced during the Global Financial Crisis." Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Broad dollar index rose modestly, with more notable appreciation against currencies of emerging market economies." Dollar strengthening. Score: 53.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Surveys "little changed on balance." SPF edged up "returning to its pre-pandemic level." TIPS "increased moderately, on net, continuing the trend." Staff index "at about the same level." Score: 50.
