## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript -- staff economic briefing. January/February 2017 meeting.

**Era context**: February 2017. One rate hike (Dec 2015), second hike (Dec 2016). New administration inaugurated. UE ~4.8%. Core PCE ~1.7%. Dollar appreciated ~20% since mid-2014. Post-election equity rally. Business/consumer sentiment surging. Fiscal stimulus expectations. Oil stabilizing.

**Staff speakers**:
- MR. WILCOX: U.S. outlook -- GDP forecast "essentially unrevised from December." Economy "slightly above the level of potential." UE expected to be "just above" natural rate (4.9%). Core PCE 1.6-1.7%. ECI "rose 2.2 percent." Consumer sentiment surged post-election but staff "not built in much of a reaction." Fiscal placeholder for tax cut.
- MR. KAMIN: International outlook -- foreign GDP near potential (~2.5%). Dollar appreciated ~20%, rising ~4% more. Net export drag diminishing. Downside risks "less worried than we were last year."

- MR. POTTER: Markets -- risk asset prices largely range-bound. Dollar appreciated. Uncertainty high re: fiscal/trade policy. EM stable.

**Dimensional analysis**:

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Economy "slightly above the level of potential at present." UE near 4.8-4.9%. "Aggregate demand will modestly outpace aggregate supply." Score: 51 -- slightly above potential.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE ~4.8%, just above natural rate of 4.9%. Payrolls solid. ECI rose 2.2%. Participation rate showing "modest upturn." NFIB confidence at 12-year high. Score: 50 -- at full employment.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "increased at a 2.5 percent annual rate" Q4. Consumer sentiment surging post-election. Manufacturing improving. Business investment mixed. Score: 50 -- at trend, sentiment boosted.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not extensively discussed. Wilcox references "weak underlying productivity growth." Score: 32 -- below trend.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Potter: financial asset prices largely range-bound. Equities up 9% since election. Credit spreads "narrowed substantially." Dollar 6% above pre-election. Rates still low. Very accommodative overall. Score: 40 -- accommodative.

7. **TREASURY_STRESS**: Treasury yields rose ~65bp since November, "split about evenly between higher inflation expectations and higher real yields." Orderly move, not stress. Score: 38 -- normal.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Lehnert: HY spreads "returned to their post-crisis lows." Far-forward spreads declined. "Increase in risk appetite." Score: 30 -- very narrow.

9. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Lehnert: P/E at "post-crisis highs." VIX at "lowest level in over two years." Valuation pressures raised to "notable." "Low level of the VIX is a little hard to understand in light of the uncertainty." Score: 62 -- elevated valuations.

10. **BANKING_STRESS**: Lehnert: Bank capital ratios "increased for all U.S. banks." "Notable increase in price-to-book ratios." "Leverage in the overall financial system isn't increasing." Score: 28 -- low stress.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Kamin: Dollar appreciated ~20% since mid-2014. "Rising only about 4 percent more." Net export drag diminishing. Potter: broad trade-weighted dollar ~6% below January peak. Score: 60 -- notably strong.

**Bright-line checks**: OUTPUT_GAP 51 slightly above potential -- consistent. CREDIT_SPREADS 30 narrow -- correct (narrow = below 50). BANKING_STRESS 28 healthy -- correct. DOLLAR_STRENGTH >50 appreciation -- correct.
