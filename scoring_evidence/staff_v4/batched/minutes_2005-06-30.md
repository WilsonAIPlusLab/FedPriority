# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2005-06-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, unsegmented. **Era context**: June 2005. Fed funds raised to 3.25%. Economy expanding moderately. Housing robust. Core CPI slowing recently. Oil prices rising.

Staff: Economy expanding at moderate pace. Forecast: growth "just above potential." Core CPI slowed in Apr-May. Financial institutions "in a comfortable capital position." Speculative-grade spreads "contracted notably."
