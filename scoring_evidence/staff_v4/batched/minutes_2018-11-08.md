- BANKING_STRESS: Vulnerabilities "moderate on balance." Asset valuation "elevated." Financial leverage/transformation "low." Household leverage "low-to-moderate." Nonfinancial business leverage "elevated" with "pickup in the issuance of risky debt and the continued deterioration in underwriting standards on leveraged loans." Score ~37.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated against most advanced and emerging market currencies." EME funds experienced outflows. Score ~57.
