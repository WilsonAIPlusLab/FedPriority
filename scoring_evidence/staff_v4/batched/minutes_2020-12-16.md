PCE 1.2%, core 1.4%. Vaccine news boosted sentiment. Equities "increased." Corporate spreads "narrowed markedly." Dollar "depreciated against most currencies." Short-term funding "stable." Staff: GDP growth "well above the rate of potential" over medium term. Inflation to "overshoot 2 percent by a moderate amount" beyond 2023.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.2%, core 1.4%. "Notably below the rates seen in early 2020." Trimmed mean 1.7%. "Reflecting substantial margins of labor- and product-market slack." Staff: inflation to gradually increase, "overshoot 2 percent by a moderate amount" beyond 2023. Score: 35.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "continuing to recover...but at a more moderate pace." "Level of real GDP remained well below its level at the start of 2020." Recovery slowing — "pandemic resurgence and increased social-distancing restrictions." Staff revised up GDP for 2021 on vaccine news. Score: 35.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 6.7%. "Employment continued to be well below its level at the beginning of the year." Payrolls "more moderate than the substantial third-quarter pace." "Changes in the composition of the workforce" distorting wage measures (lower-wage workers lost jobs disproportionately). Score: 30.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real PCE rose strongly in October" but pace more moderate. "Nominal retail sales...stepped down" in November. Consumer sentiment "moved somewhat lower." Business investment "expanding further." Housing "advanced further" on low rates. Score: 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "increased." Spreads "narrowed markedly across the credit spectrum, most notably for debt securities of the lowest credit quality firms." VIX "declined." Vaccine news and election resolution boosted sentiment. Financing conditions "remained accommodative and eased a bit further." Score: 32.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." CP/CD spreads "around pre-pandemic levels." Repo market "muted year-end funding pressure amid ample liquidity conditions." Fed repos outstanding "remained at zero." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Spreads "narrowed markedly across the credit spectrum." Corporate defaults "continued to decline." Leveraged loan defaults "largely unchanged...at somewhat elevated levels." Bond issuance "solid." Market default expectations "fell slightly but remained above pre-pandemic levels." Score: 48.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities increased. "High equity valuations." "Historically low corporate bond yields." But valuations supported by policy accommodation. Score: 38.

**BANKING_STRESS**: C&I loans "contracted" (PPP forgiveness, paydowns). "Standards for small businesses tightened." Small business delinquencies "relatively elevated." CRE bank loan growth "remained weak." But banks well-capitalized. Score: 52.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "depreciated against most currencies." "Broad dollar depreciation." Score: 45.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Survey-based "edged up." Michigan 5-10yr "slightly above its level in October." TIPS inflation compensation "rose above their pre-pandemic levels." Score: 48.
