## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, unsegmented. **Era context**: May 2005. Fed funds raised to 3%. Growth moderating unexpectedly in Q1. Energy prices elevated. Core inflation ticking up.

Staff: Growth moderated from rapid pace of H2 2004. Core measures "somewhat elevated." Staff forecast: growth "a bit above potential" but unemployment expected to hold around current level. Inflation projected to edge lower.
