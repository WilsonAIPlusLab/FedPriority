**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6% — "near its lowest level in 50 years." Payrolls "stepped down in May." AHE 3.1%. "Labor market conditions remained strong." But "some measures...had softened a bit." Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Consumer spending appeared to have picked up." Business investment "soft." "Trade uncertainty and slower growth in global activity continued to weigh." Residential investment "continued to be weak." Score: 46.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Treasury yields "fell considerably." Equities declined. 10y-3m spread "bottom decile." VIX increased. Dollar "rose a bit." Trade tensions driving risk-off. Score: 48.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were stable." EFFR "remained equal to the IOER rate." Score: 38.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Corporate bond spreads were little changed." Leveraged loan issuance "picked up." Score: 45.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities declined. Spreads roughly unchanged. "Forward P/E ratio declined modestly but remained above its historical median." Score: 42.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Financial-sector leverage remained moderate." Nonfinancial business debt "close to historical highs." CRE valuations "remained stretched." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "rose a bit." Score: 52.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Survey-based measures of longer-run...were little changed." Market-based "decreased." "Inflation expectations or the inflation risk premium appeared to have declined." Score: 45.
