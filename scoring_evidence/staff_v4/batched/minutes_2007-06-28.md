10. **BANKING_STRESS**: "Investors seemed to reevaluate the risks." Subprime concerns but system sound. "Acquisition-related financing continued." Direction: below neutral. Score ~40.

11. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Foreign exchange value of the dollar against other major currencies was little changed." Direction: stable. Score ~48.
