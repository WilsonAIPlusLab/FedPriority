# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 1997-12-16

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript — staff presentations. Key speakers: MR. PRELL (domestic), MR. TRUMAN (international/Asia). December 1997. Asian crisis deepening further — Korea, Japan, potential spread to China/HK.

**Era context**: December 1997. Fed at 5.50%. Prell: CPI "total and core increased just 0.1 percent in November" — very low inflation; "twelve-month changes were 1.8 percent for the total CPI and 2.2 percent for the core." Staff has removed prior assumption of funds rate increase. Wages: "forecast that wages and total compensation will decelerate slightly"; "pressure is building in this labor market pot to where the lid could blow off. But, for better or worse, our forecast has not been shaped by this visceral sensation." Stock market: staff eliminated funds rate hike assumption; expects "more gradual" ~15% decline. Investment surveys "extremely upbeat" — NAPM 15% increase planned; NFIB "extremely upbeat." Truman: much weaker outlook for Asia; dollar stronger vs yen (15%+); weaker EMU forecast. Oil prices down $2/barrel.

**Dimension-by-dimension assessment**:

1. **INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Prell: CPI 0.1% Nov; twelve-month at 1.8% total, 2.2% core; "right on" forecast. Very low. Score ~38.

2. **OUTPUT_GAP**: Economy still strong; investment extremely upbeat; but Asia drag increasing. Score ~56.

3. **LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Prell: "pressure is building in this labor market pot to where the lid could blow off"; wages "creeping up"; finding/retaining workers "major problem." Score ~64.

4. **CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Prell: investment surveys "extremely upbeat" — 15% increase expected; NFIB survey strong; but staff expects deceleration from international headwinds. Score ~57.

5. **PRODUCTIVITY**: Not directly discussed. Continuation of upward revision. Score ~56.

6. **FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Prell removed funds rate hike assumption. Credit still available. Asia widening risk premia somewhat. Score ~44.

7. **EQUITY_VALUATION**: Prell: "historically high" PE levels; expects 15% decline; "rude shock" possible from earnings. Score ~20.

8. **CREDIT_SPREADS**: Implied widening from Asia turmoil. Score ~55.

9. **DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Truman: "dollar is stronger in both real and nominal terms throughout the forecast period against both G-10 and non-G-10 currencies"; yen 15%+ weaker. Score ~68.
