# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2023-03-22

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: March 2023. SVB and Signature Bank closures. 25bp hike. UE 3.6%. PCE 5.4%, core 4.7%. Trimmed mean 4.6%. AHE 3-mo at 3.6% ann (slowing). Staff now includes "mild recession starting later this year" in baseline. Banking stress late in intermeeting period. Treasury yields plunged on flight to safety. Bank equities fell notably.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 5.4%, core 4.7%. Trimmed mean 4.6%. CPI 6.0%, core 5.5%. AHE 3-mo at 3.6% ann — slowing. CPH 4.5% (4Q basis). Staff expects 2.8% total, 3.5% core in 2023. Still elevated but decelerating. Score: 67.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "expanding at a modest pace." Staff projects "mild recession starting later this year." "Level of real output was projected to move below the staff's estimate of potential output in early 2024." Currently above but expected to go below. Score: 48.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.6%. Payrolls increased "at a faster monthly pace" than Q4 2022. LFPR and E-P "increased slightly." Job openings "remained high." AHE slowing. Score: 58.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Consumer spending "picked up." BFI growth "was slowing." Residential investment "continuing to decline." Manufacturing expanded "moderately" in Jan-Feb but new orders soft. Score: 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Two distinct phases. Early: yields rose, equities fell, conditions tightened. Late (post-SVB): yields plunged, equities mixed, bank stocks fell notably. "Financing conditions...were moderately restrictive overall." Corporate bond issuance "fell to near zero" after bank closures. VIX spiked to ~26.5%. "Impaired liquidity conditions." Score: 58.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "U.S. unsecured funding markets showed some signs of pressure." CP/NCD issuance dropped. Spreads widened for lower-rated. "Deteriorating liquidity conditions in Treasury, bond, and equity markets" but "market functioning remained orderly." Fed funds volumes "initially declined sharply." Score: 50.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Corporate spreads "rose" after SVB — "speculative-grade corporate bond spreads registering the largest moves." But "spreads remained at moderate levels relative to their historical distributions." Leveraged loan credit quality "deteriorated somewhat." Score: 52.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Broad equities "rose somewhat" on net (rate expectations drove gains). But bank stocks fell notably. VIX spiked to 26.5% then eased to 21% (~70th percentile). Swaption-implied vols "increased notably." Score: 50.

**BANKING_STRESS**: SVB and Signature Bank closed. "Regional banks with unusually large reliance on uninsured deposits...experienced larger declines in stock prices." "Pricing in financial markets changed notably." Fed, FDIC, Treasury intervened. "Concerns about profitability at some banks." Dollar index "rose moderately." Score: 65.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "Staff's dollar index rose moderately." Dollar appreciated on flight-to-safety. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Near-term measures of inflation expectations from these surveys moved down." Longer-term "remained within the range." Inflation compensation "declined, especially at short maturities." Score: 50.
