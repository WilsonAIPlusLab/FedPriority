# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2019-01-30

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: January 2019. Hold at 2.25-2.50%. GDP solid in Q4 2018, expected to slow. PCE 1.8%, core 1.9%. UE 3.9%. Government shutdown ongoing. Risk sentiment "fluctuated materially." Dollar depreciated. Asset valuations "abated somewhat." Nonfinancial business debt "close to historical highs relative to GDP."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 1.8%, core 1.9%. "Both measures were a little below 2 percent." AHE 3.3%. "Total and core PCE price inflation, on a 12-month basis, were estimated to be a little below 2 percent in December." Compensation "appeared to be rising at a moderate rate." Below target — modest downside. Score: 47.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "rose at a solid rate in the fourth quarter." Staff forecast: GDP growth "expected to slow from 2018 and to move down to the rate of potential output growth in 2021." GDP above potential in 2019-2020. Score: 55.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.9%. "Labor market conditions remained strong." AHE 3.3%. LFPR "unchanged." "Continued to observe that the labor market was tight." Government shutdown affecting some data. Score: 55.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real PCE appeared to have risen at a strong pace in the fourth quarter." PDFP "moderate gains." Equipment spending "appeared to be decelerating noticeably." Business investment "had decelerated in the second half of the year." Score: 50.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Risk sentiment "fluctuated materially over the intermeeting period." Equities declined then partially recovered. VIX elevated in December. Treasury yields "declined notably." Dollar depreciated. "On net, nominal Treasury yields, equity prices, and the exchange value of the dollar all declined." Score: 45.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were generally stable." Year-end pressures "roughly in line with historical patterns." No significant stress indicators. Score: 42.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Corporate bond spreads widened notably over the intermeeting period." Leveraged loan spreads also widened. "Credit conditions for businesses loosened" over 2018. Delinquencies on consumer loans "increased further." Score: 52.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad equity price indexes declined sharply over the intermeeting period." Asset valuations "abated somewhat." Score: 45.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Nonfinancial business debt remained close to historical highs relative to GDP." "Vulnerabilities associated with financial-sector leverage were moderate." Banks well-capitalized. Score: 48.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Dollar "depreciated against most other currencies." Score: 47.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Survey-based measures of longer-run inflation expectations were little changed." Market-based "decreased." Score: 48.
