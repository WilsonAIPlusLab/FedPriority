# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2007-08-07

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Wilcox (domestic outlook — financial turbulence effects, housing, consumption, inflation), Dudley (financial markets — subprime, ABX, CLOs/CDOs, ABCP, credit spreads), Johnson (international).

**Era context**: August 2007 — funds rate at 5.25%. Financial turmoil intensifying. Subprime crisis spreading to alt-A, prime, and corporate credit. Housing deepening. But real economy data still "remarkably consistent" with prior projection.

**Key evidence**:
- Wilcox: "Adjusted our outlook for the hostile financial environment." Took "about 1/4 percentage point off our forecast for the growth of real GDP over the second half of this year and next." Housing: "new-home sales to drop another 18 percent," "took the trajectory down 6 percent this round." Consumer spending cut for ARM resets — "4.7 million ARM borrowers are scheduled to undergo interest-rate resets." Equipment spending trimmed for "greater uncertainty."
- Wilcox: BUT "real economy seems to have entered the period of intense financial-market turbulence with, if anything, a little more momentum than we would have projected." Consumer spending "surprised us to the upside." "Could the implications be even worse than we have built into the baseline? You bet." Three alternative scenarios sketched: "residential investment drops 10 percent relative to baseline," "home prices drop a total of 20 percent."
- Inflation: "picture looks very much the same." Core PCE "edging down to a rate of 2 percent." "Favorable recent monthly readings" led to trimming forecast 0.1pp.
- Dudley: "Considerable financial market turbulence." "Problems in subprime mortgage credit have persisted and intensified." "Credit-rating agencies have begun to downgrade asset-backed securities and CDOs." "Problems in subprime have spread into the alt-A mortgage space and into parts of the prime mortgage market." "Corporate credit has been infected, with high-yield bond and loan spreads moving out sharply." "Stock prices have faltered." "The nonconforming residential mortgage market and the structured-finance product markets — especially the CDO and CLO markets — have been significantly impaired." "Concerns about the ability of some asset-backed commercial paper programs to continue to source funding."
- Dudley: "Market expectations consistent with considerable monetary policy easing over the next year."
- Johnson: "Global economy expanded strongly in the first half" — "in robust condition and is likely able to withstand the adjustment." But "abundance of downside risk."

1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: Core PCE edging to 2%. "Picture very much the same." Favorable readings. Score: 54 ±4.
2. OUTPUT_GAP: GDP trimmed 0.25pp; growth at ~2%. "Real economy seems to have entered turbulence with a little more momentum." Near but slightly below potential. Score: 48 ±5.
3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE at 4.6%. "Employment report once again showed a little more momentum in hiring." But "employment figures slightly stronger than expected at time of October Greenbook." Score: 52 ±4.
4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: Housing deepening (sales drop 18% more); ARM resets affecting 4.7M borrowers; equipment spending trimmed; but consumer "surprised us to the upside." Score: 38 ±6.
5. PRODUCTIVITY: Potential GDP revised down. "Slower pace of structural productivity growth." Score: 35 ±6.
6. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: "Considerable financial market turbulence." Corporate credit "infected." CLO/CDO markets "significantly impaired." ABCP under stress. High-yield spreads widened 150-200bp. Clearly tightening sharply. Score: 62 ±6.
7. CREDIT_SPREADS: "Corporate bond spreads widened about 150 basis points." "High-yield debt spreads moving out sharply." CDX indexes "rose nearly 200 basis points." Significantly widened. Score: 62 ±5.
8. BANKING_STRESS: "Large write-downs at some major financial institutions." Bear Stearns approaching banks for credit lines. Countrywide under pressure. ABCP markets impaired. "Difficulties banks were having selling off loans." Score: 58 ±6.
9. EQUITY_VALUATION: "Stock prices have faltered." Equity prices "generally down." Score: 55 ±7.
10. DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar depreciated further; "downward shift in current-quarter starting value of about 1.25 percent." Score: 35 ±7.

**Coherence checks**:
- OUTPUT_GAP (48) ↔ LABOR_TIGHTNESS (52): Consistent — near potential, labor still tight.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (62) ↔ CREDIT_SPREADS (62): Consistent — both showing significant tightening.
- OUTPUT_GAP (48) ↔ CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (38): Gap of 10, explained by housing drag versus strong labor market supporting overall output.
