# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-03-20

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: March 2013. Moderate expansion despite sequestration. UE 7.7%. European concerns rising again (Italy elections, Cyprus). Financial conditions "improved sentiment" but European-driven volatility. Equity prices rising. QE3 ongoing. Staff revised down near-term for sequestration.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation, excluding some temporary fluctuations in energy prices, was subdued"; core "increased moderately"; "gains in nominal wages remained relatively slow"; staff: "inflation would be subdued through 2015"

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity was expanding at a moderate rate"; fiscal drag expected; "expansion in economic activity was anticipated to slowly reduce the slack"; "progress in reducing the unemployment rate was expected to be gradual"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.7 percent"; "rate of long-duration unemployment...remained high"; "rate of private-sector hiring...were generally subdued"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 40 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Real personal consumption expenditures rose modestly"; retail sales "increased at a strong rate" in Feb; E&S "appeared to slow somewhat"; housing "improved further"; "consumer sentiment...remained relatively downbeat"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "small increases in productivity"; "unit labor costs also advanced only modestly"

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Generally favorable U.S. economic data releases...contributed to improved sentiment"; equity prices rising; CRE "improved somewhat"; but "renewed concerns about...conditions in Europe"; "no significant financial imbalances" found

**TREASURY_STRESS: 42 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Yields "modestly lower, on net"; normal functioning.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Corporate spreads "a little wider" but still at favorable levels; bond issuance "fairly robust"; C&I loans expanding; CLO issuance "reached a post-financial-crisis high"

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad equity price indexes increased"; option-implied volatility "low, at levels last seen in early 2007"

**BANKING_STRESS: 47 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Bank equity "rose, on net"; CDS "edged down"; bank credit expanding; CCAR/stress test results well received; CRE "improved somewhat"

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 53 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "appreciated against the euro, sterling, and the Canadian dollar" but broad index movements mixed. Net modest appreciation.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 32 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods moderate; gasoline prices rising.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 33 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not separately detailed.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations in the survey were also little changed and remained within the narrow range"
