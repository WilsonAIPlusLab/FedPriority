# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2010-11-03

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: November 2010 — QE2 announced ($600B in Treasury purchases). Recovery modest. UE stuck at 9.6%. IP decelerated. Core inflation trending lower. Housing "exceptionally weak." Dollar declining on QE2 expectations. European crisis resurging (Ireland).

### Dimension-by-dimension analysis:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: "Headline consumer price inflation was subdued in recent months." "The core PCE price index was unchanged in September, and the 12-month increase in this index continued to trend down." "Private-sector wage increases...remained subdued." "Employer benefit costs accelerated this year." Staff: "subdued rates of headline and core inflation during 2011 and 2012." FOMC statement: "inflation was below the levels consistent with the FOMC's dual mandate." Score ~21-25.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Economic recovery proceeded at a modest rate." IP "decelerated in August and edged down in September." Housing "remained exceptionally weak." E&S "posted a smaller, but still solid, gain." "Significant portion of the rise in domestic demand was again met by imports." Staff near-term outlook "little change." But revised up 2011/2012 on QE2 expectations. "Margin of slack at the end of 2011 was still expected to be substantial." Score ~37-40.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE at 9.6% for third quarter average. "Private businesses continued to increase their demand for labor only modestly." Workweek unchanged for three months. "Part time for economic reasons moved back up." "Long-duration unemployment continued to recede somewhat but was still very high." LFPR "lower than earlier in the year." Score ~18-21.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Real PCE rose at a moderate rate in the third quarter." "Real disposable personal income...increased only slightly in the third quarter." E&S "smaller, but still solid, gain." Housing "exceptionally weak." Nonresidential structures "about flat." Consumer sentiment "more pessimistic." Score ~42-45.

**PRODUCTIVITY**: Not discussed as standalone dimension this meeting. N/A.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets were generally stable." Libor-OIS "edged up but remained at levels similar to those observed prior to the emergence of euro-area concerns." Corporate bond spreads "slightly lower." Stock prices rose on QE2 expectations. "Credit terms applicable to several important classes of counterparties" loosened. "Credit conditions improved further" for larger firms but "more subdued" for smaller. Score ~52-56.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: No dysfunction discussed. Treasury yields declined on QE2 expectations. Normal price adjustment. Some modest stress in European-connected CP market but not in Treasuries. Score ~40-43.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: "Yields on investment- and speculative-grade corporate bonds declined somewhat more than those on comparable-maturity Treasury securities, leaving risk spreads slightly lower." Leveraged loan prices rose. Score ~47-50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: "Broad U.S. stock price indexes rose, on balance." Implied vol "little changed." "Equity risk premium—narrowed a bit but remained at an elevated level." Score ~50-53.

**BANKING_STRESS**: "Bank stocks generally underperformed the broader market amid concerns about the handling of mortgage foreclosure documents." CRE delinquencies climbing. But "C&I loans turned down in September after having increased slightly." SLOOS showed easing of standards on C&I loans. Mixed. Score ~55-58.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "The dollar declined about 3 percent against a broad array of other currencies." "Chinese authorities allowed the renminbi to appreciate slightly." QE2 expectations driving dollar lower. Bright-line: depreciation → < 50. Score ~32-36.

### Inflation sub-components:
- **INFLATION_GOODS**: "Prices of globally traded industrial and agricultural commodities accelerated considerably in October." But consumer goods prices not separately discussed as rising. Score ~28.
- **INFLATION_SERVICES**: Not separately discussed. N/A.
- **INFLATION_HOUSING**: Not discussed. Housing sector "exceptionally weak" but no inflation data. N/A.
- **INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: "Survey measures of households' short- and long-term expectations for inflation remained in the ranges that have prevailed since the spring of 2009." But TIPS 5-year compensation "increased." FOMC noted inflation "below...dual mandate." Score ~42.

### Cross-dimension coherence:
OUTPUT_GAP (38) ↔ LABOR (19): Jobless recovery pattern. FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (54) ↔ CREDIT_SPREADS (48): Coherent — both near neutral. All pairs reasonable.
