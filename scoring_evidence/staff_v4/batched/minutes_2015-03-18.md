# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2015-03-18

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented.

**Era context**: March 2015. Pre-liftoff. GDP growth moderated in Q1. UE declined to 5.5%. Inflation restrained by energy declines. Dollar appreciated further. "Patient" language removal expected. Strong employment reports. Receding concerns about global downside risks. ECB began sovereign bond purchases.

**Dimensions**:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: PCE only 0.25% over 12 months ending January. Core 1.25%. AHE 2%. ECI 2.25%. Compensation per hour 2.25%. Projected to remain below 2% in 2016-2017, reaching 2% thereafter. Score ~29.
- OUTPUT_GAP: GDP "growth moderated in the first quarter." Staff projects GDP to "expand at a faster pace than potential output in 2015 and 2016." UE to "temporarily move a little below" natural rate. Score ~44.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: UE 5.5%. Payrolls "continued to expand strongly." LFPR and E/P ratio rose slightly. Part-time "edged down." Job openings "moved up" to elevated level. Score ~43.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: PCE "appeared to decelerate somewhat." Housing "remained slow." Business equipment "expanding... at about the same modest pace." Structures "moved down." Score ~43.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: Equity prices "moved up, on balance." Spreads on IG and speculative-grade "narrowed notably." Volatility "moved down." Improved sentiment. Score ~34.
- TREASURY_STRESS: Yields "increased across the maturity spectrum." Curve "steepened." TIPS compensation "ended the period little changed." Score ~42.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: Spreads "narrowed notably, likely reflecting increased appetite for riskier investments." Energy spreads retraced much of run-up. Score ~30.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: Equities "moved up." Volatility "moved down on net." Expected real return on equities "moved down" (from staff stability report). Score ~42.
- BANKING_STRESS: Leverage "remained relatively low." Household debt "modest." But "valuations remained stretched for some asset classes." CRE valuation pressures. Score ~28.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: Dollar "appreciated against most other currencies" as US tightening expectations rose and foreign central banks eased. Score ~65.
