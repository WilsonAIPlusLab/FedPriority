# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2005-12-13

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, unsegmented. **Era context**: December 2005. Fed funds raised to 4.25%. Economy rebounding from hurricanes. Employment growing rapidly. Housing showing early cooling signs. Core inflation subdued. Policy no longer called "accommodative."

Staff: Economy expanding solidly Q4. Industrial production rebounded. Payrolls grew rapidly. Staff: output staying near potential. Core inflation moderate with energy pass-through evident. Equity indexes posted substantial gains.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 53 ±5 [High]** — "Core consumer price inflation was moderate"; "some signs of pass-through of higher energy costs." Staff projects core higher in H1 then lower.
**OUTPUT_GAP: 51 ±4 [High]** — "economy continued to expand at a solid rate"; staff: "output staying near the economy's potential."
**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 50 ±5 [High]** — UE steady at 5.0%; payrolls "grew rapidly in November."
**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 57 ±5 [High]** — Consumer spending "increasing solidly"; equipment "solid gain"; housing "brisk despite rise in mortgage rates."
