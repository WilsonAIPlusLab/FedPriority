# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-09-18

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: September 2013. Taper expected but FOMC surprised by NOT tapering. UE 7.3%. Rates rose 25bp more. Inflation ~1.5% y/y. Employment "somewhat slower pace." Housing improvement slowing (rate increase effect). EME stress. Staff revised down medium-term forecast (higher rates/dollar).

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Total consumer price inflation picked up in recent months but continued to be modest"; PCE "about 1½ percent over the preceding 12-month period"; core "modest"; "increases in nominal wages were still subdued"; staff forecasts "subdued through 2016"

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate rate"; staff revised down medium-term forecast; "slowly reduce the slack"; "unemployment rate was expected to decline gradually"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 32 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "declined further to 7.3 percent"; employment expanded but "somewhat slower pace"; "labor force participation rate also decreased"; "rate of long-duration unemployment rose slightly"; "indicators of labor market activity also were mixed"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 40 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "were flat in July"; E&S "appeared to be subdued"; housing "appeared to slow, possibly reflecting the rise in mortgage rates"; "consumer sentiment...moved lower"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "slight gains in productivity"

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Longer-term interest rates rose"; "liquidity and functioning had deteriorated in a number of fixed-income markets" (May-July); but credit flows "remained solid"; CRE "continued to recover slowly"; "vulnerability of the financial system appeared moderate"

**TREASURY_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: 5- and 10-year yields "increased about 25 basis points"; orderly but further tightening of term premiums.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Corporate bond yields "rose about in line with...Treasury securities"; credit flows "remained solid"; C&I loans "continued to expand moderately"; CRE loans "accelerated"; CMBS "remained strong"

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 52 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "equity prices were fairly volatile but ended the period modestly higher"

**BANKING_STRESS: 43 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: CDS for banks "remained stable at levels near the bottom of their range"; bank credit "declined in July and August" but specific to rate-sensitive assets; "loss-absorbing capital had increased"

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "ended little changed on a trade-weighted basis against a broad set of currencies, but it appreciated notably against the currencies of India, Indonesia, and Turkey"

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Core goods modest.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core services modest; overall core ~1.5%.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near-term and longer-term inflation expectations...were little changed"
