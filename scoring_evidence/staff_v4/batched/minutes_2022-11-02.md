**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Real PCE "rose modestly." Residential investment "dropped further." BFI growth "held back." Government purchases rose. Trade deficit narrowed. Score: 40.

Borrowing costs rising. Mortgage rates "nearly reaching their highest levels since 2002." Banks tightened C&I and CRE standards. Credit available but expensive. Score: 58.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP elevated. CP and repo functioning normally. Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: Spreads not explicitly described in detail for this period. Corporate bond yields "increased notably." Spec-grade downgrades > upgrades, "market-implied expectations of defaults over the next year increased markedly." Defaults "rose slightly from low levels." Score: 52.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "little changed." VIX "at the upper end." Staff: "real estate valuations remained elevated." "Prices of risky assets generally fell." Score: 50.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Banks "reported having tightened C&I and CRE lending standards." Business loans expanding but "at a slower pace." Small business access "continued its upward trend." Banks well capitalized from stress tests. Hedge fund leverage "elevated." "Elevated leverage at hedge funds and other nonbank financial institutions." Score: 50.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: "U.S. dollar appreciated against most major currencies." Yen weakened further. Renminbi "depreciated significantly." Score: 68.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Staff common index "little changed in the third quarter but remained above pre-pandemic levels." Inflation compensation measures "rose as well." Score: 55.
