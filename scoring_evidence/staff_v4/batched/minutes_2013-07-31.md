# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-07-31

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: July 2013. Post-taper tantrum. Yields rose another 30-45bp. UE 7.6%. Inflation very low ~1% y/y. Consumer sentiment at best since recession. Housing improving but mortgage rate increase beginning to show. Bond fund redemptions. EME stress. GDP growth modest in H1.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 27 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "unchanged from the first quarter to the second and were about 1 percent higher than a year earlier"; core "rose at a subdued rate...around 1¼ percent"; "gains in nominal wages and employee benefits remained modest"; staff judges much softness "transitory" but still subdued

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity expanded at a modest pace in the first half"; "rate of growth of real GDP would only slightly exceed that of potential output"; "slow reduction in the slack"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.6 percent"; employment "rose at a solid pace"; "rate of long-duration unemployment decreased somewhat"; but "share of workers employed part time for economic reasons moved up"; forward indicators "mixed"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 42 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "increased more slowly in the second quarter"; but consumer sentiment "rose...to its highest level since the onset of the recession"; housing "generally improved further" with "briskly" residential investment; E&S growth improved; nonresidential construction "increased in the second quarter"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 50 ±10 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Not discussed.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term interest rates rose significantly" (+30-45bp); "bond mutual funds and bond exchange-traded funds experienced large net redemptions"; mortgage rates rose; but "staff models attributing most of the increase to a rise in term premiums"; C&I lending standards "somewhat easy compared with longer-term norms"

**TREASURY_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Treasury yields rose 30-45bp; "staff models attributing most of the increase to a rise in term premiums"; orderly repricing but significant move.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Spreads between yields on 10-year nonfinancial corporate bonds and yields on Treasury securities narrowed somewhat"; but bond issuance "dropped" as rates rose; leveraged loans "continued to be strong"

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad equity price indexes ended the period higher"; bank stocks outperformed; second-quarter earnings "above analysts' expectations"

**BANKING_STRESS: 45 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Market sentiment toward large domestic banking organizations appeared to improve"; bank CDS "moved about in line with trends"; C&I standards "somewhat easy"; bank credit growth "slowed"

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 53 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "staff's broad nominal dollar index moved up as the dollar appreciated against the currencies of the advanced foreign economies"

**INFLATION_GOODS: 25 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Consumer energy prices "declined significantly"; core goods very subdued.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: Core services subdued; overall core ~1.25% y/y.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations, which remained within the narrow range seen in recent years"
