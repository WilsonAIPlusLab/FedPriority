# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-12-18

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: December 2013. Actual taper announced at this meeting ($10B reduction). UE declined to 7.0%. PCE inflation below 1% y/y. Employment accelerating. Manufacturing "briskly." Consumer spending improving. Housing slowing (higher mortgage rates). Yields rose further (5-10yr +21-34bp). Staff revised up GDP. "Valuations...broadly in line with historical norms."

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 27 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: PCE "less than 1 percent over the 12 months ending in October"; core PCE "only a little above 1 percent"; "increases in nominal wages continued to be modest"; staff: "inflation was projected to be subdued through 2016"
- Direction: Well below neutral — inflation notably below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity was expanding at a moderate pace"; staff revised up GDP forecast; "real GDP would expand more quickly over the next few years"; "slowly reduce resource slack"; "unemployment rate was expected to decline gradually to the staff's estimate of its longer-run natural rate"

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 33 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.0 percent in November"; employment "rose...at a faster monthly pace than in the previous two quarters"; "conditions in the labor market" gradually improving; but "rate of gross private-sector hiring was still somewhat low"

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 44 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose at a faster pace"; retail sales "increased at a strong pace"; consumer sentiment "improved significantly"; but housing "appeared to continue to slow somewhat"; E&S "was subdued in the third quarter"

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "gains in productivity were small"

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 50 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Rates rose further; but "Corporate risk spreads narrowed somewhat"; CRE "consistent with increased confidence"; business finance "robust"; "Valuations in most asset markets seemed broadly in line with historical norms"; "vulnerability...moderate levels overall"

**TREASURY_STRESS: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: 5-10yr yields rose 21-34bp; yield curve steepened; orderly repricing driven by economic data; no dysfunction.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 32 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Corporate risk spreads narrowed somewhat"; "Gross bond issuance...picked up"; leveraged loan issuance "rose"; CMBS "remained strong"; C&I and CRE loans expanding

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Stock prices were about unchanged, on net" but "some broad equity price indexes temporarily touched all-time nominal highs"; "Valuations...broadly in line with historical norms"

**BANKING_STRESS: 42 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Stock prices for large and regional domestic banking firms outperformed"; CDS "moved lower, on net"; "Relatively strong capital profiles"; bank credit "rose slightly"

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 53 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "ended the intermeeting period higher on balance"; appreciated against yen and some EME currencies.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 25 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "declines in the prices of many nonfuel imported goods"; very subdued goods inflation.

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 28 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "subdued increases in medical services prices"; services inflation also very low.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Both near-term and longer-term inflation expectations...were little changed"
