# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2010-01-27

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: January 2010 — recovery continuing. Q4 GDP boosted by inventory swing. E&S expanding. Housing recovery slowed. UE ~10%. Greek concerns emerging. Dollar appreciating.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass. OUTPUT_GAP (35) vs LABOR (14) gap of 21 points explained by jobless recovery.

---

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 28 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core consumer price inflation remained subdued." Staff forecasts "some slowing of core and headline inflation."
- Direction: Below neutral.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 35 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Economic activity continued to strengthen." E&S "rose robustly." But "resource slack would be taken up only gradually." UE ~10%.
- Direction: Below neutral — recovering but substantial slack.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 14 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE ~10%. "Hiring remained weak." "Average length of unemployment spells rose steeply." "Joblessness...increasingly concentrated among the long-term unemployed."
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 41 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer spending continued to trend up." E&S "rose robustly." But "remained well below pre-recession level." Housing slowed.
- Direction: Slightly below neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: N/A**

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 58 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financial market conditions remained supportive of economic growth." Funding stable. But "standards and terms on all major loan types remained tight."
- Direction: Modestly above neutral.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 49 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Yields unchanged. "Year-end pressures generally modest." No dysfunction.
- Direction: Near neutral.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 50 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Investment-grade spreads "near the levels that prevailed late in 2007." Speculative-grade edged down.
- Direction: At neutral — normalized.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Stock indexes "little changed." "Equity risk premium remained well above its average."
- Direction: Slightly above neutral.

**BANKING_STRESS: 61 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financing conditions for commercial real estate remained strained." CRE delinquencies rising. Standards tight. Banks repaying TARP.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 60 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Broad nominal index of the foreign exchange value of the dollar rose." Greek concerns and better U.S. prospects.
- Direction: Above neutral — dollar strengthening.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core goods were quite modest."

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 33 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Non-energy services slowed and remained modest."

**INFLATION_HOUSING: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Declining prices for housing services."

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 43 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Near-term "increased." Longer-term "edged up...near the lower end of the narrow range."
