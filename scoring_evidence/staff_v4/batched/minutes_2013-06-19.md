# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2013-06-19

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: June 2013. "Taper tantrum" period — Fed communications interpreted as signaling earlier-than-expected tapering. Treasury yields rose sharply (35-55bp). MBS spreads widened. UE 7.6%. Inflation subdued. Consumer sentiment at most upbeat since recession. Housing prices rising rapidly. Emerging markets selling off. Staff revised up medium-term GDP but down near-term inflation.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 28 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Consumer price inflation was subdued, partly reflecting transitory influences"; PCE "edged down in April"; "both the CPI and the PCE price index increased at a subdued rate over the most recent 12-month period"; "gains in nominal wages remained modest"; staff revised down near-term inflation
- Direction: Well below neutral — very subdued inflation well below target.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 36 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "economic activity continued to increase at a moderate rate"; staff "raised its projected paths for equity and home prices"; "still anticipated that the pace of expansion in real GDP would only moderately exceed the growth rate of potential output"; "slowly reduce the slack"
- Direction: Below neutral — slack narrowing gradually.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE "7.6 percent"; "rate of long-duration unemployment declined slightly...remained well above pre-recession levels"; "measures of job openings and the rate of gross private-sector hiring were about flat"
- Direction: Below neutral — gradually improving but still very elevated slack measures.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 43 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Real PCE "rose in April"; nominal retail sales "increased briskly" in May; "Households' net worth likely increased"; consumer sentiment "at its most upbeat level since the onset of the recession"; housing "generally improved further"; but E&S "appeared to slow somewhat"
- Direction: Below trend but improving — consumer side strengthening.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 48 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "small rise in productivity"
- Direction: Near neutral.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 52 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Financial markets were volatile"; Treasury yields "rose sharply"; MBS spreads "notably wider"; "increase in term premiums"; but "signs of upward pressures on the valuations of some risky assets"; equity prices for large domestic banks outperformed
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — taper tantrum tightened financial conditions via rates.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 48 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Nominal yields on Treasury securities rose sharply"; 5-30yr up 35-55bp; "increased uncertainty about the future path of monetary policy"; but no dysfunction — orderly repricing.
- Direction: Near neutral — sharp repricing but orderly, not dysfunctional.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 37 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "spreads relative to comparable-maturity Treasury yields edged higher on net"; but "Credit flows to nonfinancial businesses remained strong"; speculative-grade issuance "slowed after mid-May" but syndicated leveraged loans "remained robust"
- Direction: Below neutral — slightly wider but still at favorable levels.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 53 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Equities volatile but "signs of upward pressures on the valuations of some risky assets"; bank equity outperformed; but "equity prices for agency mortgage real estate investment trusts declined"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — some upward valuation pressures noted.

**BANKING_STRESS: 45 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Most market-based measures of the health of the banking sector...pointed to an improvement"; "rising levels of liquidity and capital"; but "a modest increase in risk-taking and leverage" through shadow banking
- Direction: Below neutral — healthy banking sector.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 52 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Dollar "little changed, on average, relative to the currencies of the advanced foreign economies, but appreciated against EME currencies"
- Direction: Slightly above neutral — modest net appreciation (EME weakness).

**INFLATION_GOODS: 25 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Very subdued goods inflation; PCE index "edged down in April."

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 30 ±8 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "one-time reduction in Medicare prices" contributed to low reading; services subdued.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 50 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "longer-term inflation expectations in the survey also were essentially flat and remained within the narrow range"
