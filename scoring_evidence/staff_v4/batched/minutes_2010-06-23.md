# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2010-06-23

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Post-2005 segmented format.

**Era context**: June 2010 — recovery slowing. European sovereign debt crisis intensifying. Flash crash May 6. Dollar appreciated on flight to quality. Consumer spending slowing. UE ~9.7%. Fed reestablished swap lines.

### Key assessments and bright-line checks: All pass.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 24 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core consumer price index rose only slightly...year-over-year change was lower than earlier this year." "Core goods prices continued to decline." "Hourly compensation...about flat."
- Direction: Well below neutral — very low and declining core inflation.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 39 ±4 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Economic recovery proceeding at a moderate pace." IP robust. But "expansion expected to be somewhat slower." Capacity utilization "still substantially below."
- Direction: Below neutral — massive slack, outlook downgraded.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 19 ±3 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: UE 9.7%. "Private employment posted only a small increase." Long-duration unemployment worsening.
- Direction: Far below neutral.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 45 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Consumer spending slowed. E&S "increased further." IP robust. Housing "dropped back."
- Direction: Slightly below neutral.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 60 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Output per hour...posted a more moderate but still-solid gain in the first quarter."
- Direction: Above neutral — above-trend growth.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 62 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: Funding markets "deteriorated somewhat." Libor-OIS "widened noticeably." Stocks fell. Spreads widened. European crisis spillovers.
- Direction: Above neutral — tightened from European crisis.

**TREASURY_STRESS: 43 ±5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: Treasury yields fell on flight to quality. No dysfunction. Normal functioning.
- Direction: Below neutral — orderly.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 56 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Spreads on corporate bonds widened." "High-yield bond mutual funds recorded substantial net outflows."
- Direction: Above neutral — widened on European crisis.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 58 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Equity price indexes fell." VIX spiked. "Equity risk premium increased."
- Direction: Above neutral — elevated stress.

**BANKING_STRESS: 59 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Investors' attitudes toward financial institutions deteriorated." Financial stocks underperformed. European and regulatory uncertainty.
- Direction: Above neutral.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 62 ±5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Flight-to-quality flows...led to a broad appreciation of the dollar." Dollar ended period up.
- Direction: Above neutral — notable strengthening.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 22 ±6 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Core goods prices continued to decline."

**INFLATION_SERVICES: 35 ±7 [Low confidence]**
- Evidence: "Non-energy services remained soft."

**INFLATION_HOUSING: 28 ±7 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "House prices declined somewhat."

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 44 ±4 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Survey measures...remained relatively stable."
