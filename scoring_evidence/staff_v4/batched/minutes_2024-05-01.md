## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes, segmented. **Era**: May 2024. Hold at 5.25-5.50%. GDP stepped down in Q1 from robust H2 2023. UE ticked down to 3.8%. PCE 2.7%, core 2.8%. Inflation "surprised to the upside." ECI 3-month "stepped up noticeably." Market rate path increased markedly. Treasury yields rose substantially. Equities declined moderately. VIX increased but moderate. Dollar increased moderately. Staff raised asset valuation vulnerability to "elevated."

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: PCE 2.7%, core 2.8%. "Considerably lower than year-earlier" but "surprised to the upside." ECI 3-month "stepped up noticeably." Some short-term expectations moved up. Staff: "more persistence in inflation in coming months." Score: 53.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: GDP "rose modestly in the first quarter" but PDFP "posted an increase that was similar to the solid gains seen over the second half of 2023." Staff expects "maintain its high rate of resource utilization." Output growth "roughly similar to potential." UE to edge down slightly. Score: 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: UE 3.8%. Payrolls "increased at a faster average monthly pace" in Q1 vs Q4. LFPR and E-P "both moved up." ECI 3-month stepped up. But 12-month ECI and AHE "both declined...relative to a year earlier." Speed of realignment "slowed." Score: 52.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: GDP stepped down but PDFP "similar to the solid gains seen over the second half of 2023." Score: 48.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS**: Equities "declined moderately." Treasury yields "rose substantially." VIX "increased significantly" but "moderate levels." Borrowing costs "generally rose somewhat...from already elevated levels." Dollar "moderate increase." Score: 55.

**TREASURY_STRESS**: "Conditions in U.S. short-term funding markets remained stable." ON RRP "leveled off." Score: 35.

**CREDIT_SPREADS**: IG "about unchanged." HY "increased moderately." Yields rose broadly. Credit card and auto delinquencies "notably above" pre-pandemic. CRE: CMBS delinquency rate "ticked down in March but remained at elevated levels." Nonperforming CRE "rose further." Score: 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION**: Equities "declined moderately." VIX at "moderate levels." Staff raised assessment: "vulnerabilities in asset valuations to elevated." "Valuations across a range of markets appeared high relative to risk-adjusted cash flows." House prices "elevated relative to fundamentals." CRE "continued to decline." Score: 40.

**BANKING_STRESS**: Large banks kept C&I standards unchanged; smaller banks continued tightening. CRE standards tightened further. "Fair value of bank assets was estimated to have fallen further in the first quarter." Hedge fund basis trade "remained elevated." Leverage "notable." Funding risks "notable." But regulatory capital "remained high." Score: 53.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH**: Broad dollar index "moderate increase." Wider U.S.-AFE yield differentials. Score: 55.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: Some short-term "moved up." Longer-term "little changed and stood at levels consistent with those that prevailed before the pandemic." Score: 50.
