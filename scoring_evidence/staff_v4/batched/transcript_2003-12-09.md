# FOMC Staff Assessment Scoring — 2003-12-09

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Transcript. Staff: Stockton (economic outlook, detailed inflation discussion), Johnson/Kos (international/financial). Era: December 2003. Economy strengthening. GDP growing well above trend. Capital spending accelerating. Manufacturing showing "signs of life." But labor market improvement sluggish — payrolls only +90,000/month. Unemployment 5.9%. Inflation very subdued. Commodity prices rising. Detailed inflation risk discussion.

**Key signals**: Stockton: "Interest rates are low, and risk spreads have come in... businesses and households." "Stock market has been up substantially and is expected to rise still further." "Dollar has fallen further and is expected to edge down further." E&S spending "post an increase at an annual rate" of strong pace. "Manufacturing IP was up 3½ percent at an annual rate in the third quarter" and "nearly 1 percent in November and more than 5 percent at an annual rate for Q4." Payrolls "increasing about 90,000 per month, on average." Core inflation expected same pace 2004-05. Commodity prices rising but "of minor consequence" for aggregate inflation.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 25 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "core prices will increase in 2004 and 2005 at about the same pace as this year." Core PCE ~1%. "Economic slack is expected to diminish only gradually." "Rapid increases in structural labor productivity hold down costs." Commodity prices rising but "minor consequence."
- **Direction**: Below neutral — low inflation persisting.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 38 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: GDP growing well above trend. But "projected decline in the unemployment rate to 5¼ percent by the end of next year." Gap closing but still below potential. Manufacturing capacity utilization still well below normal.
- **Direction**: Below neutral. Bright-line satisfied.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 30 ±5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "Private payrolls rose 50,000 in November and have been increasing about 90,000 per month, on average, over the past three months." "The workweek increased again last month." "Unemployment rate declined another tenth, to 5.9 percent." Improvement but still elevated.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — still significant slack.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 55 ±6 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "E&S spending seems likely to post an increase at an annual rate" — strong. "Consumer outlays soared this summer" with tax cuts. "Housing stands out" as strong. Manufacturing production rising 5%+ annualized. Inventory dynamics positive.
- **Direction**: Above neutral — above-trend spending.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 70 ±6 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "Rapid increases in structural labor productivity hold down costs." "The ability of firms to continue to extract greater efficiencies." Structural productivity estimates 2.5%+.
- **Direction**: Well above neutral.

### FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 32 ±6 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "Interest rates are low, and risk spreads have come in — creating favorable conditions for businesses and households. The stock market has been up substantially." Dollar falling further. Fed funds at 1%.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — clearly accommodative/supportive. Bright-line satisfied.

### TREASURY_STRESS: N/A
### CREDIT_SPREADS: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "risk spreads have come in." Credit conditions favorable.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — compressed.

### EQUITY_VALUATION: 40 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Reinhart: equity risk premium discussion suggests valuations not extreme. "Stock market has been up substantially." Not described as overvalued.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — no particular concern.

### BANKING_STRESS: N/A
### DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 20 ±7 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "the exchange value of the dollar has fallen further and is expected to edge down further." Kos: current account deficit discussion, dollar depreciation dynamics. Significant ongoing decline.
- **Direction**: Below neutral — notable weakness. Bright-line satisfied.

### INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 45 ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: Stockton: "inflation expectations are anticipated to hold steady or drift lower." TIPS breakevens "moved up since the summer" but "range in which it has fluctuated over the past two years." Michigan survey expectations "haven't broken out to the upside."
- **Direction**: Near neutral — stable, slight downward bias.
