# Second-pass scoring, 2002-08-13 (CREDIT_SPREADS, EQUITY_VALUATION)

## CREDIT_SPREADS
Participants' text: "The declines in equity prices had been accompanied by a heightened degree of risk aversion that had led to widened credit spreads in financial markets and the curtailment of credit availability to potential borrowers whose repayment prospects were viewed as questionable." This was a point raised by "a number of members". Offsetting: "interest costs had declined for borrowers with acceptable credit ratings", "the overall condition of the banking system remained sound with bank credit widely available", and mortgage financing "widely available at low interest rates". Some members thought the financial restraints might be "fairly limited at this point".

Rule applied: V4.1 / V4.1.1 clarification. "Widened" is direction only, and no spread level is stated, so the score goes 45-55 on the side of the move with Low confidence. The availability language is mixed (curtailed for weak credits, ample for good credits), so it does not move the score off the edge of the band. Direction check: more spread widening and tighter credit for questionable borrowers means more stress, so the score is above 50. Score 55, which is the top of the direction-only band, UNC +-8, Low.

## EQUITY_VALUATION
Participants' text: "While prices in equity markets had turned up from their recent lows, the cumulative losses in financial wealth incurred since early 2000 clearly were having an adverse impact on expenditures by households and the higher cost of equity capital was inhibiting business investment." Also "heightened degree of risk aversion", "current skittishness in debt and equity markets", and "some members" said "the possible persistence of turmoil in equity markets might continue to restrain" consumer spending.

Rule applied: V4.1.1 section 2. Equities are discussed as price moves (declines, then a partial turn up) and as concern about turmoil and the cost of equity capital. This goes beyond a pure wealth-effect mention, so it is not N/A. No participant gives a valuation view ("overvalued", "stretched", "undervalued", "depressed"), so the rubric default of 45-55 on the side of the move applies, with Low confidence. Equity prices are down, with turmoil seen as a drag, so the score is on the stress side. Score 55, UNC +-8, Low.
