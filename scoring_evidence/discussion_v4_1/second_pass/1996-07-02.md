**OUTPUT_GAP.** Direction: participants see the economy as running above a sustainable level of resource use, so above the midpoint. Several members said utilization of capital and labor "over the past couple of years would have been expected ... to foster rising cost pressures". In the policy discussion, "at current levels of resource use a continuation of rapid growth was not likely to be sustainable", and Mr. Stern cited "strains on capacity in labor and some other markets". Rule applied: bright-line "above sustainable" means the score must be above 50. Magnitude: language is "high levels of resource utilization" and "strains on capacity" but no "overheating" or shortages, and the unemployment rate is stated as about 5.5 percent with "only limited indications" of wage acceleration, so "modestly above" (51-58) fits rather than "clearly above". Polarity check: higher means more above potential, and the evidence says utilization is high and strained, which agrees. Score 57, with a +-6 band (the level is inferred from "high resource utilization" and growth-versus-potential language, not an explicit "above potential" statement), Moderate confidence.
