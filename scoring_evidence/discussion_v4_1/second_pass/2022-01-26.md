This is consistent with the bright line, since "accommodative" supports a score below 50 and nothing in the text says "elevated" or "wide". Score 40, +-12, Low confidence.

## EQUITY_VALUATION

Equities are never named. The only valuation commentary is generic across asset classes: "A few participants noted that asset valuations were elevated across a range of markets and raised the concern that a major realignment of asset prices could contribute to a future downturn." "A couple of these participants judged that prolonged accommodative financial conditions could be contributing to financial imbalances." "A couple of other participants" countered that elevated valuations might be less of a threat, citing healthy household and firm balance sheets and a well-capitalized, liquid banking sector. Crypto-assets were also raised by "some participants". Equities are not mentioned as a wealth-effect channel either.

Rule applied: clarification 2, a stated valuation view ("elevated valuations") is scored on the table. Direction: participants who raised valuations see them as elevated, which is above fair value and so more concern, but only "a few" said so, they did not single out equities, and a similar-sized group played down the threat. That points to the 31-45 band ("somewhat elevated") rather than the 16-30 band. Because equities are not explicitly identified, I score with Low confidence and a wide band. Score 36, +-12, Low confidence.
