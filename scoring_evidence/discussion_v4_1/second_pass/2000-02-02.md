Score 55, UNC +-8, Moderate confidence (one explicit statement plus indirect references).

## LABOR_TIGHTNESS

Evidence:
- "what were already very tight labor markets" (members' outlook paragraph).
- "taut labor markets" lent "an upward bias to the inflation outlook" (some members); "contain pressures in labor markets"; "several cited recent statistical and anecdotal evidence of larger increases in labor compensation"; "robust growth in jobs and incomes".
- "the cushion of unutilized labor resources having dwindled over recent years" (members generally agreed).
- Members' own projections hold unemployment at 4 to 4-1/4 percent in Q4 2000 (a projection, but it implies a current rate at or near the low 4s). Building activity held back by "shortages of skilled construction workers".

Direction: labor markets are tight, above the midpoint. Rule applied: UE about 4 to 4.25 percent falls in the 53-65 band by level, but the phrasing "very tight", "taut", "dwindled cushion" and skilled-worker shortages is the 66-80 band language. Compensation growth is rising but unit labor costs are not trending higher, so I stay at the low end of the very-tight band. Score 68, UNC +-6, High confidence (multiple independent statements).
