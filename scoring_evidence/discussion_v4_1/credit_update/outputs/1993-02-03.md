The evidence is on lending availability, which the clarifications (section 3, third bullet) allow with Low confidence and UNC of at least +-10. **This score rests on availability, not on spread wording.**

Availability evidence, mostly on the tight side but mixed:

Reading: availability somewhat restrictive for business and real-estate lending (Greenspan, Parry, Boehne and Jordan point that way, with the Chairman naming a supply-side cause), partly offset by two participants who see impediments easing and by one who sees firm lending in the Sixth District. Several of the weak-lending remarks could be demand weakness, which is why the score stays near the middle and the uncertainty is wide. Rule applied: clarifications section 3, availability bullet. Score 57 (somewhat less favorable than normal), CONF Low, UNC +-12.
