## Part 2: Evidence and scores

Document: intermeeting conference call, one agenda item (coordinated 50bp cut with five other central banks); policy vote at end of file (10 Yes, no No). Staff turns removed.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 40 (+-10, Moderate): Bernanke "outlook for inflation is not looking nearly so threatening", "remarkable decline in commodity prices"; Yellen "inflation risks have diminished markedly ... decline noticeably below my own estimate of price stability"; Stern "inflation risks have diminished" though core did not fall in Q3; draft statement "Inflation has been high". Level still somewhat high but pressure seen falling fast; score below 50 on participants' forward-leaning view of falling pressure.
- OUTPUT_GAP 22 (+-6, Moderate): Evans "significant resource slack ... only thing keeping us from calling this a recession"; Yellen "sharp contraction in domestic demand is under way"; Kohn "very sharp downturn". Below potential, deep.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 30 (+-8, Low): Bernanke "unemployment above 7 percent for a couple of years" (his reference to forecasts); Kroszner "wealth, which has to do with unemployment"; Evans "significant resource slack". Thin direct labor evidence; inferred slack.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 20 (+-6, Moderate): Bernanke "declines ... in consumer spending"; Yellen "recent data on consumer and capital spending and on housing confirm that a sharp contraction in domestic demand is under way".
- PRODUCTIVITY: N/A, not discussed.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 92 (+-4, High): Bernanke "Virtually all the markets ... are not functioning or are in extreme stress"; Yellen "complete breakdown in the functioning of credit markets ... affecting every class of borrowers"; Kohn "crisis of confidence".
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A, no participant discusses the Treasury market.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 90 (+-5, High): Bernanke "even if spreads are wide", "improvements in credit spreads"; Lacker on the striking LIBOR spread; Evans "very substantial credit crunch of unusual proportions"; Kohn "Banks won't lend to each other". Stated high level of spreads plus availability collapse.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A, equities not discussed (Warsh "reassessment of the value of every asset" is generic, not an equity view).
- BANKING_STRESS 88 (+-6, Moderate): Geithner "probability of default of the financial system goes down", government capital needed; Kohn "Banks won't lend to each other"; Kroszner "concern about insufficient capital ... what losses are going to be faced".
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 60 (+-8, Low): Bernanke "the appreciation of the dollar" among inflation-relieving factors; "less concern about the dollar if we're all moving together". One speaker, brief; appreciation noted so >50.
- INFLATION_GOODS 28: Bernanke "remarkable decline in commodity prices"; Fisher retailers negotiated Chinese import prices "all the way back to last year's levels".
- INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 35: Bernanke "decline in breakevens ... 10-year breakeven this morning was about 1.35 ... quite low"; "less concern about inflation expectations"; Plosser worries about inflation later in '09 (model dependent). Anchored to slightly low.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 15 (+-5, High): all speakers support an emergency 50bp intermeeting cut; Yellen "a larger action could easily be justified and is ultimately likely to prove necessary"; Kroszner "make it as powerful as possible"; only mild hawkish counter-notes (Fisher "not ... hesitant to tighten" later, Plosser "reluctantly or modestly comfortable").
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 15 (+-5, High): vote 10 Yes, no dissent; reservations are minor: Plosser "I do not like intermeeting cuts", Lacker questions balance-sheet usage ("pushing on a string") but "no objection", Evans "not sure it's the ideal time"; wording dispute over "materially" resolved.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 90 (+-5, High): Bernanke "extraordinary situation"; Yellen "complete breakdown"; Kohn, Kroszner, Warsh, Geithner, Evans (facilities, $3 trillion) all centre on financial stress and liquidity facilities.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 28 (+-8, Moderate): Bernanke "not planning any particular action at the regular meeting", "now at a very low level ... constraint"; Yellen sees further action likely; Geithner "may have to do further escalation"; Fisher/Kroszner note few bullets left; Fisher hints at eventual tightening later. Easing lean with data dependence.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 78 (+-7, High): Bernanke "feel rather unconfident about predicting the path of rates six months in the future because I'm not quite sure what is going to happen tomorrow"; Kohn "that tail has gotten very fat"; Evans "we're not very good at being able to say how the economy is going to go"; Geithner "substantial risk that this will get substantially worse"; Kroszner nonlinear regime shift.
