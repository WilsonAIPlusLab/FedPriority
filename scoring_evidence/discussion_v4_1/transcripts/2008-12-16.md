## Part 2: Evidence and Scores (v4.1 + V4.1.1; SOURCE_TYPE: discussion; participants only, staff content ignored)

Document note: members' discussion across a zero-lower-bound policy session, an economic go-around, a policy go-around on statement alternatives A/B (target range 0-1/4 percent), the roll call, and a short TALF/long-run-projection coda. Staff turns are removed, so some answers to member questions are missing.

INFLATION_PRESSURE: 18 +-6 High
- Rosengren: "inflation is below my target for several years... significant risk of deflation."
- Yellen: "a very real risk of deflation"; core PCE to fall "at least 1 1/2 percentage points" by 2010.
- Lacker: "negative overall inflation since energy prices peaked in July"; Plosser: Livingston CPI "just 1/2 percent in 2009"; Bernanke (summary of participants): "inflation looks set to decline significantly."
- Counterweights: Hoenig "the risk of deflation is modest"; Warsh discounts the deflation simulation. Disinflation/deflation-risk is the broad view.

OUTPUT_GAP: 16 +-5 High
- Kohn: "a very sizable output gap has opened up"; Pianalto: "substantial output gaps"; Bernanke: "rising slack"; Yellen: "longest and one of the deepest postwar recessions."

LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 15 +-5 High
- Rosengren: "The labor market is extremely weak"; Yellen: "mounting job losses", unemployment "at least 8 percent" in each of the next two years; Bernanke summary: "accelerating job losses"; Stern: payroll declines will "surpass" the last three recessions.

CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 14 +-5 High
- Pianalto: "orders have fallen off a sheer cliff"; Kohn: households and businesses "cut back on spending"; Yellen: businesses "hoarding cash and slashing capital spending"; Lockhart: consumer activity "pulled back dramatically"; Bullard: capex cut "in half or more."

PRODUCTIVITY: N/A (not discussed by members as a dimension)

FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 80 +-6 High
- Kohn: "Financial markets remain very strained... credit either extraordinarily expensive or unavailable"; Cumming: "huge spreads... lack of transactions and... nonprice rationing"; Lockhart: "dysfunctional financial markets"; Bernanke summary: "Financial conditions remain very strained, with improvement in some areas, but many the same or worse."
- Tight side of 50 (bright line satisfied); not scored above 85 because some facilities (CP) are reported working.

TREASURY_STRESS: 62 +-10 Moderate
- Warsh: "deterioration in repo market liquidity... fails in Treasury markets, the inability to predict market clearing prices"; Kohn: on-the-run/off-the-run gap "gapped out by 40 or 50 basis points... record levels"; Hoenig: "market dysfunction... including the Treasury market."
- Evidence is partly about repo/fails and liquidity strain, partly prospective risk from the zero bound; hence Moderate.

CREDIT_SPREADS: 84 +-6 High
- Kroszner: non-investment-grade "spreads have blown out, and the financing is not there"; "additional 600 to 800 basis points" on CRE refinancing; Fisher: long-term debt "7 1/2 percent", "high cost of debt and the spreads over Treasuries"; Cumming: "huge spreads."
- Stated high level, so above 50 (bright line satisfied).

EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A (only wealth-effect/financing mentions, e.g. Yellen "plummeting stock market", Fisher "very weak market" for share issuance; no valuation view; V4.1.1 rule 2)

BANKING_STRESS: 74 +-7 High
- Duke: banks' "entire banking system on an operating basis" could go "unprofitable"; "Cracks are appearing in C&I loans"; Kohn: "huge losses in the capital of intermediaries"; Hoenig: "the intermediation process is broken"; Rosengren: "Banking problems should be addressed expeditiously... recapitalization of problem banks"; Bernanke summary: "Banks continue to face intense balance sheet pressures."

DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 60 +-8 Low
- Single mention in Bernanke's summary of participants: "the strengthening of the dollar since the summer" weighing on exports. Direction only, so just above 50 (bright line), Low confidence.

INFLATION_GOODS: 20
- Bernanke summary: "falling commodity prices"; Lacker: negative inflation "since energy prices peaked"; Plosser: survey price indexes "appreciably below zero."
INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A (not discussed)
INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A (house prices discussed as asset values, not shelter inflation)
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 30
- Cumming: imputed deflation risk "6 or 7 percent in early October and is now 12 percent"; Bernanke summary: "falling inflation expectations"; Kohn: longer-term expectations "reasonably well anchored" but watch for "disinflationary dynamic." Drifting below target.

HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 7 +-4 High
- Pianalto: "provide maximum monetary policy stimulus as quickly as possible"; Yellen: "go as low as we can as fast as we can"; Evans: "substantial further accommodation"; Lockhart: "acting aggressively at this meeting."
- Rate to 0-1/4 percent plus balance-sheet expansion, agency MBS beyond $600 billion, possible long Treasury purchases (QE launch). Hawkish-sounding caveats (Hoenig, Plosser, Lacker) concern credit allocation/monetary base/inflation target, not for tighter policy.

DISSENT_INTENSITY: 40 +-8 High
- Final vote: no dissent. Fisher voted No in the roll call (reading out "Fisher No"); Bernanke later says Fisher "changed his vote to vote 'yes'... to maintain a united front." Plosser: "With some reluctance, I will vote yes."
- Fisher: "I'll be a minority of one... I'll vote against that"; Plosser: "deeply troubled by elements of the steps we are taking"; Bernanke: "slight majority in favor of alternative A" but chose B; base-targeting vs asset-side debate "considerable discussion and some contention" (Lacker/Plosser/Bullard/Hoenig vs Kohn/Yellen/Rosengren/Bernanke). Zero final dissents with two visible reluctant or objecting members and framework (governance) disagreement.

FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 82 +-6 High
- Financial conditions, market functioning, credit facilities, bank profitability and TALF dominate most turns; Bernanke: "The financial conditions are the most obvious difference between this recession and the earlier ones"; Kohn, Cumming, Duke, Kroszner, Rosengren, Warsh all devote major blocks to it. Rivals the economic assessment.

FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 8 +-4 High
- Statement language endorsed: "weak economic conditions are likely to warrant exceptionally low levels of the federal funds rate for some time" (Bernanke, Kohn, Yellen, Stern); Lacker: "near zero for a while"; Lockhart: hold "until... material and sustained improvements"; Cumming: balance sheet "at very high levels." Conditional rather than date-based, so not at the 1-5 extreme.

UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 80 +-7 High
- Pianalto: "ever more uncharted waters"; Kroszner: "a great deal of humility"; Kohn: "extremely uncertain" and "trial and error"; Fisher: "we simply do not know how... markets will behave"; Lockhart: "very difficult... to forecast with any confidence"; Bernanke: "considerable uncertainty about subsequent developments"; "all the uncertainties." Many members emphasize unprecedented conditions and model limits.
