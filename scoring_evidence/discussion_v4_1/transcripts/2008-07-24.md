## Financial conditions
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 72 +-8, Moderate.
  - Geithner: "the sets of basic pressures we've been living with now for 12 months are intensifying again and ... the scale of the balance-sheet pressures is in some ways greater now than it has ever been."
  - Mishkin: "I just don't see the stress dissipating ... I really am extremely nervous about the current situation"; "We keep on having shoes dropping."
  - Bernanke: "markets are still quite stressed"; Yellen: "we do continue to have money market stress, particularly at quarter-end."
  - Participants describe tight, stressed conditions (above 50); no participant disputes stress (Lacker/Stern/Plosser question the facilities' marginal value, not the stress itself).
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. Treasury market not discussed (Lacker's "spread between agency MBS and Treasury GC repo rates" is a TSLF fee question, not Treasury dysfunction).
- CREDIT_SPREADS: N/A. No participant states a spread/yield/premium level or direction, and no lending-standard or issuance statement beyond facility-design talk; Lacker's TSLF-fee/spread remark is hypothetical, not a market assessment.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A. Not mentioned.
- BANKING_STRESS: 72 +-8, Moderate.
  - Yellen: "the most recent bank failure is not going to be our last"; "I don't think that IndyMac is going to be the last failing bank"; "troubled banks can be downgraded and fail very rapidly."
  - Kohn: "the financial system and the depository system, regional banks in particular, are coming under increasing pressure."
  - Geithner: "marginal institutions slipping toward the point of nonviability, where ratings lag"; Rosengren: "a number of other OTS institutions that the market seems quite concerned about"; Bullard: "this environment of troubled banks."
  - Bank stress described as significant and rising (above 50), with a recent failure; not systemic collapse.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: N/A. Not discussed.

## Discussion-specific
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: N/A. No policy-stance discussion or rate vote (special-topic).
- DISSENT_INTENSITY: N/A (special-topic rule). Record of disagreement for the note: on the TSLF options vote Plosser voted No (9-1); several others (Lacker, Stern, Evans, Bullard) called the options/84-day TAF of marginal value or premature; Lacker "I don't think we should do the 84"; Bullard "I wouldn't rush into this." Extension votes on TSLF and Board PDCF determination were unanimous. 84-day TAF left to later notation votes.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 93 +-4, High. The whole meeting is on liquidity facilities, bank failure (IndyMac account by Yellen), collateral, supervisory ratings and moral hazard. Geithner: "we have not ... materially reduced the scale of risks to the financial system." Financial stability is the meeting.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: N/A. No monetary policy forward signal; talk of exiting facilities ("we are not in this business indefinitely", Bernanke; "weaning the banking system off these", Lacker) is about facility wind-down, not rate guidance.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 60 +-12, Moderate.
  - Geithner: "There is no way you can state these things with perfect clarity and conviction"; "we may face a point down the road when things get materially worse"; "hard to give anyone a high degree of reassurance that we know exactly how this will be received."
  - Mishkin: "this is deviating from most financial disruptions or crisis episodes"; Evans/Rosengren/Bullard: uncertain market reaction to the announcement ("I am not quite sure what the announcement effects would be").
  - Elevated, several participants, mostly about market reaction and facility effects rather than the macro outlook; hence not higher.
