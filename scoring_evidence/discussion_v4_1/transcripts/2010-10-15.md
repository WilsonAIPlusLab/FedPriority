## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document type: framework/communications/tools session (no vote, no stance go-around; Chairman: "leave discussions of the current economic outlook and policy situation to the regular meeting in November"; "I don't anticipate having any votes or final decisions today"). Treated as special-topic (see ANOMALY_NOTE). Vote read-out: none in file (file ends with "END OF MEETING"). Only conditions that members actually voiced are scored.

INFLATION_PRESSURE: 33 +-10 Low. Duke: "I actually view the low level of inflation as reducing the cost of doing everything possible to attack unemployment"; Warsh: draft statement 1 "expresses more serious concerns about deflation risks than I believe are warranted"; Lacker: target "2 percent or a little under"; Hoenig: current policy "likely to lead to ... higher inflation sometime down the road" (future, not current). Below target, only indirectly stated.
OUTPUT_GAP: N/A. No member states output relative to potential or slack in those terms.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 17 +-8 Moderate. Hoenig: "this 9.6 percent unemployment rate will decline towards the natural rate"; Dudley: "the very high unemployment rate would seem to be our first priority"; Evans: framework should last "until the unemployment rate is in the 6 to 6 1/2 percent range"; Lacker: unemployment at 5 to 5 1/4 not reachable "for four or five years". High unemployment, far from full employment.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: N/A. Not discussed.
PRODUCTIVITY: N/A. Not discussed.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 42 +-10 Low. Dudley: LSAPs are "a useful tool to ease financial market conditions"; since the last meeting "a rise in the equity market of about 3 percent and a decline in 10-year Treasury yields of about 15 to 20 basis points", implied volatility "at a post-crisis low point". Easing direction reported by one member; level not characterised.
TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. Only yield moves mentioned (Dudley); no functioning or dysfunction commentary.
CREDIT_SPREADS: N/A. No spread or availability language about current conditions (Rosengren's SBA remark concerns "credit availability" as a signalling goal only; Dudley's "risk premium would be higher" is hypothetical).
EQUITY_VALUATION: 46 +-5 Low. Dudley: "a rise in the equity market of about 3 percent" since the last meeting; price move only, no valuation view, so default on the side of the move.
BANKING_STRESS: N/A. Duke notes low longer-term rates are "a danger to the banks that put them on their balance sheets", and Raskin mentions excess reserves; neither assesses bank health.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 38 +-8 Low. Dudley: "The dollar has also weakened"; Kocherlakota: "one benefit of this policy is some downward movement in the dollar". Depreciation, modest.
INFLATION_GOODS: N/A.
INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A.
INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 42 (value only). Bullard: "five-year TIPS expected inflation rate is up to about 164 basis points today from a low of about 120"; Dudley: five-year, five-year-forward breakevens "increased a bit ... this just reverses the declines that we saw during the summer"; Yellen: aims to "combat any tendency that could develop for inflation expectations to decline over time". Recovered from low levels, still somewhat below target.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: N/A. No vote or stance go-around; the go-around is about framework and tools. Many members do favour more LSAPs (Rosengren, Bullard, Evans, Lockhart, Moore, Dudley, Yellen, Tarullo, Pianalto conditional, Duke) while Kocherlakota, Fisher, Hoenig, Warsh oppose them now, but the Chairman separated this from the November policy decision.
DISSENT_INTENSITY: N/A. No vote. Formal dissent cannot be measured (see ANOMALY_NOTE for the visible disagreement).
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 20 +-8 Low. Hoenig: "undesirable asset price movements, financial imbalances"; Kocherlakota: LSAP risks to dollar reserve status and balance-sheet interest-rate risk; Dudley on market reaction. Several passing references, but the attention is on framework and communication, not financial stability.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 15 +-10 Low. Kocherlakota: "likely to warrant exceptionally low levels of the federal funds rate into mid-2012"; Yellen: "we need to enunciate a policy bias or tilt with respect to future LSAPs"; Moore: "The Committee is prepared to expand its security holdings as necessary"; Bullard: guidance for continued purchases. Members discuss guidance design as part of an easing path; Warsh and Hoenig resist advance commitment. This is a design discussion, not an adopted signal.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 55 +-12 Moderate. Kocherlakota: "there is tremendous uncertainty about all of these questions"; "huge uncertainty about that expected accumulated stock of purchases"; Warsh: "none of us really know the probabilities of the downside risks"; Yellen: "There is substantial uncertainty about their effects"; Pianalto: "the uncertainties around the effects of purchases". Mostly uncertainty about policy effects, not the economic outlook.
