## Part 2: Evidence and scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 53 +-5 Moderate. Mester: "headline and core PCE inflation are at our longer-run goal of 2 percent"; Evans: "core PCE inflation rising to 2.2 percent in 2020"; Rosengren/Bostic/Kaplan: more ability to pass on costs, "upside risk". Several members (Quarles, Kashkari, Brainard) see "little signal of an outbreak". At target with modest upside tilt.
OUTPUT_GAP 60 +-6 Moderate. Williams: "economy operating above potential for the remainder of this decade"; Gould: growth "will further outstrip the economy's potential"; Mester: "growth will be above trend"; Brainard: "beyond most estimates of full employment". Above potential, so >50. Kashkari dissents in view ("I don't buy the argument that the economy is operating far above potential").
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 69 +-5 High. Mester: "unemployment rate has been below 4 percent since April"; Williams: "unemployment rate edging down slightly below 3½ percent ... lowest in nearly 50 years"; Barkin: "quits are back to 2000 levels"; Harker: "how tight that market is"; George: "Job openings ... outnumbered the unemployed". Kashkari/Powell: wage growth modest, "a bit of room to run".
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 60 +-6 Moderate. Mester: "both consumption and business investment remain strong"; Gould: "spending at such a brisk clip"; Quarles: "business fixed investment jumped 10 percent"; offset by Bostic ("households ... continuing forward with caution"), Barkin ("seeming underinvestment"), residential drag (Bullard, Harker).
PRODUCTIVITY 47 +-8 Low. Mester: "low productivity growth we've experienced over this expansion"; Clarida: "some pickup in measured productivity growth, albeit from a very depressed pace"; Quarles: investment will "nudge up productivity growth". Mixed, near trend.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 28 +-5 High. Mester: "Financial conditions remain accommodative"; Brainard: "very accommodative financial conditions ... highly accommodative"; George: "financial conditions overall have actually eased"; Williams: "quite supportive of growth". Accommodative so <50.
TREASURY_STRESS 40 +-8 Low. Participants discuss yields and the yield curve only (Kashkari: "yield curve ... flashing yellow"; Brainard on term premium; Clarida on Treasury supply); no dysfunction described, so normal-functioning range.
CREDIT_SPREADS 24 +-6 High. Mester: "corporate bond spreads are relatively low"; Brainard: "low spreads and loosening credit terms"; "leveraged lending ... low spreads and loosening terms". Stated low level, so <50.
EQUITY_VALUATION 27 +-6 Moderate. Mester: "Asset valuations and equity are elevated above historical norms"; Brainard: "relatively 'rich' level of current asset valuations"; Williams: "sky-high asset prices"; Rosengren: "new highs in the U.S. stock market".
BANKING_STRESS 38 +-9 Low. Mester: "Asset quality metrics for bank-reported leveraged loans have not deteriorated, but underwriting standards are loosening"; Powell: "U.S. banks are, in theory, ready" for Brexit; Harker: bankers' "loan growth hit double digits". Banks seen as sound, with leveraged-lending and nonbank concerns; no direct bank-health assessment, hence Low.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH 60 +-7 Moderate. Brainard: "1 percent appreciation in the broad dollar"; Williams: "a somewhat stronger dollar"; George: "further strengthening of the dollar poses a downside risk"; Mester: "upward pressure on the dollar". Appreciation so >50.
INFLATION_GOODS 58. Rosengren: "very visible rise in the cost of steel and aluminum", "little resistance to passing on price increases"; Kaplan/Bostic: tariff-related pass-through; Cleveland: "prices received is near a multiyear high".
INFLATION_SERVICES N/A. Only a forward-looking Williams remark ("nontraded domestic sector, such as services"); no current services assessment.
INFLATION_HOUSING N/A. House prices mentioned only as a drag on housing activity, not as shelter inflation.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 48. Mester: "Inflation expectations are well anchored near target"; Kashkari: "solidly anchored ... maybe somewhat less than 2 percent"; Evans: "ways to go before ... expectations have firmed symmetrically around 2 percent"; Clarida: "consistent, but just barely" with 2 percent.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 66 +-6 Moderate. Unanimous 25bp hike and removal of "accommodative" language; Rosengren: "rates will need to continue to gradually rise until we are mildly restrictive"; Bostic: "four increases this year"; Mester: "further rate increases will be warranted". Tempered by Kashkari ("move gradually to a neutral policy stance"), Bullard ("prepared to pause"), Harker (no December hike), George ("more patient approach"), Kaplan ("gradually and patiently").
DISSENT_INTENSITY 27 +-6 Moderate. Vote read-out: nine Yes votes, no dissents. Disagreement is on the future path and language: Kashkari objects to a path beyond neutral, Bullard and Harker prefer a shallower path, Rosengren and Bostic lean faster; Powell: "broad support ... consensus for removing the 'accommodative' language".
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 45 +-8 Moderate. Mester: "increased attention to financial stability risk is warranted"; Brainard: "financial vulnerabilities are indeed building"; Bostic: "systemic concerns" on leveraged lending; Williams: "borders on complacency"; Kashkari devotes a point to financial stability versus capital requirements; Rosengren and George also refer to it. Several participants address it, not dominant.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 67 +-7 Moderate. Statement keeps "further gradual increases" (Evans: "paragraph 2 language"); Powell: "inclined to continue rate increases"; Gould: "signaling a path of further steady but gradual rate increases"; softened by Powell ("no ... need to be in a hurry"), George and Kashkari urging data-dependent flexibility.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 40 +-8 Moderate. Clarida: "my uncertainty about those key parameters is elevated"; Powell: trade risks "very difficult to evaluate", r* "highly uncertain"; Williams: "Uncertainty associated with my projections is aligned with historical norms"; Mester, Kaplan, Evans cite r* uncertainty. Several note uncertainty on trade/r*/Brexit, but risks described as "roughly balanced".
