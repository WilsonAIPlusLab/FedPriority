## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 68 (+-6, High): "inflation remained elevated"; "Several participants noted that price increases over the past year were broad based"; "Some... underlying inflation appeared to be elevated"; risks "skewed to the upside"; "many... continued elevated inflation rates could begin to affect inflation expectations". Offset: energy-driven drop in headline, "most" expect step-down.
- OUTPUT_GAP 53 (+-6, Moderate): "economic activity had continued to expand at a solid pace"; unemployment "near most estimates of its longer-run level"; labor demand and supply "in balance"; financial conditions "supporting demand". No explicit above/below potential language; near potential with slight overheating tilt.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 52 (+-5, High): "labor market conditions were stable, with labor demand and supply in balance"; "labor market had strengthened modestly" (some); "a few... lingering signs of softness"; hiring and firing both low.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 62 (+-6, High): "strong business investment and resilient consumer spending"; "consumer spending had strengthened recently"; strain among low/moderate-income households (some).
- PRODUCTIVITY 58 (+-8, Low): "Several participants suggested that AI-related investments would likely increase the growth of productivity and of potential output in the coming years"; "considerable uncertainty... timing and magnitude"; gains mostly prospective.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 44 (+-8, Moderate): "Several participants noted that financial conditions were supporting demand"; "Some... financial conditions might not currently be sufficiently restrictive"; "Various... financial conditions had tightened over the intermeeting period". Net somewhat supportive, below 50.
- TREASURY_STRESS 42 (+-8, Low): "A couple... episodes of price volatility in the market for U.S. Treasury securities could adversely affect the financial system"; settlement-disruption incident but "ample-reserves regime had helped maintain the orderly functioning of money markets". Normal functioning, brief.
- CREDIT_SPREADS N/A: no spread, yield or premium wording; only AI capex being financed by borrowing and private credit activity having slowed, which is not about spread levels or credit availability.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 26 (+-8, Moderate): "high equity valuations of AI-linked firms"; risk of "broad-based repricing of assets"; "significant repricing of stocks" as downside risk (several). Valuation concern stated.
- BANKING_STRESS 35 (+-8, Low): "A couple... banks were a source of resilience... needed to remain well capitalized"; some concern about strains in exposed institutions and nonbank/regional bank lending to AI sector.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: dollar/FX not discussed by participants.
- INFLATION_GOODS 64: "materials for data centers, such as chips and steel, had registered large price increases"; tariff-affected items; consumer items (smartphones, computer equipment) under price pressure (some).
- INFLATION_SERVICES 66: "price increases remained elevated in core services excluding housing" (some); broad-based across goods and services (several).
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: housing/shelter not discussed (only "excluding housing").
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 56: longer-term measures "consistent with the Committee's 2 percent objective"; "survey-based measures of relatively short-term inflation expectations were higher" (several); many fear elevated inflation could affect expectations.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 62: "most participants supported maintaining"; "Several participants favored an increase of 25 basis points" (three dissents for a hike); "Many participants assessed that policy tightening would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline"; "Some... financial conditions might not currently be sufficiently restrictive". Hold with a clear tightening bias.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 55: three formal dissents (Hammack, Kashkari, Logan), all in the same hawkish direction; broad agreement on the economic assessment; disagreement is about timing of a hike; "Almost all members" agreed on statement language.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 42: dedicated paragraph; "some participants" on AI financing vulnerabilities, "a few" on borrowing, "a couple" each on private credit, corporate debt, banks, Treasury volatility, settlement incident; several items but each by few participants.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 66: "Many participants assessed that policy tightening would likely be necessary if inflation did not decline"; "will deliver price stability" retained; "A few... would likely help forestall the need for a steeper... sequence of tightening moves". Conditional, data-dependent tightening bias.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 68: "Participants judged that their inflation outlooks were highly uncertain"; "re-escalation of the conflict in the Middle East significantly clouded the inflation outlook"; "significant uncertainty" on AI and labor; "considerable uncertainty" on productivity timing.
