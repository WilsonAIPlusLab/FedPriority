## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 68: "overall inflation had moved up ... remained above the Committee's 2 percent"; "core inflation had also moved further above 2 percent" (participants generally); "vast majority ... increased risk that inflation would take longer to return" to 2 percent.
OUTPUT_GAP 53: "economic activity appeared to be expanding at a solid pace" (participants generally); no explicit potential/trend language; modestly above neutral by inference, Low-ish evidence.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 47: "unemployment rate little changed ... job gains low"; "most ... stabilization"; "most ... risks ... tilted to the downside"; "a few" see fragility.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 60: "most participants noted that business fixed investment remained robust"; consumer spending "resilient" (participants generally); some note energy strain on households.
PRODUCTIVITY 60: "several participants anticipated that higher productivity growth would put downward pressure on inflation"; "many" cite productivity gains supporting growth.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 42: "many" cite financial conditions among factors supporting growth; "several ... asset valuations remained elevated"; no tightness reported.
TREASURY_STRESS 45: "a few participants highlighted risks associated with the substantial participation of hedge funds in the market for U.S. Treasury securities" - risk only, no dysfunction described.
CREDIT_SPREADS 50: no spread wording; rests on availability: "some ... losses in [private credit] could spill over ... causing a broad credit contraction" versus "some ... did not appear to pose risks"; Low confidence, balanced.
EQUITY_VALUATION 30: "several participants noted that asset valuations remained elevated ... possibility of sharp corrections".
BANKING_STRESS N/A: only a couple on bank regulatory proposals and cyber; no assessment of bank health.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: not discussed (swap lines mentioned only as a liquidity tool).
INFLATION_GOODS 66: "several ... rate of increase in core goods prices remained elevated, at least in part reflecting ... tariffs"; fuel, fertilizer, shipping prices up; tariff effect expected to diminish.
INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: no clear services-price assessment (airfares/shipping and software mentioned only in passing).
INFLATION_HOUSING 40: "a few ... ongoing deceleration in housing services prices ... source of disinflationary pressure".
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 55: "Most ... longer-term inflation expectations remained stable"; "some ... near-term ... had risen"; "some" worry about de-anchoring.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 56: hold; "A majority ... some policy firming would likely become appropriate if inflation were to continue to run persistently above 2 percent"; "many ... would have preferred removing the language ... easing bias"; "several" would cut once disinflation on track; one preferred a cut.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 72: four dissents (Miran for a 25bp cut; Hammack, Kashkari, Logan against the easing bias in the statement), opposite reasoning; three members preferred more two-sided language; disagreement over guidance and risk balance.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 45: dedicated section; "several" valuations, "many" private credit and cybersecurity, "some" liquidity tools, "a few" hedge funds and swap lines.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 58: "could necessitate maintaining the current policy stance for longer than previously anticipated"; majority see possible firming; yet "several" see cuts later and the statement retained an easing bias (nine supported it); "not on a preset course".
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 62: "Participants agreed that developments in the Middle East were contributing to a high level of uncertainty"; "most" said it added to uncertainty about activity; business contacts "heightened uncertainty".
