## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 72 +-6 High: "inflation had increased further and remained well above the Committee's 2 percent"; "price pressures had become more broad based" (several); risks "tilted to the upside".
- OUTPUT_GAP 57 +-6 Moderate: "Most participants" said growth "that exceeded that of potential output" (AI investment) could add to inflation; unemployment near longer-run level. Above-potential language, so >50.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 50 +-5 High: unemployment "near participants' estimates of its longer-run level"; several: indicators "pointed to a balanced labor market"; many: labor market "not currently a source of inflationary pressures"; several: low dynamism.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 61 +-6 High: activity "expand at a solid pace", "strong business investment and resilient consumer spending"; AI capex "no signs of slowing"; lower-income households strained.
- PRODUCTIVITY 58 +-9 Low: some: AI investment "would likely increase the growth of productivity" but "considerable uncertainty" on timing; statement text says productivity growth strong. Mostly prospective.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 40 +-8 Low: "Some participants noted that broad financial conditions were supporting demand", high equity prices. Supportive, so <50.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS N/A: no spread/premium wording; only lower-income households relying on credit for spending, which is not availability or lending conditions.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 44 +-10 Low: "high equity prices ... driven by strong corporate earnings and optimism about AI" (some); stock gains supported spending (most). No explicit valuation concern; price level high, wealth-effect framing.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A: banking system not discussed (ample reserves is a policy-implementation item).
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_GOODS 68: "large share of goods ... transportation, ... petrochemical products, agricultural inputs—experiencing substantial increases"; AI-driven technology prices upward (many).
- INFLATION_SERVICES 65: "services price inflation excluding housing had declined little and remained high" (several); electricity pressure.
- INFLATION_HOUSING 40: "deceleration in housing services prices ... source of disinflationary pressure" (several).
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 55: majority: measures "remained at levels consistent with ... 2 percent"; but majority also highlighted risk elevated inflation "could begin to affect inflation expectations and wage- and price-setting".
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 58 +-7 High: all supported holding; "A few ... a case for raising"; several saw policy not restrictive; most saw scenarios where "some policy firming" warranted; many saw rate above current range by year-end; easing-bias language dropped.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 20 +-6 High: vote 12-0, "all participants supported maintaining"; some spread in scenario views and a few favored hike case, no dissent.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 18 +-7 Moderate: only brief mentions of financial conditions supporting demand and low-income credit reliance.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 62 +-7 Moderate: "most participants emphasized that they preferred not to repeat" the easing-bias language; "almost all" in persistent-inflation scenarios said "some policy firming would likely be warranted"; future actions data dependent.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 58 +-7 Moderate: "considerable uncertainty surrounded their inflation outlook"; "amid high assessed uncertainty"; uncertainty "remained elevated", Middle East conflict.
