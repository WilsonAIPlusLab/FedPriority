## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 42).** The old 28 rested on quoted statements that policy was "quite accommodative" and real rates near zero; neither is in the new input, so the "accommodative" bright-line cannot be invoked. The participants' actual picture is mixed and improving: "a number of structural impediments ... seemed to be diminishing" as balance sheets improved, banks were "in a much better position to augment their lending", and long-term and mortgage rates had declined. Against that, "there were few indications thus far of any easing in terms or standards on business loans" and "still cautious attitudes on the part of both borrowers and lenders". Somewhat loose to neutral, score 42, Moderate confidence.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, N/A).** No participant remarks on equity prices or valuation in the new input; the old quote about "broad indexes of stock prices" is not present. Score N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 53).** All members endorsed unchanged reserve conditions. "Some commented that a further easing move at this juncture might well have adverse effects on inflation sentiment"; "a few" referred to firming commodity prices; "in the view of one member" this might argue for a tilt toward restraint, but "they did not call for an immediate tightening". "A number of members" thought stimulating money growth could be counterproductive. Hold with a slight restraint lean, no easing advocates: 53.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** Both the long-run ranges and the directive passed by unanimous vote; "all of the members" favored the directive. "A number of members" preferred somewhat different M2 ranges and one or two individuals proposed alternative ranges or a restraint tilt, but these are tactical, not substantive. Near-consensus: 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 22).** One paragraph covers balance-sheet adjustment, bank capital positions, lending standards and commercial real estate values; "some expressed concern about the adverse effects on financial markets" of unfunded fiscal stimulus. Limited discussion as context, not the meeting's focus: 22.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 50).** "All indicated that they could accept a directive that did not incorporate any presumption with regard to the likely direction of possible intermeeting adjustments to policy"; the directive allows "slightly greater or slightly lesser reserve restraint". Only one member would have tilted toward restraint. Neutral: 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** "The members felt that the outlook remained subject to a good deal of uncertainty, and some commented that substantial deviations--in either direction--... could not be ruled out"; sources were the unknown fiscal program, restructuring, and bank lending. Money-demand uncertainty was also called "considerable". Elevated but not extreme, with the forecasts themselves fairly concentrated: 55.
