## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 50 -> 45).** Participants see conditions as supportive: "Participants agreed that financial market conditions remained supportive of economic growth", with short-term funding spreads near pre-crisis levels and no strains after the facilities wind-down; the FOMC statement repeats "financial market conditions remain supportive". Bank lending is still contracting and loan rates have risen, which holds the score near neutral rather than clearly loose. The bright-line rule (supportive => below 50) requires a score under 50. Score 45.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 35).** Direction is dovish but static: the Committee holds the 0 to 1/4 percent range and finishes the previously announced MBS purchases (no new easing). "Nearly all members" favored retaining the "extended period" language, while "one member" opposed it and "a few members" noted that "the risks of an early start to policy tightening exceeded those associated with a later start". Majority maintains accommodation with a small hawkish minority. Score 35.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 33).** One formal dissent: Hoenig wanted "a low level of the federal funds rate for some time" and room "to begin raising rates modestly". The vote was nine to one, the disagreement is mainly about communication and timing, and no other reservations are recorded apart from "a few" members' view on tightening risks. This is a typical one-dissent meeting. Score 33.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** Several passages address financial conditions: market functioning, absence of strains from closing liquidity facilities, bank lending and credit feedback loop, housing and foreclosures. "Members noted the importance of continued close monitoring of financial markets and institutions", and found no "widespread instances of excessive risk-taking". Hoenig's dissent rests on avoiding "the buildup of future financial imbalances". Meaningful but not dominant. Score 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 22).** Clear low-for-long signal: conditions "likely to warrant exceptionally low levels of the federal funds rate for an extended period", reiterated by "nearly all members". "A number of members" stressed it was "contingent on the evolution of the economy" and would not prevent prompt tightening if activity accelerated, and exit-strategy approaches were discussed without decisions; this tempers the easing bias from a threshold-style commitment. Score 22.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 35).** Incoming information was "somewhat mixed", and firms' reluctance reflects "uncertainty regarding future government policies"; participants flagged several restraining factors (fading fiscal stimulus, housing and foreclosure downside risks, possible foreign slowdown noted by "a few"). Yet most express a fairly settled outlook of moderate recovery and subdued inflation, with no language of unusual uncertainty. Somewhat elevated. Score 35.
