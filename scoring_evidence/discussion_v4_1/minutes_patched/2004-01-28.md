## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Participants' text has no spread or yield-spread language. The only credit-related statements are that "the general availability of financing on favorable terms" supports business capital spending and that the "financial condition of many business firms" has improved. Credit conditions are read as mildly favorable, below normal stress, with no level given for spreads. Score 38, Low confidence (below 50, consistent with FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS at 24 as a looser reading).

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Direction: lean dovish to balanced. All members favored holding at 1 percent and, as the minutes say, "took risks on the side of assuring the rapid elimination of economic slack" even while acknowledging an "overly accommodative policy stance" might eventually result. A move to "a more neutral policy stance" was seen as likely "at some point", and a few members wanted the accommodation language dropped entirely. Score 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Direction: broad agreement. The vote was unanimous ("None" against). The differences were about wording ("some differences of opinion" on "patience"; "a few" preferred dropping the phrase) and a "divergence of opinion about the most probable direction" of core inflation (many disinflation, a few an edge up). No formal dissents and no reluctant-support language. Score 26.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Direction: limited attention. Financial conditions enter as supports to spending ("accommodative conditions in financial markets"), and "a number of members" worried that valuations "left little room for downside risks"; "some members" were concerned about external debt and financial markets. No extended financial-stability discussion. Score 28.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Direction: easing-lean, data-dependent, loosening commitment. The Committee dropped "considerable period" for the ability to "be patient in removing its policy accommodation", explicitly "not to signal a policy tightening move in the near term" but to depend "on evolving economic conditions". Accommodation still intended, so below 50 but above the "low for long" range. Score 40.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Direction: somewhat elevated. "Several" stressed "considerable uncertainty" about the timing and extent of employment improvement, "some members" noted the productivity outlook "remained uncertain" and the output-gap/inflation link was "quite loose", and the committee's risk statement is "roughly equal". Mostly standard caveats with a few specific uncertain areas. Score 35.
