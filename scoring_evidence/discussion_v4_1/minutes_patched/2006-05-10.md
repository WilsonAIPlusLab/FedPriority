## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 56 +-9, Low).** Participants see the labor market as modestly tight. Some business contacts "reported continued shortages of certain types of skilled labor and related wage pressures in some occupations", while participants also noted "growth of total compensation on balance appeared to remain moderate". No unemployment-rate figure appears in the participants' views, so the former 4.7% anchor is dropped and confidence falls to Low.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old spread quote is not present in the new input and participants never discuss credit spreads. Only the availability of financing is mentioned (scored under FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS). N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old equity-price quote is not present in the new input; participants do not discuss equity markets. N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 40 +-8, Moderate).** Participants see the dollar as having weakened modestly. Several participants cited "the recent decline in the dollar" as "another factor that could add to inflation pressures", and the policy discussion listed "some recent weakness in the foreign exchange value of the dollar", though the effect of past moves on core prices "had apparently been limited". Weakening puts the score below 50 (bright-line); a modest, briefly discussed move gives 40.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 62 +-6, High).** Moderately hawkish. "All members believed that an additional 25 basis point firming of policy was appropriate today to keep inflation from rising", with options ranging from unchanged to 50bp, and several members saw core inflation at "the upper end of what they viewed as an acceptable range". Tempered by uncertainty about whether any further firming is needed. Score 62.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 14 +-5, High).** Near-consensus. The vote record lists "Votes against this action: None" and the minutes say "all members" favored the 25bp step; the only visible differences were about the range of options considered and the wording on inflation expectations. Score 14.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 15 +-6, Moderate).** Brief mentions only: "certain features of recently popular nontraditional mortgage products" could cause difficulties for some households and lenders, though households stayed "in sound financial condition overall", plus "strong balance sheets" and financing availability. Not a focus. Score 15.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 60 +-6, High).** Mild tightening bias with data dependence. Statement paragraph in the vote record: "some further policy firming may yet be needed to address inflation risks", but "the extent and timing of any such firming will depend importantly on the evolution of the economic outlook." Members were "uncertain about how much, if any, further tightening would be needed." Score 60.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 38 +-8, Moderate).** Somewhat elevated. Members were "uncertain about how much, if any, further tightening would be needed", it was "not yet clear" whether growth would moderate to a sustainable pace, and "investors' uncertainty regarding inflation prospects" may have risen; otherwise standard two-sided risk language. Score 38.
