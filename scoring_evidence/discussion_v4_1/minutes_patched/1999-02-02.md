## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (55).** Participants lean slightly hawkish in their concerns but hold policy steady. "All the members favored an unchanged policy stance." "Many were concerned that the odds were tilted toward rising inflation," and "in the view of some" the fall easings "might need to be reversed, at least in part." Against that, "the persistence of subdued inflation and the absence of current evidence of accelerating inflation were seen as arguing against a policy tightening move," and most were content with a symmetric directive. Hold with a mild tightening bias, so 55.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (14).** Near-consensus. Both votes record "Votes against this action: None." Members differ mildly on the risks: one view favored an asymmetric directive "tilted toward restraint," and "some" favored partial reversal of the easings. "All the members indicated that they could accept" the symmetric directive. Economic assessments are broadly shared, with no formal dissent and one mild tactical reservation, so 14.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (20).** Financial conditions get passing attention only. "Members commented that the market unsettlement ... had now lessened appreciably." Others cite the "abatement of market turmoil" and the risk of "possible disruption to foreign financial markets" and Brazilian contagion. The discussion is mainly about the real economy and inflation, so 20.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (52).** The signal is neutral. The directive says developments are "equally likely to warrant an increase or a decrease" in the funds rate. Members agreed to "be prepared to respond promptly in either direction." Only "in one view" was a restraint tilt warranted. The signal is symmetric and data-dependent with a small hawkish risk noted, so 52.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (62).** Elevated but not extreme. "The outlook for economic activity was subject to considerable uncertainty." "A number" said "the inflation process was not well understood and that inflation forecasts were subject to a wide range of uncertainty." Several noted the relationship between demand pressure and inflation "was not following historical patterns." Offsetting this, the central-tendency forecasts are narrow and the tone is otherwise confident, so 62.
