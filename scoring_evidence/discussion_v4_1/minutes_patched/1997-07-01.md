## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 60).** Participants see the labor market as tight but not extremely so. The old support (average hourly earnings increase, record participation rate) is not in the participants' text. What remains: the "lengthy period of fully employed resources", "low unemployment" (policy section), "jobs and incomes had continued to post sizable gains", with unemployment at 4.8% only in the directive summary. Against this, many members noted it was "difficult to find evidence of rising inflation pressures in 'pipeline' price data or the wage structure." Tight (53-65) rather than very tight; 60.

**INFLATION_GOODS (CORRECTED, 35).** Goods prices are seen as soft. The old PPI quote is not in the input. Participants cite "declining import prices associated with the previous appreciation of the dollar" and "favorable developments in the food and especially the energy sectors" lowering CPI inflation. Indirect evidence only, hence Low confidence; 35.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 58).** All members favored or could support unchanged policy, but "strongly supported the retention of a bias toward restraint", judged risks "decidedly tilted to the upside", and "some members" saw tightening "likely to be needed in the relatively near future"; "one" wanted to tighten at this meeting. Hold with a tightening bias, lean hawkish; 58.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Vote unanimous ("Votes against this action: None"), and "all the members" supported the directive. The only difference is one member who preferred tightening now; waiting was "an acceptable alternative". Broad agreement; 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 14).** Financial conditions appear only as context: "favorable financial conditions", "ready availability of both debt and equity finance", and "some members" noting a possible stock-market correction. Brief mentions; 14.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 68).** "All the members" wanted to keep the asymmetry toward restraint; the Committee called for "prompt action... to counter rising inflation" and stated it would be "desirable to tighten on the basis of early signs of potentially intensifying inflation". Current action is a hold with "wait and see", so a mild-to-moderate tightening bias; 68.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** "The outlook for inflation was subject to particular uncertainty"; reasons for benign inflation "were not completely understood"; the timing of any upturn "could not be predicted with any degree of confidence"; "current uncertain environment". Multiple passages but centered on inflation, with risks otherwise described as fairly clear; elevated band, 55.
