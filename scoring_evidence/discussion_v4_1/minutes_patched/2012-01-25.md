## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Participants say credit availability improved ("a number of participants noted further improvement in the availability of loans to businesses") while "a few other participants" said small businesses still had difficulty obtaining bank loans. Nothing states a level of spreads (no "wide", "elevated" or "narrow"), so this is direction only: a modest improvement, scored just below neutral at 46 with Low confidence. No bright-line violation either way.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Direction is dovish. Members agreed to keep the highly accommodative stance, continue the maturity extension programme, and extend the horizon of exceptionally low rates to "at least through late 2014, longer than had been indicated". "A few members" thought additional securities purchases "could warrant the initiation ... before long" and "other members" said they could become necessary if the economy lost momentum or inflation stayed below 2 percent. Against this, "one member" expected a "preemptive tightening ... before the end of 2014" and Lacker dissented. Easing is by guidance and a conditional readiness to buy, not a QE launch, so the score is 27.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** There was a single formal dissent (Lacker, 9-1), which sits at the typical one-dissent anchor of about 30. Deliberation also showed real differences: "several" proposed dropping or simplifying forward guidance, "several other participants" rejected that, "some" worried about thresholds, participants gave "a range of views" on labor-market slack, and a "couple" feared inflation. These are mostly tactical, with some economic disagreement, so the score is 38.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Financial conditions get their own paragraph. Participants noted easing stress after the ECB's three-year operation, said markets "would remain focused on the evolving situation in Europe", and "agreed that strains in global financial markets continued to pose significant downside risks". It is a recurring but not dominant theme beside the economic assessment, so the score is 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Strongly easing. "Almost all members" agreed to say that rates are likely to stay exceptionally low "at least through late 2014", a longer date-based commitment, and it was noted this "would help provide more accommodative financial conditions". "Some members" stressed the guidance is conditional, and Lacker objected to the date, which is only a small offset. The anchor for date-based near-zero commitments is 3-8, so the score is 7.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Participants cite "high levels of uncertainty among households and businesses", businesses cautious partly because of "uncertainty about the economic outlook", euro-area risks creating "possible increased volatility", and a "range of views" on labor-market slack. The projections themselves are otherwise stated as a fairly conventional modest-growth outlook, so the elevated reading is moderate: 52.
