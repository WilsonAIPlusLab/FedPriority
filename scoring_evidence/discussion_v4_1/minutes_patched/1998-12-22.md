## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** The old reading relied on spreads having narrowed and new-issue markets having reopened. Neither appears in the participants' text. Participants give no spread level or direction. All they say is that conditions in debt and equity markets were "increasingly supportive" and that financial conditions were "accommodative" (several), and that markets remained "unusually sensitive". That points to a level somewhat below normal, so the score stays 45, with wider uncertainty and Low confidence.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED).** Participants mention only the "rebound in stock market prices" as supportive of demand, and the prospect that "increases in financial wealth would moderate or even end at some point". They give no explicit view that valuations are stretched or depressed. Under the rubric default for price moves without valuation commentary, the score is 46, with Low confidence.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED).** The old score rested on residual tighter lending standards and construction financing, which are not in the participants' text. Participants never discuss banks or lending. They refer only to general financial markets, so the topic was not raised and the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** All members favored an unchanged stance. Policy easing since late September had "helped to stabilize a dangerously eroding financial situation" and "needed policy adjustments had been completed". Several saw inflation risks tilted to the upside, while others stressed foreign downside risk and said prompt easing would be needed if shocks came. The result is balanced, with the directive's bias removed: 50.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** The vote was unanimous (eleven in favor, none against). The members showed "minor differences about specific wording, but no strongly held opinions". Some differed on keeping a reference to money in the directive, and views on the announcement (disclosure) policy ranged widely. There was no disagreement on policy itself: 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Several passages address financial markets. Domestic financial markets are "unusually sensitive", contagion from emerging markets remains "a continuing threat", and the earlier "dangerously eroding financial situation" was stabilized. Financial conditions are also cited as supportive. This is attention across several passages, but the economic outlook still leads: 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** All members favored keeping symmetry, and the Committee "had entered a period where vigilance was called for but where the direction and timing of the next policy move were uncertain". The final directive says developments are equally likely to warrant a rise or a fall. This is neutral, with no directional commitment: 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Members "recognized" that the projection was "subject to an unusually wide range of uncertainty in both directions", and said financial markets were "unusually sensitive" to unanticipated developments. They also said the next policy move was uncertain. This is elevated, but it is not framed as unprecedented, and the financial crisis had receded: 60.
