## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68).** Participants lean clearly hawkish on the current action: "the members agreed that a prompt further tightening move was needed", and they rejected a "lesser adjustment" because "a smaller step was unlikely to be adequate". The vote record shows 12 votes for increased pressure on reserve positions and none against. The forward lean is more balanced (a symmetric directive, further action not expected soon), which holds the score below the strong-hawkish band.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** The vote record reads "Votes against this action: None", and "all the members" supported both the directive and the symmetric instruction. Differences were limited to emphasis: "Views varied to some degree" on margins of slack, and "one member" offered a monetary-aggregates perspective, while still agreeing on the action. This is near-consensus.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 20).** Attention to financial conditions is limited to one paragraph (the financial climate being "supportive", banks "easing many terms and standards for lending", "risk spreads... relatively narrow") plus a remark that a smaller step would add "market uncertainty and volatility". Most of the discussion concerns the real economy and inflation, so this is a brief-to-limited treatment.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 45).** "All the members favored moving to a symmetric intermeeting instruction", consistent with expectations "that a further policy action was not likely to be needed for some time, given the substantial nature of today's policy move". A symmetric directive does not rule out a move if developments differ. A "more decisive policy move might reduce the need for further tightening later". One member noted "more action might still be required". Net signal is neutral to mildly less hawkish than the current 50bp-type action, so slightly below 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 35).** Some factors are "harder to assess than usual": "It was difficult to assess the extent of remaining margins of available resources", "the extent of that slowing... [of inventories] and its retarding effects on near-term economic growth were uncertain", "Views varied to some degree" on slack. Elsewhere the language is confident ("a well established expansion"), so this is a somewhat elevated, not high, level.
