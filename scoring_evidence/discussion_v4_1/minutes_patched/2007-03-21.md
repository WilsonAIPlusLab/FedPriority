## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score rested on a sentence about speculative-grade bond spreads that is not part of the participants' views in the rebuilt input. Participants talk about availability of business and household credit and subprime standards, not the level of credit spreads, so the dimension is N/A.

**INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED to 55).** The old "sharp acceleration in shelter rents" is not in the participants' text. Participants expect (most) "a likely deceleration in shelter costs" to help core inflation decline, which implies shelter inflation is somewhat elevated but easing. Modestly above neutral, Low confidence given the single passing reference.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED to 50).** The old survey-based "edged lower" evidence is not in the participants' text. Most participants cite "stable inflation expectations" as helping a gradual decline in core inflation: anchored at target, so 50, Low confidence (one brief reference).

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 52).** All members favored holding at 5-1/4 percent. Inflation remained the "predominant policy concern" and "further policy firming might prove necessary", but the statement would drop its tightening-only wording and note greater downside growth risk. Hold with a slight tightening bias, near balanced.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** The vote was unanimous (ten votes for, "None" against), "all members favored" the hold and "all members agreed" on the statement; participants agreed risks to inflation were mainly to the upside. Differences were limited to emphasis (downside growth risks vs. inflation) and a "range of views" on a numerical price objective, which is a communications topic.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30).** Subprime delinquencies and tighter credit standards were mentioned within the housing and consumer-credit discussion, with participants seeing "no sign of spillovers" to the overall mortgage market and no reduction in financing availability. Limited discussion as context, not a focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 50).** The Committee agreed that the statement should "no longer cite only the possibility of further firming" and instead say future adjustments "will depend on the evolution of the outlook for both inflation and economic growth". This is a two-sided, data-dependent signal; the retained inflation concern keeps it from being easing-leaning, so neutral.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 50).** Participants cited "increased uncertainty about the outlook for both growth and inflation" and "greater uncertainty" about the expected decline in core inflation, and said it was "difficult to discern" the inflation trend. Elevated, but standard risk language otherwise, so mid-range.
