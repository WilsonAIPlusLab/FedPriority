Offsetting: one participant (Kashkari) wanted to hold until inflation was actually moving toward target, "a few" supporters were "less comfortable" with the tightening implied by the June SEP median, and "several" endorsed letting unemployment undershoot its normal rate. **DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 38).** Direction: moderate differences. Disagreement is mostly about pace and risk rather than the basic assessment. Score 38.
