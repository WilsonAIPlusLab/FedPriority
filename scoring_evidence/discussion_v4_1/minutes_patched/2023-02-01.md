## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (65 +-5, High).** Participants lean hawkish: "almost all participants agreed that it was appropriate to raise the target range ... 25 basis points," while "a few participants" favored 50bp or "could have supported" it. "All participants" anticipated "ongoing increases," and a restrictive stance was to be maintained until inflation was on a "sustained downward path." "Many" favored slowing the pace and "several" saw risks becoming more balanced, which tempers the lean and holds it below 70. Some noted that eased financial conditions "could necessitate a tighter stance." Moderate hawkish, with a slower-pace caveat.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (17 +-5, High).** The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None." and the Board action was unanimous. The only divergence is tactical: "a few participants" would have preferred or supported 50bp, and "many" of the 25bp supporters stressed slowing the pace. Views on the economy and the outlook are broadly shared. Broad agreement, near the top of the near-consensus band.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (48 +-7, High).** There is a dedicated financial-stability discussion: "several participants discussed vulnerabilities" (CRE valuations, nonbank runs, unrealized bank securities losses), "a number" stressed orderly Treasury market functioning, "several" raised climate risk, and "a number" warned a drawn-out debt-limit negotiation "could pose significant risks to the financial system." A separate paragraph covers financial conditions. This is moderate attention, not a dominant topic.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (75 +-5, High).** The signal is clearly toward more tightening: "all participants continued to anticipate that ongoing increases in the target range ... would be appropriate," and "a restrictive policy stance would need to be maintained until the incoming data provided confidence." The approved statement keeps "ongoing increases ... will be appropriate" and "prepared to adjust." The extent is data dependent and the pace is slowing, so it sits at the low end of a clear tightening signal rather than in the 80s.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (65 +-6, High).** Participants "observed that the uncertainty associated with their outlooks for economic activity, the labor market, and inflation was high," and later "discussed the heightened uncertainty regarding the economic outlook." Risks to activity were seen as weighted to the downside and there were several upside inflation risks, including the debt limit and China reopening. This is high but not extreme: no questioning of standard models, and some saw risks becoming more balanced.
