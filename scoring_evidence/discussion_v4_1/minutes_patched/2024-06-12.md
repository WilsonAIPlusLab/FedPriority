## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants did not discuss credit spreads. Credit-related remarks concern household delinquencies (many participants) and CRE/bank fragility (a few), which are not spread levels. Neither level nor direction is available, so the old neutral 50 had no support. Set to N/A.

**INFLATION_GOODS (CORRECTED, confidence).** Direction is near neutral. A few participants noted "retailers had cut prices and offered discounts" and "improvements across a range of price categories", while a few highlighted "strong increases ... in core import prices". The two roughly offset: 48, Low confidence.

**INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED).** Direction is above neutral and easing slowly. Some participants noted "shelter price inflation had so far been slow to come down"; some cited "more persistent shelter price inflation" as an upside risk. Participants also expected "the delayed response of measured shelter prices to rental market developments" to help disinflation. Score 60.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Slightly hawkish hold. All participants backed holding 5 1/4 to 5 1/2 percent and "did not expect that it would be appropriate to lower the target range" until greater confidence. "Several participants" said a hike might be needed if inflation persisted, and "a number" said policy should "stand ready to respond to unexpected economic weakness". Score 53.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. The vote record shows 12 in favor and none against, and "All participants judged" the hold appropriate. Differences were only of emphasis (patience versus readiness for weakness). Score 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Limited attention. Some participants discussed whether financial conditions were "less restrictive than they might appear" or "insufficiently restrictive", and a few noted CRE and bank balance-sheet fragility. Many noted household credit strains. These were context rather than a focus. Score 30.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Mildly hawkish-neutral. The forward signal is a hold until "greater confidence that inflation was moving sustainably toward 2 percent", conditioned on data. The Committee "would be prepared to adjust the stance ... if risks emerge" in either direction, and some wanted communications stressing a data-dependent approach "rather than ... a preset path". Score 55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Somewhat elevated. "Participants noted the uncertainty associated with the economic outlook and with how long it would be appropriate to maintain a restrictive policy stance"; "some" noted uncertainty about the degree of restrictiveness and a possibly higher longer-run rate. These are genuine but standard caveats, not "unusual" uncertainty. Score 40.
