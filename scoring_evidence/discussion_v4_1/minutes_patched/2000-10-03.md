## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 65 to 60).** Participants see conditions as somewhat tighter than before: members cited "less favorable financial conditions", "weakness in the equity prices of numerous 'new economy' firms", "tightening credit availability" for lower-rated firms, and all members noted "the tightening that had occurred in financial conditions through the spring". Direction is above 50 (bright line met). Magnitude is "somewhat tight" with no stress language, so 60, Moderate, since the old quotes cannot be verified in this input.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 63 to 55).** Participants mention only "tightening credit availability for business firms that did not enjoy investment-grade credit ratings or favorable earnings prospects". No spread level is given, so under the V4.1 level rule this is direction only: 55 on the side of tighter, Low confidence.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 60 to 52).** Several members point to "softening in equity prices", a "flat and volatile stock market" and weak "new economy" equity prices as restraints on spending, but there is no valuation or concern judgment, so the 45-55 default applies: 52, Low.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, 38 to N/A).** The input contains no discussion of the banking system, only "a growing sense of caution among business- and financial-sector executives" about financing investments. Set to N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 60 to 55).** The only dollar remark is that "a decline in the dollar from its current level ... might add to inflation pressures", implying an elevated level but no stated movement. Modestly above 50, Low confidence.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED, 51 to N/A).** The input says nothing about services prices; the old basis was services spending. Set to N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 54).** Hold steady with a mild tightening bias: "all the Committee members indicated that they favored an unchanged policy stance", and all agreed to retain the sentence that risks are "weighted toward higher inflation", though "some" thought those risks were "now less decidedly tilted to the upside". Directive held at around 6-1/2 percent.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** Near-consensus: the vote was unanimous ("Votes against this action: None") and "all the members agreed" on the risk sentence; the only difference is that "some" would revisit the wording in coming months.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 28).** Financial conditions, equity prices and credit availability appear as factors bearing on investment and housing, and one remark on caution among financial-sector executives; not a focus of the deliberation.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 57).** Retention of the risk sentence ("weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate heightened inflation pressures") and "remain especially vigilant for signs of potentially rising inflation" give a mild tightening bias; "some" saying a reconsideration "might be warranted over the next several months" tempers it. No explicit commitment to hike.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 63).** "The members recognized that marked uncertainties surrounded any forecast", augmented by oil markets and the productivity outlook; oil prices "very difficult to predict"; inflation increase "subject to substantial uncertainty". High but with a familiar set of sources, not unprecedented.
