## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 70).** Participants lean clearly hawkish: "all participants agreed that it was appropriate to raise the target range ... 75 basis points", and they "agreed that ongoing increases in the target range ... would be appropriate"; various participants saw the ultimate funds rate as "somewhat higher than they had previously expected". The lean is tempered because "a substantial majority of participants judged that a slowing in the pace of increase would likely soon be appropriate", while a few preferred to wait and some flagged the risk of overtightening. Current action is aggressive but the forward lean is moderated, so 70.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Near-consensus. The vote record states "Voting against this action: None" with all 12 voting for, and "all participants agreed" on the 75bp move and "reaffirmed their strong commitment". Differences were minor and about timing: a substantial majority expected slowing soon, while "a few other participants" would wait for clearer evidence inflation was receding. No dissents and no expressed reservations on the action, so 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** Participants gave a distinct passage to "issues related to financial stability": the "importance of orderly functioning" of the Treasury market (a number of participants, citing UK gilt disruptions), "several participants" on nonbank "hidden leverage", and a few (then several) linking the pace of tightening to "instability or dislocations in the financial system". It is a meaningful but not dominant topic among several participants, so 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 75).** The signal points to further tightening: participants "continued to anticipate that ongoing increases in the target range ... would be appropriate", the vote record adds statement language that ongoing increases are anticipated, and the terminal rate was seen as higher. The pace-slowing signal ("level ... more important ... than the pace") softens the tone from the most aggressive end, so 75.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 66).** Participants "generally noted that the uncertainty associated with their economic outlooks was high"; "many participants" said the timing of policy effects "was still quite uncertain" and that there was "significant uncertainty about the ultimate level of the federal funds rate"; "wide agreement" that heightened uncertainty about inflation and activity mattered. This is considerable, genuine uncertainty (not mere hedging), so 66.
