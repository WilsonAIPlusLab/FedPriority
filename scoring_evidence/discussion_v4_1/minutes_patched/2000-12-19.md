## Evidence for changed dimensions

**OUTPUT_GAP (CORRECTED to 52).** Participants see the economy as decelerating from an "unsustainably high growth rate" to a pace "somewhat below" output potential, but they speak of a "high level of resource utilization", "very tight" labor markets, and pressures on resources that were "likely to abate at least a little". Level is near potential with the gap closing from above, not clearly below. The "below" language refers to growth rate, so the bright line is not triggered; 52 with Moderate confidence.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED to 62).** Several members cite "tightening financial conditions in the form of declining equity prices and stricter credit terms for many business borrowers", plus "negative wealth effects of further declines in stock market prices". This is tightening (above 50), but the participants' text does not describe restrictive or stressed conditions; the old 70/High rested on market-review wording absent from the input. 62, Moderate.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to 55).** The only credit reference is "stricter credit terms for many business borrowers". No spread levels or spread language appear, so under the v4.1 direction-only rule the score sits at 55 on the side of the move, Low confidence.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to 62).** Members refer to "further declines in stock market prices" and a "sharp deterioration in the equity prices of high-tech companies", with profitability of high-tech "overestimated to a degree" (a valuation re-evaluation). Concern is real but sectoral and tied to wealth effects; 62, Moderate.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The banking system is not discussed anywhere in the participants' views; the "sound condition" phrase in the old evidence is not in the input.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to 44).** Members note that "further depreciation of the dollar" would add to import prices and that "any depreciation" would help exports, which implies some depreciation but only conditionally. Slightly below neutral, 44, Low.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants discuss labor compensation, medical costs and headline energy, but not services prices as such; the old "moderate services growth" basis is not in the input.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 38).** Hold at 6-1/2 percent with all supporting it, but "most" believed risks had shifted to weakness, "those few members" would have eased now, and all agreed to "be prepared to respond promptly". "Most" were "persuaded that a prudent policy course would be to await further confirmation". Hold with a clear easing bias. The appended January 3 call (all members supported a 50bp cut) is treated as confirming context only; 38.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22).** Unanimous vote ("None" against). Differences are minor: "almost all" saw insufficient information to ease, a few preferred easing but "could accept a delay", "some members" worried about market reaction, and "in one view" a balanced-risks statement was preferable. No sharp exchanges; 22.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30).** Financial conditions are raised by several participants as context for spending and capex ("developments in financial markets", "tightening financial conditions", wealth effects, "sizable reactions in financial markets" to the risk statement) but no stability concerns are developed; 30.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 28).** The forward signal is explicitly easing-leaning: the risks sentence is shifted "toward conditions that may generate economic weakness", the Committee "should be prepared to respond promptly to indications of further weakness", with "the possibility of an easing move early in the intermeeting period". Data-dependent rather than a commitment; 28.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** Elevated: the extent of consumer retrenchment "clearly was uncertain", the critical issue (stabilize or keep slowing) was unresolved with the weakness evidence "very recent and ... anecdotal", and the effect of the statement change was "difficult to predict". Wording is "uncertain"/"uncertainties" rather than "considerable" or "uncharted"; 55.
