## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED).** Participants see conditions as supportive but say little. The only participant passage is that "favorable financial conditions, strong corporate balance sheets, high profitability, and growth in sales would support a firming of investment spending." That is accommodative (below 50) on thin evidence. Score 40, +-8, Low. The earlier spread-based support is dropped because it is not part of the participants' views.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Neither the level nor the direction of credit spreads appears in the participants' views. The old score of 20 came from market-developments text outside the input. Set to N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED).** No participant comment on equities. The old basis was a market-developments sentence. N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED).** No participant comment on the dollar or exchange rates. The old basis was a market-developments sentence. N/A.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED).** Participants say a gradual decline in core inflation is "fostered in part by the continued stability of inflation expectations," and that "the behavior of inflation expectations" will help determine further progress. This indicates anchored expectations, close to target. Score 48 stays, with +-7 and Low confidence because the mention is brief.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** The rate is held unanimously. All members agreed the "predominant concern remained the risk that inflation would fail to moderate," risks to inflation are "mainly to the upside," and the statement should keep noting that "additional policy firming was possible." Downside growth risks are said to have diminished. Balanced action with a tightening bias, score 56.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** The vote was 11-0 ("Votes against this action: None"). "All members" agreed on the rate, on the predominant concern and on the statement language. The only variation is emphasis ("some participants" on the saving-rate upside risk; "several" on trend growth). Near-consensus, score 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** The only reference is the passing phrase "favorable financial conditions ... would support a firming of investment spending." No discussion of financial stability, leverage or markets. Brief mention only, score 8.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The Committee kept the language that "some inflation risks remain" and that "additional firming ... may be needed," with timing dependent on incoming information. Participants decided a change in the balance-of-risks language "was not warranted." That is a mild tightening bias conditional on data, not a commitment. Score 60.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Caveats are ordinary: participants "did not yet see a downtrend in core inflation as definitively established," weather "complicated the interpretation of recent data," the housing spillover could "not be ruled out," and risks run both ways. No participants stress unusual or deep uncertainty. Score 30.
