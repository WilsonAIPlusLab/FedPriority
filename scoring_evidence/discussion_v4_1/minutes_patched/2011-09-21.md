## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 30).** Participants lean moderately dovish. In the policy discussion "most members agreed that the revisions to the economic outlook warranted some additional monetary policy accommodation" and "most members judged the action as appropriate"; "two members said that current conditions and the outlook could justify stronger policy action." Against this, "three members concluded that additional accommodation was not appropriate" (the hawkish minority). The action is a balance-sheet-neutral maturity extension rather than expansion, which tempers the dovish reading from the 15-25 QE range, so 30.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 68).** Three formal dissents (Fisher, Kocherlakota, Plosser) "did not support additional policy accommodation at this time", with different reasoning: Fisher (few benefits for jobs, savers' hoarding), Kocherlakota (accommodation was well calibrated in November 2010 and inflation had risen), Plosser (little effect on growth, risk of "unwanted inflationary pressures"). Two other members wanted stronger action, and participants split on inflation risks (some saw downside, some upside; "a few" questioned the amount of slack). The dissents all point the same way and the minutes record no sharp exchanges, so below the top of the range: 68.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 58).** Several participants address it substantively: markets "volatile" and "strained at times", "risk spreads had widened appreciably", tighter European bank funding, and "participants agreed" that European sovereign and banking problems "could intensify, with potentially serious spillovers." It is a distinct paragraph and a named risk in the outlook, but not the dominant topic next to growth, labor and the policy action, so a moderate score of 58.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 6).** The Committee reaffirmed that conditions "are likely to warrant exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate at least through mid-2013", a date-based commitment, and added that it "is prepared to employ its tools as appropriate"; "a couple of members" wanted the guidance to be clearer about the conditions. The signal points to extended easing with no tightening hint. The date is reaffirmed rather than newly extended, so just above the strongest band: 6.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 65).** Participants "continued to see the outlook for growth and inflation as more uncertain than usual" and "saw considerable uncertainty surrounding the outlook for a gradual pickup"; it was noted that "the reasons for the weakness were unclear", and several said normal monetary transmission mechanisms "appeared to be attenuated." "Considerable" uncertainty with some model questioning places this in the high band, not the very-high band (no "uncharted territory" language): 65.
