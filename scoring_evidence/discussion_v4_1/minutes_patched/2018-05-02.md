## Evidence for changed dimensions

**OUTPUT_GAP (CORRECTED).** Participants see the economy as modestly above potential. Evidence: participants "noted" fundamentals supporting "continued above-trend economic growth", and "participants generally expected the unemployment rate to remain below, or to decline further below, their estimates of its longer-run normal rate." The old "well above potential for a prolonged period" phrase is not in the new input, so the score is lowered to 55 (> 50 satisfied), Moderate confidence.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED).** Direction is appreciation: "The foreign exchange value of the dollar rose modestly, but this move retraced only a bit of the depreciation of the dollar since its 2016 peak." Modest appreciation sits above the midpoint (bright-line > 50) but small in magnitude; score 54, Low confidence (one passing sentence, no participant elaboration).

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Hold now with a tightening bias. "All participants" favored leaving the range unchanged, "Most participants judged that ... it would likely soon be appropriate for the Committee to take another step in removing policy accommodation", and "participants generally agreed" gradual rises would likely be appropriate. Tempered by "a few" who saw expectations possibly below target and wanted policy adjustments to account for that. Score 60.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None"; all participants backed the hold; differences were about emphasis (most versus a few on inflation expectations; "a range of views" on the amount of firming over the medium term). Score 15.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Moderate attention. A distinct paragraph in which "a couple" discussed post-February volatility, "several" noted stronger bank capital, and "a few" urged building resilience; "some" flagged possible "financial imbalances"; several wanted to monitor yield-curve slope for recession signals. Not a dominant topic. Score 40.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Mildly-to-clearly hawkish forward signal, tempered by "gradual". "Members expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant further gradual increases in the federal funds rate"; "some participants noted it might soon be appropriate to revise the forward-guidance language" that the rate is likely to stay below longer-run levels or that policy "remains accommodative". Score 68.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Mostly normal caveats with pockets of elevated uncertainty. Risks viewed as "roughly balanced"; "a number of participants viewed the range of possible outcomes ...
