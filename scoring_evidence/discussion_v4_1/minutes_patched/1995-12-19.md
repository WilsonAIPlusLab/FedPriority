## Evidence for changed dimensions

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 45 +-10, Low).** Participants see equity prices as rising (higher equity prices supporting spending, a wealth effect on consumption) with only mild valuation concern. Key phrase: "a few members" expressed concern about "an easing action that would follow an already strong rally in bond and stock prices." Price moves with a minority's mild concern and no valuation commentary from most members: slightly below the 45-55 default, Low confidence.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The text never discusses banks or the banking system; "ample availability of credit" and "financing on attractive terms" speak to credit conditions (already captured in FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS and CREDIT_SPREADS). The old "from banks" attribution is unsupported. Scored N/A.

**INFLATION_GOODS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The producer-price and intermediate-materials evidence cited earlier does not appear in the participants' views. Members speak only of overall "consumer price increases" and "price and cost experience". No goods-specific discussion, so N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 37 +-7, High).** Moderately dovish. "all the members either favored or could accept a slight easing"; the argument was that the real funds rate "was becoming somewhat restrictive" and "downside risks to the expansion appeared to have increased". A number of members preferred no change but accepted easing, which tempers the score; the easing is small and the directive is symmetric.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 32 +-8, High).** No formal dissent (votes: eleven for, "None" against). "A number of members, though willing to accept a slight easing, preferred an unchanged policy stance" and "would not dissent"; differing views on whether inflation can fall further and on potential growth. Moderate differences, with several reluctant supporters but a unanimous vote.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 14 +-6, Moderate).** Brief attention only. "A few members" raised "possible repercussions in financial markets" of easing after a rally; credit availability and wealth effects appear as context. Typical for the mid-1990s, brief mentions band.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 48 +-6, Moderate).** Essentially neutral. All members endorsed a directive with "no presumption about the direction of a possible intermeeting change"; "the potential need for a further intermeeting policy adjustment appeared remote"; "the direction of the next policy move was not clear in the view of some members". Slight easing context keeps it just below 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 33 +-8, Moderate).** Somewhat elevated, mainly from fiscal policy: the budget debate's "timing ... and the extent of the fiscal contraction ... could not be forecast with any precision"; the dissenting camp cited "the uncertainties that were involved" and mixed evidence. Several members said no one saw greater inflation as "having a high probability", and risks were "generally in balance"; otherwise standard caveats.
