## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 38 rested on a statement about low inventory supporting strong gains in housing prices, which does not appear in the participants' views in the new input. The new text covers housing only as activity (residential investment and home sales "remained robust", supported by "low interest rates"). No participants discuss rents, shelter costs or house-price inflation, so the sub-component is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 20).** The lean is strongly dovish and unanimous. "All participants supported maintaining the Committee's current settings and outcome-based guidance" for the funds rate and asset purchases. Participants "judged that maintaining a highly accommodative stance of policy was essential", and the balance sheet was seen as providing "substantial support". The Committee also aims for inflation "moderately above 2 percent for some time". This is maximum accommodation held, with no easing or tightening change, which fits the strong-dovish band (11-25) at its upper-middle. The score is 20.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None" with 11 votes in favor, and participants "all" supported the current settings. The views are similar, with differences of emphasis only (a few on tight credit and vigilance, several on non-bank vulnerabilities). This is near-consensus, so 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** "A number of participants commented on issues related to financial stability." Several noted banking resilience and stress-test results. Several noted run-prone funds and Treasury market fragilities, and some noted elevated equity valuations, IPO activity and low risk spreads. A few noted CRE stress. This is a meaningful but single-paragraph treatment, mostly by "several", "some" and "a few" participants. It is moderate, not prominent, so 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 7).** The signal is a strong easing commitment through outcome-based thresholds. Rates stay at 0 to 1/4 percent until labor market conditions reach maximum employment and inflation has risen to 2 percent and is on track to moderately exceed it. Purchases continue "at least at the current pace" until substantial further progress. Participants noted that slower progress would be met by "increasing monetary policy accommodation". Only "various participants" stressed communicating well in advance of any future change in purchases, a faint hawkish hint, so the score is 7 rather than 3-5.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 65).** Participants "continued to see the uncertainty surrounding that outlook as elevated", agreed "the path ahead remained highly uncertain", and many cited "considerable risks" (new strains, vaccine resistance, distribution). Uncertainty is tempered by an improved medium-term outlook and by risks to inflation seen as "more balanced". This is the "considerable uncertainty" band at its lower end, so 65.
