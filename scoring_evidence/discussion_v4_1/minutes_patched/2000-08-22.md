## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** The new input says nothing about spread levels or spread direction. The only relevant participant language is that "banking institutions were continuing to report further tightening of their standards and terms for business loans" and "more cautious attitudes ... especially their lending institutions" (nonresidential construction). This is mildly less credit availability, not a statement on spreads, and the old non-investment-grade quote is not in the input. Direction-only rule applied: 54, +-8, Low.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED).** The old support was a line on household spending on services, which is not a price statement and is not in the input. Participants discuss headline/energy and core inflation in aggregate only. Set to N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Participants held the stance (all members endorsed a federal funds rate averaging about 6-1/2 percent), judging demand and supply "closing the gap," but "many members emphasized that the Committee needed to be prepared to act promptly should inflationary pressures appear to be intensifying," and all agreed risks were "weighted toward higher inflation." Hold with a tightening bias: 58.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** "All the members endorsed a proposal to retain the current stance," and the vote record lists ten in favor with "None" against. Differences were only of emphasis (many members stressing readiness to act). Near-consensus: 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Financial conditions appear as part of the policy rationale (the "recent rally in domestic financial markets," elevated real rates, bank standards, equity prices, whether conditions are "tight enough"), but no participant raises financial-stability risk. Limited, context-level attention: 20.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The vote record approves a press sentence that risks are "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate heightened inflation pressures in the foreseeable future," and all members agreed it should continue, with many stressing readiness to act promptly. Mild tightening bias without a commitment to move: 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Members said "considerable caution needed to be exercised" on the productivity outlook, that interactions were "very difficult to assess given the paucity of prior experience," that rates and equity prices might not yet reflect productivity gains, and the dollar's turnaround "could not be predicted with any degree of confidence." The inflation outlook was "subject to considerable uncertainty." Elevated but concentrated on productivity and not described as unprecedented: 58.
