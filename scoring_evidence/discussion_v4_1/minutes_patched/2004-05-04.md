## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants in the new input say nothing about credit or risk spreads; the only financial-conditions language is "accommodative financial conditions" and rising real long-term rates. The old score of 28 rested on a quote about below-investment-grade bond spreads that does not appear in the participants' views. Scored N/A (topic not raised by participants).

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The new input has no reference to the dollar, exchange rates or currencies; the old quote on appreciation against major currencies is not participant text. Scored N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 56).** All members favored maintaining the 1 percent target, so the current action is neutral, but they recognized that "the time at which policy firming appropriately would commence might be closer than previously had seemed most probable" and agreed the reference to patience "was no longer warranted." Some members noted growth "could falter, particularly if market yields were to rise sharply further", which tempers the lean. Hold with tightening bias, 56.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 17).** The vote record shows unanimity ("Vote against this action: None"). A number of policymakers worried the "measured" language could constrain future policy while others saw substantial benefits, and views on how restraining slack was differed (some members vs. others), but all members accepted the final language. Broad agreement with minor tactical differences, 17.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 16).** Financial conditions appear only in passing: "financial market conditions had already tightened on balance", "accommodative financial conditions" supporting households, and the mortgage-rate backup as a potential restraint. No financial-stability discussion. Brief mentions, 16.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 66).** The Committee dropped "can be patient" and adopted "policy accommodation can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured," with all members agreeing; the statement signals that tightening is coming but gradually. Mild tightening bias / begin-normalization language, 66.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 40).** Many members said the price jumps "increased their uncertainty about prospective price trends"; it was "unclear" whether they signaled an upturn in underlying inflation, and gap-based predictions "were subject to considerable error." Offsetting this, growth is described as increasingly well established and risks as roughly balanced. Somewhat elevated, 40.
