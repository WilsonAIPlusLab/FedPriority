## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 60).** Participants lean hawkish on the forward path while holding the rate. "Participants agreed that a gradual approach to raising the target range ... remained appropriate," "a majority of participants noted that a stronger outlook for economic growth raised the likelihood that further gradual policy firming would be appropriate," and the vote record has members expecting conditions that "would warrant further gradual increases." Tempering this, "some participants" saw an appreciable risk of an inflation shortfall and argued the Committee "could afford to be patient," and "a couple of members" worried about inflation. Current action is a hold, so tightening bias without an immediate hike: 60.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 20).** The vote record states "Voting against this action: None," with all eight voters in favor. Differences are of emphasis: "some participants" on inflation shortfall and patience versus "a couple" worried about overshooting full employment, and "a couple of members" concerned about inflation. Broad agreement with zero dissents: 20.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** Many participants noted financial conditions "had eased significantly"; "a few" watched the yield curve slope; "several participants cautioned that imbalances in financial markets may begin to emerge" amid "elevated asset valuations" and corporate debt; nonbank leverage "might be difficult to detect"; "a couple" tied overshooting to "risks to inflation and financial stability." Moderate discussion by several participants, but not a dominant topic: 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 66).** The vote record says members "agreed to update the characterization of their expectation ... to point to 'further gradual increases'," with the funds rate expected to stay "below levels that were expected to prevail in the longer run." This is a mild-to-moderate tightening signal, qualified by "the actual path ... would depend on the economic outlook as informed by the incoming data": 66.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 30).** Mostly standard caveats: "several participants expressed considerable uncertainty" about how corporate tax savings would be used; tax effects "while still uncertain"; the neutral rate outlook "uncertain"; near-term risks "roughly balanced". Localized rather than pervasive uncertainty: 30.
