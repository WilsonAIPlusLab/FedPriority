## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 24 rested on a statement about investment-grade and speculative-grade spreads that is absent from the participants' views in the rebuilt input. What participants say is general ("supportive credit conditions," "accommodative financial markets"), which is already captured in FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS and gives no level for spreads. Not discussed, so N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence was a statement on the exchange value of the dollar that does not appear in the new input. Participants' remarks on the external sector concern foreign growth and the trade deficit only. N/A.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, 40 to 47).** Members said "longer-term inflation expectations remained well contained," and the small tightening step was justified partly to "limit the risk of a rise in inflation expectations." That reads as anchored near target with a slight upside concern, not as declining expectations, which the old analysis drew from text outside the input. Score 47.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 64).** All members favored raising the target from 1-1/4 to 1-1/2 percent, and members noted that "significant cumulative policy tightening likely would be needed." The pace was to be gradual after a soft patch, so the lean is moderate rather than strong hawkish. Score 64.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 10).** "All the members favored" the increase and the vote record shows "Vote against this action: None." Some members differed on how much energy prices explained the spending slowdown, but this was an emphasis difference only. Score 10.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 14).** Financial markets and conditions appear only in passing: supportive credit conditions and accommodative financial markets as background, and a gradual move said to lower "attendant risks to financial markets and the economy." Brief mentions only. Score 14.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Members agreed that accommodation "could be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured," with timing dependent on incoming data. The signal is gradual tightening ahead, which is a mild tightening bias rather than a clear commitment to further increases. Score 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 40).** There was "considerable uncertainty" about the energy-cost outlook, some members cited "heightened uncertainty" about energy prices and geopolitical risks, and households may have saved more because the "outlook more uncertain." Against that, the Committee judged the softness short-lived and risks "roughly balanced." Somewhat elevated, localized to energy and geopolitics. Score 40.
