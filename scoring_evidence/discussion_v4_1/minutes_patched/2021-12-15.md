**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 63).** Direction is moderately hawkish. Participants judged the extra accommodation from net purchases "no longer necessary" and agreed to double the taper pace so purchases end in mid-March, though the rate stayed at 0 to 1/4 percent. "Participants generally noted" it "may become warranted to increase the federal funds rate sooner or at a faster pace"; some wanted balance sheet reduction "relatively soon after" liftoff and "a strong commitment to address elevated inflation pressures", while stressing a measured approach. Hold with a clear tightening bias plus an active taper puts the score at 63.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** Near-consensus. The vote record states "Voting against this action: None" and "All members reaffirmed" the 2 percent and maximum-employment goals. Differences were emphasis only (some saw hikes possible before full maximum employment, a few stressed that maximum employment evolves). Score 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 10).** Attention is minimal: the only reference is that "overall financial conditions remained accommodative" and a closing mention of "financial and international developments" among information to be monitored. No participant discussion of financial stability. Score 10.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 65).** Mild-to-moderate tightening signal. Participants agreed the inflation criteria "had been more than met", expected similar monthly taper reductions ending purchases in mid-March, and most judged the maximum-employment condition for liftoff "could be met relatively soon". Some said hikes could precede full maximum employment. The statement keeps the range "until labor market conditions have reached levels consistent with" maximum employment and "prepared to adjust", so the signal is tightening-leaning but still conditional. Score 65.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 65).** High, largely genuine. "Uncertainty about the economic outlook remained high"; many said Omicron "made the economic outlook more uncertain"; participants "generally continued to stress uncertainties" around labor force participation and supply chains, and policy should keep flexibility "in the current highly uncertain environment". Score 65.
