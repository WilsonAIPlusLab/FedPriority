## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score rested on a sentence about speculative-grade yields and narrowing risk spreads that does not appear in the participants' views in the new input. Participants referred only to "accommodative financial markets" in general, which is already captured in FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS, and to inflation compensation in Treasury yields. No level or direction of credit spreads is stated, so the topic is not discussed: N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 44).** Direction: slightly dovish to balanced. All members favored an unchanged policy with the funds rate target at 1 percent and felt the "currently accommodative policy stance remained appropriate" given "very low inflation" and "wide margins of unused labor and other resources". The Committee should "be prepared to adjust" and "at some point" move to a more neutral posture, but "considerable risk in taking preemptive action". A number of members wanted flexibility to drop "considerable period", which tempers the dovish side. Score 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Direction: near-consensus. The vote record shows twelve votes for the action and none against, and "all the members favored an unchanged policy". Views "differed" only on retaining "considerable period" ("a number of members" for deletion, "a majority" to retain), and individual assessments of the pace of expansion "differed somewhat"; all agreed on the risk wording and on tying the phrase to conditions. Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 18).** Direction: low attention. Financial conditions appear as supporting factors ("accommodative conditions in financial markets"), plus passing concerns by "some members" about the effect of large deficits on financial markets and about household "financial stress". No extended discussion by most participants. Score 18.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 27).** Direction: easing lean with data dependence. A majority retained "considerable period" as it "still accurately conveyed the Committee's policy intentions", and all wanted it tied to "the persistence of quite low inflation and slack in resource use". Offsetting this, the risks-to-inflation wording was changed toward balance, a number of members wanted the phrase deleted for flexibility, and a less accommodative posture "might well be necessary" if the expansion continued. Score 27.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 32).** Direction: somewhat elevated but mostly standard. Members cited "still considerable uncertainty about the speed with which those margins would be worked down" and said current-account deficits "added to uncertainty about the longer-term prospects", yet also said the data had made them "more confident that robust growth... would persist" and viewed risks as nearly balanced. This reads as caveated confidence rather than unusual uncertainty. Score 32.
