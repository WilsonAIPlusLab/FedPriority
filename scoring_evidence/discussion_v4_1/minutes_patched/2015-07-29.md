## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 53).** Participants see policy as held at the zero lower bound but leaning toward an imminent start of tightening. Most judged conditions for firming "had not yet been achieved" but "approaching that point"; some judged them "met or ... confident that they would be met shortly"; a couple warned an "appreciable delay" could raise inflation or financial-stability risks; "One member ... indicated a readiness to take that step at this meeting". Offsetting this, some thought inflation confidence was lacking and "almost all members" wanted more evidence. A hold with a mild tightening bias falls in 51-60; the dovish minority and the hold itself keep it near the low end: 53.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 30).** The vote record shows 10 in favor and "Voting against this action: None". There are distinct camps in the discussion (some not yet confident on inflation versus some who see conditions as met), and "Several" concerned about slack versus "several" viewing the labor market as near maximum employment, plus one member willing to hike but "willing to wait". No formal dissent and agreement about the near-term course: 30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 38).** The participants' section has a dedicated paragraph: "deterioration in bond market liquidity", migration of "leveraged loan underwriting to the nonbank sector", "valuation risks when term premiums were narrow", and Puerto Rico (contagion judged low). A couple of participants tied delayed normalization to "adverse consequences for financial stability". This is a limited-to-moderate treatment, one topic block among many, not a focus: 38.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 45).** The statement approved in the vote record signals liftoff conditional on "some further improvement in the labor market" and being "reasonably confident that inflation will move back to its 2 percent objective", with policy to be kept "below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run" for some time, and some participants favoring a gradual path after the first increase. Data-dependent, with a mild tightening tilt but strong low-for-long language: 45.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 42).** "Considerable uncertainty remained about when wages might begin to accelerate"; several members "underscored the uncertainty surrounding the inflation process as well as the role and dynamics of inflation expectations"; many see some downside risks from abroad; yet risks to activity are "nearly balanced" and growth is expected at a moderate pace. Somewhat elevated, concentrated on wages and inflation: 42.
