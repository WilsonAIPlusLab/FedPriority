## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 54 +-6, Low).** Participants say corporate credit spreads "widened" or were "notably wider" over the intermeeting period, as part of the tightening in financial conditions. No level is given (nothing like "elevated" or "above average"), and "widened" describes direction only, so the score sits just above neutral on the side of the move. The old 58 with Moderate confidence overstated what direction alone supports.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old evidence quoted a sentence on divergence in growth and policies and "further strengthening of the U.S. dollar". That sentence is not in the new input. Participants never address the dollar or FX, so the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 55 +-6, High).** Policy lean is a hike with a patient, softening path. "Most participants" favored raising the range 25 bp, and "a few" favored no change. Participants "generally revised down" their policy path and "many" said the Committee "could afford to be patient about further policy firming". That is a hold-steady-with-tightening-bias reading, well below the 2017-18 hiking anchor.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22 +-6, High).** The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None". The minutes only note that "a few participants" preferred no change given financial volatility and global uncertainty. That is a minor tactical difference, with agreement on the economic assessment.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 48 +-8, Moderate).** Participants gave a full paragraph to the volatile, tightened financial conditions: "sizable declines in equity prices", wider spreads, a flatter curve. Some saw tail-risk concerns and "a couple" raised financial stability risks from corporate borrowing and elevated CRE prices. Several participants addressed it, as context for policy and not as the whole meeting, which fits the moderate band.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 45 +-7, Moderate).** The signal is data-dependent and softening. "Some further gradual increases" remained, but the statement change was meant to show that "a relatively limited amount of additional tightening likely would be appropriate". "Several participants" also floated removing forward guidance entirely, and "Monetary policy was not on a preset course". This is an easing lean with data dependence, short of neutral-to-hawkish.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 50 +-8, Moderate).** Participants cited "increased uncertainty about the global economic growth outlook" and said financial volatility made "the appropriate extent and timing of future policy firming less clear than earlier". "Neither the pace nor the ultimate endpoint of future rate increases was known". Risks were still "roughly balanced", so the score is elevated but not high.
