## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED).** Participants see conditions as below the tight-neutral midpoint (supportive). Household spending was expected to stay firm "supported by strong labor market conditions, rising incomes, and favorable financial conditions", and many noted interest-sensitive spending had improved. The only direct statement is that single phrase, so the score stays 36 but confidence is Moderate with UNC +-6.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Moderately dovish. "Most participants believed that a reduction of 25 basis points ... would be appropriate", citing global developments, insurance against downside risks and returning inflation to 2 percent; a couple called it "a close call". "Some participants favored maintaining the existing target range", and a few worried that easing "could encourage excessive risk-taking". The vote was 8 to 2 for the cut. Beyond this meeting most saw policy as well calibrated, which tempers the lean. Score 38.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Notable but contained disagreement. George dissented because "an unchanged setting of monetary policy was appropriate", and Rosengren because policy "was already accommodative" and additional easing risked "inflating the prices of risky assets". The two dissents share direction, and the underlying economic assessment is broadly shared (positive outlook, muted inflation). Score 48.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Moderate attention, above a typical 2019 meeting. "Most" of those who commented highlighted "high levels of corporate indebtedness and elevated valuation pressures"; "several" cited corporate-debt imbalances and thinning bank capital buffers; "a few" cited high equity, bond and CRE valuations; "a couple" raised climate-related risks. A few linked easing to excessive risk-taking, and one dissent rested on it. It is one passage among many topics, not dominant. Score 48.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Neutral, data-dependent. Members removed the "act as appropriate" language and stressed that the Committee "would continue to monitor" incoming information; most judged the stance "likely would remain" appropriate absent a material reassessment, while "policy was not on a preset course". A couple wanted extra communication that another cut was unlikely. The signal is a pause, two-sided rather than directional. Score 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Somewhat elevated. Trade and geopolitical uncertainties "had eased somewhat, though they remained elevated"; "elevated uncertainty regarding trade developments" and a still-prominent global-slowdown risk are cited, but participants "generally viewed the economic outlook as positive" with views "changed little" since September. Standard-to-elevated caveats, not unusual difficulty. Score 42.
