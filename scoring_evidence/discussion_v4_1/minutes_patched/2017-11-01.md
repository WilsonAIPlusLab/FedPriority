## Evidence for changed dimensions

**PRODUCTIVITY (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 38 rested on the phrase "Low productivity growth" as a factor containing wages, which is not in the participants' text. The only reference is that "a couple of participants" said wage gains, once labor productivity growth was taken into account, were consistent with an economy near full employment. No participant states the level or direction of productivity. Topic not meaningfully discussed; N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 56).** Direction: lean hawkish, hold with a tightening bias. All participants favored maintaining the 1 to 1 1/4 percent range, "nearly all" reaffirmed a gradual approach to increases, and "many participants thought that another increase in the target range ... was likely to be warranted in the near term." Offsetting: "many participants" worried that low inflation might prove persistent and that removal of accommodation should be "quite gradual", and "a few" wanted firming deferred until inflation was clearly on path. Vote record: "Several other members" were "reasonably confident" that additional firming would be appropriate soon. Net slight hawkish lean: 56.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 30).** Direction: broad agreement on the action with real differences in risk assessment. The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None" and "all participants" favored holding. The disagreement is on economics and timing: "some members" concerned about inflation expectations versus "several other members" confident in near-term firming; "a few" opposed further increases while inflation stayed below 2 percent. Zero dissents with clear but non-formal camps: 30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 33).** Direction: limited to moderate attention. "Several participants expressed concerns about a potential buildup of financial imbalances" given "elevated asset valuations and low financial market volatility", balanced by remarks on low neutral rate and stronger bank capital; "some" also cited "increased risks to financial stability" from delay in removing accommodation. One paragraph plus brief mentions: 33.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Direction: mild tightening bias. Members "reaffirmed their expectation that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant gradual increases in the federal funds rate", with the rate "likely to remain, for some time, below levels ... in the longer run", and "many participants" expected a near-term increase if the outlook was unchanged. Tempered by "actual path ... will depend on the economic outlook as informed by incoming data" and close monitoring of inflation: 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 33).** Direction: somewhat elevated. Hurricanes "made it more difficult than usual to interpret some of this information"; "many participants" saw some likelihood inflation could stay below 2 percent longer, and "several" offered competing explanations (weaker inflation responsiveness, labor market slack less than estimated). Counterweight: risks "roughly balanced" and a firm medium-term outlook. Standard-plus caveats on one issue: 33.
