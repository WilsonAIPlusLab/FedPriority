## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 38 +-6, Moderate).** Participants see the labor market as below full employment but slowly firming. Evidence: "employment growth had been very sluggish to date, with employers remaining quite cautious in their hiring practices" and "gradually firming labor markets". Excess capacity in "many labor and product markets" is also noted. Score unchanged at 38; confidence lowered because the unemployment rate and April labor-demand evidence in the old write-up are not in the input.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 38 +-8, Low).** Participants describe an easy backdrop: "an environment of monetary and fiscal policy ease", housing strength "in response to low mortgage rates", policy "currently quite accommodative". The old credit-availability and equity-market evidence is absent, and what remains is mostly policy language, so uncertainty is wider and confidence Low. Accommodative language keeps the score below 50 (bright-line).

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The input has no participant remarks on spreads, bond yields or credit availability. The old score of 55 rested on text not in the participants' views. N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, N/A).** The input has no participant discussion of equity prices or wealth effects (only "gloomy business sentiment" about sales and profits). N/A.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** No participant statement about the banking system's condition appears in the input. N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 40 +-6, Moderate).** Participants note only mild dollar weakness: "the dollar had edged lower" and "modest recent declines in the foreign exchange value of the dollar" (listed among upward cost pressures). Modest depreciation, so below 50 but not far: 40.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 52 +-5, Moderate).** All members favored an unchanged stance at 1-3/4 percent, judging a firming "premature" and a risk to "a healthy expansion". They also recognized the current stance "probably was inconsistent with the Committee's inflation objective over time" and expected to "tighten policy as needed later". A hold with a latent tightening bias: just above the midpoint.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 7 +-4, High).** "All the members agreed" on the policy stance and "all the members favored" the neutral risk statement; the vote record shows ten votes for and "None" against. Near-consensus.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 5 +-3, Moderate).** Participants do not discuss financial stability; the only financial-condition references are passing ones (low mortgage rates, fiscal discipline concerns). Bottom of the scale.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 55 +-7, Moderate).** The agreed press-statement sentence says risks are "balanced", i.e. neutral. Members added that this "would not preclude a preemptive tightening adjustment" if the outlook warranted and expected to "tighten policy as needed later". Neutral formal signal with a mild tightening tilt.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 45 +-8, Moderate).** Members said "how much final demand would strengthen going forward was still uncertain", that the economy's course was "subject to a marked degree of uncertainty", and that the consumer outlook faced "uncertainties in both directions". Elevated but conventional recovery-phase uncertainty with a baseline expectation of a pickup.
