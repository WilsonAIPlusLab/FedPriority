## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 45).** Participants saw policy as appropriately on hold at 2.25-2.50 percent, with the vote record showing a unanimous decision to maintain the range. "Participants generally agreed that a patient approach ... remained appropriate," and "Participants noted that even if global economic and financial conditions continued to improve, a patient approach would likely remain warranted," which leans mildly dovish to balanced given inflation below 2 percent. Several judged patience consistent with a balanced approach; "a few participants" said the Committee "would likely need to firm the stance", and "a few other participants" suggested resource utilization may not be as high as unemployment implied. The camps offset; the net is a hold with a slight patient/easing-leaning tone, 45.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 17).** The vote record states ten votes in favor and "Voting against this action: None." Differences in the discussion were limited to emphasis: a few expecting a need to firm versus a few doubting how tight resource utilization was, and differing views on housing and business sentiment ("a few others were less optimistic"). Broad agreement with no formal dissent and no stated reservations about the decision, 17.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 36).** There was a distinct passage: "among those participants who commented on financial stability, most highlighted recent developments related to leveraged loans and corporate bonds as well as the current high level of nonfinancial corporate indebtedness," with "a few" on leverage making the business sector more sensitive to downturns, "a couple" each on bank capital and high asset valuations, and "a few" on the Financial Stability Report. "Many participants" also discussed the improvement in financial conditions as supporting the outlook. This is limited-to-moderate attention, several addressing it but as one topic among many, 36.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 42).** The forward signal is patience and data dependence: "members concurred that the Committee could be patient," and "a patient approach ... would likely remain appropriate for some time, especially in an environment of moderate economic growth and muted inflation pressures, even if global economic and financial conditions continued to improve." Policy "would continue to depend on their assessments of the economic outlook and risks." There is no tightening signal and a mild low-for-long tilt, offset by a few participants noting a possible need to firm later, 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 30).** "A number of participants observed that some of the risks and uncertainties that had surrounded their outlooks earlier in the year had moderated, including those related to the global economic outlook, Brexit, and trade negotiations," and "some" saw downside risks to growth as decreased, though "most participants observed that downside risks to the outlook for growth remain" and "these and other sources of uncertainty remained." Standard caveats with uncertainty described as easing, 30.
