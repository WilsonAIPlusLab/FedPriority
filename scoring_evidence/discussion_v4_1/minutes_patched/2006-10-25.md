## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 32 rested on the market-review sentence that speculative-grade spreads "narrowed a little", which is staff/market text and is not part of the participants' views. The participants mention only "favorable financial conditions" in general terms and never discuss credit spreads or credit availability, so the dimension is N/A.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old 62 relied on staff shelter-cost text about the pickup in core inflation. The participants do not discuss services inflation as a separate category, so N/A.

**INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED to 47, +-10, Low).** The participants say "shelter costs were not expected to add materially to inflation going forward", which signals fading rather than elevated shelter pressure. No level is given, so the score is near stable and just below neutral, with Low confidence.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 57).** Nearly all members favored keeping the rate at 5-1/4 percent; members agreed the statement should keep conveying that inflation risks were the dominant concern and that "additional policy firming was possible"; Mr. Lacker dissented because "further tightening was needed". Hold with a tightening bias, tempered by diminished downside risks to activity only on the growth side.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 28).** One formal dissent (Lacker, on the hawkish side) against ten votes in favor. All participants shared concern about inflation and the upside risks; differences were of emphasis (some noted the slowing in core inflation had allayed fears). This is a typical one-dissent meeting.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 15).** Financial matters appear only in passing: consumer spending "supported by favorable financial conditions", "strength of corporate balance sheets and profits", and "considerable strain on some small- and medium-sized residential construction firms" (several participants). No sustained financial-stability discussion.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 60).** The approved statement text says "some inflation risks remain" and that "the extent and timing of any additional firming that may be needed ... will depend on the evolution of the outlook". Members noted ample data before December to judge "whether any additional firming was needed". A mild tightening bias that is explicitly data dependent.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 45).** Most participants were "quite uncertain as to the likely pace and extent" of the moderation in core inflation, and members said "substantial uncertainty continued to attend that outlook"; one participant raised uncertainty about the severity of the slowing. Offsetting this, most members judged downside risks to activity had diminished, so the tone is elevated but not high.
