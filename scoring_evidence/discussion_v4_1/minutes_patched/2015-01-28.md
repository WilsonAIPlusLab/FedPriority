## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 46 -> 50).** Participants see spending near trend. Participants "noted that household spending was rising moderately", with oil-driven purchasing power, "low interest rates, easing credit standards, and continued gains in employment and income" as supports; business contacts were "generally solid", and information from "some parts of the country suggested that capital investment was poised to pick up". Offsets: housing recovery "remained slow", "tepid nominal wage growth" could restrain spending, and "several" noted energy-sector layoffs and possible capex retrenchment. Net at trend: 50. The old weather-softness evidence does not appear in the input.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 44).** Hold at 0-1/4 percent with a mild easing/wait-and-see tilt. "Many participants" judged the risks inclined them toward keeping the rate at the lower bound longer; "several" warned a late departure could mean excessive accommodation and "some" thought firming might begin in the near term; "one participant" wanted "more, not less, accommodation." Balanced-to-lean-dovish: 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 25).** Unanimous vote (ten in favor, none against). Participants differed on timing (many vs some), on whether low market inflation compensation reflects expectations or risk premiums, and on the usefulness of wage data, but all agreed on data dependence and the statement. Broad agreement with emphasis differences: 25.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 28).** Limited attention. Financial stability appears as a risk of an extended stay at the lower bound or a rapid later rise ("it was also suggested"), and "some expressed the concern that financial markets might overreact, resulting in undesirably tight financial conditions" if "patient" is dropped; plus a term-premium and foreign-flows discussion. Context rather than focus: 28.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 38).** Easing lean with data dependence. The vote record keeps "the Committee judges that it can be patient in beginning to normalize", symmetric sooner/later data-dependent wording, and an expectation that rates may stay below normal "for some time"; "many participants" worried that dropping "patient" would narrow market expectations, while "a number" noted limits to specific guidance as normalization nears. Liftoff is under active discussion but not signaled: 38.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 36).** Somewhat elevated, mostly standard caveats. Risks to activity "nearly balanced"; participants differ on whether falling inflation compensation signals lower expected inflation, whether wages will accelerate, and on foreign risks (China, Greece, Middle East, Ukraine). "Wide agreement that it would be difficult to specify in advance" the conditions for liftoff. Not framed as unusual uncertainty: 36.
