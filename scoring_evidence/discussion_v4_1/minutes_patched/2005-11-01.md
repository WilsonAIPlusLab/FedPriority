## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED to 52, +-8, Low).** The old unemployment-rate and survey-expectations evidence does not appear in the participants' views as given. What remains: wage gains "modest relative to continued strong productivity growth", "underlying economic slack was likely quite limited" (all members), and hurricane disruption to employment judged "limited and temporary". Near-balanced to marginally tight; little direct labor-market evidence, so Low confidence.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED to 51, +-7, Moderate).** Household spending "seemed to have held up fairly well, aside from a drop in purchases of autos", with "some participants" worried that weak confidence and high energy bills "might augur weakness ahead"; housing "remained robust" but near cooling. Business investment "seemed to remain moderate overall", though "a number of firms had boosted their plans for capital spending" and construction was "picking up in some areas". Net at trend.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to 54, +-6, Low).** The text gives direction only: "some widening of private credit spreads in recent months" judged to have "a little restraining effect". No level information, so under the V4.1 rule the score sits just on the widening side of 50 (not the old 27, which relied on a "very low levels" statement absent from the input).

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A) and DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old quotes (substantial equity gains; dollar "about unchanged") are not in the participants' views in the rebuilt input, and neither topic is raised by participants. Not discussed, so N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68).** Unanimous decision to hike 25bp to 4 percent, with all members wanting to "continue removing monetary policy accommodation in order to check upside risks to inflation"; "some members cautioned" about eventually going too far and the pace is "measured". Moderate hawkish, slightly below the consensus-for-further-increases band.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 9).** No dissents ("Votes against this action: None"); "all members" shared the view; one mild reservation noted by "some members". Near-consensus.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 15).** Brief mentions only: spreads and real rates as a "little" restraint on investment, home equity lending declines and possible housing cooling. Not a focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 66).** Members agreed "a continued measured pace of policy firming remained likely"; statement paragraph says accommodation "can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured" while promising to "respond to changes in economic prospects as needed". **UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 48).** "Considerable uncertainty" attached to the near-term outlook (net effects of consumer-confidence decline, energy prices and hurricane rebuilding), and policy "increasingly sensitive to incoming economic data"; offset by risks described as balanced and no broader questioning of models.
