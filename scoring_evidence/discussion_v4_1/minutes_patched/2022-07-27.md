## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 70 rested on a quoted passage about services inflation being more persistent than goods inflation. That passage is not in the participants' text. Participants discuss rents (scored under INFLATION_HOUSING), gasoline and commodities, supply bottlenecks and broad-based CPI measures. They make no statement about services prices as a category, so the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 74).** Participants lean clearly hawkish. "All participants agreed that it was appropriate to raise the target range ... 75 basis points," "participants continued to anticipate that ongoing increases ... would be appropriate," and "moving to a restrictive stance ... was required." Several lean-back elements temper it. Participants "judged that ... it likely would become appropriate at some point to slow the pace," "some" would hold a restrictive level for some time, "many" flagged the risk of tightening more than necessary, and "some" noted the real rate was still below shorter-run neutral. This is a 75bp hike with consensus for further increases but some discussion of slowing, so 74.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** The vote record says "Voting against this action: None." The text says "all participants agreed" on the 75bp move and "participants concurred" on most other points. The only differences are of emphasis: "many" see overtightening risk and "some" stress the real rate still below neutral. This is near-consensus, so 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 40).** There is a dedicated paragraph. "Several of the participants who commented" noted eased valuations, strong bank capital and orderly markets. "Some" discussed digital assets, "several" raised avoiding complacency, and "several" noted declining capital at the largest banks. Tighter financial conditions come up often as a transmission channel. It is a moderate, multi-participant topic but not dominant, so 40.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 78).** The signal is a clear further-tightening one. "Anticipated that ongoing increases in the target range would be appropriate" is in both the participants' views and the statement, along with continued balance-sheet reduction. It is tempered by "likely would become appropriate at some point to slow the pace," by "some" favouring a hold at a sufficiently restrictive level, and by an explicit data-dependent framing. The score is 78, within the rubric's clear-tightening band.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** "Uncertainty about the medium-term course of inflation remained high." "Heightened uncertainty" had led firms to downgrade prospects, and households were cautious "in light of uncertainty about the economic outlook." Participants also noted a GDP versus labor-market discrepancy and downside risks to growth alongside upside risks to inflation. The language is elevated but not extreme, with no scenario-range or model-breakdown talk, so 55.
