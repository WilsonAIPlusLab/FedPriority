## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 76)**: Strongly hawkish current action. All members agreed policy "was still insufficiently restrictive"; a majority believed an "unusually sizable firming" (3/4 point) was desirable and "for most members a sizable move at this point represented the most appropriate balance." Other members preferred 1/2 point but accepted the larger move. Tempered below the 81-90 band because the forward lean was neutral to modestly hawkish (majority favored a symmetric directive, expecting no near-term further action; one member preferred an asymmetric tightening directive). Score 76.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 35)**: The policy vote was unanimous, with no votes against. Views differed on size: a majority favored 3/4 point, "other members" preferred 1/2 point and "despite their reservations" accepted the majority's choice because of "the quite small difference" in effects. Differences about the directive also surfaced (one member wanted an asymmetric directive, another stressed steady policy). Mr. Broaddus did dissent on the swap-line renewal vote, which concerned FX intervention rather than the policy stance, so it adds only a little. Differences are about degree and tactics, not the assessment. Score 35.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 27)**: Limited. "Some members" noted financial conditions remained conducive to expansion, and the atypical financial developments (easier non-price credit terms, ample funds in debt and equity markets) were cited as reasons for more restraint. It was also "noted that recent developments were having an unsettling effect on financial markets," and a large move "might contribute to market stability." These are context for the policy argument rather than a main focus. Score 27.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 50)**: Neutral directional signal after a large hike. A majority favored a symmetric intermeeting instruction because "a further policy action was not likely to be called for over the near term"; one member wanted an asymmetric directive toward restraint, another urged a steady course. The Committee "could not prejudge how much, if any, additional monetary restraint might be needed." Score 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 45)**: Moderately elevated. "The reasons for that outcome remained unclear"; the extent to which worsening inflation expectations would spread "was very difficult to gauge"; members "acknowledged the difficulty of judging the precise degree of monetary restraint" and the risk of overshooting. Many standard caveats and lag arguments, but a firm consensus on direction. Score 45.
