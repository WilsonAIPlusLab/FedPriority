## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 30).** Participants see the labor market as well below full employment. The members "focused ... on the persisting weakness in labor markets", expected "growth in employment would remain limited", and cited "currently large and persisting margins of unemployed labor and other resources" as a reason for accommodation. No unemployment figure appears in the participants' text, so the earlier 6.1% / falling-payroll evidence was dropped and confidence lowered to Moderate. Score 30.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 54).** Participants see spending at or modestly above trend. Members cited "continued strength in household spending", consumer spending "sustained at a high level", housing "at elevated levels", and "a relatively brisk further advance in business spending for equipment and software". Offsets are "exceptionally cautious" business attitudes, investment limited "to replacement and cost-cutting purposes", and depressed nonresidential construction. Net slightly above trend. Score 54.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 37).** Participants say little about spreads. The only relevant remark is "the ready availability of business financing on attractive terms" as a support for capital spending, which implies favorable conditions (below 50, level reading) but gives no spread level. Score 37, widened to +-10, Low confidence.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, N/A).** The new participant text has no discussion of stock prices or valuations; the earlier "stock price indexes rose notably" was staff text. N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, N/A).** The participants' foreign-sector paragraph covers foreign growth, the current account and protectionism, not the exchange rate; the earlier "dollar changed little" was staff text. N/A.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The quoted "firmly anchored" expectations language is not in the participants' text, and no other passage addresses expectations. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 42).** Hold steady with a mild easing bias. "All the members endorsed a proposal to maintain an unchanged policy stance"; "an accommodative policy stance remained appropriate"; several said disinflation concerns were alleviated but not eliminated; vote record states inflation risks "weighted toward the downside". Score 42.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 10).** Near-consensus. "All the members endorsed" the proposal, the vote record shows "None" against, and the only differences are in emphasis ("specific views ... differed to some degree", "some expressed concern" about fiscal restraint). Score 10.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 20).** Brief to limited attention. Financial markets are noted as "well positioned to support more vigorous expansion" and banking contacts as "quite upbeat", in a single paragraph plus passing mentions of financing availability. No stability concerns. Score 20.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 25).** Mild easing bias with data dependence. Members retained the "for a considerable period" language and said the economy "might well expand at a brisk pace for an extended period before inflationary pressures began to emerge", but also said the Committee "should not usually commit itself to a particular policy stance" over a pre-set time frame and that the phrase would be removed "at some point". Score 25.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 35).** Somewhat elevated, mostly standard caveats. Business fixed investment was "a major source of uncertainty", inventory impetus was "subject to considerable uncertainty", and "both downside and upside risks continued to cloud the outlook", yet members "saw favorable prospects for strong economic growth" and risks to growth were balanced. Score 35.
