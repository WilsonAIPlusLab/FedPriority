## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 53, +-10, Low).** Participants do not describe spread levels. The only debt-market remark is the business-sentiment sentence tied to "less favorable financial conditions in debt and equity markets", a direction-only statement (mildly less favorable). Under the V4.1 level rule the old 42 had relied on corporate-yield and spread-recovery text that is not part of the participants' views. Direction-only with no level information gives 52-55 on the side of the move; the score is 53 with Low confidence. N/A would also be defensible, since this is an indirect reference.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 50, +-8, Low).** Participants mention "the absence of further large gains in stock market prices" and "leveling out in equity prices this year" as reducing wealth effects, plus "less favorable financial conditions in ... equity markets" in the business-sentiment sentence. There is no valuation commentary (no "overvalued", "stretched" or "correction"), so the neutral default band applies; 50 with a slight lean for the less favorable remark, Low confidence.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, N/A).** The new input contains no statement by participants on the dollar or exchange rates. The old score of 43 rested on a depreciation quote that does not appear in the participants' views, so it is withdrawn.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 56).** "All the members" supported an unchanged stance at about 6-1/2 percent after a long tightening, but the risk sentence stays weighted toward inflation; "Many Committee members" said additional firming "could well be needed at some point", while "a number" thought less tightening than in May would be required. Hold with a tightening bias gives 51-60; 56 reflects the tempered lean.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 15).** The vote record shows ten votes in favor and "None" against. The text says "all the members" supported both the hold and the risk sentence. The only differences are in how far inflation might rise ("differed to some extent") and how much more tightening might be needed, so near-consensus at the top of that band.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 20).** Financial conditions appear in a few passing references (the cumulative "firming in financial conditions", "less favorable financial conditions in debt and equity markets", waning wealth effects from stock prices), always as channels for spending. No participants discuss financial stability or market functioning. Brief mentions only.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** The press-statement sentence approved in the vote keeps the risks "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate heightened inflation pressures". "Many" noted further firming "could well be needed", and "Several emphasized that the press release should not convey the impression that ... further policy tightening moves" were unlikely. A number expected less tightening than at the May meeting, which tempers the signal. Mild tightening bias, 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** The members "agreed" the extent and duration of the slowdown were "subject to substantial uncertainty"; they "could not be confident" growth would not rebound; consumer spending "remained uncertain"; and "uncertainties surrounding the outlook" reinforced waiting. The uncertainty is explicit and shared but framed as ordinary data-dependence, not as unusual or unbounded, so elevated (58).
