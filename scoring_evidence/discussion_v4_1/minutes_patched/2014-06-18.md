## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 40 +-8, Low).** Participants see credit conditions as improving but give no spread level. A number reported "increases in C&I lending," "a pickup in loan demand," or "better credit quality," and small businesses "improvements in credit availability," while mortgage information was "mixed" and housing credit "restrictive" for low-score households. Direction of improvement with no level: somewhat below normal, so 40 with Low confidence. Bright-line (< 50 for favorable credit) satisfied.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The cited bank-health passages are absent from the input; participants talk about lending volumes and credit availability, not bank soundness or capital.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (FILLED, 47).** Stable and anchored in both the participants' view and the vote record ("longer-run inflation expectations remained stable"), with a couple of participants wanting stronger commitment to prevent "an undesirable decline in inflation expectations" and some others seeing "upside risks to inflation and inflation expectations." Roughly balanced, slightly below target.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 42).** Policy is held at 0-1/4 with a "highly accommodative stance" and a further $10 billion taper. "A number of participants" favored a more gradual removal if growth disappoints and "a couple" favored a stronger commitment to accommodation; "some other participants" favored a more rapid move to raise rates. Mild dovish balance.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22).** The vote was unanimous ("Voting against this action: None"). "Participants generally supported" the guidance, and "most" saw the final taper step as a technical issue. Minor differences on the pace of future removal.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 35).** "Some participants" saw low implied volatility and "signs of increased risk-taking" as showing investors "not factoring in sufficient uncertainty"; all agreed to "carefully monitor financial conditions" and noted supervisory measures. One paragraph, context rather than focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 22).** The reiterated guidance anticipates holding the range "for a considerable time after the asset purchase program ends" and keeping rates "below levels the Committee views as normal" even after goals are met. "Some participants" wanted more emphasis on data dependence. Mild easing bias with data dependence.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 30).** Standard caveats: a "surprisingly large decline" in Q1 GDP, "a range of views" on remaining slack, "most" see risks "broadly balanced", "a couple" uncertain about Japan and China. No emphasis on unusual uncertainty.
