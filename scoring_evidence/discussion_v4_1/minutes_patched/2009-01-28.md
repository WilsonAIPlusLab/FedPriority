## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 15).** Participants lean strongly dovish. Members agreed to hold the target range at 0 to 1/4 percent and to keep using liquidity and asset-purchase programs; "several expressed a willingness to expand the size and duration of those purchases in the near future", and others "stood ready to expand the program if conditions warrant". The debate is how much and which assets to buy (one member preferred Treasuries), which is the QE-era signal. Lacker's dissent is about composition and he was "fully supportive" of balance-sheet expansion, so it adds no hawkish weight. Score 15.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 30).** One formal dissent (Lacker) over targeted credit programs versus Treasury purchases. The disagreement is about tactics and distortions rather than the economic assessment, on which participants "agreed" broadly. A further single participant judged that financial markets were "working reasonably well" and that programs caused distortions, and "others" cautioned against expanding programs too far. This is a typical one-dissent meeting. Score 30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 80).** Financial conditions and stability are a dominant topic. The minutes cover a joint meeting on liquidity facilities and the balance sheet, "most participants" judging credit markets "still were not working well", extension of facility termination dates, "most participants saw the renewed deterioration in the banking sector's financial condition as posing a significant downside risk", and "a number of participants" worried about commercial real estate losses at banks. Roughly as prominent as the macro assessment. Score 80.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 10).** The forward signal is strongly easing. The approved statement says conditions "are likely to warrant exceptionally low levels of the federal funds rate for some time", that the Fed "stands ready to expand" agency purchases, and is "prepared to purchase longer-term Treasury securities". There is no numeric threshold or date, so not at the 1-5 extreme. Score 10.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 68).** Uncertainty is high. "Participants were, however, quite uncertain about the outlook"; "all but a few saw the risks to growth as tilted to the downside"; equipment investment hit by "substantial uncertainty about future economic conditions and government policies"; the fiscal impetus "far from clear". Not at the extreme because a recovery in Q3/Q4 is the central expectation. Score 68.
