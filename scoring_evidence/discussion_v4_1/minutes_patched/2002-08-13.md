## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_PRESSURE (CORRECTED, 35).** Participants see inflation as below the midpoint and contained: "The outlook for inflation remained very favorable", "keeping price inflation subdued", "risks of any significant run-up in inflation appeared to have receded". Some cost pressures (healthcare and insurance, steel, import prices) were "not entirely absent". No deflation concern appears in the text, so the old 27 is raised to 35 (26-40 band).

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 35, Moderate).** Direction below midpoint: "continuing slack in labor markets" and a "pickup in employment" still anticipated; business caution affecting "hiring". The old dissenter quote and unemployment-rate figures are not in the text and the vote was unanimous. Value kept at 35, confidence lowered to Moderate with a wider band.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 55, Low).** Participants describe "widened credit spreads" and "curtailment of credit availability" to questionable borrowers, but give no level, while "interest costs had declined for borrowers with acceptable credit ratings". Direction only, so 55 (top of the 45-55 range for a clear widening), Low confidence.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 30, Low).** Participants refer to "the depreciation of the dollar" as supporting exports and adding "upward pressures on import prices". Depreciation puts the score below 50. Two brief mentions, so Low confidence; 30 on the "depreciation" anchor.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old evidence phrase is not in the participants' text; expectations are not discussed, so N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 42).** "All the members were in favor of an unchanged policy stance" at 1-3/4 percent; "most said they preferred" a risk statement tilted toward weakness; "a further significant weakening ... might well call for a policy response". Hold with a mild easing bias.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** Unanimous vote, "Votes against this action: None." The only difference was that "a few" preferred keeping the balanced risks wording, though all could accept the change. The old analysis's claim of two dissents is contradicted by the vote record.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** "A number of members commented on financial developments" (equity wealth losses, cost of equity capital, widened spreads, governance failures), and "additional deterioration in financial markets" was named as a trigger for easing. A prominent paragraph, but not dominant.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 30).** Press statement: risks "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate economic weakness"; "several" noted this "might raise expectations of some easing in coming months" but that expectations would follow "the tenor of the incoming economic information". Easing lean with data dependence.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** "A few" stressed "the considerable uncertainty surrounding the outlook"; the pace of the business-sentiment recovery was "subject to substantial uncertainty"; "It was unclear when the associated uncertainties would diminish". Offset by members continuing to "place favorable odds" on a strengthening expansion, so elevated rather than high.
