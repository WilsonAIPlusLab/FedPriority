**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 48 +-7, Low).** Participants mention equity price increases only as an offset to higher rates and a stronger dollar in judging financial conditions, with no comment on valuation levels or concern. Under the rubric, price moves without valuation commentary default to 45-55, so the score moves from 40 to 48, near neutral, Low confidence.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old score rested on implied post-crisis capital improvements that do not appear in the participants' views. The only related text is "some participants" on leveraged loans, loosening terms and nonbank growth, which concerns credit markets and financial stability, not bank health. Banking system not specifically discussed, so N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 70 +-5, High).** Policy leans hawkish and in the continued-tightening range. "All participants" favored raising the range 25 basis points; "almost all" favored removing "the stance of monetary policy remains accommodative"; "participants generally anticipated that further gradual increases" would be appropriate. "A few" expected policy to become modestly restrictive and "a number" judged it necessary to go above the longer-run rate, while "a couple" would not favor a restrictive stance absent clear overheating. Gradual, not aggressive, so about 70.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 17 +-5, High).** Near-consensus, slightly above the floor. The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None" and a unanimous Board vote on the reserve and discount rates. Differences were limited to emphasis: "almost all" (not all) on the statement change, and "a couple" resisting a restrictive stance versus "a few" and "a number" open to one. No disagreement about the assessment itself.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 32 +-7, Moderate).** Limited discussion. "Some participants" cited leveraged loans, loosened terms and nonbank growth "as reasons to remain mindful of vulnerabilities and possible risks to financial stability"; "a number" cited risks from "significant financial imbalances"; financial imbalances also appear in the gradual-approach rationale; a few EME stresses were noted. Mentioned as context alongside the economic assessment, in line with the 2013-19 typical range.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 68 +-6, High).** Mild-to-moderate tightening signal. Participants "generally anticipated that further gradual increases in the target range" would be appropriate, and the "accommodative" characterization was dropped, though they said this did not signal "a change in the expected path". Timing and size were described as data-dependent. Gradual, data-dependent guidance in an ongoing hiking cycle sits in the 60-75 range.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 28 +-7, Moderate).** Normal, standard caveats. Participants "generally agreed that risks to the outlook appeared roughly balanced"; "some participants" said trade policy was "a source of uncertainty"; "considerable uncertainty" attached to estimates of the neutral rate, a narrow topic. No emphasis on unusual overall uncertainty, consistent with the calm mid-2018 anchor.
