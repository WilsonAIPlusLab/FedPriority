## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 50).** Participants see roughly trend-like net spending. Business investment is "expanding robustly" in places, but is "boosting GDP growth somewhat less than might have been expected"; several contacts report "mixed readings on the ongoing pace of consumer buying", higher energy prices are "still restraining consumer spending", and auto purchases depend on incentives, while housing "generally remained robust". Net 50.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 35).** Participants' only direct characterization is "accommodative financial conditions and tax incentives", with the term structure seen as already pricing a gradual tightening path. Accommodative implies < 50; the sparse treatment supports a moderately loose score (35) at Moderate confidence rather than the old High.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, N/A).** Members do not discuss spreads or credit availability anywhere in the new input; the old score relied on passages not present in the participants' views. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 65).** Moderate hawkish: "all of the members favored raising the target ... by 25 basis points to 2 percent" because the outlook "justified the further removal of the policy accommodation"; forward path described as "measured", with some members seeing a possibly more gradual path, so below the 70s.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Unanimous 12-0 vote with no dissents and no reservations on the action. The only difference: "a few members" would eventually move away from giving policy guidance, "while others" favor continued informativeness; "most members" content with current wording.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 7).** Essentially no attention: a passing "accommodative financial conditions" and "greater scrutiny regarding corporate governance"; no discussion of leverage, risk-taking or market functioning.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Mild tightening bias: the agreed statement says "policy accommodation could be removed at a pace that was likely to be measured", while "several members" stress data dependence and a path that might be more gradual or occasionally faster. Risks "balanced".

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 40).** Somewhat elevated: "the extent of economic slack was uncertain", oil prices and consequences "persisted" as uncertainties, "considerable uncertainty surrounded the likely evolution of the federal budget", and a few cite "greater uncertainties" in guidance; the election uncertainty was resolved and most views are fairly settled.
