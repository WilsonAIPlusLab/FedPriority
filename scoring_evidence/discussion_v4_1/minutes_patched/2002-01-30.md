## Evidence for changed dimensions

**OUTPUT_GAP (CORRECTED, 36).** Participants see output below potential: "currently reduced utilization of resources", "some added slack should economic growth remain below potential... as many members anticipated", and a "generally mild downturn". Mild and bottoming, not deep recession; bright line (< 50) satisfied.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 35).** Participants project unemployment "centered on 6 to 6-1/4 percent" in Q4 2002 and cite "rising unemployment" and "further anticipated increases in unemployment" (some members). Below full employment, moderate slack; Moderate confidence since the current level is not stated by members.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 42).** Several members stress household spending "held up remarkably well"; "moderate growth was a reasonable expectation"; housing "record sales in some areas" but limited further impetus. Business capex "still notably cautious", "likely to retard" the advance, communications equipment "very negative". Net slightly below trend.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 49, Low).** Equity prices "rebounded from their post-attack declines" (supportive), offset by "recent strength of the dollar" and risk of "sizable declines in stock market prices". Policy is "well positioned" but that is policy, not conditions. Net neutral with thin evidence.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, N/A).** No participant remark on spreads, credit availability or issuance. The old "changed little" quote is outside the input.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 52, Low).** Price commentary only: equities "rebounded", with some members flagging that disappointing profits could generate "sizable declines in stock market prices". No valuation judgment, so default near 50.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** Banks and credit supply are not discussed in the participants' text.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 63, Moderate).** "The recent strength of the dollar in foreign exchange markets" is expected to "continue to restrain U.S. exports". Strength is clear but magnitude is not given in the input, hence a score above 50 without the old extreme.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED, N/A).** Participants do not address services prices separately.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 45).** "All the members agreed" to keep policy unchanged; "pause seemed desirable"; risks "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate economic weakness". Hold with a mild easing bias, tempered by "emerging business recovery".

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** Vote record shows "Vote against this action: None" (Mr. Meyer absent). "All the members" supported the hold and the risk statement; "a few members" saw a case for a balanced-risks statement but agreed against it.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 18).** Brief references: stock-market wealth effects on households, equity price risk from weak profits, and concern a balanced-risks shift "could be misread in financial markets". No discussion of financial stability itself.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 40).** The press-statement sentence keeps risks weighted toward weakness; members note that conditions in line with forecasts "would provide ample opportunity to shift to a balanced-risks statement at a future meeting". Easing lean with a prospective move toward neutral.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 65).** "Members expressed considerable uncertainty about the prospective strength of final demand"; inventory turnaround "subject to a considerable degree of uncertainty"; foreign recoveries "subject to considerable uncertainty". Per the rubric "considerable uncertainty" falls in 65-80; held at the low end since inflation and the policy stance are described with confidence.
