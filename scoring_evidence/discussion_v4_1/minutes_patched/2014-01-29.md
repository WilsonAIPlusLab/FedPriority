## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (38).** Participants lean mildly dovish-to-balanced: policy is still in an asset-purchase (QE) regime, with the debate about the pace of reduction. "Most participants" judged a further modest step down was appropriate, "several" wanted a presumption of $10 billion cuts at each meeting, and "all members agreed" in the policy action. Against that, "a couple of participants" questioned reducing purchases given low inflation and slack, "a few participants" raised an increase in the funds rate "relatively soon," and the vote record says policy stays "highly accommodative" for a considerable time. Taper is a tightening step but within an easing regime; score 38.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (24).** The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None" and "all members agreed" on the taper and on retaining thresholds-based guidance. Differences are of emphasis: "a couple of participants" doubted the taper but did not press a pause, "a few participants" favored an earlier rate rise, and "some" versus "others" differed on quantitative versus qualitative guidance. Broad agreement with minor differences; score 24.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (42).** A full paragraph addresses financial developments: "several participants" on market reaction to the taper, "one participant" on a possible sharp rise in long-term rates, "a couple of others" on accommodation leading investors to "take on excessive risk and so undermine longer-term financial stability," and "participants agreed" that EM events and Puerto Rico needed watching. "Several participants suggested that risks to financial stability should appear more explicitly" in forward guidance, and proposals included leaning against "undesired changes in financial conditions." Moderate discussion by several participants; score 42.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (12).** "All members agreed to retain the thresholds-based language" (rate at 0 to 1/4 percent at least while unemployment is above 6 1/2 percent), with the anticipation that the range stays appropriate "well past" that point, "especially if projected inflation continues to run below" the objective, and the reaffirmed "highly accommodative stance...for a considerable time" after purchases end. "Several participants" wanted more emphasis on keeping rates low if inflation stayed low; the financial-stability additions and the "few" favoring early hikes are minority/tactical. Strong easing bias with threshold guidance, in the 2014 taper range; score 12.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (33).** Language is standard to somewhat elevated: participants "continued to debate the reliability of the unemployment rate," the cyclical share of lower participation was "difficult to gauge," temporary factors boosted GDP, and EM developments "could pose downside risks." Yet "a number of participants" said news "reinforced their confidence" in moderate growth and risks were seen as "more nearly balanced." Somewhat elevated, not unusual; score 33.
