## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 54 +-8, Low).** Participants say credit conditions are tighter, but no one gives a spread level. Several said business activity would be "restrained by tighter financial conditions ... and more constrained access to bank credit". Several also said the tightening from the banking stresses was "likely to be less severe than they previously expected". With direction only, the rule caps the score at 55 and requires Low confidence. The old 57 with Moderate confidence overstated what the text supports.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, 38 +-7, Moderate).** The vote record says "members concurred that the U.S. banking system was sound and resilient", and the statement repeats it. That triggers the "sound" bright line (< 50). Offsetting this, "a number of participants expressed concerns about vulnerabilities in the CRE sector", and "various participants" cited further banking strains as a downside risk. The result is healthy with minor concerns.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 56 +-5, High).** The stance is a hold with a tightening bias. "Almost all participants" judged it appropriate to maintain 5 1/4 to 5 1/2 percent. "A majority" judged one more increase "likely appropriate", while "some" judged no further increases warranted. "Several" suggested the focus should shift to how long to hold. Together this is lean hawkish, not outright tightening.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18 +-5, High).** The vote was unanimous ("Voting against this action: None"), and "all members" affirmed the commitment to 2 percent inflation. The only visible differences are about the future path (a majority for one more hike, "some" for none) and "a few" on how inflation pace affects their views. That is broad agreement with minor differences.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 35 +-8, Moderate).** Banking-sector and credit-condition topics appear several times: tighter credit conditions (several), CRE vulnerabilities ("a number"), further banking strains as a downside risk ("various"), and "sound and resilient" in the vote record. These are mentioned as context among many topics, so the emphasis is limited rather than prominent.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 60 +-6, Moderate).** The signal leans mildly toward tightening or holding tight. "All participants agreed that policy should remain restrictive for some time", and "a majority" saw one more increase as likely. The statement language ("extent of additional policy firming") keeps a tightening option open. The counterweights are "several" saying the rate is "at or near its peak" with the focus shifting to "how long", "some" seeing no further increases, and the stress on proceeding carefully and data dependence.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 65 +-6, High).** "Participants generally noted there was still a high degree of uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook", and "a vast majority" judged the path "highly uncertain". Sources include the autoworkers' strike, a possible shutdown, data volatility and revisions, and the difficulty of estimating the neutral rate. This is considerable, widely shared uncertainty, not Knightian.
