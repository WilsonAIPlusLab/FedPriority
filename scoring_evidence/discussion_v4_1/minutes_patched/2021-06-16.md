## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 52).** Participants see policy as roughly balanced with a slight tightening bias in preparation. They "judged that the current stance of monetary policy and policy guidance remained appropriate"; the vote kept the 0 to 1/4 percent range and $80 billion and $40 billion monthly purchases unanimously. Against that, "various participants" expected taper conditions "somewhat earlier", "a few" expected the rate-guidance conditions to be met earlier, and "participants generally" judged it prudent to be "well positioned to reduce the pace of asset purchases". "Several" urged patience, and "several" others preferred slowing MBS purchases first. Hold-steady with a mild tightening bias: 52.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 20).** Vote record: unanimous, "Voting against this action: None," and "All members agreed" on the target range. Differences were of emphasis (upside versus downside inflation risks, patience versus preparing to taper, MBS sequencing), with no camps on the policy decision. Broad agreement: 20.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30).** A single passage: "several participants highlighted" that low interest rates were "contributing to elevated house prices" and that "valuation pressures in housing markets might pose financial stability risks". It resurfaces when "several participants" favored reducing MBS purchases earlier "in light of valuation pressures in housing markets". Limited discussion, context rather than focus: 30.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 42).** Participants "reiterated that the existing outcome-based guidance implied that the paths of the federal funds rate and the balance sheet would depend on actual progress", the substantial-further-progress standard was "generally seen as not having yet been met", and the vote record expects an accommodative stance until the goals are achieved. The forward signal is data-dependent, with a mild move toward normalization: participants agreed "to begin to discuss their plans for adjusting the path and composition of asset purchases" and to give notice "well in advance". An easing-lean, data-dependent signal that is starting to shift: 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** "Participants judged that uncertainty around their economic projections was elevated"; the reopening was "unprecedented and likely to be uneven"; "some" said supply disruptions complicated assessing progress; "several" said "it was too early to draw firm conclusions" and that heightened uncertainty implied "significant uncertainty about the appropriate path of the federal funds rate". A "substantial majority" saw inflation risks tilted up. Elevated and shared by multiple participants, but not described as unbounded: 55.
