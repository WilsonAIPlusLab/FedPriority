## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_GOODS (CORRECTED).** The old evidence of stable, subdued core goods prices is not in the participants' views. The participants' text cites "price pressures in the energy and food sectors" among the concerns of those emphasizing upside inflation risk, while some members stressed "strong competition in numerous markets" restraining price increases. These roughly offset, so goods pressure is near neutral with a slight upside from the energy and food remarks. Score 52, Low confidence.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** The lean is balanced to slightly hawkish. All members endorsed no change in reserve pressure, the economy "did not appear to require any further stimulus" and there was no sign of inflation needing tightening "at this juncture". Against that, "a number of members" stressed the Committee "would need to be particularly vigilant" on inflation. The vote was 10-0. Score 52.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. There were no votes against and "all the members" favored the same unchanged-policy, unbiased directive. Members "did not differ greatly" on the most probable inflation path, with only "somewhat differing views" on the risks. Score 12.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** The topic gets only brief mentions: stock prices "might be vulnerable" to debt-market weakness and some members said financial conditions "had tightened somewhat". Asset values and credit availability appear as context for spending, not as a focus. Score 15.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The signal is essentially neutral. All members favored an unbiased directive that "did not prejudice possible intermeeting adjustments to policy in either direction". A number of members added that inflation "could move higher" and called for vigilance, a slight tilt toward tightening. Score 52.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Uncertainty is moderate. "Substantial uncertainties attended the economic outlook", and a number of members said outcomes could differ "considerably in either direction". Fiscal policy and the housing response to higher rates were called uncertain. Many also said risks were "better balanced currently" and "considerable volatility" was short-run. Score 42.
