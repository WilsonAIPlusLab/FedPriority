## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 46).** Participants favor holding the 0 to 1/4 percent range with a mild bias toward eventual tightening. "Most participants judged that the conditions for policy firming had not yet been achieved" and "a number of them cautioned against a premature decision" (dovish weight), while "some participants viewed the economic conditions ... as having been met" and one member was "ready to take that step at this meeting" (hawkish minority). Members see conditions "continuing to approach" those warranting normalization. Lean dovish to balanced, in the 2014-15 pre-liftoff band: 46.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 27).** The vote record states "Voting against this action: None," and all members but one wanted more evidence. Differences are about timing (most vs some, one member ready now, and a couple vs most on labor slack), not about the economic assessment. Broad agreement with minor differences in emphasis, no formal dissents: 27.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 35).** "Many participants expressed concern" that a Greek failure could disrupt euro-area financial markets with spillovers to the United States; "several participants commented on the rise in the 10-year Treasury yield"; "some participants" stressed communication to avoid an outsized market reaction and warned that prolonged accommodation might have "adverse consequences for financial and macroeconomic stability." Several to many participants address it, but within one paragraph and as context for the economic outlook: 35.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 42).** The statement retains that it "will be appropriate to raise the target range ... when it has seen further improvement in the labor market and is reasonably confident" on inflation, and notes rates may stay "below levels the Committee views as normal" for some time. Members move to meeting-by-meeting decisions, and "several" stress staying data dependent as communications are updated. No near-term commitment either way, a mild easing-leaning data-dependent signal given the not-yet-met conditions: 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 35).** Members "expressed some uncertainty about the extent of the likely near-term pickup," "some members were more uncertain about the extent of progress in the labor market," and "several mentioned their uncertainty" about Greece and China. Participants "generally saw the risks ... as balanced" and expected moderate growth. Normal-to-somewhat-elevated caveats, not unusual uncertainty: 35.
