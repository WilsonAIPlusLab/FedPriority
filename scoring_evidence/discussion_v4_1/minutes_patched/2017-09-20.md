## Evidence for changed dimensions

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants say nothing about banks, capital or lending standards. The only financial-stability language is about accommodative financial conditions, which belongs to FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS. The old quote about "regulatory and supervisory tools" is not in the rebuilt input, so the 28 had no supporting text and the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 52).** Direction: hold with a mild tightening bias. "All participants thought it would be appropriate... to maintain the current target range," while "nearly all supported again indicating... that a gradual approach to increasing the federal funds rate will likely be warranted." "Many participants thought that another increase in the target range later this year was likely to be warranted," and participants agreed to announce the start of balance sheet normalization. Against this, "many participants expressed concern" that low inflation might be persistent, "a few" saw no need for further hikes in the near term, and "some other participants" worried about upside inflation risk and financial stability if removal of accommodation was too slow. The camps roughly offset, which gives lean-hawkish hold at 52.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 27).** Direction: broad agreement. The vote record says "Voting against this action: None," and "all agreed" to monitor incoming data. Differences are about pace and about how to read low inflation: "a few" want to defer hikes, "some other participants" want to avoid overshooting, and "several others" are data-dependent. That means zero dissents with minor emphasis differences, so 27.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 35).** Direction: limited-to-moderate attention. "Many participants viewed accommodative financial conditions... as likely to provide support," "a couple of those participants expressed concern that the persistence of highly accommodative financial conditions could... pose risks to financial stability," and "some other participants" cited "an intensification of financial stability risks." A few also noted that the conditions might reflect a low neutral rate. It is one paragraph and a few policy remarks, more than passing but not a focus, so 35.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Direction: mild tightening bias. The vote record says members "expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant gradual increases in the federal funds rate" and that the rate would "remain, for some time, below levels... expected to prevail in the longer run." The balance sheet normalization program starts in October. The path "would depend on the economic outlook as informed by incoming data," and "some members" would assess whether low inflation readings were transitory. This is a mild tightening signal with a data-dependent hedge, so 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 35).** Direction: somewhat elevated, but mostly about inflation. "Several others noted that, in light of the uncertainty around their outlook for inflation," their policy decision would depend on the data, and "several noted" that convergence to 2 percent "might take somewhat longer." Participants also weighed transitory against persistent factors behind low inflation and hurricane distortions. Risks were "roughly balanced" and the medium-term outlook was little changed, so the uncertainty is inflation-specific rather than broad. That gives 35.
