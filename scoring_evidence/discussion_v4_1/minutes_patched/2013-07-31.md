## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 42 +-10, Low).** The old evidence (spreads "had not widened substantially") is not in the rebuilt input, and participants never describe spread levels. The only credit-market language is "improving credit availability" among supports for a pickup and "easing in bank lending standards" cited by several participants as offsetting higher rates. That points to modestly loose credit conditions (somewhat below normal), with no level or direction information on spreads themselves, hence Low confidence and a wide band. Score 42.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 30).** The Committee kept purchases unchanged; "almost all Committee members agreed that a change in the purchase program was not yet appropriate", and a highly accommodative stance was "warranted". "Almost all participants" were comfortable with the contingent plan to moderate purchases "later this year", "a few" others thought it "might soon be time to slow somewhat" purchases, and "a few" stressed patience. One member (George) dissented in the hawkish direction. Net: moderately dovish with a taper-leaning tilt. Score 30.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 33).** One formal dissent (Ms. George, wanting a more explicit signal of reduced purchases, citing open-ended approach and "potential costs and uncertain benefits"). Reservations were limited: "one participant" uncomfortable with dated references, "a few" wary of emphasis on 7 percent, and a split of "a few" vs "a few others" on taper timing. Typical one-dissent meeting. Score 33.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 45).** A substantial passage covers volatile asset markets, higher term premiums, whether conditions "had tightened significantly", and "some participants" noting developments "might have helped put the financial system on a more sustainable footing" by unwinding "unsustainable speculative positions". Several participants engage meaningfully, but it is one topic among many and the focus is mostly on market reaction to communications. Score 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 12).** Support "for maintaining the current numerical thresholds" (6.5 percent unemployment); some willing to lower the threshold "if additional accommodation were to become necessary"; "a number of participants" wanted more guidance; the Committee "reaffirmed its view" that a highly accommodative stance stays appropriate "for a considerable time" after purchases end. Offsetting hawkish element: purchases to be reduced "in measured steps" and ended around mid-2014. Strong easing bias on rates with a taper signal on the balance sheet. Score 12.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 38).** "Heightened financial market uncertainty about the path of monetary policy"; "uncertainty about the effects of the federal spending sequestration ... clouded the outlook"; "a number of participants" somewhat less confident about a near-term pickup; "a range of views" on inflation. Mostly standard caveats plus a somewhat elevated policy/fiscal component, not unusual or deep uncertainty. Score 38.
