## Evidence for changed dimensions

**PRODUCTIVITY (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 59 rested on a "rapid growth in structural productivity" passage. That text is not in the participants' views in the new input, and no participant is reported discussing productivity (the only nearby remark, that "the pace of technological advance could be slowing a bit", concerns the cost of capital for high-tech equipment, not measured productivity). No basis to score; N/A.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence on investment-grade and speculative-grade spreads is not part of the participants' views. The new input contains no mention of credit spreads, either level or direction, so the dimension is N/A rather than carried at 23. (The V4.1 level rule would have applied only if a level had been stated.)

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence that the dollar was "about unchanged" is not in the participants' text. Members discussed "wide external deficits" and the trade and current account balances, but said nothing about the dollar's exchange value, so N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 62).** Direction: moderately hawkish. "All of the members favored raising the target for the federal funds rate by 25 basis points," the expansion "no longer" needed "the unusual degree of monetary stimulus," and policymakers saw conditions "likely to warrant a further reduction in policy accommodation in coming quarters." Tempering: the removal was to be at a "measured" pace and "increasingly keyed to incoming data," with risks seen as balanced. Score 62.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** Near-consensus. "All of the members favored" the hike; the vote record shows twelve votes for and "None" against. Only differences of emphasis on downside risks to spending and investment. Score 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 8).** Financial matters appear only as supports to spending (low real interest rates, strong balance sheets and cash flow) and a brief note on very low household saving; the external-deficit adjustment discussion is about macro imbalances. No participant discusses financial stability as a topic. Score 8, between "no mention" and "brief mentions only".

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Mild tightening bias with data dependence. The agreed statement says "policy accommodation can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured," and the Committee would "respond to changes in economic prospects as needed." Members saw a further reduction in accommodation in coming quarters, but "many members" wanted policy "increasingly keyed to incoming data," and markets were seen as not expecting the same action at each meeting. Score 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 40).** Somewhat elevated. "Several policymakers remarked that their uncertainty about the likely pace of the expansion going forward had risen," questions "remained" about the spring shortfall, and the reasons for restrained capital spending were "unclear." Offsetting this, "recent evidence had boosted their confidence that moderate economic growth would continue and that inflation would be contained," and risks were characterized as balanced. Score 40.
