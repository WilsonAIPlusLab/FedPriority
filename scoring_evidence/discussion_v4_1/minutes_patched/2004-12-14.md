## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 66).** Participants lean moderately hawkish: all members favored raising the target 25bp, judging it appropriate "in light of the prospects for solid growth and diminished slack," and said the real funds rate target remained below the level needed to keep inflation stable. They expected cost and price pressures to become "a clearer intermediate-term risk" absent further reduction of accommodation. Tempered by the "measured" pace, balanced risks, and a few members wanting less forward-looking language. Score 66.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 13).** The vote was unanimous with no dissents, and "all members" supported the action and the statement. The only visible difference was that "a few members" wanted to drop forward-looking language while "more of the members" wanted to keep it, plus a debate on the minutes-release schedule that ended in a unanimous vote. Near-consensus. Score 13.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 38).** A limited-to-moderate theme. "A number of participants" raised domestic and global financial imbalances (fiscal deficits, the current account), and "some participants" tied prolonged accommodation and liquidity to "potentially excessive risk-taking" (narrow spreads, IPOs, M&A, speculative housing demand); "a few" read the flat curve as a growth signal. Several participants engage, but these are not the main focus. Score 38.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 65).** The statement kept the guidance that accommodation "can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured," conditioned on the Committee responding to changes in prospects. That implies further gradual tightening, a mild tightening bias rather than a strong commitment; "a few members" would have dropped the language. Score 65.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 40).** "Most participants acknowledged some significant uncertainties" (partial-expensing expiration, high-tech softening), and "participants generally acknowledged that the degree of economic slack was quite uncertain." Counterweights: risks to stable inflation seen as balanced and inflation expected to stay low, so uncertainty is somewhat elevated but not unusual. Score 40.
