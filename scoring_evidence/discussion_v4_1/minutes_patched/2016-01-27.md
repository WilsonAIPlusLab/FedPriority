## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 67 to 55, Low).** Participants cited "a widening in credit spreads" among the signs of tighter financial conditions, and "some participants" pointed to "significantly tighter financing conditions for speculative-grade firms and small businesses" and tighter bank standards. None of this states the level of spreads (no "elevated", "wide" or "above average"), so under the V4.1 level rule the widening is direction only. Score is the top of the 45-55 band on the side of the move, 55, with Low confidence. Coherence with FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (65): the tightening there is driven mainly by equity declines, dollar appreciation and volatility, so the gap is explained.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 44).** The Committee left the target range unchanged. Participants judged data "disappointing" on spending and production while labor data were strong, and "several participants" saw waiting for more information "before taking the next step to reduce policy accommodation" as prudent, noting policy was "less well positioned" to respond to downside shocks. "A couple of members" wanted direct evidence of rising inflation before further firming. Gradual tightening was still expected, so the stance is a hold with a wait-and-see lean, slightly dovish to balanced.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Vote record: ten voting for, "Voting against this action: None." Participants "expressed a range of views" on risks (most saw too little evidence of a changed balance, others saw downside risks or unbalanced risks) but this was emphasis, not prescription. Broad agreement, zero dissents.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 58).** "Almost all participants" cited tighter financial conditions (equities, spreads, dollar, volatility); a long passage covered turbulence drivers, energy-firm debt stress, tighter bank standards and CRE valuations; "most policymakers" debated how persistent tighter conditions would be. This was a major theme alongside the economic assessment but not the whole meeting, so moderate to prominent.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 44).** Participants "continued to expect that gradual adjustments in the stance of monetary policy would be appropriate" and the statement expects "only gradual increases" with the funds rate below longer-run levels "for some time", but the "timing and pace ... will depend on future economic and financial market developments", with several stressing data dependence and caution. A mild normalization path tempered by a pause and explicit conditionality, so slightly below neutral.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** "Most participants" said it was difficult to judge whether the outlook had changed materially and that uncertainty "had increased"; participants "agreed that uncertainty had increased" and the implications for activity were "unclear"; "many members" in the vote record judged uncertainty had risen, and members could not assess the balance of risks for the statement. Elevated, with multiple participants emphasizing it, but not extreme.
