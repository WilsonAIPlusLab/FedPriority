## Evidence for changed dimensions

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED).** Participants see banking-related stress as below neutral, but only indirectly: credit from "financial institutions" was "apparently improving" and "investment funds appeared to be flowing more freely into commercial real estate" (several members). Banks' own condition is not discussed, so the score moves to 40 with Low confidence and a wider band.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED).** The only dollar text is in the directive's information summary ("the trade-weighted value of the dollar... appreciated"), which is staff-derived. No participant comments on the exchange rate, so the dimension is N/A.

**INFLATION_GOODS (CORRECTED).** Participants who expected favorable inflation (some) pointed to "the absence of inflationary pressures in most commodity markets" and "intense competition in local markets"; a number saw no "general increases in commodity prices". This is mild evidence of subdued goods prices, scored 40 with Low confidence; the record-low PPI evidence was not in the input.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Slightly hawkish-leaning hold. "Most of the members concluded that... current economic conditions warranted a steady policy course", but "several members" said the Committee "might well have to consider" moving from the "stimulative stance" toward neutral if inflation concerns materialized, and "one member" favored less accommodation over time (while opposing a move now). One view saw M1 growth as possibly "overly stimulative". Score 54.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** "All the members indicated their support" of the directive and the vote record shows no votes against, on either action. Inflation-outlook views "did not vary greatly"; one member's differing preference was voiced without dissent. Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Financial conditions ("strength in equity markets", "substantial progress in improving their financial positions") are cited as a backdrop reducing downside risks, and money and credit aggregates get one paragraph, but this is context, not a focus. Score 14, consistent with the 1993-96 anchor of brief mentions.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The directive keeps an unbiased (symmetric) instruction and "most" judged it "premature to anticipate any particular policy change or its timing", while "several" flagged a possible move toward a "more neutral policy posture" if inflation pressures appear. Mild conditional tightening tilt with no commitment. Score 54.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** "Most members saw the probability of a sharp deviation in either direction... as relatively remote", with standard caveats: "uncertainties about the degree to which recent strength in spending would persist" and health care reform uncertainty. Normal, not unusual, uncertainty. Score 28.
