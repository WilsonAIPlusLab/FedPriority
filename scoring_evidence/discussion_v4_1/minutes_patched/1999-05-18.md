## Evidence for changed dimensions

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 46 +-10, Low).** Participants specifically discuss equity prices: the stock market's behavior was "a major uncertainty in the outlook" for consumers, "very large equity price increases" had bolstered confidence and spending, and a leveling "would have a significant restraining effect." This is concern about the wealth channel and unpredictability, not a statement that equities are overvalued or depressed, so the score sits near the neutral default (slightly below 50 given mild caution about elevated prices). Low confidence because there is no explicit valuation language.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 57 +-5, High).** All members supported unchanged policy with an asymmetric directive tilted toward tightening, because concerns about inflation "had increased significantly"; but they saw "a reasonable chance" the current stance would stay consistent with price stability, and "a number of members" stressed the tilt did not imply a near-term change. Hold with a tightening bias: lean hawkish, 57.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8 +-4, High).** "All the members supported" the proposal and the vote record shows no dissent (11 for, none against). Minor emphasis differences only (several members discounted the one-month CPI jump).

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30 +-8, Moderate).** The recovery in financial markets, ample financing and the stock market are discussed several times as drivers of spending and as an outlook uncertainty, but no participant frames them as stability risks. Limited discussion, context rather than focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 66 +-6, High).** The Committee adopted and decided to announce an asymmetric directive; the announcement was meant to "alert the financial markets and the public" and show "resolve to keep inflation at a low level." The directive says developments are "more likely to warrant an increase than a decrease." Mild-to-moderate tightening signal, tempered by "a number of members" cautioning against reading it as a commitment.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 62 +-7, High).** Many passages stress uncertainty: timing and extent of moderation "subject to substantial uncertainty"; stock market "a major uncertainty"; labor market pressure and productivity "subject to considerable uncertainty"; demand and productivity "a wide range of uncertainty." Offsetting: inflation record still benign and a "reasonable chance" policy stays appropriate. Elevated to high, 62.
