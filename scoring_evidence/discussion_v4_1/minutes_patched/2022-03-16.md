## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED).** Participants see spending as modestly above trend. They said "consumption and business investment continued to rise solidly" despite slower Q1 GDP (weaker inventory investment) and that "economic fundamentals remained solid" with "above-trend growth to continue". The old quotes about household spending and business fixed investment having "remained strong" and strong household balance sheets are not in the participants' views text. "Solid" wording sits at the at-trend band and "above-trend" pushes up, so 58 (down from 64), with an unchanged +-7 band and High confidence (all participants, consistent language).

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Strongly hawkish. "All participants agreed that it was appropriate to begin a process of removing policy accommodation"; "ongoing increases ... would be warranted"; "many participants" would have preferred 50 bp now (a number accepted 25 bp only because of Ukraine uncertainty); "many" saw one or more 50 bp increases as possibly appropriate later; balance sheet reduction to begin possibly in May. Bullard dissented for 50 bp. Consensus for continued tightening with a hawkish tail: 75.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus on direction and on the need for ongoing hikes. One formal dissent (Bullard, preferring 0.5 pp "in light of elevated inflation pressures"), and many participants who preferred 50 bp but accepted 25 bp, a tactical rather than assessment-level disagreement. Anchored at a typical one-dissent meeting plus several reluctant supporters: 32.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Limited. A number of participants noted the tightening of financial conditions from communications; the invasion was said to be "contributing to tighter financial conditions"; a few flagged the risk of "much tighter global financial conditions"; "policy decisions must take account of the state of financial markets". These are context, not a focus; the discussion is dominated by inflation and labor. Score 20.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Clear tightening signal. "ongoing increases in the target range for the federal funds rate would be warranted"; "move the stance of monetary policy toward a neutral posture expeditiously" with a move to a tighter stance possibly warranted; runoff "as soon as in May"; the statement (vote record) "anticipates that ongoing increases ... will be appropriate" and "begin reducing its holdings". Tempered by "nimble", "risk management" and data dependence, so 80 rather than higher.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Elevated to high. "Participants agreed that the economic outlook was highly uncertain"; "uncertainty regarding the path of inflation was elevated" and uncertainty about real activity "also seen as elevated"; war implications "highly uncertain". Uncertainty is largely war- and inflation-risk-driven while the policy direction is not in doubt, so 60 rather than 70+.
