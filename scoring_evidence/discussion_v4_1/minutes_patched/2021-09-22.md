## Evidence for changed dimensions

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants in the new input say nothing about the condition of banks. The old score rested on a phrase about banks' "solid capital and liquidity", which is not part of the participants' views here (and the second old quote was admitted as inferred). With no participant discussion, the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 58).** Policy was held at 0 to 1/4 percent and the stance "remained appropriate", but "all participants agreed" the statement should say a "moderation in the pace of asset purchases may soon be warranted". Most said the price-stability test was met or soon would be, a number said the employment test was met, some said the costs of asset purchases "might be beginning to exceed their benefits", and several preferred a more rapid taper. A number raised beginning rate increases by end of next year, offset by various participants who expected the rate to stay near the lower bound for a couple of years. Hold with a tightening bias (taper imminent): 58.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 32).** The vote record shows 11 in favor and "Voting against this action: None", and "all members agreed" on the target range and the statement. Differences were real but about timing and pace: a number thought the employment test was not yet met versus a number who thought it was; several wanted faster tapering; "a number" saw liftoff possible by end of next year versus "various" who saw the lower bound lasting a couple of years. Zero dissents with notable differences on pace and risk: 32.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30).** "Several participants expressed concern that the high degree of accommodation... could increase risks to financial stability"; "some participants mentioned the risks associated with high asset valuations"; "many" cited risk-management, including a possible tightening of financial conditions. These are scattered mentions in the risk discussion, not a focus: 30.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 55).** The vote record signals tapering soon (a gradual taper ending around mid-next year was "likely appropriate"), which leans hawkish. But participants reaffirmed that the taper "provided no direct signal about its interest rate policy", the rate guidance stayed outcome-based with an accommodative stance maintained, and only "a number" raised liftoff by end of next year. Net a mild tightening lean on the balance sheet only: 55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55).** "Participants noted that uncertainty remained high"; "a number of participants" said the course of the virus, supply chains and labor shortages "complicated the task of interpreting incoming economic data". Risks to activity were seen as "broadly balanced" and the language was moderate rather than unusual: 55.
