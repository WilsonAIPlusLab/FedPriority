## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 46).** Participants lean slightly dovish to balanced. The Committee held the 0 to 1/4 percent range, and the vote record says it "agreed to maintain the target range". A few participants anticipated that June data "would likely indicate sufficient improvement" for firming, while many "thought it unlikely that the data available in June would provide sufficient confirmation". A few participants with low equilibrium-rate estimates "cautioned against initiating policy firming in the near future". Most were content to decide timing meeting by meeting. Slight dovish tilt from the downside-risk concerns and the many on later liftoff; the few for June pull toward neutral. Score 46.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22).** The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None", so there are no formal dissents. Participants "expressed different views about the likely timing and pace of policy firming" and "different views were expressed" on the financial-stability implications of tightening. A couple of participants argued against using nominal wages in the inflation-confidence assessment. These are differences of emphasis within broad agreement. Score 22.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 38).** There is a dedicated paragraph on "financial market developments and financial stability issues". Some participants noted that term premiums "could rise sharply" at liftoff, as in 2013. Bond-market volatility was linked to high-frequency traders, thin dealer inventories and bond-fund assets, and a couple wanted a better understanding of bond-market structure. It was noted that "financial stability and the Committee's macroeconomic goals were likely to be complementary objectives". This is a meaningful but single-paragraph topic held by some participants. Score 38.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 40).** Guidance is data-dependent. Participants agreed the decision to begin firming "would appropriately depend on the incoming data". Most felt timing would be set "on a meeting-by-meeting basis" and did not want an explicit near-term signal. The retained statement language expects policy to stay "below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run" for some time. Raise "when it has seen further improvement in the labor market" is conditional, with no date. This is an easing-lean-with-data-dependence posture, nudged up by the move to meeting-by-meeting normalization. Score 40.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 46).** Participants "judged that recent domestic economic developments had increased uncertainty regarding the economic outlook". They debated whether Q1 weakness was transitory, and a few saw downside risks as having risen. "Estimates of such equilibrium real interest rates were highly uncertain." Most still saw risks as nearly balanced, so this is elevated but not unusual-extreme. Score 46.
