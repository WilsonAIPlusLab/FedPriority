## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 28).** Participants and the Committee lean dovish: policy held at 0 to 1/4 percent with exceptionally low rates "for an extended period" reiterated, and "the costs of growth turning out to be weaker than anticipated could be relatively high." "Some members" wanted a larger MBS maximum to reduce slack faster, while "another member" favored a reduction; "all members" supported buying the full $1.25 trillion, with purchases slowed gradually and flexibility to expand or scale back. QE continuing at full size with an orderly wind-down places the lean in moderate dovish, not strong dovish. Score 28.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Near-consensus. The vote was unanimous ("Voting against this action: None"). Differences were limited to emphasis: "some members" would raise the MBS cap, "another member" would cut it, and "some participants" were skeptical of resource-utilization gauges, yet "all members were able to support" the outcome. Broad agreement with minor emphasis differences. Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 52).** Several participants addressed financial conditions meaningfully, though the economic assessment remained the focus. Phrases: markets "again regarded as broadly positive" with cumulative improvement "substantial"; "others noted... many financial markets and institutions were still strained and that downside financial risks remained"; market functioning "might deteriorate as those programs wind down"; "many regional and small banks were vulnerable" to CRE losses. Moderate discussion. Score 52.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 20).** Easing-leaning, low-for-long signal. The Committee reiterated that conditions "were likely to warrant an exceptionally low level of the federal funds rate for an extended period" (no date or threshold), and asset purchases would be slowed and completed by end of Q1 2010 rather than expanded. "All agreed" on communicating the tools and willingness to withdraw accommodation "at the appropriate time," which is a modest offset. Mild easing bias. Score 20.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 60).** Elevated. "Participants expressed considerable uncertainty about the likely strength of the upturn once those supports were withdrawn," and household saving behavior "remained an important source of uncertainty in the outlook." Offsetting this, the outlook "had improved further" and the risks "had become more balanced," so uncertainty is elevated but below the 65-80 band for a pervasive "considerable uncertainty." Score 60.
