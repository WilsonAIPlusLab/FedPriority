## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants gave no statement of credit-spread level or direction. The only nearby text is that "real estate values were elevated in some sectors of the CRE market", which concerns asset valuations and financial stability, not spreads. Inferring a compressed level from general risk-asset conditions is not evidence under the rubric, so the dimension is N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence quote about equity prices being "quite high" is absent from the rebuilt participants' views and vote record; equities are not discussed. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 56).** Participants "judged that a continued gradual removal of monetary policy accommodation remained appropriate" and "most participants" judged another step would "soon be appropriate" if data came in as expected; "a couple" saw a hike warranted now. Offsetting, "members generally judged it would be prudent to await additional evidence" that the slowdown was transitory, one member wanted more inflation progress first, and "a couple of others" favored even more gradual removal; "several" saw conditions for a faster pace. Hold with a tightening bias: 56.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22).** The vote was unanimous ("Voting against this action: None"). Differences were over timing and pace (a couple favoring a hike now, a couple favoring a slower path, several citing conditions for a faster one, one member requiring inflation progress). Broad agreement with minor emphasis differences: 22.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 32).** "Several participants emphasized" that higher capital and liquidity requirements had increased resilience, "expressed concerns" that easing regulation could raise risks, and "it was noted" that CRE valuations were elevated; "some participants" also discussed financial conditions after the March hike. A limited, single-paragraph treatment: 32.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** "Members expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant gradual increases in the federal funds rate" and the rate "likely to remain, for some time, below levels... in the longer run", with "nearly all policymakers" expecting to begin reducing securities holdings "this year". Tempered by the wait-for-evidence stance and data dependence. Mild tightening bias: 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 33).** Members "judged that there was significant uncertainty about the effects of possible changes in fiscal and other government policies" and fiscal prospects were "highly uncertain"; "a few" stressed uncertainty about the weak consumer spending and weak inflation readings; "a couple" about the normal unemployment rate; "another participant" about the neutral rate. Near-term risks were "roughly balanced". Somewhat elevated, mostly specific and policy-related: 33.
