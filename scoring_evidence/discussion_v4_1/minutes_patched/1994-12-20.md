## Evidence for changed dimensions

**TREASURY_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence quoted an Orange County passage that is not in the new input. Participants discuss only losses at local governments and corporations being weathered "fairly readily"; nothing about Treasury market functioning. Per the rubric, N/A when the Treasury market is not specifically discussed.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to 35, +-10, Low).** Spreads are not discussed directly and no spread-widening passage exists. The only level indicator is that "Banking institutions remained aggressive" in lending and markets showed "a great deal of resilience", which implies ample credit availability, so the level reads below 50. Mild score, Low confidence.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 58).** A majority favored holding at least into early 1995 as earlier tightening had not yet been felt, but most of them favored an asymmetric directive because further restraint was "highly likely". A few members preferred immediate tightening, and Mr. LaWare dissented for it. Hold with tightening bias: 58.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 38).** One formal dissent on the policy vote (11-1, LaWare wanting an immediate hike), a few members who preferred tightening but "could accept" the directive, and other members who preferred a symmetric directive but "would not dissent". Disagreement concerns tactics and timing, not the economic assessment. A separate swap-line vote on December 30 drew a dissent from Mr. Broaddus on foreign exchange intervention, a different issue. Score 38.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 32).** Several members noted financial conditions were "generally supportive" and that markets had weathered losses at local governments and corporations; "a number of members" thought year-end adjustments and investor losses argued for a steady course. Limited, contextual attention: 32.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 72).** A majority wanted an asymmetric directive "tilted toward restraint" and believed the need for more restraint "highly likely", to be acted on "promptly"; the adopted directive biases toward firming. Hold now but a clear tightening signal: 72.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** Members "acknowledged that the timing and extent of the slowing in the expansion were subject to considerable uncertainty"; fiscal policy was "another source of considerable uncertainty"; inventories subject to "a great deal of uncertainty". Repeated but standard-cycle language, with confidence in the broad direction: 58.
