## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, CONF only).** Participants mention tighter credit conditions for households and small businesses and "lingering risks of further tightening in bank credit", which describes direction of credit availability, not the level of spreads. Under the v4.1 level rule, direction-only evidence scores 45-55 on the side of the move with Low confidence. Score stays 53 +-8, now Low confidence.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED).** The old evidence ("risks to the banking system had receded notably since last spring"; "vulnerabilities at some banks") does not appear in the participants' views or the vote record. In the new input, members "concurred that the U.S. banking system was sound and resilient" (low stress, bright-line below 50), while several participants flagged CRE refinancing and "balance sheet pressures faced by some lenders" and some noted "lingering risks of further tightening in bank credit". Sound with minor concerns: 38 +-7, Moderate.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Balanced to mildly dovish. All participants favoured holding the range; participants "viewed the policy rate as likely at or near its peak" and "almost all" projected a lower range by end-2024. Hawkish counterweights: "highly attentive to inflation risks", policy to "remain at a restrictive stance for some time", and a possible need for further increases. Score 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. The vote record shows 12 in favour and "Voting against this action: None"; "all participants" judged the hold appropriate. Minor emphasis differences only (a few on a possible dual-mandate tradeoff, several on holding longer). Score 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Moderate attention. Many participants said easing in financial conditions beyond what is appropriate could hinder reaching the inflation goal; several raised CRE refinancing and lender pressures; many noted rising household credit usage and delinquencies; members called the banking system "sound and resilient". It is addressed meaningfully by several but is not the focus. Score 45.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Slightly easing-leaning but data-dependent. Members saw the added word "any" as conveying the rate was "at or near its peak" while "leaving open the possibility of further increases"; participants stressed a "careful and data-dependent approach" and a restrictive stance "for some time". Almost all projected cuts by end-2024. Score 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** High. "Participants generally perceived a high degree of uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook", outlooks carried "an unusually elevated degree of uncertainty", and members said the extent of tighter credit effects "remained uncertain". Score 62.
