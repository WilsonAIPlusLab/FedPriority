## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Participants describe spreads as moving down and corporate credit as improving, but give no statement of the level (neither "elevated" nor "compressed"). Key phrases: "risk spreads had declined" (in the general outlook paragraph) and "better conditions in corporate credit markets, especially the markets for high-yield bonds and leveraged loans" (many participants). Under the V4.1 level rule, direction alone cannot justify a score as low as 32; direction only, narrowing, scores just below the midpoint with Low confidence: 46.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Strongly accommodative: the Committee agreed to "maintain the existing highly accommodative stance," keep the 0 to 1/4 percent range, continue the maturity extension program, and keep guidance through late 2014; a number of members saw the risk of fading employment gains as reinforcing unchanged guidance, and "a couple of members" said additional stimulus "could become necessary." On the other side, "one member" saw tightening as necessary "well before the end of 2014," Lacker dissented, and "other participants" worried accommodation "could erode the stability of inflation expectations." No new easing action, so not in the QE-launch range. Score 28.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** One formal dissent (Lacker, who thought rates "will most likely need to rise considerably sooner"), a second member with a similar view, and some participants with inflation worries, versus a couple of members open to more stimulus. Disagreement is mainly about the forward path and guidance; the economic assessment is broadly shared ("almost all members" supported the guidance). Typical one-dissent meeting: 33.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** "Many participants noted that strains in global financial markets had eased"; "many" credited euro-area actions; "a number of participants" stressed that a longer-term euro-area banking and fiscal solution was still needed and that disruptions "continu[ed] to pose a risk to growth." Meaningful but one theme among several: 42.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The statement language approved in the vote says conditions "are likely to warrant exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate at least through late 2014," a date-based easing commitment, kept unchanged because of a "nonnegligible risk" employment gains fade; "a couple of members" would add stimulus if momentum is lost. Conditionality ("subject to revision") and the one member signalling earlier tightening keep it off the extreme floor: 7.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** "High levels of uncertainty among households and businesses" restrain growth; "most firms ... continued to be uncertain about the strength of the recovery"; "several participants" said weather added "one more element of uncertainty to the interpretation of incoming data"; euro-area and fiscal-cliff risks. Elevated but with standard caveats and an outlook seen as "broadly similar" to January: 42.
