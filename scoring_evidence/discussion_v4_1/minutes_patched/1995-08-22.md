## Evidence for changed dimensions

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 42).** The old High-confidence score of 32 rested on phrases that do not appear in the input. What participants do say points modestly to supportive conditions: homebuyers reacting to lower mortgage rates, "wealth effects of the rise in stock market prices", and expected lower long-term rates if fiscal restraint passes. Offsetting this, "recent weakness of the bond markets" is cited by other members. Participants see conditions as somewhat on the supportive side but the discussion is brief. Score 42, Low confidence. Bright-line (supportive implies below 50) is satisfied.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old 32 was inferred from a non-existent "ready availability of financing" quote. No participant mentions spreads, credit availability or risk premia, so there is no level and no direction. N/A.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old score cited "ready availability of financing from banking institutions," which is absent. No participant discusses the banking system. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 46).** All members accepted an unchanged stance and a directive "not biased in either direction." Near-term trends were seen as favorable; the members generally believed an adjustment would need consideration over the longer term, especially with substantial fiscal restraint, and some called the real funds rate "slightly to the restrictive side." This is a hold with a slight dovish tilt. Score 46.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Vote record shows 11 in favor and none against; "all the members" backed the directive. The only differences were "some" optimistic on inflation versus "others" with reservations. Broad agreement with minor emphasis differences. Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 10).** Financial matters appear only in passing (mortgage rates, stock wealth effects, debt-market effects of a fiscal package). No stability concerns raised. Score 10.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 48).** Symmetric directive, steady policy "pending a clearer assessment of the outlook for fiscal policy," and a general belief that "consideration would need to be given to an adjustment" later, without a stated direction. Neutral, data-dependent. Score 48.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52).** Several linked statements: budget uncertainty "remained substantial" and the outcome "particularly uncertain"; the external sector "subject to particular uncertainty"; risks "still sizable in both directions." Offset by improved confidence that recession risk was reduced. Elevated but not extreme. Score 52.
