## Evidence for changed dimensions

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 54 rested on staff-style description of equity indexes moving lower, which does not appear anywhere in the new input. No participant comments on equity valuation, so the dimension is N/A, as the rubric requires when a topic is not discussed.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 57).** Direction is hold with a tightening bias. Most members endorsed leaving reserve pressure unchanged, and "all but one" saw it as prudent to assess developments first. "A majority favored" shifting the intermeeting instruction to asymmetry toward restraint, and some said the next move would likely be toward restraint. One member (the dissenter) wanted prompt tightening. Some preferred a symmetric directive, which tempers the lean. Score 57, in the lean-hawkish band (hold with tightening bias).

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 38).** There is one formal dissent: Mr. Broaddus, who argued that "additional near-term tightening was necessary". One further member voiced "strong reservations" about the asymmetric directive but, with the others favoring symmetry, accepted it. Views "differed to some degree" on inflation and "differed" on announcing no-change outcomes. All other members voted for the action, and the policy debate was about tactics more than economics. This fits the "one dissent or a few reservations" band, at 38.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 10).** Financial stability appears only in passing: lowering the debt range would underscore that rapid debt growth could have "adverse implications for inflation and financial stability", and a too-stimulative policy could cost "considerable disruption in financial markets". The rest of the discussion covers growth, inflation and monetary ranges. Score 10, brief mentions only.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 67).** The forward signal is a mild-to-moderate tightening bias. A majority backed the asymmetric directive, and "some" believed the next move would likely be toward restraint, with some urging promptness. Those favoring symmetry could accept the asymmetric instruction, and the stance is unchanged for now. Score 67.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** Several uncertainty statements: the extent of slowing "remained subject to considerable uncertainty"; "members remarked" that slack uncertainties, accentuated by the household survey change, made inflation "particularly difficult to assess"; "many expressed reservations" about long-range forecasts; velocity uncertainty "likely to persist". The language is largely genuine, but the tone is conventional, with firm central-tendency forecasts. Score 58, in the elevated band.
