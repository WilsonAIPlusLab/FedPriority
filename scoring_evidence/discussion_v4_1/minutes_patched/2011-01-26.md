## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Participants describe spreads as moving down, not as being at any stated level. The text says credit spreads on nonfinancial corporate debt "continued to narrow ... reaching levels noticeably lower than those posted several months ago" and "credit conditions remained tight for smaller, bank-dependent firms". This is direction only with no level information, so under the v4.1 rule the score sits just on the side of the move (below 50) with Low confidence. Score 46 +-6, Low. (The earlier 35, High read the narrowing as a low level.)

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Participants lean dovish: the Committee agreed unanimously "to continue to expand its holdings of longer-term Treasury securities", reaffirming the $600 billion purchase, because the recovery was "insufficient to bring about a significant improvement in labor market conditions" and inflation was "somewhat below" objectives. "A few members" said stronger data "could make it appropriate to consider reducing the pace or overall size", but "others" thought that unlikely before completion. This is a QE-continuation meeting with a slight hawkish tail. Score 28.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. "Voting against this action: None," and the statement changes were "small". Minor differences: "a few members remained unsure of the likely effects" of the purchases but "felt that making changes ... was not appropriate". The role of slack for inflation "was debated". Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** One of the discussion's main paragraphs covers financial conditions (equity gains, spreads, yield curve, credit for smaller firms). Participants also "remained worried about ... spillovers from the banking and fiscal strains in peripheral Europe", and "some participants" raised balance-sheet exit and expectations risk. This is moderate, context-level attention rather than a dominant theme. Score 33.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** The signal is extended accommodation: "the Committee reiterated its expectation that economic conditions were likely to warrant exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate for an extended period", with purchases continuing and "regularly review[ed]". Only "a few members" raised a possible reduction, and "others" thought it unlikely; "planning for the eventual exit" was mentioned by "some participants" as a precaution. Mild-to-strong easing bias, no date or threshold commitment. Score 20.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Participants "generally saw the risks ... as having become broadly balanced", with downside risks "diminished", but "continued to see significant risks to both sides". "Some participants" noted it "was not clear whether the recent pace of consumer spending would be sustained", pass-through factors were "complex and hard to monitor in real time", and "a few members" were "unsure of the likely effects" of the program. Somewhat elevated but not unusual. Score 38.
