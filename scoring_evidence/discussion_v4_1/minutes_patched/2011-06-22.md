## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 54 +-8, Low).** Participants noted that "risk spreads for many corporate borrowers had widened noticeably", which is a statement of direction. No participant gave a level (no "elevated", "wide" or "above average"), and the same paragraph says large firms keep "ready access to credit... on relatively attractive terms", so the old reading of "elevated vs. recent norms" and the 58/High score were not supported. Under the v4.1 rule (direction only, no level), a clear widening scores 52-55 with Low confidence: 54.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 44 +-7, Moderate).** Direction: roughly balanced with a slight dovish tilt from the maintained accommodative stance. Members agreed to complete the $600 billion purchases, make no change to the 0-1/4 percent range, and keep reinvesting; "a few members" noted the Committee "might have to consider providing additional monetary policy stimulus", while "a few members" saw inflation risks warranting steps "to begin removing policy accommodation sooner than currently anticipated". "Some participants" favored more accommodation if growth stayed too slow, "others" and "several" saw less slack and earlier withdrawal. Hold with offsetting minority camps and QE ending, but with extended-period language: 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 24 +-7, Moderate).** Direction: broad agreement. The vote record shows "Voting against this action: None" and "all members agreed" on the statement. Participants did differ on the medium-term path (more accommodation vs. earlier withdrawal; a few disagreed on the implications of uncertainty about policy efficacy; one participant objected to a numerical inflation objective), but these are emphasis and tactical differences without reservations about the decision: 24.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 42 +-8, Moderate).** Direction: moderate attention. Participants discussed investors' greater risk concern, the equity selloff, differential credit access, "a few participants" worrying that agricultural credit "might have eased too much", Greek and peripheral European strains with money market fund exposure, and the debt ceiling (participants "emphasized" severe disruptions). A sizable portion of the discussion, but not dominant relative to the economic assessment: 42.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 25 +-8, Moderate).** Direction: easing-leaning low-for-long. The Committee "decided to reiterate" that conditions were likely to warrant "exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate for an extended period", will regularly review its holdings and is "prepared to adjust" them, and "will act as needed". No tightening signal beyond a minority view that withdrawal might begin sooner; no new commitment either: 25.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58 +-7, High).** Direction: elevated. "Many members saw the outlook for both employment and inflation as unusually uncertain"; participants cited uncertainty over the economic outlook and tax and regulatory policy affecting hiring, said it was "difficult to know" the effects of a debt-ceiling failure, and "a few" expressed uncertainty about the efficacy of monetary policy. Multiple participants stress unusual uncertainty, but not deep or Knightian: 58.
