## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 54, +-8, Low).** Participants noted only that "risk spreads for businesses widened" amid the China/EME turbulence. This is a statement of direction with no information on the level of spreads (no "elevated" or "wide"). Under the V4.1 level rule, direction-only language scores 52-55 on the side of the move with Low confidence. The old 60 with Moderate confidence is replaced by 54.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old evidence quote ("weathered the turbulence ... without any sign of systemic stress") is not present in the new input and cannot be attributed to participants. Beyond household credit performance being "favorable" and some credit deterioration for energy producers, no participant discusses the banking system, so the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 54).** The Committee held at 0-1/4 percent, but "most participants" anticipated that "the conditions for policy firming had been met or would likely be met by the end of the year," and "some other participants" worried that delay risked imbalances; "some" preferred to wait given downside risks. One member preferred to raise at this meeting. Net: hold with a modest tightening bias, so lean hawkish at 54.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 38).** One formal dissent (Lacker, in favor of a hike). The minutes show two identifiable camps (wait for confirming information vs begin firming soon) and "some" disagreement about slack remaining beyond the unemployment rate, but these concern timing and risk weighting, with agreement that firming should be gradual. Moderate differences with one dissent: 38.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 42).** A paragraph addresses equity declines, wider risk spreads, dollar appreciation and China/EME spillovers, "some" discussed valuations, and "some other" cited risks of "economic and financial imbalances" from delay. Participants also said they "did not see the changes in asset prices ... as bearing significantly on their policy choice." Moderate attention: 42.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 42).** The agreed statement keeps the condition-based signal that it will be appropriate to raise the range once labor market improvement continues and inflation confidence builds, and adds that rates may stay "below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run" for some time. "Increasing ... gradually" is stressed. Data-dependent with a mild easing-lean on pace: 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 42).** "Most" saw risks as nearly balanced, but "many acknowledged" global developments "may have increased the downside risks," "some" judged downside risks to growth and inflation had increased, and the Committee was "weighing the uncertainties." Somewhat elevated, not unusual: 42.
