## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 34).** Participants see the labor market as well below full employment but not depressed. Key phrases: "stagnant conditions persist in labor markets"; anecdotal "persisting weakness in numerous regional labor markets"; forecasts of an unemployment rate of 5-3/4 to 6 percent with no material decline. Employers are reluctant to add workers. Score 34, below 50 in line with the unemployment-rate band. The old payroll and 6.0% quotes were not in the participants' text.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 40).** Participants do not mention spread levels or direction. The only related language is favorable credit availability: several members cite "the wide availability and low cost of capital" and "widely available financing". This points to somewhat favorable credit conditions, below 50. Score 40, Low confidence. No bright-line trigger, and it is coherent with FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 40.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 55).** Equities arise only as a risk: some members worry about consumer confidence "should stagnant conditions persist in labor markets and equity markets weaken further". There is no valuation commentary, so the default 45-55 band applies, slightly toward concern. Score 55 (unchanged), Low confidence.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** No participant discusses bank health, capital or lending standards in the new input; the old cited phrase is absent. Set to N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 28).** Members refer once to "the adverse effects of recent declines in the dollar" as an upward price influence. Direction is depreciation (score under 50) but no magnitude or breadth is given, so a modest-to-notable weakness reading. Score 28, Low confidence.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 48).** All members backed an unchanged policy; "a wait-and-see policy stance was desirable". The funds rate stays at 1-1/4 percent and risks are "balanced". Policy is held at an accommodative setting with no stated bias either way, so just under balanced. Score 48.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** The vote record shows twelve votes for and "None" against; "all the members supported" the proposal. Differences are minor (some members' concern about equities and labor, a few reservations about further housing gains). Near-consensus. Score 8.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 14).** Financial conditions appear only in passing: "generally accommodative financial conditions", capital availability, and possible equity weakness. No participant discusses financial stability as a topic. Score 14.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 50).** Members retain "a balanced risks assessment" for the public statement (confirmed in the vote record) and describe a data-dependent wait-and-see stance. They note a possible need to "adjust its policy stance substantially and promptly in one direction or the other". No directional signal. Score 50.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 72).** Members "emphasized that their forecasts were subject to substantial uncertainties, dominated ... by the geopolitical situation". All agreed forecasts have "extremely wide confidence bands". Timing of a business recovery "remained subject to a high degree of uncertainty", and a range of outcomes including persisting subpar performance or a much stronger acceleration could not be ruled out. High, but the outlook is still seen as favorable, so not extreme. Score 72.
