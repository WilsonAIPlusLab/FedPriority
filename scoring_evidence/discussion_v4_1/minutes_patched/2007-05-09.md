## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 35 rested on quotes about narrowed speculative-grade spreads and investment-grade spreads at "fairly low levels". Neither appears in the participants' views in the new input, which contains only generic references to "favorable financial conditions" and subprime turmoil being contained. No participant comments on spreads or credit availability, so N/A with N/A uncertainty and confidence. Financial conditions continues to carry the accommodative signal.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 54).** Policy is a hold: "all members favored keeping the target federal funds rate at 5-1/4 percent." The lean is slightly hawkish because "the risk that inflation would fail to moderate as desired remained the Committee's predominant concern" and "all participants agreed that the risks ... were to the upside," offset by growth risks still "weighted to the downside" though "diminished slightly". Hold with a mild tightening bias, no one arguing for a move: 54.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 6).** Vote record shows ten votes for and "None" against; "all members" favored the hold. Minor differences of emphasis only (some versus others on the effect of resource utilization on inflation, and on profit-margin absorption), no reservations. Near-consensus: 6.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 15).** Financial conditions appear only in passing: "favorable financial conditions" supporting investment, subprime turmoil "had not spread to the rest of the mortgage market", and a caution that equipment outlays could stay weak "if financial market conditions became less supportive". Brief mentions only: 15.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 55).** The approved statement text keeps "the risk that inflation will fail to moderate as expected" as the predominant concern but ties future adjustments to "the outlook for both inflation and economic growth, as implied by incoming information". Data-dependent with a mild inflation (tightening) tilt and no commitment: 55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52).** Most participants expected core inflation to slow "although considerable uncertainty surrounded that judgment"; "Members agreed that considerable uncertainty attended the prospects for inflation"; housing inventory desires "uncertain" and participants "cautioned against drawing too much comfort" from recent data. Offsetting this, the central growth projection is stated plainly and downside risks were judged to have diminished, and part of the "considerable uncertainty" wording is standard hedging. Elevated but not unusual: 52.
