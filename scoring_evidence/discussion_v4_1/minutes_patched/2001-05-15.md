## Evidence for changed dimensions

**OUTPUT_GAP (CORRECTED, 40 +-7, Moderate).** Participants see the economy below potential: growth at a "now quite sluggish pace", "continuing subpar economic growth", "associated slack in product markets", and it was "not entirely clear that the slowing ... had bottomed out". Offsets: "pockets of strength" in energy and construction, "considerable vigor in a number of regions", and labor markets "still tight by historical standards". Below 50 per the bright-line rule, but "sluggish"/"subpar" language does not support the deep-recession range, so 40.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 47, +-8, Low).** Participants say little about conditions directly. Supportive: housing cushioned by "very attractive mortgage interest rates" and "stimulative" policy. Restrictive: "negative wealth effects of stock market declines". Some members said policy "might still be considered to be only marginally accommodative", which is about policy, not conditions. Net slightly loose to neutral, with Low confidence.

**CREDIT_SPREADS, BANKING_STRESS, DOLLAR_STRENGTH, INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED to N/A).** None of these topics appear in the participants' text of the new input; the old scores relied on sentences that are not in it. N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 55, +-8, Low).** Only "the negative wealth effects of stock market declines" and "downward adjustment in equity prices globally" (a downside risk) appear. There is no valuation commentary, so the score sits at the low end of the depressed side.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 33).** Dovish. "All but one of the members" supported a 50bp reduction to 4 percent; Hoenig dissented for 25bp and two others "could have accepted" it. Members said further easing was desirable given "continuing weakness" and "decidedly downside risks", but some stressed a "more cautious approach" and that excessive stimulus must be avoided. Moderate dovish, tempered by the cautious camp and the hint that easing is near its end.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 35).** One formal dissent (Hoenig) over the size of the cut, with one strong 25bp preference (his) and two members who could have accepted it. "All the members agreed that further easing was desirable"; the disagreement concerns tactics and pace, so moderate.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 15).** Brief mentions only: wealth effects of stock declines, mortgage rates, global equity prices. The discussion is about real activity, inventories and inflation.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 35).** All members accepted keeping the risks-tilted-toward-weakness sentence (easing bias), yet "a neutral balance of risks statement could be appropriate before long" and "some members noted that the end of the easing process might be near"; it was "premature" to shift now. Easing lean with a pending neutral turn.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 58).** Many uncertainty references: "crosscurrents and uncertainties", "members were uncertain as to the degree and timing of the strengthening", "many saw ... the upturn could be weaker or more delayed", inventory duration "a key uncertainty", lagged policy effects "very hard to discern", growth weakening "of unknown persistence and dimension". Elevated, but members still hold a central view (upturn later in the year), so not in the high range.
