## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (FILLED, 50 +-6 Moderate).** Participants see spending as at trend: consumer spending and business investment "had been solid", but "many participants observed that measures of household and business sentiment remained weak", "several" saw softening consumer data, "several" said firms were "cautious about beginning new projects", and "a majority" expect growth to moderate. Net: at trend, score 50.

**PRODUCTIVITY (CORRECTED, 50 +-12 Low).** Only "a couple of participants" said AI investment "could boost productivity", while "several" said tariffs could act "as a drag on productivity". Both are prospective and offsetting, so neutral with low confidence.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 45 +-8 Low).** The only participant statement about conditions is that "several participants noted that financing from both banks and financial markets was readily available for larger investment projects", which is mildly supportive (below 50 under the bright-line rule). Policy being "moderately or modestly restrictive" is a policy stance, not conditions. Thin evidence, so Low.

**TREASURY_STRESS, EQUITY_VALUATION, DOLLAR_STRENGTH, INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old values came from Desk/staff market commentary and a housing trajectory not found in the participants' views. The new input contains no participant remarks on these topics.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 42 +-10 Low).** No participant mentions spread levels or direction. Under the level rule, the only usable signal is credit availability ("readily available" financing for larger projects, several participants), a mildly below-normal stress reading. Value kept at 42 but on different evidence, with wider band and Low confidence.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED, 48 +-10 Low).** "Some participants observed that services price inflation had moved down recently, while goods price inflation had risen", and "some" noted that wage growth "had continued to moderate" and was not expected to add to inflation pressure. Slightly below neutral to neutral, score 48.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, 57 +-7 Moderate).** Participants noted longer-term expectations "continued to be well anchored", but "several" said "shorter-term inflation expectations had been elevated" with potential spillover, "most" flagged persistent tariff effects as a risk, and "some" saw heightened unanchoring risk. Modestly above anchored, score 57.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 44 +-6 Moderate).** All participants favored holding at 4 1/4 to 4 1/2 percent and waiting. "Most participants assessed that some reduction ... this year would likely be appropriate", "a couple" were open to a cut at the next meeting, "some" saw no cuts this year, and "several" thought the range "may not be far above its neutral level". Hold with a mild easing bias, score 44.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 22 +-6 High).** Vote record lists 12 members for and "Voting against this action: None". Participants differed on the path (most expect a cut, some none) and on whether inflation or labor risks were more prominent ("some" vs "a few"), but all agreed on the decision and the careful approach. Broad agreement, score 22.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 10 +-5 Moderate).** The only touch is the readily available financing remark and the generic statement reference to "financial and international developments". Brief mention, score 10.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 45 +-7 Moderate).** The statement says the Committee "will carefully assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks" and is "prepared to adjust", a neutral data-dependent posture. Participants "generally agreed" the Committee was "well positioned to wait", while "most" anticipated a cut this year. Neutral with a slight easing lean, score 45.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 55 +-6 High).** Participants "judged that uncertainty about the outlook was elevated", while overall uncertainty "had diminished since the previous meeting"; there was "considerable uncertainty ... about the timing, size, and duration" of tariff effects. Elevated but declining, score 55.
