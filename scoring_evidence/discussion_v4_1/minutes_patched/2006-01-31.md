## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_PRESSURE (CORRECTED, 55).** Participants see inflation pressure as modestly above the midpoint: "most participants expected core inflation to move up slightly," with "continuing upside risks to inflation" from energy and commodities, though pass-through was "subdued" and core inflation "should remain contained." Core PCE projections centered near 2 percent. Modest upside, hence 55.

**OUTPUT_GAP (CORRECTED, 52).** Participants see the economy expanding "in the neighborhood of its sustainable pace," with resource utilization viewed by "some members" as "nearly full at present." Near potential, slightly firm: 52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 52).** Employment was "expanding at a fairly good pace" with compensation "rising moderately"; "some participants" flagged uncertainty over remaining labor-market capacity. Projected unemployment of 4-1/2 to 5 percent is near full employment: 52.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 58).** Consumer spending was "solid over the holiday season," confidence "high," and "participants generally anticipated fairly strong growth of capital expenditures," partly offset by housing slowing and debt-service concerns. Net modestly above trend: 58.

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS (CORRECTED, 30).** Participants described "low prevailing term premiums and bond spreads," strong equities and "an ample supply of liquidity," with investors optimistic. Easy conditions, bright line < 50: 30.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 28).** The stated level is low ("low prevailing ... bond spreads"), and a return "to more typical settings" is cast as a downside risk, so spreads are below normal. Level governs: 28.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 46).** Only price strength is noted ("recent strength in equity markets," "rallies in world equity markets"), tied to optimism rather than a stated overvaluation concern. Default band, low confidence: 46.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, 52).** Mostly anchored: "survey measures of inflation expectations had notched down" and market compensation was "little changed," while "some members" saw expectations "somewhat higher than was desirable over the long run." Roughly at target: 52.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 63).** "All members favored raising the target federal funds rate 25 basis points to 4-1/2 percent"; the stance "seemed close to where it needed to be," but "some further policy firming might be needed." Unanimous tightening action with a modest further-firming bias: 63.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 10).** The vote record shows "Votes against this action: None"; "all members agreed" on a data-dependent future path. Near-consensus, with only minor emphasis differences ("in the view of some members"): 10.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 28).** One paragraph on financial conditions (low term premiums and spreads, risk of reversal) plus housing-wealth and debt-service concerns inside the consumption discussion. Limited, context-level attention: 28.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** The statement paragraph says "some further policy firming may be needed," while participants stressed that the path "could no longer be prejudged with the previous degree of confidence." Mild tightening bias with data dependence: 62.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 38).** "Some participants" remarked on uncertainties about labor-market capacity and productivity; housing-wealth effects on saving were "difficult to predict"; the fourth-quarter slowdown was seen as largely transitory. Somewhat elevated but mostly standard caveats: 38.
