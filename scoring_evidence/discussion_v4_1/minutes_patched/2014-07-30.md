**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (FILLED, 50).** Participants expected inflation to rise gradually "as inflation expectations remained stable." The statement says "longer-term inflation expectations have remained stable" and conditions guidance on expectations remaining "well anchored." A few saw upside risk to expectations from labor-market tightening. Anchored at target, so 50.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 50).** The Committee cut purchases a further $5 billion each and reaffirmed that "a highly accommodative stance... remains appropriate," and "several" expected inflation to return "very slowly," warranting continued accommodation. Against this, "some" viewed progress as "sufficient to call for a relatively prompt move toward reducing policy accommodation," "many" said removal might come "sooner than they currently anticipated," and the one dissent was hawkish. Balanced.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 40).** There was one formal dissent (Plosser, over "a considerable time" calendar language and unacknowledged progress), with "some" participants "increasingly uncomfortable" with the guidance. "Some members" had reservations about the slack language, and "a few" versus "many" differed on measuring slack. These are moderate differences about pace and communication, not a deep split.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 35).** There was a dedicated paragraph: valuation pressures not widespread and vulnerabilities "well contained." "Some participants" discussed incorporating stability risks into the macro risk discussion and which tools would be timely. Plosser's dissent cites risk of "disruptive volatility in financial markets." It is a distinct but limited topic.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 28).** The reiterated guidance keeps rates low for "a considerable time after the asset purchase program ends" and "below levels... normal" for some time. Pushing the other way, "some" want guidance to "more clearly communicate how policy-setting would respond to the evolution of economic data," and "most" say timing depends on data. This is an easing lean with data dependence, with hawkish pressure.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 30).** Most saw risks as "nearly balanced." "Some" noted the first-quarter drop "increased uncertainty about the outlook," and "several" reported businesses "somewhat uncertain." There was disagreement on how to measure slack. This is normal caveats with mild elevation.
