## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 33 -> 46).** Participants give no level for credit spreads and no spread movement; the only related remarks are that "a couple of participants" saw reaching-for-yield behavior possibly increasing again and that credit conditions remained "tight for some households and businesses". Under the V4.1 level rule, with no level information, the score sits in 45-55 on the side of the implied (narrowing) move: 46, Low confidence.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, N/A -> 44).** The participants' views say longer-run expectations "remained stable and, by some measures, below 2 percent"; the vote record says members saw them as "stable" and the retained guidance conditions on expectations staying "well anchored". Anchored, tilted slightly below target: 44.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 28).** Dovish on the current action: "all members but one" wanted to await more evidence before adjusting purchases, "many" stressed data dependence, and the Committee continued purchases at $45bn Treasuries and $40bn MBS monthly. A tilt away from the QE-era floor comes from participants who "generally expected" trimming "in coming months", "some" who said a slowdown could come at "one of the next few meetings", and "a couple" who wanted to lay groundwork for a reduction while stressing a highly accommodative stance. Score 28.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 32).** One formal dissent: George saw "continued aggressive easing" as unwarranted and favored "slowing the pace" of purchases. Beyond that, "a couple" thought it premature to discuss winding down, "others" doubted a mechanical rule, and "some" differed on Treasury versus MBS cuts and on the inflation floor, all tactical. Typical one-dissent meeting: 32.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 33).** Financial conditions eased "notably", "a couple" saw reaching-for-yield possibly increasing, "a few" worried about the housing effects of higher mortgage rates, "one" raised bank margins, and members dropped the statement's tightening reference. Real but limited attention, mostly as context; the bulk of the text is policy communication: 33.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 12).** The 6 1/2 percent threshold guidance was retained and the statement reaffirmed "a highly accommodative stance... for a considerable time after the asset purchase program ends". Participants weighed adding language that the funds rate would stay below its normal value, "a few" favored an inflation floor, and "most" thought a lower IOER could be worth considering, all aimed at adding accommodation. Nothing was added at this meeting, and a trimming of purchases was expected in coming months, so the tone is strongly easing but not at the Evans-rule extreme: 12.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 38).** "Several significant risks remained, including the uncertain effects of ongoing fiscal drag", "uncertainty about future fiscal policy and the regulatory environment" weighed on business planning, "a number" worried about repeated fiscal impasses, and participants split on how to read the unemployment rate. Offsetting this, downside risks were "generally viewed as having diminished" and projections were unchanged. Somewhat elevated: 38.
