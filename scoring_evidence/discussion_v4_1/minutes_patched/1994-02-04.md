## Evidence for changed dimensions

**PRODUCTIVITY (CORRECTED to N/A).** The existing 58 rested on "above-trend growth in productivity" and executives' productivity reports. Neither appears in the participants' views in the new input, and the text diff shows no new passage that supplies them. The only related wording is "productive resources" and "productive capacity", which refers to slack and not to productivity growth. Participants did not discuss it, so N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 43 to 47).** Direction in words: participants see equity markets as supportive but give no valuation view. Evidence: "generally buoyant equity markets" were cited as a factor tending to boost activity, and bond prices were mentioned as having dropped. Price commentary without valuation concern defaults to 45-55; the earlier "new highs" quote is not in the input. Score 47 +-8, Low.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants do not discuss the dollar or exchange rates. The old evidence (dollar fell against the yen, rose against the mark) is absent from the new input. The only text is the directive's "about unchanged" dollar sentence, which describes the information reviewed and does not state a participant view. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68 +-6, High).** All members favored moving to a less accommodative stance; "the members agreed that monetary policy should be adjusted toward a more neutral stance". A number preferred only a slight move, other members a somewhat greater one, "many observed that additional firming probably would be desirable later". Hawkish consensus on the direction, tempered by caution about the first tightening in about five years and an unbiased directive. 68.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 20 +-6, High).** Vote record: ten votes for, "None" against. Views "differed to some extent" only on the size of the adjustment (a number of members for a slight move, other members for a somewhat greater one); "all the members indicated that they could accept" the slight move. One member preferred a lower debt monitoring range but accepted the higher one. Tactical differences only, no dissent, so 20.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 30 +-8, Moderate).** Members discussed "generally favorable financial conditions", "balance sheet restructuring" and strengthened lending-institution capital, mostly as backdrop to the expansion. Some members worried that a larger move risked "dislocative repercussions in financial markets". Several address financial conditions, but stability is not the focus. 30.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 63 +-8, Moderate).** The directive kept an unbiased intermeeting instruction ("did not include a presumption about the likely direction"), which is neutral. However "many observed that additional firming probably would be desirable later", a small move together with "expected further action" was meant to signal resolve, and a possible telephone conference was noted. Mild tightening bias, so 63.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 42 +-8, Moderate).** Inventories were "a source of particular uncertainty"; fiscal response and export markets were "subject to substantial uncertainty"; "many of the members commented on the uncertainties that surrounded the establishment of ranges" because monetary-aggregate relationships had changed. Otherwise the outlook is stated with fairly firm conviction (forecast ranges narrow, risks on the upside). Somewhat elevated, so 42.
