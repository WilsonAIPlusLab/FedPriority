## Evidence for changed dimensions

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS (CORRECTED, 35).** Participants see the labor market as loosening: "the fall in employment in August", "many participants worried that the deterioration in labor market conditions over the summer would damp the growth of income", "labor markets have weakened further". No unemployment level appears in participants' text, so the score sits modestly below neutral in the 31-45 band with wider uncertainty and Moderate confidence, rather than the earlier 27 that relied on the 6.1% figure.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (CORRECTED, 38).** Below trend: "recent readings on consumer spending had been weak despite the tax rebates", consumption "may remain soft", investment "likely to be on the soft side going forward", "a softening of household spending". Offsetting: "Business spending had held up well over the summer, and inventories appeared to be well managed." Net sluggish, not collapsing: 38.

**TREASURY_STRESS (CORRECTED, N/A).** Participants do not discuss Treasury market functioning in the new input; the earlier evidence came from market-desk text. N/A.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 70, Low).** No participant mentions spreads. The nearest content is credit availability: "tight credit conditions", Lehman-related funding difficulty "likely to lead to a further tightening of credit availability to households and firms". Read as a high stress level (reduced access), score 70 with Low confidence and wide uncertainty; level reading, so it is > 50.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, N/A).** No participant commentary on equity prices or valuations in the new input. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 50).** Hold at 2 percent agreed by all; "leaving the federal funds rate unchanged at this time suitably balanced the risks". "Some members" said a policy response (easing) could be required if strains worsen, while it was "noted" the Committee should "remain prepared to reverse the policy easing" in time. Balanced.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 12).** Unanimous: "Votes against this action: None." "Members agreed" on the hold. Only differences of emphasis on which risk might require a future response. Near-consensus.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 82).** Participants' account leads with "strains had intensified", "stresses on financial markets and institutions had increased", Lehman, GSE support, liquidity facilities and the need to bolster bank capital; the statement says "Strains in financial markets have increased significantly". Dominant, though inflation and the real economy still get substantial space.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 50).** The statement signals data-dependence ("monitor economic and financial developments carefully and will act as needed") with "downside risks to growth and the upside risks to inflation ... both of significant concern". The hawkish note about reversing easing and the dovish note about a possible response offset each other.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 78).** "Meeting participants were highly uncertain about future financial developments and their implications for the broader economy"; "the inflation outlook remains highly uncertain"; "substantial downside risks". High, consistent with the fall 2008 anchor.
