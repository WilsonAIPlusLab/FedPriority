## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 22 rested on a sentence about speculative-grade and investment-grade spreads that is not in the rebuilt participants' discussion. **EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence on equity indexes is not in the new input and no participant comments on equity valuations. Score N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old evidence on the trade-weighted dollar is not in the new input and no participant comments on the exchange rate. Score N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68).** Hawkish. "Nearly all members favored raising the target federal funds rate 25 basis points to 3-3/4 percent"; "further rate increases probably would be required" because the funds rate would remain "below the level that would be necessary to contain inflationary pressures"; a pause "had the potential to mislead the public." One member (Olson) preferred to defer, and the pace is a measured 25bp with balanced risks, so this sits in the moderate-hawkish band rather than strong hawkish.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 30).** Low-to-moderate. A single formal dissent (Mr. Olson, who "preferred that the Committee defer policy action ... pending the receipt of additional information" on Katrina), a tactical rather than economic disagreement. Members otherwise shared the economic assessment; "some sentiment" to revisit forward-looking language is a minor reservation. Typical one-dissent meeting (25-35).

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 12).** Very little attention. Passing references: "business balance sheets appeared quite strong," "financing was readily available," and house-price-driven wealth gains. No participant raised financial stability or market stress as a topic.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 68).** Mild-to-clear tightening bias. The Committee "agreed to reiterate its previous expectation" that "policy accommodation can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured," and the minutes say further increases probably would be required. Risks were described as "essentially balanced," and "some sentiment was expressed to consider changes to forward-looking aspects of the statement at upcoming meetings" because of the "considerable reduction in monetary policy accommodation" already achieved, which tempers the signal.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 60).** Elevated, driven by Katrina. "The size and timing of those effects were uncertain"; energy prices "subject to considerable volatility"; reconstruction pace "uncertain"; "underlying economic trends would be particularly difficult to assess over the next several months." Participants stressed that fundamentals were probably unaffected and the shock temporary, which keeps this below the "high" band.
