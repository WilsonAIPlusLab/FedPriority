## Evidence for changed dimensions

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score of 47 rested on equity-market strength and a Dow Jones record, which are not in the participants' views in the new input. No participant comments on stock prices or valuations anywhere in the discussion or the vote record. Score: N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 58).** Direction: lean hawkish, hold with a tightening bias. A majority endorsed unchanged reserve conditions, but "many of the members" said the Committee "probably would have to firm reserve conditions at some point... sooner rather than later"; "two members expressed a strong preference for a prompt move" and two more had a marginal preference for tightening. Messrs. Angell and Lindsey dissented because policy was "overly accommodative" (Angell wanted 50 basis points). Current action is a hold, which caps the score below 61. Score 58.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 45).** Two formal dissents in the same direction (Angell, Lindsey); "a number of others commented that the decision was a close call". The split is over timing and tactics (a turn in policy "at a meeting" versus now), while the economic assessment is largely shared. Rubric: tactics weigh less than economics, but two formal dissents and a close call reach the middle of the 31-60 range. Score 45.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 20).** Mentions are contextual: "generally supportive conditions in financial and credit markets", borrowers and lenders having "strengthened their financial positions", "ongoing though less pervasive balance-sheet rebuilding", and several members' worry that a tightening could have "a greater-than-usual effect on financial markets" with "thin trading markets around year-end" (one member). Not a central topic. Score 20.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 60).** The directive was left symmetric ("not biased in either direction"), but "many of the members" signalled that a move from an "accommodative stance to a more neutral position" would come "sooner rather than later", and a move was "still required at some point". Mild tightening bias, no explicit commitment. Score 60.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 50).** "The members viewed the extent of such moderation as a key uncertainty"; "a number of members observed that a sharp slowing... could not be ruled out"; "the members generally agreed that the outlook for developments abroad remained a source of particular uncertainty"; the tax impact "remained uncertain"; waiting was "warranted in light of the uncertainties". Several explicit but ordinary-cycle uncertainties, not extreme. Score 50.
