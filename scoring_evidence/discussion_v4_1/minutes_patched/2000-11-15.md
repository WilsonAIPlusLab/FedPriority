## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_PRESSURE (CORRECTED, 58).** Participants see inflation pressure as modestly above target and still building. "Members continued to be concerned about the outlook for inflation", core prices showed "a gradual uptrend", labor compensation "appeared to be accelerating", and "a few members" saw core inflation "near or slightly above" acceptable levels. All members agreed risks remained toward higher inflation. The old claim that upside risks had diminished is not in the text. Score 58.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 55).** Spreads "had widened, most sharply" for below-investment-grade borrowers who "faced higher credit costs". This is direction with no stated level ("wide", "elevated" or "narrow" never appears). Under the level rule that gives 52-55 on the side of the move with Low confidence. Score 55.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED, 60).** Participants described "lower equity prices" and "depressed ... stock market valuations of many firms, notably in the high-tech area", with ebbing household wealth effects. This is an orderly decline with adverse spillovers and no sharp-correction language. Score 60.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, 55).** Evidence is limited to "tightening terms and standards at banks for business borrowers" and "more cautious attitudes on the part of lending institutions", plus weak credit growth. No participant comments on bank capital or soundness, so the earlier "sound" reading is unsupported. The rubric places tightening standards and caution at 55-65, but this is credit-supply caution rather than impairment. Score 55, Low confidence.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, 52).** No actual dollar move is described by participants. "A few" thought it "might depreciate from its currently elevated level", and "some" saw possible downward pressure from current account deficits. A high level with prospective weakening nets just above neutral. Score 52, Low confidence.

**INFLATION_SERVICES (CORRECTED, N/A).** Participants give no commentary on services prices, so the dimension is N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 55).** "All the members" supported an unchanged 6-1/2 percent rate. Persisting inflation risks were a policy concern and the added restraint from tighter financial conditions was "likely to be necessary". The inflation-weighted risk statement was kept, but a stronger case than before existed for a balanced statement ("A few indicated that the decision was a close call", "several" saw a shift as advisable soon). That is hold steady with a tightening bias, tempered. Score 55.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 13).** The vote was ten to none, and all members backed steady policy. Members were "unanimous in concluding" that a shift to a balanced statement was premature, with only mild differences of timing ("close call" for a few). Score 13.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 52).** Tighter financial conditions are "a key factor underlying the economic outlook". A full paragraph covers bank standards, widening spreads, weak credit growth and equity wealth effects. In the policy discussion members noted that a shift in the published stance "might induce an undesirable softening in overall financial market conditions". The topic is prominent but framed around conditions and growth, not stability threats. Score 52.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 57).** The approved press sentence keeps risks "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate heightened inflation pressures". Members also wanted "some recognition of the emergence of increased downside risks" and viewed a move to balance as possibly advisable "in the relatively near future". No further tightening is signalled, so this is a mild tightening bias that is fading. Score 57.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52).** Whether demand would stay damped enough to offset inflation was "quite uncertain". Energy price timing had "a great deal of uncertainty", and the dollar was "another source of uncertainty". Foreign risks "might be difficult to contain". Several sources of genuine uncertainty are named, but there is no sense of unusual or unbounded uncertainty. Score 52.
