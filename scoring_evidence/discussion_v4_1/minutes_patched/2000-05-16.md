## Evidence for changed dimensions

**INFLATION_PRESSURE (CORRECTED, value unchanged at 58).** Participants see inflation pressure as modestly above target and starting to rise. Members cited "statistical and anecdotal evidence that could be read as suggesting that underlying inflation already was beginning to pick up", "early signs of a possible upturn in underlying price inflation", and that "the risks of acceleration in core prices were now appreciably higher"; against this, "unit costs ... were still remarkably subdued" and pricing power "continued to be severely constrained". Score 58, +-8, Moderate.

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The only support was a statement about widening corporate risk spreads, which does not appear in the participants' views. Participants mention only "higher financing costs" and "higher mortgage rates", which are rate levels, not spreads. N/A.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to 50).** Participants discussed the stock market only as a source of wealth effects and consumer behaviour: "some members observed" consumers' "slightly less ebullient" behaviour might reflect "the volatility and downward movement in the stock market over the course of the past several weeks", and "the experience of recent years amply demonstrated the difficulty of forecasting the performance of the stock market". There is no valuation judgement, so the rubric default of 45-55 applies. Score 50, +-10, Low.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The appreciation cited in the old analysis is not in the participants' text; the foreign sector appears only as "less of a safety valve" and "growing export demand" (several members). N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 80).** Strongly hawkish: "all the members endorsed" a 50bp increase to 6-1/2 percent as "a more forceful policy move", seeing "little risk in a relatively aggressive policy move" and the greater risk in policy being "too sluggish". Held just under the very strong band because "a number of the members" did not want to prejudge future tightening. Score 80, +-5, High.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 8).** Near-consensus: "all the members endorsed" the proposal, ten votes for, "Votes against this action: None." Only "some members" differed on whether expectations were already worsening. Score 8, +-4, High.
