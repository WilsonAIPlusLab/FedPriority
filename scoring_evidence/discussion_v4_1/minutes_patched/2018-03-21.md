Inferring a spread level from "accommodative conditions" would not be evidence. N/A under the level-governs rule.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score rested on a staff-text sentence about post-crisis regulatory reforms and stronger capital. No participant view on bank health appears in the new input. N/A.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old score rested on a staff-text sentence about the dollar's modest rise. Participants' views make no reference to the dollar or foreign exchange. N/A.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68).** The lean is tightening. "Almost all participants" favored a 25bp hike now, "all participants" saw "some further firming" as likely, and "almost all" favored a gradual approach. "A number" saw a path "slightly steeper" than previously expected, and "several" said the funds rate would likely go above its longer-run normal value for a time. The vote was unanimous. A couple favored waiting, and the gradual emphasis holds the score below the 71-80 band.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** There was no formal dissent ("Voting against this action: None"). Disagreement was limited to "a couple of participants" who saw benefits in postponing the hike, and "a range of views" on how much tightening would be needed. This is broad agreement with minor differences.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 27).** The topic gets a single paragraph of attention. "Some" noted conditions stayed accommodative despite volatility, "many" said contacts took turbulence in stride, and "a few" flagged equity valuations and risks to financial stability if conditions stay "highly accommodative". "A number" mentioned the possibility of "financial instability" from an overheated economy. This is moderate-to-limited, context rather than focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 68).** The forward signal points to tightening, gradually. The vote record says members "expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant further gradual increases", and that the strengthening outlook "increased the likelihood that a gradual upward trajectory" would be appropriate. Some participants raised statement language shifting to a neutral or restraining stance. Counterweights are that the funds rate would stay below longer-run levels "for some time" and that the path depends on incoming data.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 32).** The main uncertainty concerns fiscal policy: participants "generally regarded the magnitude and timing" of fiscal effects "as uncertain", with few historical precedents. A "strong majority" saw trade retaliation and trade policy uncertainty as downside risks. Offsetting this, near-term risks are "roughly balanced", all participants saw a stronger outlook, and inflation expectations were firmer. The result is somewhat elevated, not high.
