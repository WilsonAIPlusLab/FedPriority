## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED).** Participants gave no statement on the level or direction of credit spreads. The only credit-related content is "several participants" on the rapid growth of private credit markets, "many participants" on household credit strain (credit cards, buy-now-pay-later, delinquencies), and risks of "a sharp tightening in financial conditions". The old claim of tightening in credit-sensitive sectors is not in the text. Score stays near neutral at 52 but with Low confidence and a wider band (+-10), per the no-level-information rule.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED).** Direction: lean slightly hawkish, a hold with a modest tightening-side tilt. "All participants" judged holding the range appropriate; participants "discussed maintaining the current restrictive policy stance for longer" if inflation did not move toward 2 percent, and "various participants mentioned a willingness to tighten policy further" if inflation risks materialized, while reducing restraint was contemplated only if labor markets weakened unexpectedly. The balance sheet slowdown was described by "various participants" as having no implications for the stance. Score 56.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED).** Near-consensus. The vote record shows twelve votes for and "Voting against this action: None", and "all participants" supported holding. Differences were limited to emphasis, such as "a few participants" who could have supported the current runoff pace or a slightly higher cap, and the degree of weight on upside inflation risk. Score 14.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Moderate attention. A distinct section opens "participants who commented noted vulnerabilities to the financial system that they assessed warranted monitoring", covering bank risks (unrealized losses, CRE, uninsured deposits, cyber), "several participants" on private credit, "a few" on Treasury market resilience and "a couple" on the discount window. "A number of participants" also stressed that monetary policy should be guided by employment and inflation with other tools used first for stability risks. It is a recognizable section but not the focus of the meeting. Score 40.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED).** Mildly hawkish-of-neutral. Participants "assessed that it would take longer than previously anticipated" to gain confidence, and the statement says the Committee "does not expect it will be appropriate to reduce the target range until it has gained greater confidence". That is delayed easing rather than a tightening signal, and the language remains data-dependent and two-sided ("prepared to adjust the stance... as appropriate"). Score 55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED).** Elevated but standard in form. Participants "generally noted their uncertainty about the persistence of inflation", "many participants commented on their uncertainty about the degree of restrictiveness" (smaller effects of rates, higher equilibrium rate, lower potential), and "a number" on restrictiveness of financial conditions; members "viewed the economic outlook as uncertain". Policy was seen as "well positioned to respond", which tempers the score. Score 55.
