## Evidence for changed dimensions

**EQUITY_VALUATION (CORRECTED to N/A).** Participants' views never address equity prices or valuations; the old evidence ("asset valuations had eased from elevated levels") is not part of the new input's participants' text. Topic not raised by participants, so N/A.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED to N/A).** The old quote on "high levels of capital and liquidity" does not appear in the participants' views. The only banking reference is in George's dissent (a large hike could "adversely affect the ability of small banks to meet the credit needs of their communities"), a policy-risk concern, not an assessment of bank health. N/A.

**INFLATION_HOUSING (CORRECTED 72 to 62).** Direction: above neutral, but with thinner support than before. The "rapid house price increases" quote is gone; participants note low- and moderate-income households spending more on "essentials such as food, energy, and housing" and being "less able to bear the rapidly rising costs of these essentials", while housing activity is "softening" with mortgage rates sharply higher. Score 62.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 78).** Direction: strongly hawkish. "Almost all participants agreed" a 75 bp hike was appropriate (one favored 50 bp), "ongoing increases" appropriate, 50 or 75 bp likely next meeting, and "an even more restrictive stance could be appropriate"; "all" supported balance sheet reduction. Tempered slightly by one 50 bp preference and the dissent. Score 78.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 30).** Direction: broad agreement with one formal dissent. President George dissented because a large increase "would add to uncertainty about policy" alongside runoff; one participant preferred 50 bp. Participants "concurred" on the economic assessment and the need for a restrictive stance; differences concern size/pace only. Score 30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 27).** Direction: limited. "Many participants" noted a "notable tightening of financial conditions" helping reduce inflation; tighter financial conditions were listed as a downside growth risk; George raised small-bank credit. Context rather than a focus; no discussion of financial-stability vulnerabilities. Score 27.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 83).** Direction: clear tightening signal. "Participants continued to anticipate that ongoing increases in the target range ... would be appropriate", an "increase of 50 or 75 basis points would likely be appropriate" in July, and the statement "anticipates that ongoing increases ... will be appropriate" with the soft-landing sentence removed; data dependence noted but secondary. Score 83.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52).** Direction: elevated but not extreme. "Participants judged that uncertainty about economic growth over the next couple of years was elevated"; the "timing and magnitude" of supply-demand easing was "uncertain"; "a couple" cited conflicting GDP/GDI signals. Offsetting this, the vote record shows the Committee omitted the high-uncertainty sentence on Ukraine, and inflation views were firm. Score 52.
