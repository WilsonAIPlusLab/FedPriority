## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 53 +-8, Low).** Participants say nothing about spread levels or moves; a few cite "restrictive credit conditions" restraining equipment and residential investment, and the CRE deterioration is mentioned as a downside risk. With direction and level both absent, the score stays in 45-55 on the slightly stressed side, and confidence falls to Low.

**BANKING_STRESS (CORRECTED, 45 +-8, Low).** The old evidence phrases are not found in the new input. The only participant text is that "many" participants' downside-risk list includes "a deterioration in conditions in domestic CRE markets" and "a potential reemergence of stresses in the banking sector". No participant calls the system sound or healthy, and none describes current stress, so the score sits slightly below neutral, with Low confidence for the brevity.

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS (CORRECTED, 50 +-5, Moderate).** Some participants observed that "longer-term inflation expectations appeared to remain well anchored" across surveys and market measures. The earlier claim of rising short-term measures is not in the participants' views, so the score moves to the anchored-at-target midpoint.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 45).** All participants favored holding the range; "the policy rate was likely at its peak" and "almost all participants" judged a less restrictive stance appropriate at some point this year if the economy evolved as expected. Offsetting this, participants noted "strong economic momentum and disappointing readings on inflation" and stressed the risk of easing too quickly. Hold with a mild easing bias gives 45.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 14).** The vote record shows twelve votes for and "Voting against this action: None"; "all participants judged" the hold appropriate. Only small differences in emphasis appear (some saw the inflation increases as broad based, a few cited residual seasonality), so near-consensus, 14.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 25).** Attention is limited: some participants noted uncertainty about the restrictiveness of financial conditions, a few cited restrictive credit conditions, and CRE and banking stresses appear only as items in a downside-risk list. No extended discussion, so 25.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 38).** Participants "did not expect it would be appropriate to reduce the target range" until greater confidence that inflation is moving sustainably to 2 percent, and stressed data dependence. At the same time, almost all expected a move to a less restrictive stance this year, and policy was said to be "well positioned" to respond either way. An easing lean with data dependence gives 38.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52).** "Participants generally noted their uncertainty about the persistence of high inflation" and that recent data "had not increased their confidence"; "many" pointed to difficulty assessing immigration effects, and some added uncertainty about the effect of past policy on demand. The vote record states the outlook "is uncertain". The language is elevated but not extreme, so 52.
