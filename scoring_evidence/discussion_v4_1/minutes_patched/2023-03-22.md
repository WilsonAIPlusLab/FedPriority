**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED).** Prominent to dominant. Most of the discussion addresses banking-sector stress: the calming effect of official actions, the importance of regional and community banks (several participants), credit tightening, and the call to use "liquidity and lender-of-last-resort tools ... to mitigate future financial stability risks".
