## Evidence for changed dimensions

**PRODUCTIVITY (FILLED, 58 +-8, Low).** Participants see productivity growth as somewhat above trend: "a few participants observed that the pickup in productivity growth last year was a welcome development helping to bolster potential output and damp inflationary pressures", and a strengthening in productivity growth was listed as an upside risk. Only a few participants speak to it, hence Low confidence. Score 58.

**TREASURY_STRESS (FILLED, 42 +-8, Low).** Participants "discussed the decline ... in yields on longer-term Treasury securities" and "several" worried about a flat curve, but also noted unusually low term premiums; this is yield and curve commentary with no description of liquidity or functioning problems, so it is normal functioning. Score 42.

**EQUITY_VALUATION (FILLED, 47 +-8, Low).** Participants "noted that asset valuations had recovered strongly", with no stretched-valuation or distress language; price recovery without valuation commentary defaults to the neutral band. Score 47.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 47 +-6, Moderate).** Balanced to slightly dovish: the Committee held the range and "participants generally agreed that a patient approach ... remained appropriate"; "a majority" expected no change for the remainder of the year, "some participants" would likely raise "modestly later this year" if growth stays above trend, and "some" thought the response to labor tightening could be modest while "several" said views could shift in either direction. Score 47.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18 +-5, High).** Near-consensus: unanimous vote ("Voting against this action: None"), participants "agreed" on the hold and on the patient approach. Differences are emphasis only (a majority for no change this year versus some foreseeing a modest hike; "several" vs "a couple" on how binding "patient" is; "several" on SEP communication). Score 18.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 38 +-8, Moderate).** Moderate-limited attention: one paragraph on financial developments, with "several participants" voicing concern about the flat yield curve and "several" about nonfinancial corporate leverage and debt issuance, and "a few participants" saying lower-for-longer policy "could lead to greater financial stability risks" (a couple noting macroprudential tools). It is context rather than the focus of the meeting. Score 38.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 42 +-6, Moderate).** Neutral to mildly easing-leaning, data-dependent: "patient" approach retained, "a majority" expected "leaving the target range unchanged for the remainder of the year", decisions "would depend on ongoing assessments", views "could shift in either direction"; only "some participants" foresaw a modest rise later in the year. The vote record states the Committee "will be patient" on future adjustments. Score 42.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 52 +-8, Moderate).** Elevated: "participants also noted significant uncertainties surrounding their economic outlooks", "a few" cited "a high level of uncertainty" on trade and Brexit, and the future course of policy was described as "unusually uncertain"; "a couple" saw adverse risks as somewhat lower than in January and risks were listed on both sides, so not extreme. Score 52.
