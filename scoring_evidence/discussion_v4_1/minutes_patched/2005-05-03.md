## Evidence for changed dimensions

**CREDIT_SPREADS (CORRECTED, 53, +-8, Low).** The old score of 28 rested on the phrase "narrow risk spreads in bond and loan markets", which does not appear in the participants' text. The text says only that "equity prices had fallen and risk spreads had widened", and that this did "not appear to signal the onset of a sharp retrenchment in investors' willingness to bear risk". Participants thus describe a direction (widening) with no statement of the level. Under the V4.1 rule the score sits just above 50 on the side of the move, with Low confidence.

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH (CORRECTED, N/A).** The old score of 58 rested on "Dollar rose on balance", which is not in the participants' text. The only dollar reference is that "the impact of past dollar depreciation should work to boost exports and slow the rise in imports". That is a trade-adjustment comment inside a current-account remark, not a view on dollar strength. Dollar strength is therefore treated as not specifically discussed.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 68).** The direction is hawkish. "all members favored raising the target federal funds rate 25 basis points to 3 percent". "all members regarded the stance of policy as accommodative and judged that the current level of short-term rates remained too low". Members agreed to "continue along the course of removing policy accommodation at a measured pace". The score is held below the strong-hawkish band because "most members" saw the slowdown as transitory, "a few members" worried about a larger-than-expected demand moderation, and the pace is a measured 25bp. The vote record confirms the 25bp move.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 18).** Near-consensus. The vote record reads "Votes against this action: None." and the discussion says "all members" favored the hike. Differences are minor: "some members" wanted forward-looking language pared back and "some members" questioned the "appropriate policy" risk wording, but the Committee "all agreed to retain" the language.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 24).** Limited. Financial conditions appear as context: equity prices that "had fallen and risk spreads had widened" without signaling a retrenchment in risk-taking, and "signs of possible speculative excesses in some areas" of housing. Neither is the focus of the policy discussion.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 62).** Mildly tightening. "most members viewed the forward-looking language ... that policy accommodation could be removed at a pace that is 'likely to be measured'" as reasonable, and "all members agreed to retain" it. "Some members" wanted it pared back and "a number" noted it would not prevent "either a pause or a step-up". The signal is continued measured firming, hedged by data dependence.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 62).** High, though the outlook is described as "generally ... favorable". "there was also broad recognition of greater uncertainty attending the outlook for both inflation and output growth". "considerable uncertainty surrounded the degree of pass-through from energy prices". "For many, heightened economic uncertainty ... placed a premium on flexibility". Much of this is concrete rather than rhetorical, so it sits at the low end of the High band.
