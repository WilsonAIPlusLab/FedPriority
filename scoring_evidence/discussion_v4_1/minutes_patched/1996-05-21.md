## Evidence for changed dimensions

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (FILLED, 55).** Participants lean slightly hawkish while holding. All members supported an unchanged degree of reserve pressure, but they agreed the inflation risk balance "had shifted substantially" and that "policy might need to be firmed at some point." Some members said the Committee must "anticipate, and act to preclude, a rise in the core rate of inflation," and one view favored firming "sooner rather than later." Offsetting this, the members saw policy as "appropriately positioned" and "financial conditions were not so obviously stimulative as to counsel a need for any immediate tightening." Hold with a tightening bias gives 55.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY (FILLED, 17).** Participants show broad agreement with minor emphasis differences. The vote record has ten votes for the action and "None" against, and all members supported both the unchanged stance and a symmetric directive. Differences were limited to the risk outlook ("views differed to some extent"; some saw balanced risks, others worried about resource pressures) and the timing of any firming (a view expressed for earlier action; some wanting to come into July without a tightening presumption). There were no formal dissents and no sharp exchanges, so 17.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS (FILLED, 18).** Little attention goes to financial stability. Financial conditions appear as a driver of demand ("favorable financial conditions, notably the relatively low interest rates", wealth effects from the stock market) and in the policy discussion ("financial conditions were now exerting more restraint"), and "some members" questioned stock market sustainability. There is no discussion of financial-system risk, so this is brief-to-limited mention, scored 18.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (FILLED, 55).** The forward signal is neutral with a slight tightening tilt. All members preferred a symmetric directive, with "the probability of developments ... that would warrant a change in policy before the next meeting ... quite low." Some members said an asymmetric directive would imply a "predisposition ... to tighten" and preferred to enter the July meeting "without such a presumption," though they would act "if the evidence warranted." The "firmed at some point" language and the planned reassessment in early July keep it a little above 50, so 55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (FILLED, 62).** Participants express elevated-to-high uncertainty about inflation. The minutes say the inflation outlook "was clouded by substantial uncertainty," with food and energy effects "difficult to gauge," and that it was "uncertain how long the period of relatively restrained increases in labor compensation would last." Members cited "considerable uncertainties surrounding the relationship of output to inflation," and whether the favorable outcome would persist "was an open question." Views on risks differed, but the growth outlook itself ("solid and balanced momentum") was not described as unusually uncertain, so 62.
