## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document treated as a normal (not special-topic) meeting: it contains a policy vote (hold 0-1/4, new outcome-based guidance, two dissents) and a policy-stance discussion.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 30: "inflation remained subdued"; "participants still generally judged that the overall effect of the pandemic on prices was disinflationary"; "a number of participants projected that inflation would run below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective for a significant period"; offset slightly by "consumer prices had increased more quickly than expected". Below target.
- OUTPUT_GAP 27: "Economic activity and employment... remained well below their levels at the beginning of the year"; "labor market was a long way from being fully recovered"; recovery "faster than expected" from a "depressed" Q2. Well below potential.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 27: "regained roughly half of the 22 million jobs that were lost"; "a long way from being fully recovered"; permanent layoffs risk. Substantial slack.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 38: "consumer spending was rebounding sharply... recovered about three-fourths of its earlier decline"; services "subdued for some time"; business investment "had plummeted... appeared to have begun to turn around". Recovering but still below trend level.
- PRODUCTIVITY: N/A, not discussed (technological disruption mentioned only as limiting firms' pricing power).
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 30: "financial conditions were generally accommodative"; "Overall financial conditions had improved"; offset by "surveys of credit availability indicated that bank lending was tight". Accommodative, bright line < 50.
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A; only generic "smooth market functioning" in the context of asset purchases, no Treasury-specific discussion by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 50: no spread/yield wording; rests on availability only: "Bank contacts reported ample capacity to lend to creditworthy borrowers; however, surveys of credit availability indicated that bank lending was tight"; "pace of corporate downgrades... decreased significantly"; "delinquency rates on business loans had risen noticeably"; Fed facilities "supporting the flow of credit". Mixed, Low confidence.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A, no equity discussion.
- BANKING_STRESS 45: "Bank contacts reported ample capacity to lend"; "several participants noted the stress that small- and medium-sized banks could face from defaults on loans to small businesses and CRE". Healthy with conditional concerns.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_GOODS 58: price increases "in sectors such as consumer durables in which demand had risen".
- INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A, only services spending discussed, not prices.
- INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A, no housing price discussion.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 40: "market-based measures of inflation compensation had increased moderately... although they remained low"; "inflation running persistently below" goal; aim for expectations "well anchored". Somewhat below target.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 20: "Most participants supported providing more explicit outcome-based forward guidance" keeping the rate at the ELB until employment and inflation criteria met; asset purchases "at least at the current pace"; "A couple... preferred even stronger" guidance; "several" preferred old guidance but still "maintain an accommodative stance... for some time". Strong dovish; dissents run in both directions.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 42: two formal dissents in opposite directions (Kaplan wants more flexibility; Kashkari wants a stronger core-inflation threshold); "Almost all members" agreed on the guidance; "several" preferred retaining the old guidance, "a couple" wanted stronger; disagreement is about guidance design, not the economic assessment.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 40: a full paragraph on financial conditions, credit quality, bank stress; "a couple of participants indicated that highly accommodative financial market conditions could lead to excessive risk-taking and to a buildup of financial imbalances"; several concerned that guidance "could contribute to a buildup of financial imbalances". Moderate attention.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 6: "likely... maintain the current target range until labor market conditions were judged to be consistent with... maximum employment and inflation had risen to 2 percent and was on track to moderately exceed 2 percent for some time"; the target range "would remain at the ELB until these criteria were achieved". Strong threshold-based easing commitment, qualified as "not an unconditional commitment".
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 72: "Participants continued to see the uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook as very elevated"; "outlook for inflation was viewed as highly uncertain"; path "highly dependent on the course of the virus". High, genuine uncertainty.
