# Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document type: minutes participants' views plus vote record. Contains a policy vote (9-1) and a forward-guidance discussion, so NOT special-topic.

INFLATION_PRESSURE: 35 +-5 Moderate. "Inflation was continuing to run below the Committee's longer-run objective"; "Most participants anticipated that inflation was likely to edge lower in the near term" (oil/commodity/import prices); "a few expressed concern that inflation might persist below the Committee's objective for quite some time." Below target, edging lower, not a deflation scare.
OUTPUT_GAP: 40 +-5 Moderate. "underutilization of labor resources was gradually diminishing"; "a number of participants noted that underutilization of labor market resources remained"; "resource slack diminished" (as a future process). Below potential, gap closing.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 41 +-5 Moderate. "solid job gains and a lower unemployment rate"; "relatively few pointing to emerging wage pressures"; "A couple of participants" said part-time for economic reasons and falling participation mean the unemployment rate understates underutilization. Improving but slack remains; no unemployment level stated.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 50 +-6 Moderate. "Household spending advanced at a moderate pace"; "recent retail or auto sales had been robust" (contacts in several parts of the country); "recovery in the housing sector remained slow"; "Many participants" saw the energy decline boosting spending. Net at trend.
PRODUCTIVITY: N/A. Not discussed by participants.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 25 +-6 Moderate. "financial conditions remained highly accommodative" (some participants, despite mid-October turbulence); statement language on "highly supportive of growth" appears in vote record. Bright line (accommodative below 50) satisfied.
TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. Mid-October turbulence is mentioned in general terms only; no Treasury-market dysfunction or specific Treasury discussion by participants.
CREDIT_SPREADS: 40 +-10 Low. Availability only, no spread wording: "Contacts in several regions reported ready availability of credit, strong loan growth"; "some recent improvement in the availability of mortgage credit." Rests on availability: somewhat easy credit, below-normal stress.
EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A. Stocks appear only in a list of asset prices moved by foreign-growth concerns, no direction or valuation view stated by participants.
BANKING_STRESS: N/A. Banks not discussed (only a couple of participants on collecting data on wholesale funding markets, no stress assessment).
DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 60 +-6 Moderate. "the strengthening of the dollar over the period"; "offset to the higher dollar and weaker foreign growth" (several participants); many saw net-export effects as modest. Appreciation, so above 50.
INFLATION_GOODS: 35. "decline in oil and other commodity prices and lower import prices"; energy prices falling significantly.
INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A. Not discussed.
INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A. Not discussed (housing activity only, not shelter prices).
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 43. "Survey-based measures of inflation expectations remained well anchored" but market-based compensation declined; "many participants observed that the Committee should remain attentive to evidence of a possible downward shift in longer-term inflation expectations."
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 46 +-6 Moderate. Asset purchases concluded (all but one) and rates held at 0-1/4 with "considerable time" retained (all but one); "Some participants preferred to eliminate" the "considerable time" language (mild hawkish), versus "other participants" and "A couple" wanting it kept to avoid unintended tightening, and "A couple of others" saying guidance implied too early a liftoff (dovish); dissenter wanted continued purchases and stronger commitment (dovish). Hold steady with balanced-to-slightly-dovish tilt.
DISSENT_INTENSITY: 32 +-6 High. One formal dissent (Kocherlakota, dovish: wanted to continue purchases and commit to the range until projected inflation returns to 2 percent); 9-1 vote; moderate differences over "considerable time" wording ("Some" vs "other", "a couple"); economic assessment broadly shared.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 40 +-8 Moderate. "Many participants commented on the turbulence in financial markets that occurred in mid-October"; "Some participants" on highly accommodative conditions and further turbulence ahead; a couple on wholesale funding data; "A number of participants noted that financial stability is a necessary condition" for the longer-run goals (strategy-statement discussion); committee considered and declined a statement reference to markets.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 37 +-8 Moderate. "Most participants judged that it would be helpful to include new language" stressing data dependence; "Most participants supported retaining" below-normal-rates-for-some-time language; "considerable time" retained; statement says increases could come sooner or later than anticipated. Easing lean with data dependence, near liftoff debate.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 35 +-8 Moderate. "a number of participants noted that economic growth ... might be slower ... if the foreign economic or financial situation deteriorated significantly"; "increased downside risks in Europe, China, and Japan"; "inherent uncertainty of the economic and financial outlook"; but "Most viewed the risks ... as nearly balanced" and "many" saw limited domestic effects. Somewhat elevated, not unusual.
