# Part 2: Evidence and scores (discussion_m, participants' views only)

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 47 +-5 Moderate. "Inflation had increased in recent quarters and moved close to the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective; excluding energy and food prices, inflation was little changed and had continued to run somewhat below 2 percent"; "some participants cautioned that progress ... should not be overstated"; "a few other participants" saw data stronger than expected; "some other participants" worry about upside risk from undershooting unemployment. Near target, core slightly below, so just under 50.
- OUTPUT_GAP 49 +-4 Moderate. "Nearly all participants judged that the U.S. economy was operating at or near maximum employment"; activity "at a moderate pace", GDP slow this quarter on temporary factors. At/near potential.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 54 +-6 Moderate. "Some participants cited anecdotal evidence of a tightening of labor markets"; "Business contacts in many Districts reported difficulty recruiting qualified workers"; but "several other participants cited evidence that some slack remained"; "A few ... slack ... minimal". Near full employment, leaning slightly tight.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 52 +-5 Moderate. "Household spending had continued to rise moderately"; consumer slowdown "likely be temporary"; "Many reported that manufacturing activity ... had strengthened further"; "business fixed investment appeared to have firmed somewhat", but "several other Districts" waiting on policy clarity. At/slightly above trend.
- PRODUCTIVITY N/A. Not discussed by participants.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 36 +-6 Moderate. "financial conditions remained accommodative despite the rise in longer-term interest rates"; "several of them citing it [equity rise] as contributing to an easing of financial conditions". Accommodative, so below 50 (bright line).
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A. No participant comment on Treasury market functioning (rising longer-term rates only).
- CREDIT_SPREADS 40 +-12 Low. No spread wording. Rests on availability/risk-asset language: "prices of other risk assets, such as ... high-yield corporate bonds, and commercial real estate, had also risen significantly" (easy conditions) versus "access to mortgage credit that was still restricted for some borrowers" (housing only). Net somewhat favorable; Low confidence.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 32 +-8 Moderate. "Some participants viewed equity prices as quite high relative to standard valuation measures"; "farmland valuations ... remain quite high"; "a few" attributed rally to tax-cut hopes or risk tolerance. Valuation-concern view stated by some: elevated.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A. Banks not discussed.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A. Dollar/FX not discussed.
- INFLATION_GOODS N/A. Only energy-price effect on headline mentioned; no goods category assessment.
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A. Not discussed as inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 47. "inflation expectations that appeared to have remained well anchored" (several); market-based compensation "low" though "somewhat above the levels seen last year"; survey measures mixed (Michigan stable, NY Fed 3-year up). Anchored near/slightly below target.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 58 +-5 Moderate. "Almost all participants saw the incoming data as consistent with an increase of 25 basis points"; "a gradual pace of rate increases was likely to be appropriate"; "several other participants" judged goal essentially met, which "could warrant a faster pace"; "several" stressed gradual removal; one member (Kashkari) dissented for holding. Hike with gradual lean, some faster-leaning, one dove.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 38 +-6 Moderate. Vote record: nine for, one against (Kashkari, "premature to raise ... await additional information on the amount of slack ... and increased evidence that inflation would stabilize"). Split camps on whether inflation goal is met ("several" vs "several other"), slack views differ, but action near-consensus and "Members generally noted that the increase ... did not reflect changes".
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 35 +-8 Moderate. A full paragraph on financial conditions, equity prices, risk-asset and farmland valuations; "a number of participants" noted financial conditions posing upside/downside risks ("significant correction"). Context-level, not the focus.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 62 +-6 Moderate. "economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant gradual increases in the federal funds rate"; path "would depend on the economic outlook as informed by incoming data"; "several" willing for faster pace, "several" stressing gradual. Mild tightening bias.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 52 +-8 Moderate. "Participants continued to underscore the considerable uncertainty about the timing and nature of potential changes to fiscal policies"; "In view of the substantial uncertainty, about half ... did not incorporate explicit assumptions about fiscal policy"; uncertainty about the neutral real rate; yet "risks ... roughly balanced" and views "essentially unchanged". Elevated mainly on fiscal policy.
