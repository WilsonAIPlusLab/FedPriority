## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 32 +-6 Moderate: "participants agreed that underlying inflation currently was subdued and was likely to remain so for some time"; "core measures of inflation were subdued"; "pricing power remained limited... unit labor costs were still falling"; "Some noted the risk that... inflation could edge further below" vs "others... saw inflation risks as tilted toward the upside" (and "many participants saw upward pressures on commodity prices"). Subdued, below the preferred rate, with a two-sided risk debate.
- OUTPUT_GAP 30 +-6 Moderate: "participants agreed there was considerable slack in resource utilization"; "output well below potential over the next couple of years". Judgments of the size of the slack varied (UI extensions, lower potential vs. higher potential from productivity).
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 22 +-6 Moderate: "weakness in labor markets continued to be an important concern"; "few firms were hiring"; "unusually large fraction... part time for economic reasons"; "unemployment declining only gradually". No unemployment level quoted; layoffs diminishing.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 38 +-6 Moderate: "Consumer spending appeared to be increasing modestly"; "moderate growth in demands"; firms "limiting investment outlays"; "substantial excess capacity"; offset by "solid growth in business spending on high-tech equipment"; housing stabilized but no sizable contribution; commercial construction trending down.
- PRODUCTIVITY 66 +-8 Moderate: "unusually rapid productivity growth seen in recent quarters"; "large productivity gains, as in recent quarters"; contacts expect to meet demand via hours and productivity. Well above trend, with debate over sustainability.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 42 +-7 Moderate: "Policymakers judged that financial conditions were, on balance, about as supportive of growth as when the Committee met in December"; markets "function significantly better than early last year"; offset by "All categories of bank loans... continued to contract sharply". Supportive, so below 50 (bright line met), with credit availability holding it near neutral.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 47 +-10 Low: "private credit spreads narrowed somewhat" (direction only, no level); banks "had ceased tightening standards on most types of business and consumer loans, though commercial real estate loans were a notable exception"; loans contracting sharply. Direction-only rule gives 45-55 on side of the move; availability evidence is mixed. Rests partly on availability.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 50 +-6 Low: "volatility in equity prices increased late in the intermeeting period, broad equity price indexes were about unchanged overall". Price report only, no valuation view.
- BANKING_STRESS 53 +-10 Low: "All categories of bank loans... continued to contract sharply"; "commercial real estate loans were a notable exception"; but "banks had ceased tightening standards" and "some banks were starting to look for opportunities to expand lending". No capital or health language; mixed, near neutral.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: dollar/FX not discussed by participants.
- INFLATION_GOODS N/A: no goods-price discussion (only headline and energy swings).
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING 38: "One participant noted that core inflation had been held down... by unusually slow increases in the price index for shelter"; single participant, soft shelter inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 52: "Survey measures of expected future inflation were fairly stable"; market-based measures "suggested continuing concern... about the risk of higher medium-term inflation"; "others, focusing on risks to inflation expectations". Anchored, with some upside worry.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 36 +-7 Moderate: "members agreed that no changes" to asset purchases or the 0-1/4 range; "exceptionally low rates for an extended period" retained; purchases "gradually slow"; one member wanted "holdings" wording (flexibility to sell or buy), others judged it "premature"; Hoenig's hawkish dissent. Hold in a QE-completion setting with accommodation retained; a hawkish minority.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 38 +-6 Moderate: one formal dissent (Hoenig, wanted to drop "extended period" and begin raising rates modestly soon); economic views otherwise broadly shared, with split risk views on inflation ("some" vs "others") and a "holdings" wording difference.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 40 +-8 Moderate: conditions paragraph on spreads, equities, bank lending, standards; facility closures "prepared to modify these plans if necessary to support financial stability"; Hoenig on "future financial imbalances". Moderate attention.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 18 +-6 Moderate: "likely to warrant exceptionally low rates for an extended period"; "path of short-term rates going forward would depend on the evolution of the economic outlook". Low-for-long retained, with the hawkish dissent favoring dropping it.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 38 +-8 Moderate: "prospects for job growth remained an important source of uncertainty"; "uncertainty about sales prospects and future tax and regulatory policies"; "upside and downside risks... roughly balanced"; "judgments about the degree of slack varied". Somewhat elevated, not unusual.
