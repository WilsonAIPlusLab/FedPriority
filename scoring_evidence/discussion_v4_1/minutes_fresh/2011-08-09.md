## Part 2: Evidence and scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 46 +-7 Moderate. "Inflation had moderated in recent months"; "Participants generally noted that, with apparently significant slack ... inflation was likely to decline somewhat over time"; but "a number of participants noted that core inflation had moved up, on balance, since last fall" and "Some indicated that the rise in inflation from very low levels reflected the Committee's accommodative stance". Slightly below to near target, moderating, with a core-up minority.

OUTPUT_GAP 32 +-6 High. "apparently significant slack in labor and product markets" (participants generally); "low rates of resource utilization" (policy action text); "underlying strength of the economic recovery remained uncertain". Below potential. A couple of participants suggested potential might be lower than thought, which narrows the gap a little.

LABOR_TIGHTNESS 27 +-5 High. "overall labor market conditions had deteriorated in recent months" (generally); "exceptionally high level of long-term unemployment" (a couple); "unemployment rate to remain well above ... levels consistent with the Committee's mandate" (some members). Loose labor market.

CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 36 +-6 High. "weakness in consumer spending ... was unexpected" (most); "flattening out of consumer spending"; equipment and software "continued to advance, although at a slower pace"; housing "quite subdued", structures "remained low". Below trend, net.

PRODUCTIVITY N/A. Not discussed (the potential-output remarks are not about productivity growth).

FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 57 +-8 Moderate. "financial markets were volatile"; "reduced willingness of investors to bear risk"; "conditions in funding markets had tightened"; "still-tight credit conditions" (several). Offset: "condition of U.S. banks had strengthened". Somewhat tight.

TREASURY_STRESS N/A. Only "broad declines in stock prices and interest rates" and debt-ceiling negotiations; no market-functioning problem described by participants. The Aug 1 videoconference text is staff-reported or contingency planning.

CREDIT_SPREADS 56 +-10 Low. No spread, yield or premium wording with a level. Rests on availability: "still-tight credit conditions" (several), "funding markets had tightened", "reduced willingness of investors to bear risk"; offset by credit quality of businesses and households "continued to improve".

EQUITY_VALUATION 54 +-8 Low. Price move only: "broad declines in stock prices" attributed to weaker growth outlook and less risk-bearing; no valuation view.

BANKING_STRESS 33 +-7 Moderate. "the condition of U.S. banks had strengthened in recent quarters and ... credit quality ... had continued to improve". Healthy, with funding-market tightening as a minor offset.

DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A. Not discussed.

INFLATION_GOODS 50 (value only). "surge in energy and other commodity prices" had pushed inflation up; "prices of energy and some commodities have declined from their earlier peaks". Surge then reversal, net stable.
INFLATION_SERVICES N/A. Not discussed.
INFLATION_HOUSING N/A. Only housing activity and home prices, not shelter inflation.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 50 (value only). "Measures of inflation expectations had remained stable"; longer-term expectations "remained stable".

HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 27 +-6 Moderate. "most members agreed that the economic outlook had deteriorated by enough to warrant a Committee response"; "some members expressed the view that additional accommodation was warranted"; "A few members felt that recent economic developments justified a more substantial move"; tools discussed (forward guidance, asset purchases, maturity extension, lower IOER). Counter: "some participants judged that none of the tools ... would likely do much" and feared inflation; three dissents on the hawkish side. Majority moderate dovish.

DISSENT_INTENSITY 70 +-7 Moderate. Vote record: three dissents (Fisher, Kocherlakota, Plosser) with differing reasons (nonmonetary restraints and fear of looking responsive to markets; inflation up and unemployment down so more accommodation wrong; date seen as unconditional commitment, statement too negative). Also "some" saw tools as ineffective, "one member" worried about the date, "a few" wanted more. Clear camps, no sign of heated exchange.

FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 38 +-8 Moderate. A paragraph on market volatility (Europe, debt ceiling, funding markets, bank condition, risk appetite), "potential financial and economic spillovers if the situation in Europe were to deteriorate"; dissenter Fisher cites market volatility. Several address it, but the focus is the real economy and policy tools.

FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 5 +-3 High. The Committee moved to date-based guidance: "at least through mid-2013"; "Reinforcing the Committee's forward guidance ... was seen as a possible way to reduce interest rates"; most members found it useful. Strongest easing commitment on the scale; a few preferred state-based guidance, which does not change direction.

UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 62 +-8 Moderate. "Participants generally saw the degree of uncertainty surrounding the outlook for economic growth as having risen appreciably"; "Many participants also saw an increase in the downside risks"; "reasons for the weakness were unclear" (a couple); vulnerable to adverse shocks (several). Participants did not anticipate a downturn.
