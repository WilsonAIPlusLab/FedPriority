## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 38: "inflation was still seen as running below the Committee's longer-run objective"; "inflation had moved up recently from low levels"; "Some participants expressed concern about the persistence of below-trend inflation"; "some others expected a faster pickup in inflation or saw upside risks". Below target, edging up. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- OUTPUT_GAP 38: "Many judged that slack remained elevated, and a number of them thought it was greater than measured by the official unemployment rate"; activity "rebounding" after a Q1 decline; unemployment seen heading to its normal value only gradually. Below potential. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 38: "Labor market conditions generally continued to improve"; "Many judged that slack remained elevated"; "Aggregate wage measures continued to rise at only a modest rate"; "Several of those reports pointed to an absence of wage pressures" versus "some others" citing tight pockets. Improving from a slack level. UNC +-6, High.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 46: "Household spending appeared to have risen moderately"; motor vehicle sales "rising strongly"; but "several participants" saw soft retail and health-care data; housing "still-soft" ("Many participants expressed concern"); capital spending "likely to increase" ("several heard" plans to raise capex). Net near trend, slightly soft. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- PRODUCTIVITY: N/A. Only a passing reference to real wages in line with productivity growth; no view on productivity.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 36: "Favorable financial conditions appeared be supporting economic activity"; "improving credit conditions"; "monetary policy needed to continue to promote the favorable financial conditions"; offset by restrictive mortgage credit for low-score households and low volatility/risk-taking worries (not tightness). Accommodative/supportive, so below 50. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. No participant discusses Treasury market functioning.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 40: no spread, yield or premium wording. Availability only: "a number of participants reported increases in C&I lending", "better credit quality", "small businesses reported improvements in credit availability"; mortgage credit "restrictive ... for households with low credit scores". Rests on availability. UNC +-10, Low.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A. Equities appear only in "low implied volatility in equity, currency, and fixed-income markets" with no valuation or price-move view.
- BANKING_STRESS: N/A. Bank C&I lending and borrower credit quality are mentioned, but no view on bank health or capital.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: N/A. Currency appears only as part of the implied-volatility remark; no dollar direction.
- INFLATION_GOODS: N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A. Housing is discussed as activity and construction, not prices or rents.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 46: "longer-run inflation expectations remained stable"; "Several others, however, remained concerned that persistent low inflation in Europe and Japan could eventually erode inflation expectations"; "some others ... saw upside risks to inflation and inflation expectations". Anchored, slight downside worry.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 45 (+-6, Moderate): asset purchases cut by a further $15 billion, with "participants generally agreed" that they would likely end later this year; rate held at 0-1/4 percent. Views split: "a couple" would "strengthen its commitment" to accommodation; "some other participants" favored "a more rapid move to begin raising the federal funds rate"; "a number" worried about a more gradual path. Taper in progress, hold with considerable-time guidance, balanced camps.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 22 (+-7, Moderate): "Voting against this action: None." Differences are of emphasis (some want stronger accommodation commitment, some a quicker lift-off; "though one participant emphasized the risks" of overshooting on unemployment). "Participants generally supported the Committee's current guidance."
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 35 (+-8, Moderate): a paragraph in which "some participants" worry that low implied volatility and "increased risk-taking" show investors are underpricing risk; "They agreed that the Committee should continue to carefully monitor financial conditions"; supervisory measures mentioned. Context and a limited-focus topic, not dominant.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 25 (+-8, Moderate): "likely would be appropriate to maintain the current target range ... for a considerable time after the asset purchase program ends"; rates may stay "below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run"; "Some participants" wanted stress on data dependence, "a couple" wanted a stronger accommodation commitment, "some other" wanted a more rapid rise. Mild easing bias with data dependence; the final-taper signal is mildly offsetting.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 30 (+-7, Moderate): "Most participants viewed the risks ... as broadly balanced"; "a couple of participants expressed uncertainty about the outlook for economic growth in Japan and China"; "several saw developments in Iraq and Ukraine as posing possible downside risks"; housing delay versus structural change debated. Normal caveats with a few specific sources.
