## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Staff-style background text (the "information reviewed at this meeting" directive paragraphs: unemployment 5.5 percent, dollar "declined slightly", yields) was not used as participant evidence.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 50 +-8 Moderate. "inflation had been more subdued than many had expected"; "a number of members ... mild optimism" vs "many viewed as more likely the prospect of little or no progress toward price stability" and "some expressed concern about the potential for an upward drift". Subdued but not falling; risks tilted slightly up; net near midpoint.
- OUTPUT_GAP 53 +-7 Moderate. "generally high levels of resource use, including tight labor markets in many parts of the country"; "a number of members believed that growth around potential was a probable outcome". High resource use implies modestly above, growth near potential; no explicit "above potential".
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 56 +-7 Moderate. "tight labor markets in many parts of the country"; "after an extended period of relatively low unemployment"; wage restraint "despite tight labor markets". Tight but wages restrained.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 54 +-6 Moderate. "consumer spending appeared to be on a firm growth trend" (cautionary note from auto sales, October retail sales); "further sizable growth in business fixed investment, but at a pace well below that experienced in recent years"; manufacturing stagnating. Net at/slightly above trend, slowing.
- PRODUCTIVITY N/A. Not discussed by participants.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 36 +-8 Moderate. "the ready availability of financing on relatively attractive terms"; "ample availability of financing on relatively liberal terms"; "favorable developments this year in the bond and stock markets"; policy "somewhat restrictive" (several) but not inhibiting "declines in intermediate- and long-term interest rates, increases in stock prices, or the availability of financing". Supportive, below 50.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A. No participant comment on Treasury market functioning (Lindsey's flat yield curve point is a policy signal, not dysfunction).
- CREDIT_SPREADS 35 +-12 Low. No spread/yield/premium wording; rests on availability: "ample availability of financing on relatively liberal terms", "availability of financing from lending institutions". Per V4.1.1 availability-based, Low confidence, UNC at least +-10.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 46 +-10 Low. Only price moves/wealth channel, no valuation view: "increases in stock prices", "substantial improvement in the value of financial assets this year". Price-move default 45-55 on side of the (rising) move.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A. Banks' condition not discussed (only lending availability, used for credit spreads).
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A. Dollar not discussed by participants (only in directive background text).
- INFLATION_GOODS 40. "strong competition was holding down price inflation and ... producers were benefiting from soft prices of industrial materials".
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS N/A. Not discussed (wage/benefit costs only).
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 41 +-6 Moderate. "all but one member favored or could accept an unchanged policy stance"; "In the view of some ... an easing at some point would be needed"; one member (Lindsey) wanted immediate easing; "remaining members" preferred easing-tilted directive but accepted symmetric; others unchanged with next move open; "several" saw policy as "somewhat restrictive". Hold with mild easing lean.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 32 +-7 High. One formal dissent (Lindsey, wanted easing; vote 10-1); "somewhat differing views" on future stance, some easing-tilt reservations. Single-dissent meeting with moderate differences.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 14 +-6 Moderate. Financial conditions appear only as context: wealth effects, financing availability, "potential unsettlement in financial markets" from shutdown/debt default. Brief.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 40 +-7 Moderate. Symmetric directive retained by a majority; the remaining members saw an easing tilt as "the most likely policy course over coming months"; "an easing at some point would be needed" (some); direction "uncertain" for others. Mild easing lean with data dependence.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 50 +-8 Moderate. "outlook for fiscal policy remained obscured by the uncertain outcome"; "an especially broad range of outcomes seemed possible"; "recent data giving an uncertain picture of the underlying strength of aggregate demand"; "some mix of views" on how far slowing proceeds. Elevated by fiscal/shutdown, otherwise normal.
