- OUTPUT_GAP 40 +-7 Moderate: "growth in business activity falling short of the expansion in the economy's potential"; "risks that growth would persist below that of the economy's ... potential continued to dominate"; "reduced pressures on resources". Below potential.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 57 +-7 Moderate: "Labor markets were described as still tight across the nation" but layoffs increasing, openings "much easier to fill", wage pressures "might be easing"; unemployment expected to rise "to an average of about 4-1/2 percent". Still tight in level, easing.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 35 +-7 High: "Consumer spending and business capital investment had decelerated markedly"; "marked deceleration in final sales"; "generally disappointing retail sales"; offset by housing "well maintained ... a bright spot" and construction "relatively robust". Below trend, net.

Neutral net.

- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: medical services named once in passing as an exception; no services discussion.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: no housing price/rent discussion.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 42: "recent surveys pointed to little change in inflation expectations"; "relatively subdued expectations of inflation"; "generally benign inflation expectations".

- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 55 +-8 Moderate: "The eventual degree and duration of the softening ... were difficult to predict"; "unclear whether" short inventory correction or "more extensive cyclical adjustment"; "considerable downside risks"; "outlook for inventory investment was more uncertain"; "extent and duration ... remained uncertain".
