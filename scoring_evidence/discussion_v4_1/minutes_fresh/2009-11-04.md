Below target, subdued.
- OUTPUT_GAP 28 +-6 Moderate: "quite elevated level of economic slack"; "substantial slack in both labor and product markets"; "low industrial utilization rates". ("Above-trend output growth" is a growth rate, not a level gap.)
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 18 +-5 High: "weakness in labor market conditions remained an important concern"; "unemployment remaining high"; unusually large part-time-for-economic-reasons share, long-term unemployment.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 40 +-7 Moderate: consumer spending "appeared to be rising", non-auto "stronger than expected", but "many" said cautious sentiment would "keep new capital spending subdued"; fiscal-support reliance; "rather slow" recovery; CRE "continued to fall markedly".
- PRODUCTIVITY 66 +-8 Moderate: "aggregate data indicating rapid productivity growth in recent quarters"; firms meeting demand by "boosting productivity".
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 44 +-8 Moderate: markets "generally regarded as supportive of continued economic recovery"; "significant easing of conditions in capital markets" versus "bank credit remained tight"; "dichotomy" for large vs small firms. Supportive language puts it below 50 but tight bank credit offsets.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 47 +-10 Low: "private credit spreads substantially lower" (direction only, no level stated); bank lending standards still tightening, loans contracting, small firms constrained (availability). Direction-only rule: 45-55 on side of move, Low confidence.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 46 +-8 Low: "equity prices considerably higher" (price move only, no valuation view); default 45-55 on side of the move.
- BANKING_STRESS 60 +-7 Moderate: "rising levels of nonperforming loans expected to continue to be a source of stress"; "many regional and small banks vulnerable to the deteriorating performance of CRE loans"; CRE "possible source of increased pressure on banks"; standards still tightening.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 36 +-7 Moderate: "recent fall in the foreign exchange value of the dollar had been orderly"; "partial reversal... of the dollar's appreciation"; depreciation "would bear close watching". Depreciation, orderly, mild watch.
- INFLATION_GOODS 55: "some participants" noted "recent rise in the prices of oil and other commodities" and import-price increases could boost inflation pressures.
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: house prices discussed as activity, not shelter inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 47: policy record "stable inflation expectations"; "some" saw risk of expectations declining, "others" of rising; "all participants agreed" communication needed to "keep inflation expectations anchored".
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 28 +-6 Moderate: members "agreed that no substantive changes" warranted; "exceptionally low rates for an extended period"; "all members supported" completing $1.25 trillion MBS purchases (to be slowed gradually); "unemployment... quite elevated", inflation below objective. Dovish hold with ongoing asset purchases, slight hawkish counterweight from side-effect and exit-communication language.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 8 +-4 High: "Voting against this action: None"; "all members supported"; members "agreed"; only minor differences in risk emphasis ("some" vs "others" on inflation risks).
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 45 +-8 Moderate: extended paragraph on financial market developments, bank credit, CRE and small-bank vulnerability, credit dichotomy; members noted "excessive risk-taking in financial markets" as possible side effect (judged low likelihood).
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 12 +-5 High: "exceptionally low rates for an extended period" retained; path "dependent on the evolution of the economic outlook"; "all participants agreed" Fed should communicate "ability and intent to begin withdrawing... at the appropriate time" (mild offset).
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 52 +-8 Moderate: "uncertainty surrounding these forecasts was still viewed as quite elevated"; saving rate "an important source of uncertainty"; "most participants" saw growth risks roughly balanced.
