## Part 2: Evidence and scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 32 +-6 Moderate: "outlook for inflation remained very favorable ... continuing slack in labor markets"; "risks of any significant run-up in inflation appeared to have receded"; "members saw little reason for concern"; counterweight "examples of rising cost and price pressures were not entirely absent" (healthcare, steel, import prices). Below target, subdued.
OUTPUT_GAP 38 +-6 Moderate: "continuing slack in labor markets"; "margins of underutilized labor and other producer resources"; "fuller resource utilization" still to come. Below potential.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 38 +-6 Moderate: "continuing slack in labor markets"; "anticipated pickup in employment". Slack, no tightness language.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 44 +-7 Moderate: household spending "key role in sustaining the expansion", retail sales "well maintained", housing "strength across much of the country"; offset by business fixed investment weakness "still a depressant", sentiment "extraordinarily cautious", nonresidential construction "more bleak" to "many". Net slightly below trend.
PRODUCTIVITY 72 +-6 Moderate: "continued rise in structural productivity"; "continued robust growth in structural productivity" (repeated). Clearly above trend.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 53 +-8 Moderate: tightening side: "widened credit spreads", "curtailment of credit availability", "higher cost of equity capital", wealth losses "since early 2000" (a number of members). Easing side: "mortgage financing remained widely available at low interest rates", "interest costs had declined for borrowers with acceptable credit ratings", "bank credit widely available". Mixed, roughly neutral, slight tightness.
TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed.
CREDIT_SPREADS 56 +-10 Low: "heightened degree of risk aversion that had led to widened credit spreads" (direction only, no level) plus "curtailment of credit availability to potential borrowers whose repayment prospects were viewed as questionable". Direction/availability rule: 45-55 on side of move with availability support; Low confidence. Rests partly on availability.
EQUITY_VALUATION 58 +-8 Low: "prices in equity markets had turned up from their recent lows"; "cumulative losses in financial wealth"; "higher cost of equity capital was inhibiting business investment"; "turmoil in equity markets". Concern is depressed/damaged equities (not overvaluation), no explicit valuation level; modest depressed side.
BANKING_STRESS 28 +-6 Moderate: "the overall condition of the banking system remained sound with bank credit widely available". Sound, so below 50.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH 36 +-8 Moderate: "depreciation of the dollar" fostering exports; "in association with the dollar's depreciation, some upward pressures on import prices". Depreciation, below 50; modest.
INFLATION_GOODS 54: "steel prices, some materials costs", import prices up with dollar depreciation; offset by general subdued outlook.
INFLATION_SERVICES 52: "escalation in the cost of worker healthcare benefits", "insurance costs more generally"; unit labor costs "subdued".
INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: housing activity and prices discussed but not housing/shelter inflation.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS N/A: not discussed by participants.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 40 +-6 Moderate: "all the members were in favor of an unchanged policy stance" (1-3/4 percent); "most said they preferred" a risk statement "tilted toward weakness"; further weakening "might well call for a policy response". Hold with mild easing bias.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 10 +-4 High: vote 12-0, "Votes against this action: None"; "All the members" agreed on stance and on communicating weaker-growth risks; only "a few" preferred to keep balanced-risks wording (a wording preference, not a vote against).
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 42 +-8 Moderate: "A number of members commented on financial developments" holding back expansion; credit spreads, credit availability, corporate governance revelations, "if additional deterioration in financial markets" could call for response. A full paragraph, one of several themes.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 32 +-7 Moderate: shift to "risks ... weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate economic weakness"; "several also commented" the shift "might raise expectations of some easing in coming months" shaped by incoming data. Easing lean with data dependence.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 55 +-8 Moderate: "subject to substantial uncertainty"; "considerable uncertainty surrounding the outlook for financial and economic conditions"; "It was unclear when the associated uncertainties would diminish"; offset by "favorable odds" on strengthening expansion. Elevated but not extreme.
