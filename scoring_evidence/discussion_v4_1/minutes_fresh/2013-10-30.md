## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document type: normal meeting (policy vote and policy-stance discussion present); not special-topic.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 33 +-5 Moderate. "inflation remained subdued and below the Committee's longer-run objective of 2 percent"; "inflation was running below the Committee's longer-run objective"; "inflation persistently below ... 2 percent ... could pose risks". Below target, not concerning deflation.
- OUTPUT_GAP 36 +-6 Moderate. "substantial remaining slack in the labor market"; "the unemployment rate remained elevated"; fiscal policy "significant restraint". Below potential (<50).
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 35 +-5 High. "labor market continued to improve, albeit slowly"; "substantial remaining slack"; "selected labor markets were tight" (some Districts only; minor offset). Slack remains.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 42 +-6 Moderate. "Consumer spending appeared to have slowed somewhat"; "consumer sentiment remained unusually low"; business contacts "continued moderate growth in sales, but remained cautious about expanding payrolls and capital expenditures"; auto sales "remained strong"; housing recovery "slowed somewhat". Moderate, slightly below trend net.
- PRODUCTIVITY 38 +-8 Low. "A few participants" said improvements in productivity growth needed; "slower growth in productivity might have become the norm". Brief.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 40 +-6 Moderate. "Financial conditions eased notably over the intermeeting period"; Members "noted the improvement in financial conditions"; offsets: "tight credit conditions for some households and businesses", longer rates "well above their levels in the spring", reduced housing affordability. Eased/accommodative side (<50).
- TREASURY_STRESS 35 +-10 Low. "interest rate volatility was substantially lower than at the time of the September meeting"; no dysfunction described. Orderly, calmer.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 46 +-10 Low. No spread wording. Rests on availability/risk-taking language: "tight credit conditions for some households and businesses" (tighter) vs "reaching-for-yield behavior might be increasing again" (looser). Roughly offsetting, availability-based.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 47 +-8 Low. Only price move: "increases in equity values"; "a couple of participants pointed to signs ... reaching-for-yield"; no explicit valuation view. Side of the (rising) move.
- BANKING_STRESS 48 +-8 Low. "One participant stated that the extended period of near-zero interest rates continued to create challenges for the banking industry, as net interest margins remained under pressure." Single participant, mild.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: dollar/FX not discussed by participants.
- INFLATION_GOODS N/A: not discussed. INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed. INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: home prices and affordability mentioned, not shelter inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 45: "longer-run inflation expectations remained stable and, by some measures, below 2 percent"; "stable".
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 28 +-6 Moderate. "all members but one" favored waiting before adjusting purchases; Committee continues $40bn MBS and $45bn Treasury; participants "generally expected ... trimming the pace of purchases in coming months" and "some pointed out" taper could come at "one of its next few meetings" (hawkish tilt); "A couple of participants thought it premature"; most favored reducing IOER as possible easing signal; one member (George) wanted slower purchases. QE-era continuation with taper prospect.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 32 +-5 High. One formal dissent (George, hawkish: "continued aggressive easing ... not ... warranted"); "all members but one"; participants differ on tools (rules, IOER, open market operations) but broadly aligned on policy; no sharp exchanges.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 30 +-8 Moderate. Financial conditions "eased notably"; "reaching-for-yield" (a couple); "A few participants expressed concerns" about mortgage rates/home prices; bank NIMs (one); costs of asset purchases discussed. Limited, as context.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 15 +-6 Moderate. Thresholds retained (6.5 percent unemployment); participants examined strengthening guidance: keeping the rate "below its longer-run equilibrium value for some time", "maintaining more-accommodative policy for longer"; "highly accommodative stance ... for a considerable time"; offset by expected trimming of purchases "in coming months". Easing-bias guidance with a taper signal.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 40 +-8 Moderate. "several significant risks remained, including the uncertain effects of ongoing fiscal drag"; "consumer sentiment remained unusually low ... downside risk"; "Uncertainty about future fiscal policy and the regulatory environment ... weighing on business planning"; but "downside risks ... generally viewed as having diminished". Somewhat elevated, mostly fiscal.
