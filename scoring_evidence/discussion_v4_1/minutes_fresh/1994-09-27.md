## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document treated as a standard (not special-topic) discussion: it has a policy-stance discussion and a directive vote (11 for, 1 against). Staff-reported text and the staff-style directive preamble were not used as evidence for conditions.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 58 +-7 Moderate: "some measures of inflation had picked up recently"; "risks of some rise in inflation rates probably had increased"; "potential for additional inflation remained substantial"; offset by "pass-through ... had been muted", "price pressures remained concentrated in the early stages of production". Modestly above comfortable, not yet broad.
- OUTPUT_GAP 58 +-6 Moderate: "output near maximum sustainable levels"; "growth at a pace above the economy's long-run potential"; "increasing capacity constraints"; "unsustainable pace". Above potential, so > 50.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 58 +-7 Moderate: "many of them referred to increasing reports of scarcities of specific types of labor resources"; "increasingly tight labor markets in many parts of the country" but no overall wage inflation, only "some upward pressure on the wages of certain categories of workers".
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 64 +-6 Moderate: "consumer spending had been more buoyant than expected"; "robust expansion in manufacturing"; business fixed investment "likely to rise substantially further" though growth moderating; housing weakening "only a moderate drop".
- PRODUCTIVITY 57 +-8 Low: "successful efforts to control unit costs through ongoing gains in productivity" (passing, one clause).
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 34 +-8 Moderate: "many members stressed the ready availability of financing from increasingly aggressive bank lenders"; "capital markets were providing continued support"; financial climate "might even be providing an extra impetus to spending"; "access to ample financing". Accommodative so < 50.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 35 +-12 Low: no spread, yield or premium wording; rests on availability ("ready availability of financing", "aggressive bank lenders", "ample financing") per V4.1.1 rule 3.
- EQUITY_VALUATION N/A: equities not mentioned.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A: only lender aggressiveness / credit availability mentioned, no assessment of bank health or stress.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 35 +-8 Moderate: "depreciation in the value of the dollar"; one member concerned about the exchange value from trade talks with Japan and German elections. Depreciation so < 50.
- INFLATION_GOODS 55: price pressures "concentrated in the early stages of production", higher materials costs, "pressures on world commodity prices"; final-goods pass-through muted.
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 58: "some members expressed particular concern that ... inflationary expectations would quickly become more pronounced"; one member saw "an upturn in inflationary expectations"; offset by one member citing credibility of the Committee's stance.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 57 +-6 Moderate: "most of the members agreed on the desirability of maintaining a steady policy course"; "generally supported a shift ... to asymmetry toward restraint"; "for many members ... additional monetary restraint could well be needed"; "One member favored an immediate move" (Broaddus dissent). Hold with tightening bias.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 33 +-6 Moderate: one formal dissent (Broaddus, wanted prompt tightening); "Some members ... expressed misgivings" about the asymmetric directive; "views differed with regard to the likely need for some policy tightening". Differences of pace/timing, not of assessment.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 22 +-8 Low: credit availability and bank lending discussed as context for spending; no stability worries.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 66 +-7 Moderate: bias toward firming in the directive; "the Committee would need to act promptly and forcefully" if price pressures intensify; "additional monetary restraint could well be needed at some time".
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 55 +-8 Moderate: "very difficult to predict"; "extremely difficult to evaluate whether the earlier tightening moves were exerting a lesser effect than usual or it simply was more delayed, or whether the members might have misjudged the underlying strength"; inventories "a key uncertainty".
