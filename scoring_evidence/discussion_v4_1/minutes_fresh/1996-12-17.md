## Part 2: Evidence and scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 52 +-8 Moderate: "Inflation had remained subdued, but the members continued to view the risks as tilted toward increases"; "Measures of core inflation had displayed little trend and even some decline"; wage increases "moved higher". Subdued level, upside risk, so about neutral to slightly above.
- OUTPUT_GAP 51 +-5 Moderate: "the economy operating in the neighborhood of its sustainable potential"; growth "near the economy's potential". At potential.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 58 +-6 Moderate: "persistently tight labor markets"; "labor markets might be tighter than could be sustained over the long term"; wages rising faster. Tight; note the "benign" wage behavior relative to history.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 56 +-7 Moderate: "appreciable increases in consumer spending and business investment"; "widespread indications of robust retail sales"; "smaller though still sizable gains in business fixed investment"; offset by debt burdens, tightening consumer credit standards, motor vehicle sales short of expectations, housing mixed. Modestly above trend.
- PRODUCTIVITY: N/A. Not discussed.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 40 +-7 Moderate: "generally supportive financial conditions"; "readily available financing". Supportive, so below 50 (bright-line); offset slightly by tightening consumer credit standards.
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. Treasury market not discussed by participants (long-term yields appear only in the directive text).
- CREDIT_SPREADS 47 +-10 Low: no spread, yield or premium wording; rests on availability only: "tightening consumer credit standards" (tighter) against "readily available financing" for business (looser). Roughly offsetting, slightly below normal.
- EQUITY_VALUATION 38 +-8 Moderate: "The rise over recent years had been extraordinary and had brought market valuations to fairly high levels relative to earnings and dividends"; need "to monitor with special care price movements in the stock market". Valuation view of modestly elevated.
- BANKING_STRESS: N/A. Banking system not discussed (consumer credit standards are not a bank-health statement).
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: N/A. Only the directive text mentions the dollar rising slightly; participants discuss net exports weakness but not the dollar.
- INFLATION_GOODS: N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A. Not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A. Housing construction discussed, not housing/shelter prices.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 50: "some members emphasized... higher inflation expectations [not] to become embedded in financial markets"; a risk to guard against, not a stated drift. At target / anchored.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 54 +-6 Moderate: "all the members supported a proposal to maintain an unchanged policy stance while also retaining a bias toward restraint"; "anticipatory tightening was not yet called for"; "watchful waiting remained the prudent course". Hold with tightening bias.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 6 +-4 High: "all the members" supported both the hold and the asymmetric directive; Votes against: None. Some members stressed prompt action on inflation, a minor emphasis difference.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 20 +-8 Moderate: stock market valuations "injected an additional note of uncertainty"; "need to monitor with special care price movements in the stock market and asset markets more generally"; supportive conditions noted. One paragraph's worth, context for spending.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 60 +-7 Moderate: "view the next policy move as more likely to be in the direction of some firming than toward easing"; but "not suggesting that policy should be especially quick to react". Mild tightening bias.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 48 +-10 Moderate: "a great deal of uncertainty" on wage-price spillover; "a good deal of uncertainty regarding the outlook for inflation"; stock market "additional note of uncertainty"; against that, economy "performing quite favorably" and "uncertainties surrounding the outlook" cited as reason to wait. Elevated on inflation, rest confident.
