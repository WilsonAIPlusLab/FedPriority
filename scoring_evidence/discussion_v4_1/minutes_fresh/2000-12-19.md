## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Scope note: the input ends with an appended account of the separate January 3, 2001 telephone conference meeting (50bp easing, unanimous). That is a different meeting and is excluded from all scores below; only the December 19, 2000 participants' discussion and its vote record are scored.

**INFLATION_PRESSURE: 45 +-6 (High)**
- "core inflation had remained quiescent"; "all the members agreed that the risks of higher inflation had diminished materially"; "subdued inflation was a reasonable prospect". Offsets from "some members": labor markets "still very tight", "sharply rising medical costs", energy-price pass-through. Net: contained, slightly below midpoint.

**OUTPUT_GAP: 56 +-6 (Moderate)**
- "the high level of resource utilization"; deceleration "from an unsustainably high growth rate"; growth "somewhat below that of the economy's output potential would lessen pressures on labor and other resources". Level still above potential while growth slows; above 50.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 68 +-5 (High)**
- "what generally continued to be characterized as very tight labor markets across the country"; "labor markets in general were still very tight and likely would remain taut relative to historical experience" (some members); only "a modest reduction in labor market strains in some areas".

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 44 +-7 (High)**
- "significant slowing in the expansion of business activity"; consumer spending growth slowing, retail sales "widely indicated to have softened"; equipment and software spending "moderated substantially"; offset by nonresidential construction "well maintained in many parts of the country" and housing "holding up well in others". Net modestly below trend.

**PRODUCTIVITY: 68 +-6 (Moderate)**
- "no evidence to suggest that the underlying pace of advances in technology and related productivity growth had abated"; "Indications that rapid growth in structural productivity would persist".

**FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS: 60 +-7 (Moderate)**
- "tightening financial conditions in the form of declining equity prices and stricter credit terms for many business borrowers"; partly offset by "declines in mortgage rates". Somewhat tight; above 50.

**TREASURY_STRESS: N/A**: Treasury market functioning not discussed by participants.

**CREDIT_SPREADS: 58 +-10 (Low)**
- Rests on availability only: "stricter credit terms for many business borrowers". No spread, yield or premium wording from participants.

**EQUITY_VALUATION: 55 +-8 (Low)**
- Price moves only: "declining equity prices" (cited as part of tightening financial conditions), "sharp deterioration in the equity prices of high-tech companies"; other mentions are wealth-effect channel. No valuation view stated, so 45-55 on the side of the decline.

**BANKING_STRESS: N/A**: banks/banking system not discussed by participants (credit terms not attributed to banks).

**DOLLAR_STRENGTH: 45 +-10 (Low)**
- Only conditional references: "any depreciation in the foreign exchange value of the dollar as the economy slowed would help to bolster exports"; "Further depreciation of the dollar ... would add to import prices". Depreciation direction implied; below 50, low confidence.

**INFLATION_GOODS: 55**
- "the rise in energy prices", "very high energy costs", import-price pass-through risk, offset by "strong competitive pressures in most markets continued to inhibit business efforts to increase prices".

**INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A**: "sharply rising medical costs" is cited as a labor-compensation cost, not consumer services prices.

**INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A**: shelter costs not discussed ("high rents" refers to nonresidential property).

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 45**
- "long-run inflation expectations had not risen and might even have fallen a bit of late"; "inflation expectations contained" (few members).

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 40 +-6 (High)**
- Hold at 6-1/2 percent ("all the members indicated that they could support an unchanged policy stance") with a shift to risks "weighted toward conditions that could generate economic weakness"; "the Committee should be prepared to respond promptly to indications of further weakness"; "Those few members who expressed a preference for easing at this meeting"; "possibility of an easing move early in the intermeeting period". Hold with a clear easing bias.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY: 28 +-7 (High)**
- Vote record: 10 for, "Votes against this action: None." Reservations: "few members" preferred an immediate easing but "could accept a delay"; "In one view ... a statement of balanced risks would be preferable"; "some members" noted the shift could look abrupt. Broad agreement with minor differences.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 25 +-7 (Moderate)**
- Financial conditions appear as context: "tightening financial conditions ... declining equity prices and stricter credit terms"; risk statement "could generate sizable reactions in financial markets". Limited, not a focus.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 27 +-7 (High)**
- Risk statement moved to "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate economic weakness"; "prepared to respond promptly"; "intention of the Committee to act promptly in coming weeks, including the possibility of an easing move early in the intermeeting period". Easing lean conditioned on confirming data.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 48 +-8 (Moderate)**
- "the risks to the economy had shifted rapidly and perceptibly to the downside"; "The extent to which such developments would persist ... clearly was uncertain"; "prevailing uncertainties about the prospective performance of the economy"; market reaction "difficult to predict". Elevated, centered on whether the slowdown continues.
