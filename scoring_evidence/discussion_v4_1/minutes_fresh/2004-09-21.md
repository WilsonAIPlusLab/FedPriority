## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

Document: minutes participants' views plus vote record; includes a policy vote (unanimous 25bp hike) and policy-stance discussion, so not special-topic.

INFLATION_PRESSURE 40 +-6 Moderate: "members expected inflation to stay low"; "benign incoming data on prices"; "increase in inflation earlier in the year had importantly reflected temporary factors and core inflation would probably remain relatively low"; "a number of policymakers" saw data at or below expectations. Offset: non-energy commodity prices "remained relatively high". Below target-ish, contained.
OUTPUT_GAP 42 +-6 Moderate: "some slack appeared to remain in labor markets"; hike "sufficient to erode remaining margins of slack in resource utilization over time"; "persistence of some economic slack". Slightly below potential.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 40 +-6 Moderate: "improved modestly"; "Overall, however, some slack appeared to remain"; "many firms remained quite cautious about expanding payrolls"; "some members" noted skill mismatches and constraints in "a few sectors". Slack remains.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 53 +-6 Moderate: "household spending ... continuing to expand at a solid pace"; "Business investment also was robust"; offset by "pace ... less brisk than previously anticipated", executives "more cautious", possible sharp slowing after expensing expires. Roughly at trend, solid.
PRODUCTIVITY N/A: only a passing "pace of technological advance could be slowing a bit" (some members) re cost of capital; no productivity growth assessment.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 40 +-8 Low: "low real interest rates, strong business balance sheets"; "low interest rates to buoy residential investment"; real funds rate only now "slightly into positive territory". Supportive/accommodative (bright line < 50); thin direct evidence.

EQUITY_VALUATION N/A: equities not mentioned.
BANKING_STRESS N/A: not discussed.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: external deficits discussed but no dollar/FX movement mentioned.
INFLATION_GOODS 52: "non-energy commodity prices remained relatively high" vs "energy prices had declined noticeably from record levels" and shock "expected to wane"; mixed, near stable.
INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed.
INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: not discussed.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 46: "inflation expectations appeared to be well-contained"; statement says expectations "had eased"; caveat that they are conditioned on expected tightening.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 62 +-6 High: "all of the members favored raising the target ... by 25 basis points"; "economic conditions as likely to warrant a further reduction in policy accommodation in coming quarters"; tempered by "measured" pace, risks "balanced", "increasingly keyed to incoming data", market anticipating "some slowing in the pace of policy firming". Gradual tightening with hawkish consensus on direction, soft on pace.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 8 +-4 High: "all of the members" favored the action; "Vote against this action: None."; no reservations or camps reported; "many members" only noted data dependence.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 10 +-5 Moderate: only passing items (corporate governance focus, very low household saving, wide external deficits and possible adjustment "discussed"); no discussion of financial-system stability or market conditions.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 62 +-6 High: "further reduction in policy accommodation in coming quarters"; statement: accommodation "can be removed at a pace that is likely to be measured"; offset by "Committee will respond to changes in economic prospects as needed" and data-keyed language. Mild-to-moderate tightening bias.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 38 +-7 Moderate: "several policymakers remarked that their uncertainty about the likely pace of the expansion going forward had risen"; "reasons for this tendency were unclear"; "difficult to discern" the effect of expensing; but "recent evidence had boosted their confidence that moderate economic growth would continue and that inflation would be contained". Somewhat elevated, tempered by confidence.
