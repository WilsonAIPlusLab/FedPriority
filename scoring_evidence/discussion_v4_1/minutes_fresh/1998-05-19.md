Hold with tightening bias.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 50 +-7 High: two formal dissents (Jordan, Poole) both for immediate tightening; "a number of members... close call"; "two members... strong preference for tightening"; disagreement is about timing, same direction, not about the economic assessment.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 38 +-8 Moderate: "some members expressed concern" about complacency spreading to property, business ventures and land; Asian financial market fragility cited as a reason to delay ("even a modest tightening action could have outsized effects on the already very sensitive financial markets in Asia"); Jordan on asset prices. Several address it, not dominant.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 65 +-6 High: "All the members who intended to vote for an unchanged policy... supported the retention of a directive that was biased toward restraint"; "a policy tightening move, possibly in the near future, was a likely though not an inevitable prospect"; "wait-and-see".
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 58 +-7 Moderate: "outlook for the latter was especially uncertain"; "subject to substantial uncertainty"; "uncertainties in the outlook for economic expansion and inflation remained sufficiently great"; "considerable questions remained about the relationship of labor market pressures to inflation"; "analyses... had proved consistently wrong".
