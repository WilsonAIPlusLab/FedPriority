## Part 2: Evidence and scores

Document treated as NOT special-topic: it contains a policy vote (domestic policy directive, 11-1) and a policy-stance discussion, alongside the Humphrey-Hawkins range decision and a brief disclosure-practices item.

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 58 +-7 Moderate: "the disinflationary trend might have been arrested" (many members); "price developments were disappointing"; "projections of inflation had been raised" (member forecasts, CPI 3 to 3-1/4 percent); offset by "still ample margins of unutilized labor"; "some encouragement that the earlier upturn... might prove to be temporary". Modestly above a target-consistent pace.
- OUTPUT_GAP 38 +-6 Moderate: "still ample margins of unutilized labor and other production resources"; "many members saw significant, albeit diminished, slack in labor and product markets". Below potential.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 33 +-6 Moderate: forecasts of unemployment "around 6-3/4 percent" with "only a small decline"; consumers "concerned about the outlook for jobs"; firms "especially cautious... in their hiring decisions"; downsizing. Slack, unemployment in the 6.5-7% range.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 44 +-6 Moderate: "moderate but sustained growth"; "No sector... poised... to provide strong impetus"; "generally cautious consumer attitudes"; equipment spending "relatively robust" but "other business capital spending had remained sluggish"; housing "though not ebullient... some impetus". Net slightly below trend.
- PRODUCTIVITY 57 +-9 Low: "expectations of some further gains in labor productivity"; firms "investing in new, more productive equipment"; Angell's dissent cites "a stronger trend of productivity growth". Modestly above trend, thin evidence.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 38 +-8 Moderate: "much improved financial condition of many households and business firms"; "Lower long-term interest rates"; "very low levels of money market interest rates" (some members); Angell "excessive liquidity in financial markets"; partly offset by "difficulties in securing construction finance" in some areas. Supportive, on the loose side of neutral.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: no participant comment on Treasury market functioning (only rate levels).
- CREDIT_SPREADS 50 +-12 Low: no spread wording; rests on availability only: housing developers "encountering difficulties in securing construction finance" in some areas, set against improved balance sheets and low mortgage rates. Roughly normal.
- EQUITY_VALUATION N/A: stock mutual fund flows appear only as a money-velocity channel, no valuation view.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A: no comment by participants on bank health (only "weak bank credit" in the money-velocity context).
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: dollar mentioned only in the directive's descriptive staff paragraph, not in participants' views.
- INFLATION_GOODS N/A: no goods-price discussion.
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: not discussed.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 63: "generally held expectations among the public that inflation was not likely to decline and might in fact trend higher"; "some worsening in inflationary expectations"; Angell cites rise in gold price.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 57 +-6 Moderate: "most of the members... saw little or no reason to change monetary policy"; "A majority... favored... retain the bias toward possible tightening"; "the next policy move was more likely to be in the direction of some firming"; "Some members cautioned" policy "relatively expansive"; "One member urged a prompt move toward restraint". Hold with tightening bias. The M2/M3 range cut was explicitly "not... any tightening".
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 33 +-6 Moderate: one formal dissent (Angell, 11-1, wanting prompt tightening); "Other members suggested that a symmetrical directive might be more consistent" but accepted asymmetry; "Several members" preferred retaining the old ranges but could accept the cut; range votes unanimous. Moderate differences, mostly tactical.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 15 +-6 Moderate: improved household/business balance sheets and some construction-finance difficulty mentioned in the growth discussion; Angell's "excessive liquidity". No stability-focused discussion.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 62 +-6 Moderate: retained asymmetric directive ("slightly greater reserve restraint would... be acceptable"); "the next policy move was more likely to be in the direction of some firming"; "remain alert to the potential for intensifying inflation". Mild tightening bias.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 50 +-8 Moderate: "considerable uncertainty about the outlook"; "still uncertain outlook for government budget and other policies"; "performance... mixed"; uncertainty about M2/M3 behavior "might well persist"; counterweighted by "the expansion now appeared to be firmly established".
