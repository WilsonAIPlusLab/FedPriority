## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 32: "persisting, though decreasing, margins of slack ... would keep inflation low"; "a number of members seeing a small further decline in inflation as a distinct possibility"; "very low inflation"; deflation risk "virtually nil" (most); offset by "some countervailing upward pressure on costs and prices" (weaker dollar, commodities, benefit costs). Below target, disinflation risk receding.
- OUTPUT_GAP 38: "wide margins of unused labor and other resources"; growth "would trim slack in labor and goods markets"; "slack in resource use". Below potential, gap closing.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 36: "persisting, though decreasing, margins of slack in labor ... markets"; "some members noted that the current unemployment rate likely understated the slack in labor markets"; "employment had revived". Slack remains, improving.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 62: "household spending had remained strong"; "investment outlays had increased rapidly"; "Consumer spending remained firm"; "sizable gains in business spending ... would continue"; some members note spending could slow and household financial stress. Above trend, net strong.

- EQUITY_VALUATION N/A: equities not discussed.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A: banks not discussed.

- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: no services price discussion.

Hold with accommodative stance, hawkish minority on guidance.
