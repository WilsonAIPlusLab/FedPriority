## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 38 +-6 Moderate: "inflation was still relatively quiescent" apart from energy and medical costs; "some recent measures of increases in core prices had fluctuated on the high side of earlier expectations"; members "anticipated that inflation would remain subdued"; price inflation "relatively subdued" apart from energy and health care.
- OUTPUT_GAP 43 +-7 Moderate: "growth in output likely to remain below the expansion of the economy's potential for a while"; "growth in demand at a pace below the economy's potential should ease pressures on labor and other resources"; "continued softness in economic activity"; excess inventories and capacity in some industries. Below-potential growth but labor still tight, so only modestly below.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 58 +-7 Moderate: "labor markets in general remained tight throughout the nation", but "anecdotal reports of less scarce labor resources were becoming more frequent"; rising concerns about layoffs.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 40 +-7 Moderate: consumer spending "relatively strong" and housing "elevated", offset by "appreciable weakening in business investment spending", equipment and software declined, "a substantial volume of planned investment was being postponed, if not cancelled"; consumption expected to continue "at a relatively sluggish pace".
- PRODUCTIVITY 65 +-8 Moderate: "solid productivity growth" listed as a favorable factor; "persisting strong gains in productivity".
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 58 +-7 Moderate: "less accommodative financial conditions associated with weaker equity markets and tighter credit terms and conditions imposed by banking institutions"; financial stimulus "much smaller" than expected because of "further declines in stock market prices, more stringent financing terms for many business borrowers, and a stronger dollar"; offset by "lower interest rates".
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 58 +-10 Low: no spread, yield or premium wording; rests on availability: "tighter credit terms and conditions imposed by banking institutions"; "more stringent financing terms for many business borrowers".
- EQUITY_VALUATION 55 +-10 Low: price move only, no valuation view: "marked decline that had occurred in equity prices", "sizable declines in equity prices... as earnings prospects proved disappointing"; "volatility and additional declines in the stock market". Also used as wealth channel, but the price decline and the tighter financial conditions are discussed directly. Default 45-55 range on the side of the move.
- BANKING_STRESS 36 +-8 Low: "generally sound financial institutions" listed as a favorable factor (single mention); partly offset by banks imposing tighter credit terms.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 65 +-8 Moderate: "the strength of the dollar in foreign exchange markets"; "the strong dollar and weaker foreign growth would constrain exports"; "a stronger dollar" holding down spending. Appreciation, a drag on exports.
- INFLATION_GOODS N/A: not discussed separately.
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed separately (health care costs mentioned only as a sector within overall prices).
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: not discussed as price inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS N/A: not discussed.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 17 +-5 High: "most of the members preferred and all could support a further easing... 50 basis point reduction"; "many of the members generally believed that additional policy easing might well prove to be necessary at some time"; "A few members expressed a preference for a 75 basis point reduction". Cut of 50bp with easing bias and a minority for 75bp.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 17 +-5 High: vote record "Votes against this action: None."; all could support the 50bp cut; a few preferred 75bp and "most members agreed" on smaller, more frequent steps. Minor differences about size only.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 35 +-8 Moderate: equity declines, tighter bank credit terms and financial conditions raised as drags on investment and policy transmission in several places, but as context for the economic outlook; no stability-focused discussion.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 18 +-6 Moderate: "risks to the economy would remain decidedly to the downside" (press statement); "economic developments might suggest the desirability of a Committee conference call... to consider the possible need for a further policy adjustment"; many saw further easing as likely necessary.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 58 +-7 Moderate: "considerable uncertainty surrounded this outlook"; "a significant source of uncertainty and downside risk"; "rapidly changing and highly uncertain circumstances"; "a capital overhang of unknown dimensions"; "how large an adjustment... was still unclear".
