## Part 2: Evidence and scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 60: "members generally concluded that some increase in inflation was likely in coming months"; "a number of members expressed concern ... portending higher inflation"; "recent rise in inflation"; "only a small pickup in consumer prices". Modestly above target, rising. UNC +-7, Moderate.
- OUTPUT_GAP 54: "utilization of labor and other producer resources were still at very high levels"; "persisting pressures on labor and capital resources"; but "economy expected to be operating in the neighborhood of its potential". Slightly above potential. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 56: "persisting tightness in many labor markets"; "tight labor supply" (anecdotal); no appreciable wage increases; directive gives UE 5.4%. UNC +-7, Moderate.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 48: "sluggishness in consumer spending"; "housing market had weakened noticeably"; offset by business activity "still quite strong", equipment investment to "continue to support growth", "considerable forward momentum". Net near trend, slightly soft. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- PRODUCTIVITY 58: "fruits of heavy business investments in new capacity and more productive equipment"; "structural changes ... raising the economy's capacity for sustained, noninflationary activity" (offered as a possible explanation, "perhaps"). UNC +-10, Low.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 34: "favorable recent developments in financial markets"; "bank lending policies remained quite accommodative"; "ready availability of consumer financing"; long rates declining. Loose side. UNC +-7, Moderate.
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. No participant discussion of Treasury market functioning (only the direction of long-term rates).
- CREDIT_SPREADS 36: rests on availability only, no spread wording: "bank lending policies remained quite accommodative"; "ready availability of consumer financing"; business loan growth slowed after unusual strength. UNC +-10, Low.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A. Stocks mentioned only as wealth-effect channel ("rise in bond and stock prices ... strengthened household balance sheets").
- BANKING_STRESS: N/A. Only lending policy/loan growth mentioned; no view on bank health or capital.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 22: "relatively steep decline in the value of the dollar" (several members); "lower value of the dollar now prevailing"; "depreciation of the dollar also would add to inflationary pressures". Depreciation. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- INFLATION_GOODS 58: "Prices of many materials inputs ... had risen sharply"; firms "reporting greater success in passing cost increases through to prices"; finished goods prices not yet up significantly. UNC n/a (value only).
- INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: N/A, not discussed.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 57: "all the members endorsed a proposal to maintain an unchanged degree of pressure"; "most members expressed a preference for an asymmetric directive tilted toward restraint"; "a few members indicated that further tightening might well be needed sooner rather than later"; "a few members preferred a symmetric directive" but "could accept" asymmetric. Hold with tightening bias. UNC +-5, High.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 14: "all the members indicated that they preferred or could support" the directive; Votes against: None. Only minor differences (tilt; "opinions differed somewhat" on slowdown and inflation). UNC +-4, High.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 8: only brief references to financial markets, bank lending accommodation, consumer financing, bond/stock wealth effects, and the dollar; no stability discussion. UNC +-4, Moderate.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 62: asymmetric directive "would provide a clear signal of the Committee's intention to resist higher inflation"; "additional monetary tightening needed at some point"; but "prudent to pause and assess developments". Mild tightening bias. UNC +-6, Moderate.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 42: "extent of the slowdown was not yet clear"; "considerable uncertainty surrounded the outlook for the external sector"; "given the substantial uncertainties that were involved ... prudent to pause"; "difficult to determine whether ... temporary pause or a more prolonged pullback". Somewhat elevated, mainly tied to specific areas. UNC +-8, Moderate.
