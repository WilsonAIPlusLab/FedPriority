## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 62 +-6 Moderate: "continued to be concerned about the possibility that inflation would edge higher"; "core consumer price measures also displayed a gradual uptrend"; "a few members commented that measures of core inflation already were near or slightly above levels that they viewed as acceptable"; risks "weighted on balance in the direction of a gradual uptrend in core inflation". Offsets: headline rise mostly energy, pass-through only marginal, competitive pressures inhibit pass-through.
OUTPUT_GAP 54 +-7 Low: demand "growing at a pace a little below the rate of increase in the economy's output potential" (a growth-rate statement, no explicit level language); "growth in overall demand now seemed to have declined to a more sustainable pace"; "pressures on resources" were unlikely to mount further, and labor markets "unusually tight". Read as resources still stretched but easing.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 76 +-5 High: "labor markets were likely to remain unusually tight for some time"; "persistence of very tight conditions in labor markets"; labor compensation "appeared to be accelerating".
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 47 +-7 Moderate: business fixed investment moderation "substantial", high-tech spending "still growing at a robust pace"; consumer spending "held up well" in Q3 but "some softening", future growth "somewhat below the brisk pace of the past few years"; housing "mild softening". Net slightly below trend after a strong run.
PRODUCTIVITY 68 +-6 Moderate: "impressive further gains in productivity"; "the outlook for continued robust gains in productivity"; "fundamentally upbeat about the long-term prospects for productivity"; some doubt whether gains continue as investment slows.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 63 +-5 High: "less accommodative financial conditions for many businesses, including some further tightening since the meeting in early October"; "overall financial conditions had tightened over the course of recent months"; "tighter credit conditions for many borrowers and lower equity prices".
TREASURY_STRESS N/A: Treasury market not discussed by participants.
CREDIT_SPREADS 58 +-10 Low: "spreads in securities markets had widened, most sharply on obligations of borrowers rated below investment grade"; "tightening terms and standards at banks for business borrowers"; "considerable weakening ... in overall growth of credit". No spread level stated, so direction plus availability; rests partly on availability (V4.1.1 rule 3).
EQUITY_VALUATION 55 +-8 Low: "lower equity prices"; earnings and "stock market valuations of many firms, notably in the high-tech area" depressed; no explicit over/undervaluation view, so price-move default on the side of the fall.
BANKING_STRESS N/A: only lending standards and "more cautious attitudes on the part of lending institutions" mentioned (used under CREDIT_SPREADS); no comment on bank health or capital.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH 55 +-8 Low: dollar at "its currently elevated level" (a few members); "some members" saw possible downward pressure from current account deficits. Current level strong, prospective depreciation risk; modest strength side.
INFLATION_GOODS N/A: not discussed separately.
INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed separately.
INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: housing activity discussed, not housing inflation.
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 47: "relatively subdued inflation expectations" a favorable factor, but "the longer relatively high energy prices persisted ... the greater might be their imprint on both inflation expectations and core prices".
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 54 +-6 Moderate: "all the members supported a proposal to maintain an unchanged policy stance" at about 6-1/2 percent; "added restraint was likely to be necessary to contain inflation pressures"; risk statement kept weighted toward inflation, though "a few indicated that the decision was a close call" and "several" saw a shift to balanced "in the relatively near future". Hold with a mild tightening bias that is softening.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 8 +-4 High: "all the members supported" a hold; "they were unanimous" on keeping the inflation-weighted risk statement; vote 10-0, "Votes against this action: None." Only nuance is a few close calls.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 45 +-8 Moderate: tightening credit, wider spreads, lower equity prices and wealth effects are a key factor "underlying the economic outlook"; concern that a statement shift "might induce an undesirable softening in overall financial market conditions"; no stability or bank-health discussion.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 58 +-6 Moderate: risk statement retained "weighted mainly toward conditions that may generate heightened inflation pressures"; but members "desirable to provide some recognition of the emergence of increased downside risks"; shift to balanced seen as nearer. Mild tightening bias, eroding.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 40 +-7 Moderate: "quite uncertain" whether demand would stay damped enough; "a great deal of uncertainty" over energy prices and Middle East; dollar "another source of uncertainty"; foreign risks "seemed small". Standard to somewhat elevated.
