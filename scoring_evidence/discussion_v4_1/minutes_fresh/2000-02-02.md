## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 57 +-8 Moderate: "core inflation remained subdued"; "risks ... primarily tilted toward rising inflationary pressures"; "some members" see taut labor markets and oil/import turnaround giving "an upward bias"; "expected that inflation pressures would gather some momentum". Current level subdued, pressures building.
- OUTPUT_GAP 64 +-7 High: "demand for output had been growing even more strongly" than potential; "growing very vigorously"; imbalance to be corrected; projected unemployment 4 to 4-1/4. Demand above supply, so > 50.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 72 +-6 High: "already very tight labor markets"; "taut labor markets"; "cushion of unutilized labor resources having dwindled"; "larger increases in labor compensation" (several); unemployment 4-4.25 projected.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 70 +-7 High: "growing very vigorously"; "retail sales ... strengthened further"; motor vehicles "remarkably high level"; "robust growth in jobs and incomes"; "robust further growth in business fixed investment"; housing high but below peaks, structures softening; consumption expected to moderate.
- PRODUCTIVITY 75 +-7 High: "accelerating productivity"; "no evidence that the acceleration in productivity was coming to an end"; firms "continuing to improve the efficiency".
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 45 +-10 Low: "unless financial conditions became tighter"; stock wealth "sharp increases", markets "resilience to higher interest rates"; long-term rates "had risen considerably"; "recently somewhat unsettled conditions in financial markets". Net somewhat loose/neutral, not explicitly described as accommodative or tight.
- TREASURY_STRESS N/A: no functioning or dysfunction discussion; only a breakeven mention as inflation-expectation evidence.
- CREDIT_SPREADS N/A: no credit spread, availability or lending discussion by participants (nominal-indexed spread is an inflation-expectations measure).
- EQUITY_VALUATION 47 +-8 Low: "sharp increases in stock market wealth"; "future course of stock market prices was highly uncertain"; "remarkable resilience". Price move and wealth channel with no explicit valuation or overvaluation view, so default 45-55 on the side of the rise.
- BANKING_STRESS N/A: not discussed.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH 42 +-10 Low: "risk that ... much of the adjustment potentially occurring through a decline in the exchange rate of the dollar"; "willingness of global investors to continue to acquire dollar assets ... in question". Prospective risk of weakening, no actual move reported.
- INFLATION_GOODS 60 +-: "turnaround in ... oil and import prices"; "some nonlabor input prices already were rising faster"; energy passthrough "upward pressure on prices throughout the economy"; but "raising their prices was very difficult".
- INFLATION_SERVICES N/A: not discussed separately (labor compensation but no services-price view).
- INFLATION_HOUSING N/A: not discussed as price inflation.
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 52: "longer-run inflation expectations generally did not appear to be worsening, though ... a slight widening of the spread"; policy text on avoiding a buildup of expectations.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 68 +-6 High: "all the members supported" a 25bp tightening to 5-3/4; "a majority ... preference for a limited policy move"; "a few members expressed a preference for ... 50 basis points"; "further tightening actions might well be needed"; others "might need to move more aggressively at a later meeting".
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 22 +-8 High: policy vote unanimous (25bp, Mr. Meyer for); only formal dissent was Meyer on the money-growth ranges (a statutory-reporting issue, not stance); "a few" preferred 50bp but accepted 25bp. Minor tactical differences.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 22 +-8 Moderate: stock-market wealth effects and financial conditions as channels; "somewhat unsettled conditions in financial markets"; not a focus.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 78 +-6 High: statement says risks "weighted mainly toward ... heightened inflation pressures"; "further tightening actions might well be needed"; rates already reflect "tightening ... at this and several subsequent meetings".
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 42 +-8 Moderate: "outlook for inflation remained subject to a marked degree of uncertainty"; stock prices "highly uncertain"; energy "very difficult to predict"; uncertainty about productivity trend; but confident on direction of risks.
