## Part 2: Evidence and scores

INFLATION_PRESSURE 34 +-5 Moderate: "Inflation remained below the Committee's longer-run objective"; participants anticipate a return toward 2 percent but cite "slow growth in labor costs", "lack of pricing power", "inflation persistently below ... would pose risks". Below target, not alarming.
OUTPUT_GAP 38 +-6 Moderate: "considerable labor market slack remained" (several); "moderate pace" expansion expected; no above-potential language. Below potential.
LABOR_TIGHTNESS 35 +-6 High: UE "approaching 6 1/2 percent" but "considerable labor market slack remained" (several), "low rates of increase in most measures of wages as consistent with continued labor market slack" (a number); "a few" noted regional skilled-labor shortages. Slack remains.
CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 57 +-6 Moderate: "Consumer spending had advanced strongly in late 2013"; "business investment appeared to be on a more solid uptrend"; offset by "households remained cautious", housing slowed, "guardedly optimistic". Modestly above trend.
PRODUCTIVITY N/A: not discussed by participants.
FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 36 +-7 Moderate: "highly accommodative monetary policy", "continued improvement in credit conditions", "easing of lending standards", "still-low mortgage rates"; no adverse reaction to taper. Accommodative/supportive, so below 50.
TREASURY_STRESS N/A: only one participant's worry that "longer-term interest rates could rise sharply" (a yield-path concern, not market functioning); no Treasury market functioning discussion.
CREDIT_SPREADS 42 +-10 Low: no spread/yield/premium wording; rests on availability: "continued improvement in credit conditions", "the easing of lending standards". Mildly favorable.
EQUITY_VALUATION N/A: equities not discussed (only "household wealth" as a spending channel).
BANKING_STRESS N/A: banking system not discussed.
DOLLAR_STRENGTH N/A: dollar not discussed (emerging-market developments only).
INFLATION_GOODS N/A; INFLATION_SERVICES N/A; INFLATION_HOUSING N/A (house prices mentioned only as a spending support).
INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 45: "longer-run inflation expectations stable" but "inflation expectations at short and medium horizons might not be as well anchored"; slight downside tilt.
HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 43 +-6 Moderate: "most participants judged ... a further modest step down in the pace of purchases was appropriate" (taper continues, still adding purchases); "a couple" questioned reducing but did not favor a pause; "several" want presumption of $10 billion cuts each meeting; "a few" raised raising the funds rate "relatively soon"; others (several) want guidance stressing willingness to keep rates low if inflation stays low. Easing still in place, taper bias, mixed.
DISSENT_INTENSITY 22 +-6 High: "Voting against this action: None"; "all members agreed" on taper and guidance. Differences in views on guidance form ("A range of views"), a few early-hike voices vs. others, but minor emphasis differences.
FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 35 +-8 Moderate: "a couple of others argued that the current highly accommodative stance ... could lead investors to take on excessive risk"; "several participants suggested that risks to financial stability should appear more explicitly" in guidance; emerging markets and Puerto Rico "needed to be watched carefully". Several address it, as context rather than focus.
FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 17 +-6 Moderate: thresholds retained; "maintain the current target range ... well past the time that the unemployment rate declines below 6 1/2 percent, especially if projected inflation continues to run below"; "several" want emphasis on keeping rates low; offset by a few early-hike voices and continued taper ahead. Strong easing bias for the rate path.
UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 33 +-7 Moderate: risks "more nearly balanced"; "confidence in their projection of moderate economic growth"; but emerging-market downside risks, debate on reliability of unemployment rate, "difficult to gauge" cyclical participation. Normal to somewhat elevated.
