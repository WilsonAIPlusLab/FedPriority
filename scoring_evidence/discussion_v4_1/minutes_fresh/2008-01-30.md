## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

- INFLATION_PRESSURE 62: "Participants agreed that the inflation data ... had been disappointing"; "headline inflation had been generally above 2 percent over the past four years"; "a few participants cited anecdotal reports that some firms were able to pass on costs"; "participants generally expected inflation to moderate somewhat" (forecast, not scored). Mr. Fisher (vote record): headline "more than 3 percent". Clearly above target, not accelerating.
- OUTPUT_GAP 38: "slow growth in economic activity ... and the associated slack in resource utilization"; "pressures on resources likely to ease"; activity "weak" in first half. Below potential, bright-line satisfied.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 44: "conditions in labor markets appeared to have deteriorated"; "job growth had slowed appreciably"; offset by "Some participants ... cited evidence that workers in some sectors were still in short supply". Softening from a roughly balanced level.
- CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 28: "consumer spending had decelerated considerably"; "Several participants reported that firms ... were reducing capital expenditures"; "Manufacturing activity ... slowed or contracted in many districts"; housing "deteriorate sharply".
- PRODUCTIVITY: N/A, not discussed.
- FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 74: "broader financial conditions had tightened significantly"; "credit conditions had become generally more restrictive"; "participants generally viewed financial markets as still vulnerable"; statement "financial markets remain under considerable stress". Tight, bright-line satisfied.
- TREASURY_STRESS: N/A. Only short-term funding/money markets mentioned ("improved considerably"), no Treasury market comment.
- CREDIT_SPREADS 66 (availability-based, Low conf): no spread, yield or premium wording by participants. "The availability of credit to consumers and businesses appeared to be tightening"; "banks had tightened lending standards and pricing terms on business loans ... especially sharply on commercial real estate"; "reduced availability of nonconforming loans". Rests on availability.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: N/A. "Many participants were concerned that the drop in equity prices ... implied reductions in household wealth": wealth-effect channel only, no valuation view.
- BANKING_STRESS 56: "Banks appeared to be making some progress in strengthening their balance sheets, with several financial institutions able to raise significant amounts of capital to offset the large losses"; against that, lenders "increasingly cautious", standards tightened, "possible further deterioration in the condition of financial guarantors" (many perceived strain from downgrades). Emerging stress with offsetting repair.
- DOLLAR_STRENGTH: N/A, not discussed.
- INFLATION_GOODS: N/A. INFLATION_SERVICES: N/A. INFLATION_HOUSING: N/A (housing activity only, no shelter prices).
- INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS 55: "Some survey measures of inflation expectations had edged up"; "longer-term ... inflation compensation had climbed"; "On balance, expectations seemed to remain fairly well anchored, but participants agreed that continued stability ... was essential."
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 27: "most members believed that a further significant easing in policy was warranted"; 50 bp cut; "a relatively low real federal funds rate now appeared appropriate for a time". Tempered by "some noted that ... a reversal of a portion of the recent easing actions, possibly even a rapid reversal, might be appropriate" and Mr. Fisher's dissent for no change.
- DISSENT_INTENSITY 33: one formal dissent (Mr. Fisher, hawkish, wanted unchanged rate; 9 for, 1 against). Otherwise broad agreement ("members agreed", "concurred"), minor differences on inflation risk and fiscal timing.
- FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 66: money-market strains, tightening credit, financial guarantors, "adverse feedback loop", capital raising, and a closing discussion of ratings and risk management. Prominent theme alongside the real-economy assessment.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 28: "downside risks to growth remained, and ... the Committee would ... act in a timely manner as needed to address those risks"; statement "downside risks to growth remain". Easing lean with data dependence, partly offset by "monitor inflation developments carefully" and possible reversal.
- UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 62: "significant uncertainties surrounded this outlook"; "elevated volatility ... reflected increased uncertainty"; "several participants noted that the risks of a downturn in the economy were significant"; commodity-futures inferences "often proven inaccurate".
