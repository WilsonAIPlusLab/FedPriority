# FOMC Meeting Scoring: 2018-03-21

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). This is a segmented post-2009 minutes document, so participant vs. staff language is clear.

**Era context**: March 2018 is well into the Fed's gradual hiking cycle that began in December 2015. The economy is near or above full employment, inflation is still running just below 2%, the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act was recently enacted, and trade policy concerns (steel/aluminum tariffs) are emerging. The FOMC raised the target range 25bp at this meeting.

**Quantifier distribution**: Heavy use of "all" (outlook strengthened, further firming warranted), "most" (labor conditions strong, inflation readings increased confidence), "many" (business optimism, inflation confidence), "several" (gradual approach, wage gains moderate), "a number of" (tariff concerns, fiscal uncertainty), "some" (financial conditions accommodative), "a few" (inflation too low, participation sustainability doubts, postponing hike), "a couple" (delay hike).

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 47 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Both overall inflation and inflation for items other than food and energy continued to run below 2 percent."
- "Many participants stated that recent readings from indicators on inflation and inflation expectations increased their confidence that inflation would rise to the Committee's 2 percent objective in coming months."
- "A few participants cautioned that...the levels of these indicators remained too low to be consistent with the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective."

**Center of gravity**: Inflation still running below 2% target but participants broadly expect it to move up to target soon. The reading is slightly below neutral.
**Minority views**: A few see indicators still too low (pulls down); others confident about reaching target soon (pulls toward 50). Band ±5.

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP: 57 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally saw the news on spending and the labor market over the past few quarters as being consistent with continued above-trend growth."
- "The economy was operating at a high level of resource utilization."
- "A number of participants offered their views on the potential benefits and costs associated with an economy operating well above potential for a prolonged period."

**Center of gravity**: Participants clearly view the economy as above potential, with above-trend growth and high resource utilization. Fiscal stimulus expected to boost further.
**Minority views**: None explicitly arguing the economy is at or below potential.

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 60 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Most participants described labor market conditions as strong, noting that payroll gains had remained well above the pace regarded as consistent with absorbing new labor force entrants."
- "In some Districts, reports from business contacts or evidence from surveys pointed to continuing shortages of workers in segments of the labor market."
- "Most still described the pace of wage gains as moderate; a few participants cited this fact as suggesting that there was room for the labor market to strengthen somewhat further."

**Center of gravity**: Labor market tight — low unemployment, strong payroll gains, reported worker shortages — but wage growth still moderate, suggesting not yet severely tight.
**Minority views**: A few argue unemployment can sustainably go lower, suggesting effective tightness may be less than headline numbers imply.

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 55 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants noted incoming data suggesting some slowing in the rate of growth of household spending and business fixed investment after strong fourth-quarter readings. However, they expected that the first-quarter softness would be transitory."
- "Many participants reported considerable optimism among the business contacts in their Districts, consistent with a firming in business expenditures."
- "Strong economic fundamentals" including "high levels of consumer and business sentiment, supportive financial conditions."

**Center of gravity**: Above trend overall — strong fundamentals and business optimism, but some temporary Q1 softness. Net assessment is solidly positive.
**Minority views**: Agricultural sector reported downward pressure on farm income.

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: 28 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Some participants observed that financial conditions remained accommodative despite the rise in market volatility and repricing of assets that had occurred in February."
- "A few participants expressed concern that a lengthy period in which the economy operates beyond potential and financial conditions remain highly accommodative could, over time, pose risks to financial stability."

**Center of gravity**: Financial conditions clearly described as accommodative (loose). February volatility did not materially tighten conditions.
**Minority views**: None arguing conditions are tight.

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 71 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants expressed the view that it would be appropriate for the Committee to raise the target range for the federal funds rate 25 basis points at this meeting."
- "All participants saw some further firming of the stance of monetary policy as likely to be warranted."
- "A number of participants indicated that the appropriate path for the federal funds rate over the next few years would likely be slightly steeper than they had previously expected."

**Center of gravity**: Near-unanimous support for current hike, universal support for further firming, and some seeing a steeper path than before. Solidly hawkish in the gradual-hiking mold.
**Minority views**: "A couple of participants pointed to possible benefits of postponing an increase" — very small minority.

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY: 28 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants expressed the view that it would be appropriate for the Committee to raise the target range...25 basis points."
- "A couple of participants pointed to possible benefits of postponing an increase."
- "Participants expressed a range of views on the amount of policy tightening that would likely be required over the medium term."

**Center of gravity**: Broad agreement on the current action with only a couple preferring delay. Some dispersion on medium-term path but no fundamental disagreement.
**Minority views**: The "couple" preferring delay and range of views on medium-term path add modest dissent.

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 30 ±5 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "A few participants expressed concern that a lengthy period in which the economy operates beyond potential and financial conditions remain highly accommodative could, over time, pose risks to financial stability."
- "A few participants suggested that financial developments over the intermeeting period highlighted some downside risks associated with still-high valuations for equities or from market volatility more generally."

**Center of gravity**: Financial stability mentioned by a few participants in the context of prolonged above-potential growth and accommodative conditions, but not a major focus of the discussion.
**Minority views**: Most participants did not raise financial stability concerns prominently.

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 72 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "All participants saw some further firming of the stance of monetary policy as likely to be warranted."
- "A number of participants indicated that the appropriate path for the federal funds rate over the next few years would likely be slightly steeper than they had previously expected."
- "Several participants expressed the judgment that it would likely become appropriate at some point for the Committee to set the federal funds rate above its longer-run normal value for a time."

**Center of gravity**: Forward signal is clearly in a tightening direction — further gradual hikes ahead, with some seeing a steeper path and eventual above-neutral rates.
**Minority views**: None explicitly argue for pausing the hiking cycle going forward.

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 35 ±7 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Near-term risks to the economic outlook appeared to be roughly balanced."
- "A strong majority of participants viewed the prospect of retaliatory trade actions by other countries, as well as other issues and uncertainties associated with trade policies, as downside risks for the U.S. economy."
- "Participants generally regarded the magnitude and timing of the economic effects of the fiscal policy changes as uncertain."

**Center of gravity**: Baseline risks roughly balanced, but trade policy and fiscal uncertainty flagged as notable sources of uncertainty. Somewhat elevated but not deeply uncertain.
**Minority views**: Trade policy concerns widely shared as a downside risk, pushing uncertainty modestly above baseline.

### Optional Dimensions:

**INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS**: 44 ±5 [Moderate confidence]
- "Market-based measures of inflation compensation had increased in recent months but remained low; survey-based measures of longer-term inflation expectations were little changed."
- "A few participants cautioned that...the levels of these indicators remained too low to be consistent with the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective."
- Expectations anchored but slightly below target-consistent levels.
