# FOMC Scoring: 2014-03-19 (Minutes)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized participant views). Segmented format (post-2009) with clear participant vs. staff attribution. Quantifier language is used throughout.

**Era context**: March 2014 -- mid-recovery period. The Fed is tapering its asset purchase program (QE3), reducing purchases by $10B/month increments. The unemployment rate is approaching 6.5% (the prior threshold). Inflation is running below the 2% target. The economy is dealing with severe winter weather disruptions.

**Quantifier distribution**: "most" appears several times (forward guidance preferences, inflation return expectations). "Several" used for labor slack views, international concerns, financial stability trends. "A few" used for weather-alternative explanations, new threshold proposals. "A couple" used for inflation concern, deflation risk, credit spread observations. "One/another" for individual dissenting preferences.

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants see inflation as below target. "Inflation continued to run below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective." "A couple of participants expressed concern that inflation might not return to 2 percent in the next few years." "Most participants expected inflation to return to 2 percent over the next few years." The center of gravity is below-target inflation with expectation of eventual return, but concern about persistence. Direction: below midpoint. Magnitude: "continued to run below" is moderate language, not extreme. Score ~33-37. Polarity check: Higher = more inflation pressure. Below-target inflation = less pressure. Score below 50 is correct.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Somewhat slower-than-expected growth... in part reflecting adverse weather." "While there was general agreement that slack remains in the labor market." Weather is seen as temporary; underlying economy expected to grow at "moderate pace." "Participants expected economic activity to pick up." Fiscal restraint mentioned but diminishing. "Unemployment rate... remained elevated." Clear below-potential language. Direction: below 50. The economy is recovering but still below potential. Score ~38-40. Anchor guidance: 35-42 for 2010-14 recovery. Polarity check: Higher = more above potential. Slack remains = below potential. Below 50 is correct.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "General agreement that slack remains in the labor market." "Several participants pointed to... low labor force participation rate and still-high rates of longer-duration unemployment... considerably more labor market slack." "A couple of other participants... saw reasons to believe that slack was more limited." "Several participants cited low nominal wage growth as pointing to continued labor market slack." The center of gravity is clearly slack-remaining but improving. Unemployment rate "remained elevated." Score ~35-37. Anchor: 35-40 for 2011-14. Polarity check: Higher = tighter. Slack remains = not tight. Below 50 is correct.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Growth in economic activity slowed during the winter months." "Household spending and business fixed investment continued to advance." "Housing sector remained slow." Weather disruptions caused reduced activity, but underlying trend viewed as moderate and continuing. "Lower-than-expected retail sales" but expected to pick up. Net: somewhat below trend due to weather, but underlying advance is moderate. Score ~40-42. Polarity check: Higher = stronger. Slowed but advancing = slightly below trend. Below 50 correct.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "Participants saw financial conditions as generally consistent with the Committee's policy intentions." "A couple of participants pointed to the decline in credit spreads to relatively low levels by historical standards." "High levels of margin debt and of equity market valuations." "Growth in consumer credit to less creditworthy households." These point to accommodative (loose) conditions, not tight ones. Score ~30-33. Polarity check: Higher = tighter. Loose conditions = low score. Correct.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: The Committee is tapering ($10B reduction this meeting), which is a gradual move toward less accommodation, but still purchasing assets and maintaining near-zero rates. "Almost all participants agreed that it was appropriate... to update the forward guidance." The new qualitative guidance replaces numerical thresholds. "Most participants also believed... it would be appropriate... to provide additional guidance... that economic conditions may, for some time, warrant keeping the target federal funds rate below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run." "A few participants proposed adding new language... willingness to keep rates low if projected inflation remained persistently below 2 percent." The lean is dovish-to-balanced: tapering continues (slight hawkish direction) but strong emphasis on low-for-long rates and concern about below-target inflation. The anchor for 2014-15 pre-liftoff is 40-50. Score ~44. Polarity check: Higher = more hawkish. Tapering but emphasizing accommodation = lean dovish to balanced. Correct.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: On forward guidance, there was notable diversity: "most preferred" qualitative language, "one participant favored retaining existing thresholds," "another participant favored new quantitative thresholds," "a few participants proposed adding new quantitative language about inflation." On the tapering decision itself, broad agreement. On economic assessment, "participants expressed a range of views regarding the amount of slack." One member had a specific suggestion about balance sheet trajectory. No formal dissents mentioned, but meaningful diversity of views on forward guidance design. Score ~35. Anchor: 25-35 typical one-dissent meeting. Polarity check: Higher = more disagreement. Moderate differences on guidance specifics. Correct.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: "Several participants mentioned trends that, if continued, could become a concern from the perspective of financial stability." Specific mentions: credit spreads at low levels, margin debt, equity valuations, commodity investments, consumer credit growth. This is a moderate discussion -- "several" participants raise it, with specific examples, but it's not dominant. Score ~35-38. Anchor: 20-35 for 2013-19 typical. This is slightly above typical given the specificity of the concerns. Score ~37.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: Major discussion about transitioning from quantitative thresholds (6.5% unemployment) to qualitative guidance. Key signal: "economic conditions may, for some time, warrant keeping the target federal funds rate below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run." This is explicitly dovish forward guidance -- rates will stay low even after liftoff. But the taper is continuing, and SEP projections shifted up. The forward signal is mildly easing with data dependence. Anchor: 8-15 for 2014 taper. The meeting is in the taper phase but with strong low-for-long messaging. Score ~22-25. Polarity check: Higher = more hawkish forward signal. Low-for-long emphasis = low score. But taper continues and SEP shifted up somewhat, pulling it slightly higher than pure dovish. Score ~25.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "Uncertainty about the trend rate of productivity growth was making it difficult to ascertain the rate of real GDP growth." "Participants expressed a range of views regarding the amount of slack." Weather effects created uncertainty about underlying trends. "Some expressing concern that this component of the SEP could be misconstrued." International developments (China, Ukraine) noted as bears watching. Moderate uncertainty -- genuine unknowns about slack measurement and weather distortion, but not elevated-alarm-level uncertainty. Score ~35. Anchor: 25-35 for 2013-14 stable expansion. Correct range.

### Cross-dimension coherence:
- OUTPUT_GAP (39) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (36): Both below 50, consistent with slack-remaining recovery. Labor slightly lower reflecting explicit "considerably more slack" language. Coherent.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (44) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (25): Divergence is explainable. Current action (taper continues) is mildly less dovish than pure accommodation, but forward guidance is explicitly dovish ("low for long"). This is consistent with the taper-era pattern: gradually reducing stimulus while signaling rates stay low.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 35 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Inflation continued to run below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective over the intermeeting period." "A couple of participants expressed concern that inflation might not return to 2 percent in the next few years." "Most participants expected inflation to return to 2 percent over the next few years, supported by stable inflation expectations."
- **Center of gravity**: Inflation is running below target; most expect a return to 2% but the timeline is uncertain and a couple worry about persistence. Consistent with the 2013-14 below-target period.
- **Minority views**: "A couple of other participants suggested that factors other than economic slack had played a notable role in holding down inflation" (medical services prices). A couple expressed concern about prolonged below-target inflation.

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 39 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Data released since their January meeting had indicated somewhat slower-than-expected growth in economic activity during the winter months." "While there was general agreement that slack remains in the labor market." "Participants expected economic activity to pick up as the weather-related disruptions... dissipated."
- **Center of gravity**: Economy is below potential with slack remaining, but on a recovery path. Weather depressed near-term activity; underlying trend is moderate growth.
- **Minority views**: "A few participants highlighted factors other than weather that had likely contributed to the slowdown" (suggesting weakness may be more structural).

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 36 +/-7 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "While there was general agreement that slack remains in the labor market." "Several participants pointed to... considerably more labor market slack than indicated by the unemployment rate alone." "Several participants cited low nominal wage growth as pointing to the existence of continued labor market slack."
- **Center of gravity**: Clear consensus that labor market slack persists, with ongoing improvement but still well below full employment.
- **Minority views**: "A couple of other participants... saw reasons to believe that slack was more limited, viewing the decline in the participation rate as primarily reflecting demographic trends." Wider uncertainty band reflects this disagreement.

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 41 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Growth in economic activity slowed during the winter months, in part reflecting adverse weather conditions." "Household spending and business fixed investment continued to advance, while the recovery in the housing sector remained slow." "Lower-than-expected retail sales" with weather disruptions cited.
- **Center of gravity**: Spending and investment are advancing but below trend, with weather further depressing near-term readings. Housing particularly slow.
- **Minority views**: Limited disagreement on this dimension.

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 31 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Participants saw financial conditions as generally consistent with the Committee's policy intentions." "A couple of participants pointed to the decline in credit spreads to relatively low levels by historical standards." "One participant voiced concern about high levels of margin debt and of equity market valuations."
- **Center of gravity**: Financial conditions are accommodative/loose. Credit spreads low, equity valuations elevated, consumer credit growing -- all indicators of easy financial conditions.
- **Minority views**: None in the tighter direction; concerns are about conditions being too loose.

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN
**Score: 44 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Members decided that it would be appropriate to make a further measured reduction in the pace of its asset purchases at this meeting" (taper from $65B to $55B/month). "Most participants also believed... it would be appropriate... to indicate that... economic conditions may, for some time, warrant keeping the target federal funds rate below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run."
- **Center of gravity**: Lean dovish to balanced. Tapering continues on schedule (slightly less accommodative), but strong emphasis on continued accommodation and low-for-long rate path. Concern about below-target inflation reinforces dovish lean.
- **Minority views**: "A few participants proposed adding new language... willingness to keep rates low if projected inflation remained persistently below 2 percent" (more dovish). Some SEP projections shifted up (slightly hawkish).

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY
**Score: 35 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Most participants preferred replacing the numerical thresholds with a qualitative description." "One participant... favored retaining the existing threshold language." "Another participant favored introducing new quantitative thresholds." "A few participants proposed adding new language" on inflation floor.
- **Center of gravity**: Broad agreement on the general direction (taper, transition guidance) but notable diversity on the specifics of forward guidance design. No formal dissents mentioned but multiple alternative proposals.
- **Minority views**: Several distinct minority positions on forward guidance specifics.

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS
**Score: 37 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Several participants mentioned trends that, if continued, could become a concern from the perspective of financial stability." "A couple of participants pointed to the decline in credit spreads to relatively low levels." "One participant voiced concern about high levels of margin debt and of equity market valuations... Another participant stressed the growth in consumer credit."
- **Center of gravity**: Moderate attention to financial stability. Several participants raised specific concerns (credit spreads, margin debt, equity valuations, consumer credit), but framed as potential rather than acute concerns.

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE
**Score: 25 +/-7 [High confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Economic conditions may, for some time, warrant keeping the target federal funds rate below levels the Committee views as normal in the longer run." "Most participants favored providing an explicit indication... that the new forward guidance... did not imply a change in the Committee's policy intentions." Transition from quantitative thresholds to qualitative guidance with low-for-long emphasis.
- **Center of gravity**: Forward signal is dovish -- explicitly signaling rates will remain below normal for some time even after liftoff. But the taper is ongoing and SEP projections shifted up slightly, preventing an extremely low score.
- **Minority views**: "A number of participants observed that an upward shift [in SEP] was arguably warranted by the improvement in participants' outlooks for the labor market."

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED
**Score: 35 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**
- **Evidence**: "Uncertainty about the trend rate of productivity growth was making it difficult to ascertain the rate of real GDP growth." "Participants expressed a range of views regarding the amount of slack and how well the unemployment rate performs as a summary indicator." Weather effects obscured underlying economic trends.
- **Center of gravity**: Somewhat elevated uncertainty, particularly around measuring slack and interpreting weather-distorted data, plus international risks (China, Ukraine). Not unusually high but above baseline.
