# FOMC Meeting Scoring: 2016-11-02

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). Segmented -- participant discussion section is clearly separated from staff sections.

**Era context**: November 2016 -- late in the post-GFC recovery. The economy is near full employment, inflation is below but approaching 2%, and the Committee is in the pre-second-liftoff phase (first hike was Dec 2015, next widely expected in Dec 2016). Fed funds rate at 0.25-0.50%. Election uncertainty in background.

**Quantifier distribution**: "participants generally agreed" (strong consensus on labor improvement, case for hike strengthening), "most" (expressed view hike appropriate "relatively soon"), "many" (pickup in business investment expected; financial stability risks), "several" (cautioned inflation compensation low; slack remaining), "some" (international risks; moderate wage pressures; no immediate need to tighten), "a few" (downside risks; advocated hike at this meeting; uncertainty about investment; weakness in construction).

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants see inflation as below target but rising. "Inflation had increased somewhat since earlier this year but remained below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective." Readings "somewhat higher than expected" -- viewed as positive progress. Market-based compensation "moved up but remained low." Still below target. Direction: below midpoint, but improving. Score in the 41-47 range. Slightly below target but trending up, which is consistent with a 42-45 score.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: The staff projected "operating above its long-run potential." Participants noted Q3 GDP was boosted by transitory factors, underlying growth "relatively modest." But language about the economy "nearing full employment" and "operating above potential" is present. The bright-line rule says "above potential" language means >50. However, participants themselves are split -- "some participants expressed the view that some modest slack remained in the labor market" while others saw economy "close to or at full employment." The staff's "above potential" view and some participant agreement pushes this slightly above 50. Score ~51.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "Labor market had continued to strengthen." "Reports from some Districts pointed to a tightening in labor markets, evidenced by shortages of qualified workers." But "several others judged that appreciable slack could remain." "Some participants characterized wage pressures as only moderate." Split views but center of gravity near full employment. Score ~50-52.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Mixed picture. "Household spending had been rising moderately" but "growth of consumer spending had slowed from its brisk pace earlier in the year." "Business fixed investment had remained soft." "Residential investment had fallen again." Expected moderate pace going forward. This is below-trend to at-trend. Score ~44-46.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "U.S. financial conditions continued to be broadly accommodative." MMF reforms discussed. Low term premiums. Conditions are loose. Score ~28-32.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: "Most participants expressed a view that it could well become appropriate to raise the target range...relatively soon." "A few participants advocated an increase at this meeting." But they held at this meeting. Strong lean toward December hike. "Some participants suggested that current conditions did not point to an immediate need to tighten." Center of gravity is lean hawkish -- hold now but hike very soon. Score ~58-62.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: "A few participants advocated an increase at this meeting" vs. most wanting to wait. "Some" vs. "several" on slack assessment. Disagreement about labor market slack, but broad agreement on direction. No formal dissents at this meeting. Moderate differences. Score ~30-35.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: There is a meaningful paragraph on financial markets/stability: MMF reforms, low term premiums, reaching for yield concerns. "Many judged that risks to economic and financial stability could increase over time if the labor market overheated appreciably, or expressed concern that an extended period of low interest rates risked intensifying incentives for investors to reach for yield." Moderate attention. Score ~35-40.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: "Most participants expressed a view that it could well become appropriate to raise the target range...relatively soon." "Expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant only gradual increases." Strong signal of imminent hike but gradual path afterward. Mild tightening bias. Score ~63-67.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "A substantial majority viewed the near-term risks to the economic outlook as roughly balanced." "Participants expressed uncertainty about how long the participation rate could be expected to continue rising." Some uncertainty about international risks, policy effects. But generally balanced risks and limited discussion of deep uncertainty. Score ~30-35.

### Cross-dimension coherence check:
- OUTPUT_GAP (51) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (51): Aligned -- both near balance/slightly above. Consistent with "near full employment" language.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (60) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (65): Aligned -- lean hawkish with upcoming hike signaled. Forward guidance slightly more hawkish because "relatively soon" is a strong signal.

### Polarity checks:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: Higher = more pressure. Evidence says below target but rising. Score 43 = slightly below midpoint. Correct.
- FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: Higher = tighter. "Broadly accommodative" = loose. Score 30. Correct.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: Higher = more hawkish. Most favor hike soon. Score 60. Correct.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 43 +/-5, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Inflation had increased somewhat since earlier this year but remained below the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective, partly reflecting earlier declines in energy prices and in prices of non-energy imports."
- "Readings on headline and core PCE price inflation had come in somewhat higher than expected in recent months. Participants generally regarded this as a positive development."
- "Market-based measures of inflation compensation had moved up but remained low."

**Center-of-gravity**: Participants see inflation as below target but making welcome progress upward. The uptick is viewed positively but inflation is still short of 2%.

**Minority views**: "A couple of others saw the incoming data as suggesting that inflation could move up to the Committee's objective more rapidly than previously expected" (upside). "Several cautioned that these measures remained low" (downside). Informs the +/-5 band.

---

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 51 +/-6, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- Staff projected economy "operating above its long-run potential" (staff view, but participants discussed this context).
- "From a business cycle perspective, the increase in the participation rate in recent months was consistent with a tightening labor market and an economy nearing full employment."
- Underlying growth "relatively modest" after stripping transitory factors; "business fixed investment had remained soft."

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy viewed as near or at potential. The bright-line rule notes "above potential" language from staff context, but participants are split between "at" and "slightly above." Center of gravity is right at potential with a slight lean above.

**Minority views**: "Several others judged that appreciable slack could remain in the labor market" pulls downward. "A few participants advocated an increase at this meeting" on grounds economy was at maximum employment pulls up.

---

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 51 +/-7, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Labor market conditions had continued to improve...Reports from some Districts pointed to a tightening in labor markets, evidenced by shortages of qualified workers in some occupations, increases in overtime hours, or a pickup in wage inflation."
- "While some participants expressed the view that the economy was close to or at full employment, several others judged that appreciable slack could remain in the labor market."
- "Some participants characterized wage pressures as only moderate."

**Center-of-gravity**: Near full employment with tightening evidence, but meaningful disagreement about remaining slack. Centered around NAIRU.

**Minority views**: Wide spread -- "at full employment" camp vs. "appreciable slack" camp. Reflected in +/-7 band.

---

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 45 +/-5, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Growth of consumer spending had slowed from its brisk pace earlier in the year, residential investment had fallen again, and business fixed investment had remained soft."
- "Real economic activity was expected to advance at a moderate pace in coming quarters, primarily reflecting solid growth in consumer spending."
- "A few participants reported that uncertainty about prospects for government policy...were likely weighing on capital spending plans."

**Center-of-gravity**: Below-trend current conditions: consumer spending decelerating, investment soft, residential declining. Expected to return to moderate. Net position is slightly below trend.

**Minority views**: Some optimism about energy sector pickup in investment. Limited.

---

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 30 +/-5, Moderate confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "U.S. financial conditions continued to be broadly accommodative."
- "Domestic and global asset markets remained relatively calm over the intermeeting period."
- Low term premiums on Treasuries; MMF reforms noted but not creating tighter conditions broadly.

**Center-of-gravity**: Conditions clearly described as accommodative/loose. No stress signals.

**Minority views**: Limited discussion of tightening risks. Some noted international risks but these are potential, not current.

---

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN
**Score: 60 +/-6, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Most participants expressed a view that it could well become appropriate to raise the target range for the federal funds rate relatively soon."
- "A few participants advocated an increase at this meeting."
- "Some participants suggested that current conditions did not point to an immediate need to tighten or that some further evidence of continued progress...would provide greater support for policy firming."

**Center-of-gravity**: Lean hawkish. The Committee held rates but the strong majority signaled an imminent hike. The "relatively soon" language from "most" participants is a clear hawkish lean, though moderated by "gradual" pace language.

**Minority views**: "Some" wanted more evidence before tightening (dovish lean). "A few" wanted to hike immediately (more hawkish). Reflected in +/-6.

---

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY
**Score: 33 +/-5, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Some participants expressed the view that the economy was close to or at full employment, several others judged that appreciable slack could remain."
- "A few participants advocated an increase at this meeting" vs. most favoring hold.
- Policy action was to hold -- no formal dissents noted.

**Center-of-gravity**: Moderate differences exist on the assessment (slack vs. full employment) and on timing (hike now vs. wait), but broad agreement on direction (hike coming soon, gradual path). No formal dissents.

---

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS
**Score: 38 +/-5, Moderate confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants discussed a range of issues related to recent developments in financial markets and financial stability." Dedicated paragraph on MMF reforms, term premiums, balance sheet effects.
- "Many judged that risks to economic and financial stability could increase over time if the labor market overheated appreciably, or expressed concern that an extended period of low interest rates risked intensifying incentives for investors to reach for yield."

**Center-of-gravity**: Moderate discussion. Financial stability gets a dedicated section and "many" participants raised reach-for-yield concerns. More than passing mention but not a dominant theme.

---

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE
**Score: 65 +/-5, High confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "Most participants expressed a view that it could well become appropriate to raise the target range for the federal funds rate relatively soon."
- "Some participants noted that recent Committee communications were consistent with an increase in the target range for the federal funds rate in the near term."
- "Participants expected that economic conditions would evolve in a manner that would warrant only gradual increases in the federal funds rate."

**Center-of-gravity**: Clear tightening signal for the near term, but "gradual" path language moderates it. Consistent with the 2015-18 hiking cycle forward guidance anchors of 60-75.

---

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED
**Score: 32 +/-5, Moderate confidence**

**Evidence**:
- "A substantial majority viewed the near-term risks to the economic outlook as roughly balanced."
- "Participants expressed uncertainty about how long the participation rate could be expected to continue rising."
- "A few participants judged that significant downside risks remained, citing various factors including the low value of the neutral federal funds rate...the possibility of weaker-than-expected growth in foreign economies."

**Center-of-gravity**: Generally balanced risks with some specific areas of uncertainty (labor force participation, international). Not elevated -- standard hedging. Consistent with the 2016 stable expansion anchor of 25-35.
