# FOMC Meeting Scoring - June 14, 2023 (Minutes)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). Segmented format with a clear "Participants' Views on Current Conditions and the Economic Outlook" section.

**Era context**: June 2023 -- the Fed had been aggressively hiking since March 2022. At this meeting the Committee held rates steady at 5-5.25% (the "skip" meeting) after 10 consecutive hikes. Inflation was declining from 2022 peaks but remained well above target. The banking stress from SVB/Signature/First Republic earlier in the spring had receded but lingering credit-tightening effects were being monitored.

**Quantifier distribution**: "all/almost all" used frequently for policy agreement and inflation concern; "most" for resilient GDP, outlook communication; "many" for moderating pace; "several" for credit conditions, labor market, inflation expectations; "some" for favoring a hike, downside risks, commercial real estate; "a few" for excess savings depletion, housing services upside risks.

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants "agreed that inflation was unacceptably high" and "declines in inflation had been slower than they had expected." Core inflation "had not shown a sustained easing." Inflation is "well above" the 2% target. Core goods slowed less than expected; nonhousing services inflation showed "few signs of slowing." However, some moderation had occurred since mid-2022 and longer-term expectations were "well anchored." Direction: clearly above target, persistent, but off peak. This places it in the 65-75 range. The language "unacceptably high" and "well above" but with acknowledged moderation from peak suggests ~70.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Economic activity had continued to expand at a modest pace." GDP growth "resilient" but participants "judged that growth would be subdued over the remainder of this year." They expected "below-trend growth." The GDI discrepancy suggested momentum "may not be as strong as indicated by GDP." The economy is expanding but modestly, with participants expecting below-trend going forward. Current conditions suggest near potential or modestly above. The "modest pace" and expectation of below-trend going forward point to close to potential. Score ~50-52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "Labor market conditions remained very tight, with robust payroll gains and the unemployment rate still near historically low levels." There were "some signs that supply and demand in the labor market were coming into better balance" -- prime-age participation up, job openings/quits declining. Wage growth "still running at a pace... above what would be consistent... with the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective." Direction: clearly tight but showing early signs of loosening. The "very tight" language places this solidly above 50. Score ~67.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Consumer spending so far this year had been stronger than expected." Household wealth remained high. But "tighter credit conditions... were likely to weigh on economic activity." Business sector contacts "were mixed." Housing sector appeared to be "bottoming out." The economy overall was expanding at a "modest pace." Stronger-than-expected consumer spending combined with modest overall activity and mixed business reports. Score ~52-55.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "Tighter credit conditions for households and businesses were likely to weigh on economic activity." The "cumulative tightening of monetary policy... had contributed significantly to more restrictive financial conditions." Banking stresses "had receded" but credit tightening from that episode was ongoing. "Several participants mentioned that credit conditions had not appeared to have tightened significantly beyond what would be expected" from policy. Direction: clearly tighter than neutral. Score ~62.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: "Almost all participants judged it appropriate or acceptable to maintain the target range... at this meeting" (a hold). But "some participants indicated that they favored raising the target range... 25 basis points." Looking forward, "almost all participants noted... that they judged that additional increases in the target federal funds rate during 2023 would be appropriate." They paused to assess but nearly all signaled more hikes ahead. This is a hawkish hold -- holding now but signaling further tightening. Score ~68.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: "Almost all" supported holding, "some" favored a hike. There was a clear minority wanting a hike but no formal dissents mentioned (it was unanimous in action). The discussion shows moderate disagreement about whether to skip or hike. Score ~32.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: Banking stresses discussed but noted as "receded." Commercial real estate mentioned by "some." Credit conditions and banking-sector developments discussed by multiple participants across several paragraphs. This is meaningful but not dominant. Score ~40.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: "Almost all participants noted... additional increases in the target federal funds rate during 2023 would be appropriate." "Maintaining a restrictive stance for monetary policy would be appropriate." Upside risks to inflation were "key factors shaping the policy outlook." The forward signal is clearly hawkish -- more hikes expected. Score ~78.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "Participants generally noted a high degree of uncertainty regarding the cumulative effects on the economy." "Uncertainty about the outlook for the economy and inflation remained elevated." Lags of monetary policy, banking-sector effects, credit conditions all flagged as uncertain. The language "high degree of uncertainty" and "elevated" is strong. Score ~58.

### Cross-dimension coherence checks:
- OUTPUT_GAP (~51) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (~67): Divergence explained by the text -- GDP growth is modest/near potential, but the labor market hasn't yet adjusted (lagging indicator). Participants explicitly note they expect labor tightness to ease.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (~68) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (~78): Forward guidance is more hawkish than the current action because they paused at this meeting but signaled additional hikes. This divergence is explicitly noted in the text -- the "skip" was tactical, not a shift.

### Polarity checks:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE 70: Higher = more pressure. "Unacceptably high," "well above." Correct.
- OUTPUT_GAP 51: Higher = more above potential. "Modest pace," near potential. Correct.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS 67: Higher = tighter. "Very tight." Correct.
- FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS 62: Higher = tighter conditions. "More restrictive financial conditions." Correct.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 68: Higher = more hawkish. Hold but signaling more hikes. Correct.
- FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 78: Higher = more hawkish forward signal. "Additional increases... would be appropriate." Correct.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 70 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants agreed that inflation was unacceptably high and noted that the data... indicated that declines in inflation had been slower than they had expected."
- "Core inflation had not shown a sustained easing since the beginning of the year."
- "Core nonhousing services inflation had shown few signs of slowing in the past few months."
- "Longer-term measures of inflation expectations from surveys... remained well anchored."

**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation clearly above target and decelerating more slowly than expected, but off 2022 peaks and expectations remain anchored. Scores in the "clearly above target; broadening pressures; persistent, concerning" range.

**Minority views**: None -- unanimously perceived as too high. Uncertainty band reflects sub-component variation (goods easing vs services sticky).

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP: 51 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Economic activity had continued to expand at a modest pace."
- "Real GDP growth had been resilient in recent quarters" but "growth would be subdued over the remainder of this year."
- "GDI readings had been weak" and "economic momentum may not be as strong as indicated by the GDP readings."

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy at or just above potential, expanding modestly with expectation of slowing to below-trend. Currently not far from balance.

**Minority views**: The GDP/GDI discrepancy introduced some disagreement about the actual level of activity.

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 67 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Labor market conditions remained very tight, with robust payroll gains and the unemployment rate still near historically low levels."
- "Some signs that supply and demand in the labor market were coming into better balance" -- prime-age participation up, openings/quits declining.
- "Nominal wage growth had shown signs of easing but... was still running at a pace... above what would be consistent... with the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective."

**Center-of-gravity**: Very tight labor market with emerging signs of rebalancing. The "very tight" descriptor is unambiguous, but the easing signals prevent a higher score.

**Minority views**: "A few participants noted that their District contacts reported less difficulty in hiring" -- suggesting faster loosening in some areas.

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 54 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Consumer spending so far this year had been stronger than expected."
- "Aggregate household wealth remained high."
- "Reports from their contacts were mixed, with some pointing to softening economic conditions and others indicating greater-than-expected strength."
- "The effect of high interest rates on the housing sector appeared to be bottoming out."

**Center-of-gravity**: Consumer spending above expectations, but business sector mixed and overall activity "modest pace." Net position is slightly above trend.

**Minority views**: "A few participants mentioned that... consumers were facing increasingly tighter budget constraints" -- suggesting downside risks to spending.

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: 62 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Tighter credit conditions for households and businesses were likely to weigh on economic activity."
- "The cumulative tightening of monetary policy... had contributed significantly to more restrictive financial conditions."
- "Banking stresses had receded and conditions in the banking sector were much improved since early March."
- "Several participants mentioned that credit conditions had not appeared to have tightened significantly beyond what would be expected in response to the monetary policy actions."

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial conditions are notably tighter from cumulative rate hikes, with some additional tightening from banking stress (though that was fading). Solidly in the "somewhat tight" to lower end of "tight" range.

**Minority views**: Disagreement on how much banking stress had independently tightened credit beyond the monetary policy effect.

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 68 +/-8 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants judged it appropriate or acceptable to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 5 to 5-1/4 percent at this meeting."
- "Some participants indicated that they favored raising the target range... 25 basis points at this meeting."
- "Almost all participants noted... that they judged that additional increases in the target federal funds rate during 2023 would be appropriate."
- "Upside risks to the inflation outlook... remained key factors shaping the policy outlook."

**Center-of-gravity**: A hawkish hold -- pausing to assess but with a strong signal that more hikes are coming. The near-universal expectation of further increases tilts this well into hawkish territory.

**Minority views**: "Some" wanted to hike immediately (more hawkish); the uncertainty band captures both these hawks and the implicit doves who were relieved by the pause.

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY: 32 +/-7 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants judged it appropriate or acceptable to maintain the target range" -- the "acceptable" language suggests some reluctance.
- "Some participants indicated that they favored raising the target range... 25 basis points."
- No formal dissents are mentioned.

**Center-of-gravity**: Broad agreement on the hold decision but with a notable minority favoring a hike. "Some" represents meaningful but not deep disagreement. The "acceptable" qualifier suggests reluctant acceptance by a portion.

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 40 +/-6 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally noted that banking stresses had receded and conditions in the banking sector were much improved since early March."
- "Some participants noted concerns about the potential risks stemming from weakness in commercial real estate."
- Credit conditions and banking effects discussed across multiple paragraphs.

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial stability was a meaningful discussion topic -- banking stress, credit conditions, and commercial real estate all received attention -- but it was secondary to inflation and labor market concerns.

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 78 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants noted... that they judged that additional increases in the target federal funds rate during 2023 would be appropriate."
- "All participants continued to anticipate that... maintaining a restrictive stance for monetary policy would be appropriate."
- "Many also noted that... a further moderation in the pace of policy firming was appropriate" -- slowing pace but not stopping.

**Center-of-gravity**: Clear tightening signal -- nearly all participants projected more hikes in 2023. The forward signal is unambiguously hawkish, just below "aggressive further tightening" because the pace moderation was also noted.

**Minority views**: The emphasis on "data-dependent" and "additional time to observe" introduces some conditionality.

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 58 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally noted a high degree of uncertainty regarding the cumulative effects on the economy from both already-enacted monetary policy tightening and the potential additional tightening in credit conditions."
- "Uncertainty about the outlook for the economy and inflation remained elevated."
- "The full effects of monetary tightening had likely yet to be observed."

**Center-of-gravity**: Explicitly elevated uncertainty, particularly around policy lags and banking-sector credit effects. The "high degree" and "elevated" language places this above the midpoint but not into "very high" territory.
