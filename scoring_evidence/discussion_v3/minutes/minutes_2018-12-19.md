# FOMC Meeting Scoring: 2018-12-19 (Minutes)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). Post-2009 segmented format with clear participant language cues ("participants agreed," "several participants," "a few participants," etc.). The file begins partway through page 8 with staff projection risk language, then transitions to participant discussion.

**Era context**: December 2018 -- late in the 2015-2018 hiking cycle. The economy was strong but financial markets had been selling off sharply in Q4 2018. Trade tensions with China, slowing global growth, and equity market volatility were prominent concerns. The Fed raised rates 25bp at this meeting (the 4th hike of 2018) but signaled a more patient approach going forward. This was effectively the last hike of the cycle.

**Quantifier distribution**: "participants agreed" (multiple times), "most participants" (favored hike), "many participants" (patience), "several" (labor participation, TIPS decline, forward guidance removal), "a few" (no hike, inflation expectations lower, rising input costs), "a couple" (oil, financial stability risks).

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants see inflation as approximately at target. "Both overall and core PCE price inflation remained near 2 percent" but "core inflation had edged lower in recent months." A few noted rising input costs, but reports from other Districts suggested "some moderation in inflationary pressure." Participants "generally continued to view recent price developments as consistent with their expectation that inflation would remain near the Committee's symmetric 2 percent objective." Direction: AT the midpoint, perhaps very slightly below due to core edging lower. Score ~48.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Economic activity had been rising at a strong rate." "Economic growth was expected to remain above trend in 2019." The economy was "projected to move further above its potential." Bright-line rule: "above potential" language -> must be >50. "Strong rate" of growth + above trend/potential -> 55-58 range. Score ~56.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "Labor market had continued to strengthen." "Unemployment rate remained at a historically low level." "Contacts in many Districts continued to report tight labor markets with difficulties finding qualified workers." "Above-trend" payroll growth. "Rates of job openings and quits continued to be elevated." This is clearly tight -- strong language. Score ~62.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Household spending had continued to grow strongly" and "real PCE growth as remaining strong." However, "growth of business fixed investment had moderated from its rapid pace earlier in the year" though "more recent data pointed to a rebound." Residential investment showed "continued weakness." Business contacts "appeared less upbeat." Net: consumer spending strong, business investment moderately solid but softening sentiment, housing weak. Overall above trend but with mixed signals. Score ~56.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: Very prominent discussion. "Financial markets were volatile and conditions had tightened over the intermeeting period, with sizable declines in equity prices and notably wider corporate credit spreads coinciding with a continued flattening of the Treasury yield curve." This is clearly tighter than neutral. However, "a couple of participants noted that the tightening in financial conditions so far did not appear to be restraining real activity." The tightening is recent and notable but not crisis-level. Score ~60.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: "Most participants expressed the view that it would be appropriate... to raise the target range... 25 basis points." But "a few participants... favored no change." Forward looking: "many participants expressed the view that... the Committee could afford to be patient about further policy firming." They raised rates but clearly shifted dovish in forward outlook. The current action is hawkish (hike), but forward lean is dovish. Net: moderate hawkish for current action, but tempered significantly by patient forward signal. Score ~60.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: "Most" favored the hike; "a few" opposed. That's a meaningful minority dissent. There's also tension between those wanting to continue hiking and those wanting patience. But overall agreement on the hike from "most." 1 dissent equivalent + notable differences on forward path. Score ~35.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: Financial conditions discussed prominently -- "participants agreed that financial markets had been volatile and financial conditions had tightened." Multiple paragraphs devoted to financial markets. "A couple of participants pointed to risks to financial stability stemming from high levels of corporate borrowing... and elevated CRE prices." Moderate-to-substantial attention. Score ~50.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: Key shift here. "Some further gradual increases" still the baseline, but "the appropriate extent and timing of future policy firming less clear than earlier." "Many participants expressed the view that... the Committee could afford to be patient." Several wanted to remove forward guidance entirely. This is a clear pivot from tightening bias toward data-dependent/patient. Still mildly hawkish (further increases expected) but significantly tempered. Score ~55.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "Increased uncertainty about the global economic growth outlook." Concerns about trade tensions, global growth, financial volatility. "The appropriate extent and timing of future policy firming less clear than earlier." "Neither the pace nor the ultimate endpoint of future rate increases was known." Multiple downside risks enumerated. But "risks to the outlook appeared roughly balanced." Elevated but not extreme. Score ~48.

### Cross-dimension coherence check:
- OUTPUT_GAP (56) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (62): Both above 50, consistent with above-potential economy with tight labor market. Labor slightly higher reflects the very strong language about shortages. Coherent.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (60) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (55): HDWL slightly more hawkish because the current action (hike) is hawkish, while forward guidance has shifted notably dovish. The gap is explainable: they hiked but signaled patience. Coherent.

### Polarity checks:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: Higher = more pressure. Evidence says near target, edging slightly lower. Score 48 (slightly below 50). Correct.
- OUTPUT_GAP: Higher = more above potential. "Above potential" language. Score 56. Correct.
- FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: Higher = tighter. "Conditions had tightened." Score 60. Correct.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: Higher = more hawkish. Hiked but signaling patience. Score 60. Correct.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 48 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Both overall and core PCE price inflation remained near 2 percent on a 12-month basis, but that core inflation had edged lower in recent months."
- "Participants generally continued to view recent price developments as consistent with their expectation that inflation would remain near the Committee's symmetric 2 percent objective on a sustained basis."
- "A few participants pointed to anecdotal and survey evidence indicating rising input costs... reports from business contacts and surveys in some other Districts suggested some moderation in inflationary pressure."

**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation essentially at target with a slight downward drift in core measures; participants viewed this as consistent with 2% on a sustained basis -- neither alarming nor particularly subdued.

**Minority views**: A few saw rising input costs (upside); a few noted declining TIPS-based compensation possibly signaling lower expectations (downside). These offset, widening band modestly.

---

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP: 56 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Economic activity had been rising at a strong rate."
- "Economic growth was expected to remain above trend in 2019 and then slow to a pace closer to trend over the medium term."
- "An economy that was projected to move further above its potential" (from risk discussion context).

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy clearly operating above potential with above-trend growth, though expected to slow toward trend. Modestly above potential, not overheating.

**Minority views**: "Participants who downgraded their assessment of the economic outlook pointed to... financial market developments, some softening in the foreign economic growth outlook."

---

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 62 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "The unemployment rate remained at a historically low level."
- "Contacts in many Districts continued to report tight labor markets with difficulties finding qualified workers."
- "Payrolls continued to grow at an above-trend rate... measures of labor market tightness such as rates of job openings and quits continued to be elevated."

**Center-of-gravity**: Labor market clearly tight with broad agreement -- historically low unemployment, elevated quits/openings, widespread reports of worker shortages. Firmly above the NAIRU/full-employment level.

**Minority views**: "A couple of participants saw scope for further improvements in the labor force participation rate" suggesting some residual slack, while "a couple of others judged that there was little scope for significant further improvements."

---

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 56 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally characterized real PCE growth as remaining strong."
- "Business fixed investment remained solid despite a slowdown in the third quarter, as more recent data pointed to a rebound in investment spending."
- "Several participants noted continued weakness in residential investment."
- "Contacts in a number of Districts appeared less upbeat than at the time of the November meeting."

**Center-of-gravity**: Consumer spending strong, business investment solid but moderating with softening sentiment, housing weak. Net position above trend but with notable cross-currents tempering the strength.

**Minority views**: Business contacts becoming less upbeat; agricultural sector "depressed."

---

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: 60 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Financial markets were volatile and conditions had tightened over the intermeeting period, with sizable declines in equity prices and notably wider corporate credit spreads coinciding with a continued flattening of the Treasury yield curve."
- "A couple of participants noted that the tightening in financial conditions so far did not appear to be restraining real activity, although a more persistent tightening would undoubtedly weigh on business and household spending."

**Center-of-gravity**: Conditions clearly tightened from the previous accommodative/neutral baseline -- equity declines, wider spreads, flatter curve. Somewhat tight but not at crisis-level stress.

**Minority views**: Some saw the tightening as reflecting tail-risk concerns; a couple noted it was not yet restraining activity.

---

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 60 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Most participants expressed the view that it would be appropriate for the Committee to raise the target range for the federal funds rate 25 basis points at this meeting."
- "A few participants, however, favored no change in the target range at this meeting."
- "Many participants expressed the view that, especially in an environment of muted inflation pressures, the Committee could afford to be patient about further policy firming."

**Center-of-gravity**: Majority supported 25bp hike (hawkish current action), but the forward lean shifted notably dovish with "patience" language and downgraded rate path projections. Net lean: moderately hawkish for the current meeting, but meaningfully less hawkish than previous meetings in the cycle.

**Minority views**: A few favored no hike at all (dovish minority). The "patient" forward guidance signals broad movement toward caution.

---

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY: 35 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Most participants" favored the hike; "a few" opposed -- a notable minority.
- Forward guidance disagreement: "many" wanted patience, "several" wanted to remove forward guidance, while baseline still called for "some further gradual increases."
- "Risks to the outlook appeared roughly balanced, although some noted that downside risks may have increased of late."

**Center-of-gravity**: Moderate disagreement -- a clear minority opposed the hike, and the forward path debate showed meaningful differences. But the majority held on the current action.

**Minority views**: The dissent was substantive (policy-relevant, not just emphasis).

---

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 50 +/-5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- Multiple paragraphs discussing financial market volatility, equity declines, credit spread widening, yield curve flattening.
- "A couple of participants pointed to risks to financial stability stemming from high levels of corporate borrowing, especially by riskier firms, and elevated CRE prices."
- "Participants agreed to continue to monitor financial market developments and assess the implications."

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial conditions/stability was a prominent topic at this meeting -- most participants addressed it and it featured heavily in the risk discussion and policy deliberations. Moderate-to-substantial attention level.

**Minority views**: Specific financial stability risks (corporate leverage, CRE) raised by only a couple.

---

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 55 +/-8 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Some further gradual increases in the target range for the federal funds rate would most likely be consistent with a sustained economic expansion."
- "The appropriate extent and timing of future policy firming less clear than earlier."
- "Many participants expressed the view that... the Committee could afford to be patient about further policy firming."
- "Several participants expressed the view that it might be appropriate over upcoming meetings to remove forward guidance entirely."

**Center-of-gravity**: The forward signal is mildly hawkish in baseline (further gradual increases expected) but significantly tempered by patience language and uncertainty about timing/extent. A notable pivot from the prior tightening bias toward data dependence. Still tilted slightly toward tightening, not easing.

**Minority views**: Range from continued gradual tightening to full removal of forward guidance in favor of pure data dependence.

---

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 48 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Increased uncertainty about the global economic growth outlook."
- "The appropriate extent and timing of future policy firming less clear than earlier."
- "Neither the pace nor the ultimate endpoint of future rate increases was known."
- "In general, participants agreed that risks to the outlook appeared roughly balanced, although some noted that downside risks may have increased of late."

**Center-of-gravity**: Elevated relative to the prior hiking cycle but not extreme. Multiple sources of uncertainty identified (trade, global growth, financial conditions), but participants still felt risks were "roughly balanced." Somewhat above typical levels for this era.

**Minority views**: "Some" saw increased downside risks -- suggesting a minority felt uncertainty was higher than the consensus.
