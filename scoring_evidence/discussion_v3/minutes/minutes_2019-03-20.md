# FOMC Meeting Scoring: March 19-20, 2019

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). Post-2009 segmented format with clear participant attribution language.

**Era context**: March 2019 -- the Fed had hiked four times in 2018, reaching 2.25-2.50%. Global growth was slowing (Europe, China), trade policy uncertainty was elevated, and the Fed had shifted to a "patient" posture after the December 2018 tightening and associated financial market volatility. Inflation was running near but slightly below 2%.

**Quantifier distribution**: "Most" (inflation near target), "many" (consumer spending recovery expected; weakness in investment to abate; inflation not firming despite tight labor market), "several" (yield curve concerns; inflation expectations at lower end; patience needs regular review; data-dependent), "some" (modest policy response; flat trajectory), "a few" (slack remaining; uncertainty; productivity; revised down unemployment estimates; financial stability risks).

### Dimensional reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants see inflation as slightly below target. "Latest readings on overall inflation had been somewhat softer than expected." "Most participants, while seeing inflation pressures as muted, expected...inflation to firm somewhat and to be at or near...2 percent." "Many participants indicated that...it was noteworthy that it had not shown greater signs of firming." Core near 2% but overall below due to energy. Muted pressures, slightly below target. Direction: below midpoint. Score range: 38-42 per anchors (2013-19 below target).

**OUTPUT_GAP**: Participants note slowing growth in Q1 from temporary factors, but expect continued expansion. "A number of participants judged that economic growth...would likely be a little lower." Growth expected "near its longer-run trend rate." "Some participants indicated that if the economy evolved as they currently expected, with economic growth above its longer-run trend rate." Mixed signals: near potential with some suggesting slightly above. Direction: near midpoint, slightly above. Score ~50-52.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "Conditions remained strong, including the very low unemployment rate, a further increase in the labor force participation rate, a low number of layoffs, near-record levels of job openings." "Strong labor demand, with firms offering both higher wages and more nonwage benefits to attract workers." "A few participants cited the combination of muted inflation pressures and expanding employment as a possible indication that some slack remained." Center of gravity: tight labor market. Direction: above midpoint. Score ~58-62.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Consumer spending showed "softness" in Q1 but attributed to temporary factors (government shutdown, financial volatility). "Many participants expected consumer spending to proceed at a stronger pace in coming months." Business investment also slowing: "apparent slowing of growth in business fixed investment." "Many participants pointed to signs that the weakness in investment would likely abate." Housing sector "continued softness...was a concern." Net: below trend currently but expected to recover. Score the current assessment with forward lean. Direction: slightly below trend. Score ~42-46.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "A good deal of the tightening over the latter part of last year in financial conditions had since been reversed." "Asset valuations had recovered strongly." "Federal Reserve communications since the beginning of this year were seen as an important contributor to the recent improvements in financial conditions." Yield curve flat, but conditions have eased. Direction: somewhat loose to neutral. Score ~35-40.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: "Participants agreed that it would be appropriate to maintain the current target range." "A patient approach...remained appropriate." "A majority of participants expected that the evolution of the economic outlook...would likely warrant leaving the target range unchanged for the remainder of the year." "Some participants indicated...they would likely judge it appropriate to raise the target range...modestly later this year." Center of gravity: hold with patience, majority sees no change for rest of year. This is lean dovish / balanced hold. Direction: slightly below midpoint. Score ~43-47.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: Broad agreement on holding rates and patience. Some differences about future direction -- majority sees flat, some see potential hike later. No formal dissents mentioned. "Several participants noted that their views...could shift in either direction." Moderate spread in forward views but agreement on current action. Score ~25-30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: Discussion of yield curve at some length by "several participants." "A few participants observed that the appropriate path for policy...could lead to greater financial stability risks." "Several participants pointed to increased debt issuance and higher leverage of nonfinancial corporations." Moderate but not dominant topic. Score ~30-38.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: "Patient" is the key word. Majority sees no change for rest of 2019. "Some participants" see possible modest hike later this year. "Several participants noted that their views...could shift in either direction." Neutral to mildly dovish forward signal. Per anchors, 2019 mid-cycle patience = 35-45. Score ~40-44.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "Participants also noted significant uncertainties surrounding their economic outlooks, including those related to global economic and financial developments." "A few participants noted that there remained a high level of uncertainty associated with international developments." "A number of participants judged that economic growth...would likely be a little lower." Trade policy, Brexit, global slowdown all cited. Somewhat elevated. Score ~42-48.

### Cross-dimension coherence check:
- OUTPUT_GAP (~51) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (~60): Diverge somewhat -- labor market is tighter than output gap suggests. This is consistent with the text: growth is slowing but labor market remains strong. The divergence is explained by temporary factors in output and lagging labor indicators.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (~45) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (~42): Aligned -- both lean slightly dovish, consistent with "patient" posture.

### Polarity checks:
- INFLATION_PRESSURE: Higher = more pressure. Evidence says muted/below target. Score 40 = below midpoint. Correct.
- OUTPUT_GAP: Higher = more above potential. Evidence says near potential. Score 51 = slightly above. Correct.
- LABOR_TIGHTNESS: Higher = tighter. Evidence says strong/tight. Score 60 = above midpoint. Correct.
- FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: Higher = tighter. Evidence says conditions eased/improved. Score 37 = below midpoint (loose). Correct.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: Higher = more hawkish. Evidence says patient hold. Score 45 = slightly dovish. Correct.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 40 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants noted that the latest readings on overall inflation had been somewhat softer than expected."
- "Most participants, while seeing inflation pressures as muted, expected the overall rate of inflation to firm somewhat and to be at or near the Committee's longer-run objective of 2 percent over the next few years."
- "Many participants indicated that...it was noteworthy that it had not shown greater signs of firming in response to strong labor market conditions and rising nominal wage growth."

**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation slightly below target with muted pressures, but core near 2%. Participants puzzled by lack of firming despite tight labor markets.

**Minority views**: Upside risk from wage pressures mentioned; a few note productivity helping damp pressures. Band reflects modest dispersion.

---

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 51 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Some participants indicated that if the economy evolved as they currently expected, with economic growth above its longer-run trend rate..."
- "Several of these participants noted that the current target range...was close to their estimates of its longer-run neutral level and foresaw economic growth continuing near its longer-run trend rate."
- "A number of participants judged that economic growth in the remaining quarters of 2019...would likely be a little lower...than they had previously forecast."

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy near potential with mixed signals -- some see growth above trend, majority sees near trend. Q1 softness viewed as largely temporary.

**Minority views**: Downward revisions to growth outlook by "a number of" participants widen the band slightly.

---

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 60 +/-6 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants cited evidence that conditions remained strong, including the very low unemployment rate, a further increase in the labor force participation rate, a low number of layoffs, near-record levels of job openings."
- "Reports from business contacts predominantly pointed to continued strong labor demand, with firms offering both higher wages and more nonwage benefits to attract workers."
- "A few participants cited the combination of muted inflation pressures and expanding employment as a possible indication that some slack remained in the labor market."

**Center-of-gravity**: Labor market clearly tight with strong demand, low unemployment, firms competing for workers. Beyond full employment by most accounts.

**Minority views**: A few see possible remaining slack given muted inflation, pulling the band down slightly.

---

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 44 +/-6 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Softness in consumer spending had contributed importantly to the projected slowing in economic growth in the current quarter."
- "Many participants expected consumer spending to proceed at a stronger pace in coming months, supported by favorable underlying factors."
- "Participants also commented on the apparent slowing of growth in business fixed investment in the first quarter." "Many participants pointed to signs that the weakness in investment would likely abate."

**Center-of-gravity**: Both consumption and investment soft in Q1, but temporary factors widely cited. Expected to recover. Net position: slightly below trend currently.

**Minority views**: Some contacts report favorable business conditions; housing sector remains a concern.

---

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 37 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "A good deal of the tightening over the latter part of last year in financial conditions had since been reversed."
- "Asset valuations had recovered strongly."
- "Several participants expressed concern that the yield curve for Treasury securities was now quite flat and noted that historical evidence suggested that an inverted yield curve could portend economic weakness."

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial conditions have eased significantly from late 2018 tightening. Fed communications credited. Somewhat loose to neutral.

**Minority views**: Yield curve concerns from several participants add a note of caution.

---

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN
**Score: 45 +/-7 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally agreed that a patient approach to determining future adjustments to the target range...remained appropriate."
- "A majority of participants expected that the evolution of the economic outlook...would likely warrant leaving the target range unchanged for the remainder of the year."
- "Some participants indicated that if the economy evolved as they currently expected, with economic growth above its longer-run trend rate, they would likely judge it appropriate to raise the target range...modestly later this year."

**Center-of-gravity**: Hold with patience. Majority sees no change for 2019. Slight dovish lean from the "patient" framing and majority favoring unchanged rates.

**Minority views**: "Some" participants still see possible hike later in 2019, providing a hawkish tail. This widens the band.

---

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY
**Score: 27 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants agreed that it would be appropriate to maintain the current target range."
- Forward-looking views diverge: majority sees hold for the year, "some" see possible hike, "several" say views could shift either direction.
- No formal dissents mentioned.

**Center-of-gravity**: Broad agreement on current action (hold + patience). Differences emerge on forward direction but remain moderate. No sharp disagreements.

**Minority views**: Spread in forward views (hold vs. potential hike) accounts for score above minimum.

---

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS
**Score: 33 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Several participants expressed concern that the yield curve...was now quite flat."
- "Several participants pointed to the increased debt issuance and higher leverage of nonfinancial corporations as a development that warranted continued monitoring."
- "A few participants observed that the appropriate path for policy...could lead to greater financial stability risks."

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial stability discussed by several participants across multiple angles (yield curve, corporate leverage, low-rate risks) but not a dominant topic.

**Minority views**: Limited -- those who raised it were in the "several" to "a few" range.

---

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE
**Score: 42 +/-7 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "A majority of participants expected...leaving the target range unchanged for the remainder of the year."
- "Several participants observed that the characterization of the Committee's approach...as 'patient' would need to be reviewed regularly."
- "Some participants indicated...they would likely judge it appropriate to raise the target range...modestly later this year."

**Center-of-gravity**: Forward signal is neutral to mildly dovish -- "patient" with majority expecting no change. But not signaling easing either. Data-dependent posture with slight dovish lean.

**Minority views**: The "some" who foresee a potential hike pull the forward signal slightly hawkward.

---

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED
**Score: 45 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants also noted significant uncertainties surrounding their economic outlooks, including those related to global economic and financial developments."
- "A few participants noted that there remained a high level of uncertainty associated with international developments, including ongoing trade talks and Brexit deliberations."
- Multiple downside and upside risks enumerated: "sizable spillovers from a greater-than-expected economic slowdown in Europe and China," trade policy, fiscal stimulus falloff.

**Center-of-gravity**: Uncertainty somewhat elevated above normal. Multiple sources of uncertainty cited (global growth, trade, Brexit, fiscal). Word "significant" used regarding uncertainties.

**Minority views**: "A couple of participants remarked that the risks of adverse outcomes were somewhat lower than in January" -- slightly tempering.
