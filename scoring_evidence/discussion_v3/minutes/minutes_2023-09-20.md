# FOMC Discussion Scoring — 2023-09-20 (Minutes)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). Segmented — participant views section is clearly separated from staff sections.

**Era context**: September 2023. The Fed is at or near the peak of its aggressive hiking cycle (5.25-5.50%). Inflation has come down meaningfully from 2022 peaks but remains well above 2%. The economy has been surprisingly resilient. Labor market is cooling but still tight.

**Quantifier distribution**: Heavy use of "participants" (collective), "most," "many," "several," "some," "a few," "a couple." Key signals: "almost all" judged hold appropriate; "a majority" judged one more hike likely appropriate; "some" judged no further increases warranted; "all" agreed policy should remain restrictive for some time.

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants see inflation as clearly above target. "Current inflation remained unacceptably high," "inflation remained well above the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective," "elevated inflation was continuing to harm businesses and households." However, they also acknowledge moderation: "inflation had moderated somewhat over the past year," "data received over the past several months generally suggested that inflation was slowing." Core PCE at 4.2%, headline PCE at 3.3%. The center of gravity is: inflation is elevated but decelerating. This maps to "clearly above target" (61-75 range). The deceleration tempers toward the lower end. Direction: above midpoint. Score ~65.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Real GDP had been expanding at a solid pace and had been more resilient than expected." Participants "continued to view a period of below-trend growth... as likely to be needed" — this is forward-looking aspiration, not current assessment. Currently: "economic activity had been expanding at a solid pace." The economy is running at or somewhat above potential. The bright-line rule: no explicit "above potential" or "below potential" language for the current state, but "solid pace" and resilience suggest modestly above trend. Score ~53-55.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: "Labor market was tight but that supply and demand conditions were continuing to come into better balance." "Most participants remarked that a range of indicators of labor demand were easing." "Several participants noted that labor markets remained very tight in some sectors." Wage growth moderating but "still rising at rates above levels generally assessed to be consistent with the sustained achievement of the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective." This is tight but loosening — still above the balanced midpoint. Anchors: 65-75 for 2022-23. The easing language pulls it toward the lower end. Score ~60.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: "Aggregate consumer spending had continued to exhibit considerable strength." Business activity "continued to be solid, though several pointed to signs of softening conditions." "Many participants noted improved business conditions." Housing demand "resilient." But headwinds noted: "tighter credit conditions," "waning fiscal support," student loan resumption. Net: above trend but with acknowledged headwinds. Score ~58.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "U.S. financial conditions tightened" over the intermeeting period. "Tighter credit conditions facing households and businesses were a source of headwinds." "Bank credit conditions appeared to tighten somewhat." "Several participants judged that, over coming quarters, business activity would be restrained by tighter financial conditions." But "credit to businesses and households remained generally accessible." "Several participants noted, however, that the tightening of credit conditions resulting from the banking stresses earlier in the year was likely to be less severe than they previously expected." This is clearly in tighter-than-neutral territory, but not crisis-level. Score ~62.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: "Almost all participants judged it appropriate to maintain the target range" (hold). "A majority of participants judged that one more increase... would likely be appropriate, while some judged it likely that no further increases would be warranted." "All participants agreed that policy should remain restrictive for some time." "Several participants commented that... the focus... should shift from how high to raise the policy rate to how long to hold the policy rate at restrictive levels." This is a hold with a tightening bias — majority see one more hike but the debate is shifting to duration. Score ~63.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: "Almost all" agreed on hold (near-consensus on current action). But there's meaningful divergence on forward path: "a majority" want one more hike vs. "some" who see no further increases. "Several" want to shift focus to duration. This is moderate differences — the current decision is near-consensus but forward views diverge. Score ~30.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: Banking sector mentioned as "largely stabilized." "A number of participants expressed concerns about vulnerabilities in the CRE sector." Financial conditions discussed as headwinds. Banking stresses mentioned. But this is not a dominant topic — it's interspersed with broader economic discussion. Moderate attention. Score ~35.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: "A majority of participants judged that one more increase... would likely be appropriate." "All participants agreed that policy should remain restrictive for some time." "Several participants commented that... the focus... should shift from how high to raise the policy rate to how long to hold." "Proceed carefully." This is a mild tightening bias — one more hike possible, restrictive for extended period, but increasingly data-dependent and cautious. Score ~68.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: "Participants generally noted there was still a high degree of uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook." "A vast majority of participants continued to judge the future path of the economy as highly uncertain." "Many noted data volatility and potential data revisions, or the difficulty of estimating the neutral policy rate." Multiple specific risks enumerated. This is clearly elevated uncertainty. Score ~62.

### Cross-dimension coherence:
- OUTPUT_GAP (53) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (60): Both above 50, labor somewhat higher reflecting the still-tight conditions despite easing. Reasonable — GDP strong but labor market is the key concern.
- HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (63) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (68): Both lean hawkish. Forward guidance slightly more hawkish because majority signal one more hike while current action is a hold. Coherent.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 65 +/-7 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants stressed that current inflation remained unacceptably high while acknowledging that it had moderated somewhat over the past year."
- "Inflation remained well above the Committee's 2 percent longer-run objective and... elevated inflation was continuing to harm businesses and households."
- "Participants noted that the data received over the past several months generally suggested that inflation was slowing."

**Center of gravity**: Inflation clearly above target and "unacceptably high" but showing meaningful deceleration. Participants want more progress before gaining confidence.

**Minority views**: "A majority of participants pointed to upside risks to inflation from rising energy prices." Some noted progress in goods and housing services inflation but "significant progress in reducing inflation had yet to become apparent in the prices of core services excluding housing." Uncertainty band reflects upside risk emphasis.

---

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 54 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants assessed that real GDP had been expanding at a solid pace and had been more resilient than expected."
- "Economic activity had been expanding at a solid pace and had been resilient."
- "Participants continued to view a period of below-trend growth in real GDP... as likely to be needed."

**Center of gravity**: Economy running at a solid pace, modestly above potential. "Solid" and "resilient" language indicates above-trend current growth, but participants expect and want a slowdown.

**Minority views**: Narrow range of views on current activity — broad agreement it is solid. Uncertainty about forward path.

---

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 60 +/-6 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants observed that the labor market was tight but that supply and demand conditions were continuing to come into better balance."
- "Most participants remarked that a range of indicators of labor demand were easing—as could be seen by declines in job openings, a narrowing of the jobs-to-workers gap, lower quits rates."
- "Several participants noted that labor markets remained very tight in some sectors of the economy, such as healthcare services and education."
- "Nominal wages were still rising at rates above levels generally assessed to be consistent with the sustained achievement of the Committee's 2 percent inflation objective."

**Center of gravity**: Still tight but meaningfully easing. The center recognizes ongoing tightness with clear directional improvement.

**Minority views**: "Several" note very tight conditions in specific sectors; "most" see broad easing in indicators. Band captures this range.

---

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 58 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Aggregate consumer spending had continued to exhibit considerable strength, supported by the strong labor market and by generally strong household balance sheets."
- "Regarding the business sector, participants noted that activity continued to be solid, though several pointed to signs of softening conditions."
- "Many participants remarked that the finances of some households were coming under pressure amid high inflation and declining savings."

**Center of gravity**: Consumer spending showing "considerable strength" and business activity "solid" — clearly above trend. Tempered by growing headwinds.

**Minority views**: "Several participants" see credit conditions and fiscal headwinds as potential drags; "a few" note rising delinquencies. These concerns keep the score from going higher.

---

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 62 +/-6 [Moderate confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "U.S. financial conditions tightened, with higher longer-term rates, lower equity prices, and a stronger dollar contributing roughly equally."
- "Tighter credit conditions facing households and businesses were a source of headwinds for the economy."
- "Several participants noted, however, that the tightening of credit conditions resulting from the banking stresses earlier in the year was likely to be less severe than they previously expected."

**Center of gravity**: Conditions clearly tighter than neutral — rates up, credit tightening, equities down. But not at crisis levels; credit "remained generally accessible."

**Minority views**: Some see conditions as quite restrictive; others note less-severe-than-expected banking stress spillover. The mixed signals widen the band slightly.

---

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN
**Score: 63 +/-7 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants judged it appropriate to maintain the target range for the federal funds rate at 5-1/4 to 5-1/2 percent."
- "A majority of participants judged that one more increase in the target federal funds rate at a future meeting would likely be appropriate, while some judged it likely that no further increases would be warranted."
- "All participants agreed that policy should remain restrictive for some time."
- "The Committee was in a position to proceed carefully."

**Center of gravity**: Hold at current rate with majority leaning toward one more hike. "Proceed carefully" and data-dependence language temper the hawkishness. Firmly in the "moderate hawkish" zone.

**Minority views**: "Some" see no further hikes needed; "several" want to shift focus to duration of restrictiveness rather than further increases. These pull the dovish side of the band.

---

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY
**Score: 30 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Almost all participants judged it appropriate to maintain the target range" — near-consensus on current action.
- Divergence on forward path: "a majority" favor one more hike vs. "some" who favor none.
- "Several participants commented that... the focus... should shift from how high to raise the policy rate to how long to hold."

**Center of gravity**: Broad agreement on current action. Meaningful but not sharp divergence on the forward path. Consistent with 1 or fewer formal dissents.

**Minority views**: The "some" vs. "majority" split on future hikes creates moderate forward-looking disagreement but not deep division.

---

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS
**Score: 35 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Banking sector appeared to have largely stabilized."
- "A number of participants expressed concerns about vulnerabilities in the CRE sector."
- "Tighter credit conditions... were a source of headwinds."
- Banking stresses from earlier in the year referenced but seen as less severe than expected.

**Center of gravity**: Financial stability discussed meaningfully but as context rather than a primary focus. CRE concerns and banking stress aftermath get notable mentions but do not dominate.

---

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE
**Score: 68 +/-7 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "A majority of participants judged that one more increase in the target federal funds rate at a future meeting would likely be appropriate."
- "All participants agreed that policy should remain restrictive for some time until the Committee is confident that inflation is moving down sustainably."
- "Several participants commented that, with the policy rate likely at or near its peak, the focus of monetary policy decisions and communications should shift from how high to raise the policy rate to how long to hold."

**Center of gravity**: Forward signal is moderately hawkish — majority signal one more hike possible, and extended restrictive hold. But "proceed carefully" and data-dependence language, plus the shift-to-duration contingent, pull back from a strong tightening signal.

**Minority views**: "Some" see no further hikes, pulling the lower end of the band toward 60.

---

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED
**Score: 62 +/-5 [High confidence]**

**Evidence**:
- "Participants generally noted there was still a high degree of uncertainty surrounding the economic outlook."
- "A vast majority of participants continued to judge the future path of the economy as highly uncertain."
- "Many noted data volatility and potential data revisions, or the difficulty of estimating the neutral policy rate, as supporting the case for proceeding carefully."

**Center of gravity**: Uncertainty explicitly characterized as "high" by a "vast majority." Multiple specific risk factors enumerated (autoworkers' strike, energy prices, China, government shutdown, data revisions). This is genuine, not rhetorical.
