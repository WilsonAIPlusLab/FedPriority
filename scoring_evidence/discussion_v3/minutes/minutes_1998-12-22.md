# FOMC Meeting Scoring: 1998-12-22

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary). Unsegmented pre-2009 format with some staff/participant mixing, but participant language cues are clear ("members commented," "members recognized," "several noted," "some members observed," "all the members agreed").

**Era context**: Late 1998. The economy had weathered the LTCM/Russia/Asian financial crisis in the fall. The Fed had cut rates three times (Sept-Nov 1998) by a total of 75bp, bringing the fed funds rate to 4.75%. Domestic economy was surprisingly strong despite external turmoil. This was within the late-1990s boom period.

**Quantifier distribution**: "all the members" (3 uses - hold, symmetry, directive wording), "members" generically (many uses), "several" (2 uses - monetary aggregates, weakening industrial countries), "some" (3 uses - deceleration evidence, upside risks, money reference in directive), "a few" (0 explicit uses). The center of gravity is broadly shared views with "some" members flagging upside inflation risks.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### INFLATION_PRESSURE: 38 +/-8 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "The outlook for inflation remained favorable... there were few signs of rising price inflation despite widespread indications of very tight labor markets." "Increases in core measures of prices were limited and sizable declines in oil and commodity prices should help to moderate inflation going forward." "Several commented, however, that the risks of inflation appeared to be tilted to the upside."
- **Center of gravity**: Members broadly see current inflation as subdued and benign, with favorable near-term dynamics, though some flag upside risks from strong demand and accommodative conditions.
- **Minority views**: "Some members" see upside risks from reversal of favorable factors (oil, health care costs) and rapid monetary growth, pushing the uncertainty band upward.

### OUTPUT_GAP: 58 +/-7 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Moderate growth at a pace close to the economy's potential remained a reasonable expectation." "Marked resilience and persisting strength of private domestic demand, which had kept the economy expanding at a faster pace than most had anticipated." "Demand continued to outpace the growth of potential."
- **Center of gravity**: Economy running modestly above potential, with strong domestic demand partly offset by foreign drag. Members expect moderation toward potential but acknowledge persistent above-trend momentum.
- **Minority views**: Downside risk from foreign sector could pull growth below potential; upside from continued domestic strength.

### LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 72 +/-5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Labor markets were extraordinarily tight across the nation." "Widespread indications of very tight labor markets, including reports of further tightening in some areas." "Persistence of tight labor markets should continue to encourage relatively rapid growth in labor-saving business capital."
- **Center of gravity**: Unanimous view of extraordinarily tight labor markets. No dissent on this assessment.
- **Minority views**: None - this is one of the clearest consensus points in the document.

### CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 62 +/-7 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "High levels and strong growth of overall domestic production and demand." "Growth in consumer spending was expected to moderate over coming quarters from a very robust pace." "Housing activity had displayed a great deal of strength in recent months." Business investment: "very rapid increases in recent years" though "appreciable deceleration" expected ahead.
- **Center of gravity**: Current spending and investment are clearly above trend across all categories, though moderation is expected.
- **Minority views**: "Some members observed... there was little evidence thus far of any deceleration in business equipment expenditures," suggesting even stronger readings possible.

### FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: 30 +/-8 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Domestic financial conditions, including the rebound in stock market prices, currently were supportive of further expansion." "Accommodative financial conditions." "Increasingly supportive conditions in debt and equity markets." BUT "domestic financial markets remained unusually sensitive and subject to relatively pronounced adjustments."
- **Center of gravity**: Financial conditions are clearly accommodative/loose following the Fed's three rate cuts and market recovery, though markets remain fragile and sensitive.
- **Minority views**: The fragility/sensitivity language widens the band upward - conditions could tighten quickly if foreign shocks materialize.

### HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 50 +/-5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "All the members agreed on the desirability of maintaining an unchanged policy stance." "The direction and timing of the next policy move were uncertain." "All the members indicated that they were in favor of retaining the symmetry in the existing directive."
- **Center of gravity**: Pure hold with symmetric risk assessment. Committee has completed its easing cycle and sees no lean in either direction.
- **Minority views**: Minimal - unanimous on hold and symmetry.

### DISSENT_INTENSITY: 10 +/-5 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "All the members agreed on the desirability of maintaining an unchanged policy stance." "All the members supported a reworded directive." "Minor differences about specific wording, but no strongly held opinions."
- **Center of gravity**: Near-total consensus on both policy action and directive language.
- **Minority views**: Only minor wording differences on the directive, explicitly described as not strongly held.

### FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 42 +/-8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Domestic financial markets remained unusually sensitive and subject to relatively pronounced adjustments." "The System's policy easing actions since late September had helped to stabilize a dangerously eroding financial situation." "Greater disturbances abroad, especially if they were to be transmitted to domestic financial markets, could exert considerable restraint."
- **Center of gravity**: Financial conditions and stability are a meaningful part of the discussion - the LTCM/crisis backdrop shapes risk assessment - but the discussion is predominantly focused on real economic activity and inflation.
- **Minority views**: N/A.

### FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 50 +/-7 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "The Committee had entered a period where vigilance was called for but where the direction and timing of the next policy move were uncertain." "All the members indicated that they were in favor of retaining the symmetry." "Committee saw risks on both sides of their forecasts."
- **Center of gravity**: Genuinely neutral forward posture. No directional signal. Symmetric risks and symmetric directive.
- **Minority views**: Slight asymmetry in the text - more detailed discussion of what would prompt easing (external shocks) vs. tightening (inflation) - but this is risk assessment, not guidance.

### UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 55 +/-7 [High confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Such a projection was subject to an unusually wide range of uncertainty in both directions." "Domestic financial markets remained unusually sensitive." "The external sector continued to represent a major source of downside risk... a crisis in one or a group of important financial markets and economies could spread rapidly around the world."
- **Center of gravity**: Members explicitly flag "unusually wide" uncertainty with significant risks in both directions, driven by the volatile global environment.
- **Minority views**: N/A - the elevated uncertainty is broadly shared.

### INFLATION_GOODS: 30 +/-8 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Sizable declines in oil and commodity prices should help to moderate inflation going forward." "Declines in oil and commodity prices" cited as temporary favorable factor.
- **Center of gravity**: Goods prices are declining, providing favorable inflation dynamics.

### INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 40 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Now firmly embedded expectations of low inflation." "In part by holding down inflation expectations."
- **Center of gravity**: Expectations are well-anchored at low inflation levels - below the 2% symmetric target construct (which was not yet formalized).

### CREDIT_STRESS: 35 +/-10 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Increasingly supportive conditions in debt... markets." Past: "dangerously eroding financial situation" now stabilized. "Several large emerging economies" still fragile.
- **Center of gravity**: Credit conditions have recovered from the fall 1998 stress but markets remain sensitive; current conditions are better than normal but with residual fragility.

### PRODUCTIVITY: 65 +/-7 [Moderate confidence]
- **Evidence**: "Robust productivity growth." "Relatively rapid growth in labor-saving business capital."
- **Center of gravity**: Members view productivity growth as notably strong, helping explain the favorable inflation-employment combination.
