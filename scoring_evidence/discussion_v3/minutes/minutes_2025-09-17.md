# FOMC Meeting Scoring: September 16-17, 2025

## Part 1: Thinking Process

**Document type**: Minutes (synthesized summary of participant views). This is a post-2009 segmented format with clear separation between staff and participant sections.

**Era context**: September 2025. The economy is in a period of moderating growth with tariff-related inflation pressures. The Fed has been holding rates at a restrictive level and is beginning to ease with a 25bp cut at this meeting. Unemployment has edged up but remains low. Inflation remains somewhat above the 2% target, partly due to tariff effects.

**Quantifier distribution**: "almost all" (supported 25bp cut, well positioned after cut), "most" (appropriate to move toward neutral, appropriate to ease further over remainder of year, expected tariff effects realized by end of next year), "many" (strong high-tech investment), "several" (financial conditions supportive, weakness in housing, continued job gains consistent with stable unemployment declining, AI potentially reducing labor demand, firming consumption this quarter, some measures suggest policy not particularly restrictive), "some" (tariff effects muted, businesses would raise prices, labor market not source of inflationary pressure, households showing price sensitivity, lower consumption growth contributed to slowdown, noted less upside risk to inflation, financial conditions suggest policy not particularly restrictive), "a few" (productivity reducing inflation, labor market had been softening longer, merit in keeping rate unchanged or could have supported holding, SRF helping), "a couple" (excluding tariffs inflation close to target, net migration lowering demand/inflation, productivity gains reducing pressures, housing deterioration as downside risk), "one" (preferred 50bp cut).

### Dimension-by-dimension reasoning:

**INFLATION_PRESSURE**: Participants observed inflation "moved up since the beginning of the year and remained somewhat above the Committee's 2 percent longer-run goal." Tariff increases put upward pressure. A majority emphasized upside risks to inflation outlooks. However, some noted tariff effects "somewhat muted," a few suggested productivity gains reducing pressures, and some noted labor market not a source of inflationary pressure. Longer-term expectations "well anchored." Direction: above target, "somewhat elevated." This maps to 55-60 range. The "majority" emphasizing upside risks pushes slightly higher. Score: 58.

**OUTPUT_GAP**: "Growth of economic activity slowed in the first half of the year relative to last year." Lower consumption growth contributed to slowdown. Several noted firming this quarter. Financial conditions were supportive. No language about "above potential" or "below potential" explicitly. Growth moderated but economy still expanding. Unemployment edged up but remained low. This suggests economy near potential but slightly softening -- slightly below potential. Score: 46.

**LABOR_TIGHTNESS**: Job gains slowed, unemployment rate edged up but "remained low." Indicators "did not show a sharp deterioration." Low hiring and firing rates suggest less dynamism. Downside risks to employment "had increased." Participants expected conditions "little changed or would soften modestly." The labor market is cooling from tight but still near balance. Score: 48.

Cross-check: OUTPUT_GAP (46) and LABOR_TIGHTNESS (48) are coherent -- both slightly below midpoint, reflecting mild softening.

**CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT**: Growth slowed in first half. "Lower consumption growth had contributed to the slowdown." Several noted "some firming of consumption expenditures this quarter." Households showing "greater price sensitivity." High-income doing better than lower-income. Many noted "strong high-tech investment." Several noted financial conditions supportive. Agricultural sector facing headwinds. Continued weakness in housing. Net: below trend but with some recent firming signals. Score: 44.

**FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS**: "Several participants noted that financial conditions were supportive of economic activity." "Some participants noted that, by several measures, financial conditions suggested that monetary policy may not be particularly restrictive." This points to conditions being somewhat loose/accommodative rather than tight. Score: 38.

Polarity check: Higher = tighter. Evidence says conditions are supportive/not particularly restrictive = loose. Score below 50 = looser. Correct.

**HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN**: Almost all supported 25bp cut. Most judged appropriate to ease further over remainder of year. A few saw merit in holding. One preferred 50bp cut. The center of gravity is clearly easing, but cautiously -- 25bp with data dependence. A majority emphasized upside inflation risks; downside employment risks elevated. "Balanced approach" stressed. This is a moderate dovish action with caution. Score: 38.

Polarity check: Higher = more hawkish. Cutting 25bp with most favoring further easing = dovish. 38 is in the "moderate dovish" range (26-40). The caution and inflation concerns keep it from being lower. Correct.

**FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE**: "Almost all participants noted that, with the reduction in the target range at this meeting, the Committee was well positioned to respond in a timely way." "Most judged that it likely would be appropriate to ease policy further over the remainder of this year." But monetary policy "not on a preset course" and "informed by a wide range of incoming data." Some noted policy may not be particularly restrictive, warranting "cautious approach." Direction: mild easing bias with heavy data dependence. Score: 35.

Coherence check: HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN (38) and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (35) are aligned -- both lean dovish/easing.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY**: Almost all supported 25bp cut. One member voted against (preferred 50bp). A few stated merit in holding or could have supported holding. The spread: one wanted more (50bp), a few could have held, almost all agreed on 25bp. One formal dissent. This is moderate -- a single dissent with a few reservations on either side. Score: 33.

**FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS**: Financial conditions mentioned as "supportive" and "not particularly restrictive" by several/some. Balance sheet reduction discussed briefly. No substantial discussion of systemic risk, asset bubbles, or financial stress. Housing weakness mentioned as downside risk. This is limited discussion of financial conditions as context, not a focus. Score: 25.

**UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED**: Participants indicated outlooks for labor market "were uncertain and viewed downside risks to employment as having increased." "Uncertainty remained about the inflation effects of this year's increase in tariffs." Policy "not on a preset course." Discussion of both upside inflation risks and downside employment risks. Participants stressed balanced approach given "potentially different time horizons" for employment and inflation returning to goals. This is moderately elevated uncertainty. Score: 52.

**DISSENT_INTENSITY reconsideration**: One formal dissent (for 50bp), a few could have supported holding. This is a range from "hold" to "50bp cut" with center at 25bp. Score: 35.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 58 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants observed that inflation had moved up since the beginning of the year and remained somewhat above the Committee's 2 percent longer-run goal."
- "A majority of participants emphasized upside risks to their outlooks for inflation, pointing to inflation readings moving further from 2 percent, continued uncertainty about the effects of tariffs, the possibility that elevated inflation proves to be more persistent than currently expected."
- "Some remarked that these effects appeared to have been somewhat muted to date relative to expectations."

**Center-of-gravity**: Inflation somewhat above target with a majority seeing upside risks, but some noting muted tariff pass-through and well-anchored expectations. The "somewhat elevated" language and upside risk emphasis place this modestly above 50.

**Minority views**: A couple noted that excluding tariffs, inflation would be close to target. A few noted productivity reducing pressures. Some perceived less upside risk than earlier in the year.

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP: 46 +/-6 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Growth of economic activity slowed in the first half of the year relative to last year."
- "Lower consumption growth had contributed to the slowdown in the growth of economic activity in the first half of the year."
- "Several participants noted that financial conditions were supportive of economic activity."

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy moderating with growth slowing but still positive. No explicit "below potential" language, but the slowdown and rising unemployment suggest slightly below potential.

**Minority views**: Several noted firming consumption this quarter and strong high-tech investment, suggesting some resilience.

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 48 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants observed that job gains had slowed and the unemployment rate had edged up."
- "Readings of these indicators did not show a sharp deterioration in labor market conditions."
- "Participants generally expected that, under appropriate monetary policy, labor market conditions would be little changed or would soften modestly."

**Center-of-gravity**: Labor market cooling but still near full employment. Unemployment edged up but "remained low." No sharp deterioration. Near NAIRU with slight softening.

**Minority views**: A few saw data indicating softening for longer than reported. Downside risks had increased per "almost all."

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT: 44 +/-6 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Lower consumption growth had contributed to the slowdown in the growth of economic activity."
- "Several participants remarked that recent data indicated some firming of consumption expenditures this quarter."
- "Many participants noted strong high-tech investment."

**Center-of-gravity**: Consumption slowed in first half but showing some recent firming. Investment mixed (strong tech, weak housing/agriculture). Slightly below trend on net.

**Minority views**: Several noted financial conditions supportive; high-income households doing well. Agricultural sector facing headwinds; housing weak.

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS: 38 +/-7 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Several participants noted that financial conditions were supportive of economic activity."
- "Some participants noted that, by several measures, financial conditions suggested that monetary policy may not be particularly restrictive."

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial conditions described as supportive and potentially not very restrictive. This is somewhat loose -- below the neutral midpoint.

**Minority views**: The debate about whether policy was restrictive at all suggests some range of views.

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN: 38 +/-8 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all members agreed to lower the target range for the federal funds rate 1/4 percentage point."
- "Most judged that it likely would be appropriate to ease policy further over the remainder of this year."
- "A few participants stated there was merit in keeping the federal funds rate unchanged."

**Center-of-gravity**: Clear easing action (25bp cut) with most favoring further easing, but balanced by inflation concerns and emphasis on data dependence and caution.

**Minority views**: A few preferred holding; one preferred 50bp cut. Spread from hold to 50bp cut.

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY: 35 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Almost all members agreed to lower the target range...One member voted against that decision, preferring to lower the target range 1/2 percentage point."
- "A few participants stated there was merit in keeping the federal funds rate unchanged at this meeting or that they could have supported such a decision."

**Center-of-gravity**: One formal dissent (for larger cut), a few could have supported holding. Range from hold to 50bp. Moderate disagreement about pace, not direction.

**Minority views**: Disagreement on pace, with views spanning hold, 25bp, and 50bp.

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS: 25 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Several participants noted that financial conditions were supportive of economic activity."
- "Some participants noted that, by several measures, financial conditions suggested that monetary policy may not be particularly restrictive."
- Brief discussion of balance sheet reduction proceeding smoothly.

**Center-of-gravity**: Financial conditions mentioned as contextual input for policy assessment, not as a primary concern. No discussion of systemic risk or stability threats.

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE: 35 +/-8 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Most judged that it likely would be appropriate to ease policy further over the remainder of this year."
- "Almost all participants noted that...the Committee was well positioned to respond in a timely way to potential economic developments."
- "Monetary policy was not on a preset course and would be informed by a wide range of incoming data."

**Center-of-gravity**: Mild easing bias -- most expected further easing, but heavily conditioned on data. Some noted policy may not be very restrictive, warranting caution.

**Minority views**: Some emphasized caution given that conditions may not be particularly restrictive.

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED: 52 +/-7 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Participants indicated that their outlooks for the labor market were uncertain and viewed downside risks to employment as having increased."
- "Uncertainty remained about the inflation effects of this year's increase in tariffs."
- "Monetary policy was not on a preset course and would be informed by a wide range of incoming data, the evolving economic outlook, and the balance of risks."

**Center-of-gravity**: Elevated uncertainty driven by tariff effects, employment risks, and the tension between upside inflation risk and downside employment risk. More than routine hedging.

### Optional Dimensions:

### O1. INFLATION_GOODS: 60 +/-8 [Moderate confidence]

### O4. INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS: 53 +/-5 [High confidence]
