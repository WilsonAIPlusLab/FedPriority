# FOMC Meeting Scoring: 2017-07-26 (Minutes)

## Part 1: Thinking Process

### Document Type
This is a **minutes** document (synthesized summary of participant views). It is segmented, with clear participant-attributed language ("participants agreed," "most participants," "several participants," etc.). The "Committee Policy Action" section uses "members" language for voting decisions.

### Era Context
July 2017: Mid-cycle normalization. The Fed had hiked in March and June 2017, with the target range at 1.00-1.25%. The economy was growing moderately, unemployment was near or below the longer-run normal rate, but inflation had softened unexpectedly in recent months. Balance sheet normalization was being planned.

### Quantifier Distribution
- "participants agreed" / "participants generally" (strong consensus): economic conditions little changed, gradual approach appropriate, balance sheet normalization appropriate soon
- "most" (strong majority): inflation would stabilize around 2% over medium term; preferred to defer balance sheet start date; saw outlook as little changed; thought Phillips curve framework remained valid
- "many": recent inflation decline reflected idiosyncratic factors; balance sheet program expected to contribute modestly
- "several": risks to inflation tilted to the downside; equity price increases and low rates eased financial conditions; banking system well capitalized
- "some": concerned about recent decline in inflation; others worried about labor market overshooting or easing financial conditions
- "a few": inflation might remain below 2% longer; concerned about overshooting full employment; evidence Phillips curve not useful; inflation expectations might need bolstering
- "a couple of": favorable macro factors backing equity valuations; smaller banks taking CRE risk; real estate multifamily pace concerning

But "many participants saw some likelihood that inflation might remain below 2 percent for longer" and "several indicated that the risks to the inflation outlook could be tilted to the downside." Current readings are clearly below target, with downside risks acknowledged. This maps to the 26-40 range ("below target; slack keeping prices in check; disinflation"). The softness is acknowledged but attributed partly to idiosyncratic factors, and medium-term expectations are for return to 2%. Score around 35.

## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 35 +/-7 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Overall inflation and the measure excluding food and energy prices had declined and were running below 2 percent." "Many participants noted that much of the recent decline in inflation had probably reflected idiosyncratic factors." "Many participants, however, saw some likelihood that inflation might remain below 2 percent for longer than they currently expected, and several indicated that the risks to the inflation outlook could be tilted to the downside."
- Center of gravity: Inflation running clearly below 2% target with downside risks, though most expect medium-term return to target. Idiosyncratic factors provide partial explanation.
- Minority views: "Most" expected stabilization at 2%, but "several" saw downside risks, pulling the band slightly wider.

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP
**Score: 53 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "The unemployment rate was likely close to or below its longer-run normal rate and could decline further if, as expected, growth in output remained somewhat in excess of the potential growth rate." "A few participants expressed concerns about the possibility of substantially overshooting full employment."
- Center of gravity: Economy modestly above potential with moderate growth pace. Clear "above potential" language.
- Minority views: "A few" worried about substantial overshoot; most saw modest above-potential operation.

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 55 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "The unemployment rate was likely close to or below its longer-run normal rate." "District contacts confirmed tightness in the labor market but relayed little evidence of wage pressures." "A few participants expressed concerns about the possibility of substantially overshooting full employment."
- Center of gravity: Labor market tight -- at or beyond full employment -- but without commensurate wage pressure.
- Minority views: "A few" concerned about substantial overshoot; compositional effects may mask wage pressures.

### 4. CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT
**Score: 51 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Household spending and business fixed investment had continued to expand." "Growth in household spending appeared to have been stronger in the second quarter after its first-quarter weakness." "However, the weakness in retail sales in June offered a note of caution." "Several participants noted that uncertainty about the course of federal government policy... was tending to weigh down firms' spending and hiring plans."
- Center of gravity: Moderate, roughly at trend. Consumer spending recovering from Q1 weakness, business investment expanding, but some soft spots.

### 5. FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS
**Score: 30 +/-7 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Several participants noted that the further increases in equity prices, together with continued low longer-term interest rates, had led to an easing of financial conditions." "The level of such yields appeared to reflect both low expected future short-term interest rates and depressed term premiums." "Low yields were inducing investors to take on excessive risk in a search for higher returns."
- Center of gravity: Financial conditions clearly loose -- low rates, rising equities, compressed term premiums, search for yield.
- Minority views: One view held that easing of financial conditions offset policy tightening; another viewed equity rises as reflecting lower neutral rate.

### 6. HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN
**Score: 60 +/-8 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Participants reaffirmed their view that a gradual approach to removing policy accommodation was likely to remain appropriate." "Some participants expressed concern about the recent decline in inflation... argued against additional adjustments until incoming information confirmed that the recent low readings on inflation were not likely to persist." "Some other participants were more worried about risks arising from a labor market that had already reached full employment... cautioned that a delay in gradually removing policy accommodation could result in an overshooting."
- Center of gravity: Lean hawkish -- in a tightening cycle, holding rates but signaling balance sheet normalization soon. Soft inflation introduces patience on the rate side.
- Minority views: Clear "some vs. some" divide on urgency of further rate hikes.

### 7. DISSENT_INTENSITY
**Score: 35 +/-5 [Moderate confidence]**
- Evidence: "Some participants expressed concern about the recent decline in inflation... argued against additional adjustments." "Some other participants were more worried about risks arising from a labor market that had already reached full employment." "Most preferred to defer" balance sheet start date vs. "several participants were prepared to announce a starting date for the program at the current meeting."
- Center of gravity: Moderate differences in emphasis and reasoning, but broad agreement on the hold decision and gradual normalization approach.

### 8. FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS
**Score: 52 +/-5 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Participants also considered equity valuations in their discussion of financial stability." "Participants underscored the need to monitor financial institutions for shifts in behavior -- such as an erosion of lending standards or increased reliance on unstable sources of funding." "A couple of participants expressed concern that smaller banks could be assuming significant risks in efforts to expand their CRE lending." Extended discussion of regulatory framework and its role in fostering financial stability.
- Center of gravity: Substantial multi-paragraph discussion of financial stability including equity valuations, banking resilience, CRE risk, search for yield, and regulatory framework. More than typical for this era.

### 9. FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE
**Score: 63 +/-7 [High confidence]**

"Participants reaffirmed their view that a gradual approach to removing policy accommodation was likely to remain appropriate." "The Committee could afford to be patient under current circumstances in deciding when to increase the federal funds rate further."
- Center of gravity: Forward signal is hawkish on balance sheet (normalization imminent) but tempered on rates by inflation softness. Net: moderate tightening lean forward.

### 10. UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED
**Score: 38 +/-7 [High confidence]**
- Evidence: "Some participants... noted their increased uncertainty about the outlook for inflation." "A few participants cited evidence suggesting that this framework was not particularly useful in forecasting inflation." - Center of gravity: Somewhat elevated uncertainty, concentrated on inflation outlook and the inflation-unemployment relationship, while economic growth and labor market outlook seen as relatively stable.
