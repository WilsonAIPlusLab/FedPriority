## Part 2: Evidence and Scores

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE: 48 ±8 [Moderate confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Members commented that inflation had remained remarkably subdued, but they expressed considerable concern about the risks of rising inflation in the context of high levels of resource use."
- "Many expressed concern about the possibility of a gradual upcreep in coming quarters that might become more considerable later."
- "Nearly all the members anticipated a small decline in the rate of inflation in 1997."

**Center-of-gravity**: Current inflation is subdued and expected to decline slightly in 1997, but members see meaningful upside risks from tight labor markets. The score reflects contained current inflation with elevated forward risk concerns.

**Minority views**: "At least some members" thought even potential-rate growth posed inflation risk due to special factors dissipating, pushing the upper uncertainty band.

---

### 2. OUTPUT_GAP: 55 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Further growth in aggregate demand at a rate averaging near or a bit above the economy's potential remained a reasonable expectation."
- "The economy had considerable forward momentum at a time when it was already operating at a level... that could tend to generate rising inflationary pressures."
- "Many observed, however, that the risks to such an outlook appeared to be tilted to the upside."

**Center-of-gravity**: Economy is modestly above potential with upside risks to growth, consistent with late-cycle expansion running somewhat hot.

**Minority views**: None significantly divergent; risks uniformly seen as tilted upward.

---

### 3. LABOR_TIGHTNESS: 65 ±5 [High confidence]
**Evidence**:
- "Very tight conditions in labor markets and some upward pressures on wages."
- "If labor markets remained under pressure, nominal compensation costs were likely to pick up at some point."
- Unemployment projected at 5.25-5.5%, which was considered below NAIRU estimates of the era.

**Center-of-gravity**: Labor markets are clearly tight with wage pressures emerging, though partially offset by benefit cost savings and productivity gains. "Very tight" is unambiguous.

**Minority views**: None divergent; broad agreement on tightness.

**Center-of-gravity**: Consumer spending is above trend with strong fundamentals supporting it; business investment moderating from high levels but still growing. Net position is above trend.

**Minority views**: Some concern about consumer debt and possible stock market setback providing restraint.
