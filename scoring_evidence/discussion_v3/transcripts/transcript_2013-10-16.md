### 1. Document Type

This is a **transcript** of an **emergency/special FOMC meeting** on October 16, 2013. Speakers are individually identified (CHAIRMAN BERNANKE, VICE CHAIRMAN DUDLEY, MR. LACKER, MR. KOCHERLAKOTA, MR. TARULLO, MR. POWELL, MS. YELLEN, MR. STEIN, MR. BULLARD, MR. EVANS, MS. GEORGE, MR. FISHER, MR. LOCKHART, MR. ROSENGREN, MS. PIANALTO, MS. WILLIAMS).

The document is **50 pages** of transcript. However, **this is not a regular FOMC policy meeting**. The meeting was convened specifically to discuss **contingency planning for a U.S. Treasury debt ceiling breach and potential default on Treasury securities**. The debt ceiling had not yet been fully resolved (Congress was in the process of passing a temporary extension), and Bernanke explicitly stated the purpose: to discuss tools the Fed could deploy if the Treasury defaulted, and to debate how/when to communicate those plans.

### 2. Era Context

October 2013: This falls in the post-GFC recovery era, characterized by:
- QE3/LSAP program ongoing (the Fed was purchasing Treasuries and MBS monthly)
- Federal funds rate at the 0-25 bp ZLB
- The economy was in a slow recovery — unemployment around 7.2%, inflation below 2% PCE
- The debt ceiling standoff had just been partially resolved with a temporary extension to early February 2014

**However, none of these macro conditions are discussed in this transcript.** The entire discussion concerns:
1. Staff briefings on debt ceiling logistics and Treasury payment operations
2. Participant views on which emergency tools (options 1-9) to authorize and under what conditions
3. Communications strategy around the contingency plan
