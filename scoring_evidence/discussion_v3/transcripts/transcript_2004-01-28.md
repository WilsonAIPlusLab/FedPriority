## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** of individual participant statements from the go-around and policy discussion. Speakers are identified by name (MR. KOHN, CHAIRMAN GREENSPAN, etc.). The document covers pages 180–194 of 238 of the full transcript — meaning this segment captures the policy vote discussion/go-around portion only, not the full economic go-around from Day 1. The meeting was a two-day meeting (January 27–28, 2004).

**Important structural note**: The transcript segment available contains primarily the **policy language/statement discussion** — specifically the debate over replacing "considerable period" with "patience." There is limited direct economic assessment from participants; most economic references appear as brief context within the language debate. Some participants (Mr. Broaddus) do make brief substantive economic comments. Mr. Kohn provides the most substantive economic framing.

### 2. Era Context
January 2004 — early recovery phase post-2001 recession and post-9/11. The Fed had cut rates aggressively to 1% (the federal funds rate). The prior deflation concern had been prominent in 2003 (insertion of "considerable period" language). By early 2004, the economy was strengthening but employment had not yet recovered strongly ("new hiring remained subdued" per the draft statement). The era is characterized by: very low rates (1% FFR), below-target inflation, output gap persisting, but improving growth outlook. This is the transition period away from emergency deflation-risk accommodation.
