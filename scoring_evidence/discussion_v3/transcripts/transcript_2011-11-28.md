The U.S. recovery was still ongoing but fragile (the economy was approximately 2 years into recovery from the 2008-09 GFC trough). The domestic situation: unemployment was still elevated (~8.6% in November 2011), inflation had been running modestly above 2% earlier in the year but was not a prominent concern. The FOMC had been maintaining accommodative policy (near-zero federal funds rate, Operation Twist underway). However, NONE of this context is directly discussed by participants in this document. The meeting is entirely about the swap line proposal.
