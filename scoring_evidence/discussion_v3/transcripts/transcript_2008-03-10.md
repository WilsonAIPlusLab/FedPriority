### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** of an **emergency FOMC conference call** on the evening of March 10, 2008. Speakers are individually identified (e.g., "CHAIRMAN BERNANKE.", "MR. KOHN.", "VICE CHAIRMAN GEITHNER.", etc.). The document contains both a `go_around` section and a `policy_discussion` section, but in this case they are substantively identical — both reproduce the same emergency call content. The meeting was called on short notice to vote on: (1) authorizing the Term Securities Lending Facility (TSLF), and (2) expanding ECB and SNB dollar swap lines.

**Critical structural note**: This is NOT a regular FOMC meeting with a conventional economic go-around. There is no discussion of the economic outlook per se (GDP, labor market, consumption, CPI, etc.) from participants. The entire discussion is laser-focused on financial market dysfunction — a vicious deleveraging spiral, deteriorating term funding markets, widening spreads, and the mechanics of the proposed TSLF/swap response. The only economic references made are passing acknowledgments that "the economic outlook has worsened significantly" and that financial stability is "truly at stake." No participant offers a structured view on inflation or the output gap.

### 2. Era Context
March 2008 is the early-to-mid phase of the Global Financial Crisis. The Bear Stearns rescue was imminent (within days). The housing bust was well advanced, macroeconomic outlook was deteriorating rapidly, and financial markets were in acute stress. I note this era context but score only what the text says.
