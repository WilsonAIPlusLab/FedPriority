### 2. Era Context
December 2004: The U.S. economy was in a solid recovery following the 2001 recession. GDP had grown ~4% for multiple quarters. The Fed was in a "measured pace" tightening cycle, having begun raising rates in June 2004 from 1.00%. By December 2004 the funds rate was at 2.25% after this meeting's 25bp hike (from 2.00%). This is a mid-cycle normalization context. The era backdrop: inflation was modest (core PCE ~1.5%), output was recovering toward potential but with some remaining slack, labor markets improving but still not fully recovered.
