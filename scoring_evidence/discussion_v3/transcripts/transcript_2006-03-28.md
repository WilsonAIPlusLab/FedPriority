## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** of a two-day FOMC meeting (March 27-28, 2006). The document covers the policy go-around (beginning at page 117 of the original transcript) plus the subsequent policy discussion. Speakers are individually identified (Mr. Hoenig, Mr. Lacker, Mr. Stern, Ms. Pianalto, Ms. Minehan, Mr. Guynn, Ms. Yellen, Mr. Moskow, Mr. Poole, Ms. Bies, Mr. Warsh, Vice Chairman Geithner, Chairman Bernanke). The final portion of the document covers meeting-format discussion, which is largely administrative and not scored for economic conditions.

The document starts mid-go-around (page 117), meaning some earlier go-around statements from Day 1 are not captured here. However, the document contains extensive policy-round discussion from 14+ identifiable participants, giving a broad center-of-gravity picture.

### 2. Era Context
March 2006: This is the Bernanke era's very first meeting as Chairman (having replaced Greenspan in February 2006). The Fed was near the end of a long tightening cycle that had begun in June 2004, taking the fed funds rate from 1% up to 4.5% (this meeting deliberates on a 14th consecutive 25bp hike to 4.75%). The economy was in solid expansion, housing was starting to cool, core inflation was modestly above target, and the key question was how close the Fed was to a stopping point. I note this as background context but score purely on what the text says.

### 1. INFLATION_PRESSURE
**Score: 59 ±7 | Moderate confidence**

**Evidence:**
