## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document type
This is a **transcript** of individual participant statements from a two-day FOMC meeting (December 18–19, 2018). Speakers are identified by name. The transcript is fully segmented: Day 1 contains (a) a balance-sheet/operating-framework discussion, (b) a Desk report, and (c) the economic outlook go-around; Day 2 contains the policy go-around. Staff content (e.g., Andrew's presentation, Thomas's statement draft presentation) is present but excluded from scoring per rules. Only participant-attributed statements are scored.

Participants heard from (economic outlook and policy go-around, in order): Bostic, Clarida, Rosengren, Harker, Barkin, Bullard, Mester, Evans, Brainard, Daly, Kaplan, George, Bowman, Quarles, Kashkari, Williams, Powell (Chairman). That is 17 participants, producing a rich center-of-gravity signal for every dimension.

### 2. Era context
December 2018: The 9th rate hike of the 2015–18 hiking cycle, bringing the fed funds range to 2.25–2.50%. The economy had grown ~3% in 2018, the labor market was historically tight (unemployment 3.7%), but core PCE was running just below 2% (recent months came in ~1.8% annualized). Financial conditions had tightened sharply in Q4 2018 (equity selloff, spread widening, yield curve very flat ~15–16 bp on 2s10s). Global growth was decelerating. The text must override era stereotypes where participant language diverges.
