**Important structural note**: This transcript excerpt covers the **policy discussion and communications debate**, not a separate economic go-around. Participants' economic assessments are embedded in their policy rationale rather than presented as a dedicated economic review section. This limits direct quotation of output-gap and labor-market assessments but does not make scoring impossible — all speakers embed economic context in their policy arguments.

### 2. Era Context
December 2008 is the acute phase of the Global Financial Crisis. Lehman Brothers collapsed in September 2008. The text confirms the economy is in deep recession: Rosengren opens with "the bleak outlook calls for aggressive action," Plosser refers to "troubling times," Fisher says "the problem with the economy and the financial markets is that intermediation has broken down," and Bernanke himself references hitting the zero lower bound. The effective federal funds rate is already trading well below the prior target. This is a historically extreme dovish juncture — comparable to the worst GFC period anchors in the rubric.
