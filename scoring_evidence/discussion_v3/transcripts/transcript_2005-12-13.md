December 2005. The FOMC was in the midst of the "measured pace" tightening cycle that began in June 2004. The fed funds rate was being raised for the 13th consecutive time (from ~4.25% to 4.50% at this meeting). The macro backdrop: solid growth, energy price shocks from Hurricanes Katrina/Rita now somewhat receding, core inflation modestly above 2% but recent readings slightly benign, housing showing early signs of cooling, productivity strong. The era anchor suggests: OUTPUT_GAP around 53-57 (above-trend growth); INFLATION_PRESSURE around 55-62 (modest upside concerns, near/slightly above target); LABOR_TIGHTNESS around 53-60 (near full employment, tightening); HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN around 70-75 (active tightening cycle).
