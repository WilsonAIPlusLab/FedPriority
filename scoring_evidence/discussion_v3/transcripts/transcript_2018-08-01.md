## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** from the July 31–August 1, 2018 FOMC meeting. Speakers are identified by name. The document is fully segmented and runs to 219 pages. It contains three distinct sections:
- A **policy framework/ELB discussion** (Day 1 morning): All participants discuss effective lower bound (ELB) risks and tools.
- An **economic go-round** (Day 1 afternoon): Individual participant assessments of current conditions.
- A **monetary policy go-round** (Day 2): Participants state their policy preferences and vote.

The policy discussion for scoring purposes encompasses the economic and monetary policy go-rounds, plus the financial stability comments. The ELB discussion is primarily about hypothetical future scenarios, not current conditions — I will note this distinction in scoring but still extract relevant signals where participants embed current condition assessments within their ELB remarks.

### 2. Era Context
July–August 2018: Late-cycle expansion. The economy had just posted a 4.1% Q2 GDP headline. Unemployment was ~4.0% and falling. Core PCE inflation was ~1.9–2.0%, essentially at the 2% target for the first time in years. The Fed had hiked rates multiple times and was debating whether to continue. Trade tensions (tariffs on China, steel/aluminum) were a prominent risk. The yield curve was flattening and inversion was debated. This is a mid-2018 hiking cycle meeting.
