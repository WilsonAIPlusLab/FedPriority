## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type Identification

This is a **transcript** of a two-day FOMC meeting (June 9–10, 2020). Individual participants are identified by name (MR. CLARIDA, MS. BRAINARD, CHAIR POWELL, etc.). The transcript contains three distinct discussion phases:
1. A day-one go-around on policy tools (forward guidance, LSAPs, yield curve control), pages 12–68 of the original
2. A day-one economic round-table, pages 88–165
3. A day-two policy go-round, pages 173–203

Participants scored: Clarida, Harker, Evans, Rosengren, Bostic, Bullard, Kaplan, Barkin, George, Bowman, Brainard, Quarles, Daly, Mester, Kashkari, Williams, Powell (~17 participants with substantive remarks).

### 2. Era Context

June 2020 is the acute COVID-19 shock period. The economy had just recorded the steepest Q2 GDP contraction in the postwar era ("factor of 3 or 4 worse than any previous quarter" per Bullard). Unemployment had spiked to historic highs (~13.3% official; Powell notes true figure closer to 20%). A surprisingly positive May jobs report (+2.5M) arrived before the meeting but participants treated it with caution. The Fed was at the ELB, balance sheet had exploded. This is a known deep-recession, zero-lower-bound, acute crisis environment.

### 3. Directional Assessment — Core Dimensions

**INFLATION_PRESSURE:**
