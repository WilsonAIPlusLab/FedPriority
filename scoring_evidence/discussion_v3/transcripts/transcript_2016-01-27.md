The transcript is very long (~5,678 lines) and covers both days of the meeting. Speakers are numerous; this is a full go-around with approximately 17 participants.

### 2. Era Context
January 2016: This is the first meeting after liftoff (December 2015). The backdrop is:
- Equity market selloff (~7% S&P since year-start)
- Oil prices collapsing further (toward $30/bbl)
- China uncertainty and currency volatility
- Dollar continued appreciation
- Core PCE running ~1.3-1.4%, well below 2% target
- Labor market strong (unemployment near natural rate, 200K+ monthly payrolls)
- GDP growth weak in Q4 2015 (~0.5% annualized per Tealbook estimate)

I will score based purely on what the text says, not assume era averages.
