### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** of the October 31–November 1, 2017 FOMC meeting. It is a two-day meeting with a segmented structure: (a) a financial stability discussion (pages 28–36), (b) the economic go-round (pages 36–89), and (c) a policy discussion (pages 97–131). Speakers are identified by name. The meeting covers both Day 1 content (economic go-round) and Day 2 content (policy round). Staff content is clearly labeled (e.g., "the staff reported") and will be excluded. The go-around is well-attended with approximately 14+ identified participant speakers: Rosengren, Kaplan, Brainard, Quarles, Bullard, Bostic, Williams, Mester, Evans, George, Powell, Kashkari, Dudley, Harker, and Yellen (Chair). This is a rich transcript with ample evidence for all core dimensions.

### 2. Era Context
November 2017: Mid-hiking cycle. The Fed had raised rates in March, June 2017 (following liftoff in December 2015) and began balance sheet normalization in September 2017. The economy was growing above trend (~3% real GDP in Q2 and Q3). Labor market had tightened considerably — unemployment at 4.2%, well below the 4.6% SEP median natural rate estimate. However, core PCE inflation was persistently below target at ~1.3% year-over-year, a major puzzle. Hurricane distortions (Harvey, Irma, Maria, Nate) affected September payroll data. Market expectations for a December hike were near-certain (~80-98%). The action at this meeting was to hold rates unchanged (Alt-B), with a strong lean toward December tightening from most participants. Without overriding the text: the text heavily confirms this macro picture.
