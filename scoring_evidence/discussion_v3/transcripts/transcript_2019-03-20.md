## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** from the March 19–20, 2019, two-day FOMC meeting. Speakers are individually identified (e.g., "MR. CLARIDA.", "MS. BRAINARD.", "CHAIR POWELL."). The document is well-segmented with clear speaker attribution throughout. It contains three major discussion blocks: (a) balance sheet normalization go-round (Day 1 morning), (b) economic go-round (Day 1 afternoon), and (c) policy go-round (Day 2). All three are present and substantial. Total word count is very large — confidence is High for most dimensions.

### 2. Era Context
March 2019 is a distinctive pivot point. The Fed raised rates four times in 2018 and is now signaling a pause ("patient" stance adopted January 2019). The labor market is unusually tight (unemployment ~3.8%), but inflation has persistently undershot the 2% target despite tight labor markets — a flat Phillips curve environment. Global growth is weakening (China, Europe, UK). The yield curve is very flat. Financial conditions tightened sharply in Q4 2018 but partially reversed by this meeting. The Fed simultaneously announces end of balance sheet runoff (September 2019). This is NOT a hiking meeting; the vote is unanimous for Alternative B (hold). I will score from what the text says, not from my era priors.

### 3. Directional Reasoning for Each Core Dimension

Brainard: "underlying trend inflation continues to be running slightly below the Committee's 2 percent objective." Powell: "Conditions did not push core inflation up to 2 percent on a consistent basis last year." The nearly unanimous view is that inflation is modestly below target — not in deflation territory, but persistently and frustratingly below 2%. A few (Harker, Mester, Rosengren) project modest overshoots in their forecasts but this is a forward hope, not a current reading. Score: well into below-target territory. The anchor table puts 2013–19 below-target at 38–42. This meeting features more anxiety about the undershoot than typical 2016–17 meetings — the language is "muted," "at the low end," "persistently below." Score: **36**.

**OUTPUT_GAP:**

BRIGHT-LINE RULE: majority language is "at or near trend/potential" — score should be in the 48–52 range. Score: **50**.
