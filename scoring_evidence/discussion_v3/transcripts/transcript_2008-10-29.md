The discussion is very rich and lengthy—high-confidence scoring is appropriate for most dimensions.

### 2. Era Context
October 2008 is the acute phase of the Global Financial Crisis. Lehman Brothers failed September 15, 2008. The meeting occurs six weeks after that event. The broad backdrop: economy entering deep recession, financial markets in crisis-level stress, commodity prices collapsing from summer peaks, emerging markets now also being hit. Without letting this backdrop override text, I note it as prior context that the transcript content should dominate.
