### 2. Era Context
March 2014: The U.S. economy is in a moderate recovery from the 2007–09 Great Recession, approximately 4.5 years in. The FOMC has been tapering QE3 since December 2013 (reducing by $10 billion per meeting). The unemployment rate is around 6.7%, well above the ~5.4% estimated natural rate. PCE inflation is running around 1%, well below the 2% target. This is Chair Yellen's first formal FOMC meeting as Chair. Key debates: how much slack remains, whether to change forward guidance from threshold-based (6.5% unemployment) to qualitative, and financial stability concerns from very loose financial conditions. Winter weather severely distorted Q1 2014 data. Do NOT let this era knowledge override what the text says — use text as primary evidence.
