### 1. Document Type

This is a **transcript** of a special inter-meeting **videoconference** held on December 6, 2007 — one week before the regular December 11, 2007 FOMC meeting. The meeting was convened exclusively to deliberate on two proposed emergency liquidity tools: the **Term Auction Facility (TAF)** and a **currency swap line with the ECB**. Individual speakers are clearly identified (e.g., "MR. LACKER.", "VICE CHAIRMAN GEITHNER.", "MS. YELLEN."). The meeting ends with a formal FOMC vote on the ECB swap.

**Critical structural note**: This is NOT a standard FOMC go-around on economic conditions. There is no staff economic review discussion by participants, no go-round on the output gap, inflation outlook, labor market, or standard macroeconomic variables. The entire document (~938 lines) is devoted to the merits, mechanics, and risks of the TAF and the ECB swap arrangement. This is effectively an **emergency/special meeting** with a narrow, single-topic agenda.

### 2. Era Context

December 2007 falls in the early phase of the Global Financial Crisis. The housing market had been deteriorating since mid-2006. Subprime mortgage losses had erupted in summer 2007 (BNP Paribas freeze, August 2007 liquidity squeeze). By December 2007, term funding markets (LIBOR, commercial paper) were severely stressed. The U.S. economy had not yet entered formal recession (NBER later dated the recession start as December 2007), but financial stress was acute. The Fed had cut the fed funds rate from 5.25% in September 2007, with further cuts expected.

**Important**: I do NOT let this era knowledge override what participants actually discuss. I score only what is in the text.
