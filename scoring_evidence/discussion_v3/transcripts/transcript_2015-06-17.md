## Part 1: Thinking Process

### 1. Document Type
This is a **transcript** of the FOMC June 16–17, 2015 two-day meeting. Speakers are individually identified by name (MR. WILLIAMS., MS. MESTER., VICE CHAIRMAN DUDLEY., CHAIR YELLEN., etc.). The document includes both the economic go-around (Day 1) and the policy discussion/vote round (Day 2). It is fully segmented with clear attribution. This is a rich, lengthy transcript covering most of the voting and non-voting members.

### 2. Era Context
Mid-2015: The U.S. economy is in a post-GFC recovery, now six years old. Unemployment has fallen sharply from its 2009 peak (~10%) toward 5.5%. Inflation is well below the 2% target — core PCE ~1.2%, headline near zero. The Fed has been at the zero lower bound since December 2008. QE3 tapering concluded in October 2014. The Committee is debating the timing of "liftoff" — the first rate increase. The Greek debt crisis is an acute near-term international risk. The text should guide the scores; the era context is a check, not an override.
