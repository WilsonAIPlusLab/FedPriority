### Era Context
October 1993 -- the economy was in a moderate expansion following the 1990-91 recession. The Fed funds rate was at 3.0%, a low level. However, none of this context is discussed in the document itself.

### Key Finding: Non-Standard Meeting
This telephone conference is **entirely devoted to FOMC disclosure and transparency strategy** in preparation for Congressional testimony before the House Banking Committee (Chairman Gonzalez). The topics discussed include:
- Strategy for Chairman Greenspan's October 13 testimony
- Approach to the October 19 group testimony
- Memoranda of Discussion (MODs) and whether to resume them
- FOIA protection of deliberative materials
- Leak prevention
- Whether to release the fed funds rate immediately after meetings
- How to present a unified front before Congress

**There is zero substantive discussion of:**
- Inflation or price levels
- Output, GDP, or the output gap
- Labor market conditions or employment

- Monetary policy stance, direction, or forward guidance
- Economic uncertainty or the economic outlook

The only fleeting reference to the economy is Chairman Greenspan noting that Banking Committee members are interested in "credit crunch, state of the economy, employment growth -- the usual" -- but this is describing what *Congress* cares about, not FOMC participants' assessment of conditions.
