### Key Finding: This Is NOT a Monetary Policy Meeting
This transcript is entirely devoted to a single procedural/legal topic: the handling and potential disclosure of FOMC meeting transcripts under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA). The discussion covers:
- Whether to authorize Virgil Mattingly to consult with the Department of Justice regarding FOIA implications for FOMC transcripts
- Whether to produce Memoranda of Discussion (MODs) as a substitute for raw transcripts
- Whether to seek legislation to protect deliberative materials
- Cost concerns ($2 million estimated to construct edited transcripts)
- Fairness to former/deceased participants who cannot review their attributed statements
- The timeline and procedural mechanics of any disclosure approach

- Inflation or price levels
- Economic output, GDP, or the output gap
- Labor markets or employment
- Consumer spending or business investment
- Financial conditions, credit markets, or interest rates
- Monetary policy stance or direction
- The economic outlook or uncertainty about it
- Financial stability concerns (in the macroprudential sense)
