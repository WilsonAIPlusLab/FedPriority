# Topic-modeling files, status 2026-10-17

`cross_method_matrix.csv` and `cross_corpus_matrix.csv` are the cleaned re-run of 2026-10-07 (`05_topic_modeling/data/comparison/*_cleaned.csv`): FASTopic vs BERTopic r = 0.865 for minutes (N = 265, duplicate 2012-10-24 minutes removed, fixed PDF reader), 0.480 for transcripts (N = 225), 0.782 for the rebuilt staff-combined corpus (N = 312, new staff definition). The earlier files (r = 0.916 minutes on a corpus with a duplicate meeting, 0.861, 0.133, -0.442) are kept in `_superseded_2026-02-09/` for the record only; the earlier figures also came from the damaged pre-October staff corpus and the naive two-column PDF reader. `minutes_econ_ratio_timeseries.csv` had the 2012-10-24 duplicate row removed; its values are otherwise the February 2026 run and have not been regenerated. `transcripts_econ_ratio_timeseries.csv` is the February run. No cleaned decomposed-transcript or conference-call comparison exists.

All of the superseded figures (0.916, 0.861, 0.133, -0.442) also reproduce from the February files, so they are genuine February outputs. The causal attributions in the paragraph above are corrected as follows, and the original wording is left in place.
- "Naive two-column PDF reader": supported for the minutes only (non-word share 12.9% and 12.2% for a naive extraction versus 7.1% for `pdf_extractor`; raw minutes sentences rose from 52,515 to 62,782). It is refuted for the transcripts and conference calls. The February and current transcripts `all_sentences.csv` have identical sentence text (472,503 rows), and `02_pdf_to_text.py` was last modified 2026-10-06, after the transcript texts were written on 2026-09-27. The reader change therefore cannot explain 0.133 to 0.480.
- 0.133 to 0.480 (transcripts): the text did not change; the topic-model runs did. FASTopic is stable (uncleaned vs cleaned ratio r = 0.965); BERTopic is not (r = 0.578). FASTopic-uncleaned vs BERTopic-cleaned gives 0.466, close to 0.480. The best-supported reason is BERTopic re-clustering (cleaning, a new run and a new mapping). The cleaned r = 0.48 is itself fragile: one catch-all BERTopic cluster (topic 0, 53% of non-noise sentences, mapped to Treasury/Bond Markets) drives the BERTopic ratio, and dropping it raises r to 0.742.
- "Damaged pre-October staff corpus" (0.861): the evidence shows contamination and a different definition, not damage. The v1 corpus (62,962 sentences, 275 meetings) has 20,888 sentences (33%) that are unsegmented minutes text (`unsegmented_body` 20,460 and `full_text` 428), and only 213/99/96 meetings of transcript staff briefings. The v2 corpus (133,186 sentences) is not like-for-like, so no causal conclusion about 0.861 to 0.782 is possible.
- "Duplicated corpus" (0.916, minutes): the 2012-10-24 duplicate is real (266 meetings in February, 265 now), but one extra row out of 266 is too small to explain 0.916 to 0.865. The same re-run also changed the reader, the cleaning and the model run, and the contributions were not separated, so the causal sentence is not demonstrated.
- -0.442 (conference calls) and 0.016 (transcripts_decomposed): the cause is unverifiable. These corpora involve no minutes duplicate, no staff text and no reader change that the history shows, and no cleaned re-run exists. "Superseded" here only means that no cleaned run was done.
- Time series: the 2012-10-24 duplicate row was removed only in this dataset copy of `minutes_econ_ratio_timeseries.csv` (265 rows). The `05_topic_modeling/data/comparison/` copy (266 rows) still has it, along with `transcripts_econ_ratio_timeseries.csv`, the uncleaned `cross_*_matrix.csv` and `robustness_report.txt` there, which are all February copies.]
