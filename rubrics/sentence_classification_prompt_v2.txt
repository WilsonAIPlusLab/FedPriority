You are an expert in Federal Reserve monetary policy communication.
Classify each sentence into exactly ONE of 5 categories.

## Category Definitions

1. FINANCIAL -- Any discussion of financial markets, financial conditions, credit markets,
   banking sector health, asset prices, lending standards, financial stability, systemic risk,
   leverage, funding markets, securitization, or financial variables. This includes both:
   (a) Financial stability as an independent concern (systemic risk, contagion, bank failures,
       leverage cycles, fire sales, "too big to fail", financial fragility), AND
   (b) Financial conditions as a transmission channel (credit conditions affecting investment,
       lending standards affecting spending, interest rate pass-through, wealth effects,
       housing prices affecting household balance sheets).
   Key test: Does the sentence discuss financial markets, institutions, or instruments?

2. INFLATION -- Price stability, inflation expectations, inflation dynamics, deflation risk,
   inflation targeting, commodity prices affecting consumer prices, PCE/CPI movements,
   Phillips curve, wage-price dynamics.

3. EMPLOYMENT_OUTPUT -- Labor markets, unemployment, output gaps, GDP growth, recession risk,
   productivity, business investment, consumer spending, aggregate demand/supply.

4. REGULATION_SUPERVISION -- Prudential regulation, supervisory tools, macroprudential policy,
   stress tests, capital requirements, Basel standards, bank examination, regulatory rules.

5. OTHER -- International economy (trade, exchange rates, foreign growth), fiscal policy,
   payment systems, procedural/administrative, Fed operations, boilerplate statements,
   bibliographic references, or genuinely unclassifiable.

## Stability Intensity (FINANCIAL sentences only)

For sentences classified as FINANCIAL, rate the stability concern intensity on a 1-5 scale:

1 = Pure transmission channel. Financial variable discussed ONLY because of its effect on
    inflation or employment. Example: "Lower mortgage rates were expected to support
    household spending."

2 = Mostly transmission channel with minor stability undertone. Example: "Tighter credit
    conditions were expected to weigh on business investment and slow economic growth."

3 = Mixed. Both stability concern and transmission channel are present. Example: "The
    negative feedback loop between financial strains and weaker spending was viewed as
    an especially worrisome possibility."

4 = Mostly stability concern with some real-economy framing. Example: "The sharp decline
    in asset prices is likely to reinforce the deleveraging process occurring throughout
    the financial sector."

5 = Pure stability concern. Financial system health discussed as an independent concern,
    regardless of effects on inflation/employment. Example: "The failure of Bear Stearns
    highlighted vulnerabilities in the triparty repo market that could pose risks to the
    broader financial system."

## Examples

FINANCIAL (intensity=5): "Several participants expressed concern about the potential for
a disorderly unwinding of leveraged positions to destabilize financial markets."
-- Systemic risk as independent concern.

FINANCIAL (intensity=1): "The easing in financial conditions, including lower mortgage rates
and higher stock prices, was expected to support household spending."
-- Financial conditions as support for economic activity.

FINANCIAL (intensity=4): "Illiquid markets, extreme volatility, deleveraging, margin calls,
forced sales, especially in mortgage-backed securities, wider spreads, equity prices falling,
and lending and funding tenors collapsing toward the overnight."
-- Describes systemic financial stress process.

FINANCIAL (intensity=2): "The decline in equity prices and widening of credit spreads
were likely to restrain consumer spending through negative wealth effects."
-- Financial variables primarily as transmission mechanism.

FINANCIAL (intensity=3): "The potential for adverse interactions in which weaker economic
activity could lead to a worsening of financial conditions, which in turn could further
damp economic growth, was viewed as an especially worrisome possibility."
-- Feedback loop combining stability and transmission concerns.

INFLATION: "Core PCE inflation had risen to 2.4 percent over the twelve months
ending in July, above the Committee's longer-run objective."

EMPLOYMENT_OUTPUT: "The unemployment rate had risen to 6.1 percent in August,
and payroll employment had declined for eight consecutive months."

REGULATION_SUPERVISION: "The Board staff briefed the Committee on proposed changes
to the supervisory stress testing framework."

OTHER: "Voting for the FOMC monetary policy action were: Ben S. Bernanke, Chairman..."
-- Procedural/administrative.

OTHER: "The Federal Reserve will employ all available tools to promote economic recovery
and to preserve price stability."
-- Boilerplate dual-mandate statement without specific analysis.
