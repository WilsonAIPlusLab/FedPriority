## Overview

Meeting-level scores of what FOMC participants said, from the minutes (participants' views and the vote record) and the transcripts (all member and participant turns, staff turns removed, roll call appended). One row is one meeting document, keyed by `MEETING_DATE` and `SOURCE_TYPE`. The file has 542 rows (checked against the file on 2026-10-23): 272 transcript rows and 270 minutes rows (265 patched minutes plus 5 freshly scored 2026 minutes: 2026-03-18, 2026-04-29, 2026-06-17, 2026-07-29, 2026-09-16). It has 62 columns: `MEETING_DATE`, `SOURCE_TYPE`, 16 dimensions each with `_UNC` and `_CONF`, the 4 inflation sub-components (score only), then `METHOD`, `RUBRIC_VERSION`, `INPUT_VERSION`, `MODEL_EXCEPTION`, `NA_DECISION_CELLS`, `ANOMALY_FLAG`, `ANOMALY_NOTE`, `CREDIT_UPDATE`.

**Scope in dataset 2.0.0.** This is the only discussion table and codebook shipped. The earlier V3 table (`discussion_scores_v3.csv`), its codebook (`CODEBOOK_discussion_scores.md`), its evidence (`scoring_evidence/discussion_v3`) and `data/legacy` were removed in 2.0.0 (CHANGELOG [2.0.0]); V3 dimension names such as BANKING_HEALTH, DOLLAR_FX and FINANCIAL_TIGHTNESS do not exist in this table, and no V3 comparison is made or quoted here. (`rubrics/discussion_scoring_rubric_v3.md` is still in the rubrics folder as a historical file; it does not define any column of this table.)

**Priority groups.** Each dimension belongs to one of the dataset's three priority groups or to context (author's judgement, not an estimated result; see `data/derived/priority_variable_map.csv` and the README): price stability = INFLATION_PRESSURE and the four inflation sub-components; maximum employment = OUTPUT_GAP, LABOR_TIGHTNESS; financial stability = FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS, TREASURY_STRESS, CREDIT_SPREADS, EQUITY_VALUATION, BANKING_STRESS, FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS; context = CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT (context for the dual mandate), DOLLAR_STRENGTH (context for financial stability), HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN and FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE (policy stance), DISSENT_INTENSITY and UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED (committee process). PRODUCTIVITY is context but is listed in the map as a staff-table variable only; it is also a column here (see Dimensions).

**Read this before using the minutes rows.** Transcript rows are fresh scores. The 265 patched minutes rows (1993-02-03 to 2026-01-28, including the 2021-2026 meetings) are a *patch* of the earlier V4 minutes analyses, made one fresh LLM agent per meeting against the rebuilt input and a text diff; they are not independent fresh scores. The 5 later 2026 minutes rows are fresh scores. The earlier analyses often cited evidence that appears in neither the old nor the new input, so many old cells were corrected (see `data/derived/discussion_v4_1_minutes_patch_log.csv`). After a blind second pass, 36 patched cells that the second pass scored N/A were set to N/A (`NA_DECISION_CELLS`, `data/derived/discussion_v4_1_na_decision_log.csv`).

**2000-12-19 minutes (v2d, applied, not pending).** This row was re-scored on a corrected input (`INPUT_VERSION` `member_discussion_v2d`, appended 2001-01-03 call cut): 19 of 20 dimensions unchanged, HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 38 to 42. Verified 2026-10-23: the shipped table has HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 42 and `INPUT_VERSION` `member_discussion_v2d` for this row (the only v2d row; `METHOD` is still `patch_of_v4_analysis`). `data/derived/discussion_v4_1_minutes_vs_transcripts.csv` carries the same value (diff 7 to 11, as recorded in CHANGELOG 1.3.0; not recomputed here). The one-agent patch output `04_scoring/discussion/v4_1_scores/minutes_patch/all_scores_disc_m_v411_patched_v2d.csv` is in the project repository only, not in this dataset folder.

## Variables

| Variable | Description |
|---|---|
| MEETING_DATE | ISO date, the date in the source document's name (not always the start date). For minutes it is the last day of the meeting for 162 two-day meetings and the first day for 9; for transcripts it is likewise the date of the transcript file. Match minutes to transcripts within one day (`data/derived/discussion_v4_1_minutes_vs_transcripts.csv` does this: 224 pairs, 215 on the same date; the pair counts are taken from the README and CHANGELOG and were not recomputed here). Ten score dates are the end date of a two-day meeting and are not a `meeting_date` in `meeting_registry.csv`, so an exact-date join to the registry fails for them: 1996-01-31, 1996-07-03, 1997-02-05, 1997-07-02, 1998-02-04, 1998-07-01, 1999-02-03, 1999-06-30, 2012-10-24 (transcript rows) and 2003-09-16 (transcript and minutes rows). Use `data/derived/meeting_key_map.csv` (table `discussion_v4_1`: 531 exact, 11 inside the meeting span; verified) to map every row to its registry meeting; the registry's `start_date` and `end_date` give the span. |
| SOURCE_TYPE | `minutes` or `transcript`. (Working files use `discussion_m` / `discussion`.) |
| `<DIM>`, `<DIM>_UNC`, `<DIM>_CONF` | Score 1 to 100 (50 neutral, never 0) or `N/A`; uncertainty band written `+-N`; confidence `High`, `Moderate` or `Low`. When the score is `N/A`, so are `_UNC` and `_CONF`. The four inflation sub-components carry a score only. |
| METHOD | `fresh_score` (272 transcripts and the 5 2026 minutes) or `patch_of_v4_analysis` (265 minutes, including the v2d row). Minutes rows are patched, not fresh, except the 5 from 2026. |
| RUBRIC_VERSION | `v4.1.1` where the row was re-scored with the V4.1.1 clarifications (71 transcripts) or freshly scored under it (the 5 2026 minutes; 76 rows in all), else `v4.1` (201 transcripts, 265 patched minutes). Scoring vintage differs across rows (see the vintage-drift item at the end of the limits; it applies to the headline results and to every use of levels); the second pass used v4.1.1 but did not replace patched values except as described above. |
| INPUT_VERSION | `member_discussion_v2` (201 transcripts, 264 patched minutes), `member_discussion_v2b` (71 transcripts; First Vice Presidents and acting presidents added), `member_discussion_v2c` (the 5 2026 minutes) or `member_discussion_v2d` (1 patched minutes row, 2000-12-19). Counts verified against the file. |
| MODEL_EXCEPTION | `opus` for the 12 transcripts scored on Opus instead of the default Sonnet agent. Minutes-patch agents' models were not tracked per document (blank for all 270 minutes rows). |
| NA_DECISION_CELLS | Semicolon list of dimensions in this minutes row set to N/A by the 2026-10-07 decision. |
| CREDIT_UPDATE | `v411_blind_check_2026-10-08` on the 16 transcript rows whose CREDIT_SPREADS, `_UNC` and `_CONF` were replaced after a blind re-check under the V4.1.1 clarifications (first run: `N/A`); blank elsewhere. The other dimensions of those rows keep their first-run values. See `data/derived/discussion_v4_1_credit_update_log.csv`. |
| ANOMALY_FLAG, ANOMALY_NOTE | Agent's own flag and note. `Yes` on most transcript rows (framework sessions, missing staff answers, empty turns), so it does not discriminate. |

## Conventions and limits

- N/A cells (file counts, 542 rows). Beyond the thin-dimension N/A cells (PRODUCTIVITY, TREASURY_STRESS, EQUITY_VALUATION, BANKING_STRESS, DOLLAR_STRENGTH and the inflation sub-components, which are N/A in many rows of both source types; counts recomputed 2026-10-23, for example TREASURY_STRESS 391 of 542, INFLATION_HOUSING 421), N/A also occurs in the dimensions marked "1-100 or N/A" above, all in transcript rows except CREDIT_SPREADS: INFLATION_PRESSURE 28, OUTPUT_GAP 24, LABOR_TIGHTNESS 33, CONSUMPTION_INVESTMENT 28, FINANCIAL_CONDITIONS 18, HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 20, DISSENT_INTENSITY 20, FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 14, FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS 5, UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 5; CREDIT_SPREADS 138 (31 transcript, 107 minutes). The special-topic rule below explains the HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN and DISSENT_INTENSITY cells only; the others are cells where the document gave no basis for a score.
- Special-topic documents (no policy vote and no stance discussion: framework, repo-facility, swap-line or briefing-only calls) carry `N/A` for HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN and DISSENT_INTENSITY; 20 transcripts are in that state and 19 of them have no minutes document. Borderline calls: 1993-11-09, 1994-03-24, 1994-12-30, 1995-01-13, 2008-07-24, 2009-01-16, 2010-10-15, 2011-11-28.
- CREDIT_SPREADS: most scores, especially in the minutes, rest on credit-availability wording only (Low confidence, wide band), as the V4.1.1 clarifications allow when no spread wording exists. Of the 201 transcripts scored before the clarifications, 27 had CREDIT_SPREADS `N/A`; a blind re-check (the same procedure as the 60-transcript sample that sized the rule difference: EQUITY_VALUATION and CREDIT_SPREADS agreed within a mean absolute difference of 3.0 and 2.3 points where both were scored) scored 16 of them, all Low confidence (values 33 to 57, uncertainty +-10 or +-12), and kept 11 at `N/A`. Every kept `N/A` is a short call (4,600 words or fewer); every newly scored one is 6,574 words or longer. These 16 cells are marked in `CREDIT_UPDATE`. The check agents saw the clarifications and the first-run agents did not; both are the same model family. Transcript CREDIT_SPREADS scores that already existed (174 of the 201) were not changed.
- EQUITY_VALUATION: a wealth-effect mention alone is `N/A`; a price move alone is scored 45 to 55 with Low confidence.
- All scorers are LLM agents of one model family. There is no human coding and no inter-coder agreement. Agreement between passes shows repeatability, not accuracy.
- Scorer noise (test-retest, 2026-10-08): 15 transcripts already scored under V4.1.1 were re-scored by fresh agents on the same input, rubric and instructions, blind to the earlier score. Across 223 scored pairs the mean absolute difference was 1.8 points (standard deviation of the difference 2.5; 95.5% within 5 points; none above 10). By dimension the mean absolute difference ranges from 0.6 (INFLATION_EXPECTATIONS) to 3.1 (FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS, where 67% of pairs were within 5 points); HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN 1.5, UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 1.6, FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE 2.2, DISSENT_INTENSITY 2.3. Whether a cell was scored or `N/A` changed in 6 of 300 cells, all in rarely mentioned dimensions (TREASURY_STRESS, BANKING_STRESS, INFLATION_SERVICES); INFLATION_HOUSING and INFLATION_SERVICES have few pairs and low correlations (0.70 and 0.86). The sample is small and mostly 1994 to 2008 (13 of 15), with no document above 30,000 words, so noise on later and longer meetings is not measured. This is repeatability within one model family, not accuracy. The retest outputs are not part of this release.
- Patched minutes versus a fresh score (2026-10-10): 60 randomly drawn matched meetings (20 per era) were scored fresh from the minutes input under the V4.1 rubric and V4.1.1 clarifications, blind to the published values (`data/derived/discussion_v4_1_minutes_fresh_sample.csv`, `..._by_dimension.csv`, evidence in `scoring_evidence/discussion_v4_1/minutes_fresh/`). Over 745 pairs where both are scored, the mean absolute difference from the published (patched) minutes value is 3.3 points (82% within 5, 97% within 10); transcript test-retest noise is about 1.8. Cells the patch left unchanged differ by 3.8, corrected cells by 2.7, filled cells by 3.1. Whether a cell is scored or `N/A` differs in 38 of 960 cells (4%), mostly in thin dimensions (inflation goods, credit spreads, productivity, equity valuation, banking stress). By dimension the mean absolute difference runs from 1.8 (HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN) to 5.6 (EQUITY_VALUATION). The older dimensions in 1993 to 2000 run about 1.2 points higher when scored fresh. The headline gaps do not shrink when the minutes are scored fresh (in this 60-meeting sample, not the 224 pairs of the last item: dissent -8.4 published, -10.5 fresh; financial-stability emphasis -12.5 and -15.6; uncertainty -3.5 and -5.9). Limits: the fresh agents saw the V4.1.1 clarifications and the patch agents did not for the older dimensions; one model family; one run per document, so noise is not separated per dimension; 2000-12-19 was scored on Opus after two Sonnet runs were stopped by the API safeguard. The published minutes values were not changed.
- Rule effect on EQUITY_VALUATION: in the 60-transcript blind check of the first-run transcripts, EQUITY_VALUATION under the V4.1.1 clarifications ran about 1.7 points lower than the first run (mean absolute difference 3.0 against retest noise of 1.1), which suggests a small systematic effect of about 2 points on the 201 transcripts scored before the clarifications. It was not corrected.
- The re-scored transcripts omit the rubric's Part 1 reasoning (the API safeguard stopped many runs that wrote it); their evidence files hold Parts 2 and 3. The first-run transcript evidence files include Part 1.
- Minutes-minus-transcript gaps on 224 matched pairs: HAWKISH_DOVISH_LEAN -1.5 (sd 4.12, n=224); DISSENT_INTENSITY -8.3 (sd 6.89, n=224); FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS -13.3 (sd 9.61, n=224); FORWARD_GUIDANCE_TONE -2.5 (sd 5.85, n=224); UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED -5.0 (sd 8.91, n=224).
- **Scoring vintage drift (added 1.3.0; keep in view for every use of levels).** Re-scoring 44 transcripts on unchanged inputs on 2026-10-16/17 gave FINANCIAL_STABILITY_EMPHASIS about 6.0 points lower (se 1.0), DISSENT_INTENSITY 2.4 lower and UNCERTAINTY_EXPRESSED 2.9 lower than the original scores; economic and market dimensions moved by less than 1 point; minutes-minus-transcript gaps on same-day pairs were unchanged (CHANGELOG 1.3.0). The 5 2026 minutes rows were scored in that later vintage, so levels of those three dimensions are not comparable between the 2026 rows and the 1993 to 2020 rows. Likewise 201 transcripts carry RUBRIC_VERSION `v4.1` and 71 `v4.1.1`. The 224-pair gaps above use the 1993 to 2020 rows only. Minutes rows are patched, not fresh (except the 5 from 2026), and no part of the table has human validation. Vintage figures (44 transcripts, 6.0 / 2.4 / 2.9) are from CHANGELOG 1.3.0 and the project repository and were not recomputed here.
